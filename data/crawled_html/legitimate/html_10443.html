Dance Blender 2025 11/26/2025 City: Vienna Dances: Blues, Fusion, Tango, West Coast, Other ✨ Vision
We envision a space where social partner dancers come together as social partner dancers first and then dancers of a style, to recognize that there is one language of social partner dancing and we chose whether or not to dance dialects of that language.

- 3 party nights on 2 dance floors + a super pre-party, pre-party, and after-party!

- music from professional musicians and international DJs!

19th Rock That Swing Festival⇉ Munich
19th Rock That Swing Festival 2/12/2026 City: Munich, Germany Dances: Lindy, Blues, Balboa For almost two decades the festival has been celebrating the diversity of the dances and music of the Swing and Rock'n'Roll era from the 20s to the 50s. Over a span of 5 days, fantastic dance nights, hot live music, shows, competitions, numerous workshops, dance legends, and passionate dancers from all over the world create an inspiring and unforgettable atmosphere. At the Rock That Swing Dance Camp more than 70 of the world's best teachers give classes during the daytime offering a vast variety of more than 300 workshops. Dancers at any level, from beginner to intermediate to advanced and all the way up to teacher, can create their own individual schedule.
