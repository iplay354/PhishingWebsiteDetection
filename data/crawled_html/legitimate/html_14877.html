Our Story est. 1977

Come join us at Canal View Cafe, your home away from home on the beautiful eastern shore of Oneida Lake in Sylvan Beach! For 49 amazing seasons, we’ve been welcoming folks to our slice of paradise right next to the historic Erie Barge Canal. What started with just 7 tables and a coffee counter has grown into a beloved gathering spot, now seating 90+ friends old and new!

Our walls tell stories! Browse through Sylvan Beach memories while you dine, or grab a seat at our classic antique oak bar, where we’re always pouring craft beers and great wines.

Boating on Oneida Lake? With easy canal wall access, we’re easy to reach by water. Tie up and pop in!

At Canal View, you’re never just a customer – you’re part of our family. Our regulars (who quickly become friends) know that the real magic here is more than our delicious food – it’s the warm vibes, those million-dollar views, and, of course, a warm welcome from Jeannie and her crew.

Can’t wait to see your smiling face soon!
