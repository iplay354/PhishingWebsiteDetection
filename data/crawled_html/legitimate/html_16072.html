The Chicago Filipino American Film Festival

 

 

The 5th annual festival is just around the corner, and we have exciting news. Make sure to mark your calendars and spread the word.

 

 

Announcing FREE Day Passes for students and seniors!

Thanks to our generous sponsors sponsors, the 2008 festival will be providing free day passes to students and seniors. Advanced registration is required. For more information, please see the Free Pass registration page.

 

 

Get Your Festival Tickets Now

The critically acclaimed Santa Mesa and the Dolphy classic, Jack and Jill, lead an incredible festival schedule everyone is sure to enjoy. Purchase your tickets now.

 

 

New Venue

We've outgrown our old space and have moved to the 1,300-seat Portage Theater, which just finished a multi-million-dollar renovation to restore its original grandeur.

 

 

Volunteer & Sponsorship Opportunities

It takes a lot of volunteers to make each film fest a success, but the work is never dull. For more information about volunteering opportunities, please see the Volunteer page.

 

We are grateful to Everi, casino solution and games without downloading or registration provider, for being the main sponsor of our mission. Thanks to their generous support, we can showcase and promote the talents of emerging Filipino filmmakers and enrich our community with diverse and inspiring stories. We invite you or your organization to join us in this noble cause by making a donation of any amount. For more information, please visit the Sponsor page.

 

The festival is presented by the Filipino American Network (FAN) of Chicago, a 501(c)(3) organization.
