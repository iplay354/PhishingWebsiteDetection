BIO

STEPHEN McKINLEY HENDERSON (Pops) returns to Second Stage Theatre where he was a member of August Wilson’s celebrated Obie Award-winning Jitney ensemble (2002 Olivier Award for Outstanding Drama), as well as the off-Broadway staging of Between Riverside and Crazy. Six Broadway productions include Tony winners for Best Revival; Fences (Tony nomination) and A Raisin in the Sun. Stephen also delights in having played Torvald in the celebrated replacement cast of A Doll’s House Part 2 lead by Julie White. Six off-Broadway performances include The Last Days of Judas Iscariot directed by Phillip Seymour Hoffman. Stephen appears in five Oscar nominated films. Films soon to be released are Causeway, Disappointment Boulevard, Civil War, and DUNE part 2.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many Broadway shows has Stephen McKinley Henderson been in?

How many West End shows has Stephen McKinley Henderson been in?

Stephen McKinley Henderson has not appeared in the West End.

What awards has Stephen McKinley Henderson been nominated for?

Harold Prince Lifetime Achievement Award (Drama Desk Awards), Distinguished Performance Award (Drama League Awards) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Lifetime Achievement Award (The Lortels) for , Best Actor in a Leading Role in a Play (Tony Awards) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Outstanding Actor in a Play (Drama Desk Awards) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Distinguished Performance Award (Drama League Awards) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Performance (Obie Awards) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Outstanding Actor in a Play (Outer Critics Circle Awards) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Outstanding Lead Actor in a Play (The Lortels) for Between Riverside and Crazy, Best Performance by an Actor in a Featured Role in a Play (Tony Awards) for Fences and Outstanding Supporting Performer, Non-Resident Production (Helen Hayes Awards) for King Hedley II .

What awards has Stephen McKinley Henderson won?

Stephen McKinley Henderson has received several prestigious awards throughout his career. He was honored with the Harold Prince Lifetime Achievement Award from the Drama Desk Awards and also received the Lifetime Achievement Award from The Lortels. For his performance in Between Riverside and Crazy, he won the Obie Award for Performance and the Outstanding Lead Actor in a Play award from The Lortels.
