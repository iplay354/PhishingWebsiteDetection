Welcome to the
Douglas Blasdell Outreach Program
In memory of our friend, Douglas, so that his caring and compassion may live on

Hosted by the Los Angeles City College Foundation (LACC), all donations to the Douglas Blasdell Outreach Program will go towards student scholarships, Gay and Lesbian awareness programs, HIV education material and other initiatives.

LACC Foundation is a non-profit 501c3 organization, so all donations to the Douglas Blasdell Outreach Program are tax deductible. Upon making your donation, the LACC Foundation will issue you a receipt for tax purposes.
