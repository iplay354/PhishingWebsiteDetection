The Military Hiring Conference Alternative

                                                     Direct Hire Civilian Jobs For Transitioning Military 

CMC Technical is one of the oldest military to civilian recruiting firms specializing in assisting technical military personnel with their transition into a civilian career. Since 1978 we have helped thousands of enlisted personnel secure employment with some of the best companies in America. Positions are available throughout the United States. No matter where you want to go, or what you want to do,we can help get you there. Put the recruiting firm with over 30 years experience to work for you. Our services are completely free to all transitioning military personnel. Hiring companies pay all costs.

 

If you are separating in the next year and have experience in the  electronic, electrical, or mechanical fields, submit your resume online and hiring companies will have the opportunity to view your qualifications. If you do not have a resume, fill out our request form and we will send you additional information.
