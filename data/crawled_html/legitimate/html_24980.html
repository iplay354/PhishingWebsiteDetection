Dominique Donnarumma received her Bachelor of Music degree from the University of Maryland and her Master of Music degree in Vocal Performance from Catholic University.

She has done chorus work with the Summer Opera Theater Company and the University of Maryland Opera Chorus, in productions of La Boheme, Cendrillon, Il Trovatore, Little Women, and Tosca. At Catholic University, Ms Donnarumma performed the roles of Cornelia in Giulio Cesare, and the Old Lady in Candide. Ms. Donnarumma originated the roles of Annunciata in Brother Fire, Mozart in Mozart and the Grey Steward, and Polyhymnia in Michael Oberhauser's Magnum Opus, at Catholic University. Companies: Golden Rose Opera Works, New York Opera Forum, One World Symphony, and New York Lyric Opera Theatre. Notable roles: Armida (Rinaldo), Micaëla (Carmen), and Skyler White (Breaking Bad: Ozymandias).

Ms. Donnarumma was a prizewinner for the 2007-08 and 2008-09 Barry Alexander International Vocal Competition. In January of 2009, Ms. Donnarumma made her Carnegie Hall debut in the Barry Alexander 2008-2009 Prizewinner's Recital, in Weill Hall. She later returned to Carnegie Hall to participate in the 2009 Alexander and Buono Gala. Ms Donnarumma is a student of Michael Warren.
