WELCOME TO THE BALLYMENA SHAMROCK CSC WEBSITE

Welcome to the official home of Ballymena Shamrock Celtic Supporters Club; we are based in County Antrim, in the north of Ireland. We are a CSC with a total membership of over 120, with members from all over Counties Antrim and Derry.

Founded in 1990, Ballymena Shamrock CSC has continuously served our community with a family ethos, we provide for boys and girls of all age groups from a few years old to members in their 80’s. The clubs main aim is to provide the cheapest means possible to get Celtic fans to see Celtic, and at the same time we enjoy a social setting where our members, their families and their friends can have a bit of craic.

The aim of the site is to promote the image of both Celtic FC and Ballymena Shamrock CSC in a positive manner as well as serving as a source of information for all Hoops fans at home and abroad. Enjoy perusing through our club web site and it's activities. Hail Hail.

Celts Sparkle After Aoife Leads Them Out

Submitted by Ballymena Shamrock CSC on Thu, 01/12/2011 - 8:55pm

Young Aoife Webb proved to be a real lucky mascot for the hoops when she led the team out for Saturdays SPL encounter with St Mirren.

Following taking part in the pre-match preliminaries with captain Beram Kayal, the 11 year old from Maghera took up one of the best seats in the house to watch the action.

And what a game to be chosen for as the youngster from County ...... Derry lol witnessed one of the best performances of the season so far with the celts running out 5-0 winners.
