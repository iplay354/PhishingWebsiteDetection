Camp Fire Inland Northwest has proudly served as an innovative leader in youth development since 1914, fostering a legacy of empowering young people.

Programming for all local youth is available year-round:

Camp Sweyolakan has something for everyone! Located on Lake Coeur d'Alene, campers have the chance to unplug from technology and explore the natural world around them. Join us during the summer for overnight & day camp sessions.

Click here for Camp Sweyolakan

Nestled on the Little Spokane River, Camp Dart-Lo offers youth a place to explore the rustic outdoors close to home. Join us for day camps throughout the summer and shorter sessions during the school-year during breaks.

Click here for Camp Dart-Lo

Leadership, outdoor experiences, environmental awareness, friendship and fun play a big part in our Compass Clubs! Programs are held once a month in Spokane Valley for 1st-5th graders. Campers can join at anytime throughout the year!

Click here for Compass Clubs
