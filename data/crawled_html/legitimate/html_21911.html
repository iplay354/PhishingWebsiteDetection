Lessard - Collins Family Ancestors

Below is a reverse genealogical tree of my mother's, father's parents, Dona J. Lessard and Isa May Collins from their generation and back. I am in the process of replacing my original 2010 research that was mainly based on the speculation of others used in their own trees online with a tree that can be verified with historical records. These include state birth, death and marriage records, US Census records, cemetery records and local directories. I have also referred to published texts were cited. The main tree includes generations 1-6, where the numbers correspond to the number of "greats" of my great-grandparents. Generation 6 is numbered 6.01 through 6.64 and continued below the main tree in their respective sections. The portion in blue has been fully revised, but the portion in red should be used with caution.

Collins-Webster Section

Isa May Collins' parents were Oren Eugene Collins and Flora Mary Webster. With few exceptions, Isa's Ancestors arrived in Amesbury, Salisbury, or Haverhill, MA or Hampton, NH from England in the early to middle 1600's. Several branches moved to Kingston, NH by around 1750. By 1800, they were converging on Hawke, NH, which later became Danville, NH. Overall, these families' net migration following their initial arrival in New England was less than 20 miles during approximately 350 years.
