Improving healthcare through education, research, and interdisciplinary collaboration in bioethics and medical humanities

The Center for Bioethics and Medical Humanities supports both the Institute for Public Health and Medicine and the Institute for Artificial Intelligence in Medicine. We enhance the educational and research missions of these institutes and all of Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine.

Master of Arts in Medical Humanities & Bioethics

Our MA program examines medicine and its ethical implications from the diverse perspectives of the humanities, including philosophy, history, literature, anthropology, and the law. Learn more about our dual-degree and other options.

Education in Palliative and End-of-life Care

The EPEC Program educates healthcare professionals in communication, ethical decision-making, psychosocial considerations, and symptom management. Learn more about the diverse training options offered.
