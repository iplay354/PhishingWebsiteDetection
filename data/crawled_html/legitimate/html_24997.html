A Truly Canadian Artist

Donald M. Flather was a multi-talented man who combined his love of the outdoors with his love of art to produce many wonderful works over his lifetime. His paintings are widely held in Canada, the US and Britain.

This Website has been established as an eternal memorial devoted to the life and art of Donald M. Flather to display all of his works in a virtual collection. It is intended to allow anyone to enjoy the works of Donald M. Flather.
