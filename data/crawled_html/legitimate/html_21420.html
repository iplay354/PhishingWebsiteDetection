Gideon's Way (1964-65)
   US title: 'Gideon, C.I.D.'
Episode Guide  compiled by The Classic TV Archive
with contributions by:
Trevor Douglas (New South Wales, Australia)(all episodes)
Alan Keeling (Warley, West Midlands, UK), David Moore (UK)
references:
Library of Congress (telnet://locis.loc.gov)
Internet Movie Database (https://www.us.imdb.com)

Network DVD
New World production
for
ITC-Incorporated Television Company, Ltd. (Lew Grade)
Executive Producers Robert S. Baker and Monty Berman
Script Supervisor Harry W. Junkin
Based on the character created by John Creasey

UK Police series 1964-65   26 episodes x 60 min bw
starring
John Gregson as Commander George Gideon
with
Alexander Davion as Det. Chief Insp. David Keen
Daphne Anderson as Kate Gideon
Richard James as Mathew Gideon
Giles Watling as Malcom Gideon
Andrea Allan as Prudence Gideon

Premise:
Cases of a CID Police Inspector in London's Scotland Yard.

John Gregson also starred in - Shirley's World (ITC) (1971) starring Shirley Maclaine
