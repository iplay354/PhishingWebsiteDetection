About Dr. Lent

Beverly Hills plastic surgeon Dr. Warren Lent, a diplomate of both the American Board of Plastic Surgery (ABPS) and the American Board of Surgery (ABS), specializes in nasal reshaping, treatment of facial aging, and breast and body contouring. A caring plastic surgeon known for attention to detail, Dr. Lent’s expertise has been featured on such television shows as Good Day LA, Dateline, KTLA, Extra as well as in multiple national and local publications.
