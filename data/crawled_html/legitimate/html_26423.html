Welcome to East Bay Shuttle!


  • East Bay Shuttle provide quality transportation services from East Bay to all major airports: We are Bay area airport shuttle serving San Francisco Airport, Oakland Airport, San Jose Airport and Amtrak Emeryville, Oakland train stations, or the Port of San Francisco Cruise Ships. East Bay Shuttle are a Walnut Creek and San Ramon based airport transportation company that services not only the entire Contra Costa counties but also San Francisco, San Jose , Marin ,Solano and Alameda counties .East Bay Shuttle are committed to providing you with excellent service. East Bay Shuttle are equipped with a full fleet of luxury vans, friendly and reliable drivers.

Instead of having the hassle to drive and park at the airport, leave your car safe at  home and relax in a East Bay Shuttle van traveling to your destination. No matter what time of the day or night you want to travel, we can pick you up. Please make a reservation ahead of time and you will be all set for your trip. Pricing is very competitive compared to the prices of a taxi cab, a private sedan or other shuttle companies. East Bay Shuttle is the best way to travel.

Our luxurious vans will come to pick you up from your location and take you to the destination of your choice. We also can pick you up at your port arrival and return you to your Contra Costa , Solano or Alameda County destination. We provide service every day. We do request that you make your reservation at least 24 hours in advance. 

East Bay Shuttle provides DIRECT, PRIVATE, NO STOPS door to door airport shuttle service from Contra Costa, Solano, and Alameda Counties to all major Bay Area airports (San Francisco, Oakland and San Jose; Amtrak Emeryville train station, or the Port of San Francisco cruise ships). In addition, we provide shuttle service to the San Francisco Ports (PIERS) and (ALL) Amtrak rail stations. We also provide transportation services to parties, weddings, golf, and wine country tours. Charter Shuttles for DIRECT, NO STOPS service, from your location to your destination.

This means:

  • No More Waiting - We will pick you up at the time most convenient for YOU. 
  • Fastest ride - We will drop you off directly at your destination, no intermediate stops.
  • No Hassle - We have a fleet of recently purchased shuttles, driven by professional and courteous drivers that know their way around the Bay Area. 


  • Cheap Rates - We offer competitive prices, and, additional discounts are often available

  • We also provide charter vans for weddings, parties, or any other occasion. Hourly rates available. For your convenience our friendly staff is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
