Welcome to the Multnomah County Circuit Court, Fourth Judicial District. We are one of the 27 Judicial Districts in the Oregon Judicial Department. The Multnomah County Circuit Court has four court locations: 
  • Multnomah County Courthouse (Central Courthouse)
  • Justice Center
  • Juvenile Justice Center
  • East County Courthouse

The judges and staff of the Multnomah County Circuit Court make the following pledge to each litigant, defendant, victim, witness, juror and person involved in a court proceeding:

We will listen to you.  

We will respond to your questions about court procedure. 

We will treat you with respect.
