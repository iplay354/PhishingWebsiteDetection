Voted Best Greek Restaurant in Orange County

– OC Weekly Reader’s Poll 2019 –

“Stepping into this hidden restaurant, you are transported to the Meditteranean. Soothing blues surround white-cloaked tables that will soon hold feasts of feta and kalamata olives, tangy dolmades (stuffed grape leaves), and the smoothest hummus in the county. The chicken Athenian is stuffed with so much flavor you will forget you’re sitting in a Tustin strip mall. The lamb in the paidakia is tender and herbaceous, its olive oil marinade brightening the rice pilaf on your plate. Do try to save room for the baklava; its sweet aroma will haunt you for days if you don’t.”

– OC Weekly

Our Story:

On April 1, 1996, Yanoula Christakis Wallace opened the doors of Christakis, a restaurant that represented her homeland and the passion she felt towards her culture. Whether you are looking for a quick lunch or celebrating a special event, Christakis Greek Cuisine is the place to enjoy traditional Greek dishes at a reasonable price.

Since 1996, we’ve used only the finest and freshest ingredients to create traditional Greek cuisine. Today, the family at Christakis continues to bring the authentic spirit and warmth of Greece to Orange County. Come visit us!
