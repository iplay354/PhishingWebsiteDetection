Welcome to Dublin Pubs

This site is dedicated to the diverse range of traditional & modern pubs in Ireland’s capital Dublin. We also feature a range of local clubs, bars, restaurants, hotels, attractions and more.

Pubs – For those who enjoy a drink, Dublin’s pubs collectively offer hundreds of beers and whiskies with traditional Irish food and live entertainment.

So many people visit, tour and enjoy every minute of Dublin and its beautiful city features. Visitors sample hundreds of beers including real ales, and whiskies finely distilled and aged. Then there’s the extensive menus of traditional food, perfect accompanied by evening after evening of live music, entertainment, and craic.

Accommodation – Some of Dublin’s pubs and clubs offer accommodation – their cheerful hospitality makes Dublin one of Europe’s most popular cities.

Things to do – Visitors wishing to peruse the delectable charms of Ireland's capital have their wishes granted in abundance. Dublin is a city replete with attractions – from the ancient spires of Dublin Castle, to the historic Guinness Storehouse, to the reputed nightlife abounding with song and dance.

If you’re already here in Dublin and raring to see the majesty, beauty and nightlife defining its spirit, please explore our website for the knowledge you need to enjoy your stay to the fullest. Dublin’s many wonderful venues are just waiting to be enjoyed by holidaymakers and local residents alike. Browse our site and book a holiday if you haven’t already. Go on a tour, see an event, visit a restaurant or just take a pew, breathe, and take in the cosmopolitan atmosphere.

If you like our website and find it useful, or have any other feedback, please let us know what you liked and didn’t like. We’d also love to hear how your trip went – get in touch, we may even publish your story with your permission.
