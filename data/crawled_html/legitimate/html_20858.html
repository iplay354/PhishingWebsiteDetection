craig kelly is my copilot

All profits from sticker sales go to the Craig Kelly Memorial Scholarship run by Avalanche Canada, supporting education and safety in the backcountry.

Celebrate the legacy of snowboarder Craig Kelly with this two-pack of the classic “Craig Kelly Is My Copilot” stickers. These stickers have been designed to mirror the original sticker design.

These stickers are laminated for durability and suitable for outdoor use and they are sticky! Each sticker measures 1.5 inches tall and 9 inches wide. Stick 'em on your snowboards, cars, skateboards, friends, skis, dogs, etc.

I run this project with my kids. We don’t make a profit. We want the world to have these stickers and remember Craig Kelly.

I read Eric Blemhs epic book The Darkest White about Craig, who was one of my childhood heroes, realized these stickers were no longer available and fired up this project with my kids. Please contact me if you have any questions or concerns.
