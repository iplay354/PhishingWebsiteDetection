Camps & outdoor education

Camp Fire Camp El Tesoro offers overnight summer camp, nature day camp, grief camp, outdoor education field trips, family camping weekends, plus camp rentals and retreats.

afterschool &
teens in action

Afterschool programs featuring STEAM based curriculum in Fort Worth & Aledo ISDs, summer and school break day camps, and Teens in Action leadership program.

early education workforce development

Camp Fire is committed to preparing the early childhood workforce through various educational programs to meet the needs of educators at every stage of their careers.

Youth thrive here!

Camp Fire First Texas has been an inclusive, innovative leader in youth development since 1914.

Woven into every Camp Fire experience is our research-based and measurable framework for thriving. We provide opportunities for youth to grow and succeed in life. Programs include camping, licensed afterschool enrichment, teen services, environmental education, and school readiness, in addition to professional development for early educators.

In Camp Fire, children and youth find a safe, fun and inclusive place – a place where they form lasting relationships, develop a sense of belonging and make positive contributions to the lives of their families and their community.

CAMP FIRE CREATES POWERFUL YOUTH EXPERIENCES

Through playful, inclusive, affordable, and powerful out-of-class experiences Camp Fire connects young people to the outdoors, to others, and to themselves. We provide youth and educators with a space where they feel like they truly belong.
Camp Fire’s programs enable people to develop essential skills that have long-term benefits and make a positive social impact in the world.
