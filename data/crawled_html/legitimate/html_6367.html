South Florida’s First Choice FBO

In 1979, Banyan Air Service set the lofty goal of becoming the premier South Florida FBO for business aviation. In 2006, and every year since, we accomplish our goal by providing exceptional aviation services to our customers through dedication to quality, safety, and efficiency while maintaining a commitment to growth and the development of our team.

Our 24-hour, full-service fixed base operation is conveniently located between Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach which makes Banyan the perfect gateway for flights to and from the Bahamas, Caribbean, and South America.

  • Fuel and ground services, 24/7, 365 days a year

  • Avionics installations and service
