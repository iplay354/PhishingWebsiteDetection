A Beverly Hills Furrier with Over 75  Years of History

Founded in 1940 by Merrill Lowell, Edwards-Lowell began as a visionary fashion house blending artistic inspiration with advanced fur techniques. From herringbone-textured mink to custom couture designs, Lowell set the gold standard in American fur fashion.


Over the decades, our pieces have been worn by some of the most iconic names in entertainment — Joan Crawford, Ella Fitzgerald, Mae West, Rosalind Russell, and Sammy Davis, to name a few. With a permanent presence in Beverly Hills for most of our history, we’ve served as both a fashion destination and a trusted service provider for luxury outerwear across the region.

Complete Fur Services On-Premises

Unlike most modern retailers, Edwards-Lowell operates a full-service facility on-site. Your garments never leave our care. We proudly offer:

Cold Storage

Our two-story, climate- and humidity-controlled vault ensures that your furs, leathers, and luxury outerwear are safely preserved year-round. This facility is located on-site in Los Angeles for optimal control, accessibility, and turnaround time.

Learn More

Cleaning and Conditioning

We provide fur-safe cleaning and conditioning using proprietary techniques. Services include inspection, stain removal, lining cleaning, reconditioning of natural oils, and expert glazing. Our team handles furs, shearling, leather, wool, and fur-trimmed garments.

Learn More

Repairs and Restyling

Our master furriers repair everything from minor seam openings and loose hooks to full pelt replacements. We also offer restyling services to modernize older garments into vests, jackets, or accessories with updated silhouettes.

Learn More

Fur Rentals

Our extensive collection of vintage and designer fur garments is available for studio shoots, music videos, television productions, red carpet events, and other professional styling needs. Both men’s and women’s garments are available by request.﻿

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you store garments on-site?

Yes. Our cold storage vault is located in our Los Angeles facility and does not involve any offsite third parties.

Can I rent a fur for one day?

Yes. Rentals are available for short-term needs, including single-day use for photo shoots or events.

Do you clean leathers or shearlings?

Yes. We offer cleaning services for shearlings, leathers, wool coats, and fur-trimmed outerwear.

Can older furs be restyled?

Absolutely. Our team can modernize vintage pieces into custom designs such as vests or accessories.

Do I need an appointment to visit the salon?

Appointments are encouraged for personalized service, but walk-ins are welcome during business hours.
