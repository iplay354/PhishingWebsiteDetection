Incident: Colgan DH8D near Toronto on Nov 7th 2011, open door indication
By Simon Hradecky, created Thursday, Nov 10th 2011 21:07Z, last updated Thursday, Nov 10th 2011 21:07Z

A Colgan Air de Havilland Dash 8-400, registration N345NG performing flight 9L-4901 from Toronto,ON (Canada) to Newark,NJ (USA), was climbing out of Toronto's runway 24R when the crew reported an open door indication and diverted to Buffalo,NY (USA) for a safe landing.

The aircraft was able to continue the journey after about one hour on the ground and reached Newark with a delay of 90 minutes.
