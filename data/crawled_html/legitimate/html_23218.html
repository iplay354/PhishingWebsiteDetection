See you at La Veillée de l’avant-veille!

December 3, 2024

De Temps Antan will be celebrating its 20th anniversary at La Veillée de l’avant-veille on 30 December at Club Soda in Montreal. It’s an invitation from Le Vent du Nord, who is hosting the event for 27 years! Come and dance and sing along with two exceptional traditional bands!
