Boston Mineral Club

One of the largest mineral clubs in New England, serving Eastern Massachusetts and beyond, for those who love rocks, minerals, fossils, geology, gemstones, lapidary, crystallography, and anything else mineral related.

You can join us at our Monthly Meetings on the Harvard University campus.
