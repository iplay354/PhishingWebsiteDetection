Local Real Estate Expertise!

B.I.C. Realty provides unparalleled real estate services to buyers and sellers of homes, lake cabins and lots, land and commercial property throughout Northern Minnesota. We represent our valuable clients with trust and integrity on all types of real estate transactions.  With over 150+ years of experience, our  professional real estate team is waiting to give you personalized attention and the professional service you deserve in buying or selling property.   

We provide real estate services in Northern Minnesota from the Iron Range to the Canadian Border including Cook, Tower, Lake Vermilion, Elbow Lake, Pelican Lake, Britt, Angora, Crane Lake and the surrounding areas.   With its diverse topography, numerous lakes & rivers and unique terrain, it’s no wonder why so many people choose to live, work and play in Northern Minnesota!
