Stan Bowman History as Chicago Blackhawks General Manager

Stan Bowman was named the named the ninth General Manager in Blackhawks history on July 14, 2009.

Bowman was promoted to President of Hockey Operations while maintaining his GM title on Dec. 16, 2020.

Bowman was relieved of his job on Oct. 26, 2021.

For a list of draft picks, check here. Note: Bowman was been responsible for drafts beginning in 2010.

Here is a comprehensive list of the moves Bowman made as general manager of the Chicago Blackhawks.
