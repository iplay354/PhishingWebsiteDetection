Chairmans Foods specializes in producing high-quality entrées, main dishes, sides, soups, queso, and more for foodservice operators and retail stores. Our success over the years has been built on open communication, integrity, and with a deep commitment to southern hospitality.

Where consumers have needs and chefs and customers seek answers, Chairmans Foods is there.

With hundreds of proven recipes and a proprietary product development process, we provide the solutions to serve delicious products to restaurant and retail customers. Whether specialty proteins, soups, sides, breakfast, or desserts, our brands deliver consistent, high-quality, great tasting products that reduce labor and operating costs.
