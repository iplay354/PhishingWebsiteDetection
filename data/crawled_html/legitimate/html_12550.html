Holocene Holiday

❄️ Step back in time to the Ice Age! December 27, 2025, Burpee Museum transforms into Holocene Holiday, a festive celebration of mammoths, glaciers, and Ice Age adventures. Discover how glaciers carved Illinois, explore the survival stories of extinct giants like the Columbian Mammoth and Smilodon, and meet the resilient species that still thrive today. Enjoy themed crafts and hands-on workshops. A perfect holiday experience for families, friends, and curious minds of all ages!

Lights Out!

Lights Out at Burpee – A Flashlight Adventure
January 23, 2026 5:00-8:00 PM

The Burpee Museum, Rockford’s premier natural history museum, invites you to the 3rd Annual Lights Out at Burpee event! Experience the museum like never before as you explore three floors of prehistoric wonders with only a flashlight to guide you. Bring your own flashlight for this unique after-dark adventure featuring real dinosaur bones and fascinating exhibits.
