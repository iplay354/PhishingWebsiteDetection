Cambridge Youth Soccer (CYS) is an independent charitable organization which provides a healthy team sports experience for children and youth in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Currently CYS provides programs for ages 4-18 with an in-Cambridge league and travel teams in the New England Club Soccer League (NECSL).Cambridge Youth Soccer is affiliated with the Massachusetts Youth Soccer Association (MYSA).

Please Support CYS! 

Everyone plays!

It’s a core CYS principle. Every Cambridge boy and girl should have the opportunity to play soccer regardless of family income. To make this happen, CYS provides approximately $100,000 of financial aid annually to all families who need it. Nearly a third of our 2,000 players receive some financial support.

To make this level of financial aid available, we rely on the generosity of families like yours. Please consider giving a tax-deductible donation to CYS and help make the joy of playing soccer a reality for a young girl or boy!

Thank you!
