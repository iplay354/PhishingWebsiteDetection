JACOB(4) 1794-1853

Born in Mason County, Kentucky, the third child of Abraham Chenoweth and Rebecca Kerr, Jacob was an infant when his parents moved from Kentucky to the Scioto River Valley that would become Pike County, Ohio. By the time he reached the age of 20, a veteran of the War of 1812, he married there, in what was then Ross County, Sally 'Sarah' Foster, a Maryland native. His cousin, John C. Chenoweth, had previously married Sally's sister, Elizabeth, and there had been several cross marriages between the Foster and Chenoweth families in Ohio. They farmed here for about ten years, before moving with their four children to Darke County, Ohio. Here they would have eight more children. In the 1850 Census, Jacob and his family is found in Washington Township, Darke Co., OH. Jacob died here at the age of 60, and Sally, after about a year, moved on to Dewitt County, Illinois. She died fifteen years after Jacob, at the age of 71, while visiting family in Taylor County, Iowa. Jacob is buried in the Carnahan cemetery in Washington Township, Darke Co., OH. Jacob's descendants can be found mainly in Illinois, Montana, and California.
