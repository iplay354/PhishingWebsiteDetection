Growth Insight
Rigetti Computing reported a GAAP net loss of $201 million in Q3 2025, significantly impacted by changes in derivative warrant liabilities, compared to an adjusted net loss of $10.7 million. The company also posted a Q3 operating loss of $20.5 million, indicating challenges in achieving profitability while heavily investing in R&D. In Q2 2025, Rigetti's net income dropped nearly 220% year over year, with a reported loss of $39.7 million, which was larger than the analyst consensus estimate of $0.06 per share. The company's revenue for Q2 2025 was $1.8 million, down 41% year over year from $3.1 million in Q2 2024. Additionally, Rigetti's Q3 revenue fell to $1.94 million, an 18% decline from $2.37 million in the same period last year. Despite these financial challenges, Rigetti raised approximately $46.5 million in new capital after Q3 2025, boosting its cash reserves to about $600 million as of November 6, 2025, following a secondary stock offering that raised $350 million earlier in 2025. The company also secured a $5.8 million U.S. government grant to develop quantum networking technology, indicating some federal support for its R&D efforts.
