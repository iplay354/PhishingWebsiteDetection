Frequently Asked Questions


What kind of vehicles do you work on?

With a few exceptions, most Domestic and Foreign Cars and Light Trucks (1 ton and under)

Is there anything you don't repair?

We don't do repairs that require specialty equipment. There are specialty shops better equipped for those repairs.

Can I drop off my car after hours?

Yes, you can. We have a convenient key drop slot near the entry door.

What kinds of payments do you take?

Cash, Checks, Debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and AMEX.
