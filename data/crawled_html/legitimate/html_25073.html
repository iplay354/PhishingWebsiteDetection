Mobile Showrooms

Designer Library

Your Project Manager will guide you through our Designer Library as you browse our curated selection of premium carpet and flooring styles.

If you’re working with an interior designer or looking for fine carpets and hardwoods, our Designer Library was created with you in mind.

The Process

Getting your new floor shouldn’t be a chore. That’s why we’ve created a simple, guided process.

Meet Your Project Manager

Your Project Manager serves as your single point of contact throughout the process, starting with a 15-minute call to discuss your project and plan the time and place to meet in person.

Choose the Floor You Love

Your Project Manager will bring a Mobile Showroom to your home, or you can meet with your Project Manager (and interior designer, if you're working with one) at our Designer Library.

Enjoy Your Professionally-Installed Floor

Your Project Manager will carefully select the best installation team for you and follow up to ensure that you love your new floor.
