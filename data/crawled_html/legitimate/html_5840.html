Welcome to Axium
Continuing Education

Axium strives to provide excellence in the coordination and administration of medical education and research meetings, and to ensure an environment conducive to all innovative trends in medical science and research.

Axium blends academic, clinical, and strategic excellence with flawless program execution to consistently deliver continuing education activities of the highest quality. Axium is unique in its organizational structure, built around a core of scientific expertise and application to clinical practice.

Axium has organized numerous successful Investigator, Advisory Board and CME meetings for major Pharma companies in the US, Canada, Europe and South Africa.

Our Services

Axium’s meeting professionals will provide comprehensive event management services, including site selection, contract negotiation, travel management, invitation design and distribution, meeting materials design and production, registration management and onsite event management.
