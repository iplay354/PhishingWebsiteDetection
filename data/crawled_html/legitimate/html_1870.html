What is Whitepages?

Whitepages is the leading authority in people search and the modern evolution of the phone book, established in 1997. It provides comprehensive contact information, including cell phone numbers, for over 250 million people nationwide, along with access to background reports compiled from criminal and public records across all 50 states. Every day, Whitepages answers over 2 million searches and powers top search tools for people, phone numbers, addresses, and property investments: Whitepages , Property Intel , 411 , PeopleSearch , and Switchboard.
