Scott’s Mortuary, Inc. is dedicated to providing services to the families of the Tri-County Areas (Berkeley, Charleston and Dorchester Counties) with care and compassion.

Since 1982 the Tri-County Areas has trusted Rev. Milton E. Scott and Scott’s Mortuary, Inc. with helping them plan the celebrations of lives lived. 

We serve every familiy in the Tri-County Areas with great pride. We are able to offer a wide range of services to meet your families needs and customs. We will listen to you and your wishes to help plan a celebration consistent with your expectations. We will take the time to plan every detail and help to relieve the burden on your family during your time of loss.

Our website contains information about our caring staff, our services and links to resources to help you get through this tough time. Please give us a call to see how we can help.

 

 

Subject: COVID-19 Safety Plan- Putting Families First 

We are privileged to serve the those who desire our services during their time of need. We take the safety and health of your family very seriously. We always strive to exceed the expectations for every family and will continue to do so during these challenging days ahead. We want to ensure you; we are taking extra precautions in our facility by sanitizing it daily. We need your help, COVID-19 is sweeping the nation and requiring our immediate attention and adjustments in the way we manage our services. Please be considerate and help us as we try to keep our families, staff and facility safe. “Putting Families First”. 

We are soliciting your help by observing the following recommendations: 

  1. If you are sick, please do not visit the funeral home. This includes nausea, vomiting, cough, fever, chills, 

sweats, fatigue or any other symptoms.

  1. Please wash your hands frequently. Use hand sanitizer upon entry and exiting the funeral home. It is recommended that you bring your personal hand sanitizer, facial masks, and nitrile gloves, if needed. 
  2. Governor Henry McMaster has advised to limit public gatherings to 50 – or less to prevent the spread of COVID-19. Scott’s Mortuary, INC has carefully considered this recommendation and adopted it into practice effective immediately for funeral services. 
  3. To accommodate the 50 – guest limit, you will be asked to restrict your funeral service to immediate family 

and close friends.

  1. If more than 50 people want to attend the funeral service, please note that a family representative will be appointed to manage the guest list. Additional guests will be invited to participate via live- streaming if authorized by the family. 
  2. The CDC advises to limit gathering to 10 people of less. The initial viewing of your loved one should be limited to immediate family only. Public viewing will follow this recommendation. No more than ten people at a time will be allowed in the funeral home for viewing. We ask the families we are privileged to serve to help us communicate this to close friends and acquaintances. 
  3. We strongly recommend that all persons continue to follow the guidelines of social distancing; reframing 

from shaking hands, hugging and kissing.

  1. Please note these are recommendations adopted by Scott’s Mortuary, INC. Please remember that your attendance at any funeral service or public gathering is at YOUR OWN RISK. Finally, we at Scott’s Mortuary understand that this maybe an inconvenience but, it’s our standard to protect all those we encounter as well as ourselves. We’re praying that these changes are temporary as we deal with COVID-19.
