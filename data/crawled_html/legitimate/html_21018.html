James A Rhodes State College – Criminal Justice College Degree

If you are looking to expand your Criminal Justice college education, then James A Rhodes State College may fit your needs.

James A Rhodes State College

4240 Campus Dr, Lima, Ohio 45804-4242

Website: www.rhodesstate.edu
General Info: (419) 221-1112
Campus Setting: Rural: Fringe
Campus Housing: No
Student Population: 3385 (3385 undergraduate)

James A Rhodes State College offers courses designed for Criminal Justice college degree and/or certificate program completion. James A Rhodes State College is a 2-year, Public higher learning institution, which serves up one or more of the programs below:

  • Criminal Justice
  • Criminology
  • Forensic Science
  • Homeland Security
  • Law Enforcement Administration

  • Criminal Investigations
  • Private Security
