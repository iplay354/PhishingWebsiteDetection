Welcome to Asian Canadian Wiki!

Asian Canadian Wiki is both an interactive website dedicated to promoting Asian Canadian community and a database of all things Asian Canadian - from artists, scientists, leaders, to cultural centres, community organizations and festivals. We're still in our development stages but our hope is to create and document a wide collection of all things Asian Canadian to promote a sense of community and a means of finding out who's out there and all the great initiatives going on in our community.
