Telus BlackBerry Tour 9630 Available for $229.99 Pre-Order At Best Buy Canada

Adam Zeis
7 Jul 2009

Yesterday we learned Best Buy was accepting pre-orders for Verizon customers, and it looks like BestBuy.ca has the Telus Tour available for $229.99 - $20 cheaper than a direct Telus order. Telus customers can jump over to BestBuy.ca and check out the Tour. Odd that neither the Verizon nor Sprint Tours show on BestBuy.com yet. The Telus online pre-orders will be good for in-store pickup only. Check out BestBuy.ca for more info.
