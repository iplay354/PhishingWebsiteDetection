About US

Comfort Holidays Pvt. Ltd. is an IATA accredited Travel & Tour Agent, established in the year 1994, under the leadership of Mr. Mammen Varghese & Mrs. Sheenu Mammen, who have spent several fruitful years in travel trade. Since its inception in 1994, Comfort Holidays has always understood the requirements of the global & corporate traveler and served millions in the process, in respect of booking of Air tickets, Visa process, travel insurance, forex, accommodation, transport, Cruise Booking & convenient, comfortable travel planning.

With decades of hard work and adherence to ethics and integrity, we have emerged as one of the selected travel brand for Corporate & Individual traveler. We are positioned as a leading b2c & b2b travel agency based in Bangalore, Karnataka, South India.
