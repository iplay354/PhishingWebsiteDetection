Our Stripes Never Fade

Think of your degree as the foundation for the rest of your career. Today’s employers expect more from entry-level to executive positions, and a professional education will help you exceed those demands. At Mizzou’s Trulaske College of Business, you’ll learn everything you need to know to get a head start in today’s competitive business market.

Explore Your Future At Trulaske

The Trulaske College of Business offers a wide range of degree and certificate opportunities that encourage students to pursue a future they are passionate about. Maximize your college experience and build a professional network while making friendships that will last a lifetime — schedule a tour today! 

 

FLEXIBLE AND CUSTOMIZABLE: THE TRULASKE CROSBY MBA

The Crosby MBA provides global access to award-winning Mizzou faculty and an opportunity to earn an MBA at a pace that fits in your busy schedule. The 100% online Crosby MBA has flexible enrollment options for working professionals who wish to pursue their graduate degree part-time and for students that wish to enroll full-time and complete their degree in as few as three semesters.
