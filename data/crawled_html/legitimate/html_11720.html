Four Dazzling Locations in Newburyport, Beverly, Cambridge, & Brooklyn

At Market Square Optical, we believe in centering patients and offering comprehensive eye exams, contact lenses, and designer eyewear. With four locations in Newburyport, Beverly, Cambridge, and Brooklyn in Massachusetts and New York — we're committed to curating an unparalleled eye care journey centered on trust, precision, and quality.

Industry's Best 30 Day Return & 2 Year Warranty

Market Square Optical prioritizes patient satisfaction, so we stand by our products with a 30-day return policy and a 2-year warranty. If you find that a product doesn't meet your expectations, contact us within 30 days of your purchase, and we'll guide you through the return process. Please note that this only applies to non-custom or non-altered products returned in their original condition.

Additionally, if your purchase encounters any problems within two years, we'll repair it promptly. If repairs aren't possible, we'll replace the item. Your trust means everything to us, and we're dedicated to resolving any concerns efficiently, making your experience with us smooth and worry-free.
