Our History

Welcome to Atlantic ENT Associates, your New Jersey Ear Nose & Throat Specialist.

Atlantic ENT Associates offers comprehensive services including adult and pediatric ear, nose and throat care; sinus care for more advanced issues with procedures like Balloon Sinuplasty; treatments for sleep problems like sleep apnea and snoring; and allergy treatment.

At Atlantic ENT Associates, a New Jersey Sinus ENT specialist, has a team of experts, including doctors, physicians and clinicians who are dedicated to providing you with the highest quality of care. You’ll find that all of our physician is board-certified in Otolaryngology and Facial Plastic Surgery.

Balloon Sinuplasty

4 Simple Steps to

Relief

01.
Consultation

Comprehensive history as well as ear nose & throat physical exam. Nasal endoscopy is performed right in the comfort of our office where you and the doctor can easily and painlessly get a clear look and evaluation of the nasal anatomy.

02.
Patient Education

Our doctor takes the time to discuss the anatomy & physiology of the sinuses as well as the procedure from start to finish. We have detailed brochures as well as video simulations that patients can view.

03.
In Office Sinus Procedure

The balloon sinuplasty procedure is relatively short and painless. Patients are made comfortable with oral medications prior to arriving at the office. The procedure is done right in our patient rooms in a spa like atmosphere in less than 30 minutes.

04.
Recovery

Patients leave and resume normal activity the following day. Most patients report feeling relief within the first 24 hours.
