Services

Dr. Archambault

Founded in 1983, the Orange Park dental office of Dr. Greg Archambault and Dr. Hagerty is devoted to restoring and enhancing the natural beauty of your smile. We only use conservative, safe and state-of-the-art procedures to result in beautiful, long lasting results…and we’ve got the smiles to prove it!

Cosmetic Dentistry

If you need a cosmetic dentist, it’s time to book an appointment with Dr. Archambault and Dr. Hagerty! Our practice is dedicated to pairing the most advanced technology with the most personalized and comfortable dental care in Orange Park. If you’re looking to make your smile a top priority – call the Orange Park office of Dr. Archambault and Dr. Hagerty today!

Implants & Restorations

If you are missing a tooth or teeth, it is important to fill the gap to prevent further damage to your mouth. Dental implants maintained by Dr. Archambault and Dr. Hagerty are a superb restorative dentistry solution for our patients who are missing one or a number of teeth and would like worry-free restorations.

FAQ

Is there onsite parking?
Yes. Our parking lot accommodates over 20 vehicles.
Do I need to arrive early for my first appointment?
Yes. The check-in and medical application takes around 30 minutes.
What do I need to qualify for financing?
So qualify for Care Credit, you will need an established credit history and average credit score.
Do you offer financing?
Yes, we offer financing through Care Credit. More details can be obtained from our office on your visit.
Do you offer cosmetic dentistry?
Yes. We offer a wide range of cosmetic dental procedures.
What is gum disease?
Gingivitis is the mildest form of periodontal disease. It causes the gums to become red, swollen, and bleed easily.
What do I need to bring to my first appointment?
Proof of identification and insurance information.
