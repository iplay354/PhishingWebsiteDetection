For adventure and fun for the whole family, Coconut Creek is one of the top things to do in Panama City Beach. Experience the excitement and challenge of playing Coconut Creek’s three 18-hole mini-golf courses: two tropical-themed courses and one sea-themed course. Also, test your navigational skills, finding your way through the awesome giant-sized Gran Maze.
