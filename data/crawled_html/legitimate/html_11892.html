Three Twos: 1966 Pontiac GTO Hardtop Coupe

This 1966 Pontiac GTO hardtop coupe has our favorite Tri-Power engine and 4-speed manual transmission combo. Described as a very good runner, it comes with PHS paperwork that includes a reproduction window sticker and invoice. Some question around engine block numbers might concern the show crowd, but it wouldn’t bother us while running it through the quarter mile, especially if it means the entry price is lower as a result. Find it here at Car Buffs in Concord, California for $33,995 (and lowered to $29,995 here on Craigslist).

1966 was the first year the GTO was a stand-alone model instead of an option package for the Tempest LeMans, and a record number of units were sold. Fresh styling that included curvy rear quarters, louvered tail lamps and recessed rear window helped attract buyers and this car shows that those features remain as appealing as ever. The seller says the car has original quarter panels, clean floor pans and trunk floor and no frame issues. Glass and chrome are described as good and the Fontaine Blue paint is the car’s original factory color.

The black interior lacks the usual console and is described as having a mix of new and original parts. The instrument panel houses the optional rally gauge cluster that includes a tachometer. In contrast to modern cars, the wood trim on the dashboard is made of real wood. The car has power brakes and the optional quick-ratio manual steering. It came from the factory with a handling package and a limited-slip differential; it now sports new KYB shocks and a non-positraction rear end.

1966 was the last year for the 389 engine and the last year for the Tri-Power option on Pontiacs. This car appears to have one of the Ram Air kits offered by dealers at the time (and the aftermarket today) for Tri-Power cars that feeds cold air from the hood scoop to the triple carburetors. The engine compartment looks nicely detailed and the car has correct radiator hoses and clamps as well as date-coded Packard ignition wires.

We like the fact that this car is light on options and has just those that add to the driving experience. Though the seller says the car isn’t perfect, it certainly presents well and looks like it would be both fun to drive and the center of attention wherever it goes.
