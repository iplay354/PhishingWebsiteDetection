All Campus Dining Services (CDS) locations are cashless. If you use Mizzou Meals (dining plan), Tiger Cash or Gold Cash, you can purchase using your Mizzou ID card. We also accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit and debit cards.

Save on Dining

Dining at Mizzou means good food, happy people and comfortable places. Eating on campus is affordable and convenient with all-you-care-to-eat dining halls, restaurants, cafés and markets.

Check Your Dining Plan Balance

Did you know you can check the balance on your dining plan in four different ways? You also can see where your balance should be each week to carry you through the entire semester.

Add Funds to Mizzou Meals Dining Plan

Running low on Dining Dollars? All dining plan holders can add unlimited Dining Dollars except for Level 1 Plan holders, in which a maximum of $200 can be added per semester (or $400 per year).

Add Funds

Work With Campus Dining Services

Student employees work an average of 12 hours per week and training is provided. Benefits include meals at no cost when you are working, competitive pay, professional development training and scholarship opportunities.

Change Your Plan

You can switch dining plans through the fifth week of classes each fall and spring semester.

Tell us What You Think

Text us what you think! We want your feedback about Campus Dining — the good, the great and everything in between. Be on the lookout for signs in each of our dining locations with QR codes that you can scan and text us your input any time.

Dietary Needs

Campus Dining Services strives to provide choices at every location that can meet a wide variety of dietary practices, restrictions and food allergies. If you have a specific concern or question about what is available, please contact us.
