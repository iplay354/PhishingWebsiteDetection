Merry Christmas everyone (even if a day late). I bring goat tidings of goat joy. Available NOW in-game is a ... special bundle for your enjoyment. Claim your own with /promo holiday25

First off is our 4th installment in the Christmas Animals series - the Yule Goat. You can spawn in your very own "man-sized" goat. As in recent years, you may re-eggify the animal and it will keep its original lore. Due to this, the goat will also not drop any horns if it should go ramming around.

Next up is our continuation of the Ugly Sweaters. This one appears to have a drab look at a glance, but imagine that there is a goat head on the front of it. Rumor from the farmyard is that something special will happen if you crouch while wearing this sweater! (has a cooldown of 5 seconds)

I have also included a 2025 Christmas edition of my player head.

Finally is the continuation of the Holiday Candle. This year I have been given opportunities to reflect on various "Why". Everyone is at a different point in life. But we can remember that just like this candle we can hold hope and optimism for the future. No matter how dark it may feel, we can find our way home. Family and home are not set from birth and can change with time. My wish for you all is that you can enjoy your holiday season and rejoin us again next year.

Happy Independence Day everyone!

Later in the day, but I have released some fresh Independence Day promos; available via /promo iday2025. There is a 2025 edition of the Independence Day Firework, but also a new promo entity, Hot Dog. Thanks to Cyberazaz101 for suggesting the concept.

The Hot Dog is a baby wolf that is of the Rusty (brown) variety and will stay permanently baby. A wiener dog. You can re-eggify it and it will keep its lore. It is extremely friendly, so don't expect it to help you fight off mobs.
