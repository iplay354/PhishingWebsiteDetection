Lead Architect

George Clemens

George has been designing new homes and additions in Chagrin Falls and throughout Northeast Ohio for over 20 years. Prior to starting his own firm, he worked at top architectural design firms in San Francisco, New Haven and Cleveland.

A 1981 graduate of Cleveland Heights High School, George received a liberal arts education at Williams College (B Arts ’86) and studied architecture at Yale University (M Arch ’93)

His inventive approach to sustainable architecture has resulted in two U.S. Patents for innovative residential construction methods; his Wall System is the foundation for the sustainable Elements of Living™ home design series.
