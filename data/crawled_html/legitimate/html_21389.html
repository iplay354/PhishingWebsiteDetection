Going Far Together

In keeping with our mission as a socially just, equitable, and inclusive model urban university, CSUDH has chosen Going Far Together as the theme for the university’s strategic plan. This strategic plan is anchored by the pillars Thriving Students, Thriving Educators, Equitable Access, Culture of Care, and Pillar of the Community.

These pillars affirm that every student, faculty, staff, and administrator is supported and empowered as change agents.
