We have turned our farm hobbies into a small business. On the farm we make maple syrup , raise Christmas trees and have alpacas that we raise for the fiber to make many of our soft and warm clothing items and crafts. Alpaca socks are our most favorite item.

Our Clipper blade sharpening and clipper repair business has grown , we repair most all brands of clippers and sharpen all types of blades. We have expanded and are a distributor of Double K grooming products, and a certified repair center for all Zareba fence charger brands including Blitzer, Red Snap’r, American Farm Works, Rutland and International.

We welcome visitors daily to come and see the alpacas and learn about what we do.
