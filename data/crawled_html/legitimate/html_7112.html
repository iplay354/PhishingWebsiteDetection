Becca Bartlett is a dynamic 22-year-old singer-songwriter and performer from Newfoundland who enchants audiences with her compelling vocals and masterful lyrical storytelling. Her unique voice seamlessly blends pop and folk, creating memorable melodies with profound emotional depth. With a perfect mix of ethereal charm and down-to-earth warmth, Becca’s performances leave a lasting impression on her listeners.

A nominee for the 2024 MusicNL Pop Artist of the Year, Becca is a versatile performer who accompanies her songs with guitar, piano, bass, or ukulele. Influenced by artists such as Hozier, Joni Mitchell, Adrianne Lenker  and The Beatles, she infuses her music with a rich tapestry of sounds and styles.

Currently studying voice performance with Berklee College of Music, Becca is honing her craft and collaborating with talented musicians from around the globe.

Becca has earned notable recognition,Including 2 MusicNL "Pop Artist of the year" Nominations, an ArtsNL emerging artist of the year nomination. In 2021 she was awarded the Focus on Youth Performing Individual Award from the City of Mount Pearl and Her 2024 ballad “Wrong” won an Arts and Letters Award. In 2025, Becca attended "The Person First- Mentor Meetup" where she was mentored by the legendary artist Seal. 

You can explore Becca’s growing discography on all streaming platforms and local radio. Experience the captivating artistry of Becca Bartlett, where each performance promises an unforgettable journey through melody and emotion.
