A Future-Forward Educational Experience

The world today is evolving at a rapid pace, reshaping everyday life. Rutgers Business School continues to meet these challenges using innovative solutions for real-world problems. Through unique programming, improved technology, and impactful research, RBS provides a future-forward educational experience.

Top-Tier Academics

Our portfolio of undergraduate majors and master’s programs reflects the needs of today's market. You have the opportunity to earn a degree in a field that matches your interests, and be assured that the curriculum will prepare you for future success. Built into each program is personalized career preparation with a dedicated career management team.

Leader in AI

Rutgers Business School continues to be a future-focused institution by investing in access to AI technologies for its faculty and students. AI is more than a trend in today’s society. It has been adopted by businesses and incorporated into strategic initiatives, which changes the competitive landscape for graduates entering the workforce. Through research, classroom integration, and curriculum changes, RBS prepares the leaders of tomorrow to effectively navigate this emerging technology.

Undergraduate Pathways to Future Success

We recognize that there are areas of business that are currently defining the future of the job market. The Road to Success Programs focus on specific industries to provide undergraduate students with the tools and resources to excel in these fields.

Undergraduate students interested in earning their master’s degree in a shorter time for less cost can participate in our Gateway Program. Take graduate courses as an undergraduate RBS student and earn your degree faster.
