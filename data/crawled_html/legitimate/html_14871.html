Welcome to Canal D' Amour Village

Everyone needs a place to lay their weary head.
For travellers seeking a place to rest and rejuvenate, Canal D’Amour Village at Peroulades is an excellent choice. A family-run beachfront business situated 38 km from Corfu airport, a short 5-minute stroll from the bustling Sidari region with easy access to the finest attractions the captivating North West part of Corfu Island has to offer.

Nestled in a welcoming atmosphere, Canal D’Amour Village boasts two outdoor pools, a picturesque sandy bay at its doorstep, stunning views, and guarantees a memorable visit in Corfu. Additionally, there is a restaurant and a pool snack bar on-site offering a variety of Greek dishes, snacks & beverages for your pleasure.

Our delightful accommodations are situated in two separate buildings, providing a wide range of room options to suit various preferences. Whether you’re seeking relaxation by the pools, beachside adventures, or exploring the vibrant Peroulades – Sidari region, Canal D’Amour Village provides the ideal base for your Corfu getaway.Come and experience the warmth of our hospitality and the beauty of this enchanting destination.

Canal D'Amour: Where Love and Natural Wonders Converge

Discover the enchantment of Canal D’Amour, a renowned destination celebrated for its extraordinary geological formations.
Carved by the relentless forces of the sea and winds, the sandstone rocks have transformed into unique and porous shapes, creating a truly remarkable landscape.

At Canal D’Amour, you’ll find a small beach nestled amidst these remarkable rock formations. Take a leisurely swim, stroll along the sandy shores, or simply bask in the serenity of this captivating coastal paradise. Explore the beach and encounter two canals that have captivated visitors for generations.

Legend has it that one of these canals holds a special power. Couples who swim through it together are believed to be blessed with eternal love and an enduring bond. It’s a testament to the romantic allure of Canal D’Amour, making it a popular spot for couples and honeymooners seeking to strengthen their love.
Immerse yourself in the beauty of Canal D’Amour, let the legend and breathtaking surroundings inspire cherished memories with your loved ones.

FAQ

We are here to assist you and make your holiday experience enjoyable and hassle-free.If you have any additional questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact our customer service team.

Is it possible to visit Canal D'Amour village without being a guest at the apartments?

Absolutely! You are more than welcome to visit Canal D’Amour village and enjoy all it has to offer, including access to the swimming pools, our onsite snack bar & restaurant, even if you are not a guest at the apartments. Whether you want to indulge in a refreshing swim, grab a delicious snack, or savor a delightful meal, the amenities are open to all visitors. Take the opportunity to immerse yourself in the beauty of the village, relish the stunning views, and make lasting memories in this enchanting destination.

How do I make a reservation at Canal D'Amour Village?

To make a reservation, you can visit our website and browse the available apartments. Once you have chosen your desired apartment, locate the ‘Book now’ button to make your booking online. Alternatively, you can contact our customer service team for assistance with your reservation.

What amenities are included in the apartments?

Our apartments are equipped with essential amenities such as a functional kitchenette, private bathroom, comfortable beds, dining area, balcony or patio and outdoor furniture. Additionally, amenities like free Wi-Fi, satellite television, hairdryer, safety deposit box, and linens/towels are provided. 

Upon request, a baby cot can be provided, as well as an iron and ironing board. Wake-up service is also available. Room cleaning is carried out daily, and towels are changed daily or upon request.

Specific amenities can vary between apartments, so please refer to the individual apartment listings for more details.

Are pets allowed at Canal D'Amour Village?

No, pets are not allowed at Canal D’Amour Village. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in adhering to this policy.

What is the parking situation at Canal D'Amour Village?

We offer free on-site parking for our guests. There is no need to make a reservation. Our parking property is private, ensuring a secure and dedicated parking area for our guests.

What type of internet access is available at Canal D'Amour Village?

We provide Wi-Fi internet access for our guests. You can enjoy free internet connectivity throughout the public areas of our property. Stay connected and browse the web without any additional cost during your stay with us.

Does Canal D'Amour Village offer airport shuttle service?

Yes, we provide airport shuttle service for our guests. However, please note that this service is subject to a surcharge. If you require transportation to or from the airport, our team will be happy to assist you with arranging the shuttle service for a convenient and hassle-free journey.

What food and drink options are available at Canal D'Amour Village?

At Canal D’Amour Village, we offer a range of food and drink options to enhance your stay. Start your day off right with our delicious breakfast. Throughout the day, you can visit our on-site pool bar and restaurant to enjoy a variety of beverages, quick bites or light snacks, dishes from our restaurants menu and house specials. Enjoy a delightful dining and beverage experience without having to leave the comfort of our village.

Is there an ATM available at Canal D'Amour Village?

Yes, we have an ATM located on-site for your convenience. You can easily access cash whenever you need it without having to leave the premises

What should I do if I encounter any issues during my stay at Canal D'Amour Village?

Your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities, and we want to ensure you have a seamless experience. If you encounter any issues or have any concerns during your stay, we kindly ask you to reach out to our dedicated customer service team right away.
