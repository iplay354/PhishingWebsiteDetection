JOSEPH FOSTER(5) 1809 -1892

Joseph was born in Pleasant Township, Franklin Co., OH where his parents John C. Chenoweth and Elizabeth 'Betsy' Foster had married. When he was about 9 years old, his family moved west to Darke Co., OH. Just before his 22nd birthday, Joseph married the 16-year-old JoAnna Skidmore, the daughter of Nathaniel Skidmore and Martha Cole. JoAnna's sister would marry Joseph's brother. Joseph was a farmer. They settled in Darke County and had 7 children, all born there. Between the 1850 Census where they are recorded in Darke County and 1859, the family moved west to Montgomery County, IA. Here they are found in both the 1860 and 1870 Census and on records of the Good Hope Methodist Church. Three sons served from here in the Civil War on the Union side, two, William and Richard, dying in that cause. Joseph died in Montgomery Co. at the age of 82. JoAnne preceded him in death, but the date is unknown. Joseph is buried in the Franklin Grove cemetery in Page Co., IA. Joseph's descendants can be found mainly in Iowa, Nebraska, Oklahoma, Colorado, Michigan, Minnesota, Idaho, Oregon, and California
