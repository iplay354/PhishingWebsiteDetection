$30 Life-Saving Curb Numbers

Be Seen - Stay Safe: Bold Curb Numbers Save Time in Emergencies


Do you know the purpose of having a curb number?  It ensures your home is easily located during a 911 emergency, day or night.  When every second counts, don’t risk uncertainty—make sure emergency personnel, delivery drivers, and visitors can find you or your loved ones without delay.  Display your address prominently with our bold, durable curb painting service for peace of mind and enhanced safety.


We use durable oil-based enamel, not cheap spray paint, for long-lasting results.  Our method is to hand-paint using a stencil and paintbrush, as seen in the after photo.


Our Detailed Process:

  • Thoroughly prep the curb: wire brush to remove loose paint and debris, then clean with biodegradable TSP for a spotless surface.
  • Precisely measure and mask with painter’s tape to ensure perfectly aligned numbers and background.
  • Hand-paint a bright white background using durable oil-based enamel, allowing 24 hours to dry.
  • Return to center a stencil, secure it with tape, and level it for accuracy.  Hand-paint stenciled bold black numbers, followed by any necessary touch-ups for a flawless finish.

Our meticulous approach ensures your address is bold, durable, and reliable, providing peace of mind in critical moments.
