First Fruits

This weekend marks the start of Kwanzaa, a seven-day holiday celebrated by African Americans and other members of the African diaspora. The holiday has its roots in African “first fruit” harvest festivals and serves as an affirmation of family and social values. Kwanzaa is organized around seven core principles: unity, self-determination, collective work and responsibility, cooperative economics, purpose, creativity, and faith. On each of the seven days, participants light a candle and discuss one of the principles.

Today’s Other Holidays

This weekend features St. Stephen’s Day as well as Boxing Day in many countries, two post-Christmas holidays with diverse origins.

St. Stephen’s Day

December 26 commemorates the life of St. Stephen, a Christian deacon in Jerusalem who is believed to have been the first Christian martyr. In Ireland the holiday is also called Wren Day, because in the past a wren would be hunted and killed, then attached to a pole or branch of holly decorated with ribbons. The wren would be taken door-to-door by “wren boys,” young boys who asked for money in exchange for burying the wren. The day’s festivities also included parades featuring boys and men—also called “straw boys” or “mummers”—dressed up in masks and costumes or in suits made of straw (pictured below), who collected money for charity along the way. Although wrens are no longer part of the festivities, the parades continue in many parts of Ireland.

 Boxing Day

Boxing Day also occurs on December 26 and is observed in Britain and some Commonwealth countries, particularly Australia, Canada, and New Zealand. Explanations for the origin of the name have varied, with some believing that it hails from the opening of alms boxes that had been placed in churches for the collection of donations to the poor. Others have held that it came from the boxes of gifts given to employees on the day after Christmas, since the work of servants was required for the Christmas Day celebrations of their employers. Today, however, Boxing Day has become a day associated with shopping and sporting events.
