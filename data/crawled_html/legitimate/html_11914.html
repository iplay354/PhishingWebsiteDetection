Re: Can you recommend Hotel near/on Metro Line in Montreal
We're just back from a trip to Montreal and stayed at Hotel Du Fort
http://www.hoteldufort.com which is a couple blocks from the Atwater
Metro station on the Green line. We had a superior room for $135CAD
or about US $95 before taxes. It was a very large clean room with
kitchenette (small fridge, coffee maker, microwave). Not much of a
lobby but the staff was very helpful. We liked that there was a taxi
cue 1/2 block away, 2 grocery stores (including 1 open 24-hours)1/2
block away and lots of restuarants nearby. Would definitely stay there
again.
