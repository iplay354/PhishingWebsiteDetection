Church Planting

A Season of Scattering

At Catch The Fire, our mission has always been to foster environments where people can encounter the transformative presence of the Holy Spirit. For many years, people have journeyed to Toronto, drawn by the profound outpouring of the Father's Blessing. This season, however, marks a pivotal shift in our ministry—from gathering to scattering. As we transition from a centralized place of revival to a globally dispersed movement, our vision is to spread the revival fire by establishing supernatural churches across the world.
