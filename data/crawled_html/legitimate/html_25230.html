Let The Journey Begin

Posted on May 19th 2011

Welcome !

Welcome To “Down The Rabbitt Hole.com”! Here you will find all the files that are necessary for your OS if you use Linux Squared, or Plug-ins and Scripts for your WebSite if you use WordPress as your CMS.

This site is an unusual mix of software for Linux and plug-ins and Scripts that you will need to make your life and Webmaster experience much easier. In trying to come up with a workable solution, I just found it too much of a challenge to supply the needs of everyone spread out over several sites. Hence this one.

This site was originally designed as a WordPress Blog. After reflection I reasoned that I didn't need the weight of WordPress to deliver the limited amount of information you will find here. Therefore, in 2016 I re-wrote all the code to HTML5 and CSS3, yet kept the WordPress feel with the original theme design.
