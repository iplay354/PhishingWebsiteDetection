National Bank of Canada


The National Bank of Canada is a financial institution with a long history. Originally established as the Banque Nationale in 1859, the National Bank of Canada has grown into one of the six largest banks in all of Canada.

What is known today as the National bank of Canada was officially established in 1979, when the Bank Canadian National and The Provincial Bank of Canada merged. From this point forward the bank continued to grow into the booming financial institution that it is today. With a large selection of services offered, the National Bank of Canada is one of the premier choices for those looking for a stable bank to do business with.

The National Bank of Canada offers both business and personal accounts for it's customers. The business accounts at National Bank allow your business to handle most all of your necessary financial transactions with one convenient institution. From day-to-day business banking to accounts for handling payroll and HR, National Bank of Canada is a wise choice for those looking to establish a business account.

National Bank also offers a large selection of services to those customers looking to establish a simple personal account as well. There are various accounts and packages that an individual can choose from when setting up their personal account, so most anyone will be able to choose an account that will work best for them. The National Bank of Canada also offers services such as mortgages, investments, asset management, insurance, personal loans and other lines of credit for those looking for a financial institution that can provide all of their needs in one place.

With more than 546 branch locations, as well as international offices located in major cities such as New York, Paris, Hong Kong and Beirut, the National Bank of Canada is a wise banking choice for those who want access to their money from almost anywhere. National Bank of Canada is also the recipient of many awards and honors, which help customers feel confident that they are doing business with one of the finest banks in the world.
