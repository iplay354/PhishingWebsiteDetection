About D.A. Burns & Sons

D. A. Burns is recognized as a leader in our industry and is highly recommended by design professionals who rely on our expertise for the cleaning of installed carpet, Oriental and area rugs, upholstered furniture, stone and tile as well as the fabrication of custom carpet designs, edge treatments and rug repair and reweaving.

We are pleased that most of our work comes through repeat business and referrals from decades of satisfied clients. They value our experienced service team, convenient scheduling, friendly service and kept promises. We keep our promises, with no surprises!
