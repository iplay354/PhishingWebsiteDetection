Mission

To screen, select, and represent outstanding private equity partnerships, ensuring those General Partners are prepared to efficiently and successfully achieve their fundraising goals.

Our process pivots on relationships, reputation and results. Our trusted relationships with top-tier General and Limited Partners provide the vital link for successful and enduring investment partnerships.

Firm

Headquartered in San Francisco and founded in 2001, Denning & Company LLC is a leading boutique private equity advisory firm providing consulting and fundraising services to both established and emerging private equity partnerships worldwide. We are attracted to challenging assignments.

With over 70 years of combined industry experience, we continually build upon our trusted relationships with top-tier General and Limited Partners to create successful and enduring investment partnerships. We trust, respect and like the people with whom we choose to work.

  • public and corporate pension funds
  • insurance companies
