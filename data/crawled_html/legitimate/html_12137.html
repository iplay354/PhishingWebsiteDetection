The pros and cons of Learning English language courses online￼

June 4, 2022 Bruce Rhodes Language School

Taking English language courses online has so many benefits that it’s difficult to name them all, but it also has some drawbacks. If you’re thinking about learning English online, you’ll want to be aware of all of the benefits and drawbacks to ensure it’s a good fit for you.

The pros

You can learn from home

One of the most appealing aspects of learning English language courses online is that you can do it from home! Instead of wasting time travelling to school on crowded public transportation for hours on end, you can just roll out of bed and start learning.

There is no such thing as a minimum or maximum number of learning hours.

Most of the English language courses online do not have a hard limit on the number of hours you must study, giving you the freedom to learn as frequently or infrequently as your schedule allows.

A degree is not required

There are several learning online possibilities available that do not require a degree, so don’t be concerned if you skipped university or only have a high school diploma; there is a career out there for you!

Lesson plans and academic resources are frequently supplied

Unlike learning in a classroom, most English language courses online will provide you with lesson plans and materials ahead of time, so all you have to do is read them and show up for your classes.

 free to make your schedule

 This is a major deal! When you study online, you have total control over your schedule. You have complete control over when to study. Do you dislike early mornings? You are not required to learn them! Do you never want to study on a weekend again? Not a problem! Furthermore, each week might be different; if you need to learn more, you can increase your hours, and vice versa.

The cons

Having to rely on technology might be tough at times

We’ve all seen how unfriendly technology can be sometimes, especially while travelling and relying on internet access at a hotel or hostel. The easiest way to avoid this and reduce stress is to always have backup equipment on hand.

Many English online agencies have restrictive cancellation policies

If you take a course, you are required to learn it. Many agencies have tight cancellation procedures, making it impossible to cancel your lessons, even for health reasons. If you cancel an already booked English class, you may be charged a penalty and may have difficulty obtaining future reservations.

In conclusion, the benefits of English language courses online outweigh the drawbacks, but these are the things you should think about before delving into the realm of online English learning.

What does forklift operation safety training involve?

October 2, 2021 Bruce Rhodes Forklift

A forklift is a piece of vital machinery in the manufacturing industry. They are also very useful in packaging and other services industries. Forklift operation safety training is very important as the operator is equipped with very useful skills in their operations and this will make him avoid accidents in the workplace.

When dealing with forklifts as an operator, it is vital to undergo the following training on various aspects of forklift operation: 

  1. Forklift handling – this involves the general handling of forklift e.g while in motion, while parked, general servicing, etc. 
  1. Moving – this will deal with safety issues that are required to be administered while the forklift is in motion. 
  1. Operation – this involves safety while you are operating the forklift. It will deal with safety issues while you are lifting loads, putting the loads down, ignition, etc. 
  1. Stopping – This will involve on safety standard to be administered while the forklift is stopping. 
  1. Starting – This will deal with the safety issues while the forklift is starting. This is when the forklift operator is preparing to start working. 
  1. Refueling – this involves the safety standards to be administered while refueling the forklift. 

Why is forklift operation safety training necessary? 

A forklift operator needs to operate the forklift effectively and adhere to all safety rules and guidelines. The forklift operator will therefore require forklift operation training so that he can keep himself and others safe. This will also include the safety of the forklift itself. Some of the importance of this training include： 

  1. Unnecessary expenses will not be incurred. 

As far as forklift operation training is concerned, a lot of costs are incurred. In the long run, this training will save on other unnecessary costs e.g. if an accident occurs because the forklift operator was not properly trained, this will mean that a lot of costs will be incurred on treatment, and this will also lead to the productivity of work going low. This can be very costly to the company. To save on this, it is best if the forklift operators are properly trained on safety issues. 

  1. Forklift lifespan will be increased. 

Forklift operation safety training not only deals in individual safety but also in the safety of the forklift itself. The forklift operator will be taught how to take very good care of the forklift and in doing so, the forklift will be serviced and kept clean. This will make it extend its years of operating. 

  1. Safe environment 

During the operation of the forklift, a lot can happen including accidents to the operator or other workers. It can also damage property in the working place if safety guidelines and rules are not administered properly. These accidents are likely to happen if the operator has not undergone the forklift safety training. Proper training will create a safe working environment for the forklift operators and other workers. 

Forklifts are very important in the movement of goods from one point to another. Various forklift operation safety training is required to avoid accidents and make the working environment safe. A forklift operator should wear a seat belt, control speed, observe load blocking, make good use of horns, and other safety measures to avoid accidents.
