Donnelly College is an affordable and accessible Catholic college based in Kansas City, Kansas. We have a bachelor's program, associate degrees, nursing programs and certificates offered to the most diverse student body in the Midwest, according to U.S. News & World Report.

Founded by the Benedictine Sisters of Mount St. Scholastica and the Archdiocese of Kansas City in Kansas.
