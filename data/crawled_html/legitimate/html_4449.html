The Letter Black is tagged as: alternative rock, female vocalists, hard rock, rock, alternative metal

The Letter Black is fronted by female vocalist Sarah Anthony, followed by her husband Mark Anthony (guitar / background vocals) Mat Slagle (drummer) and Terry Johnson (guitar).Breaking the Silence, now known as the Letter Black, immediately separated themselves from the underground through an undying work ethic and undeniable live performance. Their debut album, “Stand” was produced by Travis Wyrick (P.O.D., Pillar, 10 Years). The album immediately caught the attention of many record labels and thus began the buzz. When added with their solid live show and unyielding mission to touch lives, The Letter Black proved to be more than just another run of the mill rock band. After being courted by several labels, The Letter Black decided to call Tooth and Nail Records home. They felt the staff at T&N understood their vision and knew it was the best fit for them. The group will enter the studio this spring with legendary producer Toby Wright (Korn, Alice In Chains, 3 Doors Down) to record their Tooth and Nail debut, which is tentatively set for a late summer/ early fall release.
