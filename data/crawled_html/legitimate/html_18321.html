Welcome To Cohen & Fitch

Unfortunately, our society is not immune to the abuse the power by those in positions of authority. This is can be painfully true whether it is dealing with police on the street or with situations and people at the workplace. At Cohen & Fitch LLP, our mission is to protect against the perversion of justice in all contexts. While many people know that they have constitutional rights that are meant to protect them from such abuses of authority, few understand how to properly enforce those rights. The sad fact for many is that power structures that exist in the criminal justice and employment context can be intimidating and discouraging to most individuals.

At Cohen & Fitch LLP we bring over 15 years of trial experience fighting for and defending the civil rights of abused, underprivileged and disenfranchised individuals. We understand the limits of lawful police conduct and the line between inappropriate and illegal behavior in the workplace and will fight vigorously to get you the results you deserve.

New York City Employment Violation Attorneys

If you are employed anywhere you are entitled to certain equal rights under Federal, State and local New York City laws. Nevertheless, employers frequently exploit, discriminate and ultimately create hostile working conditions for their employees. These acts can be obvious or subtle, but can cause economic damages as well as severe mental, emotional, psychological and in some instances physical injuries, but you have the right to be compensated for all of them. Also, when these acts are taken by supervisory or managerial staff or are the result of the organization’s policy, the entire company can be held to answer for the damage caused by:

  • Sexual Harassment
  • Gender Inequality
  • Retaliation
  • Unequal Pay
  • Race Discrimination
  • Disability Discrimination
  • Age Discrimination
  • Hostile Work Environment
  • Wage & Hour Claims

However, just as an employer takes advantage of the power dynamic in these situations, so too do the attorneys defending the companies’ bottom line when you seek to enforce your rights. Therefore, essential to your success is having attorneys on your side that are relentless and who understand the work involved in overcoming the hurdles in these cases. Cohen & Fitch LLP has extensive civil rights experience, knowledge and determination to level the playing field between employer and employee.

Where other attorneys may refuse these cases because of these challenges, Cohen & Fitch will never back down at any stage of the case and you will never be forced to "settle" for less than you deserve.
