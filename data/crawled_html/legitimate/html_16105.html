Tony Pajaczkowski

Tony Pajaczkowski played thirteen years in the CFL, elven with Calgary (1955-1965) and two with Montreal (1966-67).  With the Stampeders, Pajaczkowski won five West All-Stars at offensive guard (1960, 1962, 1963, 1964 and 1965), and with Montreal, he won an East All-Star at defensive end in 1966.  Pajaczkowski also won four CFL All-Stars at offensive guard in 1962, 1963, 1964 and 1965.   Pajaczkowski was the Calgary nominee for Outstanding Lineman in 1960.  Besides being one of he best offensive lineman of his time, Pajackowski also handled kickoff duties for the Stampeders for several years.  Pajaczkowski was elected to the Canadian Football Hall of Fame in 1988.
