Welcome to my site

Dr. Prasenjit Chatterjee after completing his MBBS from Burdwan Medical College proceeded to Tata Memorial Hospital where he obtained his MD ( Rad. Oncology) in 1997. His Clinical experience commenced as a visiting consultant at Meharbai Tata Memorial Hospital in Jamshedpur in 1997-98. After that he was attached to Chittaranjan National Cancer Institute ( Regional Cancer Centre ) from 1998 -2001. He is presently attached to the following hospitals:

. Apollo Multispeciality Hospital, Kadapara, Kolkata
. Manipal Hospital, Saltlake, Kolkata
. Woodland Hospital, Kolkata
. Manipal Hospital, Dhakuria, Kolkata
. Apollo Clinic, Taratala.
