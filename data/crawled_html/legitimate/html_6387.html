About Barasat City

Barasat is a city on the outskirts of the State Capital of West Bengal, Kolkata and is the district headquarters of the district North 24 Parganas. It is a part of the area covered by Kolkata Metropolitan Development Authority. The city is an important railway and roadway junction. Barasat is also the name of a sub-division centering the city..

About Us

Barasat Municipality was established on 1st April 1869, but up to – 1882, it had not any independent existence, like other offices it was controlled by Magistrate Office. During that time there was no Chairman of the municipality; Magistrate was the Controller of municipality. Later on after 1882 Barasat Municipality had got its own formation as an autonomous body and became local government.
