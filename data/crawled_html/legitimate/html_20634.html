TDT / Spanish TV System Installation and Maintenance

At The Sat & TV Guy, we also provide high-quality TDT (Digital Terrestrial Television) installations and maintenance services across the Costa Blanca and Valencia regions of Spain.

TDT in Spain is the country’s digital terrestrial television service, similar to Freeview in the UK. It replaced the old analogue TV system and is free-to-air, meaning there is no monthly subscription fee

Our TDT services encompass:

Aerial Installations and Upgrades: We install high-performance UHF digital TV aerials, designed to capture Spanish TDT signals effectively. Our installations ensure optimal signal reception, even in areas with challenging terrain or signal interference.

Signal Amplification: To enhance signal strength and quality, we offer masthead amplifiers that boost the UHF digital TV signal before any potential loss down the coaxial cable. This approach ensures a strong and stable signal throughout your property.

Set-Top Box Integration: We assist in selecting and installing compatible digital tuners, whether integrated into your television or as standalone set-top boxes, to facilitate seamless reception of Spanish TDT channels.

Community System Solutions: For apartment complexes or shared living arrangements, we provide multichannel programmable TV amplifiers capable of distributing TDT and analogue TV signals across multiple units. These systems are programmable to accommodate new channel frequencies as they become available, ensuring long-term adaptability.

Maintenance and Repairs: We offer comprehensive maintenance services, including fault diagnosis, equipment servicing, and system upgrades, to ensure your TDT system operates at peak performance.

As Spain is transitioning to DVB-T2, we can help to ensure that your TDT system is DVB-T2 compatible and futureproof.
