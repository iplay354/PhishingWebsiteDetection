Welcome

As the Alumni Association for Epsilon-Kappa Zeta of Lambda Chi Alpha, we intend to foster monthly correspondence on the Alumni Board activities, as well as undergraduate operations. Building upon our time as undergraduates, we aim to facilitate events that are exciting, and allow brothers to stay in touch, reconnect, and build new relationships.

Now in our 83rd year on campus, we have a long list of alumni brothers spanning several generations, and that list continues to grow annually. The Epsilon-Kappa Zeta Alumni Association intends to connect those generations, their stories, and their memories.
