News About This Theater

  • Jul 4, 2007 — Ohio Theatre for sale

Opened as Schine’s Ohio Theatre on 17th November 1927 with “Tea For Three” starring Lew Cody and Aileen Pringle and vaudeville on stage. The architect Peter M. Hulsken teamed up with fellow architect Lyman T. Strong to design the theatre which was built in just over five months.

Originally operated by the Schine Circuit, it was later taken over by Warner Bros. Circuit Management Corp. by 1941. Later operated by RKO-Stanley Warner which closed it on September 26, 1978 with The Band in “The Last Waltz”. It put it up for sale.

By 2008 it was a concert venue, by 2014 used for special events, by 2018 it was back to being a concerts venue. By 2019 it was briefly used as an Escape Room. It became a restaurant, and by 2020 it was operating as a nightclub. By October 2022 it was undergoing a refurbishment to convert into a restaurant & performing arts venue which opened in 2023 as the Ohio Theatre Lima.
