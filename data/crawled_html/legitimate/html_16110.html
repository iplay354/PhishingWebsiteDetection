CFLapedia, the Canadian Football League Encyclopedia

Now updated through the 2024 season!

This website is an online encyclopedia dedicated to the Canadian Football League (CFL). The goal of this site is to collect the playing records of every player who ever played in the CFL along with the stories that accompany those statistics.

From early Canadian football to the formation of the CFL to modern day, CFLapedia contains a wealth of information on the professional Canadian game. Check out the Players A-Z section which is an attempt to list every player to have played in the CFL, along with their basic information such as position(s), uniform numbers and years played.

Team information is available including all the American CFL teams from the 1990s as well as teams which pre-date the CFL.

Also, be sure to stop by the Year by Year section where stats for each year are going online. For more stats and rosters, check out the CFL statistics on StatsCrew.com.
