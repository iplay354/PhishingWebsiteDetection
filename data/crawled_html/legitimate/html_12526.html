“Welcome to the proud home of the Burbank Vikings! Since 1969, we’ve been dedicated to shaping the young athletes of Burbank, instilling values of teamwork, perseverance, and sportsmanship. With a rich history rooted in community spirit, the Burbank Vikings offer a dynamic range of sports experiences, including football, cheer, track and field, and cross country. Join us in fostering a legacy of athletic excellence and camaraderie that extends far beyond the field. Go Vikings!”
