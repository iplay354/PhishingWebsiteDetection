  • MellowAds will be back soon

    After a year of holding the domain, we’ve decided it’s time to bring Mellow Ads back — but with a new, simpler approach.

    Originally, Mellow Ads was a powerful ad network serving the crypto space. Unfortunately, the original platform went offline after the passing of its founder. Since then, we acquired the domain with the hope of restoring it to its former glory. But after much consideration, we’ve decided to go a different route.

    Mellow Ads is now being rebuilt as a PTC (pay-to-click) earning platform — stripped down, straightforward, and focused on Bitcoin rewards. Users will be able to earn BTC for viewing ads, while advertisers get direct exposure to real users. No fluff, no KYC, no nonsense.

    It’s the same spirit as before — just leaner and more accessible.

    Stay tuned. We’re keeping things simple, but we’re here to stay.
