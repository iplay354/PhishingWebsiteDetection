Glad you've joined us, Chris Biggs & Ken Gustin, acoustic musicians calling Manhattan KS home.  Poke around, lots to see!

Testimonials

"It was an absolute delight to work with Ken and Chris on the Rhythm of the Rails project. Collaborating with them was easy because of their professional demeanor and can-do attitude.  Promoting them was a piece of cake, too, because of the tools they already have in place and their willingness to do whatever needed to market the concert. Most importantly, their performance was top-notch.  They're talented musicians who know how to connect with an audience, read an audience and deliver an amazing concert that appeals to all ages and backgrounds.  It was our pleasure to include them as part of our season of artists." - Wayne Kruse, Chairman, Marshall County Arts Cooperative

"Chris Biggs & Ken Gustin are a couple of Great Plains gems. Good pickers, singers, harmonizers, these guys know how to do it across the whole spectrum of American roots music. In 20 minutes, I heard everything from Kenny Baker to Mississippi John Hurt to Grandpa Jones, plus original tunes that rivaled the familiar stuff. These two are well versed and masters of their craft." - John Depew, Kansas songwriter, multi-instrumentalist and performer

"An evening with Chris Biggs and Ken Gustin means a dance or two and sitting back to enjoy the warm blend of their voices traversing old ballads, an ode written while falling in love, a rocking “mobile home with mag wheels” piece, a lament launched by the imagined image of a loved one in rain, a story of fishing in Belize, drinking Belikin beer. Even their guitar-only pieces have the feel of stories with a touch of poetry." - Kris Polansky, Kansas Authors Club Poet of the Year 2021-2022.

"Brothers and sisters, these gentlemen are the real deal!" -    Brother Jeb, founding member of The Grateful Dudes

"During my time at Tallgrass Tap House, I had the pleasure of booking Ken Gustin and Chris Biggs several times to play in our various venues, and let me just say, they never disappointed! Every time they joined us, they not only packed the spaces, but they garnered ample interest from other diners throughout the rest of the restaurant, and very quickly became a guest and staff favorite to have perform with us! Ken and Chris are such a wonderful addition to the music scene in MHK, and we are so looking forward to having them back as soon as possible! " - Amanda Owings, Tallgrass Tap House.

"Singularly, Chris and Ken are two very talented musicians but together as a duo they are incredible!   I had the opportunity to see them in person and I came away impressed!  Take advantage of any opportunity to see them in person!  You won’t be disappointed!" -  Tom, Retired Entrepreneur from Massachusetts

"Love the blog posts!  Find out where this dynamic duo will be working their musical magic next, followed by an entertaining, inventive mix of fictional realism and solid guitar info, with a hilarious coda of "Next Time" asides.  Fun!" - John Eberly, 'Pataphysician 

"Better catch these guys soon, AND OFTEN!  Must see entertainment with amazing vocals and guitar playing!"  - Anonymous
