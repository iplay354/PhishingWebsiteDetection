Welcome

Blue Ridge Crossing is a lifestyle center located just east of Kansas City’s professional sports stadiums. Formerly, The Blue Ridge Mall; Kansas City’s first ‘Shopping Mall’, Blue Ridge has been serving the Kansas City Metro Area since 1956.

Enjoy shopping and dining at nationally known retailers and restaurants like: Starbucks, Applebees, Wal-Mart Supercenter, Verizon Wireless, T-Mobile, GNC and Mattress Firm. At Blue Ridge, there are plenty of choices for the entire family.

Familiar faces, all new spaces – that’s the new Blue Ridge Crossing.

Blue Ridge Crossing is conveniently located at the intersection of I-70 and Blue Ridge Boulevard.
