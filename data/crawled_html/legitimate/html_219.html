Frequently asked questions

What is Slideshare?

Slideshare is the presentation-sharing platform for anyone looking for slide inspiration, to showcase their knowledge, or to build on their own ideas. With over 25M+ presentations and 70M+ active users, Slideshare is the place where big ideas live.

Why does Slideshare look different?

Slideshare is stepping into a bold new chapter with a refreshed identity. While the core of what you love remains the same — access to over 25 million decks, powerful AI tools, and a global network of 70+ million curious minds — Slideshare now carries a cleaner, sharper look. This rebrand emphasizes our renewed mission: helping people transform their ideas into opportunities. With this fresh energy, Slideshare continues to be the trusted home for expert insights and standout presentations. This update is just the beginning, designed to better serve creators and thinkers like you.

Do I need a subscription to access Slideshare?

No subscription is required to access Slideshare — you can still browse, read, and upload content. However, a subscription gives you additional benefits such as the ability to download presentations and enjoy an ad-free browsing experience.

What is included in a Slideshare subscription?

A paid subscription includes the following exclusive benefits:

  • Download presentations
  • Copy and paste text directly from presentations
  • Enjoy an ad-free experience
  • Complimentary access to millions of documents on Scribd
How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription anytime by visiting your account settings. If you cancel, you'll continue to have access to your subscription benefits until the remaining time expires.
