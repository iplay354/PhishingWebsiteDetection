THE FARRARS: Malinda Horatio Chenoweth(5) 1820-1856

Malinda Horatio Chenoweth was born in Franklin Co., OH, the daughter of John Foster Chenoweth and Margaret 'Peggy' Ferguson. She married in Madison Co., OH at the age of 17 to Henry Farrar, the son of Jonathan Farrar and Mary Kilby, English immigrants. This marriage took place at the home of Malinda's parents. Malinda and Henry lived most of their lives in Madison County. They had 10 children, seven of who lived to marry. The Farrers lived in many locations in Madison County. They eventually built a home on property that Malinda received from her father. But within a year of finally getting her own home, Malinda died at the young age of 36 from complications with her last child, who survived her by about a year. Henry remarried and lived to his 80th year, relocating to Marshall Co., KS with several of his children. Henry is buried in the Kirkwood cemetery, London, OH. The Farrar descendants started a family newsletter before the turn of the 20th century, which continues to this day and has left a rich heritage of this family. Malinda's descendants can be found mainly in Kansas.
