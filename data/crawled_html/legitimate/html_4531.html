The Alternative Reality Web Zine is an online literary magazine specializing in alternative reality fiction, including science fiction, fantasy, horror, historical and cross-genres. It was founded in association with ARWZ.com and the Alternative Reality Writers' Zone. This magazine seeks to highlight alternative reality fiction in all its genres and cross genres by featuring discussion and original writing of both established authors and upcoming writers. This magazine also strives to host thoughful conversation of alternative reality literature in all its forums, giving publishing opportunities to writers and audiences alike.

ARWZ Literary Zine is available online at ARWZ.com free to readers. The magazine publishes new content (whether reviews, fiction, poetry, articles, columns, editorials or interviews) every Tuesday and Thursday. At the end of each month, the completed issue is sent free to subscribers. To subscribe, send a blank email to: [email protected]. An email message with further instructions will follow.

ARWZ Literary Zine is a sister site to the Alternative Reality Writers' Zone. ARWZ Literary Zine grew out of the ARWZ Webpage community as a way for writers and fans of alternative reality fiction to have a voice in the literary discourse of their genres. ARWZ has always sought to be a venue for writers to connect with their target audience. Here at ARWZ Literary Zine, writers and audiences are united in their common interest of alternative reality fiction. ARWZ Literary Zine encourages both writers and fans to submit their opinions for publication. Writers get to have a hand in publishing, audiences become more involved in the literature that captures their imagination. The primary goal of ARWZ Literary Zine is to bring audiences and writers together in the discourse that shapes emerging trends in alternative reality fiction. In doing so, we hope the voices of both groups will have a greater impact on the genre.
