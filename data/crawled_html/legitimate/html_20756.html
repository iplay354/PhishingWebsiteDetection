Serving Savers and Homeowners since 1886

“Serving savers and homeowners since 1886,” The Covington Savings and Loan prides itself on customer satisfaction.  Founded by a group of Covington businessmen more than 135 years ago, the Association has grown to be an integral part of the local villages.  Offering a wide array of banking services to the surrounding communities, the Covington Savings and Loan strives to meet the diverse needs of its customers.
​
Throughout its long history, The Covington Savings and Loan Association has become a cornerstone of the communities it serves.  Our employees look forward to assisting you in the near future.  Simply stop by any of our locations to begin your ultimate banking experience!

FDIC INSURANCE COVERAGE

All of a depositor's accounts at an insured depository institution, including all noninterest-bearing transaction accounts, will be insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) up to the standard maximum deposit insurance amount ($250,000), for each deposit insurance ownership category.
​​
For more information about FDIC insurance coverage of noninterest-bearing transaction accounts, visit: https://www.fdic.gov/index.html.
