Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer an existing 401(k) into a Fidelity Gold IRA?

Yes. Fidelity allows rollovers from employer-sponsored plans like 401(k)s into an IRA, including a Gold IRA. The process typically involves opening a Fidelity IRA, requesting a rollover from your former employer’s plan, and ensuring funds are transferred directly to avoid taxes or penalties. Fidelity’s support team can walk you through the paperwork and timeline to make the process seamless.

What types of gold can I hold in a Fidelity Gold IRA?

While Fidelity offers limited access to physical bullion, investors can usually hold IRA-approved coins and bars that meet IRS purity requirements. More commonly, Fidelity investors gain exposure to gold through ETFs, mutual funds, and mining stocks. This makes it easier to invest without worrying about physical storage but still benefit from gold’s potential as a hedge.

Are there fees for maintaining a Fidelity Gold IRA?

Yes, but they vary depending on the type of investment you choose. ETFs and mutual funds may have expense ratios, while physical gold could involve transaction costs and storage fees. Fidelity is known for keeping its fees competitive, but it’s important to review the specific costs tied to your chosen investment to avoid surprises.

How secure is my gold with Fidelity?

For physical precious metals, Fidelity uses third-party depositories that are insured and meet strict security standards. If you invest through ETFs or funds, your holdings are protected electronically in your IRA account. Either way, Fidelity emphasizes strong security and transparency to safeguard your investments.

Who is a Fidelity Gold IRA best suited for?

A Fidelity Gold IRA is ideal for investors who want the stability and credibility of a large financial institution while adding gold exposure to their retirement portfolio. It works well for those who prefer ETFs, mutual funds, or gold-related stocks rather than directly managing and storing physical gold themselves.
