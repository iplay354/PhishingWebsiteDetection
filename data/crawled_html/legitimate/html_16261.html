Important Notice to Chantilly Plumbing Customers

  • Falcon Has Acquired Chantilly Plumbing
  • Members Will Receive Uninterrupted Service
  • All Your Favorite Chantilly Plumbing Team Members Joined Falcon

Need a Plumber in Chantilly, VA?

If you’re a customer of Chantilly Plumbing, please read the notice below to learn more about our transition to Falcon Heating, Air Conditioning, and Plumbing and what it means for your service.

If you’re here because you need plumbing help in Chantilly, call us at (703) 596-9998 for fast, reliable assistance from our expert team.

Valued Chantilly Plumbing Customers:

It has been our pleasure providing you, your families, and all our Chantilly neighbors with expert plumbing service for the past 24 years. However, after much consideration, we have decided to sell our business. This decision was not made lightly, and it comes after significant thought about the future and the best interests of our valued customers.

We are pleased to announce that Falcon Heating, Air Conditioning, and Plumbing has acquired Chantilly Plumbing. Falcon shares our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction, and we are confident that this transition will bring positive changes and continued high-quality service to you.

Falcon Heating, Air Conditioning, and Plumbing is a family-oriented company, well-established in Northern Virginia with a strong focus on customer service and dedicated to improving the overall reputation of the home service trades. We are confident their expertise and dedication will ensure a seamless transition and enhance your overall experience.

What does this mean for you?

  • Uninterrupted Service: Your current services and agreements with Chantilly Plumbing will continue without interruption.
  • Same Friendly Faces: Many of our team members will be joining Falcon, ensuring that you will still see familiar faces and receive the personal attention you have come to expect.
  • Enhanced Offerings: Falcon not only provides complete plumbing repair, service, and installations, but they also have expertise in heating, air conditioning, and indoor air quality. From replacement systems and repairs to routine service and annual maintenance plans, Falcon is your hometown expert!

We are grateful for your loyalty and trust over the years. It has been a pleasure serving you, and we are confident that you will be in excellent hands with Falcon Heating, Air Conditioning, and Plumbing. If you have any questions or concerns during this transition period, please do not hesitate to contact Falcon.

Thank you for your years of support. We know you will be happy with the expertise and focus on customer satisfaction that Falcon will provide you on all your future plumbing and home comfort needs.
