    Biografie Bobby Burns

    Bobby Burns aka Bartjay returns to the scene after some years of relative silence. And what a comeback! Holland is the word in the international House scene and this tune definitely emphasizes the position of the Dutch. Bobby Burns, the multitalented music & video producer of this catchy tune got help from rapper Casablanca Connect and this cooperation will definitely lead to a summer 2007 hit. Anthem. Hi rotation by djs Sidney Samson, Gregor Salto, Tony Cha Cha, Dj Jean,..
