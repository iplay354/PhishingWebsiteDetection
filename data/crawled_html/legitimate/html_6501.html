FACTS ABOUT THIS WEB SITE

     During the summer of 2002 I spent seven weeks in South Africa. I did a lot of research on the Jewish Community of Witbank, and have accumulated enough material to add to this web site, to keep me busy for many years. 

     I (we) have two Witbank web sites. This one, which I started in August 2002, and my second one here: 

      http://www.mannbarry.net/Witbank.html

   Please send me your email addresses so that I can let you know whenever new information is added, to either of our two Witbank Jewish web sites.

    Please look at all the photos on this web site, and help me identify all the people shown in the photos. Email me any corrections to names,  addresses etc. Also corrections to any misspellings and any  other information that you would like me to add to this web site. Please feel free to send me your comments and suggestions for this web site.
