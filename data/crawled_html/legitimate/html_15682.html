Welcome to CCS

Welcome to the Columbus Computer Society (CCS), a non-profit organization based in Columbus, Ohio, where technology meets community. CCS dedicates itself to promoting technology for the betterment of individuals and communities, founded on principles of unity and shared values.

We aim to provide a platform for technology enthusiasts to learn, share, and grow. Through education, outreach programs, and advocacy, we strive to ensure equitable access to technology for all.

Join CCS!

Join us in our mission to enhance technology’s current and potential uses and to create a forum for exchanging ideas and knowledge among members. Explore our site to learn more about our initiatives and events and how you can join our tech-forward community!

If you are not yet a member, please consider joining CCS to support the organization and our programs. A family membership costs only $35 per year, and a corporate membership is $175.
