"Destination Immersions – no more than 7 days no more than 8 people

About

Dessau International’s “destination immersion” concept was unique with no more than 8 travelers on a journey of  7 days. Partiipants benefited from becoming much more familiar with destinations visited.

Each immersion was designed with a unique theme adding value to travel experience.

  • People to People Cuba , interacting with entrepreneurs beyond Havana
  • Midevial Paris Region, exploring rich sustained history & sustained influence
  • Bonaire SCUBA, diving or snorkeling in marine park on island rich in history
  • Panama Canal & Birding, see new & old locks enjoying hummingbirds & macaws
  • Chile Vineyards, experiencing micro climates and influence on wine production
  • Copenhagen Hygge, understanding viking influence & experiencing cosyness

Each destination was one with which we have extensive knowledge & practical experience.

Immersions can took place with as few as 4 participants but no more than 8.

We adapted to assist with a private family immersions.

Louis, the chief exploration officer speaks 5 languages and been involed with group travel involving as many as 40 participants. Having traveled to 58 countries he has opted to pursue the less travelled destinations providing participants a less crowded experience.

Operations ceased 1 October 2025.
