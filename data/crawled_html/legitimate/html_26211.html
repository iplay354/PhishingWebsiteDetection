How to Choose the Best Aircraft Flooring Material for Your Fleet
Selecting aircraft flooring material is more than a matter of aesthetics—it’s a decision that impacts everything from regulatory compliance to fuel efficiency and passenger experience. As aircraft interiors evolve with newer configurations and more demanding performance specs, manufacturers require aerospace flooring solutions that deliver on every front: flame resistance, anti-slip safety, structural durability, and low lifecycle cost. The challenge is finding a material that not only meets the spec sheet, but holds up across galleys, aisleways, entryways, and high-impact service zones.
