Glossary

Full Stack SEO: A comprehensive approach to SEO that covers all aspects of search engine optimization, including on-page, technical, content, and performance SEO. It ensures that a website is fully optimized to perform well in search engine rankings.

On-Page SEO: The practice of optimizing individual web pages to rank higher in search engines and earn more relevant traffic. This includes optimizing content, meta tags, headers, and images.

Technical SEO: The process of optimizing a website's infrastructure to ensure it meets the technical requirements of search engines. This includes site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, indexing, and the implementation of schema markup.

Content SEO: The strategy of creating and optimizing high-quality content that is both relevant to users and aligned with search engine algorithms. It involves keyword research, content creation, and optimization.

Performance SEO: A focus on improving the performance-related aspects of a website, such as site speed, mobile optimization, and core web vitals, which are crucial for search engine rankings and user experience.

Online Reputation Management (ORM): The practice of monitoring and influencing how a person or business is perceived online. ORM involves strategies to protect and enhance online reputations, often by addressing negative search results and promoting positive content.

Digital Marketing Strategy: A plan that outlines how a business will achieve its marketing goals through online channels. This includes SEO, social media marketing, content marketing, and online advertising.

Mea Culpa Marketing: A marketing strategy used by Chris Abraham where he presents himself in a humble and disarming manner to build trust and connect more authentically with his audience.
