WILLIAM(5) OF FULTON CO., IL1811-1884

William, the fourth son and 6th surviving child of John and Mary Ann 'Polly' Switzer Chenoweth, is recorded as being born in Ohio. As his family lived continuously at this time in Washington Co., Indiana, it may be that his mother was visiting at the time of his birth. At the age of 23, he married in Washington County, Martha Ann Jones, who was 15 at the time. After their first child they moved to Fulton County, Illinois where they would live the rest of their lives and found a large continuing family. Many of their descendants still live in this area today and hold an annual picnic in August. Curiously, William is omitted as a child of John and Mary in Cora Hiatt's book. William, a farmer, lived to the age of 73 and Martha to 80, outliving him by 14 years. William and Mary are buried in the Temple cemetery, Fulton Co, IL.
