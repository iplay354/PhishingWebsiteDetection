    Our Services

    Concordia SAE

    Concordia SAE, located in Montreal, Quebec, is a student-run chapter of SAE International at Concordia University. A world-renowned engineering organization, SAE International hosts the Collegiate Design Series competitions for students. These yearly international competitions allow students to apply classroom knowledge to real-life situations, challenging students to design, manufacture, test, and compete with a vehicle in a dynamic environment.

    AeroDesign

    The Concordia SAE Aero Design team is a student-run group that competes in the SAE Aero Design Collegiate Design Series organized by SAE International. These are extremely competitive events, with universities from around the world competing with RC Aircrafts designed, manufactured and tested by students.

    + Learn More

    Formula

    The Formula SAE Electric competition is very similar to the international Formula 1 competitions. It is comprised of 5 dynamic events: Acceleration, Skid Pad, Autocross, Efficiency, and Endurance, as well as a design presentation during which the team explains their design process and decisions.

    + Learn More

    Baja

    ​Concordia BAJA Racing has a history of building off-road vehicles spanning for over 20 years. Every year, the BAJA team has to design a complete vehicle and build it in order to compete in official SAE Collegiate Design Series competitions, where we compete against other universities from across the world.

    Message From the President

    My name is Alexander Parthimos, the president of Concordia SAE.

    ​

    After years of working with SAE, I can confidently say that we are a student association devoted to innovating and competing at the highest level. Our teams dedicate themselves to promoting sustainable engineering practices through the use of modern technology and renewable energies. We participate in numerous events across North America and compete against other university teams, encountering real world industry experiences. As such, we cover Business, Financial and Technical operations to gather industry attention to our teams and performance. We also hone our technical expertise to produce innovative and successful designs.

     

    None of this would be possible without the sponsors who help us across many fronts. From giving us access to technical expertise and specialized equipment to products, parts, and funding, our sponsors have been essential in achieving our goals. The knowledge and resources from the industry allow us to work on different projects and compete in the SAE International collegiate competitions. By sponsoring Concordia SAE, you will be given the opportunity to be involved with a green student association that represents itself not only in competitions but also within the community.

     

    Concordia SAE has been around for more than 40 years. With our growing endeavors each year, the need for both technical expertise and resources has been steadily increasing as well. Thus, we would like to thank our recurring sponsors for their continuous support, and we look forward to working with new sponsors who are willing to support the future of Concordia SAE engineering.

     

    Alexander Parthimos

    President - Concordia SAE
