George Dixon

Dixon was a ninth round draft pick of the NFL Green Bay Packers in 1959, but was released after the exibition season and joined the Montreal Alouettes that year.  In his short rookie season, Dixon demonstrated his potential with 38 carries for 301 yards and a oustanding 7.9 yard average.  Injuries plagued Dixon during his career and kept him from the 1000 yard club in the next two seasons as he ran for 976 yards in 12 games in 1960 and 806 yards in 11 games in 1961.  1962 was Dixon's best as he smashed that elusive thousand yard mark, and ran for 1520 yards and a 7.0 yard average carry to lead the East in rushing.  Those stats earned Dixon the CFL Most Outstanding Player award and All-Canadian status in the first year for the CFL All-Star awards.  1963 was almost as good as Dixon ran for 1270 yards and a 6.7 yard average to once more lead the East in rushing and claim his second All-Canadian All-Star team award.  Dixon was also an East All-Star in both 1962 and 1963.  Injuries once more hit Dixon in 1964 as he played in just 12 games and then only 6 in his final season in 1965.  Many of the running backs from that era were not used much as a receiver, but Dixon was a dangerous receiver out of the backfield.  His best season receiving was 1960 when he caught 33 passes, incuding 9 touchdown passes to match his 9 rushing for a total of 18 for the season.  Those 18 TDs gave Dixon the East scoring title in 1960.  Dixon ended his career with 5619 yards rushing and was elected to the Canadian Football Hall-of-Fame in 1974.  George Dixon passed away on August 6, 1990.
