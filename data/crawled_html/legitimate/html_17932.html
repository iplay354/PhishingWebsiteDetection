Welcome to Cleveland Clinic EMS Education

The Cleveland Clinic EMS Education Department's mission is to elevate pre-hospital care through excellence in education, training, and innovation. We empower EMS professionals with cutting-edge knowledge and skills, fostering a culture of continuous learning and collaboration. We are proud to provide complimentary online education and support to local Fire and EMS departments, with content spanning all aspects of EMS pre-hospital care. Whether improving your skills or looking for specific EMS education topics for re-certification, the Cleveland Clinic EMS Education team and our Healthcare Education and Training portal are a one-stop shop for your educational needs. Our dedication to EMS Education and evidence-based practices ensures the highest quality of care by our EMS Partners, making a positive impact on patient outcomes.

Cleveland Clinic Med Command Education

As part of the Cleveland Clinic Medical Command, it's crucial to stay current with your Continuing Education (CE) requirements. Access your Cleveland Clinic EMS Learning Management System provided through Prodigy EMS to complete your state and department-specific training, recertification, and more. Prodigy EMS offers comprehensive, engaging courses designed by experienced EMS professionals to ensure compliance with certification standards and enhance your clinical skills. Visit Prodigy EMS today and stay ahead in your EMS career.
