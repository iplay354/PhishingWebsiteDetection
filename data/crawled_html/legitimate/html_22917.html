About Delaney Design

I'm Bob Delaney, and I own and operate Delaney Design from my office in Yarmouth, Maine. I've been living and working in the Greater Portland area since 1998 and have been working in the graphic design industry since 1985. During my 40 years of experience as a graphic designer, I have worked in print shops, corporate art departments, and ad agencies in the areas of graphic design, print production, and web site design.
