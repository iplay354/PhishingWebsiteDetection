Chicago Blackhawks GM Stan Bowman on 670 The Score

April 29, 2011 Tab Bamford 3 Comments ben smith, Chicago Blackhawks, Joel Quenneville, john scott, kyle beach, Marian Hossa, NHL, Stan Bowman, Western Conference

Here’s audio from Hawks’ GM Stan Bowman’s interview on the Danny Mac show on Friday, April 29 on The Score in Chicago. He addresses a number of isses Hawks fans have been asking about, including Kyle Beach’s future, where was Ben Smith all year, and why is Marian Hossa playing for his country. Worth a listen. Great interview!
