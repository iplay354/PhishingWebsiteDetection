Located at the Guam Premier Outlets Mall in Tamuning, the Guam Megaplex Cinemas was opened by the Wallace Theatres chain in 2000. The theater has fourteen screens, and features first-run Hollywood films, as well as Tagalog-language films on occasion. In April 2013 it was taken over by Regal.
