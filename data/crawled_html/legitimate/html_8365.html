Black Hammer Productions WAS an interactive media company working in the New York City metropolitan area

Black Hammer is a leading developer of iPhone, iPad and Android applications, games, web sites, educational software, animations, and museum kiosks. Our clients (and publishers) come from a wide range of industries (including publishing, education, entertainment, healthcare, pharmaceutical, travel and transportation, financial services) and include prominent names such as Scholastic (the I SPY CD-ROM series), Nickelodeon (Dora and Diego games), PlayFirst Games, Hachette Filipacchi, Bombardier Aerospace, General Mills and Oxygen.
