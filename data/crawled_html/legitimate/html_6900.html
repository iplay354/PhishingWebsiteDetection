Welcome to Bay East Animal Hospital!

Bay East Animal Hospital has been providing excellent veterinary care to the greater Green Bay area since 1973. We practice preventative medicine to help you keep your dog or cat healthy, and we’ll help you navigate diagnostic options and treatment when your pet is unwell.

We're proudly AAHA Accredited!

As an accredited member of the American Animal Hospital Association, the team at Bay East Animal Hospital is dedicated to providing excellence in small animal care. Our hospital is evaluated regularly by AAHA to ensure that we meet or exceed the association’s standards of excellence. AAHA standards are recognized around the world as the benchmark for quality care.
