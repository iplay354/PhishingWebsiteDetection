Our Story

Founded in 2009, Cedar River Cellars is our continued jouney of 30+ years of experince with brewing beer, mead, cider, and wine.

We are an award winning winery with a sense of place. We chose our winery name due to our proximity to the river in Renton, WA. We wanted to have a name that is recognizable by people local to our community.

Our small-batch approach allows us to pay meticulous attention to detail throughout the winemaking process, resulting in wines of remarkable depth, complexity, and balance.
