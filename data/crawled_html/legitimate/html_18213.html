Meet Coach

Gary Waters

The Character Coach

With over 40 years of coaching and leadership experience, Coach Gary Waters has dedicated his life to building character and culture in individuals and organizations. Through learning character culture participants develop practical ways to strengthen integrity, respect, and values essential for thriving in today’s world.

In addition, Coach Waters’ academic background in business and education equips him to speak effectively on leadership and business challenges. Known for his dynamic and high-energy presentations, Coach Waters inspires audiences to lead with conviction, serve others, and foster cultures that elevate both people and performance. His keynotes and workshops leave lasting impact, equipping leaders, students, teams, and organizations to pursue excellence with character at the core.
