About Us

Since 1991, Calera Capital has built businesses of enduring value by partnering with founders, families, and management teams. Our success is rooted in our commitments to:

Strong Partnerships

For us, partnership is not a buzzword. It’s core to our founding DNA as a firm. We stake our reputation on our record of shared success with our partners, building businesses and value over time.
