Today's Featured Biography

 

 
Betsy Lehmann

Living it up in St. Louis, but still a loyal Chief's fan.
Andy and I have been married for 15(gasp) years. Our two girls are amazing, every day they make me laugh and smile, and cry and pull my hair out sometimes too. They are a blessing.
I work part-time at a funky store/cafe called Kangaroo Kids. I am a Certified Lactation Counselor, which is a fancy way of saying that I help women and babies with breastfeeding. Those of you who have done this, breastfed babies I mean, know it can be challenging at times. Kangaroo Kids is a breastfeeding and maternity support center along with being a children's and maternity resale shop, we also have a cafe called Child's Play Cafe. It is a fabulous place to work and I get to spend time with my girls too since it is part-time.
I did the high school teacher thing for a few years before kids.
Andy (Bro. Andy to you all) is still a Campus Minister/Teacher at Christian Bros. College High School.
