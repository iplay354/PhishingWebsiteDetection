Build Your Business In
Cherokee County, South Carolina
Nestled at the foothills of the scenic Blue Ridge Mountains, Cherokee County offers an ideal blend of natural beauty and strategic location. With Interstate 85 running through the heart of the county, you're just 40 minutes from the vibrant city of Greenville, SC, and 40 minutes from the thriving metro of Charlotte, NC. Explore how Cherokee County can give your business the competitive edge it needs to succeed.

Our Core Industries
Nowhere does it better than here. Cherokee, SC supports the needs of a wide range of industries. From Manufacturing to Automotive and Energy, we lay the groundwork for business success.
Automotive Cherokee County is well positioned for Automotive and Automotive support businesses. Located only 27 miles from BMW Manufacturing, Cherokee is an ideal location for Tier I providers.
Learn More Learn More
Advanced Materials Cherokee county has a long history with textiles. It has been an epicenter for advanced materials since 1899 when the first textile mill opened and is still in operation today.
Learn More Learn More
Manufacturing With a workforce of nearly 900,000 within a 60 minute drive, Cherokee has a skilled workforce to meet your manufacturing needs.
Learn More Learn More
Logistics 22 miles of Interstate 85 bisect Cherokee County. Cherokee County is centrally located between the SC Inland Port and and the Norfolk Southern Intermodal facility at Charlotte Douglas International Airport. Cherokee is ideally positioned to meet the needs of your Business.

Energy A strong and supportive business climate combined with an abundance of resources and a readily available workforce makes Cherokee County the prime location for energy innovation.
