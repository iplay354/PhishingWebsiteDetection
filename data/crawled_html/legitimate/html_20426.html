Introducing the 2025 Cook PVI℠

The Cook Political Report is pleased to introduce the 2025 Cook Partisan Voting Index (Cook PVI℠) for all 435 congressional districts. First introduced in 1997, the Cook PVI measures how each state and district performs at the presidential level compared to the nation as a whole.

What is The Cook Political Report?

The Cook Political Report is an independent, non-partisan newsletter that analyzes elections and campaigns for the US House of Representatives, US Senate, Governors and President as well as American political trends.
