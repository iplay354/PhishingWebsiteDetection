About Us

Capital Cycle Corporation was founded in 1972 and has now completed fifty years supplying BMW motorcycle enthusiasts with quality parts and accessories at competitive prices. Originally based in Washington, DC, Capital Cycle out-grew the warehouse there and moved to Sterling in what was then the Virginia countryside. Sterling is now a vibrant center of industry with our neighbors including Dulles International Airport, Orbital Sciences and many Internet-related businesses.

We were the first source for BMW parts, and we have attracted many imitators but we are still the best! Our warehouse is home to over 15,000 different part numbers and our customer base now exceeds 50,000.

Our success has been built on sourcing parts and accessories from only those manufacturers who meet the rigorous quality standards of the major vehicle makers; in particular, those who meet BMW’s standards. This means that we still source all of our parts and most of our accessories in Europe, mainly Germany. Dealing directly with the manufacturers enables us to offer excellent value. It also provides our customers confidence that the goods they are buying meet the required quality standards.

This online store lists only a fraction of the parts we hold in stock and from time to time we also have special offer items that are not normally stocked. Please check back regularly for these items. In addition, we can supply all parts and accessories that BMW hold in North America.

If you cannot find what you need in the catalogue or on the web site, just call; our toll-free order line is 1-800-642-5100. We will be happy to identify the part and ship it right to your door. Thank you for your support and business over the years - we look forward to serving you for many years to come!
