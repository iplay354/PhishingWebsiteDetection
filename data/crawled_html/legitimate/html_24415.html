Overview

The San Fernando Valley is a diverse, cultured and cosmopolitan region, offering a vast array of attractions to visitors. Huge (260 square miles) and populous (over 1.8-million residents), the SFV boasts everything from historic movie studios to rugged mountains; sleek luxury hotels to stunning parks; museums and galleries to luxury retail and vintage boutiques; the LA Rams practice facility and a 100-acre mixed-use complex in development.

To Do

Contrary to its portrayal in movies like Valley Girl and Fast Times at Ridgemont High, the Valley's cultural scene includes museums, ornamental gardens, theaters, concert venues and vibrant displays of art. Along with the NoHo Arts District, attractions are spread across the sprawling region, from the eponymous city of San Fernando and neighboring Sylmar in the north; to Burbank in the east, and Chatsworth on its western edge.
