Nescafé Brand Campaign

Client: Nescafé 
Role: Art Director, Designer

In 2014 we were tasked to develop a new brand campaign for Nescafé Coffee. We wanted the campaign help tell the story behind its product. I got the opportunity to rethink the look and feel of Nescafé Coffee. 

Created a few mood boards in an effort to reinvent Nescafé's image, educate consumers, build awareness, and giving more presence to the brand. We presented 3 directions how we would tell that story. I worked on the campaign’s look and feel, and site concepts. 

Honda Pitch

Project: Integrated Pitch Campaign
Role: Art Director, Designer

In early 2014, Honda was due for a major brand overhaul so MXM threw its hat in the ring to pitch campaigns and visual ideas. I was tasked to create multiple digital and interactive experiences for Honda's HRV digital AOR work. Creative included advertising campaigns, high-level pages, several rich-media banners, mobile and social for the digital component of the 2015 Honda HRV campaign.

The goal of the site design was to become a hub of Honda’s performance, stories, and cultural happenings related to the brand. The site was designed to feel fun, innovative and informative. Unfortunately the account went to another agency, but because I was able to work on quite a few design concepts I wanted to show what will never see the light of day.

Camera IQ Branding

CAMERA IQ BRANDING

Client: Camera IQ 

Role: Art Director, Designer

In 2018, I was asked to design the brand identity for a new and upcoming tech company: Camera IQ. Camera IQ is the first camera experience manager. They help marketers create new worlds that customers love to explore. Camera IQ involves the world of augmented reality and filters. They create realistic branded experiences for masses. Since the company was focused on “captivating experiences” I wanted the look and feel of the company to look smart, fresh, and modern.

I was responsible for the look and feel of the company’s image, style guides, web and mobile site design, photography, and logo design.

La Lechera Campaign

Client: Nestlé’s La Lechera
