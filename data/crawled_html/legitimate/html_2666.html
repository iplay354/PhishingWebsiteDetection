Architecture of Dun Laoghaire Rathdown

1904 – Methodist Church, Dun Laoghaire, Co. Dublin

Architect: George F. Beckett

In a town of spires and towers, this small Methodist Church on Northumberland Avenue has neither. A simple gothic revival design with interesting massing of entrances in relation to the main body of the church. The Architect was Mr. George F. Beckett who, as a young man, won a competition for the best plan. The church cost over 5,000 pounds to erect.

Published January 27, 2010 | Last Updated November 19, 2024
