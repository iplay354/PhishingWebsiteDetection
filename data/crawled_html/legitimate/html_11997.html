WHAT SETS US APART

A Boutique Firm With Big Firm Capability

With twenty-five highly capable and trusted attorneys, Brody Wilkinson is a boutique law firm that provides clients with a wide range of services characteristic of a much larger firm. We strive to be our clients’ first call when a legal issue or need arises and provide one-stop services – whether we handle a matter directly, collaborate with another firm, or refer the matter to a trusted firm in our network. We believe that this is our brand and what makes us so distinctly special. Our strategic affiliation with Primerus, a global alliance of small to mid-sized law firms, extends our reach and capability well beyond the borders of Connecticut and New York, where we maintain offices, to most of the United States and across forty countries.
