Industrial Electrical Equipment Sales and Services

Atlas Electric, Inc. specializes in the sales and service of electrical power distribution and control equipment. We stock a huge inventory of new, surplus, and reconditioned equipment, parts, and components.  Our professional sales, engineering, and technical staff have years of experience and knowledge in the industry and are ready to assist with anything from hard to find or obsolete circuit breakers to complete substations. We are committed to providing you with quality, competitively priced electrical products and services backed by our one-year warranty.

Custom Electrical Equipment Design and Manufacturing

Our Custom Manufacturing and Integration Department provides new original equipment solutions for virtually any application. Atlas Electric has over 30 years of experience designing, manufacturing and integrating Electrical Power Distribution Equipment for a wide variety of industries such as Mining, Lumber, Pulp and Paper, Gas and Oil, Food Processing and Foundries. Our sales and engineering staff will work closely with you to design a solution tailored to your specific application, budget, and time frame.
