Frequently Asked Questions

To join Columbus REALTORS®, you must first be a licensed real estate agent in the state of Ohio. From there, you can apply for REALTOR® membership, which connects you to powerful tools, professional standards, and a trusted network across central Ohio. Learn how to become a REALTOR®

Membership dues and fees may vary depending on your role and membership type. Visit our Membership Dues & Fees page for the most accurate and up-to-date information.

Yes! We offer Affiliate and Sustaining Memberships for businesses and professionals affiliated with the real estate industry. Typical affiliate members include:

  • Moving companies
  • Title Agents
  • Appraisers
  • Mortgage lenders
  • Home inspectors

Access to the Columbus and Central Ohio Regional Multiple Listing Service, Inc. (MLS) is available to licensed brokers and their agents who are members of the MLS. As a wholly owned subsidiary of Columbus REALTORS®, our MLS connects over 700 real estate offices and thousands of professionals. 

By joining the MLS, you gain: 

  • Real-time property listing data
  • Collaboration tools across brokerages
  • Increased market exposure and visibility

Learn how to become an MLS member


Get engaged by:

  • Joining committees
  • Serving as committee chairs
  • Running for a seat on the Board of Directors

Whether you’re looking to grow your voice in the industry or give back to your peers, there are meaningful opportunities to lead and make an impact.
