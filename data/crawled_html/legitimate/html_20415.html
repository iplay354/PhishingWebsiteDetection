What's Happening in Cooke City

Our live webcam is looking east towards Republic Mountain and Yellowstone National Park.

Ice Cream Dogs

The dog days of summer require both cool mountain splendor and ice cream! We have both at the Cooke City Sinclair. Wherever your mountain adventures take you, be sure to stop in for some Wilcoxson's ice cream (made locally in Livingston, MT) and enjoy the beautiful mountain views from our picnic tables. Dogs not included!

Seasonal Gift Baskets

Looking for the perfect memento from your vacation? Or maybe a gift for a special someone you wish was sharing Cooke City mountain views, abundant wildlife, and starry night skies with? Check out our selection of stickers, magnets, pins, jams, candles, coffee and other travel sized gifts. Perfect to fit in a carry-on, on a water bottle or car bumper, or even simply enjoyed with your morning breakfast!

Backpacking Food

Whether you are hitting the trails hard for a day hike, going on a multi-day adventure, or just meandering around the many town trails, the Cooke City Sinclair has you covered! We have backpacking food, trail snacks, and icy cold singles to celebrate reaching the top of that peak.

Fin Coffee Break

Best doughnuts and coffee in town! Views aren't that bad either... Freshly made doughnuts every morning. Freshly ground coffee every day. And our world-famous cold brew. Made in house using the best mountain water in the world.

Dinner Groceries

Packing for Yellowstone National Park, the Beartooth Highway, and all the other exciting adventures along the way, leave you with little room for groceries? We have your back. Stop into the Cooke City Sinclair for locally sourced meat, dry goods, dairy, seasonal produce, as well as all the snacks and grab and go food you need for your vacation. We also have a large selection of beer, wine and drink mixers.
