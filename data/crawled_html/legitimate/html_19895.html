Guns N' Roses map out first U.S. tour in five years

    Believe it or not, it’s been three years since Guns N’ Roses released their previously long-awaited LP, Chinese Democracy. It’s been five years, however, since they’ve toured the United States. Now, with a new guitarist in tow – DJ Ashba, if you recall – the band plans on supporting its “latest” effort with a string of fall dates. Hey, better late than never, right?

    The jaunt, which sports opener Buckcherry, kicks off Halloween weekend in Orlando, FL and runs until the end of November. In that time, they’ll hit major markets like Miami, Atlanta, Minneapolis, Chicago, and East Rutherford. As The Audio Perv reports, the Monster Energy-fueled tour will eventually expand to 30 dates. In the meantime, peep the roster below and plan accordingly.

    Guns N’ Roses’ Chinese Democracy is still available via Best Buy. Remember?

    Guns N’ Roses 2011 Tour Dates:
    10/28 – Orlando, FL @ Amway Center
    10/29 – Miami, FL @ AmericanAirlines Arena
    10/31 – Greenville, SC @ BI-LO Center
    11/02 – Atlanta, GA @ Philips Arena
    11/04 – Houston, TX @ Toyota Center
    11/05 – Dallas, TX @ Gexa Energy Pavilion
    11/08 – Omaha, NE @ Qwest Center
    11/09 – Norman, OK @ Lloyd Noble Center
    11/12 – Kansas City, MO @ Sprint Center
    11/13 – Minneapolis, MN @ Target Center
    11/15 – Chicago, IL @ Allstate Arena
    11/17 – East Rutherford, NJ @ IZOD Center
    11/19 – Hartford, CT @ Comcast Theatre
    11/20 – Wilkes-Barre, PA @ Mohegan Sun Area
    11/25 – Worcester, MA @ DCU Center
    11/26 –  Camden, NJ @ Susquehanna Bank Center
