Why Join Sertoma?

If you’re looking for a service organization to join please consider the following:

  • We’re a true SERVICE organization. This means we get out and work hard to raise the funds we make available. We have a history of service and hard work and believe there is great honor in both.
  • We’re a great place to network. The club is made up of professionals of all ages and is a great opportunity to meet new people.
  • We pay dues so 100% of proceeds can be donated to the organizations we help.
  • We start each meeting with a prayer and the Pledge of Allegiance, and we’re proud of it.
  • We actively encourage civic participation and make that possible through weekly meetings with local leaders of all types. The biggest names in the area speak at Sertoma.

Did You Know?

  • Since 1968, Columbia Sertoma has donated more than $3.5 million to the Carolina Children’s Home.
  • The Heritage Golf Tournament started as a Columbia Sertoma project at Fort Jackson.
  • Columbia and Richland Sertoma donated the initial $25,000 to the Riverbanks Zoo to help make it a reality.
  • Columbia Sertoma helped to eradicate polio in the Midlands.
  • 13 Columbia organizations have received financial assistance from Columbia Sertoma in the last 12 months.
  • Columbia Sertoma has started more than 30 area Sertoma chapters since 1950.
  • Sertoma members pay dues so that 100% of all proceed dollars are donated to charity.
  • The Sugar Bowl in New Orleans was started by a Sertoma Club.
 
About Columbia Sertoma

The Columbia Sertoma Club is one of the oldest and most prestigious SERVICE organizations in the Columbia area. Started in 1950, its mission is twofold: to raise money for other local charitable organizations by coordinating and hosting fundraising projects and events, and to encourage civic engagement by hosting weekly meetings featuring community leaders of all types.

Since 1950, the club has assisted approximately 100 local charities and raised approximately $4 million.
