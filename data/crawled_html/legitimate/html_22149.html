About David Alpert

Read About Me

DAVID ALPERT is a nationally recognized, award winning singer/songwriter of children and family songs.
Originally a Connecticut native, David worked as a songwriter for many years in Los Angeles, CA writing everything from jingles for advertising to musical soundtracks for commercial videos, even pop songs for publishing companies and performing artists in the music industry.
David also worked for the Los Angeles Unified School District with children ranging in ages 2 through 12, where he developed a better understanding of every child’s innate creative ability, especially musically & lyrically.
Upon returning to Connecticut he completed a series of children’s songs specifically designed to promote        self­-esteem and self­-confidence that encourage children to realize their own musical ideas even if only in the  most basic form.
