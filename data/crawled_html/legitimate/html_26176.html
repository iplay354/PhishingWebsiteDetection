Dunedin Ice Hockey Association

Dunedin Ice Hockey Association was formed in 1990 with the key goal to initiate, organise, conduct, foster, and promote the sport of Ice Hockey at all competitive, representative, social and learner levels within the Otago region.
We have a membership of around 300 players ranging from preschool,to masters. As a club we are strengthened by the huge number of families and volunteers who support hockey across all levels. DIHA is committed to providing quality experiences and opportunities to participants and supporters of all ages, gender and level of ability.

The DIHA constitution lists the objectives of the DIHA as:

  1. To initiate, organise, conduct, foster and promote the sport of amateur ice hockey in the Dunedin area, making it accessible to all ages and skill levels.
  2. To make Rules and By-Laws for the control of the sport and its administration in Dunedin, and to require all members to recognise the DIHA as having the final right to determine, whether by way of appeal or otherwise, all disputes in relation to the sport, this Constitution, Rules and By-Laws.
  3. To facilitate, initiate, organise and conduct programmes and competitions designed to develop the skills of its players, teams, coaches and officials.
  4. To foster and promote good sportsmanship.

Dunedin Ice Hockey Association is a registered not for profit incorporated society and proud member of Ice Hockey New Zealand.
