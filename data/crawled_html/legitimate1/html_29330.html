Our Mission

Choosing an educational placement for your child is one of the most important decisions that you as a parent will have to make. At the Child and Career Development Center, our goal is the same as yours - to help your child reach his/her full potential. We specialize in providing a caring, nurturing learning environment, which encourages and enables all children to experience success.

The Chester County Intermediate Unit will not discriminate in employment, educational programs or activities based on race, color, religion, national origin, age, sex, disability, marital status or because a person is a disabled veteran or a veteran of the Vietnam era.
