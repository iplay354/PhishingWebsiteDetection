Fire Protection Services

At Judd Fire Protection, our mission is to provide reliable, experienced fire protection services to keep our clients’ property and personnel safe. With over 24 years of experience in the industry, we have the knowledge and technical capability to install, inspect, and service a full scope of fire protection systems. We serve clients in commercial and residential properties throughout Maryland, including Baltimore and Westminster, as well as Northern Virginia, West Virginia, Delaware, and Pennsylvania.

Estimating, Design & Installation

Our NICET Certified Engineers have more than 65 years of combined experience in the design and installation of fire protection systems, including sprinkler systems, fire alarms, and fire extinguishers. Our technicians take pride in the quality of their work and we provide ongoing training to maintain high standards.

Inspection

Inspection is a critical component in maintaining the performance of your fire protection system. We fully comply with all requirements set forth by authorities having jurisdiction and all work is done in strict compliance with the NFPA (National Fire Protection Association). We recommend inspections to be conducted quarterly or yearly to prevent malfunction and/or failure.
