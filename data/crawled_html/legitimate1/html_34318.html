Christmas Opening Hours

Our offices will be closed from Wednesday, 24 December to Monday, 29 December. Reopening on Tuesday, 30 December. Motor Tax Office and Cash Desk will also be closed on Wednesday, 31 December. Should you need us in an emergency over the Christmas period, call 1800 211 525. The Planning Counter will close to the Public on Tuesday, 23 December at 4pm and will reopen on Friday, 2 January at 10am. Telephone and email queries will be dealt with on December 30 & 31 December as normal.

Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant

Got a vacant or derelict property? Turn it into a home! Get up to €50,000 with a Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant and up to €70,000 for a Derelict Property This is your chance to breathe new life into unused spaces and create homes where they’re needed most. Click on the link or contact the Vacant Homes Unit Team at VacantHomesOfficer@longfordcoco.ie.

Free Parking in Longford Town

Get yourself into Longford Town and shop local and bag a bargain this Christmas. Free off-street parking all day for Black Friday Weekend, 28 & 29 November before celebrating "Christmas on the Square" on Sunday 30th with markets, live music and festive family fun. Santa arrives to light up Longford at 6pm. Free off-street parking continues on Saturday 6th December and every day from December 13th to 31st. This is an initiative of Longford County Council, supporting local businesses.
