Fontwell Park

Fontwell Park is a charming and unique National Hunt racecourse situated in the picturesque West Sussex countryside. It is the only racecourse in the UK to feature a figure-of-eight steeplechase course, providing a distinct challenge for jockeys and a thrilling spectacle for spectators. The venue hosts over 20 race meetings annually, including the feature Grade 2 National Spirit Hurdle. Alongside racing, Fontwell Park offers popular themed events like Ladies Day and family fun days. Those looking to enjoy a little more from their day can choose from a range of high-quality race day hospitality options such as private boxes and dining in the Premier Restaurant.

Beyond the races, the venue also serves as a versatile private hire venue for a wide range of functions - most notably weddings, with the beautiful, historic Fontwell House as the perfect backdrop.
