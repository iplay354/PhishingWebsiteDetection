In-Vitro Diagnostic Products & Solutions

At AliveDx, we empower diagnostic insights to transform patient care and innovate for life. With over 30 years of experience in in-vitro diagnostics, we advance global testing in autoimmune, allergy and related clinical fields. Our products and solutions: Alba, MosaiQ and LumiQ deliver clinical and economic value by simplifying and improving lab workflow efficiency. Through innovation and reliability, we help healthcare professionals deliver accurate results that enhance patient care.

A Drive to Provide Solutions for Clinicians and Laboratories

With over 30 years of experience in immunohematology and in-vitro diagnostics, our products and solutions address today’s challenges in clinical and laboratory environments. We are committed to meeting the global needs of patients and clinicians.

MosaiQ®

Our MosaiQ solution is designed to shorten time-to-diagnosis for patients by providing faster in-vitro test results. As an easy-to-use single solution, the platform performs multiple tests and gives maximum flexibility to laboratorians and clinicians.
