Stamporama - the Online Stamp Club

Stamporama began with 6 members in January 1996 with one idea -- trading duplicates among the members.

Today Stamporama has 1103 members from 44 states, 11 Provinces and Territories of Canada, 6 states of Australia and 28 total countries worldwide. Members range from 8 to 88; beginner to expert. And they collect everything and are happy to share this collective expertise on the very active, gently moderated discussion board.

Our members study and collect stamps, covers, FDCs, rates, usages, routes, ephemera, Maximum cards, seals, body bags and crash covers, the machines creating the cancellations and postmarks, spray-ons and other transit markings not technically cancellations, history behind the man sending the stamp.

Trading days are long gone; instead we employ both a traditional online auction and a unique approval service. All are free and without fees.
