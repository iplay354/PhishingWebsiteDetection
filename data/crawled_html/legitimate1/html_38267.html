THE MARKHAM EXPERIENCE

At Markham College, education is viewed as a lifelong journey that begins at age three and continues well beyond graduation at eighteen. We are a bilingual Peruvian international school rooted in British heritage and pioneering innovative education in Peru and across South America. Our aim is to inspire students to become lifelong learners who are ready to face the challenges and opportunities of the future.

Our core principles guide us in shaping the future of Markham College, uniting us as one cohesive educational community.

BELONGING

Building an inclusive community

OPPORTUNITY

Discovering individual brilliance

RESPONSIBILITY

Empowering people to act positively

TRUST
