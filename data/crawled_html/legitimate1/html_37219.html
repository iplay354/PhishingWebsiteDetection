Women’s Entrepreneurship Day Organization

Join us in a global movement empowering women! As the force behind Women’s Entrepreneurship Day on November 19th, we work year-round to drive women’s economic independence—sparking leaders, innovators, and entrepreneurs to fuel growth, shape policies, and uplift communities. When women thrive, families, communities, and nations flourish. Say YES and be part of a powerful movement creating a world where every woman becomes a force for equality and unstoppable impact!
