From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
German film director (born 1959)
Rainer Kaufmann
Born (1959-06-06) 6 June 1959 (age 66)
Frankfurt, West Germany
OccupationFilm director
Years active1989–present

Rainer Kaufmann (born 6 June 1959) is a German film director. He directed more than thirty films including The Pharmacist [1] and Operation Sugar [de], a film about human trafficking.[2]

Selected filmography

[edit]
  • Dann eben mit Gewalt (1993, TV film) — (based on a novel by Jan de Zanger [nl])
  • The Little Innocent [de] (1994, TV film)
  • One of My Oldest Friends (1995) — (based on a story by F. Scott Fitzgerald)
  • Talk of the Town (1995)
  • The Pharmacist (1997) — (based on a novel by Ingrid Noll)
  • Long Hello and Short Goodbye (1999) — (screenplay by Jeff Vintar)
  • Cold Is the Evening Breeze (2000) — (based on a novel by Ingrid Noll)
  • Queen of Cherries [de] (2004, TV miniseries) — (based on a novel by Justus Pfaue)
  • Marias's Last Journey [de] (2005, TV film)
  • Four Daughters (2006, TV film) — (based on a novel by Inger Alfvén)
  • Runaway Horse (2007) — (based on a novella by Martin Walser)
  • A Grand Exit [fr] (2008, TV film)
  • The Best Is Yet to Come [de] (2008, TV film)
  • Thanksgiving [de] (2009, TV film) — (based on a novel by Volker Klüpfel [de] and Michael Kobr [de])
  • In aller Stille [de] (2010, TV film)
  • Blaubeerblau [de] (2011, TV film)
  • When the Foehn Wind Is Blowing [de] (2011, TV film) — (based on a novel by Jörg Maurer [de])
  • Milk Money [de] (2012, TV film) — (based on a novel by Volker Klüpfel [de] and Michael Kobr [de])
  • Operation Sugar [de] (2012, TV film)
  • Seegrund. Ein Kluftingerkrimi [de] (2013, TV film) — (based on a novel by Volker Klüpfel [de] and Michael Kobr [de])
  • Unexpected [de] (2014, TV film)
  • Die Puppenspieler [de] (2017, TV film) — (based on a novel by Tanja Kinkel)
  • Der Polizist und das Mädchen [de] (2018, TV film)
  • Lost in Separation [de] (2019)
