No. 29 Design is a Boston-based design studio focusing on
new construction design and new development design since 2012.

Our goal when designing a home is to create a space that speaks to you. Whether you are looking for a distinct style or everyday practicality we strive to give you both. The place you call home should be an extension of who you are and how you live.

We work with architects, developers, investors and contractors to fully detail the interiors of a home. Whether this is a client's home or a development project, the interior architecture and interior design should be as seamless as possible. Our job is to create a cohesive space before the walls are even constructed.

Each projects start at floorplan stage or even with a walkthrough after purchase. This enables us to hit the ground running. We work together with your construction team to provide the best design vision. This includes drawings, specifications for kitchens and bathrooms, tile, stone, built-in cabinetry, hardware, paint schedules, lighting plans and flooring.
