El Dorado San Felipe Rentals - Homes and Condos

El Dorado Ranch, a gated community with many amenities, is the biggest resort in the San Felipe area and is located on both sides of Highway 5 near the KM 175 marker.  There are El Dorado Ranch rentals available on both the sea side and mountain side.

The seaside section of El Dorado Ranch is called La Ventana del Mar where the fully furnished popular condos are available for long or short term reservations.  Amenities include an 18-hole golf course, tennis courts and two large swimming complexes with jacuzzis.

 

Our El Dorado Ranch mountain side homes offer better rates and are closer to our newly built state of the art Cachanilla swimming pool.  This is also the preferred vacation spot with vacationers who are searching for peace and quiet while on vacation.

La Hacienda San Felipe Rental Homes

La Hacienda is a beautiful gated community located along the South Beach corridor of San Felipe known for having pristine beaches.  The La Hacienda resort is just seven to eight minutes south of town toward the San Felipe airport.  Turn left before the airport at the "La Hacienda" sign.
 

Amenities which come with our San Felipe rentals are listed on each property profile.  Most of our accommodations have an attached garage and access to one or more swimming pools and tennis courts.  There are three restaurants for your convenience at El Dorado Ranch.
