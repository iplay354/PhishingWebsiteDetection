Welcome to The Petty Archives

With over 2,000 pages of content covering 50+ years, we offer the most comprehensive archive of Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers in the known universe.

Our site is free, fully searchable, easy-to-browse, ad-free, and supported by fans.
