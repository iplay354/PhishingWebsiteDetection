Host to the world's greatest golfers and most prestigious tournaments

Founded in 1926, The Riviera Country Club has always been the pinnacle of golf. Ranked as one of the top 50 golf courses in the world and a top 25 in the United States, the 18 holes continuously challenge the world's best. Currently home to The Genesis Invitational, Riviera will also host golf's elite in 2028 as the official site of the Olympics golf tournament.

Golf

George C. Thomas, an amateur architect who agreed to design the golf course free of charge created his crowning achievement and even today, Riviera is considered one of the finest natural layouts in all of golf.

Tennis

One of the oldest and largest tennis clubs in the Western United States, Riviera features 24 courts. With members competing on the pro circuit to beginners, our devoted membership is one of the most engaged in the US.

History

Our storied history includes, the 1948 US Open, the 1983 and 1995 PGA Championships, the 1998 US Senior Open, and the 2017 US Amateur. Take a step back through time and explore all of Riviera major events.
