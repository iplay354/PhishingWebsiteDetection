WHO WE ARE

We are a group of clean romance authors, book bloggers, and voracious readers who share a deep and abiding passion for clean romance.

WHAT WE DO

Established in 2012, Clean Romance has spent years finding ways to recommend the highest quality of clean romance novels across all genres.
​Now, in 2021, we have rebranded to do just that.

HOW WE DO IT

Through an anonymous and thorough recommendation and review system, we assess novels in a fair and unbiased manner, ensuring each novel passes a rigorous process before being recommended.

WHAT TO LOOK FORWARD TO

We have dozens of fun and engaging content ideas for our followers, including novel recommendations, new release notifications, cover reveals, author spotlights, and so much more.

Bookstagram?

We are always on the lookout for great bookstagrammers who are passionate about the good, clean, wholesome, books they read. If you are someone who is constantly reading and want to recommend a title for consideration  we would love to hear from you.
