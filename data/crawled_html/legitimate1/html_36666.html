Festive Breakfast with Santa at the PCCM

The Philippine Canadian Centre of Manitoba (PCCM) welcomed families and community members to its annual Festive Breakfast with Santa on Saturday, December 13, 2025, filling the centre with warmth, laughter, and holiday cheer.

Coordinated by PCCM Program Director Gary Alejo, the event brought together parents and children for a joyful morning of celebration at the PCCM. Families enjoyed a free hot breakfast, festive activities, and special gifts from Santa Claus, much to the delight of the children and guests in attendance. The gathering created a welcoming and inclusive space where families from diverse backgrounds could come together and celebrate the spirit of the season.
