Welcome to the UF ISE Department!

Founded in 1965 based on years of coursework with a focus on engineering and management, the department has consistently expanded its teaching and research missions to include other areas of ISE, such as optimization, information systems, and supply chain and logistics systems. Department excellence in these areas has allowed for the recruitment of outstanding faculty and students who have enhanced the existing curriculum and made major research contributions.
