Exacter Helps Prevent Wildfires in High Fire Threat Districts

Electrical transmission surveys aimed at wildfire mitigation prioritize High Fire Threat Districts to ensure the most at-risk areas are inspected. Exacter sensors, weighing only 16 lbs., can be mounted alongside LIDAR, infrared, videography, or other technologies on the same helicopter, with no operator required onboard—pilots can simply turn the sensor on or off. These lightweight sensors can be easily transferred between helicopters, maximizing reach during preventive maintenance cycles or downtime. With a rapid report turnaround, including emergency results within one hour, this system streamlines inspections while enhancing the opportunity to identify potential ignition sources.

Patented technology and analytics from Exacter® provide a variety of unique solutions

Transmission

Exacter's airborne technology gathers data for utilities to analyze transmission system health, pinpoint wildfire issues, and identify problematic components.

Distribution

Vision Analytics predicts and locates equipment-related outages, enabling utilities to improve performance with Exacter assessments and asset management strategies.

Substation

Exacter integrates RF partial discharge and ultrasonic tech to swiftly identify substation equipment threats, pinpointing invisible pre-failure signatures for immediate reporting.
