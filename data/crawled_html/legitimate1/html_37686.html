About Coosa-Alabama

The Coosa-Alabama River Improvement Association, Inc. Coosa-Alabama promotes the multipurpose use of waterways. We also advocate for the environmental protection of the Alabama, Coosa, and Tallapoosa river basins.

Our Rivers

The Alabama, Coosa, and Tallapoosa rivers combined flow from Georgia to the Gulf of Mexico. Together they form a crucial transportation route and contribute to our state’s economy, ecology, and recreation.
