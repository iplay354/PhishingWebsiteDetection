The PAC News & Notes

The Purchase PAC 2025-2026 Season

(June 30, 2025) Our 2025-2026 Center Series of world-class performing arts events will begin in October of 2025. The season will feature a broad range of events that celebrate the incredible inventiveness and diversity of American artists and art forms, and the ideals that have nurtured and sparked such broad-ranging creativity. In addition to the performances themselves, there will be multiple opportunities for creative encounters and audience engagement. Tickets will go on sale Monday, July 21. About The Purchase PAC

For over forty years, The Performing Arts Center, Purchase College (The Purchase PAC), a four-theatre complex located on the campus of Purchase College, SUNY, has been the hub of a creative community that brings together internationally acclaimed artists, rising stars, creative practitioners, and an inspirational array of loyal patrons and friends in a celebration of the performing arts. It is this mix of artistry and friendship that has forged The Purchase PAC’s legacy as the apex of the performing arts in our region – and it is the same bond that sustains us.
