Plagiarism detection

Protecting the originality of your documented content is essential, whether for academic works, business or personal documents.

With our plagiarism detection tool, you can quickly see if your text resembles other text available online. It works this way: the system compares your content with a massive database, searching for any similarity. If there is any similarity in another location on the Internet, you will be immediately notified. It's a practical way to ensure that your work is unique and protected.
