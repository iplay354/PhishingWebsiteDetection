Choosing a crib for your baby is an important step in creating your dream nursery. Not only are luxury baby cribs often the centerpiece of a nursery,but they will also be a place where your baby spends a significant amount of time. At AFK we create luxury baby furniture to fit your personal style and beautiful designer baby cribs complete your dream nursery. Our vast collection of custom made unique baby cribs are the perfect choice for your nursery - we even offer the option of designing custom baby cribs with luxury crib bedding!

                            

In the world of luxury baby furniture, AFK stands out from other high end furniture brands. With limitless combinations and options of customizable collections, AFK produces some of the most unique nursery furniture available. This is because our designer furniture is handmade through impeccable wood furniture design with careful attention to detail. Each piece is carefully constructed to produce the quality furniture our clientele desires. From everything including baby furniture sets, armoires & display cabinets, bookcases, changing tables, to upholstered seating, AFK is the best option. We create beautiful high end furniture to bring your dream home to life!

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Create the perfect room for your child and memories that last a lifetime with beautiful fine furniture from AFK. At AFK we manufacture beautiful luxury furniture that will inspire joy throughout your home with collections like our best selling Gramercy Collection. The Gramercy Collection includes a multitude of amazing kid’s furniture, including desks & vanities, night tables & side tables, children’s bunk beds, and toy chests that are the ultimate addition to your home and life. We work with you to incorporate our unique designer furniture and lovely nursery decorwith the goal of designing your ideal children’s room or baby bedroom and creating beautiful luxury interiors.
