A Leading University in Wisconsin

Since 1866, the University of Wisconsin-Platteville has been a regional leader among 4-year colleges in Wisconsin with challenging academics; renowned faculty; and notable alumni. And our faculty, staff, and students are applying their research and skills to impact our communities in a positive way.

At UW-Platteville, you’ll gain practical, real-world experience that equips you for success in today’s competitive job market. Hands-on learning, industry partnerships, and some of the most affordable tuition rates among colleges in Wisconsin, make UW-Platteville a smart investment in your future. We are leaders in science, technology, agriculture, education, and engineering fields, with unique initiatives in forensic investigation and renewable energy. We are applying this expertise to be here for you, now and in the future.

Campus life includes diverse student organizations, clubs, and an NCAA Division III athletics program. As one of the leading accredited colleges in Wisconsin, we foster an inclusive environment, encouraging lifelong connections, collaboration, creativity, and leadership.
