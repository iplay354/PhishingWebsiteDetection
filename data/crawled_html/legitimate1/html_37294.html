Established in 1958

Welcome to Southern California's most trusted name in the bridal industry for over 60 years. Behind the doors of Lili Bridals, our beautiful boutique showcases gowns from the top designers in the industry. Lili truly has something for everyone: Whether your style is classic and simple, whether it’s boho, vintage-inspired, or a sparkly-princess ballgown we have something for your unique style and budget.

Mother-of-the-bride, mother-of-the-groom, and anyone looking for formal eveningwear for that special occasion can find something at Lili Bridals.

Imagine there was a place where just being yourself was celebrated. Where there are no judgments and no restrictions to just being 100% uniquely you…you’ve found it! It’s Lili Bridals.

Coming through our doors and experience our service about which many customers have said "When I come in here it feels like I‘m with family!" Or "It just feels GOOD in here!" Our warm and inviting atmosphere simply shines from the moment you walk through our doors.

Stop searching, take a breath, relax and book your appointment now.

We can’t wait to meet you, we can’t wait to help you find your dream dress and we simply cannot wait to celebrate YOU!
