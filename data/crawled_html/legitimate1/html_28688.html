OpenMethods metablog: From a DARIAH Core Service to a DARIAH-IT Community Service

What is OpenMethods? OpenMethods is a metablog highlighting curated content about Digital Humanities Methods and Tools, an important area currently underrepresented in the DH peer-reviewed literature especially, but not exclusively, from a European perspective. With over 250 posts contributed by committed Volunteer Editors, OpenMethods metablog gives accessibility and visibility of scholarly conversations on digital methods.
