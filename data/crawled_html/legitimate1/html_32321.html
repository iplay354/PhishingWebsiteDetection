Undergraduate Degrees

With a broad range of courses across music performance, composition, production, and performing arts, our undergraduate degrees are your first step towards a career in today’s creative industries.

With our suite of music and performing arts postgraduate courses, you can develop your career, as you train to become innovative professionals in the creative industries.

Why choose Leeds Conservatoire?

Study at Leeds Conservatoire and become part of a dynamic, creative community with great links to the industry. 

Here are our top 12 reasons to launch your career in the music or performing arts industry right here at Leeds Conservatoire.

Find out more

Partnership Opportunities

The conservatoire is partnered with a host of arts and education organisations in Leeds and the North, helping us provide the highest quality education and facilities to students and members of the public.

Active collaboration with industry-leading partners, including Opera North, Orchestras Live and Black Lives in Music, enhances the learning experience and gives our students opportunities to make connections and boost their employability.

Find out more

A state-of-the-art conservatoire open until late

Students have access to our facilities until late during term-time.

That means more time composing, recording, rehearsing and performing.

Explore our facilities

Performance Opportunities

Students play at our regular showcase nights, perform at venues throughout the city, including the Brudenell Socal Club, Belgrave Music hall, and Oporto.

In addition, many of our students join the Contemporary Pop Choir and other extra-curricular ensembles that we run.

View performance opportunities

One-to-one tuition

Our staff are experts in their field, and are active musicians, actors, practitioners, authors and researchers.

They are joined by a wealth of industry professionals and visiting professors who regularly come to the conservatoire to impart specialist knowledge to students. 

Meet our tutors

11 December 2025 New Report Recommends Measures to Protect Young People Working in Music

Leeds Conservatoire welcomes a new report from Youth Music, launched in response to a growing number of young creatives calling for improved safety and rights in music.

Find out more
about 'New Report Recommends Measures to Protect Young People Working in Music'
25 November 2025 First Bronze Awarded in REC Pilot for Small and Specialist Institutions and Research Institutes

Luminate Education Group, of which Leeds Conservatoire is a member, has been awarded the first Bronze as part of a pilot project to develop a pathway for the Race Equality Charter (REC) to small and specialist institutions and research institutes.

Find out more
about 'First Bronze Awarded in REC Pilot for Small and Specialist Institutions and Research Institutes'
24 November 2025 Lesley Garrett CBE Supports Emerging Talent at Opera Meets Musical Theatre Symposium

An innovative symposium, which took place at Leeds Conservatoire, explored in detail the crossover between opera and musical theatre and how the two genres can learn from one another.
