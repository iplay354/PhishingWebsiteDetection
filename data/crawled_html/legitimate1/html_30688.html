The Art of the Unexpected

interior mythmaking & palm beach interior design

Featured Projects

Every project begins with a conversation—but we’re listening for more than what’s said aloud. We pay attention to what draws you in—your obsessions, your rituals, the objects you collect without thinking. Sean studies the way light filters through your space at different times of day, the hidden symmetry of walls, the sacred geometry waiting to be uncovered. The goal is never to impose a pre-existing look. We’re here to extract the essence of a home and make it sharper, smarter, and more intentional.

Our sourcing is unapologetically global and relentlessly specific. A sculptural glass chain from a Seattle artist becomes the starting point for an entire dining room. A sofa arrives to be deconstructed, rebuilt, and reupholstered—defying the limitations of its manufacturer and creating something truly custom. We’re known for a design process that brings personal expression to new levels.

With projects spanning Palm Beach, the Hamptons, and Greenwich, the Sean Rush approach is fearless but never careless. We love to collaborate with our clients—knowing when to push and when to rein it in, when to go full theater and when to strip a space down to its most elemental form. What remains is a home that feels unrepeatable—a place that could only belong to the person who lives there.
