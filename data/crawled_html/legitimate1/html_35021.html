WHAT IS YOUR DATA TELLING YOU?

Real success takes more than capturing data. It requires understanding. Connect your entire operation to better decisions with real-time insights from FreeWave.

Your Data Insights
Drive Decisions.

Decisions drive business change everyday. Having the information to make critical decisions quickly takes intelligence and trust. Your data holds those decisions and it's FreeWave that connects that data for you. Whether through real-time monitoring or long-term learning, we will help you uncover cost-saving opportunities, productivity optimizations, and more.

CONNECTED NETWORK

Bring your data from edge to enterprise with connected devices. These reliable devices are ruggedly built to perform in near-impossible conditions, allowing you to easily connect to and access your operation’s remote data from anywhere.​​

Learn More

MANAGED NETWORK

Manage and control connected devices with our Data Platform. This allows real-time device management and control from anywhere you are. Permission based access and unique identifiers make managing Zentry connected devices simple.

Learn More

CONNECTED SENSORS

Data can be complicated. Data collection doesn’t have to be. Seamlessly integrate your preexisting sensors with our connected devices and data platform to easily gather the information you need to make more informed decisions in the field and the office.

FREEWAVE INSIGHTS® DATA PLATFORM

Whether monitoring equipment or managing workflows, you need to stay on top of isolated details and big-picture processes alike. Accessing and understanding this data can be a make-or-break moment for your business. That’s where we come in. Our IIoT Data Platform helps you make sense of your data. With extensive cloud-based storage and edge computing capabilities, this easy-to-use platform allows you to process and analyze your data in real time using a full suite of analytics, management, and tracking tools.

Learn More

CONNECTED DEVICES

Data is everywhere. Connectivity, on the other hand, is not. And when you have equipment operating in the middle of nowhere, you can’t afford to miss out on important data due to connectivity issues. Bring your data from edge to enterprise with our radios and edge computing gateways. These reliable devices are ruggedly built to perform in near impossible conditions, allowing you to easily connect to your most remote WiFi-enabled devices and access your data from anywhere.
