About the Girls' Frontline series

Developed by Mica Team in Shanghai, Girls' Frontline is a mobile tactical game where the player commands highly advanced androids known as Tactical Dolls as a member of Griffin & Kryuger Private Military Company.

It has spawned a vast cross-media franchise including the games Project Neural Cloud, Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium, Girls' Frontline: Fire Control and Reverse Collapse: Code Name Bakery.

About this wiki

This wiki is a community-driven database for all entries of the Girls' Frontline series and its game servers in all regions. We provide translated information on non-English content. We aim to stay up-to-date with CN media.

We host 4,045 articles and 59,841 files. If you like what we do or have any feedback for us, check out our Discord server!
