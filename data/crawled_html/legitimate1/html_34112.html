About the Office of Undergraduate Studies


Focused on student success, the Office strives to ensure every undergraduate student's access to campus courses and programs, enhancement of the academic experience, and graduation. The Office encompasses many critical units, programs, and activities, including:

  • Living-Learning Programs such as the Honors College, College Park Scholars, and Carillon Communities
  • UMD Fellows Program, Individual Studies, and Asian American Studies
  • Letters and Sciences, New Student Orientation, Academic Achievement Programs, Pre-College Programs, Southern Leadership Management Program (formerly Hillman Entrepreneurs), Incentive Awards Program, and the National Scholarships Office
  • Air Force ROTC, Army ROTC, and Naval ROTC
