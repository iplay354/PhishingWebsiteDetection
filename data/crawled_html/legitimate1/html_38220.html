Straits Quay

Bustling with tourists, local visitors and Seri Tanjung Pinang’s well-heeled residents, Straits Quay will offer a full range of attractions from boutiques to bistros, seafood restaurant to seaside cafes, bars to boat rides. All set against the irresistible backdrop of historical Georgetown and spectacular views of the sea. Spread across 2 storeys facing the marina, retail lots offer the best frontages and views on the island. Just above, another 6 storeys of the fully sold-out luxury Suites at Straits Quay create a ready catchment of customers and holidaymakers, adding life, activity and vibrant colours to this unique retail enclave.
