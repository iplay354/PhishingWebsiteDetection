Photographs


Loaches Online represents the greatest international grouping of practical experience with Loaches to be found on the Internet. It also represents the greatest collection of images of loach species that you will find anywhere, thanks to the photographic skills and contributions from many members. We are lucky to have the talents of many gifted photographers who share their skills and fish for all to enjoy.

We ask that others please respect the photographers copyright of their work and do not use these images without first obtaining permission from the originator.
