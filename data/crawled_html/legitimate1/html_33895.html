People at MEDIUM risk typically:

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different than 9-1-1?

When you press your button, you’re connected instantly to our 24/7 U.S.-based monitoring center—no need to search for your phone, dial a number or explain who you are. We already know your name, location, and emergency contacts, so we can act fast. Unlike 9-1-1, which only routes you through EMS dispatch, we send the right help right away—whether that’s EMS or someone you trust like a neighbor, caregiver, or family member. It’s faster, more personal, and built around you.

Can devices be worn in the shower?

Yes! All our wearable devices are water-resistant and designed to be worn in the shower—one of the most common places where falls can happen. However, please keep base units for home devices dry!

Am I eligible for a free or discounted device?

Depending on your insurance plan, you may be eligible for a discounted or free device! We recommend reaching out directly to your health insurance provider to find out if medical alert devices are covered and which options you may qualify for.

Even if you aren’t covered by your health insurance, at Medical Guardian, it’s our goal to make getting a device as affordable as possible. All of our devices are available for purchase via phone or online. Check out our website for promotions, and you can usually snag a device for a discount!
