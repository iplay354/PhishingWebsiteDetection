    IDI’s Story is About its Graduates

    IDI’s goal is to make its graduates some of the most successful and well-known designers in the country. As a small, progressive college, the Institute has been able to adapt to design changes and trends. It has recognized the importance of active participation and involvement in the major interior design professional organizations, and maintains student chapters of ASID and IIDA.
