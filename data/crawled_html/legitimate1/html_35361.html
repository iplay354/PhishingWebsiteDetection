FIND ME

I am an award winning personal brand, headshot photographer as well as one of the top family photographers in the UK, photographing families of all ages, babies, children, teenagers and also older families whose children are in the teens and 20’s/30’s.

I have been awarded the title of being the Top 100 Female Entrepreneurs in the in UK as part of the F:Entrepreneur Campaign for 2020. I am also a Silver winner in the Children and Family category of the Best Business Women Awards 2020 and more recently a GOLD winner in the creative category of the Best Business Women Awards 2023 and more recently Kingston Borough Business Awards, Winner of the Freelance Category 2024.

The majority of my work takes me throughout London including Kingston upon Thames, Richmond, Kew, Teddington, Barnes, St Margarets, Chiswick, Putney, Wimbledon, Wandsworth, Clapham, Chelsea, Teddington, Twickenham, SW London, Kensington, Mayfair and throughout Surrey including Coombe, New Malden, Thames Ditton, Esher, Cobham, Claygate and Weybridge.
