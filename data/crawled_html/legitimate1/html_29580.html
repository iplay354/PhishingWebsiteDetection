Welcome to April Hamilton

Creating Soulful, Timeless and Elegant designs

Based in Cobham, Surrey, April Hamilton is a luxury interior design studio working across the UK and internationally. The studio creates timeless interiors, tailored to your lifestyle, family and the unique character of your home. 

From the initial consultation to visual concepts and completion, our highly skilled design team, led by Founder and Creative Director Rosie Ridgway, meticulously plan and design every detail to finesse your home to perfection.

As a leading interior design company, we take pride in our attention to detail and customer satisfaction. Our experienced interior designers, Rosie and Charlotte work closely with you to bring your vision to life.

April Hamilton

Welcome to April Hamilton, a luxury interior design studio dedicated to creating beautiful, timeless interiors tailored specifically to you. Your home is an extension of your personality, and we take pride in shaping spaces that reflect your lifestyle, tastes, and the unique character of your home.

From the moment we begin working together, we will be there to guide you every step of the way. Whether you’re looking for a bespoke kitchen, a serene bathroom, or a complete home transformation, every detail is meticulously planned to ensure your vision comes to life effortlessly and beautifully.

Based in Surrey, we offer an intimate, boutique experience. Rosie & Charlotte’s personal involvement and dedication ensures that you receive a service that is not only luxurious but also tailored to your individual needs. At April Hamilton, we aim to make the entire design process seamless, enjoyable, and, most importantly, all about you.

With our reputation for precision design and exceptional project management, you can trust us to handle every aspect of your project with care and expertise. Let April Hamilton elevate your home beyond your expectations—because you deserve nothing less.

What Makes Us Different?

As a boutique interior design company, we pride ourselves on being agile, responsive, and truly attuned to your needs. We understand that every project is personal, and that’s why we make your experience not only collaborative but enjoyable from start to finish. With over 25 years of experience, we’ve carefully built a trusted network of specialists—ranging from custom lighting and furniture designers to expert joiners, builders, and soft furnishing artisans—ensuring every detail is crafted to the highest standard. These partnerships allow us to offer you a seamless, reliable service that you can always count on. Let’s create something extraordinary together!

Bespoke Luxury Interior Design and Global Reach

We provide exceptional designs to private clients mainly in London, Surrey, and Sussex, while also extending our services to the continent, including Majorca and France. The majority of interior design projects we undertake require bespoke pieces designed especially to reflect the character of your home, ensuring they are functional, practical, and beautiful. To achieve this, we incorporate world-class brands into our schemes, impressing even the most discerning of clients.

Don’t shy away from layering your lighting. Wall lights, table lamps, pendants, step lights, thoughtfully placed overheads… each one adds depth, warmth and that irresistible softness that makes a house feel like home

We also love introducing deeper, moodier tones into a scheme. When a space doesn’t call for much furniture, rich colours can deliver the statement the room needs all on their own

And when it comes to paint? Be bold. It’s one of the easiest elements to refresh… so if you take a risk and it doesn’t land, it’s an effortless fix. But when it does work… the transformation is magic! ✨🤎
