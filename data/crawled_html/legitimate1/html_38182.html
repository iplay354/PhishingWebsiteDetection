What Our Riverside Resort Offers

We offer amazing views of the river Ganga making it one of the best riverside resorts in Rishikesh. You will enjoy the beauty of nature right from your room balcony.

Shared Stay: Guests can share a room with one, two, or three other people. These rooms are perfect for families, groups, or solo travelers.

Private Bathrooms: Every guest can enjoy privacy and convenience since each room has its own private bathroom.

Clean Rooms: We maintain our rooms well and equip them with all necessary amenities and cleanliness.

24/7 Hour Room Service: We offer room service both during the day and late at night. You can order food and drinks to your room whenever you want.

Onsite Activities: Guests can enjoy a variety of fun and relaxing experiences, including yoga sessions, bonfire nights, nature walks, and adventure sports arranged right from the resort.

Why choose Simply Heaven Rishikesh?

Simply Heaven Rishikesh is the one of the best spot for a riverside stay in rishikesh. You can enjoy the beauty and peace of the area. Here's why it's the best choice:

  • Great Location: This resort in Rishikesh by the river gives both fun and peace. The resort is a short walk from the river. This makes it easy to join activities like river rafting, yoga, and meditation. Reaching via Neelkanth Road is also easy.
  • Deluxe Cottages: For a comfortable stay, the resort offers luxury cottages that include air conditioning and fans.
  • In-Room Facilities: Each room includes beds, sheets, quilts, pillows, and charging points for your phone, camera, and laptop.
  • In-House Activities: Guests can enjoy indoor games like ludo, chess, carrom, and badminton. Outdoor activities include trekking to a waterfall and walking along a pebble beach.
  • Tasty Food: Our restaurant uses fresh, local ingredients to prepare tasty dishes, offering both treats and healthy options.
  • Comfort and Nature: You will find modern and cozy rooms that let you enjoy nature. They offer beautiful views and green surroundings.
  • Adventure: Our resort is a great place to start adventure sports like bungee jumping, trekking, and river rafting.
  • Rooms & Services: We give cozy rooms with the various modern amenities you need. Several rooms had stunning views of mountains and the holy Ganga River
  • Tasty Meals: Our resort serves three meals each day. We serve a variety of local and international dishes. Tea, coffee, and snacks are also available all day long.

This is why Simply Heaven is one of the best riverside resorts in Rishikesh.
