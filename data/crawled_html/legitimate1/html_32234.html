Our Story

Founded in 2001, NaturalMotion made its mobile debut with Backbreaker Football for iPhone in 2009. We continued to innovate with titles like NFL Rivals and My Horse, before achieving breakthrough success with CSR Racing in 2012—our award-winning, number one grossing game.

Today, NaturalMotion is proud to be part of Zynga, a global leader in interactive entertainment with a mission to connect the world through games.  Zynga is the mobile label of Take-Two Interactive. With games downloaded over 6 billion times, we connect players in 175+ countries through fun, strategy, and a little friendly competition

From thrilling casino spins to epic strategy battles, mind-bending puzzles, and social word challenges, our diverse game portfolio has something for everyone. Fan-favourites and latest hits include FarmVille™, Words With Friends™, Zynga Poker™, Game of Thrones Slots Casino™, Wizard of Oz Slots™, Hit it Rich! Slots™, Wonka Slots™, Top Eleven™, Toon Blast™, Empires & Puzzles™, Merge Dragons!™, CSR Racing™, Harry Potter: Puzzles & Spells™, Match Factory™, and Color Block Jam™—plus many more!

 

Our Games

We’re passionate about wowing players through innovative creativity and technology. Our team loves pushing boundaries and making the impossible happen—then seeing millions of players experience our creations.

Our portfolio showcases this ambition, from the high-octane thrills of CSR Racing to Star Wars: Hunters™. Currently, we’re developing the next chapter in our racing legacy with CSR 3, alongside an exciting unannounced project that we can’t wait to share.

Access All Areas

Access All Areas is NaturalMotion’s innovative web-based virtual studio that opens the doors to game development for everyone. Launched in December 2022, this immersive platform helps parents, children, and educators discover the magic behind their favourite games.

Step inside our 360-degree interactive virtual environment and take an exclusive “behind the scenes” tour of a real game development studio. Explore diverse career paths, discover what skills power the industry, and connect with educational tools and community resources—all designed to showcase the incredible talent that brings games to life.

This ground-breaking project was made possible through Zynga’s social impact fund, established in 2020 to champion charitable and educational initiatives that promote diversity and inclusion in gaming and beyond.
