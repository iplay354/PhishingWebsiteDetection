Our Mission

Our mission is to grow, strengthen, and promote the economic health and vitality of Downtown Miami. As an autonomous agency of the City, the Miami DDA advocates, facilitates, plans, and executes business development, planning, capital improvements, and marketing and communication strategies.

Discover Downtown Miami in 3D

Downtown Miami’s Interactive 3D Development Map is a real-time digital twin that lets users explore current buildings, active construction, and upcoming projects across key districts. With customizable layers for building status, transportation, public spaces, and zoning, this tool enhances transparency and supports smarter planning and decision-making.

Downtown Miami a Thriving Hub for Small Businesses

Downtown Miami is a thriving hub for small businesses, contributing to the area’s dynamic and diverse economic landscape. They contribute to the vibrant and diverse character, providing unique products, services, and experiences for both locals and visitors.

Permit Clinic

Need help opening a business or resolving permit issues in Downtown Miami?
The Miami DDA, in partnership with the City of Miami and Miami-Dade County, offers free assistance to businesses and residents with Permits, Certificates of Use (CU), Business Tax Receipts (BTR), and Code enforcement issues tied to current or prospective leases.
