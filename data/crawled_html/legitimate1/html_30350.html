Service & Rentals

Serving Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region has been our pleasure since 1973, and we have and wish to continue excelling in giving awesome service to every rider and cycle that walks — or rolls — through the door, no exceptions.
We will gladly pedal the extra mile for you.

We are stocking a selection of the latest Aventon & Velotric E-bikes in various styles and sizes. Check out the E-bike page to see specifics.

Explore the adventure-ready lineup of Salsa mountain and gravel bikes at our shop. Built for performance and exploration, Salsa bikes are your perfect trail and back road companions.

Finally, you'll find a selection of used bikes that have all been checked and updated by our professional service department. Looking to get into — or back into — riding but not ready to commit to a big purchase? Let us fit you on something used. 

Welcome to Colorado Springs Bicycle Shop. We are excited to have you as a client, fellow bike rider and friend. We'll pedal the extra mile for you.
