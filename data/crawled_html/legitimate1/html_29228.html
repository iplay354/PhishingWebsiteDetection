Bright Design now has the "QUICKEST" search facilities in MOTORSPORT Photography!! Allowing viewers to SEARCH the entire website in MINUTES for photographs from various events using your registration details. No need to spend hours searching galleries.

At Bright Design, we are extremely flexible to your needs and specifications. We understand your time is very valuable, therefore we have developed this website with the new concept of indexing each photograph from various events, to save your valuable time. Anyone can now search for photographs using a registration number or a boat name from the search area on the home page.

Most photographs from events have been identified with a code for quicker searching.
  • For BOATS search the vessels NAME (no name use registration number or race numbers),
  • For CARS search the vehicle REGO number (no plates search a sponsor name).
  • For RACE CARS search the vehicle RACE number (no plates search a sponsor name).
  • For all motorbike images please search "BIKE" plus any visible race/rego numbers
  • For all people, staff, officials, volunteers and crowd photographs please search
    CROWD / CREW / OFFICIAL
  • Photographs with no identification details available have been coded with "tbc".
  • Should you not find your photos please do not hesitate to contact us and we will send you a link.
