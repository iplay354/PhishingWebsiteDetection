Opening Hours

"At Active, our mission is to provide islanders with accessible, well-balanced sports and leisure facilities that inspire everyone to lead healthier, more active lives. As Government owned facilities, we deliver engaging fitness programmes for our members and work closely with partners like Jersey Sport to ensure our facilities meet the evolving needs of Jersey’s sporting community."

Flexible classes for flexible people

We offer a range of Group Fitness classes, from Pilates to HIIT, that cater for all abilities. With over 100 classes per week across our centres, there's something for everyone! 

Our professional instructors will provide essential support and guidance if you are just starting out on your fitness journey, and will push those of you with more experience so you get the best results.

Make a splash with Active

At Active we're passionate about swimming. Our teaching team love to teach people of all ages and offer lessons for Adults and Children from the age of 3 years. Some of our instructors are qualified Trainer Assessors and also teach people to become teachers themselves!
