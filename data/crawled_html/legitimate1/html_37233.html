Why not treat yourself to a few days in a hotel after your cruise ends ?

Time in motels or hotels will help you forget the pressures of work and everyday life and get into the cruise holiday spirit. Of course, you may prefer to spend a few extra days at the end of your cruise in hotels in the port of call where you disembark. Finish your holiday with time in a new city or at splendid seaside resorts. Or you may be considering a two-part holiday, a whole week in a different hotel following a week-long cruise. If you are staying longer than a day or two, think about half board or full board hotel deals.
