2026 USPMGA Memberships information

2026 Members of the United States ProMiniGolf Association

To represent USPMGA, a player must pay a yearly

 

Membership fee in January is $35.00.  The USPMGA will honor 20 points to that  player’s national ranking if their dues are paid by the last day in January.  The membership fee goes up to $50.00 starting February 1st. Payments made will have 10 points deducted for each month after January that the dues are not paid.

*Please make checks to USPMGA and send to mailing address on contact page*

Become a member:  Fill out the form online and then send it.  Your card will be mailed to you or you can request to have them hold it till you arrive in Myrtle Beach

Mail your check to:
