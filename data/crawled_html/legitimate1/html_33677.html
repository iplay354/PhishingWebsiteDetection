The Tidewater Story

Born as a flip flop-only brand in the South in 2002, Tidewater has since expanded to include more styles and reach many more people.

We're headquartered in Winston Salem, North Carolina, but these days, you can shop our collections here on our website or find us in boutiques and specialty shops across the country.
