Visit Green Bay, Wisconsin

This Has Always Been a Place To Discover

At Discover Green Bay, you’ll find our energy contagious! We revel in spreading joy and building memories—working together to deliver unforgettable experiences that keep you coming back. We also pursue opportunities that will inspire change and growth in our community and industry—to make every Green Bay discovery beyond legendary.

Green Bay

Take the scenic route and discover fall fun. From Green Bay Packers history, fall colors, trails and nature to museums, public art and indulging in local flavors, there is plenty to discover while visiting Green Bay.

Explore Green Bay

Wrightstown

Wrightstown embodies small-town charm withoutdoor adventures like kayaking on the Fox River or cycling to Ledgestone Vineyards. Visit local eateries, enjoy the riverside views, and catch a Waterboard Warriors ski show for a quintessential summer experience

Explore Wrightstown

Lawrence

As one of Wisconsin’s fastest-growing towns, Lawrence offers a mix of rural charm and urban development. Enjoy scenic drives along Lost Dauphin, visit local golf courses, or unwind in its expansive park system.

Explore Lawrence

Bellevue

A growing suburban gem, Bellevue offers a perfect mix of outdoor activities, shopping, and dining. Explore its extensive parks, discover unique local shops, or visit the dog park for some furry fun. Bellevue is a vibrant community welcoming all visitors.

Explore Bellevue

Hobart

Hobart’s serene parks and trails, like Duck Creek Trail, offer a tranquil retreat for nature lovers. Perfect for biking, hiking, or simply unwinding. Hobart’s welcoming atmosphere makes it a delightful stop on your journey.

Explore Hobart

Oneida

Oneida is a cultural haven, home to the Oneida Nation. Explore the Oneida Nation Museum, stay at the welcoming Oneida Casino Hotel, and enjoy local cuisine at Cedar and Sage Grill House. With its rich heritage and natural beauty, Oneida is a truly unique destination.

Explore Oneida

Howard

Howard offers a perfect blend of small-town charm and outdoor adventure. Explore scenic trails and relaxation in the parks or enjoy a day of fishing or boating on Duck Creek. The community's welcoming vibe is matched by its array of unique local eateries and shops, making Howard a delightful destination for visitors of all ages.

Explore Howard

Suamico

Suamico invites you to check out its natural beauty and tranquil waters. Paddle along the Suamico River, hike through picturesque landscapes, or visit the renowned NEW Zoo & Adventure Park. After a day of outdoor fun, indulge in Suamico's diverse dining options and discover the area's friendly, small-town charm.

Explore Suamico

De Pere

Historic and vibrant, De Pere boasts a bustling downtown and the stunning Fox River waterfront. Take a stroll along the riverwalk to Voyager Park or explore St. Norbert College’s events and cultural activities. De Pere seamlessly blends urban charm with natural beauty.

Explore De Pere

Allouez

Allouez is a treasure trove of charm, blending small-town warmth with city convenience. Stroll along the East River Trail, picnic in Green Isle Park, or immerse yourself in history at Heritage Hill State Historical Park. Each street unveils a new corner of beauty and local culture.

Explore Allouez

Algoma

Algoma’s lakeside charm is an ideal escape. Take a scenic drive to this peaceful town and discover its quaint downtown, cozy eateries, serene beaches, and unique shops. It's a hidden gem that feels tailor-mad for a relaxing getaway.

Explore Algoma

Scott

Scott offers a serene escape into Wisconsin’s natural splendor. Visit Wequiock Falls, dine at Royal Scot Supper Club, or find solace at the National Shrine of Our Lady of Champion. This small town is perfect for a peaceful retreat.

Explore Scott

Ashwaubenon

Entertainment thrives in Ashwaubenon, just steps away from Lambeau Field. From concerts at the Resch Center to the family-friendly Green Bay Rockers baseball games, there’s no shortage of excitement. The Titletown District offers fun and food for everyone, making it a must-visit destination.

Explore Ashwaubenon

Denmark

Denmark invites you to relax and explore its scenic beauty. The Devil’s River State Trail offers picturesque views, while Duck Creek Vineyard and Denmark Distilling provide delightful local flavors. Don’t miss a softball game at Circle Tap or a leisurely walk through this charming village

Explore Denmark

Upcoming Events

Local and national entertainment regularly takes the stage. Wineries grow their own grapes to produce wines right here. Artists find their inspiration here. We have botanical gardens and a zoo. Use the Green Bay calendar of events to plan something today or months from now!
