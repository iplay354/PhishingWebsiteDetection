    Wodonga Council

    Wodonga is a vibrant and growing city, nestled on the Murray River with a population of more than 40,000. 

    Located between Melbourne and Sydney on the Hume Highway, Wodonga is surrounded by rich agricultural valleys, adjacent wineries and historic towns.
