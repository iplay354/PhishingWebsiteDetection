ANNUAL LUNCHEON

New York City Ballet’s 2026 Annual Luncheon program will celebrate Principal Dancer, Megan Fairchild, as she prepares to retire from her 25-year career with the Company and embark on a new chapter. From her beginnings in Utah to her rise as one of ballet’s brightest stars, Megan has performed nearly every major role in the Company’s repertory, and inspired generations of dancers through mentorship and
advocacy. Always a highlight of New York City’s winter social season, the Annual Luncheon will begin at 11 AM with the dynamic onstage program, and will continue into the afternoon with an elegant seated luncheon on the Promenade of the David H. Koch Theater.
