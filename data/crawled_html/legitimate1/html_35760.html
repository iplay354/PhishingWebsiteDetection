Cindy Couling – Mixed Media Artist, Illustrator, Potter

Cindy Couling has worked as a professional commercial artist, illustrator and graphic designer since the early 90's. Past and present clients include: Klutz Press, Scholastic, Young and Rubicam, Metro Newspapers, among others. She expresses her whimsical, colorful style in a variety of media including pencil crayon, block prints, ceramics and pottery, watercolor, pen and ink and whatever medium strikes her fancy. Collage and artist trading cards are her newest passions. Cindy enjoys observing people and incorporating their unique expressions and personalities into her work.

She is the owner of LunaGraphica Inc. A graphic and web design firm located in Sunnyvale, California.
