Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about enrollment, programs, and what makes us different

What makes Mitsu Sato different from other beauty schools?
We are a Wella Signature School—an elite designation that sets us apart from schools like Paul Mitchell and Z Hair Academy. This means exclusive access to Wella Professional products, advanced training, and global career networks. Plus, we include free MUD Makeup and Bella Lash certifications with our programs (valued at thousands of dollars), and our exclusive 16-chapter social media marketing course that no other local school offers.
How much does tuition cost?
Tuition varies by program and payment plan selected. We offer multiple financing options and financial aid for those who qualify. Call us at (913) 341-7286 to speak with our admissions team about current tuition rates, payment plans, scholarships, and available financial assistance tailored to your specific situation.
What certifications are included with the programs?
All cosmetology and esthetics programs include MUD Makeup Artistry and Bella Lash Extension certifications at no additional cost—a significant value-add that saves you thousands of dollars. As a Wella Signature School, you'll also receive advanced Wella Professional training and certification, giving you a substantial competitive advantage when entering the job market.
How long are the programs?
Program lengths vary by course and schedule. Our Cosmetology program requires 1,500 hours of training (typically 12-15 months full-time), Esthetics programs range from 6-9 months, and Nail Technology from 3-6 months. We offer flexible scheduling with both full-time and part-time options to fit your lifestyle. Contact us for specific program duration details and schedule options.
Do you offer financial aid?
Yes. We offer comprehensive financial aid options for those who qualify, including federal student aid, scholarships, grants, and flexible payment plans. Our knowledgeable financial aid team will walk you through all available options and help you find the best solution for your financial situation. Call (913) 341-7286 to discuss your financial aid eligibility and options.
What is your job placement rate?
We maintain a 94% job placement rate. Our career services team works closely with top salons, spas, and beauty businesses throughout the Kansas City metro area and beyond. As a Wella Signature School, our graduates have direct connections to Wella partner salons worldwide. Most students secure employment within 6 months of graduation, with many receiving multiple job offers.
Can I work while attending school?
Absolutely. Many students maintain employment while attending Mitsu Sato Hair Academy. We offer flexible scheduling options including full-time and part-time programs, with day and evening classes available. Our admissions team will work with you to create a schedule that accommodates your work commitments and personal obligations.
