About Mehndi Madness

Our Passion for Henna

At Mehndi Madness Henna, we are dedicated to celebrating the art of henna. Our passion lies in creating stunning designs that enhance your beauty for any occasion. Our owner, Krysteen Lomonaco has been servicing the PNW since 1996. Krysteen initially took her training in London, England and has continued her education in design and cultural awareness. You can find the Mehndi Madness team at the Seattle Center annually Memorial Day through Labor day as well as traveling all over the PNW. Check out our events calendar!
