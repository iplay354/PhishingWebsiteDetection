Christine Kohut Interiors is a boutique design firm located on the New Hampshire Seacoast, about 45 miles north of Boston.

With an deliberate focus on clients’ distinct personalities and objectives, the goal is to bring comfort, luxury, and happiness in ways that reflect the unique people who live there. That attention to detail changes the way people feel about their homes in addition to how good it looks and how well it functions.

Christine Kohut Interiors makes people fall in love with their spaces again.

That is the art of making a house your home.
