Welcome to our SDH Platform

Welcome to www.solar-district-heating.eu, the website of the Horizon 2020 project ‘SDHp2m – solar district heating … from policy to market‘ and the Intelligent Energy precursor projects ‘SDHtake-off‘ and ‘SDHplus‘. These projects are supporting the market rollout of solar district heating in Europe.

This website provides you with updated news, informations and know-how on solar district heating as well as projects, products and services.

View the results and products of the SDHp2m project.

District heat suppliers, utilities and contractors

District heat suppliers, utilities and contractors

Get in direct contact with Europe’s most experienced operators of solar district heating systems, experts and professionals. Benefit from the project products, services and events you find on this website.

Local communities, housing and industry enterprises

Local communities, housing and industry enterprises

Learn from the best practice examples presented on this website and find experienced partners for developing a solar district heating project in your community.

Policy makers and support scheme managers

Policy makers and support scheme managers

Support the rollout of solar district heating by establishing a favorable energy policy and market framework. Learn from best practice demonstration and incentive programs presented on this website.

Component suppliers, service providers and professionals

Component suppliers, service providers and professionals

Upgrade your product assortment and services for solar district heating technology by making use of expert advice or training courses or simply offer your products and services on this web platform.
