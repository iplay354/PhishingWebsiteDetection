Handmade Artisanal Wreaths

Our wreaths are handmade to order by skilled artisans, using flowers and herbs grown on 20 acres of farmland in Central California.

Celebrating Over 35 Years

A family-owned and operated farm, Creekside Farms has been in business since 1988, with much of their success stemming from long time dedicated employees and three generations of family.

Top Quality Ingredients

The flowers, culinary herbs and greenery used in our wreaths are grown without the use of harmful pesticides and chemicals.
