Welcome

Welcome to Gatton Manor Hotel and Country Club, nestled in 200 acres of serene beauty that merges gracefully with the surrounding Surrey countryside.

Our ethos is simple: to provide an experience akin to staying with a close friend, where comfort and warmth are paramount.

Located in the lush Surrey Hills, Gatton Manor is more than just a retreat—it’s a haven for all. Whether you’re here for a round of golf, a leisurely stroll through our grounds, or to enjoy a refreshing beverage with a view of the green, you’ll find our welcome as inviting as the scenery.

Boasting a mature 18-hole championship golf course, Gatton Manor is often referred to as one of Surrey’s best-kept secrets. Crafted by the esteemed golf architect Commander John D Harris, our course is a unique blend of challenge and beauty. With creative use of natural elements like streams, lakes, and tree-lined fairways affecting play on many holes, each round promises a new experience.

As we embark on our journey of refurbishment, our commitment to excellence remains unwavering. During this initial phase, rest assured that the high standards you’ve come to expect from our course will not only be met but exceeded.
