About Team JMC

Team JMC is sporting club formed over 10 years ago by a group of mates looking for a club that puts enjoyment first. We are all about having fun, competing, supporting each other and pushing ourselves that little bit harder.

We are open to everyone, no matter what level of ability. Our mantra is talent is optional but enthusiasm essential. Great results are always pleasing but taking part, giving it a real go and finishing with a smile on our faces, proud of what we have achieved is way more important to us.
