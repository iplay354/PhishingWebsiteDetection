How long will the project take from design to installation?

For exhibition booths, you can expect fast delivery and short lead times. Most of our projects take four to six weeks, from briefing to delivery. For bespoke tradeshow stands, you can expect the project to take eight to twelve weeks, depending on the complexity of build construction.

Our standard operating procedures for exhibitions also translate into speed and efficiency in retail and office interior fit outs, which will give you faster and more deliverable expectations. We can deliver in record time, which may take six to eight months for other interior shop fit out companies to achieve.

What exhibition centres do you work with?

We’ve worked with clients exhibiting at many venues, in particular at ICC Sydney, Sydney Showground, Carriageworks, Sydney Masonic Centre, MCEC, Melbourne Showgrounds, GCEC, Brisbane Convention and Exhibition Centre and premium locales such as Hilton Sydney. No matter the size or scale of your exhibition project, we can help you.

What is your pricing structure?

Our initial meeting includes a free strategy plan and discussion about your vision for the project. Our designer will likely plan and sketch freehand to gauge what you are looking for. After this, if you want to engage more, we can proceed to quotation, stage 1 designs and final design, production quote and installation. Just tell us what you’re after, and we’ll work with you to get you there.

Who owns Colab Design Group?

The Director of the Colab Design Group, Dennis Abalos, is an industrial designer/design director who founded the business in 2012. A skilled designer and maker of furniture, homewares and lighting, he brings his passion for custom solutions for clients to the Colab team. He has been awarded many accolades for his work, including the winner of the global Red Dot Design Award, runner up in the Launch Pad awards, and finalist in numerous design awards such as IDEA Awards, Qantas SOYA Craft and Design Awards and the Bombay Sapphire Design Awards.
