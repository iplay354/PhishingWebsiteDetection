    About Naropa

    Located in Boulder, Colorado, Naropa University is a Buddhist-inspired, nonsectarian liberal arts university that is recognized as the birthplace of the mindfulness movement. Naropa offers undergraduate and graduate degree programs that emphasize professional and personal growth, intellectual development, and cultivating compassion.

    The Naropa Difference

    How is Naropa different from other universities? At Naropa, a liberal arts education balances rigorous academics with powerful interpersonal skills and self-awareness to educate the whole person. Naropa’s contemplative approach is inspired by Buddhist philosophy and the conviction that we can build a diverse, contemplative, enlightened society when we have transformed education to affirm the basic goodness of every person.

LET’S GO

Is Naropa University accredited?
Yes, Naropa University has been accredited by the Higher Learning Commission since 1986. Learn more about our accreditation

Where is Naropa located?
Naropa University is located on two campuses in Boulder, Colorado, a city which has won many accolades and awards for its outdoor recreation, vibrant arts community, and overall quality of life. Learn more about Boulder

Is it hard to get into Naropa University?
Naropa has a holistic admissions process that considers various aspects of an applicant’s background and potential.

What is Naropa University known for?
Naropa University is known for its unique contemplative education approach and its strong emphasis on mindfulness and social engagement. Viewed as the birthplace of the modern mindfulness movement, Naropa has an innovative approach to learning that goes beyond academics.

Informed by ancient Eastern educational philosophies, contemplative education at Naropa experiments with another way of knowing through its joining of rigorous liberal arts training and the disciplined training of the heart. Transcending the belief that knowledge arises in the thinking mind only, this educational philosophy invites students to embrace the immediacy of their interior lives as a means for fully integrating what they learn.

Does Naropa offer low-residency or online programs?
Online Degree Programs Naropa University offers four online Bachelor of Arts degree programs: BA in Art Therapy Online BA Environmental Studies Online BA Interdisciplinary Studies Online BA in Psychology Online Low-Residency Master's Degree Programs Offering you the flexibility of taking most of your classes online, low-residency programs combine online learning with in-person intensives, which immerse you in contemplative practice and coursework—and build community. You can maintain your work and personal life and live the experience of contemplative education. Choose from six master’s degree programs in the low-residency format: MA in Clinical Mental Health Counseling: Mindfulness-Based Transpersonal Counseling Concentration MA in Clinical Mental Health Counseling: Somatic Counseling Concentration (Beginning in Summer 2026) MA in Ecopsychology MA in Religious Studies: Yoga Studies Master of Divinity MFA in Creative Writing

Can you transfer credits to Naropa?
Naropa has approved the transfer of up to 90 credits for undergraduates starting in Fall 2024. Apply as a transfer student by submitting an official college transcript as part of your application.

How many students attend Naropa?
As of the fall of 2023, Naropa has 1,117 students, 749 of which are students enrolled in one of our graduate programs.

The Naropa Difference

How is Naropa different from other universities? At Naropa, a liberal arts education balances rigorous academics with powerful interpersonal skills and self-awareness to educate the whole person. Naropa’s contemplative approach is inspired by Buddhist philosophy and the conviction that we can build a diverse, contemplative, enlightened society when we have transformed education to affirm the basic goodness of every person.
