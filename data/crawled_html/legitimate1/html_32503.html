Our Product Range ...

Glove Club is more than a glove supplier. While disposable gloves remain our best-selling products, we also provide a wide selection of PPE, hygiene products, and medical consumables. Our glove range covers every major industry requirement, from healthcare and food service to industrial safety and specialist applications.

Nitrile Gloves

Our nitrile gloves are powder-free, latex-free, and resistant to punctures, chemicals, and oils. They are trusted in dentistry, laboratories, food processing, and automotive sectors where durability and barrier protection are essential.

Latex Gloves

Renowned for their excellent fit and elasticity, latex gloves offer superior tactile sensitivity, making them popular in healthcare, cleaning, and beauty industries. We supply both powder-free and lightly powdered options that comply with EN 455 medical standards.

Vinyl Gloves

An affordable option for lower-risk environments, vinyl gloves are widely used in food preparation, catering, and general cleaning tasks. Comfortable and durable, they are an excellent choice for short-term or frequent glove changes.

Safety Gloves

Our safety gloves include cut-resistant, heat-resistant, and impact-resistant varieties. They are engineered for construction, automotive, and manufacturing environments where workers need advanced protection without compromising dexterity.

Sterile Gloves

Our range of sterile gloves is individually packaged and manufactured under strict medical standards, making them suitable for surgical, clinical, and high-sterility environments where infection control is critical.

Rubber Gloves

Durable and reusable, rubber gloves provide excellent grip and protection against cleaning chemicals, detergents, and hot water. Commonly used in domestic cleaning, janitorial services, and catering environments, they are designed for comfort during longer use.

PVC Gloves

PVC gloves provide strong resistance to oils, acids, and chemicals, making them an excellent choice for industrial, laboratory, and cleaning applications. Durable and reusable, they are designed for environments where heavy-duty protection is required.

Gauntlet Gloves

With extended cuffs that protect beyond the wrist, gauntlet gloves are designed for chemical handling, agricultural work, and industrial cleaning. They provide additional splash and forearm protection compared to standard gloves.

Polythene Gloves

Lightweight and economical, polythene gloves are ideal for food handling, petrol stations, and quick-change environments. Designed for single use, they provide basic protection where hygiene matters more than durability.

Cotton Gloves

Soft and breathable, cotton gloves are ideal for handling delicate objects, reducing perspiration under disposable gloves, or providing light protection in dry environments. They are also widely used in cosmetic treatments and archival work.

Specialty & Industrial Gloves

We also provide cut-resistant gloves, chemical-resistant gloves, and heavy-duty work gloves for industrial use. These are engineered for safety in challenging environments where standard disposables are not enough.

Other Consumables

Beyond gloves, Glove Club supplies a full range of medical and hygiene consumables, including:

  • Face masks & respirators

  • Couch rolls & aprons

  • Dental consumables

  • Cleaning chemicals & disinfectants

This makes us a true one-stop shop for healthcare, hospitality, and industrial businesses across the UK.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between nitrile, latex, and vinyl gloves?

Nitrile gloves are highly resistant to punctures and chemicals, making them ideal for medical and industrial use. Latex gloves provide the best fit and tactile sensitivity but may cause allergic reactions. Vinyl gloves are a cost-effective solution for short-term, low-risk tasks such as food handling. At Glove Club, we supply all three options so you can choose the right glove for your industry needs.

Which gloves are food safe?

Both vinyl and nitrile gloves are approved for use in food preparation and catering. They comply with UK and EU food safety regulations, ensuring hygiene standards are met. Latex gloves can also be used in food service, but businesses often choose nitrile or vinyl due to allergy concerns.

What size gloves do I need?

Gloves should fit snugly without restricting movement. Sizes typically range from small to extra-large, and we provide a detailed size chart on our product pages. Choosing the correct size ensures comfort, prevents tearing, and maintains safety during use. If in doubt, contact our team for guidance.

Are nitrile gloves recyclable or eco-friendly?

Standard nitrile gloves are not biodegradable, but some specialist recycling programs exist. We are actively exploring eco-friendly alternatives, including biodegradable disposables, to reduce environmental impact. In the meantime, we recommend responsible disposal in line with local regulations.

How quickly will my order be delivered?

At Glove Club, we pride ourselves on fast dispatch. Orders are processed quickly to ensure you receive your products without unnecessary delays. While we cannot guarantee next-day delivery on every order, our logistics team works to ship items as promptly as possible, and most customers experience very short turnaround times. For urgent requirements, we recommend contacting our customer service team to confirm delivery estimates.

What certifications do your gloves meet?

All our medical-grade gloves meet EN 374, EN 455, CE, and UKCA standards. We work with manufacturers that hold ISO 9001 certifications to guarantee consistent quality and compliance with UK health and safety regulations.

Can I buy gloves in bulk for my business?

Absolutely. Glove Club offers bulk-buy discounts for businesses, hospitals, care homes, and catering companies. By ordering in larger volumes, you save money while ensuring you never run out of essential supplies. Trade accounts are also available for repeat buyers.

What industries do you supply?

We supply a wide range of industries, including healthcare, dentistry, care homes, catering, hospitality, beauty salons, tattoo studios, laboratories, and industrial sectors. Our products are versatile enough to serve both medical and non-medical environments.

How do I know which gloves are right for my industry?

The right glove depends on your working environment. For healthcare and laboratory use, nitrile gloves are preferred due to their chemical resistance. For food handling, vinyl gloves are often used. For salons and spas, latex or nitrile provides comfort and dexterity. Our team can guide you in choosing the correct glove.
