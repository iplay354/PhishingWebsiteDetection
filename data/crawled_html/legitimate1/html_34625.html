Basic info about your wedding planner and our weddings in Central Park

 

Hi, I’m Claire.  I provide one-on-one planning and guidance for weddings and elopements in New York's Central Park.  I’ve been doing this since 2012 and have planned hundreds of weddings since then, so have lots of experience.  I can answer all of your questions, and probably a few that you haven’t even thought of!

 

I specialize in weddings in beautiful Central Park.  We conduct big weddings, small and intimate weddings, elopements and vow renewals.  I can tell you what legal paperwork you need to get married in New York, suggest the loveliest spots in Central Park for your wedding ceremony and photos, answer all of your queries, write your ceremony, and secure the best photographers, videographers and celebrants in town for your wedding day.  

 

I work directly with couples to create a detailed plan of their wedding day.  I will guide you through the decision making process.  All you will need to do is tell me what you want for your special day and I make it happen.  This takes away any stress of planning a destination wedding or elopement.  You’ll have a plan with locations, maps and timings, giving you all the support you need to feel certain that your wedding day will go smoothly.  


We offer competitive prices for our weddings in Central Park.  We can provide a bespoke package or you can purchase one of our standard services, we are clear about our prices.  We can accommodate last-minute, same-sex, multicultural and interfaith wedding ceremonies or renewal of marriage vows. ﻿ We can provide ceremonies in English and some other languages.

 

We will deliver exactly what you want on your wedding day.  Each couple is unique, and we work with clients to give them the tailor-made wedding to fit their needs.  Please feel free to contact us by email and we will use our extensive contacts in New York to accommodate your wishes.

 

We are trustworthy and thorough in our planning and our people are talented, reliable and enthusiastic about love, weddings, and Central Park.  Check out our reviews on Google.  Click the links below to read our reviews on Wedding Wire.  We have received only five star reviews to date since 2012!
