We Are ARISF Through its members, ARISF represents, a million athletes and many billions of fans all over the World. ARISF is represented on IOC Commissions and provides valuable input reflecting the views of its Member International Federations, assuming a key role in the periodic review of the Olympic Sports Programme, assisting the recognised sports in their efforts to secure broad social recognition and in their will to feature in the future Olympic Games.
