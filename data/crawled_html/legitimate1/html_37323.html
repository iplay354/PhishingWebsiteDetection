Strengthening Mental Health Worldwide

This website contains tools, resources and information about international programmes and initiatives, comitted to strengthening mental health around the world.

The network is a collaboration of research colleagues in over 40 countries worldwide, committed to developing knowledge about mental illness related stigma and discrimination, both in terms of their origins and their eradication. It is led by King’s College London.
