  1. COMMUNITY HIGHLIGHTS

  1. Montgomery Honors Former Village President with Street Designation: Marilyn Michelini Way

    Village Board members and staff gathered alongside Marilyn Michelini, her family, and close friends to officially designate Marilyn Michelini Way, honoring Montgomery's first female Village President, who served from 2000 to 2012. Read on...

  2. Police Department Emphasizes Safe Driving This Holiday Season

    The holiday season is a time for joy, connection, and celebration. To help ensure those celebrations do not end in tragedy, the Montgomery Police Department is joining law enforcement agencies across Illinois in a significant traffic safety effort. Read on...

  3. Montgomery Honors Newly Retired Oswego Library Executive Director with “The Skilton Way” Street Sign

    At the December 8 Village Board Meeting, Montgomery honored Sarah Skilton for her remarkable career as Executive Director of the Oswego Public Library District, recognizing her dedication to literacy, community enrichment, and public service. Read on...

  4. Montgomery River Run Presents a $2,000 Donation to CASA of Kendall County

    At the Monday, November 24, Village of Montgomery board meeting, staff presented a $2,000 check to CASA of Kendall County, helping its vital mission to advocate for the best interests of children in the local court system due to neglect or abuse. Read on...

  5. The 2024 Property Tax Rebate Program Application Period is Now Open.

    The Village Board authorized a Property Tax Rebate Program for the 2024 tax year. As a result, Montgomery residential property owners who pay property taxes to the Village may be eligible for a rebate. The application is online and open through 12/31/25. Read on...

  6. Welcome Officer Chaidez, the Newest Sworn Member of the Montgomery Police Department

    Montgomery is excited to welcome Officer Jovani Chaidez as the newest sworn member of the Montgomery Police Department. As a Montgomery resident, Chaidez brings an understanding of our community and a commitment to serving the place he calls home. Read on...
