An evening at Keith House
Feb 3, 2026

Join us for a relaxed, one-of-a-kind First Tuesday at Keith House — a space where light is the star.

The main room is set up like a Quaker gathering space with pews on all sides, and the ceiling opens to the sky at sunrise or sunset, filling the room with incredible shifting colors.


In the center of the room, you’ll hear a live set of Latin American and Spanish pieces, a little Bach, and some charming 19th-century works by Dr. Isaac Bustos. As the music plays, the changing light above creates a simple but unforgettable atmosphere

.
It’s an easy, peaceful way to spend an evening — sky, sound, and good company. Join us!
