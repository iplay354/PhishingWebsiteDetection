STYLE STARTS FROM WITHIN

STYLING AND IMAGE CONSULTANCY

WE ARE HERE TO HELP

We believe that fashion embodies so much more than clothing.

Fashion is beyond the physicality of pretty clothes. It is a multi-faceted notion that traverses art, culture, identity, and politics. Participating to the greatest depth in the widest sociality, fashion tells stories, which is essentially Veronica Li’s objective. With our professional skills and unique eye for aesthetics, we utilize the practice of styling to enhance brand image, sell products, dress influential public icons, create interesting stories that inspire, and boost individual confidence.

Style is not defined by a group of trendsetters. It is whatever you deem it to be. Our tailor-made creative services emphasize on individualized experience. We deliver sophisticated, refined, and elegant looks with quality through understanding your personal stories and tastes. We value our clients' opinion as we strongly believe that mutual trust and communication are what lead to successful happy business.

About

Hi, I'm Veronica

Veronica is stylist and image consultant from Vancouver, currently based in Hong Kong. Her roots started from fine arts, multimedia to styling with over six years of experience in the fashion industry. Veronica has been the creative force behind numerous editorial and commercial productions like such for Lane Crawford, HK Tatler, #legend , LA MER, Calvin Klein and Calzedonia. She has styled celebrities including Grace Chan, Louisa Mak, Anjaylia Chan, and Fiona Sit.

Recently, Veronica has brought her wealth of fashion experience and passion to the world of journalism. She has written fashion columns as well as produced photographs for Luxe Style Sing Tao Daily, ELLE HK Star Column and VOGUE Hong Kong sharing her styling tips, beauty advice, lifestyle, and diverse inspirations to a wider audience in today’s dynamic, ever-changing world. As an influencer herself, Veronica has collaborated with a variety of A-list brands like Hermès, Dior, Chanel, Bvlgari, OMEGA, Forevemark, LaMer, Chanel Beauty, H&M, Charles & Keith, ba&sh, DFS & T Galleria,Rosewood Hotels to name a few.
