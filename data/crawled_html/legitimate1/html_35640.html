Kimberly Diede

fiction author, encourager

Kim writes family sagas and women’s fiction that you’ll find hard to put down. She truly believes we are never too old for second chances at love and life.

Kim enjoys spending her short summer months on the lakeshores of Minnesota and North Dakota. Nothing is better than time with family at their cabin. Her love of tradition and all things vintage comes through in her stories.

​In a world too often filled with sadness and tragedy, her mission is to offer stories of optimism and hope. If she can keep you reading past your bedtime, she has accomplished what she set out to do. As a life-long reader, she loves to get lost in the pages of a great book and strives to give you the same experience.
