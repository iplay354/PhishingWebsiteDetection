Chokma! The Chickasaw Nation website is where you will find the latest information regarding tribal programs and services, events, facilities, history, culture and more in one convenient location.
The Chickasaw Nation remains strong and we are dedicated to meeting our mission to enhance the overall quality of life of the Chickasaw people. A diverse economic portfolio generates funds to support a variety of quality programs and services in the areas of education, health care, youth, aging, housing and more which directly benefit Chickasaws and their communities.

Homeowner Preparedness Grant

This program offers up to $5,000 for natural disaster preparedness equipment and construction. A link to the application can be found at Chickasaw.net/StormPrep.

Chickasaw Nation Reuse Center

The Chickasaw Nation ReUse Center helps keep items in good condition out of the landfill by offering donated items to shoppers for free. For hours and donation guide visit, Chickasaw.net/ReUse.
