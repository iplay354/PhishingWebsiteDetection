Rapid review Q+A

Are you working at one of the 12 Trusts named in the Government’s rapid review of maternity services? The RCM is committed to being by your side as this national review progresses.

We understand that for some members this may be a challenging or unsettling time. If you have any questions or concerns, we are here to support you. We’ve published a Q&A to answer your key questions and explain how you can access further support.
