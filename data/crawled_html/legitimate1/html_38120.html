About Wildlife Control Supplies

Since 1998, WCS has been the industry leader focused on bringing quality products to Wildlife Professionals. Our extensive expertise can help you with any wildlife situation. Our superior customer service delivers products in a timely manner at competitive prices.


Our expansive inventory covers every facet of nuisance wildlife control supplies, from traps and baits to exclusion tools and odor solutions. Whether you're handling a single event or managing ongoing wildlife challenges, these solutions will meet your needs. With quick links to shop by animal or product type, finding the perfect wildlife control equipment has never been easier.


Partner with WCS to elevate your wildlife management strategies with cutting-edge tools and reliable support. Poor-quality animal control equipment often fails at critical moments, causing unnecessary frustration, additional costs, and potential safety risks. With WCS, you have access to proven tools to manage nuisance wildlife with confidence and ease. Browse our selection of new and popular animal control equipment to safely manage, capture, and deter unwanted wildlife.
