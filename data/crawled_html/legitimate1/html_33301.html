A Litte About Us

Come, play and indulge in your wildest fantasies.

At Executive Fantasy Hotels, we believe that a life without passion
is not worth living. This belief is why we pour our hearts into everything that we do. For more than three decades, we have committed ourselves to providing our valued customers with the highest levels of cleanliness, comfort, and sophistication. Of course, we understand that discretion is of utmost importance, which is why privacy is a priority at each of our ten locations. We pride ourselves on our meticulous attention to detail, and we put careful thought and consideration into the design of every room we create.

Our well-appointed accommodations boast a variety of seductive amenities and range from sleek executive suites to imaginative playrooms. As the first hotels in Miami to introduce our acclaimed themed rooms, EFH offers an escape from the every day, where couples can relax and indulge.

You can find us strategically positioned throughout Miami and Hialeah near Miami International Airport, Coral Gables, Brickell,
and Downtown, allowing for that quick escape you crave right in
the heart of the City.
