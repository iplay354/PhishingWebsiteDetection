Welcome to the website of the NXDN™ Forum

The NXDN™ Forum was established in 2008, and has grown into a multi-faceted membership that includes radio manufacturers, measurement test set equipment manufacturers, silicon and protocol stack suppliers. The members of the NXDN™ Forum are dedicated to meet the digital migration needs for the next generation of land mobile radio communications.
