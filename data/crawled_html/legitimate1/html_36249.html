International engagements

African Bar Associations Presidents Summit 2025

8–9 October 2025 | Daystar University, Nairobi, Kenya

Law Society of South Africa President, Nkosana Mvundlela, attended a two-day summit bringing together Bar Association Presidents from across Africa to explore the role of lawyers in advancing environmental justice, climate action, and public interest litigation. The event features legal practitioners, judges, civil society, and international organizations in dialogue on strengthening the environmental rule of law.
