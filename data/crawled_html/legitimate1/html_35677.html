What is SSAGO

The Student Scout and Guide Organisation (SSAGO) is a non-uniformed organisation that enables you to continue or begin your Scouting or Guiding journey whilst at College or University.

As a National Organisation we have clubs at over 38 colleges and universities across the United Kingdom. Where no club exists there is the chance to either start your own or join the organisation as an Independent Member.

SSAGO clubs generally meet at least once a week during term time with activities being broadly classified into three categories: adventure, social and community. SSAGO also provides a link to local Scouting and Guiding to enable members to continue or begin working within their section whilst at university.
