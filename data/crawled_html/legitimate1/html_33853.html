What is a Loyalist?

In general, the United Empire Loyalists were those who had been settled in the 13 colonies at the outbreak of the American Revolution, who remained loyal to the British crown and took up the Royal Standard, and who settled in what is now Canada at the end of the war.

First time logging in?

HELP for those logging in for the first time:

  • Click the link above, “Click here to set up your account.”
  • Enter your email address (must be same one as is stored in your membership record)
  • Click on “Get new password”
    • If you then see an “Error: The email address isn’t correct“ message, be sure you have entered your email address correctly. If so, then your email address has probably not been stored correctly in the membership system —  Please sent an email to loyalist.trails@uelac.org from your correct email address indicating your name, the branch you belong to, and that you received an email incorrect error when trying to log in. We will check into the problem and get back to you – have patience.
  • You will then receive an email message from WordPress with a link in it.
  • Click on the link.
  • A page will show in your browser. In the “New password” box, the system will have inserted a sample password. At this point the password has NOT been set; it is only an example.
  • You can replace that password by deleting it and entering the password you would like to use in that same box (either way, be sure to record the password).
  • Click on “Reset Password”  (Now a password is being set)
  • A smaller box will then appear; in the green box, click on “log in”
  • On this screen, enter your email address and the new password, and click “log in”
  • You should then see the UELAC Members page, which has a link to the “My Account” page
