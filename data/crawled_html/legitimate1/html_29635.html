Rethink Your Mat

Our evolutionary design allows you to practice anywhere and without a mat!

YogaPaws Gloves & Socks provide an inspiring and unique way to stabilize your foundation, and provide support and grip, when practising Yoga or doing Pilates. 

They can be used with or without a mat and for a range of activities including: Yoga, Pilates, Weight Training, CrossFit, Stand-Up Paddleboarding, and Cycling

Elite vs Skin Thin

There are 2 versions of YogaPaws depending on the feel you prefer and how you plan to use them.

  •  

    Elite (Padded)

    Ideal for the Maximalist who:

    • Needs a little extra support or loves inversion poses
    • Likes a little bit of cushioning while exercising
    • Practices literally anywhere and on any surface
    ORDER ELITE
  •  

    SkinThin (Non-Padded)

    Ideal for the Minimalist who:

    • Needs high grip on a slippy or sweaty mat
    • Practices when travelling but prefers not to take a mat
    • Likes to lightweight and barehand feeling

    ORDER SKINTHIN

Ultimate Grip, Total Comfort, Real Support - Forget the Mat

Born from a mother-daughter shared passion for yoga and travel, YogaPaws was founded in California in 2001. Our patented ergonomic design embodies our dedication to innovation, quality, and versatility. 

YogaPaws are more than just grip socks or wrist supports—they're your gateway to a freer, more adaptable practice.
