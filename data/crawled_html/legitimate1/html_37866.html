    About The

    Diocese of South-West America

    The Diocese of South-West America was established on April 1, 2009, by Order No. 145/2009, issued by His Holiness Baselios Mar Thoma Didymos I, then Catholicos of the East and Malankara Metropolitan, on the Apostolic Throne of St. Thomas. The headquarters of the Diocese, the Urshlem Indian Orthodox Christian Center, is located in Houston, Texas. The Diocese comprises the parishes throughout the Southern and Western regions of the United States of America. The Diocesan Metropolitan, the spiritual and administrative head of the Diocese, is canonically elected, formally installed, and officially recognized by the Order of the Catholicos.
