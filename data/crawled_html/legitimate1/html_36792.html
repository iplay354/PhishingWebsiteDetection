3D Internet - Metaverse

Using the best characteristics of the Internet, we created WalkTheWeb - open-source 3D Internet software to mirror those same great qualities in a 3D virtual world Metaverse that is owned by everyone:

  • Global Accessibility
  • Unlimited Growth
  • Plethora of Information
  • Unparalleled Communication

...while empowering people in education, business, collaboration, innovation, artistry, architecture, marketing, economic opportunities, and entertainment.

Founding Family

Walk The Web is about giving 3D Internet to the world as a free and open Metaverse. Designed to be owned by everyone who contributes to make it successful. We believe that the Metaverse is not meant to be owned, controlled, or harnessed by one company. As it grows throughout the world, it will be a complete thriving economy of visitors, businesses, educators, gamers, artists, architects, and coders. It is the entertainment, school, market, and playground for the future.

  • COO / Vice President - Karen Dishno
  • CIO / Founder - Dr. Aaron Dishno
