Delighted to assist you

Your Trusted Main Dealer for New & Used Cars and Vans – Serving Oxfordshire, Hertfordshire, Herefordshire, Lincolnshire & Bedfordshire.

Since 1919, Hartwell has been helping drivers across Oxfordshire, Hertfordshire, Herefordshire, Lincolnshire, and Bedfordshire find the perfect vehicle. As an official main dealer for Ford, BYD, Omoda, GWM, Jaecoo, and Chery, we offer a wide range of new and used cars and vans to suit every budget and lifestyle.

Our used vehicle stock includes low-mileage, certified pre-owned models, affordable first-time buyer cars, family SUVs, practical hatchbacks, and a strong selection of used Ford vans—all fully inspected for your peace of mind.

At Hartwell, we don’t just sell vehicles—we keep them running at their best. Our manufacturer-approved service centres are equipped with the latest diagnostics and staffed by factory-trained technicians, offering expert servicing, MOTs, and repairs for Ford, BYD, Omoda, GWM, Jaecoo, and Chery vehicles. We use only genuine parts and accessories and provide flexible service plans and regular seasonal offers.

Looking for a great deal? Take advantage of our latest finance offers, including 0% APR on selected models and generous part-exchange options to make upgrading easier than ever.

Visit your local Hartwell dealership or browse our stock online to discover your next car or van today.
