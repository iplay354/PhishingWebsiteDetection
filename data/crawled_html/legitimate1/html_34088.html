Together, We Inspire Excellence

Preparing young minds to thrive in today's global society. Cave Creek Unified School District offers well-rounded, rigorous academic programs at all of our five elementary schools, middle school and high school — each one with its own signature curriculum and point of pride.

Make your Tax Credit Donation Today!

Donate up to $400 today, and receive up to $400 dollars off your taxes on tax day! Uses for donations include: Before/After school sports or clubs, standardized testing fees, and many more!

Feed It Forward

Did you know? The "Feed it Forward" program allows families to add an additional amount to their meal account payment to help provide meals to children at your school or pay off negative balances!
