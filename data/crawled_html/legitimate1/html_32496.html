Siddha.com.my is a website that presents an authoritative summary of the Beliefs & Practices of the Saivite Hindu Religion in 180 languages of the world, thereby making it available to 99% of humanity in their own language and cultural ethos and make Hinduism a universal heritage. It belongs to all. In the process it is envisioned that these language-cultures are enriched with the ethos of the Hindu religion and culture, as well as make Hinduism truly global.

The website also provides a forum for the international community to read further on Hindu insights, as well as for discussion and exchanging ideas, sharing goodwill and goodness.

This site is open to all. In order to post in the forum, one has to register as a member.
