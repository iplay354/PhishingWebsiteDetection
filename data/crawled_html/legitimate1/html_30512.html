Winter Breaks in Mid Wales | Cosy Stays, Dark Skies & Days Out

Winter Breaks in Mid Wales | Cosy Stays, Dark Skies & Days Out

Mid Wales is a great place to slow down in winter. Quiet paths, open landscapes and welcoming towns make it easy to get outside, explore, and then warm up somewhere comfortable. Stay for a weekend or a longer break and enjoy Powys, Ceredigion and Meirionnydd at a calmer pace.

Places to stay for winter

Looking for a cosy base for your winter trip? Choose from cottages with log burners, small hotels, friendly B&Bs, coastal stays and rural escapes across Mid Wales. Whether you want a town break with good food on your doorstep or a quiet spot close to walking country, you’ll find plenty of options here.

Links to key collections:

  • Cottages & self-catering

Things to do in Mid Wales in winter

Winter is a good time to see the landscapes without the crowds. You can still pack a lot into a short break — with options for dry days, wet days, and everything between.

Ideas for your visit:

  • Walking and cycling on lower-level routes, forests and riverside trails, plus bigger hill days when the weather’s right.

  • Waterfalls, reservoirs and viewpoints that look their best after winter rain.

  • Dark skies in some of the clearest night-sky areas in Wales — ideal for stargazing on crisp evenings.

  • Castles, museums and galleries for easy indoor exploring.

  • Wildlife spotting from red kites inland to winter seabirds along Cardigan Bay.

  • Food and local produce in cafés, pubs and restaurants across our towns and villages.
