Life at Perimeter

New Here?

We are real people with real hurts, burdens, and questions. We believe the answer to all of these is Jesus, whose love brings us into right relationship with God, self, and neighbor. We call this kingdom flourishing, and it’s only possible through Jesus. Come discover the good news of Jesus with us, as we learn to follow Him and flourish in His kingdom together.

Union With Christ

Everything about the Christian life flows from our union with Jesus. To be “in Christ” is more than following His example — it is sharing in His very life. Romans 6 invites us into this profound truth: His death becomes our death, and His resurrection becomes our new life. Join us in exploring how this foundational reality reshapes who we are and how we live.
