What makes us different

We're different than a bank. We're a full-service, digital-first credit union. And as a credit union, we're member-owned and not-for-profit, which means fewer fees and more benefits for you. Our members have access to these perks every day, and after our quick application process, you could, too:

  • We make our members feel valued, cared for, supported, and empowered to take control of their finances and dreams. Our sophisticated online and mobile banking platforms put you in control, so you can manage your money whenever, wherever you want. Plus, you can get 24/7 assistance via chat, send us a secure message in digital banking, or contact us via phone. 

  • Preventing fraud is a team effort. Our fraud prevention and security teams work hard every day to ensure your personal information and money are safe. And we provide you with tools like free account alerts, free credit score service, and card lock/unlock ability to detect and stop fraud. Learn more here.

  • We’re different than a bank. PSECU members enjoy big bank perks like competitive rates on loan and deposit products without the big bank fees. Our profits go back to you – our members – in the form of debit card rewards, credit card cash rewards, no- and low-fee products and services, and more!

  • “Members achieve more” isn’t just a tagline for us. It’s a part of everything we do. We provide lifelong value to our members as their trusted financial partner by maintaining a focus on innovation like digital banking enhancements and improvements based on member feedback.

Become a Member

*Only current PSECU members with general membership or tentative trust accounts who are 18 years of age or older and in good standing as defined in the PSECU Bylaws are eligible to participate.  Joint owners are not eligible to participate. Referrers must visit refer.psecu.com and follow the on-screen instructions to refer friends, family members or colleagues (“Friends”). Referrers will be provided with a Personal Link that allows the Referrer to refer Friends for PSECU membership. Referrers and Friends who open accounts and/or loans by fraudulent, suspicious, or illegal means, including but not limited to providing PSECU with fraudulent or fabricated information, are not eligible to participate. Referrers are not permitted to share their Personal Link via YouTube or Reddit.  Any Friend that comes into the referral system through YouTube or Reddit will not be a valid, eligible referral under this Program. A referral for a Friend coming through YouTube or Reddit will be declined and the Referrer will not receive the referral bonus for that Friend referral. If PSECU discovers that a Referrer posted the Personal Link on YouTube or Reddit, that Referrer is ineligible to participate in this Program.  Referrers are limited to sharing their Personal Link up to 25 times within a 7-day period. There is an overall referral bonus cap of $500 paid out per member per year regardless of the referral program(s) or referral bonus offer(s) participated in by a PSECU member in a calendar year. Under this referral program, the referring member will receive $100 deposited into their Regular share up to the overall referral bonus cap of $500. A valid referral requires that a Friend must:

  • Meet PSECU’s membership requirements and be at least 18 years of age at the time the PSECU general membership application is completed;
  • Complete the PSECU application for a general membership account by accessing it through the referring member’s Personal Link and follow the on-screen instructions; and
  • Be approved for PSECU membership. 

This referral program is subject to change without notice and may end at any time. Click here for complete terms and conditions.
