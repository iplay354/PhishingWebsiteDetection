Knitting patterns to inspire your heart-felt creations

HeartStrings patterns for today's modern knitter take your knitting beyond the ordinary with clear, comprehensive instructions. I am Jackie E-S at HeartStrings FiberArts, here to serve you, and dedicated to advancing the art and enjoyment of lace knitting through innovative designs based on tradition in updated contemporary techniques.

Featuring patterns for lace knitting and beaded knits

HeartStringsFiberArts.com is the Showcase for Jackie E-S Design Collections of lace knitting patterns and beaded knits. Knitting patterns to inspire your heart-felt creations

Do you have a passion for creating exquisite wearables and accessories that will be admired and enjoyed now and for years to come? Welcome to HeartStrings FiberArts. HeartStrings lace knitting patterns are available worldwide from a committed network of retailers – your choice of either hard-copy format mailed to you promptly, or instant electronic delivery in downloadable pdf format.

Yarn-generic patterns for creative freedom using yarns YOU like

Feel confident knitting with HeartStrings' clear, comprehensive instructions. Each lace knitting pattern includes skill-building techniques because learning new things makes knitting even more fun!

As a long-time knitter and needlearts enthusiast, Jackie E-S established HeartStrings to bring quality instruction to knitters who want their items to be special, as well as practical and fun to knit.

HeartStrings is pleased to bring you clearly-written, complete patterns that are a joy to knit into your own heart-felt creations. Please stay a while, be inspired, and enjoy the extensive selection available for you to choose from featuring lace knitting patterns and more. I am here to serve you.

Thank you for interest. I am here to serve you and help keep your knitting a pleasurable experience. If you have questions or need assistance with purchasing or knitting my HeartStrings patterns, let me know. Sincerely, Jackie E-S
