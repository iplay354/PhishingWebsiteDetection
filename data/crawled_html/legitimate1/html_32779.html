Karam means
generosity in
Arabic.

At Karam, we empower displaced Syrians to become the next generation of bold, compassionate leaders, ready to shape a brighter future.

Built by Syrians,
for the
future of Syria.

Why We Empower?

OUR IMPACT

Creativity

Through dynamic education and hands-on mentorship, we’ve equipped over 3,000 young minds with the skills and confidence to lead, innovate, and build their futures.

Resilience

We stand alongside displaced families wherever they are, providing the resources and support they need to rebuild their lives with dignity and strength.

Future

From higher education to career pathways, our programs open doors for those forging a new chapter—one defined by opportunity, ambition, and hope.
