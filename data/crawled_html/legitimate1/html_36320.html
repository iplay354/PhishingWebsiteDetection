Let's Talk!

About the Venue

Let your story begin at Venue at the Grove. Experience your grand entrance through a breathtaking orchard of pecan trees and serene outdoor environment and escape to a wondrous retreat from the Arizona desert. We understand the importance of your wedding, so Venue at the Grove will be exclusively yours during your special occasion. We are easily accessible, located within minutes from Downtown Phoenix, and offer more than two acres of sophisticated ambiance that make an ideal setting for beautiful weddings and all of life’s celebrations.

Our Services

From your wedding to all of life’s events beyond your big day, let us handle the work so you can enjoy spending time and creating memories with your guests. Our brand promise is personable, stress-free planning and seamless execution.

Learn More

Why Choose Us

We believe in creating meaningful, lasting memories.  Your wedding is a life event and we want to handle the details so you can be free to enjoy creating memories with your beloved family and friends that you’ll cherish and enjoy reminiscing.  Our philosophy is simple, we want you to be as stress-free as a guest at your wedding!
