Promoting the historic environment, its protection, conservation and heritage-led regeneration for people and communities.

Ulster Architectural Heritage is the lead independent voice for built heritage in Ulster. A not for profit organisation, operating across the nine counties of Ulster, we have over 50 years expertise in promoting the value of built heritage, encouraging its protection, conservation and heritage-led regeneration.

PROMOTION – PROTECTION – CONSERVATION – REGENERATION

Ulster Architectural Heritage seeks to ensure that Ulster’s historic built environment is preserved and used for the benefit of present and future generations. Support Ulster Architectural Heritage, join the Society today!

Ulster Architectural Heritage works across a number of projects to promote the historic built environment, its protection, conservation and heritage-led regeneration. This includes Heritage Angel Awards, Heritage at Risk, European Heritage Open Days, and much more. Ulster Architectural Heritage works with and for people-our members, owners, communities- including trusts, local and central government, other heritage groups and organisations.
