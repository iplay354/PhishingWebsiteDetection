About the World Federation

Service Since 1976

Registered in the United Kingdom, The World Federation of Khoja Shia Ithna-Asheri Muslim Communities has existed for over 50 years. We are a faith-based organisation, consisting of about 150,000 community members worldwide, mainly in Canada, East Africa, India, the Middle East, Pakistan, the United Kingdom, and the United States of America.

The World Federation is made up of member organisations based in different regions: The Federation of Khoja Shia Ithna Asheri Jamaats of Africa, the Council of European Jamaats, Khoja Communities Australasia, India Federation, the Organization of North American Shia Ithna-Asheri Muslim Communities, and the Federation of Khoja Shia Ithna Asheri Jamaats of Pakistan. The role of The World Federation is to facilitate endeavours of its member organisations through strategic planning and providing necessary resources for effective implementation.

Words from our President

Leading with faith and purpose

Watch Video

Alhaj Safder Jaffer

President

The World Federation is your organisation and is there to deliver key priorities for the community at a global level. In my speech (video attached), I outlined the key pillars through which we intend to deliver our manifesto pledges. In summary, we have five major aspirations for the term 2024-2027: to develop a 2050 vision, to build homes for 30% of community living below $3,000 per annum, to strengthen community-run schools and develop life-long Islamic learning programmes, to launch a Khoja digital ID card and increase the use of AI, and to upgrade WF-Aid and International Outreach. We continue to pray to Allah (SWT) to guide us in our endeavours and bless our efforts. We need your support in achieving our goals as we continue to strive for our community.

Orphans Appeal

“When I lost my husband, I feared for my son’s future. Life was a daily struggle, but after Hussein joined the WF-AID supported orphanage home, everything changed. He found care, education, and hope and I found peace knowing his future is bright again.” Fatima, a widowed mother

ICAIR Conferences

“[I am] very happy with the trajectory of ICAIR and their efforts to bridge the gap between research and the community. It is always interesting to get an insight into what the Shia scholars are thinking about and the differing perspectives on a topic, thereby helping us — the lay public — become more informed.”

Gaza Appeal

"After losing my husband and home, I became both mother and father to my three children. When my baby Adeeb fell sick from malnutrition, I feared I’d lose him too. Thanks to [WF Aid] clinic’s care and kindness, the treatment brought him back to life." S Al-Khalidi

Tarbiyah Storybooks

"We were first recommended the Tarbiyah Series books by some friends. My child's favourite book is The Builder of the Ka'bah. He really enjoys learning about the history of the Ka'bah and how it was built by Nabi Ibrahim (a) and Nabi Isma'il (a). I really recommend these books to all parents. I’ve learned a lot myself. My child has learned a lot too." Laith, Manchester

Water Appeal

“Before the borewell [Ali Asghar Water Appeal (AAWA)], we spent hours fetching water, and my children missed school to help. Now, with water nearby, our lives have changed — my children study, I can work, and peace has returned to our home.” Mama Fatuma, a widow and mother of five

Medical Care

“When Sakina came to us, she was losing both her sight and hope. After the cataract surgery, she smiled and said she could finally see her children again — a powerful reminder that restoring sight also restores dignity and life.” Ophthalmologist

Tarbiyah Cirriculum

Isn't it wonderful to have a curriculum presented online and the kids go: "this is better than my school portal". At a time when kids feel a little lost as to where and how they fit - here is a curriculum that gives them confidence in their faith. Imam Hasan Centre Sydney Australia

Fill My Cup Appeal

“Our daughter, Husna, used to go to school hungry, and it broke our hearts. Now, with the daily nutritious drink, she’s energetic, focused, and happy — and we have peace knowing she’s growing strong through the Fill My Cup Program and has a chance to a brighter future.”

MCE Cirriculum

The MCE Curriculum is exceptional. It provides valuable insights into Islamic teachings, effectively engaging students in their spiritual and intellectual development. The curriculum is well-rounded, seamlessly connecting beliefs, morals, history, and fiqh through Qur'anic verses, with a commendable focus on practical application. Sheikh Muhammad Kamran
