Welcome to Mount Olympus Awards!

Mount Olympus Awards is a leading provider of high quality custom designed varsity letterman jackets, varsity letters, metal inserts.  Our products include in-stock letterman jackets, design your own varsity letters, wool blankets, custom chenille patches, embroidered patches of all kinds, varsity letter pins.  You can design and order custom letterman jacket and varsity jacket awards and patches with no minimums, completed and ready to wear out of the box!  If you are a school, team or other organization, you can visit our wholesale only site at MOA Team Supply and get factory-direct pricing (and in some cases, below wholesale pricing) on all of your varsity jacket, chenille letter, and embroidered patch needs.  Thousands of individuals, teams, colleges, schools, corporations, major film studios, and retail stores have entrusted us with their business.  They are comfortable doing so because they know we put our reputation behind every product that goes out the door. Our three-fold focus on quality, competitive price, and speed of delivery is unparalleled.  For more information on Mount Olympus Awards and its letterman jacket, varsity jacket, and award letter business, visit our About Us.

 

We Do Corporate Letterman Jackets, Letterman Jacket Patches, and Varsity Awards, Too!

Mount Olympus Awards regularly works with small business, Fortune 500 companies, colleges, universities, start-ups, religious groups, private schools, academies, sports teams, athletic leagues, booster clubs, and every other group imaginable, producing custom letterman jackets, personalized letterman jacket patches, complex embroidery products, and other affinity apparel, often bringing to life visions that were originally drafted on cocktail napkins or sketched on a loose sheet of paper.  If you can dream it, we can probably do it, deliver it faster, and at a better price than any of our competitors.  Request a quote for your custom designed patch!
