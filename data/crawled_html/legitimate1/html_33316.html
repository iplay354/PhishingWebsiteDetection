Saint Michael School is a Catholic community committed to academic and moral excellence because Christ is the center of our lives.

100% dedication to living the virtues together and forming disciples of Christ through the incorporation of the Disciple of Christ - Education in Virtue curriculum.

Average student to teacher ratio in grades K-8.

Designated Purple Star School committed to serving and supporting military families.

Established with the support of St. Michael Parish in 1954, St. Michael is a Catholic community committed to academic and moral excellence. St. Michael students are part of a dynamic school community in which they are valued for their unique spiritual, intellectual, physical and emotional needs in a safe learning environment. Students experience the supportive presence of priests, religious and lay faculty and staff who work together for the academic and moral development of every child.  At St. Michael School, students put their faith into action as we live the virtues together, guided by the Disciple in Christ - Education in Virtue curriculum brought to St. Michael by the Dominican Sisters of Mary, Mother of the Eucharist.
