A Legendary Stay Begins

San Antonio Historic Hotel

Experience history at the Menger Hotel, a historic partner of the Alamo and the oldest continuously operating hotel west of the Mississippi. Located near the River Walk and Alamo, it blends architectural grandeur with modern comforts. The newly unveiled Plaza de Valero, a vibrant pedestrian oasis, offers shaded seating, live entertainment, and lush landscapes, connecting you to San Antonio’s rich culture.

Rich History

The oldest continuously operating hotel west of the Mississippi, The Menger traces its history back to William Menger, who opened the Menger Brewery in 1855 followed by the hotel in 1859.

    Most Famous Restaurants

    Dining & Drinks

    Step into a world of timeless charm at the Colonial Room Restaurant and Menger Bar, where San Antonio’s culinary heritage comes alive.

    Learn More

    The Colonial Room

    Step into this Victorian-era dining room and be transported back in time with traditional Texas cuisine, 20th century jazz, and soft mood lighting.

    Menger Bar

    Step into history at The Menger Bar, an 1887 replica pub where Theodore Roosevelt once recruited his famous Rough Riders.

    Mengerita Tacos

    Enjoy delicious Tacos that "Eat Good"! offering a Tex-Mex upgrade to traditional taquerias, conveniently located beside The Menger Bar.
