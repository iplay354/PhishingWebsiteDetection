What the JCCCW has to Offer

 

Learn Japanese

Enroll in classes at the Seattle Japanese Language School, where we’ve been teaching for 120 years!

Adult Online & Youth In-Person classes available.

Events & Programs

We host and attend a number of cultural events and activities throughout the year that you don’t want to miss!

Tour Our Museum

Learn Japanese American history by touring the Northwest Nikkei Museum and listening to our Speaker Series Podcast.

Visit Us

A Seattle historical landmark, the Japanese Cultural & Community Center of Washington campus has played a significant role in our community. Take our Virtual Tour.

Get Involved

Help make the JCCCW the best it can be by becoming a member, volunteering or joining the team.

Shop for Treasures

Visit the Hosekibako Japanese Resale Shop or shop now at Hosekibako Online to find wonderful Japanese items at reasonable prices!

 

Join us at upcoming JCCCW and community events!
