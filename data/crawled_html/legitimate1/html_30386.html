Is a New Kitchen Out Of Your Budget?

You don't need to change the existing carcass or frame of your kitchen cabinets, since they tend to outlast the regular doors and drawer fronts. However, replacing the worn-out elements instantly provides a fresh modern look without requiring significant renovations or expenses.

We offer a wide range of replacement doors and drawer fronts available in standard sizes that fit all conventional frameworks including B&Q, Ikea, Wickes, Howdens, Homebase, Magnet, and Benchmarx ranges, and they can be purchased in a variety of heights and widths to suit your existing setup. Don't worry if you have non-standard kitchen units, as we also offer made to measure kitchen doors to fit your unique requirements.

With options starting from just £7.83, you can transform your space and achieve a stunning new look on a budget. Whether you're updating all of your kitchen cabinet doors for a completely new look or just replacing a few worn ones, you can quickly and easily achieve a fresh look for your cooking, dining, and entertaining space.

We also offer a comprehensive range of replacement kitchen worktops in compact, laminate, solid surface, and solid wood styles - perfect for completing your kitchen refresh. To help tie everything together, you’ll find coordinating splashbacks, upstands, and edging strips in matching colours and finishes. Whether you prefer classic or contemporary looks, our wide variety of styles ensures a cohesive design across your kitchen doors and worktops.

Kitchen Doors Colours and Styles to Match All Requirements

We understand that every individual has their own unique tastes. That's why we offer an extensive variety of colours that are sure to suit any preference. Whether you gravitate towards the classic elegance of white, the inviting warmth of wood, something more daring like bold black or anthracite shades, or eye-catching greens and blues that make a statement – rest assured that we have exactly what you need.

If you are unable to find the exact colour you require, we also offer a colour-matching service for paint codes from the major manufacturers. Simply select your preference from our hand-painted kitchen doors range, enter the paint code, and select your measurements.

Additionally, our collection features a diverse range of styles including panels, slabs, and arches with both modern and traditional finishes available. This ensures that you can effortlessly design your ideal space.
