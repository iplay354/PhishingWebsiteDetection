      Ingenuity at Work

      Committed to quality, innovation and excellence, Tension produces custom envelopes and printed products across countless industries.

      Your Experts in Envelope and Printed Products

      Tension has been providing solutions for our customers for generations. With sales and manufacturing locations across the country, Tension is here for your printing needs.

      About Tension

      Direct Mail

      Capture your recipient’s attention with a thoughtfully designed direct mailpiece. Tension’s striking direct mail offerings enhance your campaign and come in a wide range of options, including interactive features, specialty papers, finishes, inks, and unique designs.

      Printed Products

      Tension’s product offerings go beyond traditional envelopes, encompassing a wide range of print marketing materials, including fliers, folders, and many other components of integrated marketing campaigns. We deliver the products you need to achieve the results you want. 

      Transactional Envelopes

      Make a statement with your billing and other transactional mailpieces. Tension’s custom business envelopes can help you deliver your bills, invoices, statements or fundraising appeal that inform and engage your customers.
