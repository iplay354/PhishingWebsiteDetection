The Royal Treatment

M'Lord & M'Lady's distinctive home on James Street, Downtown Burlington, has come to symbolize the unique level of service that the staff has become known for. Our hair salon provides complete hair care for the entire family, including haircurs, styling, highlighting, perms, and complete hair makeovers including colour correction. Both M'Lord (men's) and M'Lady (women's) services are available. We are open Monday to Saturday. Book an appointment online or call us at 905-632-1443.
