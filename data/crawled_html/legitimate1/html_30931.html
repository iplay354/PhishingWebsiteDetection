Why solar cook? 

  • It does not require fuel such as gas or wood (save money!)
  • It doesn't use electricity either (save money again!)
  • It is smokeless
  • You cannot bring a normal stove camping
  • In an emergency such as a power outage, or a fuel crisis the sun is still available!
  • It is just as fast at cooking food!
