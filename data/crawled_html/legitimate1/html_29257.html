About Us

Alpha Chi Omega is about empowering you to live as your true self, to reach your potential and to be the real, strong woman you are meant to be.

#WHYALPHACHI

Women who find their place in Alpha Chi Omega discover opportunities to connect, lead, grow and serve among sisters who celebrate them for the best versions of who they are and who they want to become.

Learn more >

In Alpha Chi Omega, women form bonds of sisterhood that are strengthened by the values we share — wisdom, devotion and achievement. We share a commitment to be leaders in redefining what a women’s organization can be; to respond to the hopes, dreams, fears and realities we all face; and to inspire and transform one another.

Learn more >

Alpha Chi Omega continues to support you at every stage of your life. Whether it’s through networking, structured programming or the bonds of sisterhood, as we change and the world around us changes, Alpha Chi Omegas remain devoted to one another.
