About David Barry London

Established in 1985

We started up in London's famous East End clothing area as a women's outerwear manufacturer and grew to a fair size selling our own labels 'David Barry' ladies padded fashion coats and classic styles, and 'Kesta London' luxury wool blends globally and made 'own label' for other well known retailers. As designer and manufacturer, we are proud to carry the genuine Harris Tweed Orb and other accredited labels of distinction and continue to produce stunning women's capes, coats, jackets and gilets.
