OFFICE HOURS


​WPJC is open for office hours Tuesdays and Thursdays 12-4pm. 
​
Stop by to check out our Community Resources and visit the Peace Library Catalog for books or DVDs .
If those times don't work for you, contact us via email to set up an appointment or arrange a pick up time!

Parking
  • 1 and 2 hour pay-the-meter spots M-F until 6 p.m. Street parking is free after 6 p.m., as is the nearby Commercial Street Parking Garage. Downtown parking map: https://www.downtownbellingham.com/parking
  • Pay-by-phone (after 6 p.m.) parking in the lot across the street (license and credit card needed)
  • Please do not park in the Daylight lot adjacent to our building; this is for residents only.
