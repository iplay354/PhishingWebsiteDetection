Sovereign

JSPR presents the Sovereign collection – a majestic display of modern artistry. Its handcrafted, half-round petals form a sculptural crown of light, creating a sense of regal presence in any setting. Designed to be a true centerpiece, Sovereign transforms private homes and grand projects alike with its commanding, luminous beauty.

Komorebi, inspired by the Japanese word for sunlight filtering through leaves, brings the poetry of nature into any interior. Elegant and enchanting, Komorebi transforms public and private spaces with the magic of nature’s light.
