FAQS

Answers to commonly asked questions

  • AWS re:Invent is our biggest, boldest, most comprehensive event of the year, where the global cloud community unites to build the future. During a week of groundbreaking keynote announcements, hands-on technical sessions, and interactive learning opportunities, cloud pioneers and innovators build revolutionary solutions that change the world, from cloud migration to generative AI. Customized learning paths and formats are available to fit every attendee’s needs and skill level, from new developers to seasoned experts. With direct access to experienced AWS architects, leaders, and partners, attendees get the support they need to pursue their most ambitious goals and professional milestones. Plus fun, memorable activities like re:Play, the most legendary networking party in tech, foster community spirit and help forge invaluable, lifelong industry connections.

  • AWS re:Invent provides hands-on opportunities to explore and demo the very latest innovations, resulting in immediate value you can take back to your organization. You'll make connections that last a lifetime.

    1. Immersive learning opportunities across more than 1,000 technical sessions, covering a range of experience levels, formats, and topics, from an intro to AWS core services to 500-level sessions on emerging technologies like generative AI.
    2. Expert-driven insights on how to use cutting-edge AWS services to drive innovation.
    3. Direct access to trusted AWS Partners in the Expo who can help you scale your efforts, as well as one-on-one access to AWS experts.
    4. Direct access to AWS customers from virtually every industry. Past customer speakers have included leaders from companies like Anthropic, JPMorgan Chase, PagerDuty, Rocket Companies, Splunk, and more.
    5. Networking opportunities, social events, and fun community activities like re:Play that help you make lasting connections with cloud innovators from across the globe.

  • No matter your knowledge or experience level, there’s something for you. Whether you’re looking to accelerate your organization’s cloud journey,
    hear the latest innovations in AWS technology, or advance your career with cloud computing skills, you’ll leave re:Invent ready to dream bigger
    and achieve more.
