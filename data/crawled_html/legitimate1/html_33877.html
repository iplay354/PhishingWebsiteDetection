Welcome to
​creating a simpler life!
Welcome! Rich and Kathie here, and we are building an off grid homestead from scratch on 135 acres of forest in the Adirondacks!

This dream began in 2017 as a five year plan and currently we are in the process of building our unconventional off grid earth sheltered house!

The goal, of course, is to live a simpler and more sustainable life that is manageable into old age!
