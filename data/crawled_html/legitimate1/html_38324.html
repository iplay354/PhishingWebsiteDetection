    Matisse Acrylic Paint

    Matisse Derivan is the oldest acrylic paint manufacturer in Australia and one of the oldest in the world, producing the world’s finest-quality artist colours and mediums since 1964. Renowned for innovative non-toxic formulas and highly pigmented, uniquely Australian hues, our versatile range of art materials is endlessly customisable—perfect for all creative projects.

    Structure Formula

    Matisse Structure Formula is a rich heavy-bodied impasto paint that artists adore. Thick and highly pigmented, it is ideal for application with a brush or palette knife for striking textured effects. Combined with the range of Matisse Mediums, Matisse Structure showcases exceptional flexibility of application and finish.


    Learn more

    Flow Formula

    Matisse Flow Formula is a medium viscosity acrylic paint that glides from the brush. Matisse Flow Formula has all the attributes of the Structure Formula; however, it has a thinner viscosity to allow free-flowing brushstrokes or use through an airbrush (with minimal dilution). This allows the paint to be used for fine brushwork with or without water dilution. This paint gives a low-sheen finish and good levelling.


    Learn more

    Fluid Formula

    Matisse Fluids are high-flow acrylics with a uniquely smooth, liquid-like viscosity and intense pigment loading. These colours spread easily with a brush and are ideal for tinting. Water fast once dry, Matisse Fluids are an excellent choice for wet-on-dry layering, airbrushing and wet-on-wet watercolour techniques. They dry to a refined glossy sheen.

    Learn more

    Ink

    Matisse acrylic Inks feature a vibrant range of intermixable colours that include intense primaries, stunning metallics and mesmerising iridescent hues. These brilliant and heavily pigmented colours are highly versatile, designed to suit a variety of applications from calligraphy, technical drawing, airbrushing, watercolour techniques and more.

    Learn more

    Background Colour

    Build your art on a solid base with Matisse Background Colours – a versatile, highly pigmented acrylic gesso. Suitable for almost any surface, it provides excellent coverage and seals as it primes. Its matt, low-tooth finish resembles gouache, making it ideal for painting, mixed media, and as a pastel primer. 

    Learn more

    Matisse Studio

    Matisse Studio is a versatile, medium-bodied paint designed to meet the creative needs of both students and professional artists alike. This vibrant new range offers the best features of Matisse Structure and Flow acrylics, showcasing the same high-quality pigments in larger 120ml tubes at a more affordable price.

    Dry Mediums

    Matisse Dry Mediums offer an extensive range of unique minerals and man-made additives that blend seamlessly with Matisse and Derivan mediums. Many create remarkable textural effects, both coarse and smooth, while others add bulk without weight—ideal for artists working on large-scale canvases.

      Origin Colours  

    Matisse Origin Colours push the boundaries of acrylic paint innovation, combining rare and unique pigments with expert colour making. Expanding the range of available hues, these collections redefine creative possibilities for artists.
