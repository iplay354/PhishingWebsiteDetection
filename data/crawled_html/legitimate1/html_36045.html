Welcome to Avon Schools

Introduction

ALL belong, learn, and grow

Avon Community School Corporation is located just west of Indianapolis, Indiana. We offer children high-quality educational instruction with a keen focus on improving student achievement. We serve nearly 11,000 students from preschool through graduation in 13 schools: one early learning center,  eight elementary schools, three middle schools, and one high school.  Approximately 1,600 employees work diligently to advance the academic success of our diverse student population.

We're glad you are here.
