Welcome to the 8 Blue Birds Gift Shop

Celebrate the bond with a beloved pet, or miniaturize your favorite bird in nature. Each miniature is handmade from polymer clay, wire, and resin. They are weather resistant and reasonably durable, though I recommend you treat them with care. 

 I have been passionate about birds since I was very young, and I hope to share this love with you. 

All available items are organized into collections based on both the kind of item and the species of bird. 

Jewelry

Showcase your love of birds with a one-of-a-kind piece of wearable art.

Each pendant comes with an 18 inch satin or woven cord with a 2 inch extender chain. Some can be worn multiple ways, with a double-sided design that showcases all of the bird's plumage.

Figurines
