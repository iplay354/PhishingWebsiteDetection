Professional & Caring Staff Welcome You

Ahwatukee Animal Care Hospital’s experienced team of Phoenix veterinary doctors and skilled veterinary staff welcome you to our veterinary practice and boarding resort. Ahwatukee Animal Care Hospital and Pet Resort is a full-service small animal hospital providing comprehensive healthcare services, boarding, and daycare to pets in Ahwatukee, Phoenix, and surrounding East Valley areas for over 20 years. Ahwatukee Animal Care Hospital is a client-oriented small animal hospital dedicated to building connections between people and their pets and helping pets lead long healthy lives!

Our Mission

At Ahwatukee Animal Care Hospital we are committed to providing quality care at every stage of your pet’s life. Our services and facilities are designed to assist in routine preventative care for young, healthy pets and early detection, treatment, and surgery as your pet ages. 

Ahwatukee Animal Care Hospital is proud to be an accredited member of the American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA). Our accreditation demonstrates our commitment to the highest standards of veterinary care. AAHA regularly evaluates our hospital to ensure that we meet or exceed the association’s standards for patient care, hospital services, and equipment. AAHA standards are recognized around the world as the benchmark for quality care in veterinary medicine.
