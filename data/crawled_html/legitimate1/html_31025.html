About Us

Sekg is a tech startup based in Barcelona focused on experience analytics for users and companies. Our mission is to revolutionise how experiences are measured, analysed and improved in entertainment and ensure users are always kept at the center of the stage.


Our flagship product is Antidote , a game launcher where companies and gamers merge into a single community. Within the platform, developers and publishers can iteratively improve their games based on players feedback and insights and players can help build and enjoy the experiences of the future.

About Antidote

A complete user experience solution for PC and mobile games. Leverage all its tools, powered by AI, to manage your research remotely and get faster actionable insights about your players’ experience.

Game UX Research, made easy:

1 - Game
Setup Your Game

✓ Describe your game, including the name, genre and on what platform it supports.

✓ Choose the desired distribution channel (local installation, cloud streaming, access keys or others).

2 - Project
Launch Your Playtest

✓ Choose which research study you want for your sessions.

✓ Select the data collection options for the study (face, gameplay, think-aloud comments, controller or touch screen input, and more)

✓ Create and customize your own surveys.

✓ Include and define any specific instructions for your players.

✓ Provide NDAs or other confidentiality agreements to your participants .

3 - Players
Invite Players

✓ Find and recruit your ideal participants from Antidote's diverse player community from all over the world.

Or:

✓ Invite your existing player base with shareable links and assign them to your desired playtest sessions.

4 - Results
Analyze Results

✓ Access the results in real-time.

✓ Analyze playtest results within the platform, from answers to surveys to gameplay recordings and comments.

✓ Export data in CSV or Excel for further analysis or request a report from our UX team.
