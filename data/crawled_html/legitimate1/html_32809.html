About GALA

Future-proofing the globalization community through education,

 information, and cooperation.

The Globalization and Localization Association (GALA) is an international nonprofit trade association.

We serve an international community of organizations and individuals who work in the global language industry to facilitate multilingual communication and global business. GALA’s mission is to help our members succeed and to drive industry development by building professional communities, sharing knowledge, and advancing best practices.

Creating an account is the first step to engaging with GALA. 

If your organization is a member, you will be able to connect to its membership and enjoy full access to member benefits.

Once logged in, you can:

  • Access member benefits (if organization is a member)
  • Register for events
  • Update your profile
