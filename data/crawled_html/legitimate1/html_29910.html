Why choose BidItUp?

BidItUp is the nation's largest and most established industrial auctioneering service.

BIDITUP AUCTIONS WORLDWIDE is also the world's largest privately held asset management, disposition, turn over and repurposing firm. From one machine, to an entire plant, BidItUp is the go-to source for all used machinery, new machinery and industrial needs.

We bring together the expertise, innovation, and resources to craft and customize the perfect solution to achieve your goals.

Streamlined Solutions to Maximize Your Value

BidItUp: Your Global Leader in Industrial Auctions, Liquidation Services, and Used Industrial Equipment/ Machinery Appraisals:

At BidItUp, we turn industrial assets into capital with precision and efficiency. Whether it’s a single machine or an entire facility, our auction and liquidation services are tailored to meet your unique needs and deliver top-dollar results.

Industrial Auction Services

When it comes to industrial auctions, we lead the way with flexible formats designed for your convenience:

  • Online Auctions – Participate from anywhere in the world.
  • Onsite Sales – Traditional auctions with a professional edge.
  • Hybrid Webcast Events – The best of both online and in-person experiences.

No matter the approach, our goal is clear: maximize your returns while minimizing complexity.

Industrial Asset Liquidation Services

For specialized or niche assets, liquidation often provides the best value. We combine modern auction methods with proven liquidation strategies to deliver exceptional results. Whether you need to clear a space quickly or achieve maximum value for proprietary equipment, we’ll develop a tailored plan that fits your timeline and objectives.

Certified Equipment Appraisals

Accurate, reliable valuations are the cornerstone of successful auctions, liquidations, and financial planning. Our certified appraisers leverage real-time market data to provide expert assessments, including:

  • Fair Market Value Appraisals
  • Auction Value Assessments
  • Fair Market In-Place Value Appraisals

With certifications from the American Society of Appraisers (ASA) and the Association of Machinery & Equipment Appraisers (AMEA), we deliver appraisals that set the industry standard for accuracy and trustworthiness. From east to west, our appraisers cover all the numbers!

Beyond Auctions: Comprehensive Asset Management

BidItUp is more than just auctions. Under the Industrial Assets Corporation umbrella, we offer a full range of services to meet your asset management needs:

  • Orderly Liquidation – Structured solutions for smooth transitions.
  • Turnkey Plant Sales – Complete sales solutions for entire facilities.
  • Distressed Financing – Strategic support for businesses in challenging circumstances.
  • Plant Remediation and Continued Operations Management – Solutions to keep your operations on track during transitions.

Whether you’re planning for growth, managing a transition, or facing a challenge, we are your trusted partner in maximizing the value of your industrial assets.
