Welcome to George Press Jewelers

Welcome to George Press Jewelers! With over 45 years of experience, we are proud to be the leading destination for custom designs, bridal jewelry, designer collections and jewelry repair. At George Press, we strive to create a shopping experience like no other, one that will leave you with lasting memories. Our dedicated team is committed to exceeding our valued customers expectations at every opportunity. Whether you need expert advise, appraisals, repairs or replacements, our highly skilled master craftsmen are here to cater to your every need. Our Services

We offer a comprehensive range of expert services to meet all your jewelry needs. We provide extensive jewelry repairs, ensuring your treasured items are restored to their original beauty. Our appraisal services offer accurate and detailed valuations for insurance or resale purposes. Additionally, we specialize in engraving, pearl restringing, and thorough cleaning and inspection to keep your jewelry in pristine condition. We also offer gold buying services, providing a fair value for your items. Visit us to experience exceptional craftsmanship and personalized service tailored to your needs.
