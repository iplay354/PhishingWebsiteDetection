THOMAS HELWYS BAPTIST CHURCH

Welcome to our website! We are a friendly, down-to-earth, inclusive church in Lenton, Nottingham, UK.

We are a whole mixture of people of different ages, cultures and backgrounds. Our worship is relevant and fairly informal, and we seek to love God and serve the local community.

Our Sunday service is at 10:45am. To find out more about us, click one of the links on the left or better still, pay us a visit! Directions and contact details are available via the Contact Us page.
