ACM Design is a full service boutique architectural design and interior design firm based in Asheville, North Carolina. From garden to hearth, our award-winning team of architects and interior designers guides you in the creation of your ultimate retreat — your mountain dream home. Whether you are building a new custom home or renovating your current residence, we offer a full suite of services that can be tailored to suit your needs. Our team is passionate about uncovering the details you never knew you always wanted and incorporating them into a design that is as beautiful as it is functional. Led by Principal Amy Conner-Murphy, our firm serves the mountain communities throughout Asheville and Western North Carolina, as well as Upstate South Carolina, and the Mountain areas of East Tennessee. Our connection with these communities and familiarity with the terrain makes us uniquely qualified to design the mountain home of your dreams.

Architecture

Our Architectural Design Services focus on high-end custom residential projects including new construction and home remodels and renovations, and renovations of historic homes.  Whether your style is European romantic, mountain modern, rustic, industrial, traditional, or craftsman, our mountain home architects will help realize your vision.

Interior Design

Our philosophy is simple — your home is a reflection of your best self. With that in mind, our interior design service focuses on creating stylish, thoughtfully planned interiors that will beautify and enhance everyday living for you and your family.

Outdoor Living Design

Our outdoor living designs are an extension of your home that will expand your living space, elevate your entertaining and enrich your relaxation. Whether you are looking for experience-based spaces — like outdoor cooking and entertainment stations, yoga studios, meditation spaces, or reading nooks — or more traditional outdoor areas — like sport courts, swimming pools and spas, or firepits — we will work with you to create your own personal oasis.

Our Portfolio

We invite you to explore our portfolio of custom home architecture, whole house renovations, interior design, and outdoor living spaces including new luxury mountain homes, mountain lake houses, and mountain estate homes. As a full service architectural firm, we are well-versed in the design standards for aging in place and accessibility and all of our projects incorporate sustainable design and building practices.

Our Approach

At ACM Design, we take a holistic approach to architecture. From how the siding on your exterior relates to its surroundings to the design of the floor plan and how it functions for the family, we create homes that respond to their environment and feel a part of the terrain. Whether you are selecting stone, timbers, or tile patterns, we engage you in the process and encourage you to consider how each of those elements will function and play into the overall conceptualization of your home. Our clients are central to our process. We focus on understanding the nuances of your daily routine and how you’re going to move through the house, as well as how you want to feel when you are there. Every question we ask contributes to creating a sense of place and a design that says, this is safe, this is my respite, this is home.

Throughout the process, we are your advocates. From the overall concept to the smallest detail, we consider the various components of the design, as well as the implications of each decision, so that you can move forward with confidence and excitement about your choices. Each of our projects embodies the work and efforts of many different design and construction professionals — all of whom are excellent at what they do — and our role is to ensure schedules stay on track, work is performed to specification, and the unique character of your custom home shines through.

ABOUT ACM DESIGN
ACM Design Architecture & Interiors, based in Asheville, NC, specializes in custom residential architecture in Western North Carolina, Upstate South Carolina, and the mountains of East Tennessee. By integrating the disciplines of architecture, interior design, and outdoor living, we focus on creating a complete vision of home that reflects the individual personalities and priorities of our clients.
We design unique, beautiful, and functional home spaces with a direct connection to the outside--homes balanced with quality, longevity, and environmental sensitivity.
