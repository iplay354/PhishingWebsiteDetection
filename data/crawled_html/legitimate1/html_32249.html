Capella University is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission.
Accreditation and recognitions provide the assurance we meet standards for quality of faculty, curriculum, learner services, and fiscal stability.

*The baccalaureate degree program in nursing, master’s degree program in nursing, Doctor of Nursing Practice program and post-graduate APRN certificate program at Capella University is accredited by the Commission on Collegiate Nursing Education (http://www.ccneaccreditation.org).

Capella University’s Bachelor of Social Work and Master of Social Work programs are accredited by the Council on Social Work Education’s Board on Accreditation. For more information about social work accreditation, visit: https://www.cswe.org/accreditation.
