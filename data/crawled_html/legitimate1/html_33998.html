WELCOME TO MEDILINK-GLOBAL

Medilink-Global is principally the regional Medical Claim & Benefit Management Solution provider and Third-Party Administrator (TPA) with more than 20 years of proven track record and extensive experience in Malaysia, Singapore, China, Bangladesh & Indonesia.

 
BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Medilink-Global was established in year 2000 in Malaysia, offering Healthcare Benefits Management Services (also known as Third-Party Administration) to the insurance companies and other healthcare payors in the region. The services provided have proven track records in several Asian countries including Malaysia, Singapore and China, servicing several major insurance companies, thousands of corporates and millions of members through its established network of more than 3,500 healthcare providers worldwide.

Its in-house developed, comprehensive powerful, robust, reliable, scalable and bespoke Claims & Benefit Management Systems (also known as Electronic Claim Clearing System <ECCS> 4.0) enables it to provide medical claims processing services towards better efficiency, smarter decision-making and stronger risk control manner. The system has been licensed and installed for several major insurance companies and significant corporate organization in Asia region (Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, China and Vietnam).

TECHNOLOGIES AND SOLUTIONS
✔️ Electronic Claims Clearing System (ECCS 4.0), a complete suite of system designed for healthcare claims processing and data management; consists of back-end application modules, payor portal, provider portal, member portal as well as mobile apps for end-users

✔️ Medibridge/ Healthcare-Insurance Industry Exchange, a complete suite of system that promotes digitalization of workflow and data flow between healthcare providers (Hospitals) and healthcare payors

✔️ Test N Track : TestNTrack is an AI-enabled technology that enhances Rapid Test Kits (RTK) application in medical diagnosis especially in rapid mass testing.

COVID-19 RAPID TEST KIT (RTK) SCREENING SERVICES
This service is available only in Malaysia. It helps organizations to protect lives and jobs with rapid mass testing using our TestNTrack application. The RTK test kit used is registered with MDA. The AI-enabled digitization solution helps to provide faster and more secure results. The whole screening process is paperless, all results are captured and made available online for frontliners, labs, employers and members.
