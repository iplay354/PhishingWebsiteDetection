What Is Evolven?

While it’s well known that configuration changes are the root cause of most stability issues, IT still struggles to understand the risk associated with a configuration change; and due to various reasons, IT still struggles to know what actually changed within an environment - until now…

Evolven lets you finally know – at all times - the state of your end-to-end environment - from applications to infrastructure, from on-premises data centers to the public cloud.

Evolven’s Configuration Risk Intelligence means no more guessing and no more fragmented insights from having to use multiple products or experts to understand what is going on. Providing a unified view of the end-to-end configuration state of your environment, Evolven uses AI-based analytics to detect and prioritize risks triggered by actual, granular changes in configuration, application, infrastructure, and data. to prevent and rapidly resolve stability, compliance, and security issues.

With Evolven, you will experience fewer incidents, improved productivity, and faster MTTR if an issue does occur.
