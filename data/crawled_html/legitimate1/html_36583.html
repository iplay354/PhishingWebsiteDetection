2019 "Curtain Call" Awards Wrap-up

In 2019 the ATG was pleased to be able to return to a full Gala Dinner format "Curtain Call" Awards Night, which was celebrated by around 270 guests from across SA's theatre community at the Carrington Function Centre.

The night was hosted by the fabulous, and wonderfully entertaining, Catherine Campbell, and highlighted some great South Australian theatre talent.

Congratulations to all award winners!
