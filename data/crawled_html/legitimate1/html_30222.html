ASTRONOMY FOR A BETTER WORLD!

Our mission is to further the use of astronomy, including its practitioners, skills and infrastructures, as a tool for development 

credits: Amirreza Kamkar/IAU OAE

The Office of Astronomy for Development is a joint project of the International Astronomical Union (IAU) and the South African National Research Foundation (NRF) with the support of the Department of Science, Technology, and Innovation (DSTI).

Changing lives through astronomy

We work with community groups, non-profits, astronomers and other researchers, education and outreach specialists, and other experts around the world. Whether it is changing student attitudes to STEM through the exciting lens of astronomy or creating opportunities for rural entrepreneurs through astro-tourism, we believe astronomy can have a positive impact due to its deep cultural ties, its hold over popular imagination, and its natural affinity to multi-disciplinary work.

Get started

What we do

The IAU OAD is a global coordinating centre of Science for Development. We support scientists, young people, and passionate changemakers from around the world to engage in astronomy to promote sustainable development. Within our ecosystem, we 

  • Develop openly accessible resources
  • Offer free online courses, lowering barriers to become a changemaker
  • Nurture global communities of practice
  • Provide grants and technical support for astronomy-for-development interventions around the globe

Outcomes

Our astronomy-for-development interventions have resulted in positive outcomes in:

  • Employment and Livelihoods: revenue generation and job creation in remote communities
  • Skills development: improved skills in coding, science, data science and machine learning 
  • STEM education: increased positive attitudes towards STEM education
  • Gender equity: reduced dropouts amongst girls in rural schools
