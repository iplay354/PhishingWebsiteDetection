CTP (Standardized Test) Highlights

59% of middle school students taking the CTP scored at or above the 95th percentile on one or more subtests as compared to public and private schools across the nation. 34% of our middle school students scored at or above the 95th percentile on three or more. Pictured are those students who scored at or above 95th percentile on five or more subtests. Congratulations to these students, their families and their teachers!
