Color

**final price may vary based on stylists' recommendations to achieve desired result**

  • root color $77 & up

    This service is for when you want to color just your roots (as long as they are less than 2 inches long). This is a color maintenance service.

    **This service does not include blow dry.
  • single color roots to end $110 & up

    This service is for when you want an all-over-change or an update to your current hair color. The color is applied from your roots through the length of your hair, sometimes altering the end-color based on hair health, hair history, as well as desired color.

    **Does not include blow dry.
  • color gloss $60 & up

    This service is normally used as a 'finishing' to most blonding services. It's also used to refresh your all-over color, add shine or cut out unwanted warmth. Basically, everyone could use a color gloss!

    **Does not include blow dry.
  • color melt $61 & up

    This is a modern way of blending your roots which results in your color/highlights growing in more seamlessly. We use this as an add-on to many blonding services, as well as, tonal changes. Occasionally, we’ll use 2 or more colors to create a seamless blend.

    **Does not include blow dry.
  • root shadow $55 & up

    This service is a dark root touch-up that allows a lived-in look with a seamless blend. We use this as an add-on to many blonding services, as well as, tonal changes.

    **Does not include blow dry.
  • face frame highlight $77 & up

    This service is for when you are looking for lightness/brightness around your face or when you are maintaining between larger highlighting services.
  • partial highlight $145 & up

    A perfect service if you are looking for some extra brightness/lightness or to maintain your highlights in between your full highlight services. Focus areas are, on the top of the head and around the face.
  • partial plus $170 & up

    This service is a little more than the partial highlight but not quite a full highlight. For when you want a partial with a few pieces underneath for your ponytail or to create extra lightness around the bottom of your hair.
  • full highlight $193 & up

    This service is for when you want a whole head of highlights. Whether it's an all over blonde look or more dimensional, the foils are applied underneath the back all the way to the front. Normally this service is only needed 2-3 times a year with a partial highlight for maintenance.
  • balayage $231 & up

    Pronounced: (baa-lee-aazh) Is a customized highlight service in which we hand-paint sections throughout your hair creating a soft, lived-in natural look.
  • double process $154 & up

    For the already existing platinum blonde client. This service is bleaching and toning your roots up to ¾ inch and includes a color gloss as a finishing to add shine and cut out any unwanted warmth. We recommend booking a platinum card for clients with longer roots or going blond for the first time.
  • all over bleach $110+ per hour

    If you are looking to go for a solid, all-over- blonde look, this is the service for you. Normally this service is for those with no pre-existing color in their hair.

    *Consultation required*

About


Hair is our medium

Alchemy is about so much more than hair. It's about perspective, aesthetic, obsessive attention to detail, and hopefully a bit of humor.

20 years ago, Alchemy was born as a creative hub for hairdressers who thought of their work as art.

Hair is our medium.

There are so many factors when referring to style, music, textiles, verbiage, time periods, foods, shoes—it all matters.

The beauty of this work is it is ever-changing. In turn, we are constantly evolving. We come together every 2 weeks to develop new techniques, refine the ones we have, and /or share our newest ideas.

We also really want people to like what we do. It’s our job to make people feel good.

Our name is synonymous with personalized, thoughtfully designed hair.

We enjoy what we do. And what we do is make people look good.

We have our own version of service. It is really important for us to be honest. We truly want you to like what we give you. We want you to be inspired by the magazine you're reading, we want you to enjoy the drink you’re drinking, and we want you to love your hair. We provide a service, and we want to do it really well. We want people to leave the salon having learned something about their hair and feeling confident in the way they look.
