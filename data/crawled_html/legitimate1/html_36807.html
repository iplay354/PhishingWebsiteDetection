Into The Light

Greetings in the name of the Almighty and Merciful God. Salam alaikum.

This website is both for Christians and Muslims to know how they view the Taurat (Torah), Zabur (Psalms), Sahaif (books of the prophets), Injil (Gospel) and the Qur'an (Koran). In part, it is a response to misunderstandings of Muslims, who have never received complete teaching about Isa Al-Masih (Jesus the Messiah, the Christ) from the viewpoint of his followers and their Scriptures. Our desire is to help both Muslims and Christians to know why God, who has revealed Himself, is so interested in all of us and why do Christians and their Scriptures give such importance to Jesus in this whole matter. Most of the articles, comments in response to queries and discussion points on this website are written by Dr. Steven Masood. You may see his info here.

Honorific Titles Please note that although honorific titles are not displayed along side the names of Jesus, the prophets and apostles, they are held in the highest respect. For more on the use of honorific titles see our discussion topic: Peace Be Upon Him
