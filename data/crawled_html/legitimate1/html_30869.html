Transit App / MyRide App

Transit is BFT’s official mobile app for trip planning and real-time bus information. With Transit, you can easily access up-to-the-minute arrival and departure times for nearby bus routes.

MyRide makes it easy to use the BFT bus system, allowing you to plan a complete bus trip by entering your starting and ending locations and preferred departure or arrival times. You can see where any bus is along its route in real time using GPS technology.
