WHO WE ARE

Fighting For Families Throughout Alabama

In a fraction of a second, an unexpected injury can change everything for you and your family. The pressures of life in Alabama become amplified when you are forced to miss work, pay hefty medical bills, fight insurance battles, and bear the pain of surgery and/or rehabilitation. Your family may be struggling under the burden, and worst of all, you don’t know whom to turn to for help.

That’s Where We Come In.

Martinson & Beason, P.C. has been protecting the rights of accident and injury victims throughout Alabama since 1937. Our personal injury lawyers have the experience, skill, and resources to take on all types of personal injury and accident cases. More importantly, we care about your well-being, understand the challenges you and your family face, and truly want to fight for you, our neighbor. Contact us to find out how we can help.

Our Alabama accident attorneys handle a variety of cases, including personal injury, car accidents, truck accidents, product liability, nursing home abuse, and wrongful death cases. Our skilled attorneys also practice probate and estate administration, business and corporate law, and real estate law, in addition to other areas of law. Whether you are in Huntsville or Florence, Birmingham or Tuscaloosa, Anniston, or Mobile, we can handle your case. Call our firm today.
