About Us

Big Duck Canvas is the leading source for factory-direct wholesale fabrics, proudly serving makers, designers, retailers, and manufacturers across the United States. As the largest online wholesale canvas and fabric distributor in the country, we’ve earned our reputation by delivering exceptional quality, reliable service, and one of the most diverse selections of bulk fabric available anywhere online.
Our wholesale fabric store specializes in a wide range of cotton canvas, cotton duck cloth, numbered duck fabrics, waxed canvas, vinyl, natural muslin, linen, and outdoor performance textiles—all offered by the yard or in full bulk rolls. Whether you're creating custom products, reupholstering furniture, designing outdoor gear, or sourcing textiles for commercial manufacturing, Big Duck Canvas provides the materials and expertise you need to succeed.

Who We Serve

We proudly support DIY makers, upholstery professionals, soft goods brands, interior designers, home décor specialists, and industrial fabricators. With a commitment to competitive bulk pricing, volume discounts, and a robust wholesale fabric program, we’re built to meet the needs of B2B customers at every scale.
Our team works closely with small shops and large-scale manufacturers alike—offering custom dye lots, specialty fabric sourcing, cutting services, and personalized customer support that sets us apart from other online fabric suppliers.

What We Offer

  • Wholesale Cotton Duck Canvas in a wide range of weights—including 7 oz, 10 oz, 12 oz, and heavyweight numbered ducks (#4, #8, #10)
  • Tenting and Utility Canvas for yurts, wall tents, teepees, glamping, and rugged outdoor use
  • Luxury Linen Fabric lines ideal for home décor, curtains, tableware, and upscale interiors
  • Natural Muslin Fabric perfect for pattern-making, lining, draping, and backing
  • The largest selection of dyed 10 oz canvas, a top choice for cornhole bags, tote bags, slipcovers, upholstery, crafting, and sewing projects
  • Outdoor & Marine Fabrics like 600 Denier polyester, RHINOTUFF Army Duck, and solution-dyed acrylics including AquaDuck and SeaDuck
  • Wholesale Vinyl Fabric in marine-grade, upholstery-grade, and fire-retardant PVC vinyl options
  • A curated selection of Made in the USA fabrics, proudly supporting domestic mills and American manufacturing

Why Choose Big Duck Canvas?

We’re more than just a wholesale fabric company—we’re a partner in your creative and commercial success. Our team is made up of fabric enthusiasts who understand the needs of our customers and take pride in delivering dependable, high-quality materials. Whether you're prototyping a product, producing large runs, or sourcing textiles for resale, Big Duck Canvas is your trusted source for premium wholesale textiles and factory-direct fabrics.
Explore our ever-growing selection of wholesale cotton canvas, outdoor fabrics, upholstery textiles, and marine materials—and experience the difference of working with a supplier who truly understands fabric
