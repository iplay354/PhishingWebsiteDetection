About Our Organization

Christopher Jennison
President

The Bethesda-Chevy Chase Rescue Squad is a philanthropically operated organization that provides state of the art emergency medical, fire, and rescue services to the Bethesda-Chevy Chase area and surrounding Maryland communities.

Our Services

Our dedicated members provide emergency medical, fire and rescue services to communities in Montgomery County, Maryland.  As an integrated part of the local fire-rescue system, BCCRS emergency medical technicians, firefighter/EMTs and paramedics respond to help members of our community during emergencies of all types, such as heart problems, strokes, vehicle crashes, and fires.
