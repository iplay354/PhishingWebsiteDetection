K-tainer is your innovative partner for everything related to containers, locally and globally!

We are the sustainable pioneer for the future of tomorrow.

K-tainer was founded in Rotterdam in the year 2000, the gateway to the European market of over 500 million consumers. K-tainer is a privately operated company that has maintained its integrity over the years.

About K-Tainer

Quality

With two certified IICL inspectors on staff, K-tainer guarantees quality. Our containers are inspected and photographed for clear customer visibility before sale.

Transparent

Due to our size, we can quickly answer all your questions with direct communication. We uphold our commitments and clearly communicate what is possible and what is not. It's all about trust.

Service-oriented

We strive to ensure that all deliveries or customer requests meet expectations. Furthermore, we are happy to provide advice and are open to receiving advice, always willing to collaborate with customers.

Innovative

As the only container trader with a Lean & Green star, we have demonstrated how our One-Way Transport services can improve your ecological footprint. With us, you can also buy and personalize containers online.

Specific Request?

Do you want to have a container customized according to your specific needs? K-tainer specializes in modifying all types and sizes of shipping containers. Container modification has become increasingly popular over the years. Curious about the possibilities?

One-Way Transport

In addition to container rental, K-tainer also offers containers for One-Way Transport. These are containers that would normally be transported empty but are now offered for free use if they are going to a specific destination.
