KenSu Jewelry

KenSu Jewelry is inspired by the human need both to adorn and to communicate through jewelry. Equally important is the power jewelry has to express the human values of strength, love, and faith.

KenSu Jewelry emphasizes the wear-ability of its designs, even when working with materials that verge on excessive. Known for its larger more bold hand hammered cuffs and semi precious stone necklaces, rings and earrings. Opting for Elegant power pieces over dainty. KenSu Jewelry pursues an aesthetic of simple elegant beauty.

The Designers vision is transmitted through the natural beauty　
of the materials used. Integrating precious and semi precious Lapis Lazuli, Amethyst, Turquoise, Aquamarine, Topaz (to name a few) plus many other unusual cut gems. All original designs are created by carving in wax or fabricating in precious metal. Cast in silver, the pieces seek strength in both simplicity and weight. All precious and semi-precious stones are individually set by hand.
