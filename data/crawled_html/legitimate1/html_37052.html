Connecting, empowering and championing the voluntary, community, faith and social enterprise sector to shape a brighter future for the North West

Find out how we can help you

At Voluntary Sector North West (VSNW), we are proud to be the driving force behind collaboration, advocacy, and innovation for charities, community groups, and social enterprises across the North West.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to empower the voluntary, community, and social impact sector to shape a better future for the region. Through strong partnerships, we bring people and organisations together, amplify their voices, and ensure they have the tools and support to thrive.

As the first regional infrastructure organisation of its kind in England, VSNW plays a unique role. We connect with over 50,000 organisations across the North West—providing a platform for collaboration, campaigning for investment, and tackling inequalities to create stronger, healthier communities.

Become a member - it’s free!

VSNW exists for its members. We prioritise our members when we consult the sector to gain a better understanding of its needs, and determine how we can best support its activities.

Become a member

Benefits of becoming a VSNW member:

  • Access to networking and collaboration opportunities.
  • Influence in regional and national decision-making processes.
  • Training, webinars, and resources to strengthen VCFSE operations.
  • Our relationships with VCFSE sector leadership groups and local government bodies provides access to important networks and decision-makers at regional level.

Working in partnership across the North West

  • Co-designing solutions with local voluntary organisations rather than imposing a single model.
  • Acting as a network of networks, bringing together grassroots groups, equalities organisations, and larger voluntary sector providers.
  • Supporting regional partnerships, sometimes leading initiatives, sometimes providing behind-the-scenes coordination.
  • Advocating for investment and policy influence, ensuring voluntary organisations are recognised in key decisions.
  • Facilitating collaboration between the voluntary sector and public agencies.
  • Piloting new delivery models in areas like health and social care, economic development, and fair funding.

VSNW Funding and Commissioning Awards

The VSNW Funding & Commissioning Awards recognise and celebrate commissioners and grant funders who go above and beyond to support the VCFSE sector in the North West.

These awards spotlight fairness, transparency, innovation and strong collaboration – helping us to build a shared understanding of what good looks like in practice.
