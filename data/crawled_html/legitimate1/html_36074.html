St. Clement of Ohrid
Macedonian Orthodox Cathedral

The Macedonian Orthodox Cathedral “St. Clement of Ohrid” has been the epicentre of the Macedonian Orthodox community in Canada for over 60 years.

On October 31, 1962, a Charter and church congregation were formed, effectively commencing the construction of what would become the Canadian Cathedral Church of the American-Canadian Diocese of the Macedonian Orthodox Church – the spiritual matriarch to tens of thousands of Orthodox Canadian-Macedonians. After months of fundraising, in April 1964, the construction of St. Clement began. Within one year, the cathedral was completed. April 18, 1965 was a day that Orthodox Macedonians in Canada will remember forever – the Consecration of the Macedonian Orthodox Cathedral “St. Clement of Ohrid”, in Toronto.

Today, St. Clement not only tends to the spiritual needs of Toronto’s Macedonian community, but it is also home to several prominent educational, cultural and charitable organizations that actively preserve and promote the Macedonian ethnic and cultural awareness.

What is this?

This online donation platform is proudly supported by

The donation page was proposed by Glen Day of Heritage Funeral Centre to help St. Clement secure a new, easy and reliable source of online revenue. Although this is not intended to replace attending worship in person, we want you to know that your online donations will have a far greater impact on St. Clement than traditional “collection tray” donations.

For each dollar donated throughout the year, you the donor will receive a tax credit from St. Clement, and Heritage Funeral Centre will match your donation up to the first $5000 in new revenue generated through this online platform.

Thank you for your support of St. Clement and may God bless you.
