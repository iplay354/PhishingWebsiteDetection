Free Move-In Truck


Our FREE Move-in Truck Program covers the daily rental fee (a value of about $80), and up to 50 miles of travel for one driver. You must be at least 21 years of age with a valid driver’s license and proof of auto insurance. An additional $10 fee must be paid for each additional driver.

Climate Controlled Storage

Heated in the winter and cooled in the summer, climate controlled storage units are ideal for furniture, electronics, and several other household item which won’t maintain its quality in extreme hot or cold environments. Not everything belongings in a climate controlled unit. Certain items you’d store in a garage or shed can be stored in standard non-climate controlled units.
