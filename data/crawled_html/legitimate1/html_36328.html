  • BattleWords

    BattleWords is a turn-based multiplayer scrabble-like word game for Android, iOS and Windows 10 Mobile. Queue for random opponents, invite your friends to play or play offline by yourself. Earn 23 achievements, 7 badges, track stats and unlock avatars.

  • Trivia Vs Friends

    Play your favorite trivia categories in various modes including online versus your friends or random opponents, offline by yourself or in a hotseat party mode (same phone, many players). Choose among the many trivia categories, sit back and enjoy!

  • Crystal Weather Icon Pack

    Our Crystal Weather Icon Pack is designed with high precision. It will look great on dark and semi-dark backgrounds. Included are day, night and neautral icons as PNGs (128, 256 & 512px) as well as the Adobe Illustrator work files for each icon.

  • Monochrome Weather Icon Pack

    Our Monochrome Weather Icon Pack is designed with a single color and high precision. It will look great on dark and light backgrounds. Included are day, night and neautral icons as PNGs (64, 128, 256 & 512px) as well as the Adobe Illustrator work file.

  • Simplistic Weather Icon Pack

    Our Simplistic Weather Icon Pack is designed with few colors and high precision and works great on dark and semi-dark backgrounds. Included are day, night and neautral icons as PNGs (64, 128, 256 & 512px), SVGs as well as the Adobe Illustrator work file.

  • Modern Weather Icon Pack

    Our Modern Weather Icon Pack was created for the weather service in one of our apps. These icons are designed to work well on both dark and light backgrounds. Included are icons for day, night (both half moon & full moon) as well as neautral weather.

  • Golf i Sverige

    Golf i Sverige is an app (Android, iOS) and a website for finding golf clubs in Sweden. Sort golf clubs by proximity to you. Each course has a slope calculator, images, map, directions and much more. The app is only available in Swedish.

  • Metrum Conversion

    Tired of not knowing how fast you're allowed to drive when driving in foreign countries? Do you think Celsius is confusing and prefer to know the temperature in Fahrenheit? Metrum is a brand new unit converter capable of converting most of every unit you are likely to come across.
