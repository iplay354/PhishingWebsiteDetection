Legacy – Reawakening is my biggest adventure yet. A game filled with more puzzles than ever before in the series.

It is a first-person point and click puzzle game set deep underground. You play as an archaeologist exploring a massive cave system filled with ancient ruins and strange machines.

At the center of it all is a sleeping robot guardian who has lost its memory. To wake it up, you need to explore the caves, solve mechanical puzzles, and find missing memory pieces. As you fix the robot, you will uncover the truth about the people who built this place and what lies behind the locked vault doors.

If you like “escape room” games or classics like Myst, you will feel right at home. There is no fighting—just you, the puzzles, and the mystery.

Need a Hand?: Includes a hint system to help you if you ever get stuck.

Explore a Beautiful World: Walk through ancient caves and discover mysterious machines.

Solve Tricky Puzzles: Fix broken technology and solve logic challenges to move forward.

Uncover the Story: Find shards of memory to help the robot remember the past.

Relaxing Gameplay: Play at your own pace with no fighting or enemies.

Language: Available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Swedish, Korean and Japanese

Available on Play Store, App Store, Steam and now in VR for Meta Quest Store!
