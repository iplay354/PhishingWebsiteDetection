About Sinfonia Viva

Sinfonia Viva is the Orchestra of the East Midlands, with a vision for every community to be inspired by music.

You can experience our work in many ways including orchestral concerts, creative workshops, family events and relaxed, dementia-friendly performances. We are committed to making classical music accessible to all.
