Who We Are

Seaside Furniture & Gelco Furniture Stores in New Jersey

Welcome to Seaside Furniture Stores in New Jersey! Seaside Furniture & Gelco Furniture has been family owned and operated for over 55 years, and has been proudly serving Ocean & Monmouth counties for over 130 years! Our furniture stores in New Jersey are located in Toms River and Brick, NJ, offering a great selection of furniture for every room of your home. Our furniture stores carry everything you need whether you are shopping for a entertainment center or a traditional writing desk.

We are happy to announce that our furniture stores are under new ownership and excited to continue the Seaside tradition to our customers at the Jersey Shore with a relaxed shopping environment that you have come to enjoy. The new showroom has new looks, new styles, new colors, and new options in all areas of the store from bedroom, dining, entertainment to living room furniture. All furniture across the store is available in an abundant variety of finishes, fabrics, and styles to your personal liking so you can get the style and look that you want! The NEW Seaside Furniture & Gelco Furniture Store is proud to offer exceptional quality furniture at reasonably low prices to our customers, with most of our furniture made right here in the USA. Come in see why the NEW Seaside Furniture & Gelco Furniture Stores in NJ are "STILL the comfortable place to shop!"
