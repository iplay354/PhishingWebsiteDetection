DEPARTMENTS

The Naval Postgraduate School offers our warfighters graduate programs, masters and PhDs, both on and off campus and Distance Learning, through our academic departments.

PROGRAMS & DEGREES

NPS offers defense-focused graduate degree programs in a wide variety of disciplines to eligible U.S. and allied military, DoD civilians, and a limited number of defense contractors.

CENTERS & INSTITUTES

NPS research centers, institutes and Academic Groups deliver defense solutions and develop interdisciplinary expertise across the institution's applied research and education programs.

NPS ONLINE

NPS Online provides warfighters defense-focused education through tailored and accessible graduate-level distance learning and on-location opportunities.

THE NPS DIFFERENCE

Only NPS synchronizes student operational experience and graduate education with applied research and faculty expertise to deliver innovative warfighting solutions and leaders educated to understand and employ them. More.
