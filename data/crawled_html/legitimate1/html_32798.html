Welcome to UUFNW

We are a religious community without dogma or creed, where questions are welcomed and answers are diverse.
We believe it is more important to be together in relationship than to agree – even on fundamental theological questions.
We ground our faith in love, justice, freedom and responsibility. We invite all to worship, learn and celebrate with us, regardless of the many things that divide us in our world.
Whether you are a long time member or making your first visit, we’d love to see you here.
