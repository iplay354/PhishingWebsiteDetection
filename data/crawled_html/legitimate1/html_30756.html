We are the American Institute for Conservation

We are the leading membership association for current and aspiring conservators and allied professionals. Along with our foundation, we support and empower professionals, institutions, and the public in preserving and protecting our irreplaceable cultural heritage.

Professional Membership 

Our Professional Members go through a rigorous application process and are assessed by their peers. Use our Find a Professional tool to search for an professional or see if someone is on our list.
