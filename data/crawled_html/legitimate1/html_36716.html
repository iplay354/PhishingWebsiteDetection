Welcome to the Chicago Cycling Club! We’re a friendly, diverse group including a wide range of ages, ethnicities and professions who find common ground in a love of biking. We organize events and rides throughout the year ranging from weekly fitness and training rides, Chicago history and architecture tours, fine beer tours, relaxed socializing rides, and tours to interesting food destinations (one of our mottoes is “we ride to eat”).

Come ride with us and attend our meetings. Membership is not required but joining is easy (Join Us).

PLEASE pre-register for rides so the ride leader knows how many people to expect and can recruit supplemental ride leaders if needed.


Note: PayPal transactions do NOT process instantly. They are usually processed within 24 hours.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.
