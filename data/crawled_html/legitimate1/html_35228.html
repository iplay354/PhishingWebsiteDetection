Offshore Injury Lawyers

The Nation’s Leader in
Maritime, Jones Act &
Offshore Injury Cases

 

Working offshore is not for the faint of heart. Neither is taking on maritime employers who insist on putting profits before the safety of their crews. At Arnold & Itkin, we never back down from a fight. Not when it means we can help an offshore worker or a family that has seen everything turned upside down by a serious maritime accident.

Maritime cases are complex, and defendants will throw everything they've got against injured workers' claims. Taking on oil and gas giants requires significant resources and experience. Our trial lawyers bring that and something else: a passion for helping others.

People come to our firm during the most difficult times of their lives. Often, they can't see a chance for a brighter future. Our goal is always to make sure they are given an opportunity to put the pieces back together, because our clients are our family.

Known nationwide as leaders in maritime law, Arnold & Itkin has handled the biggest offshore injury cases in history. Our attorneys have set and broken numerous records for the country's highest results, with more than $20 billion won for our clients. That figure represents lives changed, at-fault parties held responsible, and futures rebuilt.

Time and again, we have gone up against the largest companies and held them accountable for their wrongdoing. From representing over one-third of the Deepwater Horizon crew to helping widows of the lost El Faro crew members, we have helped seamen and families after every major maritime disaster over the past two decades.

And we will keep helping those who need it most. Call today at (888) 346-5024 to learn more.
