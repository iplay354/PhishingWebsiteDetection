FILM FESTIVAL WINNING FILM TRAILERS (2019)

 Global Trailer Film Festival - Winning Film Trailer - Tales From Tin Pan Alley

Tales From Tin Pan Alley

Beverly Hills, CA -- Emerging Artist Award for Best Trailer of the film festival goes to Henry Scott-Irvine for his documentary/music, film trailer Tin Pan Alley.

Synopsis: Britain's music street 'Tin Pan Alley' has resided in London's Denmark Street since Francis, Day & Hunter the first music publishers moved-in back in 1906.
 

 Global Trailer Film Festival - 2nd Place Film Trailer Winner - Frames on Women

Frames On Women

Beverly Hills, CA -- Emerging Artist Award for 2nd Place Film Trailer winner Andre Spencer for his documentary 'Frames on Women.

Synopsis: Frames On Women is firstly about the complex problem of the representation of women in film; and, secondly, about giving fair visibility to women through Portuguese cinema. Considering the vast majority of contemporary cinematic depictions is still linked to the male gaze, it is imperative to counteract it.
 

 Global Trailer Film Festival - 3rd place Winning Film Trailer - The Son of South Florida

The Son of South Florida

Beverly Hills, CA -- Emerging Artist Award for 3rd Place Film Trailer Winner Christian Hansen for his documentary 'The Son of South Florida'.

Synopsis: A documentary focusing on the life of the late Jose Delfin Fernandez. Jose's harrowing journey from Cuba and his instant success in MLB is a story that needs to be told. A documentary focuses on Joses social and cultural impact in the Cuban and Latino population in South Florida.
 

 Global Trailer Film Festival - 4th Place Winning Film Trailer - Nemausus

Nemausus

Beverly Hills, CA -- Emerging Artist Award for 4th Place Film Trailer winner Quentin Uriel for his New Media film trailer 'Nemausus'.

Synopsis: Ben just turned 30. He's a shy man, and is in love with his coworker Diane. He's got two main passions in life : Historical reconstitutions with his figurines club, and his job at the Tourism Office in Nîmes, which consists in taking visitors on board across the city.
 

 Global Trailer Film Festival - 5th Place Winning Film Trailer - We The Women of Afghanistan: a silent revolution

We The Women of Afghanistan: a silent revolution by Nahid Shahalimi

Beverly Hills, CA -- Emerging Artist Award for the 5th Place Film Trailer Winner Nahid Shahalimi for her feature documentary Trailer 'We The Women of Afghanistan: a silent revolution'.
