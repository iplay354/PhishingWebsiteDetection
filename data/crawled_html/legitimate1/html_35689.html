      PERGOLAS MELBOURNE

      Maximise your outdoor space, regardless of the weather.

      You do want a perfect balance between protection from the harshness of our high-summer sun and the opportunity to enjoy your outdoor space throughout the year as joyously as possible. Whether relaxing yourself or entertaining friends and family, it’s wonderful to have enough cover, yet still to enjoy the breeze as it offers a touch of relief.

      The freedom of choice you crave can be provided by the addition of a pergola – a low maintenance solution which can be topped with angled shade blades to deliver the level of cover you need. Our Melbourne’s Best Verandas experts will work with you to assess which of our stunning range of design and colour possibilities will add the style you seek and the quality you expect. Our clients do appreciate our sales pressure free approach as we take time to ask, understand and only then explain.

      PATIOS MELBOURNE

      Create a stylish outdoor area with endless entertaining possibilities. 

      You might simply want to relax yourself, enjoy al fresco dining experiences, provide your children and their friends with a great play area, or have a deck for relaxing get-togethers. With a world of possibilities for making maximum use of your patio area, you will have many careful choices to make. These will include the size and shape of the patio area, or whether a flat, gable or curved roof will best suit your deck, veranda, or pergola. You might want to consider the ease-of-use of automated louvres to control the level of relief you would appreciate from the many vagaries of our Melbourne climate. Providing a degree of privacy might also be important, should you live in an overlooked location.

      As a proud Australian family business, our friendly and approachable Melbourne’s Best Verandas experts know that our sales pressure free approach is much appreciated. We always take the time to really understand how you’d want your patio to look, how you intend to use it, and then we can talk you through so many exciting tailored design possibilities. Quality products, crafted to the standards you would expect, such as those produced by Fielders, Stratco and ColorBond, and backed by extensive guarantees, are always installed with much care and attention. As you consider integrating a new patio as part of your property, do join so many satisfied customers who know that our name is our promise: MBV = Melbourne’s Best Verandas.
