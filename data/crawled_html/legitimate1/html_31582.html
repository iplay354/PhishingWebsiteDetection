Acknowledgement of Country

The Sydney Swans acknowledge the Traditional Owners of Country across all the lands on which we operate and play our great game. Elders are the knowledge keepers of our culture, stories, dance and song lines, and we respectfully acknowledge and pay our respects to the Elders past, present and emerging.
