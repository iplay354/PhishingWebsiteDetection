Broadband for All

GovOps and its Department of Technology lead the effort to achieve Broadband for All. Through the construction of the Middle Mile network and community engagement, we’re helping to make broadband for all possible for nearly all Californians – to have access to affordable internet, digital devices, and digital literacy training by 2026.

Generative AI in California

Governor Newsom signed an executive order to study the development, use, and risks of artificial intelligence (AI) technology throughout the state and to develop a deliberate and responsible process for evaluation and deployment of AI within state government. GovOps is leading the GenAI work across the administration. Learn more about all the work done so far.

Engaged California

The State proudly announces the launch of Engaged California, an innovative new program designed to foster meaningful dialogue and strengthen trust between Californians and their government. Our goal is to better understand where government can be more efficient, collect valuable input on priorities, and test the platform. The first use case of the program is focused on survivors of the LA firestorm and those who have been impacted by the recovery and rebuilding effort. 

Supporting state operations

We oversee state government improvement, including the strategic management of state resources and advancing innovation in government. In addition, we have direct responsibility for 13 state departments and offices within the Agency, including the departments of Technology, Human Resources, and General Services – which support all departments in the executive branch.
