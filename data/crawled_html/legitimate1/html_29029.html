Drug and Alcohol Addiction Treatment Centre

Step Away is a trusted rehabilitation centre in South Africa, offering professional,
compassionate support for those facing drug or alcohol addiction.

Located in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), we provide a safe, structured environment where
treatment is grounded, consistent, and designed to support real change.

Our multidisciplinary team is here to guide you or your loved one through every
stage of recovery, with dignity, respect, and proven clinical care.

Fully licensed by the Department of Health and Social Development,
we are committed to helping you reclaim your life — one step at a time.

 

Treating the Whole Person,
Not Just the Addiction

Addiction doesn’t live in isolation — and neither should recovery. At Step Away, treatment goes beyond managing symptoms. We work with each individual to understand the deeper patterns behind substance use, the emotional pain that fuels it, and the physical toll it takes.

Our approach combines clinical care with practical support to address every layer of a person’s experience:

  • Psychological - Personal therapy, trauma-informed care, and emotional regulation tools
  • Physical - Medical supervision, nutrition support, and health stabilisation
  • Social - Group therapy, peer connection, and relationship rebuilding
  • Spiritual or values-based (where appropriate) - Exploring purpose, meaning, and identity beyond addiction

Addiction is rarely the problem on its own — it’s often a response to unresolved stress, trauma, or long-term emotional strain. That’s why our treatment model integrates medical care, psychological therapy, and behavioural support. Each programme begins with a comprehensive assessment, allowing us to identify the underlying drivers of substance use and build a focused, evidence-based plan for recovery.

What to Expect at Our Rehabilitation
Centre in South Africa

Our facility in Port Elizabeth provides a structured, medically supported environment designed to support focused, uninterrupted recovery. From admission onward, each individual follows a clearly defined treatment plan shaped by clinical assessments, therapeutic goals, and the unique nature of their substance use.

The programme includes:

  • Daily therapeutic sessions, combining individual counselling, group therapy, and psychoeducational workshops
  • Medical supervision, including detox support where necessary, overseen by a licensed healthcare team
  • Structured routines, with designated times for therapy, rest, nutrition, reflection, and optional recreational activity
  • Confidential, non-judgmental care, with strict protocols in place to ensure dignity and privacy throughout

Step Away is a licensed rehabilitation centre in South Africa, accredited by the Department of Health and Social Development. Our approach balances professional oversight with a calm, restorative setting — giving each person the best possible foundation for long-term recovery.

 

Why Location Matters in Addiction Recovery

Step Away is situated near Sardinia Bay, a quiet coastal area on the outskirts of Port Elizabeth.
Surrounded by nature and removed from the distractions of city life,
our setting offers the physical and psychological distance many people need to begin their recovery with focus and clarity.

Changing environments can play a critical role in early recovery. Being away from familiar triggers, routines,
and social pressures allows individuals to engage more fully with treatment. At our rehab centre in Port Elizabeth,
clients benefit not only from expert care, but also from the calm and containment that our location provides.

For those outside the Eastern Cape — and even outside South Africa — our centre offers a discreet,
affordable,and professionally run alternative to larger, more clinical facilities.
Many of our clients travel from across the country or abroad to access treatment in an environment that supports both structure and restoration.

A Closer Look at Our Rehab Programmes

Step Away offers structured, short-term residential treatment for substance dependency, with the core programme running over a period of 21 days.
Each intake begins with a clinical and psychological assessment, ensuring that treatment is appropriate, targeted, and aligned with the individual’s needs and history.

Our programmes are designed to address:

    Alcohol and drug dependency, including dual or cross-addiction
    Withdrawal management, where required, under medical supervision
    Co-occurring conditions, such as anxiety, depression, or trauma-related symptoms
    Psychoeducation and skills development, with a focus on relapse prevention, emotional regulation, and behaviour change

Treatment is delivered by a multidisciplinary team that includes a psychiatrist, medical doctor, psychologist, registered nurse, occupational therapist, social worker, and specialised addiction counsellors.
Collaboration across disciplines ensures each person receives consistent, coordinated care throughout their stay.

Where necessary, programmes can be extended to allow for deeper work or post-stabilisation support.

 

Inpatient Treatment

Inpatient care offers a structured, full-time environment where individuals can step away from daily stressors and focus entirely on recovery. With 24/7 clinical support, a defined daily routine, and a safe, contained setting, it provides the stability needed to begin addressing the underlying causes of addiction — without distraction or external pressure.

 

Outpatient Treatment

Outpatient care offers ongoing therapeutic support while allowing individuals to maintain aspects of daily life, such as work, family, or studies. It’s a flexible option for those who are stabilised but still benefit from structured sessions, accountability, and guidance as they navigate recovery in real-world environments.

 

21 Day Programme

Our 21-day inpatient programme offers focused, intensive treatment designed to stabilise clients within a defined timeframe. It’s ideal for those who need structured support but plan to return to work or family commitments after three weeks. Progress is closely monitored throughout, and extensions are available when clinically indicated.

Ready to Take the First Step?

If you or someone you care about is struggling with substance dependency, reaching out is the first and most important step.
At Step Away, we offer professional guidance, structured support, and a space to begin again — with clarity, care, and clinical integrity.

All enquiries are handled confidentially, and our team is available to answer your questions, explain the admission process,
