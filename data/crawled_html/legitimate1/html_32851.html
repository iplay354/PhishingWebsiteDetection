Hi! I'm Amy

I went from a struggling working photographer to a multiple-six-figure-a-year creative entrepreneur and now I help others do the same

I’m a professional photographer turned creative business coach and go-to instructor for product photography, content marketing, and business coaching for over 6000 creatives who LOVE my signature, no-muss no-fuss teaching style.

I’m all about simple, effective strategies for GREAT content and business strategy that will help you stand out, grow, and sell more with ease – so you can free up time to do more of what you love.

My students, members, and clients have seen INCREDIBLE things happen in their businesses – like increasing their revenue by nearly six-figures, retiring their spouses, going viral, leaving their day jobs, and building the businesses of their wildest dreams.

It’s my absolute pleasure in life to help so many grow their businesses into thriving, fulfilling ventures that allow them to live a life they truly love – with less stress and more joy. And I’d love to help you do the same.
