About Me


Life via painting ...


Somewhere at the beginning there is a tiny grain of inspiration enabling me to embark on not easy journey into the heart of an art. Through painting, I see the world and myself in new shades of understanding where fresh challenges result and gaping deeps urge to explore ... If I lose myself in workflow, not aware where the painting begins and where myself ends, then delightful harmony arise.

Still, I evolve as an artist and a human being. This upgrowth never ends and can be seen across my work frequently.

I dedicate myself to both traditional and digital creation, whether paintings, illustrations or even pet portraits. One of my greatest inspiration is the Nature herself. Her variety and freshness bears infinite space for creativity, understanding, growth.

Art guide ...
Painting reveal ....

Such life fills me with serenity.

Vera
