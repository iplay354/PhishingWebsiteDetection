I2C Slave Modules

Sip Modules: SIP modules as small, simple pcbs with a single row of I/O pins along one edge, and our standard I2C connector. The only pins you need to wire up, are the I/O pins you are using. Everything else is done for you. Because the pins are 0.1" spaced, they are easy to put on proto-boards of all kinds. You can even put standard 0.1" plugs on them. 

Modules:

  • 8 bit I/O port with PCF8574 
  • 8 Push buttons + 8 LED's using PCF8574 
  • 12 Bit +/- 10V Precision ADC with Current Sensor
  • 8 Bit ADC, DAC
  • 6 Digit Event Counter
  • 4 Way Bus switch for bus expansion
  • Bus Monitor Board
  • 4 digit Numeric Displays Numeric displays are unbeatable for readability! LED and LCD, 0.3", 0.56", 0.8", 1.0", 2.3", 4.0"
  • 82C96 Bus Extender and Galvanic Isolator
  • 82B715 Bus Extender
  • Constant Current Pullup module
  • Adaptor Board for different connectors
  • Alphanumeric LCD Adaptor: A simple interface to standard LCD modules

Specialist I2C Slave IC's

  • BL301Versatile Display Driver: Drives a mixture of Alpha LCD's, LED and LCD modules, and does binary-> text conversions for you
  • BLRS232 Interface: Send RS232 from I2C. Ideal for emitting diagnostic messages to a PC
  • BL310 Latching Relay Driver: Latching relays offer many advantages, IF you have an easy way to drive them
  • Shaft Encoder IC: Counts pulse from quadrature encoders, both fast positions sensors, and cheap potentiometer types
