Shamrock Farm is a diversified family farm established in 1993

Located in the Comox Valley, on Vancouver Island, B.C.

Our focus is growing a wide range of high quality seasonal vegetables and specialty cut flowers for our local community.

We follow sustainable farming practices. We do not use pesticides or herbicides on our farm, and we do not grow GMO crops.

Shop our farm products online, visit our farm gate stand, and find us at the Comox Valley and Campbell River Farmers’ Markets.

Restaurant and florist sales available.

The farm is generally closed to the public as we are an authentic working farm but we do open for scheduled Open Farm Days.
