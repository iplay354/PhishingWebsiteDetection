Ethical elephant

our Brand story

Ethical beauty

Galvanized by a revealing PETA video on fur and animal byproducts, 13rushes was born out of a dire market need for better, kinder alternatives. Our vision is simple yet profound: to champion a cruelty-free lifestyle that doesn’t compromise on quality, showing respect and care for our planet’s animals.

homegrown, handcrafted

Cruelty-free always

NO ANIMAL HAIR

13rushes embodies the conviction that a cruelty-free life is not only possible but paramount. Our commitment to opposing fur farm cruelty is unwavering. Each brush we create features hypoallergenic, textured synthetic bristles for a compassionate touch.

handcrafted

From start to finish, each brush undergoes 12 production stages by 26 craftsmen. Unlike machine-cut brushes, every bristle filament in our brush is trimmed only at it’s ends. This leaves the bristle tips untouched for unmatched softness.

local & proud

As a homegrown startup, we team up with seasoned local makeup artists to create brushes that are not only practical but also excel in performance
