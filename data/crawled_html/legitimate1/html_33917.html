Welcome

We are a Scottish Episcopal Church in the heart of Edinburgh

You are welcome to attend any of our services in person or online.

Visit our Online Resources page

Advent & Christmas at OSP

Welcome to Advent and Christmas 2025 at Old Saint Paul’s. As nights draw in and festive lights return, we invite you to join us in celebration—whether you’re looking for hope, community, or a quiet moment of reflection. From candle-lit services and seasonal carols to joyful Christmas worship, our programme includes Advent wreath lightings, carol services, a children’s Crib Service, Midnight Mass, and High Mass on Christmas Day. Whether you come to our historic church or join online, all are warmly welcomed. Check out our full schedule and discover how you can take part this festive season.

Check out our Advent & Christmas Schedule

The White Rose

The White Rose is a monthly online magazine for the OSP community. Each month, one of the beautifully written and well-researched articles of the issue is featured on our website for a wider readership.

As we enter the quiet expectancy of Advent, we invite you to explore three new reflections that deepen our shared journey of faith. “Christ the King: A Sermon” offers a stirring meditation on Christ’s reign, power and our hope; “We Do Not Presume” asks whether we come as volunteers or true participants in God’s work; and “The Service of Nine Lessons and Carols” traces a beloved tradition that shapes our Advent and Christmas worship. May these pieces nurture reflection and strengthen our life together in this season of watchful hope.

Joining OSP

Old Saint Paul’s is a large church in the heart of Edinburgh’s Old Town, with members gathered from across Edinburgh and beyond. As you settle in to Old Saint Paul’s, you may wish to become involved as a member and be part of this committed community.

Worship

Our main worship service at Old Saint Paul’s is High Mass on a Sunday Morning. On Sunday evenings, we gather for Solemn Evensong and Benediction.

On weekdays, there is Morning Prayer and Low Mass.

Who we are

Old Saint Paul’s is a gathered congregation that meets in the centre of Edinburgh.

People are drawn to Old Saint Paul’s for many different reasons – their love of liturgy, church music, devotion to Anglo-Catholic worship, or their search for a church where they feel at home.
