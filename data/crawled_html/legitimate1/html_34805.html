Berkshire’s Best Country Mansion Hotel

Easthampstead Park is one of the most exclusive 4* hotels in Berkshire boasting 92 beautifully designed bedrooms.  

Rooms are located in the Mansion House (main building) as well as in our newly built Hepburn House (just adjacent to the mansion house). All offer peaceful and spacious accommodation.  

Our classically furnished mansion house bedrooms and modern furnished Hepburn House rooms, feature large comfortable beds, spacious en-suite bathrooms and are equipped with all the amenities you need for a great stay.

WOKINGHAM & BRACKNELL LOCATION

Easthampstead Park is located in Berkshire between the cities of Bracknell and Wokingham and has convenient transport connections via the M4 and M3 motorways. The hotel is situated within easy reach of Bracknell Rail Station and also Wokingham Rail Station.
