WE ARE CHANGING!

We're very excited for the changes Pica Pica Beauty will go through starting 2023 and beyond.

We are infinitely grateful you have been a part of our journey since our humble beginnings in 2016, but it is now a time to change our ways.

Due to our constant commitment for high quality, but also the desire to improve our shipping speeds and improve our customer service, we have now decided to cut down on the amount of products we hold regularly, as well as introducing seasonal monthly product highlight.

As much as we will still have some products in stock for longer amounts of time, our shop is going to hold pop-up products for limited amounts of time - so if you don't purchase one month, there might not be a chance to get them the next.

We decided this move will allow us to introduce more variety, as well as give our customers more unique and limited edition products.

As usual we are infinitely grateful for your support and really hope this change will bring an enthusiastic response from our community :)

The changes will officially take effect from April 1st 2023!

*Whilst this happens you will see much less products in our stock, and as a result easier navigation.

If you have comments or suggestions do let us know on our official Facebook page - Pica Pica Beauty.

OUR BRAND

Pica Pica Beauty is a business established to provide beauty accessories to everyday customers, as well as professionals.

 Our mission is to bring some colour and variety to the world of makeup, hair, nail and other accessories, hoping to become, at the same time, the market leader specializing in accessories only!

We'd like to abolish the trend, that makeup brushes can only be sold in large beauty chains as an addition to colour makeup - We believe they deserve their own special space in the market in as many variations possible!

 (That's right, no makeup here).

Our designs are fresh and innovative, setting Our products apart from Your traditional brushes. 

We are also here because we love our planet! <3 And we want to provide products that naturally complement and coexist with its resources without damaging it.

We love and embrace our differences. Pica Pica beauty is not just your next stop shop. it's a completely new movement!
