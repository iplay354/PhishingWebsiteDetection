2025 National Planning Awards

Prioritizing Zoning Reform

Planning-led zoning reform is key to tackling many of the nation's greatest challenges, especially the housing supply crisis. With adequate support, zoning reform can increase housing choice, lower housing costs, bolster local economies, address inequities in our communities, and connect people to opportunity.

Learn More

Fall 2025 AICP Certification Cycle

AICP Certification helps you make an impact in your career, your projects, and your community. The streamlined One Path to AICP Certification enables you to achieve your planning goals. Start assessing your readiness and prepare today!

Learn More

Critical Resources for Members

Access critical resources about the shifting federal landscape, post-disaster recovery materials, and information on applying for reduced dues or CM exemption for members in transition. Resources and materials are available free to APA members.
