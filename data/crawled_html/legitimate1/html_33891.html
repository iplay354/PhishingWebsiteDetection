PEEC offers opportunities for year-round exploration of the natural world.

The Pocono Environmental Education Center (PEEC) is located within the 77,000 acre Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area and is the education partner of the National Park Service.

PEEC advances:

ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION

SUSTAINABLE LIVING

& APPRECIATION FOR NATURE THROUGH HANDS-ON EXPERIENCE IN A NATIONAL PARK

Within driving distance of New York City and Philadelphia, PEEC offers a variety of options for all visitors from school field trips and scout programs to public hiking trails and nature programs.
