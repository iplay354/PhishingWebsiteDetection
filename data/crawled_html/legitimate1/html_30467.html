At the Vox Theatre, we believe that every occasion deserves to be celebrated in style. While we offer competitive pricing, what truly sets us apart is our dedication to providing unparalleled service and attention to detail. Whether you're planning a corporate gathering, wedding, birthday party, or special celebration, we're here to ensure that your event is nothing short of unforgettable.

Nestled in the historic Rosedale district of Kansas City, the Vox Theatre stands as your versatile and budget-friendly venue, perfect for weddings, corporate functions, and special events.

Discover the essence of timeless charm at our venue in the heart of Kansas City's historic Rosedale district. With affordability and flexibility at our core, the Vox Theatre is where unforgettable moments come to life, reflecting our sincere dedication to realizing your vision.

Key Details

  • The Vox Theatre event space can host up to 299 guests and has many customizable floor plans and styles that make for a very intimate and comfortable space.

  • The rental fee for our venue ranges from $3,500 - $5,000 depending on season.

  • Located in Kansas City, KS, on the western edge of the Kansas - Missouri border near the Country Club plaza. The location offers the perfect blend of historic charm, convenient accessibility, and vibrant surroundings, providing an ideal backdrop for unforgettable events.

  • We provide access to a diverse range of vendors, each offering unique services to make their special day unforgettable. From talented florists who can create stunning arrangements to skilled photographers capturing every precious moment, our vendors are renowned for their professionalism, creativity, and attention to detail. Whether you're seeking delectable catering options, elegant decor, or entertainment that keeps the dance floor buzzing, our curated selection of vendors ensures that every aspect of your event is top-notch. With their expertise and dedication, our vendors collaborate seamlessly to bring your vision to life, making your celebration truly extraordinary.

  • Our open vendor policy allows you to work with any licensed and insured catering and bartending service you prefer. In addition, we also have recommendations and preferred vendors at your leisure to ensure your event is exceptional!
