Where is Ballyhoura?

The Ballyhoura region takes its name from the Ballyhoura Mountains which cross from County Limerick into North East Cork. It is an area of undulating green pastures, woodlands, hills and mountains encompassing the East of County Limerick and North East County Cork. Easily accessible from the M7, N20, N24, N73 and M8, the Ballyhoura region offers ancient and unspoilt landscape, an abundance of significant heritage sites, thrilling outdoor activities and friendly people, making it the perfect place to discover rural Ireland.
