Our Story - Celebrating over 40 years

Lighting & Tile Store

In 1978 Canberra was a very different city and so was our business. Back then we were operating out of our family garage providing families and local builders with a select but quality range of tiles. As Canberra grew so did we. We witnessed an amazing evolution of our nation’s capital and saw our backyard business turn into a major retail and wholesale operation. We have evolved and become a place you come to when you want to create something unique. Our team is passionate about high quality products and beautiful design, and we work with our customers to select excellent tiles, lights, bathroomware and kitchen appliances.

Leading Tile, Lighting and Bathware Store

Since 1978, Cirillo Lighting & Ceramics has been a premiere lighting store and tile shop in Canberra. What began as a family owned business operating out of a family garage has grown into a full service home supply business. Today, our store offers a range of quality, innovative products that help make any house a more comfortable and efficient home.

Cirillo Lighting & Ceramics continues to grow, offering a range of home products to customers in Canberra and throughout Australia. Some of our current offerings include:

  • Tiles
  • Lighting
  • Subway Tiles
  • Marble Tiles
  • Herringbone Tiles
  • Penny Round Tiles

One of the reasons that so many of our Australian customers keep returning to us is our dedication to customer service. We discuss their needs with them, and help them find the right products and solutions for these needs.

We are also dedicated to environmental sustainability. We are more just than a lighting or tile store that sells whatever products are most popular. We know the role of our industry in improving the sustainability of the environment for us and for our children. That’s why we work with suppliers who adhere to the manufacturing of recycled materials and the production of eco-sustainable products. We care about the future of our planet, and we consider the impact on it with every product we use and sell.

Every detail of your home has an impact on the quality and comfort of your life. We want to be a part of creating the perfect home for you and your family. Cirillo Lighting and Ceramics has based our business on our family values. We know how important family is in our lives and in society.

Contact us with any questions you have. Let us help you get the results from your home project that will turn your house into the best home it can be!
