Current Events

January 9, 2026

Member’s “Show & Tell,” Raffle, and Potluck

26th Seasonal Splurge on Sharing Storytelling Slideshows, Snacking on Scrumptious Sustenance, and Scooping up Society Supporting Swag

  • Show-and-Tell: Members and guests may share anything of botanical or society interest. Limit of about 10 slides or 5 minutes narration.
  • Raffle: There will be a raffle of books and botanical items. A number of items have already been donated. Bring cash!
  • Potluck: Bring a potluck dish to share with other members and guests. Botanical labeling of dishes is encouraged.

Mark your calendar for the next NEBS meeting on January 9, 2026 at the Division of Fisheries and Wildlife Cronin Building, 1 Rabbit Hill Road, Westborough, MA (Room 108 – Southeast Conference Room). Start time is 2:30pm. In person only.
