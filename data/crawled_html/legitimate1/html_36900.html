How can Dr. Ken help?

These are the most common ways Dr. Ken supports patients. Each option includes what it covers so visitors understand whether it fits back pain, neck pain, headaches, sports injuries, or jaw pain.

Spinal Manipulation:
Chiropractic Adjustments

Hands-on techniques to improve joint movement and reduce stiffness. Often used when pain is linked to restricted mobility or irritated joints.

  • Back pain, neck pain, mid-back stiffness
  • Posture-related discomfort and mobility limits
  • Sports strains and “locked up” joints
Learn more →

Dry Needling

Targets muscle trigger points that can cause referred pain and stubborn tightness. Great when muscles are the main limiter, not just joints.

  • Muscle tightness, knots, and tension patterns
  • Shoulder/neck tension and overuse pain
  • Glute/hip tightness that aggravates the low back
Learn more →

Acupuncture

A gentle approach often used to calm pain sensitivity and support recovery. Useful for chronic discomfort and stress-driven tension.

  • Chronic pain and recurring flare-ups
  • Headaches, tension, and sleep disruption
  • Stress-related tightness and recovery support
Learn more →

Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) Pain

Jaw pain is often connected to neck tension, muscle overload, and joint irritation. Treatment focuses on improving comfort and function.

  • Jaw pain, clicking, or limited opening
  • Headaches linked to jaw or neck tension
  • Clenching/grinding (bruxism) related soreness
