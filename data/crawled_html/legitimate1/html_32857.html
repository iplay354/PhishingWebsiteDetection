Life Begins at the
Genetics & IVF Institute

Genetics & IVF Institute, Inc., founded in 1984, was the first outpatient IVF center in the United States. Since then, we have grown into an international family of companies providing compassionate and comprehensive reproductive technology services and products to patients and physicians worldwide.

Our Products & Services

Since 1984, Genetics and IVF Institute has offered a unique combination of fertility expertise and reproductive genetic capabilities.  Our services help build healthy, happy families around the world.

Donor Sperm

Fairfax Cryobank is one of the world's largest sperm banks, providing high-quality donor sperm since 1986 and now selling to patients and clinics in more than 50 countries. We perform the most extensive genetic and infectious disease testing of all sperm banks. Fewer than 1% of applicants make it through our screening process to become sperm donors. With one of the largest inventories of cryopreserved reproductive specimens in the world, we have extensive expertise both in freezing sperm and in storing and safely transporting frozen sperm, eggs and embryos across the United States and around the world.

  • Visit Fairfax Cryobank

Donor Egg

Fairfax EggBank and GIVF have provided patients with donor eggs since 1988. Today, our extensive network of IVF clinic partners provides us with the ability to recruit and retrieve donors nationwide. While we accept fewer than 1% of applicants into our egg donor program, our donor database offers one of the largest and most diverse selection of immediately available frozen donor eggs in the United States.

  • Visit Fairfax EggBank

Surrogacy Services

Fairfax Surrogacy is a full-service surrogacy agency helping intended parents around the world find a surrogate to carry their baby. Fairfax Surrogacy conducts a comprehensive and high-quality screening process that allows us to more quickly match intended parents with carefully screened surrogates. Our high standards for surrogates help us ensure a healthy, safe outcome for all involved.
