SOUTH AFRICAN SOCIETY FOR ENOLOGY
& VITICULTURE (SASEV)

Your leading partner of scientific knowledge
in the South African grape and wine industries since 1977.

SASEV (South African Society For Enology & Viticulture) serves as a forum for presenting the latest technical and scientific information to all South African wine and grape producers to improve the quality of South African table grapes, drying grapes, wines and related products.
