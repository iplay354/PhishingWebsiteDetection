Mission & Philosophy

About Us

Welcome to the home of the Manchester-Shortsville School District, also known as Red Jacket Schools!  The Manchester and Shortsville communities value public education and are committed to providing the best resources available to prepare our students for their future.  With highly qualified staff and a supportive community, we are committed to excellence in academics, arts and athletics. 

Our mission is that we will challenge all learners and work in partnership with students, parents, and the community to achieve high standards.
