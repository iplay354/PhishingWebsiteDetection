For Stories
That Matter

Most businesses struggle to clearly tell the story of their products. Rootip enables easy and engaging product storytelling so that businesses can grow and develop products that customers love.

Business Challenge

Businesses struggle to clearly communicate their product stories & impact at the point of sales

Product stories and impact are competitive edge to many businesses and critical when customers making a purchase decision.

  • Retail Space: When a customer picks up a product in a retail space they have no visibility into the impact and stories
  • Online: Most customers landing on a product page don’t go all over website to find impact content since most stories are under “about us”, “sustainability” and blog posts. Also as an e-commerce the last thing you want is for the user to leave the shopping flow

BUSINESS

REGULATIONS
