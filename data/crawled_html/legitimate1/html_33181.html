What is Parent Hub?

Parent Hub is an APP, SMS and EMAIL messaging system that helps you have better communication with parents.

It enables you to reach every parent – instantly, and be confident that you have.

Schools use it to simplify their communication strategy, go paperless, and transform their whole approach to empowering parents with the information and updates that matter most to them.

Is it really free?

Yes – Parent Hub really can be used free forever!

There’s no charge for signing up, integrating with your MIS, or sending unlimited messages to our free Parent Hub app.

Optional extras, including text messaging and email sending, are available on our premium plans so you can reach every parent from the get-go.

Just scroll down the page to see what you can enjoy free out of the box.

How do we get started?

The choice is yours.

You can sign up now for free, or book an online demo and we’ll give you the grand tour!

Parent Hub has the simplest launch process, and with our free 1:1 training you’ll be enjoying better communication with parents in no time.

We’ll guide you from sign-up through to integration and roll-out, and be on hand at every step to ensure you get off to a great start!
