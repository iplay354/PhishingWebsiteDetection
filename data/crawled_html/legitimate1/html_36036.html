Frog Street Curriculum

Built for Today's Classrooms. Backed by Research. Designed to Reduce Teacher Stress.

You know what makes early learning work: consistent routines, strong relationships, and curriculum that supports growth across all domains. But you also face new daily challenges, behavioral disruption, teacher fatigue, and disconnected resources.

Meet the Needs That Matter Most

Curriculum That Solves Real Classroom Challenges

Behavior Challenges

Brain-compatible SED strategies from Conscious Discipline® reduce classroom disruption from day one. Our integrated approach transforms reactive moments into teaching opportunities through consistent language, visual supports, and predictable routines that build self-regulation.

Learn More

Teacher Burnout

Plug-and-play lessons save planning time and simplify decision fatigue. Clear teacher scripts, ready-to-use materials, and embedded professional development notes mean less prep time and more confidence in delivery.

Learn More

Curriculum Overload

The integrated system covers all learning domains: literacy, math, science, SED, and family engagement. No more combining disparate resources, everything works together in a cohesive, research-aligned system.

Learn More

Family Engagement Gaps

Bilingual take-home tools and emotional vocabulary help families extend learning. Materials use the same language and strategies as classroom instruction, creating consistency between school and home.

Learn More

Pre-K (4-5 years)

Comprehensive kindergarten readiness through intentional skill-building, vocabulary expansion, critical thinking, and classroom structures preparing children for success.

Preschool (36-48 months)

Balanced instruction that harnesses curiosity through structured exploration, increased language development, and social skills practice with embedded academic foundations.

Toddler (12-36 months)

Movement-based learning that supports emerging independence, emotional vocabulary, and sensory exploration through consistent routines and self-regulation foundations.

Infant (0-18 months)

Responsive caregiving routines that build secure attachment and brain architecture through sensory-rich experiences and relationhship-based interactions.

Why Programs Trust Frog Street

Designed for Consistency, Clarity, and Instructional Strength

Frog Street programs are built for ease of use, alignment to standards, and daily fidelity. Key features include:

  • Daily and weekly lesson plans with clear objectives and assessment connections
  • Conscious Discipline® integration for brain-compatible behavior strategies that reduce disruptions and build community
  • Embedded language, social-emotional development, and routines that strengthen core skills through repetition and extension
  • Multi-language materials designed for diverse classrooms and family engagement
  • Head Start, QRIS, and Pre-K alignment with comprehensive documentation for compliance and reporting
  • Family engagement and coaching tools that extend learning beyond the classroom through consistent messaging
