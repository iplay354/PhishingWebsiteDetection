PROPERTY IN GONUBIE

Gonubie is a quiet, prestigious coastal village in South Africa. It is located 13 miles north of East London and is a popular seaside destination, located at the mouth of the Gonubie River.

Popular Beaches & Holiday Destination:
Gonubie Beach is a popular Blue Flag beach and offers beautiful sandy beaches, accessed by a raised boardwalk. It is also a popular dolphin and whale watching spot. On the northern side of Gonubie lies the tranquil Gonubie River, offering an abundance of bird life and water sport activities. It is easy to see why Gonubie is so popular amongst locals and tourists alike.

PROPERTY IN EAST LONDON

East London is situated at the start of the Wild Coast, half-way between Port Elizabeth and Durban. Nestled between the Nahoon and Buffalo Rivers on the Indian Ocean, East London remains South Africa's only river port.

East London is a popular destination among locals looking to invest in property on the Sunshine Coast. In more recent years, East London Real Estate has been increasingly sought after by foreign investors and international tourists because of its unique surroundings, wild unspoiled beaches and lagoons. It's easy to see why East London has become such a popular choice in real estate investments.
