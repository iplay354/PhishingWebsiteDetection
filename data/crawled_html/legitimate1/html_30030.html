God's Word to Women, Inc. teaches the Biblical truth that men and women are equal (in terms of substance and value, privilege and responsibility, function and authority) in all areas of life, ministry, and marriage. In reaching and teaching a hurting world, we are responsible as Christians to teach ALL that Jesus taught (Matthew 28:18-20) that God's ways may be known on earth and His salvation among all nations (Psalm 67:1-2). The Gospel Message of the Lord Jesus saves, heals, delivers, and liberates those who believe in Him.
