WHO WE ARE

About 360 Istanbul


360istanbul, which has many international awards, is located on the top floor of the historical and ancient Mısır Apartment. It serves lunch and dinner from world and modern Turkish cuisine, accompanied by a breathtaking 360-degree view of Istanbul, with a selection of wines and a rich bar menu. 360 Istanbul, which hosts famous artists and DJs from abroad, turns into a Club with its live shows.

This concept consists of 4 basic elements; Food – Atmosphere – Entertainment and Ambiance. No element in 360istanbul is more valuable or less valuable than the other. All of them are in harmony with each other.

360 FOOD

FOOD, ENTERTAINMENT, AMBIENCE and ATMOSPHERE factors form the basis of the 360 Istanbul concept, which was developed by the knowledgeable and experienced professional team of 360 Entertainment Group. The fact that each factor is equally important and complements each other ensures that we can maximize customer satisfaction by bringing the concept into a whole.

360 ENTERTAINMENT

FOOD: A creative menu of dishes and drinks made of fresh and quality ingredients. ENTERTAINMENT: A diverse and exciting entertainment program. AMBIENCE: A pleasant environment with a comfortable and extraordinary interior design. ATMOSPHERE: Opportunity to have a pleasant meal accompanied by a panoramic view of Istanbul.
