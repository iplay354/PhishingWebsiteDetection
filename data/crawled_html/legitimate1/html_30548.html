File a Complaint

The Consumer Protection Division protects consumers from fraud and ensures fair competition in the marketplace. To file a complaint, go here.

Assisting Crime Victims

The Victim Assistance Section provides services and assistance to victims of violent crimes. For 24/7 victim support, call the Iowa Crime Victims Helpline at 1-800-770-1650 or  text "IOWAHELP" to 20121
