Specialising in classic cars since 1963

Hexagon Classics, based in London, has one of the largest collections of classic cars for sale in UK & Europe.

We have been specialising in classic cars since 1963 and have over 100 classic cars in stock including some of the most desirable models including Ferrari, Jaguar, Aston Martin, Mercedes and BMW.

As a specialist in collectable air-cooled Porsche cars we have over 40 models in stock at our Highgate showrooms.

We specialise in low mileage, limited edition cars with a proven history – with a particular focus on 911, 993, 964. 997.2 and 991 variants.
