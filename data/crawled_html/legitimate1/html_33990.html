Ratio Storage Series

Rico Modular Sofa

Create a lounge layout that suits your space with this modular system from Ferm Living. Featuring signature soft organic curves and plush upholstery, each component of Rico features a deep seat for added comfort.

String Workspace System

String Furniture’s modular shelving system offers endless ways to store and display things all around the home, and this particular workspace configuration is perfect for a home office. We love how there’s ample room for both hidden and visible storage, with a locking cabinet for important documents.

SHOP NOW

Japanese Cabinet

Kristina Dam Studio’s Scandinavian interpretation of a storage and display cabinet is inspired by the sleek shapes found in Japanese design. We love its timeless and elegant expression that beautifully combines warm oiled oak and tempered glass.

Asano Paper Moon 2

Designed by Shigeru Uchida, the Paper Moon 2 is a handcrafted Washi table lamp made in Japan’s Gifu, where the art of crafting paper lanterns has been practiced for 90+ years. We love the soft warm glow of this light and every light from Asano.

SHOP NOW

Dream View Bench

MUUTO’s sculptural bench embodies calm contemplation and effortless form. With gentle curves that echo the body’s natural posture, this brushed stainless steel bench has a reflective surface that transforms any environment.

Panthella 250 Portable Lamp

Verner Panton’s iconic Panthella lamp is downscaled with this portable version perfect for windowsills, shelves, and tables. The Louis Poulsen Panthella series is defined by its unique expression where both the stand and shade serve as a reflector.

Elevate Modular Shelving
