General & Cosmetic Dentistry

Preventive Dentistry with a Holistic Touch and a Biological approach

Your smile is the first thing others notice when they first see you! Now is the perfect time to improve your smile, improve the way others see you, get better first impressions, better friendships, better relationships, maybe a better job or promotion, a better life and ultimately a better you!

We are a wellness dentistry office, we believe that your oral health is linked to your overall health. We take instant Digital X-Rays assisted by AI as a second set of eyes, that helps you see what the doctor sees in an xray. Intra-oral pictures, and a digital Dental Health Scan with the new iTero 5D Lumina, and we do Laser-assisted Hygiene, Laser Periodontal Treatment and Laser surgery, using less or no anesthesia. We do most fillings using the laser, which means no drilling and no anesthesia. We also offer Conscious Sedation, Smile Design, Invisalign, Laser Therapy for faster healing, Veneers, oral surgery, Implants and more!

Our newest technology reduces pain and anxiety, while providing the desired outcomes in a shorter appoinment

We practice Preventive Dentistry and use the most bio compatible and NON TOXIC dental materials and procedures! NO silver-mercury (old, dark amalgam fillings), NO metal restoration! We SAFELY remove old amalgam/mercury fillings. When you choose a Biological Dentist you know you are getting a holistic-healing experience for your overall health!

Complimentary Oral Cancer Screenings for all our Patients! We are a Digital Impressions practice, no goop, no gag, we use DryShield isolation and the ReLeaf suction system for maximum comfort!

We use an Invisalign simulation engine to show your future treatment progress in minutes for free, we'll email it to you together with a 3D digital scan of your teeth!
Bring your laptop and finish your work remotely, we have free guest wifi!



You are safe

We use 10 medical grade HEPA filters plus 3 enhanced suction vacuums around the office since 2020, this is part of our protocols to keep you and our staff safe. We also use a pre-treatment antiseptic rinse for all Patients.
Working in any health care facility, including a dental office, is harder, more stressful and costlier these days, so we kindly ask for your understanding and patience when calling and while in the office. Choose to be kind, thank you!
