Industry Leaders

We provide regional leadership, through thoughtful research and measurable results, that improves the personal and economic well-being of Tampa Bay residents.

The Tampa Bay Partnership is a coalition of regional business leaders, joined by our shared commitment to improving the personal and economic well-being of Tampa Bay residents.

As the region’s premiere research and public policy organization, we foster collaboration between our top employers and a diverse group of government and nonprofit partners, to address the toughest challenges facing our community today and create new opportunities for the future.

Focus Areas

The Partnership Activates The Region Around Critical Issues.

Transportation

Transportation is the greatest economic challenge facing Tampa Bay today. With an estimated 750,000 new residents by 2030, our potential to excel and compete requires that we expand transportation options and improve regional connectivity.

Learn More

Education

A well-educated society has more opportunities for a bright future. The Partnership works to provide research and activate the community to advance educational attainment, and skills-based opportunities at every age, from early-learning, to higher education and trade schools.

Learn More

Workforce

Tampa Bay employers will need to fill more than 850,000 job openings in the next five years. The Partnership works to close gaps in skills and experience, and fundamentally infuse best practices in training and hiring to ensure employers have qualified workers, and that the community has additional pathways to prosperity.

Resiliency

Storms and flooding without infrastructure adaptation may cost the region nearly $82B in losses by X year. Our research provides resiliency and mitigation recommendations to protect against bigger, more powerful, and more frequent storms which threaten the region’s coastal and low-lying areas due to climate change.
