Kalani High School is a public high school that was established in 1958 and is accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges. Enrollment is approximately 1400 students in grades 9-12 and reflects the ethnic variety of the state. Approximately 20% of the students qualify for free/reduced lunch with about 25% of students who live out of district boundaries and are on a Geographical Exception to attend.
