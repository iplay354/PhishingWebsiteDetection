Joseki matching

Given a corner or full-board position, SmartGo shows you the moves played by professionals, sorted by frequency. It does this by finding matching positions in the whole game collection. Explore the matching games to see how games typically develop after a certain opening or joseki.

Tree View

The tree view in SmartGo makes navigation easy. By compressing regular black-white-black-white move sequences and emphasizing branch points and annotations, the tree view reveals the structure of an annotated game.
