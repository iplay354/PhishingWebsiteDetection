We’re doing everything in our power to power everything you do.

Power is more than electricity – it keeps life moving. From celebrations to small moments, we’re here to keep your connections strong. Whether volunteering, sharing energy tips and tools or weathering the storms together, Alabama Power works to move families and communities forward.

Saving energy starts here.

We understand that managing energy costs can be tough, especially with Alabama’s unpredictable weather. From My Power Usage and energy-saving tips to free home energy reports, savings programs and flexible payment options, we’re committed to offering you every opportunity to save.
