After designing and producing our dresses for many years, Siri is closing.  It has been a privilege and journey working with Saks Fifth Avenue, Neiman’s, Nordstrom and hundreds of specialty stores run by amazing hard-working owners throughout the US and Canada. I leave with many memories and fondness of our customers who shopped at the Siri Boutique in San Francisco and later in South San Franciso as well as our online customers from afar.

Our timeless, quality dresses in our beautiful fabrics are now deeply discounted.  Go to our In STOCK page.  As always, I am available to help by email or phone.

With heartfelt thanks,

Beverley Siri
