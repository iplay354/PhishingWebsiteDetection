Our Strategic Plan

Strategic Plan

The District's strategic plan is a pathway to successfully fulfill our promise to connect with, inspire and empower our students. We built this strategic plan to provide system coherence and focus our work on five critical student learning outcomes. This plan lays out our vision, strategies and measures by which we will attain these goals.

School Board Approves Resolution to Place EP&O Levy Renewal on February 2026 Ballot
November 17, 2025

At its November meeting, the Orting School Board approved a resolution to place the district’s Educational Programs and Operations (EP&O) Levy Renewal on the February 11, 2026, ballot. This measure is not a new tax—it is a renewal of the levy voters approved in 2022, which is set to expire at the end of 2026.

NONDISCRIMINATION STATEMENT

Orting School District does not discriminate in any programs or activities on the basis of sex, race, creed, religion, color, national origin, age, veteran or military status, sexual orientation, gender expression or identity, disability, marital status, or the use of a trained dog guide or service animal and provides equal access to the Boy Scouts and other designated youth groups. The following employees have been designated to handle questions and complaints of alleged discrimination and their offices are located at 121 Whitesell St NE, Orting, WA 98360: Civil Rights Compliance/Title IX/ADA Coordinator Carrie Joy, Director of Human Resources 360-893-6500, ext. 4028 joyc@orting.wednet.edu; Section 504 Coordinator Dr. Jennifer Westover, Exec. Director for Student Support Services 360-893-6500, 4027 westoverj@orting.wednet.edu. Information about the nondiscrimination and sex-based discrimination policies and grievance procedures, and how to report a concern or complaint can be accessed here. Title IX inquiries may also be directed toward the U.S. Department of Education, Office for Civil Rights.
