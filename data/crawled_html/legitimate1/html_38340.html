Who is SIX AM?

SIX AM is an experiential and digital media platform covering all facets of underground electronic music culture, resource, technology, and lifestyle. Following our humble beginnings as a Los Angeles-based local blog and club sub-promotion company, we have since seen our global outreach, industry experience, scope and knowledge expand exponentially with each passing year.
