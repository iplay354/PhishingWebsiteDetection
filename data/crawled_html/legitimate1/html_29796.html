Who We Are

We are a husband-and-wife team who have been inspired by travel for most of our lives.  

Steve Jermanok is one of the most prolific travel writers in America. He has penned more than 2000 stories and 10 books while working as a columnist for National Geographic and The Boston Globe, and author for Lonely Planet and Frommer’s. Steve’s contacts around the globe and global experiences allow us to give you that personalized service that makes you feel at home even in the most remote of places.

Lisa Leavitt comes from the art world, where she wore many hats at esteemed art museums including the Jewish Museum, the Metropolitan Museum in New York and the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston. Some of those hats included writing, shipping art works, and finally curating art exhibitions at the Danforth Museum of Art in Framingham, MA. Her years in the art world has made her exceedingly detail oriented and has trained her to be highly selective when it comes to choosing quality.
