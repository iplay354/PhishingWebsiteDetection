Our Process

Our competent team works in an organized manner for understanding the requirements of our partners, and we follow a process in development of a project for ultimate results.

  • Planning
  • Design
  • Development
  • Launch

Planning

We believe that level of success depends on Planning phase. This is the reason we put lot of emphasis on Planning. Upfront appropriate planning always reduce margin of errors, keep project's goals clear, on-time and within budget. Be it waterfall or rapidly growing Agile methodology, we follow steps mentioned below.

  • Stakeholders Interview
  • Identify Business Requirements
  • Business Requirement Document
  • Workflow Sketching
  • Assess Risk & Finalize Scope

Design

Design phase establish the foundation for the Development phase. Design phase depends on the chosen service as well as requirement. We prepare blueprint - high level design - covering every aspect of the captured requirement. We determine the project structure & architecture that describes a final product, and that will successfully meet your business needs.

  • Prototype Design
  • Architecture Design
  • Technical Specification
  • Performance & Scalability Review
  • Test Specification

Development

Development phase consist of dividing work into modules/units and to work on them as per decided milestone. A team of developers/designers/testers are assigned to get the final successful result by following best industry practices. In agile development process, we further divide in iterations or sprints.

  • Actual Web Design & Refinement
  • Coding
  • Unit & Integration Testing
  • Deployment Plan

Launch

After successful completion of Development phase, it is time to deploy and launch the project into production environment. We make sure that every aspect of the launch process is followed perfectly. We even consider minute detail in each area such as security, disaster recovery or any other administrative part.

  • Deployment plan & diagram
  • System configuration document
  • Security & backup Plan
  • Disaster recovery plan
