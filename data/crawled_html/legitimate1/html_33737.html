About FlopsyShop.ie

We're an online babies and kids pre-loved clothing store. All items are hand picked and checked to ensure they are in great condition before being shipped to your door. 

We stock boys and girls clothing from newborn to 7 years of age. Everything is organised on the website by size, brand such as Next, F&F, M&S and Mamas&Papas, or type such as jackets, pants, shorts, dresses and shirts. 

FlopsyShop frequently attends fairs such as The BabyMarket.ie and the annual Wear a Hug Fair and All About Baby Fair. We've been featured on PurposeHub, RSVP, Mummypages and the Evening Echo.
