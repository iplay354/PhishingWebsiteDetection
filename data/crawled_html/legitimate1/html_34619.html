Meet Rachel
Hello friends. I'm Rachel - a recipe creator, organic gardener and home lover. Simply put, I'm someone who loves to create and keep her hands busy. If you love to keep your hands busy, you are in the right place. My recipes are for people who cherish the joy that comes from cooking homemade meals and my home is for anyone who hopes to infuse a bit of charm into their daily life. Our farmhouse is on 7 acres in Michigan where we care for the land, animals and our community. Thank you for joining us today. I hope you will stay for a bit and make yourself at home.
