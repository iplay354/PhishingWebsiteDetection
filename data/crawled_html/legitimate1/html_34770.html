January 30th, 2019 UPDATE

Mage's Initiation Released

In the proud tradition of Sierra classics, we're excited to announce our latest game: Mage's Initaition: Reign of the Elements. A lovingly-crafted Adventure/RPG that harkens back to King's Quest and Quest for Glory.

Join D'arc, a studious young Initiate, on his first foray into the outside world. His quest: to become a Mage. Wander gloomy forests, ascend snowcapped mountains, explore a scorched wasteland, and battle fierce foes using a combination of Stats and Sorcery as you seek to save Iginor from chaos. Just another school field trip? Think again!

Available now on Steam, GOG, and the Humble Store for Windows, MacOS, and Linux.



February 24th, 2011 UPDATE

King's Quest III Redux Released

Following in the tradition of enhanced Sierra remakes, we're proud to present King's Quest III Redux: To Heir is Human. The game resumes where our previous remake, King's Quest II: Romancing the Stones, ended. Featuring beautiful storybook artwork, an enchanting musical score, full voice acting, new scenes, quests, characters, and locations to explore, plus a royal serving of adventure, this is AGD Interactive's most polished remake ever -- a must play!
