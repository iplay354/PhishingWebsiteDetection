Commercial Printing & Publishing

Masthof publishing company, bookstore, and commercial printing is passionate about preserving history through books and helping our customers be successful! We are a local publishing company that considers it an honor to help people become authors.

Our retail store features titles on history, genealogy, and biographies. We also carry Christian, devotional, and children's titles.

If you are self-publishing or seeking a publisher, we enjoy helping authors publish their works by preserving their words in the form of a printed book. Our printing equipment offers high quality printing services utilizing cutting edge technology to exceed our customers’ expectations. We are a full-service digital and offset printing company, as well as bookstore. Whether you need us to print books or brochures, we are happy to help you.

The bookstore is a gold mine of local authors, especially history and genealogy books. We have many genres ranging from children's books, Pennsylvania Dutch, Anabaptist, Mennonite, Amish and more!
