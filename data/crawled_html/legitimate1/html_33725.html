Four easy Steps to Homeownership

  1. Register for an Orientation
  2. Review Qualifications
  3. Learn About Partnership
  4. The Selection Process

Orientation

The first step to learning about homeownership

Register For An Orientation

Learn how you can transform your family’s life and become a homeowner. Register for your orientation here:

Learn more

Qualifications

The second step to learning about homeownership

Review Qualifications

Homebuyers are selected based on need for improved housing, a steady source of income, and willingness to partner with Habitat for Humanity of Orange County. 

Learn more

Partnership

The third step to learning about homeownership

Learn About Partnership

Partnering with Habitat OC means you agree to a one-time equity contribution. This is investing a commitment of time, not just money, into helping build our community. 

Learn more

Process

The fourth step to learning about homeownership

The Selection Process

The final step, our Selection Committee recommends you to our Board of Directors where to decide final approvals! Find out more about our selection process here
