  • THE AUE TEAM

  • SPECIALTY CONSTRUCTION

    AUE has many specialized talents, but one project type we are well versed in are Aquariums. AUE has designed aquarium projects all over the world and provides designs for unique systems such as large fish tanks, Concrete displays, or specialty tanks.

  • OFFICE & MIXED USE

    AUE has designed several offices and Mixed use type projects.

  • Mullions

    AUE is uniquely specialized in the design of mullion systems. AUE works with mullion manufacturers to provide efficient mullion systems to projects of all sizes including schools, high rises, etc.

  • Light Gauge Framing

    AUE in specialized in metals stud structural design. AUE works directly with several metal stud contractors and provides metal stud bearing wall buildings and metal stud infills for high rises.

  • INDUSTRIAL

    AUE provides designs for industrial type structures such as mobile switching centers for cell phones or water treatment vessels.

  • Housing & Hospitality

    AUE has provided services for many housing and hospitality functions. AUE designs efficient wood over PT structures allowing more height with less cost than typical mid-rise or high-rise construction.

  • Community Spaces

    AUE has designed several community centers and community type projects. These projects require more interaction with the architect and the public’s desire for a spectacular space for all interested parties.

  • Banking

    AUE is specialized in banks and credit unions. We understand some of the specific issues that impact banking type building such as vaults, SD boxes, security and other heavy teller equipment. We have designed banks all over the country

  • Retail

    AUE is specialized in retails design and works on retail roll out projects throughout the US and Canada. AUE retailer clients include name brands such as REI, Carter’s, Hugo Boss, Sur La Table and more.

Welcome to AUE

AUE is a structural engineering firm celebrating 20 successful years. In 2003, we opened our first office in Washington State and in 2013 expanded to New York. In addition to our two offices, we have remote employees across the US. This enables us to provide quick service to our clients for projects in North America as we are licensed in all 50 states in US and several territories in Canada.

We encourage you to explore our website to learn more about our firm and project experience. We thank you for your interest.
