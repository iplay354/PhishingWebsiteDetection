Our Impact

Shop with Purpose, Empower Lives.

We are dedicated to making a real difference in the world. Every time you choose Tillage, you are joining us in the fight against human trafficking.

We donate 3% of every purchase to non-profit organizations that are on the front lines of rescuing and rehabilitating trafficked humans.

When you shop with Tillage, you’re not just buying high-quality, sustainable clothing—you’re making a powerful statement. You’re helping to bring freedom and empowerment to individuals who need it most.
