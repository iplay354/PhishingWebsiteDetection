Back to School

Posted by Just Asia 12:53 PM


Hey Y'all!
      
      Welcome back to another school year!  First off; I pray that each and every one of you have an amazing year!  I've met all of my kiddos; and I have a HUGE range to deal with this year.  If this is your 1st time visiting my blog; WELCOME!  I am an early interventionist; I teach students with special needs; and I am itinerant.  Today I am here to share what I put together for this upcoming week for my 3 year olds.  Here's what I've come up with:

I put together this very simple picture schedule.  If they can get through all 4 of these things that will be great!  If not; each student will do what they can and we will work up to it over time.  


Picture Schedule (3 year olds)

I chose this story because it is hands on and it gives busy little hands something to do to help them engage:



Next; I want them to engage in 1 or more given tasks.  I chose object to picture matching using toys; a red and yellow color sort; and a craft using pre-cut shapes.

Object to Picture or Picture to Picture Matching

Red & Yellow Color Sort

School Bus Craft

Once they are done with their tasks; they get to choose.  I made a Free Choice board using a lot of Cause & Effect toys with the exception of Mr. Potato Head and Magnetic Blocks.  I included a prompt to encourage speech:
Mostly Cause & Effect Toys Choice Board

That's all I have for now.  I've included the templates for the Picture Schedule, Tasks, and Choice Board HERE.  If you would like the pattern for the craft; it is a FREE DOWNLOAD in my TpT store; just click on the photo.

Thanks so much for taking the time to read my post.  Have a happy and blessed school year!
