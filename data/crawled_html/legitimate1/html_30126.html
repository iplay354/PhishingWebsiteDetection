ALYSSA NICOLE

EST. 2010

Recognized as one of the Best Bay Area Designers by CBS News, Alyssa Nicole is one of the leading designers in San Francisco, California. Alyssa Nicole was founded by mother and daughter Sonia and Alyssa Casares in 2010. Alyssa Casares, the designer behind Alyssa Nicole, began designing collections at the age of 16. Alyssa, a graduate of the Fashion Institute of Design & Merchandising, began her line as a handcrafted ready-to-wear collection. Alyssa Nicole has evolved into a demi-couture collection handcrafted in San Francisco and Los Angeles, California.

COLLECTION

The focus for Alyssa Nicole is to design collections that embody an element of exclusivity and high quality. With each collection, Alyssa sets out to capture romance by designing ladylike silhouettes and using lightweight fabrics to create graceful movement. Alyssa strives to design garments that embody true elements of femininity. Alyssa Nicole is focused on American made quality craftsmanship with each collection limited run with handcrafted pieces. Alyssa Nicole’s collections have been sold exclusively online and in designer boutiques nationwide.

FEATURES

Alyssa Nicole has been featured in Forbes, Vogue Italia, ELLE UK, WWD, WGSN, Refinery 29, Racked SF, San Francisco Chronicle, SF Gate, Modern Luxury Brides California, Girls Life Magazine, 7x7, Teen Vogue, CBS San Francisco & on style blogs worldwide. In 2019, Alyssa Nicole became a Macy's Fashion Incubator designer in residence at Bloomingdale's within San Francisco's Westfield Centre. With exclusive demi couture collections each season, Alyssa Nicole has become one of California's most coveted emerging designers. Alyssa Nicole is sustainably and ethically handcrafted in California.

PRESS

ATELIER

Our collection is designed and drafted by hand in our Atelier. From sketch to production, our process is haute couture design. Alyssa Nicole is limited run with handcrafted pieces.

MADE-TO-MEASURE

We offer made-to-measure special order pieces from our collection. Customize fit by sending us your measurements. Don't know your size? Book a fitting.

SUSTAINABILITY

The textile industry uses a variety of chemical dyes and treatments, which are hazardous to our health. Alyssa Nicole is sustainably sourced, designed out of recycled deadstock fabrics.

ETHICALLY MADE

We produce a limited run of our made-to-order collection each season. Alyssa Nicole is carefully handcrafted in our design Atelier in San Francisco, California.
