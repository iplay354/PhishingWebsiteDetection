NEUROANATOMY is an annual journal of neuroanatomy and neuroscience. It is mainly published as an electronic journal in Adobe PDF format. Although all the articles' copyright holder is neuroanatomy.org, NEUROANATOMY is an open access journal. The term open access gives the right of readers to read, download, distribute, copy, print, search, or link to the full texts of the articles free of charge.

This website is a scientifical rather than a consultation tool. The authors have, as far as it is possible, taken care to ensure that the information given in this website is accurate and up to date. However, neither the authors and the editors, nor the publisher, can accept any legal responsibility for any errors or omissions that may be made in the course of implementation of the information contained herein.

MESSAGE FROM EDITORS #1

Welcome to the web site of our journal,

NEUROANATOMY is a peer-reviewed journal. Articles for the journal are chosen by the editors and published in English to make it easier for international use.

The basic aim of the journal is to present a medium where all people concerned with neuroanatomy will have a chance to introduce their manuscripts in an annual period.

The journal welcomes again manuscripts [Review Articles, Case Reports, Letter to the Editor] by January 1st 2023, after ten years of cessation. The accepted manuscripts will be published [e-publication only] on the 11th Volume.
