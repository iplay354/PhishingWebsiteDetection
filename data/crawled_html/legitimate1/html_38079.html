    About Us

    Custom Cabs Inc. was established in 1998 and since then we strive everyday to provide complete solutions for residential and commercial elevator needs providing the highest standards in Engineering, Design, Manufacturing, Service and Installation. We are the premier elevator designer, manufacturer, and installer of high-end custom elevators. Round Glass elevators, curved glass entrances, or any other shape. We fabricate elevators with any exotic materials, for interior and exterior along with wrought iron elevators.
