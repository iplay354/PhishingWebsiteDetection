Nicky Hager

Nicky Hager is an author and investigative journalist based in Wellington, New Zealand. He has written seven best-selling books.He specialises in investigating hard-to-document subjects, such as 

  • the military
  • intelligence agencies
  • public relations activities and
  • the unseen sides of politics.

This site contains a collection of his writing including articles, books, speeches, op-ed pieces and book reviews.
