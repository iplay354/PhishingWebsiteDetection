How to use temporary tattoos

1. Clean your skin with a damp cloth and make it free of all oil, hair and makeup. Make sure to dry your skin afterwards.

2. Remove the clear, protective top layer from the temporary tattoo.

3. Press your tattoo firmly onto clean, dry skin with the design side down.

4. Press the wet cloth against the back of the tattoo. Make sure the cloth is well wetted.

5. Wait for at least 30 seconds before removing the paper. There's no need to rush.

6. Rinse your brand new tattoo gently with water for the best possible results.
