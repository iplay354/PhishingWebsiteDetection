Our Philosophy

At Daisy Designs, we are committed to creating exceptional and eco-friendly wedding stationery that brings your vision to life. Whether traditional, whimsical, or anything in between, we offer custom and semi-custom options that celebrate your unique love story. We partner with couples to craft memorable stationery that not only reflects their style but also honours our commitment to sustainability.

A WORD ABOUT SUSTAINABILITY

At Daisy designs, we have a strong focus on the environment. We carefully select papers from our suppliers that meet our standards, with sustainability in mind. We also use printers with similar values. This all helps us reduce our carbon footprint. We will consistently base our actions on sustainability principles to protect our planet and future.
If you follow along with us, you will see we have a passion specifically for birds and animals. Birds are good indicators of environmental health, all while being pollinators, insect and rodent control and seed dispersal.
