About Me

I prefer to use natural light, but as winter approaches and the cold creeps in, it becomes a challenge. Most photo shoots in the winter months will take place in the morning or early afternoon to try to capitalize on natural lighting. My toolbox includes Nikon Speedlights and White Lightning Strobes, so if the light isn’t cooperating, I got you covered!
