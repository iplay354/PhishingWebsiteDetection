What is Notis?

Notis is an engaging, fun-to-use app developed from the student perspective while supporting the PBIS/MTSS framework by providing you with a tool for managing Tier 1 behavior interventions and more.

Forget about using multiple apps to manage positive behavior interventions, student engagement, and community involvement. Notis brings it all together into one place with an intuitive interface.

  • Help students get involved beyond the classroom.
  • Encourage and acknowledge positive behavior.
  • Streamline student rewards and interventions digitally.

These benefits – and many more – are yours with Notis!
