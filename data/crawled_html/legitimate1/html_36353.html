Timeline of Legal Changes in US Sports Betting

Understanding the legal landscape helps you see why offshore sportsbooks remain popular, even as regulated markets expand. Here’s a timeline highlighting significant events in US sports betting legislation:

  • 1992 – PASPA Enacted: The Professional and Amateur Sports Protection Act (PASPA) is signed into law, effectively banning sports betting nationwide, except in a few states, such as Nevada
  • May 2018 – PASPA Overturned by Supreme Court: The US Supreme Court ruled in Murphy v. NCAA that PASPA is unconstitutional, allowing individual states to legalize and regulate sports betting
  • 2018–2021 – First Wave of State Legalization: States like New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Indiana, and Colorado launch legal online sportsbooks. Offshore betting remains the only option in states without legislation
  • 2022 – Expansion Continues: New York, Louisiana, and Kansas join the list of states with legal online sports betting, creating more competition for offshore sites
  • 2023–2025 – Legislative Push and Slow Progress: Several states, including Texas, California, and Georgia, propose bills to regulate sports betting. However, political gridlock and lobbying slow the pace, keeping offshore sportsbooks relevant in many regions
