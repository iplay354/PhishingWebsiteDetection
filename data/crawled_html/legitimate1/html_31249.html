Acentech At A Glance

Acentech is an employee-owned company of acousticians, engineers, scientists, and technical consultants, providing acoustics, audiovisual, telecommunications, security, and vibration consulting to clients across the globe. Acentech’s multi-disciplinary team brings extensive experience to critical areas of design helping our clients find solutions for their most complicated challenges.
