Hi, I am Tamara Chloé

Welcome to TC Style Clues, my online home where style, travel, and timeless living come together. I’m Tamara, a writer, singer, and traveler based in Amsterdam and Indonesia. My world is inspired by Parisian apartment chic, the old-world charm of English manors, and the romantic details of the Victorian era. Here, you’ll find my loves: occasional fashion that feels eternal, travel stories from Bali and Indonesia’s hidden Kei Islands, and the art of slow living at home, surrounded by antiques, history, and beauty that never goes out of style. This isn’t just about fashion or travel—it’s about weaving a life that feels as rich and meaningful as the stories we carry. Welcome in- Make yourself at home!
