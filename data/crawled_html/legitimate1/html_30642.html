The Bluebook of Pianos is an invaluable handbook for anyone who owns or plans to buy a piano. After an illustrated discussion of piano structure that explains the workings of both grand and upright pianos, the book gives careful advice for purchasing a new or used piano. Attention is given to important considerations such as price, tone, construction, consumer ratings and brand name recognition.

Our  information starts with people,  not computers,  and  understanding how you are going to use it is important so we  specialized in learning  about your  needs,  your  uses,  and finally,  designing  these pages  to  address your  situation. Within the limited confines of a single website it is impossible to pass on to you all of the piano knowledge that we should like you to possess.  No one has all of the answers, but we do have an extensive piano information library from which to do piano research, and we offer it to as wide an audience as possible. ~
