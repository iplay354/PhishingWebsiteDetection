Devonport is....

... one of the most historic neighbourhoods in Plymouth. We are located to the west of the city, with extensive frontage onto the river Tamar. This is one of Plymouth's character areas, even our names are quirky, such as Mutton Cove, Pottery Quay and North Corner

This website explores Devonport's history, covering .....

  • Modern History highlighting the decade 2000-2010, under the 'Millennium' tab.
  • With all earlier history under the 'Historic' tab.
