  1. City of Holland Resident Survey - Please Participate

    Hope College’s Frost Center for Social Science Research is conducting a resident survey on behalf of the City of Holland. In December, text messages with links to this survey will be sent to randomly selected residents. Read on...

  2. Two Dates for 2026 Coffee with Council

    Please join us for Coffee with Council on Wednesday, January 7, 5-6 pm at City Hall, OR Saturday, January 10, from 9 - 11:30 am at the Holland Civic Center. Light refreshments will be served. Read on...

  3. FY 2025 Popular Annual Finance Report

    The Finance Department is pleased to present the Popular Annual Finance Report for Fiscal Year 2025. (PAFR) It is a condensed, easy-to-read, and understandable financial audit report for the City. Read on...

  4. Christmas Tree Pick Up & Extra Holiday Trash

    Curbside pick-up for Christmas tree recycling runs December 29 through January 9, 2026. Residents may drop off Christmas trees at Bouws Pool December 29, 2025 - January 16, 2026. Excess holiday trash pick-up is available December 26 - January 2, 2026. Read on...

  5. Happy Holidays

    Non-emergency City offices, including City Hall, will be closed on December 24 & 25 and January 1. We wish everyone a safe and happy holiday season! Read on...
