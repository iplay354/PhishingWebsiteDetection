A Romantic Adults Only Hideaway

Our Guest Rooms

Each room at our Harwich Bed and Breakfast is its own little retreat; stylish, comfortable, and made to make your stay on Cape Cod unforgettable. Set inside a beautifully restored 1850s sea captain’s home, this luxury adults-only Bed and Breakfast in Cape Cod brings historic charm together with modern comfort for the ultimate romantic getaway. We’ve designed each of our eight rooms with relaxation in mind. You’ll find plush furnishings, luxury linens, and en-suite bathrooms with custom-tiled walk-in showers stocked with BeeKind toiletries.

Guests can choose from three unique room styles: our Luxury King Rooms, Deluxe King Rooms, or the Cozy Queen Room. The Luxury Kings come with all the extras: Gas fireplaces, private patios, vaulted ceilings, or even a deep soaking tub for a little extra indulgence. Some also feature a dry bar with coffee and tea service, a beverage fridge, or a rooftop deck overlooking the rose garden. All rooms include thoughtful touches like personalized climate control, wireless chargers, USB & C ports, and sound machines, because it’s the small comforts that make a big difference in your Cape Cod getaways. 

Perfect for a romantic getaway or a relaxing New England escape, our boutique Cape Cod Bed and Breakfast offers warm hospitality and an experience you simply won’t find at a big hotel. Relax, recharge, and discover the best of Cape Cod with us. Book directly with us for exclusive specials, and come see why so many couples call their stay here unforgettable.

Gourmet Breakfast & Heated Pool

From the moment you arrive in Harwich, you’ll feel the easy charm of our Cape Cod Bed and Breakfast. Every detail here is intentional, from the warm welcome to the way breakfast is plated each morning. Each day begins with a gourmet breakfast made just for you, featuring fresh eggs and herbs from our gardens. Maybe it’s a flavorful frittata, homemade granola, or a warm pastry right out of the oven, but whatever’s on the menu, it’s always served with creativity and attention to detail.

The atmosphere of our Boutique Inn is exactly what a Cape Cod getaway should be: relaxed, refined, and a little bit indulgent. Outside, inviting spaces call you to slow down. Stretch out by the seasonally heated pool, wander through the gardens, or cozy up by the outdoor fireplace as the evening cools. Every corner of this luxury Bed and Breakfast reflects the casual luxury you crave from your New England getaways. We make it easy to unwind, reconnect, and remember what a real vacation feels like.

Personal Concierge

From the moment you arrive, we’re here to make your Cape Cod getaway as easy and memorable as possible.
