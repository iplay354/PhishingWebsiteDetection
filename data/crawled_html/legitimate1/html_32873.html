    Healthcare services for a connected healthcare system

    At Carelon, we make connected care possible so that better outcomes, affordability, and experiences are easier to achieve.

    What we offer

    Imagine a whole system for whole health


    With our approach to healthcare services, health plans, employers, government agencies, and more can solve a decades-long challenge: how to improve outcomes and experiences while lowering costs in a fragmented healthcare system.

    Together, we’re doing it through connection. We bring together clinical and operational expertise, advanced technology, and partners in new ways so organizations can impact whole health positively and affordably for individuals and communities.

    Your frontline and back-end capabilities for truly connected care


    Streamlining the healthcare system requires a new perspective and set of capabilities. Ours support a personalized, integrated approach to healthcare that’s long been missing — from frontline care delivery to back-end operations.

    Advance the goals you've been working toward

    With Carelon, better health, costs, and experiences don’t have to be goals for the distant future.

    Experience health that’s more affordable


    With the power of our clinical and operational solutions, challenges that can be costly for organizations and individuals, like cancer, back pain, and severe mental illness, become easier and more affordable to solve.

    Strengthen care through connection


    In a connected healthcare system, collaboration and integration take the lead, replacing fragmentation. Our whole-health solutions and unique partnerships help organizations reduce complexity and deliver care more efficiently — without point-solution fatigue or legacy silos.

    Improve everyone’s every day


    In healthcare, every experience matters. Through our data-driven insights, technology, and associates, the compassionate, personal experience that everyone in healthcare — from care providers and payers to researchers and individuals — deserves becomes possible.
