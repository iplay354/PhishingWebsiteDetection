Commercial Awning & Canopy Specialists

Bespoke commercial awning solutions hand-built in our London workshop

Back in 1894, John Dean, the founder of Deans Blinds, built the world’s first commercial awning. We are very proud of this fact, and his classic Victorian-style canopy, remains a popular choice in our range. These days, we call it the Deans Shop Awning. Today, Deans Blinds are the UK’s leading supplier of commercial awnings and canopies, supplying UK-wide retail chains, shops & stores, restaurants, hotels and pubs. Our extensive range of awnings and canopy solutions can be totally customised for your business requirements.

Inside Deans

Deans – London’s original blindmakers since 1894

Deans of Putney have a history that spans three centuries as the leading UK manufacturer and installer of blinds and awnings. The company was founded in London by a policeman’s son, Tom Dean, in 1894 and was later taken over by his brother John.
