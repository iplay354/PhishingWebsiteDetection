ORANGE FRAZER PRESS

Your Personal Independent Book Publisher In Ohio

Talk With Our Publisher

Orange Frazer Press, Ohio’s leading book publisher, is an independent book publisher located in Wilmington, Ohio. They are a distinguished local publishing company founded over three decades ago, and have worked tirelessly to become one of the most respected book publishers in Ohio and beyond. We offer custom book publishing services, self-publishing services, and more. Orange Frazer has worked with authors and clients near and far to publish:

  • Anniversary books

  • Children's books

  • Coffee-table books

  • Museum books

  • Business books

  • Corporate Histories

  • Memoirs

  • Fine art books

  • Sports books

  • Cookbooks

  • College and university histories

  • and more!

At the very core of our operations, Orange Frazer Press shines as a beacon of publishing excellence. What sets us apart isn't just our unwavering commitment to quality craftsmanship, but our genuine passion for nurturing heartfelt connections with both authors and clients. We take pride in helping each and every one of our clients’ work shine through creative, detail-oriented, and thoughtful self-publishing services.
