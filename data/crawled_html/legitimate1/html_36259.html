Welcome to Eastern Seals (UK) Ltd website

We provide a wide range of O-Rings including FEP Encapsulated O-Rings, Vulcanised O-Rings and O-Ring Cord. Eastern Seals supply countless industries around the world from Automotive and Aerospace to Oil and Gas.
