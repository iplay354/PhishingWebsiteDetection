From Barewall Art Gallery

HOW TO BUY ART

Buy online, over the phone call 07932 717 718 or visit the art gallery Tues to Saturday 11am to 3pm daily. We are also open at other times by appointment. You can also enjoy free delivery on orders over £35 across the UK.

We also have a mailing list. Please join for invites to art events, opening nights, artist studios and talks. If you enjoy art then please support the gallery we do invest back into our town centre including our building restoration, putting on talks and walks, and publishing books on the artists, also we support and encourage young up and coming and contemporary artists through our work with external partners on exhibitions like the annual Three Counties Open Art show at the Burslem School of Art, with Appetite, Keele Arts, British Ceramics Biennial and Clay College.

We also manage artwork exhibited for sale at the award winning Lunar Restaurant, World of Wedgwood, Barlaston.

About Us

Barewall Art Gallery based in Burslem, Stoke on Trent established in 2010, offers British 20th century modern and contemporary; art, ceramics and sculpture by artists from and connected to Staffordshire, Cheshire and the North. The gallery also has a hand selected range of gifts and artist signed prints.
