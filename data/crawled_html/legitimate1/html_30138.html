"Ok - So What is Amazon S3?"

  • Amazon S3 (Simple Storage Service) is the worlds largest cloud hosting service provided by Amazon.com - perfect for video

  • Amazon S3 is free to join, and is a pay-as-you-go sevice, meaning you only ever pay for any of the hosting and bandwidth costs that you use, making it very attractive for start-up companies looking to minimize costs

  • On top of this, the fully scalable, fast and reliable service provided by Amazon, makes it highly effective for marketers all over the world

  • Amazon offer the first 5 gigabytes for Free!

  • Amazon also provides the CloudFront service. Your files are copied to cloud servers around the world and so your viewers access the nearest copy, speeding up loading times and playback.
