Pinocchio

30 December - 4 January
R CAP
★★★★★ ‘A beautiful, brand-new musical brimming with fun and magic’ (WhatsOnStage) has arrived in the Globe. Bring the whole family together for this ★★★★ ‘absolute treat’ (The Telegraph) by Charlie Josephine and Jim Fortune, directed by Sean Holmes.
Festive Events

A Midsummer Night's Dream

29 December - 31 January
AD BSL R CAP
Performed for the first time in the Sam Wanamaker Playhouse, discover the shadowy underbelly of one of Shakespeare’s most magical plays. Headlong Artistic Director Holly Race Roughan directs, with co-direction from Naeem Hayat.
Info & Tickets
Sam Wanamaker Playhouse

The Tempest

17 January - 12 April
AD CAP R BSL
On an island, four lost souls tell a story of past wrongs, revenge and desire. Award-winning theatre-maker Tim Crouch directs Shakespeare’s tale of power and freedom.
Info & Tickets
Sam Wanamaker Playhouse

Deep Azure

7 February - 11 April
R CAP AD
Tristan Fynn-Aiduenu makes his Globe directorial debut with this lyrical epic by the late Academy Award-nominated actor and writer Chadwick Boseman.
Info & Tickets
Sam Wanamaker Playhouse

Playing Shakespeare with Deutsche Bank: Romeo and Juliet

5 March - 12 April
CAP BSL AD R
Our critically acclaimed production returns to mark the 20th year of Playing Shakespeare with Deutsche Bank. This 90-minute 'fiery-footed thriller' ★★★★ (The Guardian) is created especially for young people and is the perfect introduction to Shakespeare for everyone.
