What Is Emergency Plumbing?

Emergency plumbing is the service you call upon when a plumbing issue is imminently causing, or potentially causing immediate and lasting damage to your property, jeopardising your safety or affecting your daily life. Unlike other plumbing mishaps which require less urgency, plumbing emergencies require immediate attention, which is why we offer our emergency plumbing services at all hours.

Many homeowners don’t know when a plumbing problem constitutes an emergency. However, there are a few situations where calling in an emergency plumber is a must! For example:

  • Blocked Sinks, Drains, Toilets and Pipes: It is also vital that you call an emergency plumber for sewage overflow, since not only can this create a health hazard through raw sewage back ups, but it also puts one of the most commonly used fixtures in the home out of use, which you need to get back up and running again as soon as possible.
  • Burst Pipes (Or Risk of Burst Pipe): Where pipes are already burst or at high risk due to them being frozen or otherwise, you should call an emergency plumber. Burst pipes carry the risk of flooding which can cause lasting damage and make life very inconvenient. Although you can shut off your water valves to limit this, our emergency plumbers can provide a sustainable, long term fix to the problem.
  • Leaks: Whether it is a leaking radiator or a gas leak, you could be at risk of injury and your property could be caused irreversible damage. You need to contact an emergency plumber as soon as you notice a water or gas leak in order to minimise the risk and make your home a safe place again.
  • Central Heating Problems: Central heating breakdowns can be especially urgent if you are living with elderly people, those that are suffering from illness or have children – particularly if your heater breaks during the cold winter months. For this reason, calling an emergency plumber is always a good idea to get your central heating back up and running.

When responding to a plumbing emergency, you should take care to follow procedures that were discussed with you in your most recent maintenance appointment, such as turning off certain valves. Although, the chaos of a burst pipe or leak throws you off, our friendly customer service advisors can guide you through the early steps before sending our emergency plumber to tackle the tough bits!
