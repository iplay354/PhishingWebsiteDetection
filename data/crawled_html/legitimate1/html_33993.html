WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

100% ORGANIC
HEIRLOOM QUALITY
DESIGN CONSCIOUS
ETHICALLY MADE

Our products are made with super-soft certified organic cotton and non-toxic, eco-friendly dyes. Our toys are made from untreated Indian hardwood or hand-knit with organic cotton yarn.

Heirloom quality construction creates products that aren’t only meant to be used, they’re meant to be loved. Durable materials and timeless design make them great as gifts and keepsakes.

Our eye-catching designs stand out from the crowd. Each collection puts a unique spin on a concept, whether based on a current trend or beloved baby classic. They photograph well and look great online.

Our workers, mainly women in developing countries, are paid a living wage while in a safe workspace. We help create conditions that enable them to build better lives for themselves and their families.
