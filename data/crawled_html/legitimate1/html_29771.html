Greenville Technical College Celebrates Graduates at Commencement

(12/18/2025) Commencement for Greenville Technical College graduates was held at the Bon Secours Wellness Arena on Thursday, Dec. 18. Included were 397 participants, 20 military veterans, 173 graduates with a GPA of 3.4 or higher, and 18 graduates with a perfect 4.0 GPA. Additional students graduated but did not participate in the ceremony with 1,379 awards projected this semester and an expected 1,216 unduplicated graduates.

Triple Triumph: Three Members of One Family Graduate Together

(12/18/2025) When the audience at commencement hears Carver Blassingame’s name on December 18, a graduate will step forward and proudly receive the diploma he’s worked years to achieve. A few minutes later, the same name will be called again, and although the audience may think there’s some mistake, a second handshake will take place, marking another degree conferred.
