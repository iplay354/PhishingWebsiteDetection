WELCOME TO ASHLEY INN & SUITES

YOUR COASTAL RETREAT HOTEL IN LINCOLN CITY

BOOK NOW

BEST RATE GUARANTEED

Discover the charm of Ashley Inn and Suites, a top choice among Lincoln City hotels. Nestled just off the Oregon Coast Highway, our coastal retreat hotel welcomes you with warmth and comfort from the moment you step into our inviting lobby.

Each of our recently renovated spacious rooms boasts contemporary furnishings, refrigerators, microwaves, flat screen TVs, and complimentary in-room WiFi. Experience ultimate relaxation with our incredibly comfortable mattresses, plush pillows, and thoughtful amenities designed to make your stay unforgettable. As a leading option for Lincoln City hotels, we offer free high-speed wireless internet in public areas, complimentary parking, an indoor saltwater swimming pool and hot tub, 24-hour front desk, daily housekeeping, and much more.

Explore the outdoors and the wealth of activities Lincoln City has to offer. The Ashley Inn and Suites is perfectly situated for easy access to the city's exciting outdoor adventures. Families will love the seven miles of sandy beach, the longest stretch among Oregon coastal towns. Discover tide pools, play in the sand, and hunt for treasures. Keep an eye out for gray whales along the Oregon Coast year-round.

In addition to outdoor fun, Lincoln City offers numerous indoor attractions. Test your luck at Chinook Winds Casino or shop to your heart's content at the Lincoln City Outlets. Browse antique stores, watch local artists blowing glass, or explore the many gift shops throughout town. Indulge your taste buds at a variety of restaurants featuring Northwest specialties, Oregon Coast seafood, international cuisine, and barbecue.

At Ashley Inn and Suites, one of the top-rated Lincoln City hotels, we pride ourselves on offering competitive rates, personalized service, and exceptional amenities along the Pacific Coast. Allow us to be your relaxing home-away-from-home while staying in the Siletz Bay area. We look forward to hosting you as our guest!
