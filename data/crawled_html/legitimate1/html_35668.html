    Nasher Prize

    Established in 2015, the Nasher Prize is an award dedicated exclusively to excellence in contemporary sculpture. Winners of the Nasher Prize embody the tenets of the award: to recognize a living artist whose body of work has had an extraordinary impact on our understanding of sculpture.
