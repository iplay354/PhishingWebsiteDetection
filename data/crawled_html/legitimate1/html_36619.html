The natural home for graduates

What’s special about Wolfson?

Research-led

At Wolfson College, graduates, postdoctoral researchers and Fellows are united by the common pursuit of research in an environment dedicated to supporting their work.

Inclusive

Since its foundation, Wolfson College has worked to provide an inclusive home for exceptional scholars who might otherwise have faced barriers to postgraduate study.

Sustainable

Wolfson is committed to tackling the climate emergency through its actions, investments and research community. We are proud to be the first zero carbon Higher Education Institution in the UK.
