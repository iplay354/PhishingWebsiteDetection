Contact

I look forward to hearing about your plans for your wedding day. Please get in touch to arrange a visit where you can look through some whole weddings, start to finish. We can work out what style of photography you enjoy the most and discuss your ideas over some lovely coffee, red wine or home-made cookies. I am a Panel Member, Judge and Advisor to the Guild of Wedding Photographers, recently receiving an ‘Outstanding Contribution’ award.

Prices

Wedding photography packages tailored to your unique requirements. Details can be found here. Coverage starts at the Bridal preparation stage and goes through until just after the dancing starts. The fully processed photographs are provided on a USB device with rights to copy and print. The images are uploaded to a web ordering service so that your friends and family can order their own pictures and products. Special prices for winter and midweek weddings.
