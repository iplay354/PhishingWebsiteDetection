The Moorings Portmagee, on the Skellig Ring

Thank you for taking the time to visit us here at The Moorings Portmagee. Award-winning boutique hotel accommodation comprising The Bridge Bar and The Moorings Restaurant. Everything you could possibly need under one roof.

We’re the perfect place to base yourself on the spectacular Wild Atlantic Way to explore:

  • Boat tours to Skellig Michael – World-renowned Unesco World Heritage site and filming location for Stars Wars The Force Awakens and The Last Jedi.
  • The Ring of Kerry
  • Valentia Island
  • The Skellig Ring Drive – one of Lonely Planet’s worldwide 10 Best in Travel 2017
  • Ballinskelligs, The Glen and Saint Finian’s Bay beach

Our bar is open for breakfast, lunch and dinner as well as the best of Irish traditional music and song.

We’re so excited to welcome you all to our special corner of South Kerry, with spectacular scenery on our doorstep and a wealth of history, culture and local traditions all around us.

Gerard & Patricia

Whether you are culturally curious or love great escapes, the Skellig Coast has something for everyone.

The Wild Atlantic Way is one of the wildest, most enchanting and culturally rich coastal touring routes in the world. Wherever you travel along the Wild Atlantic Way you’ll find magic, adventures, history and beauty in abundance.
