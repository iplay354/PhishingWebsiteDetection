About

Inspired by over 30 years of living in Bali, Walker Zabriskie brings his unique line of handcrafted furniture and home decor to Palm Beach's iconic Worth Avenue. Influenced by many years of travel, Walker's style has evolved to fully embracing natural and sustainable materials. We welcome you to the experience!
