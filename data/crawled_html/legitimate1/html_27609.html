Discover the Top Websites Ranking on Google Worldwide

The table below contains the 1000 most ranked websites on Google across 75 countries. It is created based on our extensive database of over 270 million domains enriched with ranking and traffic data from our Google SERPs database. Relying on the data in this table, you can compare leading websites by the number of keywords they rank for on Google and their estimated organic traffic volume.

The table data is automatically updated every 90 days.

Check the Top 1000 Websites in the US And 74 More Countries

By default, the table lists the top 1000 websites worldwide. However, it can also provide you with region-specific stats for free. Select a country from the drop-down list above the table, and hit Search to see the top 1000 websites in a particular location.

About the data in this table:

  • Rank is established based on the the number of keywords the Domain ranks for on Google.
  • Keywords in SERPs correspond to the number of keywords contained in our SERPs database for which the Domain is ranking organically.
  • Estimated organic traffic displays the approximate number of monthly visits all organically ranked keywords bring to the Domain. Estimated organic traffic is calculated based on our proprietary formula.

Uncover Top Websites Ranking In Your Niche For Free

Obtain more specific data on domains from a particular Google product and service category using DataForSEO Labs API.

With the Domain Metrics By Categories endpoint, you can receive a list of the most popular domains in the target niche along with their historical ranking data from Google SERPs, valuable current and historical domain metrics, such as ETV, impressions ETV, estimated paid traffic cost, the total count of SERPs that contain returned domains, and more.

To get this data for free, register with DataForSEO and head to the DataForSEO Labs section of our API Explorer. Then, make a request to the Domain Metrics By Categories endpoint by selecting it under Functions.

In the request settings, indicate a target category(-ies), set up preferred sorting (e.g., by organic Estimated Traffic Volume, or ETV), and specify other necessary parameters.
