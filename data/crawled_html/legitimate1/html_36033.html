  • Why Easy Weddings

    Search Suppliers

    Choose from over 6,000 wedding businesses across the UK. From wedding photographers and videographers to marriage celebrants and wedding dress stores, these suppliers have been extensively reviewed by thousands of couples just like you — simply visit their profiles to read the reviews. Browse our wedding directory to get started.

  • Explore Venues

    From historic palaces and countryside manors to modern greenhouses and luxury hotels, our extensive wedding venue directory includes over 1,000 wedding venues throughout London, Surrey, and beyond. Our local venue experts can help you book your venue — we'll gather pricing, coordinate site visits, and get your venue sorted at no cost to you.

  • Planning Tools

    Our simple, free wedding planning tools will help you stay on track whilst you plan your wedding. Your virtual wedding to do list, budget calculator, guest list, RSVP tracker, seating planner, and countdown to your wedding day can all be accessed in one place once you sign up for an account with Easy Weddings.

  • Mobile Apps

    The Easy Weddings on-the-go wedding planning app is available on the App Store and Google Play. You can use the free app to manage your wedding to-do list, budget, guest list, supplier browsing, vision board, and take our style quiz. Download the app today to start planning your wedding from the palm of your hand!

  • Advice and Inspiration

    Our comprehensive collection of over 4,000 wedding articles and blog posts covers everything you need to know about planning your wedding. Topics include real wedding coverage, advice from wedding experts, style trends, celebrity weddings, tips on crafting a good toast, dress inspiration and so much more.
