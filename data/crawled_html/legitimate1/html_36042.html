Legal centre

The Young Workers Centre is an accredited community legal centre in Victoria. We provide free advice and representation to young people under the age of 30 with employment law issues.

The legal centre will be closed for new enquiries from Monday 22 December 2025 to Monday 26 January 2026. We will be available to existing clients from Monday 19 January 2026.
