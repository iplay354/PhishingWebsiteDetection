Shop with Confidence at

Long Jewelers

With over 47 years of experience in the jewelry industry, David Long has unparalleled expertise in the fine jewelry and time pieces. An award-winning jewelry designer in his own right, David Long founded Long Jewelers with the mission of bringing the Virginia Beach Community fine jewelry pieces of the highest quality available. Keeping this mission alive, year after year, David has been exceeding customers' expectations with exceptional customer service and exemplary inventory. This tradition of excellence has resulted in Long Jewelers growing into one of Virginia's largest fine jewelry retailers. Named 2017 Retailer of the Year by the Retail Alliance, clients can be certain that they will receive the best experience and quality at Long Jewelers.

Your premier jeweler, Long Jewelers takes great pride in being an authorized retailer of many of today's most popular designers, as well as for offering a wonderful assortment of comprehensive fine jewelry services to keep your pieces brilliant and beautiful.
