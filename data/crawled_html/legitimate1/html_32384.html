2026 HTCC Calendars Have Arrived!

We are excited to announce that the 2026 HTCC calendars are now available! These beautifully designed calendars feature images of our temple deities, important Hindu festivals, and key events throughout the year, based on the Vedic Panchangam.

You can click here to request your new calendar. If you are already a lifetime member of HTCC, we will ship the calendar directly to your address at no additional cost. For non-members, please visit the calendar page to learn more about obtaining one.

Note: All current HTCC members and donors will receive printed copies automatically. If you have any questions, contact us at officemanagers@htccwa.org.
