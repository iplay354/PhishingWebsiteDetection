OUR PROCESS

Your dreams designed and built under one roof.

The DESIGN + BUILD Process fluidly combines the design and construction phases so the complete remodeling costs are inclusive of all materials and clear prior to signing an agreement. Gone are the steps of interviewing and coordinating with multiple players to the remodeling process. The organic synergies of having designers, project managers, and construction managed under one roof provides one point of responsibility and accountability for your project from start to finish, making for happier customers and a smoother process.

The Cabinetry is located in Norwell, Massachusetts, and provides home remodeling services throughout Boston, the South Shore, and Cape Cod. The Cabinetry Team of experienced designers and craftsmen uses a DESIGN + BUILD Process that allows homeowners to benefit from a more streamlined and cost-effective home remodel project. Explore our portfolio of remodeled kitchens, bathrooms, mudrooms, pantries, and more. The Cabinetry is woman-owned and has been providing impeccable, customized design and service since 1960. The Cabinetry is conveniently located between Boston and Cape Cod on the South Shore in Norwell, Massachusetts, near Hingham, Scituate, Hanover, and Cohasset.
