Welcome!

Since the early days of Guitar Craft up until today, a vast array of composers and ensembles from all over the world performed and recorded many hours of guitar music, from solo pieces to chamber configurations and circulations.

We are gathering here new and classic pieces in order to make the repertoire of the Guitar Craft Tuning -also known as New Standard Tuning- available to everyone.

We invite you to find the music here, participate and support the community.

Music Contributors

Fumihito Hatano

Japan

Okinawa

This piece was extracted from the coda section of the first version of "Okinawa", and rearranged. The music was composed based on the Okinawan scale and was recorded in 1999 by TUKTUK, a duo of Fumihito Hatano and Shinkuro Matsuura.

Contact: https://zumguitars.com
Donate: https://patreon.com/ZumGuitars

Robert Fripp

UK

Eye of the Needle

One of the founding themes of Guitar Craft. Studio recording from "Show of Hands" (1991) by Robert Fripp & The League of Crafty Guitarists.

California Guitar Trio

Japan/USA/Belgium

Yamanashi Blues

The classic hit by the most prominent professional spinoff of the early GC years. Check their website!
