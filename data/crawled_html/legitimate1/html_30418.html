Cine EI and how to use it.  I have prepared a series of guides to using Cine EI mode on the PMW-F5, PMW-F55, and PXW-FS7. Check out the links at the top of the page for guides to these cameras and many others.

The site is run and maintained by DP Alister Chapman, this web site is primarily aimed at users of Sony’s XAVC, XDCAM and FS products (PMW, NEX, PXW etc), but you will find a wide range of information on all aspects of television and video production, so no matter what camera you use there is likely to be information helpful to you. The Tech Notes page  has a list of many easy to understand articles on technical issues and the science of shooting better video.

In Alisters Blog there are articles on camera setup, technical features, equipment reviews and industry news. If there is anything you would like to know about do ask using the blog comments or through the forum.

Alister attends mosts of the major trade shows, including CabSat, NAB, IBC, Broadcast Asia and many others.

What is XDCAM? XDCAM is a name used by Sony for many of its digital camcorder products. It originally referred to Optical Disc camcorders, then later solid state cameras using SxS memory cards. It is still in use for a few cameras today.

XDCAM-USER.com is run entirely by Alister Chapman. It is not directly affiliated to Sony or Sony’s XDCAM product line. The views expressed in the blog are those of Alister and other blog contributors. You can also follow Alister on Twitter and on Facebook for all the latest news and updates.

Alister runs tours and workshops for individuals and groups both large and small. He is an excellent teacher and works with Sony, F-Stop Academy and Santa Fe Workshops as well as other educational establishments to provide  quality video production training and education around the world. He also runs his own workshops and adventure tours. You can find more information on  these here.

Alister is not a Sony employee. He is a freelance DoP/DIT and consultant. He is a Sony Ambassador and ICE (Independent Certified Expert) and because of his technical expertise and practical experience (over 30 years in broadcast TV and film production) is asked from time to time to work closely with Sony on training events and trade shows as well as being paid to write articles, produce videos and other training materials for Sony.
