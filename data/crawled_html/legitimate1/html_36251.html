Modern and elegant kitchen, bath and closet cabinetry. Timeless furniture and accessories.

Located in Chicago’s River North’s design district, our showroom is a classic Chicago brick and timber loft. The warm wood beams and distressed brick is the perfect backdrop to showcase our sleek, modern and custom designed European kitchens. We additionally carry bath cabinetry, walk-in-closets systems, lighting, furniture, wood flooring and doors.

Earth-friendly & well-engineered to last.
