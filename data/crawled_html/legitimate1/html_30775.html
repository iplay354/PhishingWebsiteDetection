About Eastwicks Paper and Ink

Welcome to Eastwicks Paper and Ink, a creative studio where handmade greeting cards and simple, joyful crafting come together. I’m Katy Ingram, and every card I create begins with intention — thoughtfully designed, carefully crafted, and made to brighten someone’s day.

Born from my love of paper, color, and the joy of creating, Eastwicks has grown into a place where I share both my designs and the process behind them. Through step-by-step video tutorials and online classes, I make cardmaking approachable, fun, and inspiring for creators of all experience levels.

Each card is crafted with care, blending quality materials with imaginative, modern design. I believe a handmade card carries a warmth that store-bought simply can’t replicate.

Most of all, Eastwicks Paper and Ink is a welcoming community for anyone who loves to make things by hand. I’m so grateful you’re here — and excited to share this creative journey with you.
