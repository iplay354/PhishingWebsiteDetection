Sensory leads the industry in wake word detection, enabling seamless voice activation across devices. Our AI-powered solutions for speech recognition, emergency vehicle detection, domain-specific voice assistants, biometrics, natural language understanding, and more offer unparalleled accuracy, privacy, and customization for your brand.

Award-Winning On-Device Speech-to-Text

Sensory’s TrulyNatural™ is the on-device STT engine that makes voice interfaces faster, more private, and more cost-efficient. By acting as a smart gateway to the cloud, it enables seamless LLM integration without the latency, expense, or privacy risks of streaming audio.

Reinvent Driving with AI

Sensory Automotive is a full voice and vision suite of AI technologies that operate efficiently in the car (without connecting to a network) and enables hands-free, distraction-free driving. Custom wake words (hotwords) can be used to trigger the automotive head unit, phone, onboard commands, and more, enhancing almost every aspect of the driver experience.

Build a Private and Secure Smart Home

Sensory’s software solutions provide consumers with the convenience of natural language for voice control and feature access in a simple and intuitive way without requiring private data to be sent to the cloud. For manufacturers, Sensory’s custom wake word technology supports private brands with an out-of-the-box working experience that requires no apps, wifi, or extra hardware purchases.

Consumer & Home Electronics 

Functional, Flexible, Embedded Speech Technology

Sensory’s low-power, high-accuracy speech recognition technology is ideal for manufacturers of hearables, wearables, fitness accessories, and wireless headphones with an intelligent voice user interface. Sensory’s always-on wake word (hotword) technology is customizable, ultra-efficient, and supports multiple wake-up phrases for seamless integration.

Banking, Medical, & Enterprise

Improving the user experience, but not at the expense of privacy.

Privacy concerns are understandably the subject of many conversations today. Our embedded software, or AI on the edge, addresses these consumer concerns in a multitude of ways. The high accuracy of our wake word (hotword) technology minimizes the likelihood that devices are listening when you don’t want them to. Our TrulyNatural software enables devices with an extremely intelligent natural language user interface while keeping voice data private and secure; voice requests never leave the device, nor are they ever stored. At Sensory we are committed to improving the user experience, but not at the expense of privacy.
