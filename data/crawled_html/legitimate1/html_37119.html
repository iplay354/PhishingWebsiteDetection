WELCOME

We, Medhavi & Dhaval Kotecha, wife and husband duo, are Jacksonville Wedding Photographers and founders of DKreate Photography. We are based out of Jacksonville Florida and love to travel around the world for our wedding assignments. Our style of shooting is a mix of documentary and storytelling, where we strive to capture the emotions that unfold during the wedding.

We have shot weddings in the majestic palaces of Udaipur and Jaipur, the serene beaches of Goa and Thailand as well as in locations like Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, and Delhi. We have been blessed to shoot weddings in Ponte Vedra, Orlando, New York City, Miami, Cape Cod, Ypsilanti, Columbus, Atlanta, and our home, Jacksonville.

May it be a small intimate wedding at your farmhouse or a grand big fat wedding at a luxurious destination, do write to us and we would love to be a part of your big day!
