A Magic Place to Live

Pelican Waters has long been regarded as Queensland’s waterfront lifestyle of choice and a selection of residential home sites, including rare ocean access, and waterview land for sale on the Sunshine Coast.

People are moving to Pelican Waters not only for lifestyle, but as a business change.

A 35 year project to date, the Pelican Waters residential community has sold more than 2,950 home sites since first released to the Sunshine Coast market in 1989.

With the establishment of the Greg Norman designed Pelican Waters Golf Course and waterfront home sites with direct ocean access, the estate has attracted buyers from all around Australia and overseas.

    Sunshine Coast Living at its Best

    Pelican Waters is not a thoroughfare like most other developments on the Sunshine Coast and is a destination in its own right which brings the residents together to form a close knit community.

    Located within close proximity to Brisbane and international and domestic airports, Pelican Waters has its own facilities including shops, schools and child care, aged care, medical centres, and dining.

    Caloundra is within five minutes drive and Pelican Waters has easy access to the Sunshine Coast University Hospital, Maroochydore, Mooloolaba and Noosa. Bribie Island, Moreton Island and the Glasshouse Mountains are all close by.

    Pelican Waters Marina & Marina Village

    Pelican Waters continues to evolve as one of Queensland’s most desirable destinations to live, work and play with the unveiling of the master planned community’s breathtaking Marina and Marina Village development, bringing with it a wealth of exciting new lifestyle, residential, and business and investment opportunities.

    Years in the planning, the Pelican Waters Marina and Marina Village will create a vibrant new heart for the southern Sunshine Coast, with a dynamic mix of dining, retail and commercial opportunities surrounded by a modern mixed-density residential development featuring cutting edge urban design and a relaxed surf-side atmosphere.
