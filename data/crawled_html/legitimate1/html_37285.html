Meet Kenji

Multiple James Beard Award-Winning Chef, Author, and Food Nerd

Multiple James Beard Award-Winning Chef, Author, and Food Nerd

I’m J. Kenji López-Alt — chef, writer, and a guy who can’t stop asking why. I explore the science behind everyday cooking and share what I learn through books, videos, and live events that help home cooks cook smarter, not harder. My work has earned James Beard Awards, a New York Times bestseller (or three), and a whole lot of messy dishes. Whether I’m testing burger techniques, diving deep into wok science, or cooking for my family, my goal is to bring more joy into your home kitchen and empower you to cook the way you want to.
