‘You Won’t Be Able to Stop Us’: Pan-Africanist Kemi Seba Defies International Arrest Warrant

By Sputnik, Dec. 18, 2025

The Pan-Africanist, who had supported the coup attempt in Benin, stated in a video that he had already taken “precautions” “for some time.”

Kemi Seba, whose real name is Stellio Gilles Robert Capo Chichi, fiercely criticizes President Patrice Talon, particularly his ties to his “master,” Emmanuel Macron.

“Patrice, you’ve issued an arrest warrant against us, but you’ve picked the wrong Black man. I’m telling you plainly: you will never stop us,” he declared.

On December 12, Benin’s Court for the Repression of Economic Crimes and Terrorism issued an international arrest warrant against the activist. He is wanted for “apology for crimes against state security, incitement to hatred, violence, and rebellion.”
