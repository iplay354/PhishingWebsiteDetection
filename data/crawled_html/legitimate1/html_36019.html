What is Allpoint?

What is Allpoint Network? Allpoint is a network of over 55,000 ATMs worldwide that provide surcharge-free cash access for participating banks, credit unions and prepaid card providers.

No, Allpoint is not a bank. We are a network of ATMs that banks, credit unions and prepaid card providers can join to provide their cardholders surcharge-free cash access.
