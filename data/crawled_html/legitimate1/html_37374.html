New Cosmic Collection

Cosmic Collection

A celestial journey through color and movement  

Step into a world where celestial phenomena inspire earthly elegance. The Cosmic Collection is a curated series of five colorways that reimagine cosmic events as immersive palettes that infuse spaces with emotion, energy, and story. Each transforms a vast selection of our acoustic solutions – linear baffles and ceiling systems, ceiling tiles, acoustic pendants – into surfaces alive with depth and luminosity. Let the Cosmic Collection, a study in contrast and fluidity, imbue your next project with a sense of otherworldly wonder. Start your journey here! 

Polina Uplight

Discover the new Polina Uplight, a minimalistic indirect pendant that complements the Polina family of acoustical pendants and sconces. At once novel and timeless, the hexagonal luminaire with a nominal 10" diameter highlights ceilings and interior architecture while providing comfortable indirect lighting. Learn more about Polina Uplight.

Where Lighting and Acoustic Innovation Comes to Life

Step inside our 300,000-square-foot, vertically integrated campus on Chicago’s South Side and discover how we bring lighting and acoustic solutions to life. From empathetic research and collaborative design to hands-on prototyping, precision manufacturing, and rigorous testing—every step happens under one roof.

At Focal Point, we don’t just make products—we craft lighting and integrated acoustic systems that enhance architecture and human well-being. Our commitment to quality, speed, and sustainability ensures that every luminaire and acoustic solution we deliver is built with purpose and pride, right here in the heart of America.

Manufacturing in Chicago since 1994

Our architectural luminaires and acoustic solutions are designed, engineered, manufactured, and shipped from our vertically integrated Chicago facility. As such, we can support projects that must meet the requirements of various programs such as the Buy American Act (BAA), Department of Transportation (DOT) Buy America, or Build America, Buy America Act (BABA).

Saros Duo

A tandem healthcare luminaire supporting wellness and recovery  

Inspired by the restorative effect of gazing at the horizon and based on the latest research on integrative lighting, Saros Duo™ provides a novel option for healthcare patient rooms, examination rooms, and ambulatory care areas of healthcare facilities.

The tandem overbed luminaire offers multiple light sources which may be programmed to support circadian entrainment, examination modes for precise assessments, reading, and nightlight, thus meeting the needs of patients, clinicians, and caregivers.

Discover Saros Duo now!

Plyta

Playful Form. Serious Performance.

Plyta introduces a playful perspective on ceiling design, blending soft-formed acoustic shapes, expressive color, and integrated illumination into these versatile pendants. Let Plyta Circle, Ovo, and Triangle bring light, pops of color, and sound clarity to any space. Discover Plyta now!

Declare

Several product families, offering a cohesive package to outfit all areas of a project are currently Declared, including: ID+ 3.5” Downlights, Wall Washers, Adjustable Accents, and Cylinders, Equation 2 architectural troffers, Seem 2 linear luminaires, and Seem 2 Acoustic, lit and unlit acoustical baffles.

Quickship Program

Optimized to deliver more value than ever

Our Quickship Program makes it easier than ever to select products that ship in 5 or 10 days for entire projects. Consistent options offered across product families for architectural troffers, linear luminaires, cylinders, and downlights, as well as acoustic tiles and baffles give you the selection you need for those quick-turn projects.
