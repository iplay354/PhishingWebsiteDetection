Options organized around phases of care

Reach Out offers a step-by-step guide to helpful resources for survivors at any point in their healing journey.

Find resources on and off campus

Resources are specific to your school and local community. Each resource includes a description, complete contact details, and hours. Subcategories make it easy to find resources at the local, state, and national levels.

Know your reporting options

Reach Out simplifies reporting by organizing your options into easy to understand categories. Simply tap on any of the categories to learn more about the reporting process and who you can contact to file a report.

Quick access to your school's website and policies

Do you have questions about Title IX or sexual misconduct? Links offers one tap access to digestible and relevant information from your school's policies and procedures. Free!

For Students

Navigating the various options of support off-campus can be complicated. That's why we created the Reach Out Network. Members of the Reach Out Network are clearly marked within Reach Out, and provide custom mobile pages with their message tailored specifically toward the high school and college populations. These mobile pages are intended to provide a welcoming environment and address the needs of a student survivor.

CappMail

Anonymous Messaging

More Info

CappMail

  • CappMail allows students to anonymously connect with designated resources via email routed through our servers.
  • It can be difficult for survivors of sexual violence to share their experience with someone they hardly know. CappMail is another way for survivors to ask questions and get advice.
  • Preserving anonymity ensures that survivors are in control. And when survivors feel in control, research has shown, they are more likely to seek help.
  • Anonymity also creates additional opportunity to build trust, and when a survivor trusts a resource, they are more likely to come out of the shadows and benefit from the services offered.

For more information contact info@capptivation.com.

Reach Out Reporter

An education and reporting tool for Responsible Employees and Campus Security Authorities.

More Info

Reach Out Reporter

  • The app is tailored to the specific needs of various categories of employees, including: campus security authorities, responsible employees, confidential employees, mostly-confidential employees and non-responsible employees.
  • Reach Out Reporter augments Reach Out with special training modules, a reporting tool and a unique real-time feature called the Guide.
  • Reported incidents are routed to the appropriate authorities as designated by the college and, if applicable, in compliance with any memoranda of understanding with third-party resources such as local police.
