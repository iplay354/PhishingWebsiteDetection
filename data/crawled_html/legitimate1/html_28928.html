New Model 4414 Stacker Trailer

We're taking hauling to the next level with the introduction of our new Model 4414 stacker trailer. This stacker trailer is inspired by the Model 4410 and its unique exterior smooth rivetless side sheet design. The Featherlite Model 4414 stacker trailer with internal lift allows two levels for hauling all types of cargo, including cars, side by sides, motorcycles, golf carts and more. The triple axle 4414 is available in multiple lengths with an interior height of 11’ and exterior width of 8’6”.

Millions of Trusted Miles
