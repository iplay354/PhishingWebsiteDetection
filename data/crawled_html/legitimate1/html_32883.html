Our Jewelry Services in Davenport & DeWitt

Keep your beloved jewelry looking beautiful with our expert jewelry services. Our Davenport and DeWitt stores offer a wide range of jewelry services so that you can enjoy your favorite pieces for years to come.
Jewelry and Watch Repair Services

When your jewelry needs maintenance, trust our skilled jewelers to get the job done right. We provide the best jewelry and watch repair service in the area so you can enjoy your favorite pieces for years to come.  Battery is $15. Cost for links starting at $25. *See store for luxury watch needs.*

Jewelry Restoration

Have a treasured piece of jewelry that needs some TLC? Our team can restore your beloved pieces to their former glory.

Appraisals

To protect your jewelry, we offer comprehensive appraisals for insurance purposes. Our experienced gemologists and appraisers provide accurate appraisals so you know the value of your beautiful pieces.

Sell Your Gold

Don’t let your old gold jewelry sit in a drawer any longer! Bring it to Necker’s Jewelers and we can provide you with fair compensation for any gold items you no longer want. We routinely offer the highest rates for gold, silver, and diamond buy-backs in the region, and we will be happy to help you get the most value!
