Himmapan.com is my personal project. I'm fascinated with Thai Culture and Art History, especially about Himmapan creatures; mythical creatures that exist only in Asian legends.

Mission: With hours of research time on Art History and Himmapan creatures, we’d like to develop the most extensive and visually engaging resource about Himmapan creatures through the use of modern technology and our understanding.
