Students: Scholars and Changemakers

Our students come from all over the world. They’re undergraduates and graduates, balancing school and work. They’re professionals taking continuing education programs to strengthen their skills for their next role. They’re on campus and online. What unites them all is their drive and persistence. They come to NYU SPS to tackle challenges, learn by doing, and further their careers. Our students are here to push beyond the classroom and transform their ambitions into achievements.

View student stories

Faculty: Industry Leaders, Classroom Mentors

NYU School of Professional Studies faculty hail from backgrounds as varied as the students they teach. Many currently work in the industries our students aspire to, including companies and organizations like Audible, Tishman-Speyer, JP Morgan Chase, the United Nations, Marriott, and more. They leverage their invaluable insights and experience to create learning opportunities that are practical, profound, and plugged into the latest trends.
