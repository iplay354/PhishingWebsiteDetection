Your Go To Hometown Furniture Store

Railway Freight Furniture is a hometown, family owned furniture store providing you with good southern hospitality, customer service and fantastic prices on name brand furniture. At Railway Freight Furniture our goal is to help you create a home, an office, a dorm room, or a man cave (whatever your case may be) with a look that suits your personal needs.   We have experienced sales associates that will guide you in selecting pieces that will create an inviting atmosphere that you can be proud to share with others.  At Railway Freight Furniture we keep up with the latest styles and are excited to provide furniture selections from Liberty Furniture, Ashley Furniture, Coaster Furniture, Catnapper Furniture and much more! We also strive to assist you after the sale with delivery of your selected furniture and any other needs or concerns that you may have once your furniture has been delivered. 

Every day we hope to be YOUR furniture store. We value your business! We enjoy creating relationships with our customers and would be thrilled to assist you for countless years in your furniture purchases. Please look over our webpage and dream until your heart is content. Then, come by and select those pieces that will make your personal space the sanctuary it should be.
