Asterisk is the ideal private event venue for corporate gatherings, nonprofit fundraisers, educational events, weddings, and more! With 10,000 square feet of event space, your event can be everything you have dreamed of at an affordable rate! Our light and bright Main Hall creates a stunning and fresh atmosphere to gather your guests in celebration. At night, our state-of-the-art lighting system can bring wonder and enchantment to your party. In addition to the Main Hall, our venue also offers a Rooftop Patio that provides a unique, urban setting for distinct, smaller events, cocktail hours, and after-parties.
