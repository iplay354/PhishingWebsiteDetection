About EZY SWITCH

EZY SWITCH is an SMS Controller (text controller) which allows you to remotely control generators, water pumps, critical plant & equipment, monitor security systems, alarms and has many more applications.

Have a Security System?

Save on monthly monitor fees by receiving your own monitor notifications via text to your mobile.

Own a Holiday Home?

With EZY SWITCH you can remotely turn on your heating and hot water, welcoming you to a warm home when you arrive.

Work Rurally?

Save yourself the travel time and monitor farm machinery and milk sheds remotely, whether you are on or away from the farm.
