Campuses

Antioch University spans coast-to-coast, offering in-person, online, and low-residency programs our locations fosters a unique community of learners driven by a shared mission: to empower individuals dedicated to advancing social, economic, and environmental justice.

Whether students are in person or online, they become part of an interconnected network of changemakers determined to win victories for humanity. United in purpose, our campuses offer diverse, flexible pathways that prepare students to lead in their communities and make a meaningful difference on a global scale.
