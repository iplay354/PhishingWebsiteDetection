Stay & Explore

Our newest travel style, Stay & Explore, combines the benefits of an escorted tour and the comfort of a central location. Stay in just one or two hotels to experience the best of the region without having to pack and unpack. Enjoy the freedom to tailor your experience to your preferences, with optional tours or explore on your own.
