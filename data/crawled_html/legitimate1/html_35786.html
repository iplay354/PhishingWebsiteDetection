What's Happening Around the Company
Michigan approves Related Digital’s Saline project

Michigan is leading the way in responsible digital infrastructure development. On December 18, the Michigan Public Service Commission approved Related Digital’s Saline project, enabling a multi-billion-dollar investment that will deliver a more than 1-gigawatt data center campus for Oracle as part of OpenAI and Oracle’s Stargate partnership. This action supports thousands of union construction jobs in Michigan and allows full construction to begin as scheduled in Q1 2026, unlocking nearly $5 billion in construction work and subcontracting opportunities for Michigan-based companies.

Related Beal President Kim Sherman Stamler honored with 2026 Pinnacle Award

The Greater Boston Chamber of Commerce Women’s Network has awarded Related Beal President Kim Sherman Stamler the 2026 Pinnacle Award in the category of Achievement in Management – Private, elevating a total of nine outstanding women professionals who have demonstrated significant achievements in business, government and management. Stamler and the other winners of this prestigious award will be celebrated on January 30 at the Pinnacle Awards ceremony, which will bring together elected and appointed officials and business community leaders representing diverse industries from the region.

Related Argent moves forward with next residential building in London's Brent Cross Town development

Related Argent and JV partner Barnet Council have announced that Plot 24, Brent Cross Town's next residential building, has received approval to move forward. Designed by Cowen+Partners and situated within a vibrant 180-acre mixed-use community, Plot 24 will deliver a range of exceptional new residences, including affordable housing, alongside a variety of retail units located on the neighborhood's new high street.

Learn More
Related Companies celebrates Riverwalk Commons East on NYC's Roosevelt Island

On November 19, Related Companies joined Hudson Companies, The Roosevelt Island Operating Corporation (RIOC), state and city officials and community members for the ribbon cutting of Riverwalk Commons East, a brand new park and dog run located on the east side of NYC’s Roosevelt Island. The new park features a combination park and dog run with swings, picnic areas and beautifully landscaped green spaces, which are all open to the public.

The opening of Riverwalk Commons East follows this year’s completion of luxury residence Riverwalk Heights, which marked the culmination of the vibrant Roosevelt Island Southtown development. This multi-decade collaboration with our partners includes 9 residential buildings, which offer around 2,000 residential units across approximately 19 acres.

Affordable housing applications now open at Willets Point Commons

On December 12, Willets Point Commons started accepting applications for affordable rental residences through NYC Housing Connect. This first phase of Willets Point Commons is offering 880 new all-affordable residences: a first, monumental milestone toward 2,500 affordable, workforce and moderate income housing at Willets Point.

Related Affordable unveils revitalized affordable residences in Tennessee

Related Affordable has completed comprehensive renovations at its first three preservation projects in Tennessee: Cumberland Crossing, Midtown Estates and Horizon Square Apartments. Related Affordable has provided residents with modernized apartments and upgraded common spaces, and most importantly, they have preserved each community's long-term affordability for years to come.

Related California breaks ground on Alveare affordable community in Los Angeles

On November 5, Related California joined Housing Authority Of The City Of Los Angeles (HACLA) and the California Department of Housing & Community Development for the groundbreaking of Alveare, the first phase of a mixed-use, multigenerational community in downtown Los Angeles. Once complete, Alveare will offer 303 homes for families, seniors and people in need of permanent supportive housing, becoming the largest affordable housing development in downtown LA.
