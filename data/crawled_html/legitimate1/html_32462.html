Starmatter

The patented hubless rear-wheel motor delivers 737 lb-ft of torque directly to the road. By removing the chain, it cuts maintenance, sharpens control in every corner, and gives Verge its unique look.

The TS Pro’s chassis is designed to hold our 20.2 kWh battery, the largest on the market, delivering you up to 217 mi of range. When it’s time to recharge, NACS fast charging adds 124 mi in less than 35 minutes.

Every part of Verge is built to be serviced, repaired, or upgraded with ease. A modular architecture means less downtime and more riding, wherever you are. Because a superbike should last for years, not seasons.

Starmatter is Verge’s intelligent software platform, the brain of your superbike. Over-the-air updates keep your TS Pro or TS Ultra improving as you go. With every ride, your Verge becomes smarter and more your own.
