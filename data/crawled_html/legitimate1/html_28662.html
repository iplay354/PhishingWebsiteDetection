DeSales is a regional Catholic elementary school, located in the Town of Lockport NY, within the Diocese of Buffalo. The school is a designated STREAM school, a center of excellence that inspires individual students to reach their greatest potential, both inside and outside the classroom, while providing students with the fundamental building blocks to be innovative, critical-thinkers, and responsible future leaders. DeSales offers a nurturing community dedicated to advancing the highest academic standards and an exceptional faith-based education, rooted in the values of love, compassion, mercy, sacrifice and justice as taught by St. Francis de Sales.
