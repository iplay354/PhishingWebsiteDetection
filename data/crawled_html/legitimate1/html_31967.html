WHAT WE DO

Workplace support

At R&CA, our in-house team provides practical advice and guidance on workplace matters, including wage tables, industrial relations (IR), and compliance requirements. If you’re dealing with staffing challenges or need clarity on employment obligations, we’re here to ensure your business stays informed, protected, and operating with confidence.

Advocacy

We represent the hospitality industry at all levels of government and in the media, advocating for better conditions, practical reforms, and meaningful change. Your challenges are our priority, and we work to ensure your business is supported by fair, future-focused policy.

Training & upskilling

We offer access to a wide range of industry-specific training programs - from food safety and compliance to leadership and business skills. Building your team or sharpening your own skills? Our training is designed to set you up for long-term success in this fast-moving industry.

Member community

Connect with like-minded hospitality professionals who understand the day-to-day realities of the industry. Share insights, seek advice, and be part of a supportive network that recognises achievements and celebrates key milestones along the way.

Exclusive deals

Save on essentials - from insurance and tech to suppliers and services. With access to exclusive member-only benefits. We also help you cut energy costs with access to competitive rates and smarter energy solutions tailored for hospitality businesses.
