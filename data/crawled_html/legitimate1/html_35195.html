Our DNA

Believe.

We offer the security of a globally recognised and well established curriculum combined with contemporary, dynamic and cutting-edge practice. 

Learn More

 

Inspire.

We believe healthy, authentic, responsible young people develop through a broad spectrum of activities outside the classroom.

Learn More

Succeed.

1991

1991

Innovative education based on a strong tradition

Bavarian International School opened its doors for the first time on the 18th of February 1991. The school celebrated its 30th anniversary in 2021.

2

2

One school, one spirit, two campuses

BIS offers two campuses: the Haimhausen Campus, approximately 15 km north of Munich and the City Campus located in Munich-Schwabing (since 2016).

75%

75%

A truly international school

With around 75% of students from international families BIS is a truly international school. 25% come from local, German families.

1,260

1,260

Great community from age 3 to 18

1,260 students attend BIS under the motto “Believe. Inspire. Succeed.” Approx. 680 students in the Secondary School, and 320 (City Campus) and 260 (Haimhausen Campus) in Kindergarten & Primary School.

70

70

 

A school for global citizens

The 1,260 students at BIS come from 70 different nations and speak around 50 different home languages. They are all united by the international, open mindset.

35

35

Academic excellence

BIS once again proved its reputation as one of the leading international schools in Germany and Europe. The graduating class of 2023 of 82 students achieved an IB Diploma (DP) average grade of 35 points (pass rate: 96%), which is almost five points above the global average.

180

180

International top teachers

The 180 teachers at BIS are among the best in the world. They are innovative, digitally affine, constantly developing their skills and come from more than 30 different nations.

22

22

Small class sizes for great education

22 is the maximum class size in the Early Childhood Centre (Kindergarten & Pre-school) and 24 in the Primary and Secondary School up to Grade 10. In Grades 11 and 12 there is a maximum of 18 students.

80

80

Flourishing personalities in a holistic way

BIS offers so many opportunities outside the classroom for personalities to flourish holistically. We offer around 80 After-school activities per week.
