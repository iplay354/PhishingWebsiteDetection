Isn't it time you explored the artistic side of Photoshop? 
Here's just one example of what's possible!

On the left is the straight out of camera image.  On the right, the final art piece, beautifully created and hand painted in Adobe Photoshop. There is NO freehand painting here.  The scene is a dollhouse, the subject is life-size and the two photographs are composited together and painted. This would be considered an "advanced" piece - however, the skills necessary to create this type of work are attainable!

I understand how overwhelming it can feel, but there is a shortcut to success.

  • Strategically mastering just FIFTEEN Photoshop tools will completely transform your art forever!

  • Fast track your way to your maximum potential
