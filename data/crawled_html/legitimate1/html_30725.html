Multi-layer security for comprehensive, compiler-based protection

Guardsquare’s products incorporate the full spectrum of mobile app security technology, including:

  • Multiple layers of protection that reinforce one another and redundantly protect your app through defense in depth.

  • Automatically injected RASP checks that are inserted into random locations in your code and at a higher volume than is possible with manual injection.

  • Polymorphic protection which ensures that your mobile app security defenses automatically change with each build, forcing attackers to start over.
