BEYONDSHIPS CRUISE DESTINATIONS AND PORTS

If getting there is half the fun, the other half must be experiencing the places where the ships go.  Beyondships' cruise destination profiles are written from a cruise traveler's perspective.  They seek to provide basic information about the destinations.  In addition, there are links to other websites that may provide additional information including cruise line sites, which have information on the shore excursions usually offered in that port.  Finally, our cruise destination and shore excursion feature articles and visual pieces seek to bring the experience to life by focusing on specific aspects of visiting these destinations.
