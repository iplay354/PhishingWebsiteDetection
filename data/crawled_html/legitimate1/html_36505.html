Hillsdale’s Heritage

Enriching young minds since 1898, the Hillsdale Public Schools provide a nurturing environment where every child can thrive. Our dedicated educators cultivate a love of learning for students in pre-kindergarten through eighth grade, fostering a strong foundation for future success. Upon graduation, students continue their academic journey at Pascack Valley High School, a renowned regional high school district offering a comprehensive and challenging curriculum.
