ABOUT IN-SIDE

IN-SIDE is a digital platform designed to connect clients and lawyers in a virtual way so that legal services can be purchased digitally and provided using technological tools. This platform is, at the same time, a social network, a document manager, a process manager, a CRM, an electronic billing and payments module and a digital marketing tool.
