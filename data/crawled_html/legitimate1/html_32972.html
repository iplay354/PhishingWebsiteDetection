At Larkin University and our Colleges of Pharmacy, Physician Assistant and Biomedical Sciences it’s all about impacting Lives – yours, your community, and the people and patients you will serve.

In a sea of sameness, Larkin is different. The University and its College of Pharmacy, its School of Physician Assistant Studies and College of Biomedical Sciences were borne out of a health care delivery pioneer – Larkin Health Systems – with deep roots and commitment to the needs of local communities they have served for two decades. For these Larkin institutions it has never been about pivoting to meet or exploit a trend or a perceived opportunity, but rather a singular focus on healthcare where everything done in the classroom, in a lab, on a clinical rotation, or on a research initiative begins and ends with a patient in mind and serving the greater good.

We invite you to explore all that our Colleges and Programs have to offer and how our innovative, collaborative educational approaches best prepare our students to contribute to today’s healthcare system team-based care models.

Larkin University Launches New Physician Assistant Program 

The Accreditation Review Commission on Education has granted accreditation – provisional status – to Larkin University for their new Physician Assistant Program (ARC-PA)

“This is a transformative milestone for Larkin University,” said Dr. Rudi Ettrich, President of Larkin University. “We are thrilled to expand our commitment to healthcare education and look forward to training the next generation of physician assistants who will serve our communities with skill, compassion, and purpose.”

This approval allows the University to officially launch its PA program with the first cohort of students beginning October 1, 2025, with an entering class size of up to 50 students.

Larkin University continues to grow as a leader in health sciences education, with this new program playing a key role in expanding access to high-quality healthcare training in South Florida and beyond.

ABOUT LARKIN UNIVERSITY

Striving to have a positive impact on the future.

Our educational programs, research and scholarly activity, and development of solutions promote the common good of health care and society  – this is a fundamental building block of our being. 

Within an exceptional community of students, faculty, and administrators, our culture and environment stimulates new thinking and a better understanding of the communities our students will serve, while developing leaders that will improve human healthcare worldwide.

Testimonials from our students & alumni

"Larkin University is an amazing school to learn and become a better student! The professors and dean want you to succeed. They guide us down the right path. The curriculum was very precise and challenging. It allowed me to step out of my comfort zone and unlock a higher potential. Larkin University makes us students feel like a family, they help us achieve our dreams."
Mariam ZakirClass of 2022
Larkin University has been a blessing for me. I did not know what my plans would be for the year 2021-2022. I was working full-time, finishing up a post-bac program, and trying to prepare to apply to dental schools. Seeing my grades and scores were not where they needed to be, I had to research applying to numerous master programs around the country. Larkin was one of those schools and I was able to get accepted into the program. They gave us the option to do online or in-person learning for the year, and I was able to do online due to having to stay at my current job and near my family. I was even able to visit my professors and classmates a few times in MIA! The learning style challenged me academically in a way I've never really seen before. Our professors were great. They challenged us in studying and learning not only as a student, but as a clinician as well. Everything we did was health-based. Completing this program is one of the most rewarding accomplishments for me. I am very grateful for how close we are with our professors and dean of the university. It has helped me learn new study habits, test- taking strategies, and critical thinking. I look forward to hearing about the continued growth of the College of Biomedical Sciences 
Jordan Duru Class of 2022
This past year as a student at Larkin University has not only equipped me with the knowledge and habits to be successful at the next step in my journey, but it also instilled an even greater sense of confidence in me. Larkin’s Master’s program has cemented my drive to become an adept PA; while simultaneously allowing me to prove to myself that I have the discipline to overcome any obstacle that I may encounter. I was never one to have relationships with my professors, however I will always consider Dr. Perez and Dr. Bidot mentors. They have left a lasting impact on me. I truly believe that this program has given me a competitive advantage, and I have been left with such appreciation for Dr. Echols, Dr. Perez, Dr. Bidot, and Larkin University.

My name is Mateya Carson and I graduated from the Larkin University Biomedical Master’s degree program in Summer 2022. I relocated from Charleston, SC and had never even visited the city of Miami, FL. I pursued my master’s only after a stressful gap year where I felt lost in where I fit in the pre-medical crowd. Larkin University greatly exceeded my expectations in every way. Although the school is still establishing their name in the realm of postbacs, the administration and staff welcomed me with open arms and supported me throughout my year. And this was achieved with not only their resources, but their sound advice and their genuine care for the student’s education and personal wellbeing. I feel that this program is one that is necessary in these times, an undiscovered gem because although they have a set mission and protocol, they are still very open to making adjustments that work for each incoming class. Aside from the lectures and presentations I believe we all had a bond with our professors and each other that made learning from them much more enjoyable. Transparency and consideration for all is an element they hone, and it is reflected as the students also develop a sense of belonging and the mindset that their excellence is achievable.
Mateya Carson class of 2022

I gave them a call and ended up talking to Dr. Echols for 3 hours. Once I made the decision to attend Larkin, I was pushed by faculty members just as Dr. Echols said I would be. Having experienced physicians as professors gave me a better understanding of what to expect in the near future. The small class sizes at Larkin made me comfortable to ask questions and work with my classmates. If you’re a pre- med or pre-dental and you want to prepare yourself for the rigors of professional school, Larkin University is the place for you!
