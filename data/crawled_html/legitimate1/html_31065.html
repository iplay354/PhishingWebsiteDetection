Use Fire Risk Assessment Mobile App

If you are a fire risk assessor or property owner then you could save time and money using our Fire Risk Assessment App rather than using a traditional paper-based assessment.

Fire Risk Assessment Software

Developed in cooperation with Fire Safety Professionals who have over 60 years of experience between them, the app is based upon the British Standards PAS-79 methodology with interactive control measures.

What is the Online Fire Safety Assessment Tool?

As per UK government rules, all non-domestic premises owners must have a fire risk assessment performed every year. Paper-based checklists are time-consuming and laborious, but we have developed an alternative.
The Fire Risk Assessment App records each fire safety parameter on the app. An assessor can download the app onto either their Android or iOS system and carry out single or multiple fire risk assessments. All the data is then saved in the cloud and can be accessed multiple times.

Registration and user management

Register on the app and carry out a fire risk assessment.

Fire risk assessment companies and fire risk assessors

If you are an independent fire risk assessor or a company, you can use our free trial and generate one free report. You can also purchase our gold and silver plan starting £10.

Based on the fire safety checklist, questions appear one by one on the app. You can insert comments in each section and an action plan is generated for each missing fire safety parameter.

The report automatically informs you what is present and what is missing in the premises. An action plan can be made based on the generated report and overall risk rating received in the assessment. You can download a pdf report of this at any time and this is stored in the cloud.
