Reasons To Love Libra Fine Arts

  • Hand Drawn With Love

    Every illustration is carefully hand-drawn, giving each card its own unique and personal touch. Created to inspire genuine, meaningful connections.

  • Proud To Be British

    We’re passionate about shopping local and supporting small businesses. That’s why we proudly work with British printers and suppliers for all of our products.

  • Family Run

    As a family-run business, people are at the heart of everything we do. We're proud to be a fair trade employer, supporting talented individuals and creating meaningful connections through every piece we make.

  • Kind To Trees

    All our products are consciously created in small batches in the U.K. We're committed to only using paper from certified sustainable forests
