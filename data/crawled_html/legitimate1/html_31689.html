#1 MASTER ANXIETY, STRESS & CAR SICKNESS

A car journey without a well-made dog travel bed could lead to traumatising experiences – even when they are adults. Anxiety, stress and motion sickness are common problems that many dogs face during car rides.

At least one in five dogs suffers from symptoms such as nausea, panting, shivering, squealing, barking or drooling. For this reason, it is important to address these problems promptly.

Frist Class Protection & Safety

How to transport a dog in the car? In a collision at 50 km/h, a small dog can pose a life-threatening risk to the car’s occupants. To minimise the risk of a serious accident our dog car seat is designed with efficient safety elements, isofix hooks, and an anti-slip coating on the bottom.

The dog seat includes a standard dog belt to secure the dog to the seat. The included large belt can be securely fastened around the front seat or the headrest on the back seat. Additionally the dag seat can be secured using isofix.

DESIGNER DOG CARRIER

L’élianne ® Designer Dog Carrier is a combination between functionality and a fashion statement.

Our luxury dog carrier bag is perfect for carrying and travelling in the car or on an airplane. It is designed for puppies weighing up to 5 KG or small dogs weighing up to 8 KG, such as Dachshunds, Chihuahuas, or Italian Greyhounds, Pomeranians, with a maximum weight of 12 KG.

Explore our luxury dog bag collection, exclusively available for purchase online, and elevate your travel experience to a premium level!”

HIGH QUALITY & LUXURY DETAILS

Our luxury dog bag is designed from 100% waterproof nylon & vegan leather finishes. These high-quality materials are carefully selected for durability and long-lasting use. The dog carrier bag has a functional design, machine-washable & lightweight.

DESIGNER AIRPLANE DOG CARRIER

Airline approved: An iconic L’élianne ® in-cabin dog carrier for the airplane combines luxury and functionality for a successful plane trip together. Our luxury dog carriers are plane approved. You are allowed to take your dog with you in the cabin in Economy Class, Business Class within Europe.

LUXURY AIRPLANE DOG CARRIER

Approved Design: Our plane dog bag complies with the rules and dimensions of airlines such as Tui, KLM and Transavia.

Multifunctional: Besides air travel, the dog bag is ideal for car trips, vet visits and daily outings. For this reason, you can carry a puppy, small dog or a cat comfortably and safely.

Functionality: The bottom is made of sturdy, hard material for proper stability. The reinforced mesh materials provide ventilation and visibility.

Design: The luxury dog carrier bag is crafted from high-quality, durable materials and exquisite details. The exterior is composed of 50% nylon & 50% PU-vegan leather, which is waterproof. The slim design and compact size fit perfectly under airline seats and in luggage compartments.
