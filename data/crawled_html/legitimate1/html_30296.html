Access Industries is a private holding company and global investment firm based in New York. Founded by Len Blavatnik, Access identifies companies with strong business models and management teams, and optimizes their value through financial backing and expertise.

In 2020, Access took Warner Music Group public, delivering a 14x+ return.

In a dramatic turnaround for the music business, the record company was valued at $15 billion on its first day of trading, quadruple the $3.3 billion value when Blavatnik bought the company in 2011, which was widely seen as a contrarian bet at the time.

Visit Warner Music Group

LyondellBasell is a leader in the global chemical industry creating solutions for everyday sustainable living.

As one of the world’s largest producers of polymers and a leader in polyolefin technologies, the company develops and manufactures innovative products that facilitate sustainable transportation, food safety, clean water, and more.

Visit LyondellBasell

Now the world’s leading sports entertainment platform, DAZN offers live and on-demand events, betting, ticketing and e-commerce in more than 200 markets.

It is the dominant player in Italy, Spain, Germany, Portugal, Belgium, Canada, Japan and Taiwan where it holds top-tier domestic football broadcast rights. Globally, DAZN is the leading destination for boxing, combat sports and women's football, and is the home of NFL Game Pass.

Visit DAZN

In January 2025, Constellation agreed to buy Calpine for $26.6B, creating the US’s cleanest and most reliable energy producer and its leading retail electricity supplier.

The deal ranks as one of the largest-ever transactions in the US power sector and among the most significant private equity exits in history. Calpine, the US’s largest generator of electricity from natural gas and geothermal resources, was initially acquired in 2018 for $17 billion by Access Industries and a consortium of investors. The sale to Constellation underscores the critical value of Calpine’s reliable energy solutions, particularly as the US faces a historic surge in power demand fueled by reindustrialization, onshoring, and AI-driven demand.

Visit Calpine

Faena residences and hotels are among the finest real estate in the US and Latin America.

In 2014, Access partnered with developer and hotelier Alan Faena to build the glamorous Faena Hotel, Miami Beach. The Group also developed the Faena Art District in Buenos Aires, a 300,000-square-meter construction space that began with the reconstruction of the El Porteño Building and the creation of the Faena Hotel+Universe. The Faena New York, a flagship luxury hotel located at the One High Line, is set to open in 2025.

Visit Faena

In 2006, Access acquired the Grand-Hôtel du Cap-Ferrat, the premier hotel property located on the Côte d’Azur, overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

In 2015, Access selected Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts to manage the hotel. This holding epitomizes Access’ long-term approach to investing: acquiring an under-performing property and investing in the physical plant and operations to drive performance and enhance asset value.

Visit Grand-Hotel du Cap-Ferrat

Access Entertainment has an equity investment in, and co-finances films with the widely celebrated and award-winning independent production and distribution company A24.

Len Blavatnik and Danny Cohen also serve as Executive Producers on some of A24's most successful productions, such as The Iron Claw, Civil War, The Legend of Ochi, and the Oscar-winning and 2023 Cannes Film Festival Grand Prix winner, The Zone of Interest. Other films include Ari Aster's Eddington starring Joaquin Phoenix and Pedro Pascal and Celine Song's Materialists starring Dakota Johnson, Pedro Pascal, and Chris Evans.

Visit A24

Featured in TIME100's Most Influential Companies of 2023, Spotter empowers top YouTube creators to accelerate their business and unleash their full creative potential.

Launched in 2019, Spotter has deployed nearly $800 million to a wide array of YouTubers —from MrBeast to Aaron “AB” Brown and Kinigra Deon — who reach billions of subscribers.

Visit Spotter
