BIFA 2025 WINNERS ARE HERE!

The BIFA 2025 ceremony took place on Sunday 30 November at Roundhouse, hosted by Lou Sanders & Harriet Kemsley. Head to BIFA’s Instagram for red carpet and ceremony highlights.

AND THE NOMINEES ARE…

On Monday 3 November, Ben Hardy & Saura Lightfoot–Leon presented the BIFA 2025 nominees: the very best of British independent film. The craft winners will be announced on Thursday 20 November, with winners across all other categories announced at the ceremony on Sunday 30 November. The BIFA Podcast supported by kia

The BIFA Podcast is back for its fifth year, now supported by KIA. The new season features Will Poulter, David Jonsson, Celia Imrie, Tim Key, Stephen Merchant and more, sharing the moments that sparked their love of cinema with hosts Karis Aldridge and Joy Hunter.
