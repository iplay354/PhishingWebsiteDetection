About Us

The Twin Cities’ Premier Home Remodeling Contractor

Founded in the Minneapolis, Minnesota, area in 2007, Snap Construction® has grown to become one of the most successful and highly rated home improvement companies in not just the Twin Cities, but the nation. We provide homeowners and business owners with value driven solutions for:

  • Roof replacement
  • Replacement windows
  • House siding
  • Entry and patio doors
  • Insulation services
  • Custom Decks

You can count on Snap Construction to deliver a custom solution, top-quality product, and skilled installation backed by a lifetime warranty.
