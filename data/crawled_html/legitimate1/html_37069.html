Our Lady of the Rosary Library: Pray and work for souls

Our Lady of the Rosary Library is dedicated to glorifying God and saving souls, the most profitable endeavor. We offer literature to help readers perfect themselves and secure their salvation. To non-Catholic readers: Salvation exists only within the Catholic Church, founded by Our Lord. We urge you to study this claim with an open mind through our articles. To Catholics: Your salvation isn’t guaranteed. Fight daily against the world, flesh, and devil. Let this drive you to learn about the Church, seek truth, and grow in fervor. Above all, win souls for Christ by ordering our affordable material in bulk and distributing them zealously. We invite all to read, print, and share our material unchanged. Questions?
