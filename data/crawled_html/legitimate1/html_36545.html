About TRISTAN

Established in 1973, TRISTAN stands for elegance, remarkable quality, and responsibility in fashion.

Beautifully crafted and timeless garments

Eco-conscious practices and materials

Fashion for a happier, healthier planet

TRISTAN is the home of quality craftsmanship in Canadian fashion. We’re makers of fashion-forward clothing for contemporary men and women. 

When it comes to our apparel, we prefer a more sustainable approach: every garment is conciously crafted using premium materials and organic fibres for a difference you’ll notice in every wear.

No passing fads or trends here—just impeccably-made clothing that stands the test of time.
