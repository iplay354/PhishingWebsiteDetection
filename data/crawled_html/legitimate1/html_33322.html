Our Story

a little bit about us

From their first bed to bunk beds and everything in between, we help create a beautiful room for your kids to sleep and dream! We are passionate about kids sleeping on the right bed as we have Children ourselves and know how important it is for them to be happy in their beds and have a great nights sleep.

Awesome Beds 4 Kids supply some of the best quality kids beds, car beds, bunk beds, space saver beds, white beds, trundle beds, bedroom furniture, wall decals and innerspring mattresses from some of Australia's and overseas best manufactures, so you can be assured your child sleeps on a bed that's not only healthy to sleep on but one that offers great support while they are growing.

We can deliver your bed from one of our many depots throughout Australia using only professional new furniture freight handlers. Some products are even available to be picked up from Gold Coast, Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne. If you have any questions please call one of our team on 1300 556 243
