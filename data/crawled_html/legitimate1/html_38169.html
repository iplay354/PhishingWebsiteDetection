24 SPORTS

NSWIS partners with the National Sporting Organisations to deliver programs for athletes that compete in Olympic and Paralympic sports. This includes support to athletes competing in new Olympic events for surf, skate and sport climbing.

The NSWIS Diving program at Sydney Olympic Park Aquatic Centre, the Winter Sports Program in Jindabyne and the Canoe Slalom Program at Penrith are all recognised as National Training Centres.
