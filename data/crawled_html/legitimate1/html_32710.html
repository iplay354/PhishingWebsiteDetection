    VAlues

    • SOLID 14K GOLD

      We choose to work with only the best materials. all of our jewelry is composed of solid 14k gold, and we do not skimp on it. Other materials, like silver, don’t meet our quality criteria to be made into jewelry. We do not plate. We do not dip. We do not use vermeil. we do not fill. Our solid 14k gold jewelry is designed to resist time and be passed onto generations.

    • FULL CUT DIAMONDS

      We choose to work with only brilliant full cut diamonds. Round diamonds are cut either single or full. Single cut diamonds have 17 facets, whereas full cut diamonds have 57-58 facets. These facets make the sparkle in full cut diamonds unmatched to single cut diamonds. Single cut diamonds trade at a significant discount to full cut diamonds. Single cut diamonds don’t meet our quality criteria to be made into jewelry.

    • RECYCLABLE PACKAGING

      we strive to reduce waste, and we are committed to responsible packaging. 100% of our packaging is recyclable and 44% is made from recycled content. our packaging is designed with durability and the environment in mind. It is our responsibility to extend our sustainability efforts to our packaginG, which is why our jewelry pouches and jewelry boxes are reusable. lastly, in efforts to reduce paper use, our shipments do not include packing slips or return labels.

    • SUSTAINABILITY

      Our vision of sustainability includes adherence to our values of impeccable craftsmanship, longevity of design and materials, and a conscious creative and production process.
      Our international supply chain is carried out in accordance with the requirements included in the standard of the Responsible Jewellery Council. International RS production houses obtain RJC certification as well as environmental certification.
