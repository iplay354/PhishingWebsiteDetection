    A little bit about me:

    I am a freelance graphic designer from Southern California with a Psychology degree from Cal State Long Beach. After a brief stint at Art Center College of Design and opening a warehouse punk venue, I launched my own design business.

    My clients include Hermès, Gucci, Nike, Adobe, Instagram, United Nations, Coach, and Penguin Random House among others. Forbes describes my work as "all about the mashup of context and perception, with a massive injection of color". I like to surf in my free time.
