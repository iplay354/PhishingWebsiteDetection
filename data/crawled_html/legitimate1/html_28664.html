What is Mental Health? Mental health is the expression of 12 core brain capacities, which are divided into four main functions. Due to uncontrollable life events, those brain capacities constantly change.

  • In the moment – as our involuntary fight-flight system detects threats and puts us in a hyper-focused but inflexible state

  • In the long run – as our risk of mental conditions like anxiety and depression cross clinical thresholds
