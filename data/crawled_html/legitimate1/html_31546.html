Welcome To Faith Veterinary Clinic

Faith Veterinary Clinic is pleased to serve Church Lane Plaza and the surrounding areas. Our goal is to provide you and your pets with the best veterinary experience possible.

At Faith Veterinary Clinic, everyone is considered a leader and an integral member of the health care delivery team. Every day at Faith Veterinary Clinic, it is our friendly team’s responsibility to help make your veterinary experience a positive one to ensure that both you and your pet are at ease. In addition to mandatory veterinary knowledge and training, our team is selected for kindness, compassion, patience, professionalism, integrity, and agility.
We are here for you, we are here for your pet, and we are your neighborhood’s favorite vet. Please don’t hesitate to contact us today at (732) 658-6777 to request your pet’s appointment!

About Our Clinic

Faith Veterinary Clinic is proud to serve Church Lane Plaza and surrounding areas. We are dedicated to providing the highest level of veterinary medicine along with friendly, compassionate service.

We believe in treating every patient as if they were our own pet, and giving them the same loving attention and care. We are a group of highly trained, experienced animal lovers who are devoted to giving our patients the best care possible.

Welcoming New Clients

At Faith Veterinary Clinic, you can expect state of the art medical care for your pet. We believe in nurturing the human-pet bond and creating a harmonious relationship between people and pets. You can expect to be greeted by a courteous receptionist, clean exam rooms, friendly doctors, and caring technicians. We appreciate the role we get to play in your pets’ health care.
