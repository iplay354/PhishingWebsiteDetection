Grand Traverse Resort and Spa

Welcome to the ultimate Michigan resort and spa experience. You want to escape the rigors of everyday life—to disconnect and discover the bounty of somewhere new, somewhere miles away from ordinary.

Stay

Whether you choose accommodations at the Hotel, the Tower, or a spacious Resort condominium, you'll find total relaxation after a day of enjoying the region's treasures. Leave the chores at home. We'll take care of the rest.

View all accommodations

Whether you choose accommodations in our six-story Hotel, 16-floor Tower, or a spacious Resort condominium, you'll find total relaxation after a day of enjoying the region's treasures. Our variety of room types will give you the flexibility to stay in comfort and style no matter the occasion.

Explore Rooms

In Northern Michigan, the onset of winter doesn't mean that the fun has to stop—there’s a whole new set of activities and events once the snow flies! Whether you book a winter family vacation or a romantic up-north getaway, you’ll find plenty of winter activities and holiday events at Grand Traverse Resort and Spa.

Explore Activities

Experience championship level golf, unwind at our premier spa, make a splash in one of our pools or savor exquisite tastes at our multiple on-site restaurants. Enjoy seamless connectivity with high-speed Wi-fi and take advantage of our proximity to downtown Traverse City and the Cherry Capital Airport.
