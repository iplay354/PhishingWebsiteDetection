What's on

To get involved or find out more, pop in to our Welcome Hub on St Leonards St (E3 3BT). We’re open 9:15am-4:30pm on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, and 9:15am-1pm on Thursday. Come say hello 👋

FESTIVE PERIOD CLOSURE DETAILS, ADDITIONAL CONTACTS & SERVICES

We will be closing our Centre doors from 3pm on Wednesday 24 December 2025 until 9am on Monday 5 January 2026.

If you have an urgent issue visit the Tower Hamlets Council website for a list of emergency numbers.

For mental health support please ring NHS 111 Option 2 for the crisis intervention service or Samaritans on 116 123.

For homelessness support, Shelter’s emergency helpline can be reached on 080 800 4444.

Bow Foodbank (St Stephens Health Centre, William Place, E3 5ED) will be open on Christmas Eve between 10am and 2pm. It will be closed on Christmas Day (Dec 25), Boxing Day (Dec 26), New Years Day (Jan 1) and Jan 2, 2026. Bow Foodbank will be providing emergency bags on the December 30 and 31 2025 and back to usual on Tuesday 6 January 2026.

The park and gardens (Bob’s Park) will continue to be open from 9am until 5pm every day.

Bromley by Bow Health Practice will be closed at 6.30pm on Wednesday 24t December until 8am on Monday 29 December and will be closed New Years Day.

ArtFix Café at the Bromley by Bow Centre will be closing from midday on Wednesday 24 December 2025 until 9am on Monday 5 January 2026. The café will only be serving hot drinks and Christmas chocolates on Wednesday 24 December.

We wish you all a Merry Christmas and a safe and peaceful festive period.
