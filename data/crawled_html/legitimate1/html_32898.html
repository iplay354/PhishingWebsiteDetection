BMW of Asheville - Your Arden, North Carolina BMW Dealership

We welcome you to BMW of Asheville, the foremost BMW dealership serving the greater North Carolina area with pride and excellence. Situated in Arden, North Carolina (formerly in Fletcher, NC), our dealership extends its services to neighboring areas such as Franklin, Hendersonville, and Waynesville. Whether you're a first-time BMW buyer or a seasoned BMW enthusiast, we have just the right BMW vehicle for you at a price that fits your budget.

Our team of friendly and knowledgeable sales staff, backed by a team of highly skilled mechanics, is committed to getting you on the road as soon as possible. To start your journey, you can select your preferred BMW model from our extensive inventory of new and pre-owned cars. Our North Carolina BMW dealership boasts a full line-up of BMW models, be it sedans or SUVs, new or pre-owned, we have something that suits your style and needs.

We take great pride in offering an array of Asheville cars for sale to our North Carolina BMW drivers, including the new BMW X3 and other BMW SUVs. Additionally, we carry a diverse range of BMW cars like the BMW X4 and the ever-popular BMW Convertibles. At BMW of Asheville, we strive to create a lifetime customer experience by consistently providing world-class services to each of our esteemed customers.

We Keep You Going

In addition to offering new and pre-owned BMW vehicles, BMW of Asheville is also equipped with a state-of-the-art Arden BMW Auto Service department that is committed to keeping your BMW in top-notch condition. If you already own a BMW that is in need of service or repair, simply make an appointment with us, and our highly skilled technicians will get your car up and running in no time. As a token of our appreciation, we offer complimentary loaners to our service center customers so that you can continue with your daily routine without any inconvenience.

Our Certified Pre-Owned BMW cars offer a fantastic alternative to a new BMW. Our CPO BMW vehicles at BMW of Asheville go through a rigorous 185+ point inspection, ensuring that the vehicle you're purchasing is not only reliable but will also last for years to come. With our CPO BMW cars, you get the peace of mind that comes with knowing you're driving a vehicle that has been thoroughly vetted by our experts.

Don't wait any longer to experience the ultimate driving machine. Visit BMW of Asheville today and take a test drive of your preferred BMW model. Whether you're in the market for a new or used cars in Asheville, our BMW dealer is the place to look. Our car dealers in Asheville, NC is conveniently located just 10 minutes south of Carmax Asheville via the Andrew Jackson Highway at 31 Skyland Inn Drive, Arden, NC 28704, and our sales team can be reached at 888-893-7743.
