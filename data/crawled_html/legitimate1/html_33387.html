ron geesin official website

Composer, performer, sound architect, interactive designer, broadcaster, writer and lecturer.

"accomplished composer and musician, experimenter on the free form edge, poet and comedian with a taste for the absurd."

  • Co-composer of Pink Floyd’s ‘Atom Heart Mother’
  • Composer for ’Sunday Bloody Sunday’ (dir. John Schlesinger)
  • One of the first one-man record companies
  • Inventor of The Tune Tube interactive installation
  • Restorer of many jazz 78s
  • Researcher and author of The Adjustable Spanner (& Vol.2)
  • Overall, manic expressive and Neo-Renaissance Man
