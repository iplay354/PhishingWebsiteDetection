Dive into a World of Wonder at St Andrews Aquarium!

Tucked into the cliffs above the stunning St Andrews Bay, our family-run aquarium is a splash-tastic adventure just waiting to happen! Since 1999, we’ve been proudly welcoming visitors into our magical, mysterious underwater world — and trust us, it’s anything but ordinary.

From the moment you step inside, you’ll be face-to-fin with all kinds of amazing animals. Say hello to our playful penguins, come nose-to-nose with sharks, and meet our colourful cast of tropical fish (yes, Nemo and Dory are here too!). But that’s not all… we’ve got cheeky meerkats, snappy crocs, dazzling poison dart frogs, and even a few eight-legged surprises for the brave!

More Than Just an Aquarium…

We’re passionate about animals — and it shows! With over 120 species to discover, every visit is packed with fun, facts, and furry (or scaly!) friends. Whether you’re a curious kid or a grown-up with a wild side, there’s something new to learn around every corner. Plus, we’re big on conservation, and you’ll leave knowing more about how to protect our planet and the creatures who call it home.

So grab a coffee, pick up a souvenir from our gift shop, and get ready to explore the wonders of the deep — all without getting your feet wet!
