Food For All Our food hub is on a mission to create an equitable food system in North Carolina. Learn More

WHO WE ARE

Farmer Foodshare is a Triangle-based, nonprofit food hub whose mission is to increase access to fresh, local food for all and to support the viability of marginalized NC farmers to create an equitable food system.

WHY WE'RE HERE

We envision a vibrant and equitable regional food system, where local farms thrive and every person living in our foodshed can access the fresh, local food grown here.

WHAT WE DO

We support historically marginalized NC farmers and contribute fresh, local produce to the emergency food system.

How It Works

We purchase high-quality produce year-round from local growers, which is both donated to food distribution organizations and sold to local food service institutions. All income from our wholesale program is returned to the hands of our local farmers and reinvested in food access programs.

Together with our community, we’re growing a healthier foodshed in North Carolina for all.
