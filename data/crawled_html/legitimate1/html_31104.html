Nathan Hassall Music: Exquisite Live Music for London

Hello and welcome to Nathan Hassall Music's brand new website

Nathan Hassall Music aims to provide you with the finest in live music entertainment. Whether you are looking to hire a fantastic Jazz Band, an elegant String Quartet, or an amazing function band to complement your event; or if you are interested in seeing a great concert in London we are confident that you have come to the right place.

Our Musicians & DJs for Hire service purely focuses on providing only what we feel is the best live music to corporate events, parties, or weddings and we champion the skill of our musicians and the inimitable ambience they can bring to your event above all else. We truly believe there is no comparison to having a live musician, band or ensemble perform for you and your guests, and we are sure that if you hire one of our acts then you will completely agree with us. Nathan Hassall Music offers an exclusive selection of hand-picked or specifically created jazz bands, solo acts and classical ensembles. This means that we are confident the service we provide is second to none as our acts are made up of only the finest musicians taken from London’s thriving music scene.

Thanks to the revamp of our website Nathan Hassall Music now also has specific sections of the website dedicated to our valued Wedding and Corporate Clients. Here you will find our current Special Offers and Recommendations that are tailor made for each type of event and all the information included is aimed at making your life easier, helping you to save some money whilst you are at it.

The Concerts section of Nathan Hassall Music is the newest addition to our business. Here we aim to bring to you some of the best and most interesting jazz and classical concerts in and around the London area, and all of our concerts will be supplemented online with unique content including concert previews, musician interviews and video footage taken from past events. In this sections you can also reserve tickets via email at discounted prices, check out our featured acts and listen to their music, as well sign up to Nathan Hassall Music’s newsletters which will keep you up to date with the latest news and offers on what we are sure will be some of the best concerts in town.

We hope you enjoy your visit to Nathan Hassall Music and please remember that we do love to hear from you whatever your query, comment or suggestion. Please feel free to get in touch with us at anytime.

Best Wishes

Nathan Hassall (Saxophonist & Agency Director)
