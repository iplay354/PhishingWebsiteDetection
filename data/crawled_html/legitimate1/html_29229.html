Introduction

My day job is with Red Hat as a Distinguised Engineer, where I am helping build out and establish a service portfolio, on and around OpenShift in various environments.

Outside of my day job, I was the Project Lead for The CentOS Project for about 10 years. Additionally, I contribute to various open source projects in and around the infrastructure space - I enjoy working on Security, Automation, Management and scale related challenges for connected compute. Application of technology for real world solutions and improvements is what drives me.

I enjoy travel, ideally over land and in my car. In the nearly thirty years I’ve held my driving license, I’ve driven nearly a million miles.
