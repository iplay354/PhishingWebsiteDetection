Starting out

If you’re thinking about becoming self-employed, or recently started working for yourself, congratulations! You’ve taken the first steps towards a way of working that has enormous potential for your freedom and flexibility. 

It can feel scary and overwhelming to make the leap, but you don’t need to know everything about consulting, contracting or running a business to get started, or to become extremely successful. 

We’re here to support you and help you make educated decisions when you’re just starting out.

Start your self-employment journey

You have the ideas, passion, and courage to take the first steps into self-employment. We have all the guidance and support you need to forge your own path. Let’s build your future together.

Member benefits at a glance

From side hustles and freelancers to limited company directors and umbrella company workers, the world of self-employment is incredibly diverse. That's why IPSE membership is tailored to the different ways in which self-employed people work. Here are just a few reasons our members choose IPSE membership.

Get protected

We help you understand and manage your business risks, safe in the knowledge that we can provide tailored insurances and helplines that protect against the unforeseen.

Stay connected

We connect you to a strong and supportive network of fellow IPSE members, staff and experts that can be relied upon to advise on your needs. We help you source expertise, engage with policymakers and collaborate together to access business opportunities.

Feel empowered

We empower you with the right tools that help create a healthy and rewarding business now, and for the future. We help you develop your career through inspirational and practical content, and offer financial and wellbeing services for peace of mind.

Add your voice

Together we campaign and influence government to create a fair business environment that encourages self-employment as a career choice.
