Whether you're seeking your first Costa Rica yoga retreat experience or you're a seasoned practitioner looking to deepen your practice in paradise, Blue Osa provides the perfect hideaway. Why Choose Our Costa Rica Yoga Retreat?

Blue Osa stands apart from other yoga retreats in Costa Rica through our unique combination of luxury eco-accommodations, revolutionary AYAMA® (Applied Yoga Anatomy + Muscle Activation) teaching methodology, and unparalleled beachfront location. With over 30 years of expertise, our founder Yogi Aaron has created a sanctuary where ancient wisdom meets modern comfort, earning features in BBC, Wall Street Journal, and Outside Magazine.

 

 

What makes Blue Osa the best yoga retreat Costa Rica has to offer? It starts with our intimate setting—just 13 rooms with a capacity of 36 guests—ensuring personalized attention and genuine community connection. Our small-group approach means our friendly staff knows your name, and every detail, from the bread baked fresh daily to the freshly grown farm-to-table meals, is done mindfully and with love.

Practice yoga in our elegantly designed, open-air studio overlooking the Pacific Ocean. Pamper yourself in our full-service eco-spa featuring natural treatments like locally-sourced fruit facials, body scrubs, Watsu, deep tissue massage, and acupuncture. Swim in our chemical-free lap pool. Experience true sustainable living in our solar-powered micro-grid property designed to leave the smallest carbon footprint possible.

 

 

In the spirit of yoga, we encourage mindfulness through morning silence, pre-dinner gratitude rituals, and rotating place settings that foster fresh conversations and connections. Whether you choose a weekend wellness escape, week-long immersive retreat, or our transformative 200-hour or 300-hour yoga teacher training programs, you'll discover why guests consistently rate Blue Osa as Costa Rica's top-rated yoga retreat on TripAdvisor and BookRetreats.

Costa Rica Yoga Retreat FAQs

How much does a Costa Rica yoga retreat cost at Blue Osa?

Expand

Our Costa Rica yoga retreat packages range from $1,440 for a 4-day weekend retreat to $2,300 for a full week, including all luxury accommodations, farm-to-table meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks), daily yoga classes, spa amenities, and welcome gifts. Yoga teacher training programs range from $2,990 (200-hour) to $4,490 (300-hour). Pricing varies based on room type, occupancy (single or shared), and season. Visit our packages page for current rates and special offers.

What's included in your Costa Rica yoga retreat packages?

Expand

All Blue Osa retreat packages include: luxury eco-accommodations, three farm-to-table meals daily, daily yoga classes, complimentary yoga props and equipment, welcome ceremony and gift, beach and pool access, WiFi, spa amenities, and most activities. Teacher training includes certification materials, textbooks, and lifetime alumni support.

Do I need yoga experience for your Costa Rica retreat?

Expand

Absolutely not! While Blue Osa is a yoga retreat, you don't need any yoga experience—or even an interest in yoga—to have an amazing time here.

Many guests come with partners or family members who don't practice yoga at all. Non-yogis love Blue Osa for our pristine private beach, rejuvenating spa massages and body treatments, chemical-free lap pool, organic farm-to-table cuisine, and incredible Osa Peninsula adventures including waterfall rappelling, canopy zip-lining, whale watching, sportfishing, and wildlife tours.

Blue Osa is about disconnecting to reconnect—turning off the noise of daily life to rediscover what matters. Whether you're here for yoga, adventure, spa relaxation, or simply to be in community with welcoming, like-minded souls, you'll find your place. Many first-time yoga students discover their lifelong practice at Blue Osa, but you're equally welcome to spend your days on beach walks, enjoying therapeutic massages, and connecting with our supportive community—yoga optional.

When is the best time to visit a Costa Rica yoga retreat?

Expand

Costa Rica offers year-round yoga retreat opportunities! The dry season (December-April) features sunny days perfect for beach yoga and outdoor adventures. The green season (May-November) offers lush rainforest scenery, fewer crowds, occasional refreshing afternoon showers, and special pricing. Blue Osa operates year-round with weekly retreats and yoga teacher training programs.

How do I get to Blue Osa from the airport?

Expand

From San José (SJO) you have three transportation options: (1) Domestic flight to Puerto Jiménez ($299 to 330 round-trip, 40 minutes), (2) Shared shuttle service ($200, 5-6 hours), or (3) Private transfer ($450, 5-6 hours). We provide detailed directions and can arrange all transportation. The journey is part of the adventure!

What makes Blue Osa different from other yoga retreats in Costa Rica?

Expand

Blue Osa is Costa Rica's only beachfront yoga retreat on the Osa Peninsula, offering the revolutionary AYAMA™ methodology for pain-free movement. We combine luxury eco-accommodations, award-winning farm-to-table cuisine, 30+ years of teaching expertise, intimate group size, and a pristine location in the world's most biodiverse region. Featured in BBC, Wall Street Journal, Outside Magazine, and Travel + Leisure.

Can I come alone to your Costa Rica yoga retreat?

Expand

Absolutely! 60-70% of our guests travel solo. Our welcoming community atmosphere makes it easy to connect with like-minded travelers from around the world. Many solo guests form lifelong friendships at Blue Osa. Single occupancy rooms are available, or choose shared accommodations at reduced rates to meet fellow travelers.

What should I pack for a yoga retreat in Costa Rica?

Expand

Pack light! Bring comfortable clothing for yoga and tropical weather, swimsuit, reef-safe sunscreen, natural insect repellent, hiking shoes, flip-flops, light rain jacket, reusable water bottle, headlamp/flashlight, and any personal medications. We provide yoga mats, props, beach towels, eco-friendly toiletries, and hair dryers. Full packing list sent upon booking confirmation.

 

Is Blue Osa suitable for couples or only solo travelers?

Expand

We welcome both! Couples love our romantic beachfront setting, couples' spa treatments, private accommodations, and opportunities for quality time together. Many couples choose our Yoga Retreat in Costa Rica as a unique alternative to traditional beach vacations, combining adventure, wellness, romance, and personal growth.

Do you offer yoga teacher training at your Costa Rica retreat?

Expand

Yes! We offer both 200-hour and 300-hour Yoga Alliance certified teacher training programs featuring the revolutionary AYAMA® methodology. Our intensive 14-day trainings (200-hour) and 28-day advanced trainings (300-hour) combine traditional yoga wisdom with modern pain science and functional anatomy. Training dates offered monthly. Visit our teacher training pages for schedules and registration.

What type of food is served at Blue Osa?

Expand

We serve farm-to-table, plant-based cuisine featuring fresh tropical fruits, vegetables, and superfoods from our garden and local farms. Meals are prepared from scratch daily with love and mindfulness. We accommodate all dietary needs—gluten-free, vegan, allergies—just let us know your requirements when booking.

How safe is Costa Rica? Is Blue Osa safe?

Expand

Costa Rica is one of the safest countries in Central America, with a stable democracy, no standing army since 1949, and a welcoming culture known for "Pura Vida." The country consistently ranks as one of the top travel destinations for safety and is very popular with solo travelers, families, and retirees.

Blue Osa is exceptionally safe. Our remote location on the secluded Osa Peninsula means we're far from any urban areas, accessible only by small plane or a scenic 5-6 hour drive through pristine countryside. We have 24-hour on-site security every night, and our property is private—we do not allow outside guests or public access. Only registered guests and staff are permitted on the grounds.

Our intimate size and low guest count mean our team knows everyone by name, and our beachfront location offers complete privacy and tranquility. Most guests find Blue Osa to be one of the safest, most peaceful places they've ever visited. Many solo travelers—especially women—choose us specifically because of our secure, welcoming environment.
