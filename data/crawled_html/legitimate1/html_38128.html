Help Sustain and Strengthen Monergism

For 25 years, Monergism has served the church by freely distributing trusted Reformed theology to readers around the world. As this work has grown, so has our vision for the future.

Our long-term goal is to make the entire public-domain corpus of Reformed theology available in clean, searchable, and accessible formats, supported by continually improved taxonomy and careful curation. This includes professionally produced audio editions available through the Monergism Library App, the introduction of advanced retrieval tools that allow readers to search, cross-reference, and meaningfully interact with the full theological library, and the development of original resources such as curriculum on our upgraded website.

To pursue this responsibly, we must build a small team to help carry a workload that has grown far beyond what one person can sustain. We still need $18,600 to meet our 2025 operating budget, and an additional $150,000 to hire essential staff. If Monergism has served you or your church, please consider partnering with us today.

Monergism (CPRF) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit ministry. All donations are tax-deductible.
