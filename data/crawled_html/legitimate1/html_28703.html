Education in Beauty

At Salon Setup, our educators are more than instructors—they’re qualified beauty therapists and educators with a passion for empowering others. 


With over 2 decades of hands-on industry experience, they bring depth, real-world insight, and lasting value to every Certification Course—and beyond.


Each educator holds advanced qualifications in Adult Education and brings a diverse skill set from across the beauty and aesthetics field. Their expertise spans advanced treatments, clinic ownership, and education delivery, giving students practical and relevant training that works in the real world.


Our team remains actively involved in the NZ beauty industry, volunteering for industry reform initiatives, managing professional support networks, running their own skincare clinics, and developing private label skincare ranges. 


Their continued involvement ensures that what we teach is current, credible, and aligned with the future of the beauty industry in New Zealand.


Explore our training options

All our courses are comprehensive and includes training manuals, hands-on guidance, certification & on-going post-training support in the form of forums, videos and zoom meeting catch ups.


We offer flexible learning options to suit every schedule and learning style—choose from classroom-based training, virtual sessions, blended delivery, or our Self-Directed Learning Portal.
