Welcome to Estonian Banking Association!

Estonian Banking Association (EBA) was founded on July 3, 1992 by 21 commercial banks. EBA is a full member of European Banking Federation (EBF)

EBA has currently 10 member banks who represent approximately 98% of Estonian banking sector's assets.

The mission and the primary objective of EBA is to promote the development of banking activities, to improve the banking operations of our member banks and to institute good business practices and ethics.

Strategic objectives of the Estonian Banking Association

  • Ensuring the trustworthiness and transparency of banking sector
  • Development of self-regulation and impeccable managerial culture in banking
  • Ensuring on business continuity of banking services
