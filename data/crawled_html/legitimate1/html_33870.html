Welcome to David J. Peterson's Web Thing

Hi!

You've reached my site, which is devoted to (some of) the languages I've created. These languages were created for the purposes of fun, so I hope you have fun rifling through them.

Brand new visitors are probably here because they've heard about my work on HBO's fantasy series Game of Thrones, or some other show/film. For the most part, I post about that work elsewhere, such as my AO3 or my wiki. This site is a record of my work before 2009. If you'd like to see examples of some of the work I've done in the past, though, I'd recommend looking at this page, this page and this page (and go to this page if you can't stand the colors on my site).

Today is a good day to be alive. Enjoy it!
