I’m Darren Wershler, a writer, professor and researcher at Concordia University.

I specialize in media history and media archaeology. My older work concerns experimental literature.

Right now, I’m in the process of completing THE LAB BOOK: Situated Practice in Media Studies, a book I’m writing with Jussi Parikka and Lori Emerson.

My research occurs in the Milieux Institute, where I co-founded the Media History Research Centre and am Director of the Residual Media Depot. I teach classes in the Centre for Interdisciplinary Studies in Society and Culture and the English department.

I blog here.
