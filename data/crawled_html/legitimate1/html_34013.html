About Strange Music, Inc.

Strange Music, Inc., founded by Tech N9ne and Travis O'Guin, is an industry-leading powerhouse in the world of independent music. With a relentless commitment to artistic integrity and exceptional music, we've nurtured a roster of incredibly talented artists and have cemented our place as a staple in the hip-hop and rap communities.

Celebrating over two decades of groundbreaking music, we continue to push the boundaries of creativity and innovation. Discover more about our artists and latest releases at strangemusicinc.net.
