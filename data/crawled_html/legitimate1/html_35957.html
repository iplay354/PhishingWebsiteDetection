Emerald Isle NC Vacation Rentals

Come for a week... Stay for a lifetime.

Located on the Southern Outer Banks of North Carolina, Emerald Isle is the true gem of the Crystal Coast. The beautiful blue-green colors of the ocean will captivate you as you relax in your beach chair while sinking your toes in the warm sand. Because Emerald Isle is a south facing beach, you will not want to miss the view of the sun as it both rises and sets over the water. Whether your idea of a vacation includes fishing, golfing, or just kicking back to enjoy all that the beautiful beach has to offer, it is our pleasure to assist you in making your island vacation dreams come true. Browse our collection of vacation rentals in Emerald Isle and surrounding areas and find your perfect home away from home. 

 

 

At Sun-Surf Realty, you'll find a large selection of incredible homes ranging from Oceanfront/Side, Soundfront/Side, to quaint properties located on the Mainland (Swansboro, Peletier, Newport), and even Pet-Friendly homes the whole family will love. With spaces large enough for multi-family vacations or cozy enough for a quick couple's getaway, you're sure to find the perfect spot for your next adventure. When you're ready to reserve your stay, remember to do it directly with Sun-Surf Realty for the best rate on your property and the best service in the region. Our years of experience in the Emerald Isle market ensure we've thought of everything to make your stay one you'll remember forever.

Find Your Paradise in Emerald Isle

 

Whether you’re visiting for a quiet beach getaway, a large family vacation, special occasion, or you’re looking to relocate for good, Emerald Isle has something for everyone. At Sun-Surf Realty, we pride ourselves on providing guests and owners with exceptional experiences that keep families coming back to book or partner with us year after year. Our goal is to ensure each guest enjoys a peaceful vacation in a fantastic home where every need is anticipated. For our owners, we promise to go above and beyond to treat your investment like it’s our own. By delivering unmatched service to both our visitors and owners, we guarantee a level of quality you won’t find anywhere else.

 

 

Beyond the incredible beaches of Emerald Isle, North Carolina, you’ll enjoy tons of recreational activities like sport fishing, kayaking, surfing, nature tours, museums, aquariums, and plenty of landmarks, parks, and charming coastal townscapes all within a short distance from your property. Spend your days wandering boardwalks, dining at delicious restaurants, taking in local festivals, or just relaxing on your balcony as you watch for dolphins in the distant waves. No matter how you choose to spend your Emerald Isle vacation, one thing is certain: it’s a trip you’ll want to repeat again and again.
