The Long Bay Effect

Focused on lifestyle and community, Long Bay is home to numerous reserves, parks, playgrounds, and green spaces. Add the vibrant Long Bay Village and the family-friendly Long Bay Beach nearby, and it’s clear that Long Bay offers the perfect blend of coastal charm and city convenience.

Long Bay Beach

The jewel in the crown is Long Bay, arguably Auckland’s nicest beach. It’s a stunning 1km long stunning stretch of safe, white sand, family friendly beach set in the beautiful Long Bay Regional Park. It’s perfect for swimming, picnics, dog walking, BBQ’ing, paddle boarding and playing with the kids on the fantastic, all-inclusive Long Bay playground.

It’s not the only beach in the area, head north along the sand at low tide or overland via the Long Bay Coastal walk to discover the very secluded and often totally unpopulated Granny’s and Pohutakawa Bay.

Long Bay Regional Park

Take the coastal track from the North End of Long Bay Beach to Okura River for impressive cliff top views and vista’s over the wider Hauraki Gulf. If the tide is low walk around the shore line and discover secret beaches and endangered shorebirds that thrive in the safety of the Long Bay Okura Marine Reserve. If you’re in the right place at the right time you might also see Orca and dolphins.

Long Bay Village

Long Bay Village has it all. From New World for weekly groceries to a diverse range of eateries, a dentist, gym, barbershop, doctor and everything in between.

Long Bay Surf Club is your local, often hosting live music, sports games on the big screen and delicious range of food and Beverages. Pop to Little Local for coffee or Xtra Ordinary for your Asian-fusion fix - it won’t take long for you to become a well-known local.

Long Bay Market

The Long Bay Village Market is held on a monthly basis, hosting Auckland’s leading artisans. You can take home sustainable goods including clothing, jewellery, plants, organic fare, homewares and more.

Accompanied by live music, creative workshops and fresh food offerings from the Long Bay Village tenants, the market offers a fun day out for all ages.
