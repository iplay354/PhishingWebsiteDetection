Welcome to CENTREBANK

CentreBank, previously known as The Veedersburg State Bank, was founded in 1917 by 3 families with a total of 25 stockholders.

Upon opening, the Bank only consisted of one branch located in Veedersburg, Indiana; it remained the only bank in Veedersburg for over 80 years. In 1982, CentreBank expanded with a drive up location in Sterling, followed by new branch offices in Covington (1990), Kingman (2005), Hillsboro (2015), and the newest—Rockville (2024).
