NDSU Impact

As North Dakota’s land-grant, R1 research university, NDSU ranks among the nation’s best in higher education. Students and faculty work together to tackle real challenges, fuel the workforce and shape careers of the future.

Bison Athletics

NDSU is more than academics, it’s home to champions. With 284 conference titles and 30 national championships combined in all sports since 1925, Bison Athletics bring the thunder of game day and the pride of the Herd to life. Go Bison!
