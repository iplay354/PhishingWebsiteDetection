v.1.8.1 - Update Highlights





Community Update

As some of you might remember, last year we had to share concerning details regarding the situation of Project Reality. Our financial struggle left us no choice but to start a Kickstarter campaign to sustain the development of the game. Today, we can proudly declare that our goal has been reached. We are very grateful to our dedicated community, especially everyone who has donated or will do so in the future.






As we mentioned last year, reaching our goal would not only ensure the survival of Project Reality but also open new possibilities for the team to work on new and unique content. Today, we finally have the opportunity to share some of that content with you.









Space Battles

Project Reality can into Space! During the last few years, we have repeatedly received requests from the community, asking for a less realistic gamemode with more intensive combat. We decided to listen to your feedback and we are now releasing Space Battles! In this new mode, players will fight over a giant spaceship orbiting a habitable planet in the Andromeda galaxy.
We have worked tirelessly to give you distinctive weapons, such as TAR-5000S by [R-DEV]ANW1173, as well as WIST-10000 and QSZ-92000 by [R-COM]M.MCC. You will be able to enjoy new weapons on the Andromeda map and spaceship made by [R-DEV]Ason.









Donbas

Aside from Space Battles, we are also proud to announce the release of Donbas. It is a brand new map, set in modern-day eastern Ukraine. The map, together with multiple new assets, was designed by [R-DEV]Ason, while [R-CON]Kenzzan further enriched the map's eastern ukrainian atmosphere.












Unfortunately, with the end of winter, our Medelivka seasonal map has to go. We are certain it offered plenty of fresh experience and hopefully, you will soon be able to fight in its trenches and snowy forests all year round!


As usual, all the new content is accompanied by various bug fixes and quality of life improvements.

Winter Update



The holidays are over, but winter is still going strong. With that we bring you the next small update for Project Reality including a new winter map Medelivka. This snowy, winter forest located in eastern Europe features various NATO armies fighting Russia as well a fictional battle for our World War 2 German and Soviet factions.



Together with this we bring you many small improvements to textures, some new toys and the usual bunch of bug fixes and general improvements.

We wish everyone a happy new year and enjoy this new iteration of Project Reality!

For more information, please feel free to join us on our public forums to discuss this and other news. Also, be sure to connect to Project Reality through social media to stay informed and receive up-to-the-minute updates, the occasional leaked bit of information, and more! See you on the battlefield!



- The Project Reality Team
