Y.O.U. Welcomes Four New Board Members

"Potential is universal. Opportunity is not. Our job is to fill the gap."

— Wes Moore, Author, Speaking to Y.O.U. supporters

 

We envision a future where all structures and systems support young people in designing their own paths to success and bringing about the changes they want to see in the world. We serve youth using three strategies — identity-affirming relationships, expanded learning opportunities, and resources to meet emerging needs — to close the opportunity gap and support youth in defining and achieving their goals.

Y.O.U. by the Numbers

Y.O.U. serves over 1,400 youth and reaches over 4,000 family members each year through afterschool and summer learning programs, clinical counseling, street outreach, and family engagement. These services have a powerful, positive impact on youth’s academic, social, and emotional growth and development.

  • 93

    of Y.O.U. youth report greater confidence after participating in Y.O.U. programs.

  • 85

    of Y.O.U. youth are improving or maintaining their academic achievement.

  • 95

    of Y.O.U. youth are improving or maintaining their ability to set goals for their future.

  • 94

  • 88
