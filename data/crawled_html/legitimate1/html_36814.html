Join Us for the 2026 College Panel!

This free, virtual event gives college-bound students the chance to hear inspiring success stories from peers who have achieved their higher-education goals despite learning differences. Designed to empower and inform, the panel offers practical guidance for juniors and seniors preparing for college, motivation for freshmen and sophomores, and encouragement for families supporting their students on this journey.

Neuhaus Education Center Celebrates 45 Years of Literacy Impact

Neuhaus Education Center is proud to celebrate 45 years of impact—nearly half a century of advancing literacy through teacher training, advocacy, and community engagement. To honor this milestone, we created a special 45th Anniversary Milestones Booklet to showcase the milestones, impact, and stories that shaped our journey.

Structured Reading & Spelling

This innovative program is structured with the necessary skills, materials, and content for educators and facilitators to make a significant impact on high school and adult learners facing literacy challenges. Structured Reading and Spelling (SRS) is a research-based professional development designed to equip educators with a comprehensive curriculum that supports older students and adults struggling with literacy.

Learn More

Structured Literacy Modules

The Neuhaus Structured Literacy Modules discuss the components of evidence-based, structured literacy instruction. It is the only non-certification Neuhaus course that covers a variety of essential literacy topics in a single class.
