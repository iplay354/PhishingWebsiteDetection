Success Stories

"Reflecting on our journey with Get Moving!, it feels like just yesterday we were eager to get started, and now my boys are proudly yellow belts. Time has flown, and your team has made a significant difference in our lives. I've witnessed a positive transformation in my sons' personalities— their confidence, obedience, and discipline have all grown remarkably. Beyond that, we've found a sense of belonging and community, and from day one, you made us feel truly valued. "

Alvin Martinez, Father of Aslan and Kion
Aslan and KionGet Moving Program Participants
Luis Rodriguez joined the Get Moving! Basketball program in April 2024, and from the start, he displayed a quiet determination and a genuine love for the game. Coach Brian recalls that Luis was initially quite shy, but his passion for basketball was undeniable.

Over the past year, Luis has truly blossomed. He arrives each week eager to learn, improve, and give his best. His confidence has grown, and he's developed into a strong leader on the court.
Luis RodriguezGet Moving Program Participant
Kennedy has been in Prodigy for over 5 years. Kennedy has become a very positive role model for the other girls in our Prodigy class. Kennedy is now an ambassador for Prodigy. The life skills and the willingness to listen, I think, is what made Kennedy as well as the other youth find some peace within themselves. They learn it’s okay to get upset; however, it’s how we react to the situation. Kennedy is sharing what she has learned with the other girls.
Kennedy DavenportProdigy® Cultural Arts Program Participant
We sincerely thank the Hispanic Federation for their generous donation in support of University Area CDC’s Hurricane Relief Fund. Their commitment enabled us to provide immediate assistance to families, individuals, and businesses in the University Area who experienced devastating losses, including homes, belongings, and essential resources. This partnership strengthened our ability to respond effectively during this challenging time and helped restore hope and stability to our community.

We deeply appreciate the Hispanic Federation’s dedication and its significant impact on the lives of University Area residents.

GROWING OUR COMMUNITY…
ONE BLOCK AT A TIME.

Since 1996, University Area CDC has championed positive change in the economic, educational and social levels of the community
through youth programs, adult education, affordable housing, workforce and resource assistance, and community engagement.

As fearless champions of University Area residents, we will empower them with the essential
tools needed to attain better jobs, better health and better futures for their children.
