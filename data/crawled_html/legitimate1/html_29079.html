If you’re planning your wedding then you need to be at this show!

The I Do Wedding Exhibitions are not to be missed if you are planning your BIG DAY.

Here at I Do we host the largest one day wedding exhibitions in the UK.

Featuring the very best suppliers from wedding venues, bridal wear, cake suppliers, entertainers, wedding rings, photographers and plenty more to help you plan the best day of your life. We’ve got it all at the I Do Wedding Exhibitions!

Catwalk

Our catwalk really is something to behold. Witness a professional choreographed Milan-style catwalk using professionally trained runway models, showcasing the latest bridal wear, mother of the bride and grooms wear collections.

VIP Experience

Want to make your day extra special? Book an I Do VIP Experience package and celebrate your nuptials with your whole wedding party! Includes fast track entry, upgraded goody bag, glass of prosecco & reserved catwalk seating.

Live Stage

On before each catwalk show is the live stage entertainment! Featuring instrumentalists, wedding bands and even singing waiters, the live stage gives you a real feel of how these entertainers will perform on your own wedding day.
