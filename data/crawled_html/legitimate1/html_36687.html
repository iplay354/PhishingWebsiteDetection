What is Duplex Stainless Steel Pipe?

Duplex Stainless Steel Pipe is a type of stainless steel piping  material characterized by a two-phase microstructure consisting of approximately equal proportions of austenite and ferrite. According to technical standards such as ASTM A789/A789M and ASTM A790/A790M, duplex stainless steel pipes are engineered to offer superior mechanical strength and enhanced resistance to stress corrosion cracking, pitting, and crevice corrosion compared to conventional austenitic or ferritic stainless steels. Common grades include UNS S31803 (2205) and UNS S32750 (2507), which contain balanced compositions of chromium（typically 20-28%), nickel (4.5-8%), molybdenum (up to 4%), and nitrogen to stabilize the dual-phase structure and optime corrosion performance and toughness.
