December 17, 2025

District 103 is pleased to announce the appointment of Ali Finn as the new Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum, Instruction & Assessment.

December 2, 2025

All new Early Childhood students may now register via the Early Childhood Intake Form for the Lincolnshire-Prairie View School District 103 half day and extended day option for the 2026-2027 school year!

DW Ranked Among Top Illinois Middle Schools
November 3, 2025

Daniel Wright Junior High School earned an impressive #11 ranking among Illinois middle schools, according to U.S. News & World Report’s 2026 Best Elementary and Middle Schools in the Nation. 

District Honored for Student Data Privacy Protection
