Why study with CNM?

At CNM you’ll find students from all walks of life. The main reasons why they choose CNM are:

  • CNM is the UK’s No 1 training provider for Natural therapies
  • CNM’s options to study part-time, full-time or online
  • CNM’s unmatched focus on clinical application and practice
  • CNM’s commitment to train students to become successful practitioners
  • CNM’s naturopathic approach to wellness, looking at the whole person, rather than symptoms.
  • 80% of CNM graduates are practising (part time or full time)
  • CNM’s highly qualified lecturers who are all practising themselves
  • CNM’s good reputation in the UK, Europe and internationally

Our Mission

  • To provide thorough, practice-orientated training in natural therapies such as Naturopathy, Nutrition, Herbal Medicine, Acupuncture, Homeopathy, Health Coaching and Natural/Vegan Chef.
  • To train students to become highly competent, successful practitioners.
  • To make sure CNM courses are widely recognised and respected in the UK, Europe, and internationally.
  • To continuously raise the profile of natural therapies in society.
  • To support and promote CNM graduates after qualification.

UK Principal’s Study Fund – BSc Nutritional Therapy, Naturopathic Nutrition, Herbal Medicine, Acupuncture, Homeopathy

Offer valid from Monday 8th December 2025 until 24th December 2025. Applicable to new students (who have not enrolled on a CNM BSc (Hons) or Diploma course in any capacity before), enrolling for a CNM BSc (Hons) in Nutritional Therapy or a CNM Diploma in Naturopathic Nutrition, Herbal Medicine, Acupuncture or Homeopathy lasting a minimum of 3 academic years. To qualify the enrolee must pay the enrolment fee within the offer period. Eligible students will receive £100 discount against study fees in year 1, £150 in year 2 and a further £250 in year 3 to a total of £500 (course fees – not pre-requisites, where applicable). For clarity, any students who defer from their original schedule as stated on their enrolment form, or do not proceed with subsequent years of their course will not be entitled to any discounts (except for those that were previously received during those academic years completed). This offer cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount including student discount, ambassador or referral incentives etc. Discounts are non-transferrable, and no cash alternatives will be offered. CNM reserves the right to amend, terminate or withdraw from the offer at any time.
