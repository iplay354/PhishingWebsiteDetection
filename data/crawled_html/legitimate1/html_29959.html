Chalet at Winghaven

Chalet at Winghaven

Size –

3 Bedrooms • 3 Bathrooms 3 Baths • Sleeps 6-8
Amenities – Allows pets Internet Wood Stove Mountain View

Located on the south slope of Burney Mountain in the beautiful Mountains of North Carolina, the Chalet at Winghaven is a spacious, sunny log home. Close to Asheville (twelve miles to downtown) and all its attractions, the Chalet is surrounded by 38 acres of picturesque hardwood forest. Wild turkeys rustle the fallen leaves in winter and lead their young in spring. An abundance of birds and other wildlife can be spotted from the sunny porches and a large south facing deck. Amazingly this natural oasis is hiking distance from the local pizza place and the closest large grocery store is less than four miles away!
