Bringing people into a closer relationship with Jesus through visual media.

We create and distribute visual, audio, and print media that is easily and freely accessible to the world in a language everyone can understand.

Speaking in a language everyone can understand.

The unreached and the next generation speak the same language: visual media.

We spread the Gospel message through Christian media to visual learners worldwide.

It is estimated that over 3 billion people are unreached by the Gospel in our world today. Scripture reading is at an all-time low among Americans—especially the next generation. But with smartphone ownership growing at 14.9% annually, now, more than ever, people are engaging with visual media. RevelationMedia brings the Gospel message through Christian media directly to the devices people already have in their hands.
