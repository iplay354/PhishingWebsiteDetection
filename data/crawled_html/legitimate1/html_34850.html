Premium Natural Stone Supplier

STS Stone is an established Sydney-based natural stone supplier with over 30 years of experience.

You’re here because you’re looking for quality, materials that last a lifetime. Natural stone isn’t just a design choice; it’s a statement. A commitment to authenticity, durability, and timeless beauty that only nature can create. At STS STONE, we offer an extensive range of premium tiles and slabs, making us your trusted, all-in-one partner for any residential or commercial project. For over 30 years, we’ve collaborated with Australia’s leading architects, designers, and builders to bring beautiful stone to life. Whether you’re creating a contemporary home or a timeless retreat, our team is here to guide you, from stone selection to installation advice.

The Seaside House is a refined, mid-century-inspired coastal home designed by Alexandra Kidd with Rich Carr Architects.Stone: Antarctic White & Travertine Vein Cut.

Featured in The Local Project, Cove House is a six-bedroom Sydney home inspired by sandstone ridges. Stone: Crema Valencia Limestone Flooring.

Kambala Residence, a serene family sanctuary designed by Bevan Boss Designs and Alexandra Kidd Atelier. Stone: Florence Marble, Arabescato Marble, Oro Luce Limestone. 

Designed by Stafford Architecture, Old South Residence features strong lines, curved forms, and a striking cylindrical staircase. Stone: Travertine Grigio Chiaro, Statuario Marble.

Designed by Group GSA, the Chanel Headquarters in North Sydney is a masterpiece of luxury and design. Stone: Florence Marble.
