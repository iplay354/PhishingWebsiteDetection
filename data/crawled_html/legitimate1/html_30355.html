When Do I Need Garage Door Repairs?

Garage doors are a part of your home’s exterior, which means they’re exposed to the elements and more likely to suffer from damage. If you’ve noticed that your garage door is sticking, grinding, squeaking, or not opening or closing properly, it may be time for some repairs.

A few common issues with garage doors include:

1

The safety sensors on the garage door are not working properly. This could be because they are not aligned correctly, or they are broken.

2

You notice gaps or cracks in the panels of your garage door when it is closed. This could mean that there is something wrong with the hinges or tracks, which will require replacement.

3

The garage door starts making a lot of noise when opening and closing, which indicates that something is wrong with the garage door opener mechanism.

4

Sticking: If your garage door sticks when you try to open or close it, the springs may be broken or worn out.

5

Not opening or closing properly: If your garage door doesn’t open all the way or closes slowly, there might be a problem with the motor or gears inside.

Garage door repairs are necessary when the garage door is not working correctly, either because a part of the system has broken or is out of alignment. If your garage door does not close properly and you have to keep pushing it up manually, or if you can’t open your garage door at all, call Team Taylor Doors for help!
