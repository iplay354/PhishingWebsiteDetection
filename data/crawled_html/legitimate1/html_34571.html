Diverse Range of Roles

Actor, Author, and Environmental Advocate

I am Ed Begley Jr., an actor known for several notable roles in television and film. Through Begleys Best, I'm delighted to showcase my story and passion for acting. I was inspired by the works of my Academy Award-winning father to become an actor. I first caught audiences' attention with my portrayal of Dr. Victor Ehrlich on the long-running hit television series "St. Elsewhere," for which I received six Emmy nominations.

I starred in David Mamet's "November" at the Mark Taper Forum and have appeared in several other works by this amazing playwright, including "The Cryptogram" in Boston, New York, and Los Angeles, as well as "Romance," also at the Mark Taper Forum.
