Big City Bride

We are the Chicago wedding planner with more than 20 years of planning Chicago’s most beautiful weddings and the care of a family business. Our company is based out of our charming Lincoln Park office, where we work one-on-one with you to create the Chicago wedding you’ll adore, while a full team of planners assists behind the scenes.

Our unique approach features a team of full-time wedding planners and employees of Big City Bride. We keep office hours that will accommodate most any schedule. We do not limit time or communication with our clients. We welcome an open-line of communication so we can help when you need us most.

Of all our accolades, we are most proud of the hundreds of happy couples and their families who say that hiring Big City Bride was the best wedding planning decision they made. Our goal is to ensure you’ll say the same.
