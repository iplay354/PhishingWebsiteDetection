Society of Operations Engineers

SOE is the professional home for members working to inspect, maintain and manage the equipment and machinery which keeps people and businesses safe on a daily basis. We are a Professional Engineering Institution and registered charity, and our members work to implement smarter ways of working, improve maintenance and inspection processes, and embed environmentally friendly and sustainable engineering practice. SOE actively champions the next generation of engineers, and is passionate about making a global difference to the world around us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our frequently asked questions.

What is the Society of Operations Engineers (SOE)? +
SOE is a professional membership organisation that supports individuals and organisations in engineering-related fields, offering training, development, and accreditation opportunities.
How do I reset my password if I’ve forgotten it? +
If you've forgotten your password, click on the forgotten password link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and you'll receive instructions to reset your password. If you face issues, contact support at [email protected].
How can I become a member of SOE? +
You can apply for membership directly on our website. Visit the join now page, choose the appropriate category (e.g., Student, Associate, Member, Fellow), and complete the online application form.
What are the benefits of SOE membership? +
Members receive access to industry-recognised qualifications, training resources, professional development support, and networking opportunities within the engineering sector.
How do I contact SOE for further assistance? +
You can reach us via email at [email protected] or by calling +44 (0)20 7630 1111. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, excluding public holidays.
How can I find events or training sessions hosted by SOE? +
Visit our events section to view a calendar of upcoming workshops, webinars, and networking opportunities. You can register for events directly through the website.
How do I renew my membership? +
You can renew your membership online by going to your account and navigating to the subscriptions & payments page then buy clicking the pay now button. Payments can be made via card or direct debit.
Where can I find resources and publications from SOE? +
Members can access exclusive resources, such as technical guides and industry updates, in the member portal. Non-members can explore some publications via the Resources tab.
Does SOE offer support for career development? +
Yes, SOE provides career support through mentorship programmes, CPD (Continuing Professional Development) resources, and guidance for achieving professional qualifications such as EngTech, IEng, or CEng.
