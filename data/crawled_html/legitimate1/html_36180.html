Tropic Air, the Airline of Belize

Tropic Air connects travelers to the most stunning destinations in Belize with ease and convenience; with flights to San Pedro, a hub for exploring the Belize Barrier Reef; flights to Placencia, known for its pristine beaches and laid-back charm; flights to Dangriga, the gateway to the Garifuna culture; flights to Caye Caulker, the ultimate island escape; flights to Belize City, the country’s commercial center; flights to Corozal, a peaceful northern retreat; and flights to Punta Gorda, a lush haven in the south; and other international flights. Discover Belize’s beauty, culture, and adventure with Tropic Air’s reliable domestic flights.

Our Values

Safety

Safety is our top priority and will never be compromised. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of safety in every aspect of our operations, ensuring the well-being of our passengers, crew, and aircraft.

Service

From booking to boarding, we are dedicated to providing professional service and care to ensure a seamless and enjoyable journey. Service excellence and passenger experience is at the heart of everything we do.

Sustainability

We are working to preserve the natural beauty of the communities we serve for future generations, by investing in renewable energy sources and seeking opportunities to reduce our carbon footprint.
