Booking A Session

Louisville Photography Sessions start at $75.

 

The session fee covers the photographer’s time and talent from designing, directing, posing, and using my technical expertise during your session.

 

After your portrait session, purchase only the images you LOVE. You will never pay for any images you don’t want.

 

However, my job is to create beautiful portraits of you that you will love so that you want them all.

 

(Not in love?, No worries. I offer a personal guarantee with all my sessions).

 

On average clients invest $500 to over $2000  for their custom heirloom prints to display proudly in their home in the form of albums and wall art and to share via digital collections.
