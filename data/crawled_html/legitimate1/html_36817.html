Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to A2Z Ozone! We manufacture and sell high-quality ozone generators using Corona Discharge Technology for residential and commercial applications. Below are answers to our most common questions. For technical questions not covered here, please contact us at [email protected] or call +1 502 499 4977 (option 1).

What are ozone generators used for?

A2Z Ozone generators are designed for a wide range of applications including air treatment, water purification, odor elimination, food storage, pool treatment, and more. We have an option for almost any ozone application you need.

Where can I find how-to guides and videos?

How-To Blog: Visit our How-To Blog https://a2zozone.com/category/blogs/how-to/ for articles on using products, removing odors, cleaning vegetables, treating water, and more.

Video: Check out our YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@atozozone for visual demonstrations and helpful references.

Odor Guide: See our Odor Type Page: https://a2zozone.com/a-complete-guide-to-odor-removal/ for a comprehensive guide on how ozone can help eliminate specific odors.

How does the ceramic plate compare to Corona Discharge Technology?

The ceramic plate in our ozone generators produces Corona Discharge—they’re the same technology.

Do your products meet FDA/EPA requirements?

The FDA does not approve specific ozone generators. While the EPA regulates ozone generators (we display our EPA number on our website and units), regulation compliance is different from approval. Any company claiming FDA or EPA approval is misleading.
