Who we are

With more than 63,000 members in affiliated clubs in every state, the District of Columbia, and more than a dozen countries, GFWC members work in their own communities to support the arts, preserve natural resources, advance education, promote healthy lifestyles, and encourage civic involvement, issues related to domestic and sexual violence awareness/prevention, and advocating for children.

GFWC International Day of Service

The General Federation of Women’s Clubs (GFWC) is united in its dedication to volunteer community service. The GFWC International Day of Service brings together clubs from around the world to stand united against domestic and sexual violence.

Learn about the 2025 GFWC International Day of Service

Signature Program: Domestic and Sexual Violence Awareness and Prevention

GFWC creates and leads education, public awareness, and service projects for club members and local citizens at the community level; collaborates with national domestic violence networks and organizations; supports existing activities and established programs; advocates for victims and survivors; and provides educational opportunities to survivors.

GFWC aims to be a powerful voice for victims and survivors.

WHRC: Women’s History and Resource Center

Founded in 1984, the GFWC Women’s History and Resource Center (WHRC) collects, preserves, interprets, and promotes the history of GFWC. The WHRC documents the social and political contributions of GFWC clubwomen from 1890 to the present through the GFWC archives and related special collections.

GFWC International President Suellen B. Brazil Honored by Women’s Suffrage National Monument Foundation (WSNMF)

Last week, WSNMF President and CEO Anna Laymon honored GFWC International President Suellen B. Brazil for GFWC’s support of the monument’s vision. Laymon presented Brazil with a bust of iconic suffragist Inez Milholland.

Milholland famously led the 1913 Woman Suffrage Procession as the elegant “herald on the white horse,” becoming a lasting symbol of the women’s suffrage movement. The bust was accepted on behalf of GFWC in recognition of the organization’s early belief in the monument and its unwavering commitment to bringing the vision to life. Under GFWC International President Brazil’s leadership, the tireless dedication of GFWC members has proven invaluable to the project’s success in raising funds and celebrating the official site location on the National Mall.

Reflecting on the partnership, Laymon praised the collaboration and leadership that made the site location milestone possible. “Suellen’s leadership has been absolutely transformational, and the support of the vast GFWC membership extraordinary,” said Laymon. “It has been a pleasure working with Suellen and members of the GFWC Executive Committee to achieve this celebrated milestone together, making a historic week truly unforgettable.”

To make a year-end donation, visit: womensmonument.org/donate-gfwc/

It's WHRC Wednesday! Continuing with our GFWC Headquarters "Fun Facts" in honor of 1734 N Street's 150th anniversary, here's one that is often a surprise to visitors: the GFWC International President moves in during her term, using an apartment built specially for this purpose.

This somewhat unusual arrangement helps to make GFWC Headquarters more than a place of business, and even more than a repository for GFWC's long history; it is a home. And during the holidays, while the second floor is for public parties, the President's Apartment can be a place for quieter celebrations. For example, GFWC President Mary Katherine Miller (1974-1976) hosted a small family gathering here during her second Christmas in office, complete with a decorated tree and a home-cooked meal. In her December 1975 column in "General Federation Clubwoman," Miller wrote,

"[My husband] Carroll and my sister, Miss Virginia Lee Barnes, will join me here for the holidays at your beautiful GFWC headquarters where I'll do our cooking of turkey 'n all. A MERRY, MERRY CHRISTMAS – HAPPY HANUKKAH – and may all the things you want the most be yours!"

Speaking of Headquarters, it will be closed for the holidays December 24th through January 2nd; WHRC Wednesdays will resume next year on January 8th. Please accept our best wishes for a wonderful holiday season, and a very happy New Year!

Take action today! 📢

Join GFWC in urging the Senate to pass the bipartisan Trafficking Survivors Relief Act of 2025 — vital legislation that would allow survivors to expunge certain convictions tied to crimes they were forced to commit during their exploitation.

The House has already passed this bill unanimously. Now it’s the Senate’s turn to ensure survivors aren’t punished for the abuse they endured. Traffickers routinely coerce victims into acts like financial fraud, drug-related offenses, and identity theft, leaving them with criminal records that follow them long after escape.

It’s time to break the cycle. It’s time to give survivors a chance to rebuild.

Tell the Senate to pass the Trafficking Survivors Relief Act now!

To make your voice count, visit the GFWC Legislative Action Center to send a message to your senators: www.gfwc.org/legislative-action/

During the 2023 GFWC Annual Convention held in Louisville, the Epsilon Sigma Omicron (ESO), an honorary educational society, hosted a fun "My Name In Print" breakfast. Guests paid $20 per entry to be a character in a future book published by Author Robin Yocum. The proceeds from the drawing benefited the GFWC Access to Education Fund Scholarship for low-income and at-risk students.

The winner was Jolie Frankfurth, 2024-2026 GFWC First Vice President. Much to her delight and surprise, Robin drew her name from a large silver punch bowl. International Past President Deb Strahanoski (2022-2024) had the pleasure of reading Jolie`s name out loud, much to her surprise and astonishment.

Needless to say, it was a surreal, fun experience, as you can see all three shared a serendipitous moment!

To read about Jolie`s character and to purchase a book, Mr. Yocum recommends visiting your community bookstore to support local commerce.

In the event you`re unable to get out, you can also order on Amazon at: https://www.amazon.com/Last-Hitman-Novel…/dp/B0DZ1XXRCQ/

The ESO Breakfast occurs annually during the GFWC Annual Convention in support of GFWC Community Service Program: Education and Libraries.

This Giving Tuesday, we’re inviting our community to help complete a transformation that has been years in the making. Our Facade Restoration and Beautification Project is nearing its final phase, and thanks to generous supporters, we have already raised $118,000 of our $125,000 goal.

One of the most important—and labor-intensive—parts of the restoration involves stripping away layers of old, deteriorating paint.

This step is essential because it:
✨ Reveals and protects the building’s original materials
✨ Prevents moisture damage caused by trapped paint layers
✨ Allows us to apply new, durable, historically appropriate finishes
✨ Restores the beauty and integrity of a space central to our mission
