    BUS SERVICES

    D2A2

    Going between Detroit and Ann Arbor Michigan? Take D2A2. It's an express bus service. Whether you are going to a Tigers game, concert, festival, or commuting to work, D2A2 can get you there. All buses have the amenities you can expect from a premium bus service. Tickets start as low as $2. The bus departs nearly every hour on weekdays and has plenty of options on weekends.

    Buy a Ticket

    MICHIGAN FLYER

    Michigan Flyer is the most convenient way to get to DTW. It's an airport transportation shuttle bus that loops between the Detroit Metro Airport, East Lansing, Brighton, and Ann Arbor. You'll enjoy a luxurious bus with large roomy seats, charging outlets, Wi-Fi, and an onboard restroom. With multiple departures daily and affordable rates, why drive?
