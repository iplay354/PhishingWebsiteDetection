Our Locations

TENTH

Located just blocks from downtown and accessible from the TART trail, our Tenth Street location offers a European-style market packed with local, organic, fresh goods and a daily hot bar and cafe featuring coffee drinks, smoothies and sandwiches.

Address
260 E. Tenth Street Traverse City, MI 49684

Hours
8am – 8pm • Cafe hours: 8am – 6pm

Directions More Info

WEST

Conveniently located on US-31, our West side location is the same great co-op, just with a larger store footprint to offer you more of what you love. West is also home to a full-service meat and seafood counter, deli, salad bar, and cafe.

Address
3587 Marketplace Circle Traverse City, MI 49684

Hours
8am – 9pm • Coffee Bar & Café: 8am – 6pm

Directions More Info

Sprout Cafe

In partnership with Cowell Family Cancer Center, we’re proud to serve visitors, staff, and patients our signature coffee beverages, juices and smoothies, as well as a selection of pre-made sandwiches, salads, and baked goods.

Address
217 S. Madison St. (at the Cowell Family Cancer Center)

Hours
Mon - Fri 7am-2pm
