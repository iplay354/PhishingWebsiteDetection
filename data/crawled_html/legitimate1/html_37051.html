First-Hand Thoughts on the Knox Experience

  • I received a lot of support, not just from people who were studying here at the time, but also those who were working in Admissions. They were very open to my questions and very welcoming.

    Filza Khan '24

    Computer Science Major

    Meet Filza

  • You meet people from all different majors, all different interests, and all different skill levels. It's a really good mix of people, and it allows you to build connections with them from all sorts of different backgrounds with similar interests to you.

    Matthew Sprenger '24

    Political Science Major, German & Psychology Double Minor

    Meet Matthew

  • So much of my experience at Knox began with an impromptu discussion with a peer or a faculty member, which then grew into research projects, independent studies, and more. Learning how to expose myself to these kinds of opportunities has been a skill that I have been able to deeply hone as a Knox student.

    Isaac Hughes '21

    Environmental Studies & Philosophy Double Major

    Meet Isaac

  • The Knox experience is truly unlike any other. The campus is on the smaller side, but for me, that just meant that my relationships ran deeper. Knox is special because of the people here.

    Kylie Hoang '20

    History Major, Museum Studies & Environmental Studies Double Minor
