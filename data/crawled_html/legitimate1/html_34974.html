About Me

My two teenage sons had serious issues with acne. One boy had it on his face and the other had it on his back. I had no idea that the way you battle acne on the back and face are different. I was treating them both the same and we weren't having any success in treating either one of them. When I did find a product that worked well for one, it did nothing for the other. Body acne is especially difficult to battle. Our blog will show you what you can do for both body and face acne and actually end up with clear skin and how a dermatologist can treat different issues.
