TV program viewing times

Our TV program can be viewed on broadcast TV on digital channel 44 (C31) or streamed live on the Internet at the URL https://ctvplus.org.au/stream/melbourne/. Live broadcast and streaming times are Saturday January 3, 2026 at 10.00 pm. It will be rebroadcast at 1.00 am on Monday January 5, 2026. There is no replay facility for a show at present, but we are investigating how that might be achieved.

NuCountry membership

NuCountry exists to support and promote country music, particularly Australian musicians and is run entirely by volunteers. It was founded back in the 1980’s and has a history in broadcast radio and television.

Needless to say there are costs involved in producing the television show, hosting the website and meeting the usual compliance costs.

Our loyal members and sponsors contribute towards meeting our costs. If you wish to support country music and NuCountry, please become a member or renew your membership.
