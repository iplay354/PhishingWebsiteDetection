Welcome. I’m so glad you’re here.

I’m Sheri Yutzy, a Mennonite fantasy author.

I write atmospheric fantasy stories inspired by real times, places, and people.

THE SELLER OF SECRETS, my debut YA romantasy novel, is inspired by historical Florence, Italy, and based on legends of the Renaissance-era Medici family. THE SELLER OF SECRETS, will be available from Keylight Books in April 2026.

Be sure to sign up for my newsletter on the right so you don’t miss my monthly updates and in depth conversations! You’ll also receive my curated list of 25 Atmospheric Fantasy Novels.

About Sheri

Sheri Yutzy lives in a small Ohio city in an old Italianate home with her musician husband and four beautiful children. When she’s not writing, she’s tending her garden, baking something, or pulling her youngest children in their wagon.

Find Sheri online at Instagram @sheriyutzyauthor and Facebook @sheriyutzy.
