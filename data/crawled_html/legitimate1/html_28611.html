Discover the Best of Bedford

Love Bedford is your comprehensive guide to everything unique in Bedford town centre. It’s a vibrant showcase of businesses, exclusive offers, exciting events, culture and heritage, the latest news and everything that makes this town truly special. Love Bedford also contains a directory for essential services, how to get here and where to stay.

Join our Bedford Directory

We’re excited to announce that the lovebedford.co.uk directory is now open to businesses across Bedford and the surrounding area – and best of all, it’s completely free to join.

Our site attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors each year – people actively looking for local inspiration, events places to go, and businesses to support.

Whether you run a shop, salon, café, or offer services, this is your chance to get discovered by a wider local audience.

Universal Studios Bedford

We are properly excited about the Universal Studios plans for Bedford. If it goes ahead, it could put our town on the map in a way we have never seen before, bringing new jobs, more visitors, and a big boost for local businesses. We have put together a clear, no waffle guide to what has been submitted, what it could mean for Bedford, and what happens next, so you can get the facts fast and keep up with the latest updates.

Love Bedford

Love Bedford is a long-established brand created in 2005 to celebrate and promote everything great about Bedford — its businesses, its people, and the events that bring the town to life.

We’re here to shine a light on the best our town has to offer: from independent shops and local services to cultural highlights, community stories, and things to see and do.

Through our website and active social media presence, Love Bedford connects residents and visitors with what’s happening in and around the town — helping to support local businesses and keep Bedford thriving.
