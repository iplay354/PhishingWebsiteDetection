Frequently asked questions

Can You Rank My Website Page #1 in Google?

If any SEO consultant or agency is promising you rankings in a month, you should run far away. The SEO process involves numerous variables and rankings. I use all of the best SEO practices that Google recommends doing, which will increase the chances of your website rankings over time.

What is SEO?

SEO is an acronym for Search Engine Optimization. SEO involves a variety of strategies to improve a website’s rankings. Such strategies include setting up your Google Analytics, creating high quality content, technical SEO, and outreach.

What are the Benefits of SEO?

 

  • Improve your branding
  • Increased rankings
  • Increased organic traffic
  • Increased calls/web forms
  • Increased Sales
  • Save $ on paid ads
SEO or PPC?

This depends on your needs. If you’re looking for immediate traffic and results, Pay-Per-Click may be the way to go. If you’re trying to capture traffic long term, SEO is the way to go. You can also use both to compliment each other.

Why is SEO Important?

SEO is important because it helps people find your website and come in contact with your brand for the first time. If you have a a good web design, you have the potential to convert that person into a client for life. When you stay up to date on the latest SEO trends, you’ll ensure that your website is receiving the traffic it deserves!

How Long Does it Take to Start Seeing Results?

There’s too many variable to provide a concrete answer. A business with an established website could generally see results within a few months (2-6). A brand new website would require a longer commitment before you start gaining any traction.

Do I have to Sign a Contract for your Monthly SEO Services?

I do not require you to sign a contract. SEO is a monthly commitment, and you should be prepared to have some type of monthly budget if you’re shopping for SEO services.
