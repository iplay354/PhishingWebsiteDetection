About Us


Welcome to Weekend Kitchen, a not-so-typical kitchen and home store tucked away at 16 Main Street in the charming village of Essex, CT. Step inside and you’ll discover an ever-changing collection of unique finds—an eclectic blend of new and vintage treasures curated with care.

Within our shop, you’ll also find Green Grape Press, our bespoke letterpress studio. Using antique presses, we handcraft cards, stationery, invitations, and other ephemera the traditional way—one piece at a time. Each design is lovingly hand-set and printed on luxurious 100% cotton papers with the finest inks.

But Weekend Kitchen is more than a store—it’s also a place to cook, learn, and gather. Our cooking classes, led by some of the shoreline’s most talented chefs, are designed to be both fun and educational. Whether you’re a passionate vegan, devoted carnivore, or somewhere in between, you’ll find a class that excites your palate and inspires your kitchen adventures.

At Basil Health, I bring together my background as a PhD-trained expert in Nutrition and Immunology with my certifications as a National Board–Certified Health and Wellness Coach (NBHWC) and a Certified Culinary Coach. I offer personalized coaching and culinary guidance designed to help you create sustainable habits, improve your nutrition, and feel more empowered in your everyday life. Whether you’re looking to make lasting lifestyle changes, explore healthier ways of eating, or gain confidence in the kitchen, my approach blends science, coaching, and practical skills to support you every step of the way.
