FAQ - NTI Express Auto Care

What are the services offered by NTI?
NTI provides a one-stop car care services including car wash, detailing, repair & maintenance, body work, PPF filming, car trading and auto insurance.
Do I need an appointment to drop by at NTI?
Car wash is on a first come first serve basis. Detailing, maintenance and other services can be arranged by booking.
What type of payment options does NTI provide?
We accept any form of electronic payment in Hong Kong (Octopus, Visa, Master Card, AMEX, FPS, WeChat Pay, Alipay, Samsung Pay, Apple Pay)
Can I buy the packages online?
You may download the NTI Mall App (Google Play Store and Apple Store) to purchase NTI car services and products.

Can the packages be used at all NTI shops?

Yes, all packages can be used at any NTI branch.

Can one package be used for multiple cars?
Packages can be used for multiple cars under the name of the registered package owner.
Can I wash my car after applying traditional coating / filming?
NTI utilizes pH neutral eco-friendly detergents that does not affect any materials including traditional coating and filming.
