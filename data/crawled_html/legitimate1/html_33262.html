        The ESF Europe 2026 conference, to be held in Antwerp from 24‑26 February, gathers industry leaders, innovators and policymakers around the theme of decarbonising Europe’s downstream sectors. It focuses on implementing clean technologies—like carbon capture, renewables, advanced recycling, hydrogen and digital optimisation—to align with EU industrial sustainability goals. Attendees will explore practical case studies, policy frameworks and strategies aimed at transforming refining and chemicals industries into greener, more competitive actors.
