Every Car Dealership needs a Mobile Dealer App

M-Link: The mobile app link between you and your customers

M-Link is a complete mobile solution that provides your dealership or auto group with a branded mobile app for your customers to shop, schedule service, earn rewards, and more. Manage your branded mobile app with your Mobile App Command Center to customize your app, schedule push notifications, create loyalty rewards, and many other features to keep your customers engaged and retained.

Customer Retention Features

Communication

Reach and engage with your automotive customers with the power of Push Notifications. Go even further with automated notifications based on location or beacon technology.

Service Scheduling

With just a few taps customers can schedule service. Reward those customers with a built in fully featured, fully customizable loyalty rewards program.

Digital Retailing

Customers bring the showroom home with live inventory feeds, complete with photos. Schedule test drives, apply for financing, and free soft credit pulls natively in the mobile dealer app.
