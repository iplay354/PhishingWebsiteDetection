The HardingFPA is a suite of applications for compliance with international guidelines on Flashing and Spatial Patterns in Broadcast Video, including Ofcom, ITU, NAB-J and ISO.

Our analysis engine is physiologically defined and detects only genuine violations. Results are consistent across all editions and content formats. When video does fail, we provide detailed frame-by-frame results in visual 'heat map' and tabular form, to enable you to fix it creatively, without degrading your content.

There are no hidden fees. Our packages include expert advice on fixing failures, product support and incremental software updates.

Trusted by post houses, broadcasters and regulators since 2001. Consistent industry wide results, and interoperability with our technology partners, including Telestream, Venera, Clearcast and Vanderquest.
