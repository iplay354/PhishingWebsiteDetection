Introducing a Leading Valve Manufacturer & Exporter in India

Our journey began in 1993 as a modest shop in Ahmedabad, and by 1995, we expanded to a 2000 sq ft workshop, laying the foundation for our growth. Spearheaded by the entrepreneurial spirit of Mr. M F Kagdi, we swiftly rose to prominence, driven by our passion for innovation. Today, we boast a floor space area of over 1,50,000 sq mtrs, a testament to our exponential growth and scale.

Specializing in automation products, we have evolved into one of the largest manufacturing units in the industry, offering comprehensive “one-stop solutions.”

Our relentless pursuit of excellence has led to multiple patents, a testament to our commitment to pushing boundaries.

What sets us apart is not just our cutting-edge products but also our unwavering dedication to customer satisfaction. Our strong after-sales service support has fostered enduring relationships, with clients staying loyal for over 30 years.

We extend a warm invitation to visit our factory in Ahmedabad and witness firsthand the blend of innovation, quality, and commitment that defines us.
