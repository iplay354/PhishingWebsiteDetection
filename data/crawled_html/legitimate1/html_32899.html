About The Film

The Commitment is an inspiring film about an interracial gay couple who, in their quest to start a family, discover something very valuable about their relationship. Set against the backdrop of adoption, this moving and heartfelt film explores the universal struggle of two people to remain committed to each other through both the happiest and the most difficult of times.
