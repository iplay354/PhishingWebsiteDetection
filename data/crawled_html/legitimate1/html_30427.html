St. Clare Memorial GArden





















Our St. Clare Memorial Garden project began in 2020.


During the planning of this project, we decided that our Memorial Garden should include space for family, friends and benefactors. This will be a place to pray and to remember our loved ones who have journeyed to their Eternal Home. Each memorial stone will have 3 lines and 13 characters per line available for engraving. Since space is limited, stones will be reserved on a first come, first served basis. See the links below for a reservation form, or contact us, and we will send you a reservation form.
