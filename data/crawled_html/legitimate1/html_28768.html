MEMBERSHIP

Welcome to Wilderness Ridge, Lincoln's premier country club. Enjoy our 18-hole Championship Course, 9-hole Executive Course, Aquatics Center, Tennis & Pickleball Complex, Fitness Center, Lodge Restaurant, and Members-only Bar & Grill.

THE LODGE RESTAURANT

Enter through the oversized carved front doors of The Lodge Restaurant and immerse yourself in one of the most unique dining atmospheres. Open to the public, The Lodge welcomes everyone to enjoy its exceptional cuisine and warm hospitality.
