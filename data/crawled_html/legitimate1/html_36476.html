Visit the LST-325 in Evansville, Indiana

Visiting a historical museum provides a tangible connection to the past. Through artifacts, exhibits, and interactive displays, museums bring history to life, allowing us to understand the lives, struggles, and triumphs of those who came before us. This deepens our understanding of our society, cultures, and world. Museums serve as valuable educational resources, fostering critical thinking, inspiring curiosity, and preserving our collective memory for future generations.

The Landing Ship Tank, or “Large Slow Target” as the LST was called by her crew, revolutionized amphibious warfare, enabling large-scale landings of troops and equipment on hostile shores. Its design and capabilities influenced the development of subsequent amphibious landing ships.

You can read more about the history of the LST 325 and her long journey home here.

Check the weather before you visit as we may be closed for tours. ice or snow on The decks presents a hazard to crew & visitors.

Current Exhibitions

Immerse yourself in history by exploring our diverse range of exhibitions. From interactive displays to rare artifacts, our exhibitions offer an educational and engaging experience for all visitors.

Do you have items that you would like to donate to the museum?  

ARTIFACTS

Historical Treasure

Our collection of artifacts showcases the rich history of WWII. Each item tells a unique story and provides insight into the lives of those who served. Discover the significance behind each piece and its connection to this pivotal period in history.

INTERACTIVE DISPLAYS

Engaging Experiences

Step into history with our interactive displays that bring the past to life. Through hands-on experiences and multimedia presentations, visitors can gain a deeper understanding of WWII events and the impact they had on the world.

RARE MEMORABILIA

Unique Collections

Delve into our rare memorabilia collection, featuring one-of-a-kind items from the WWII era. From personal belongings to military paraphernalia, each piece offers a glimpse into the past and honors the sacrifices made during this time.

SPECIAL EXHIBITS

Limited-Time Displays

Experience our rotating special exhibits that highlight specific aspects of WWII history. These temporary displays offer a fresh perspective and allow visitors to explore different themes and events related to the war.
