fort worth interior designer

At Amy Horany Interiors, Amy has spent well over a decade fine tuning her unique layered, lived-in, yet welcoming aesthetic. Her highest priority when designing a space is ensuring the home instantly feels laid-back and inviting, while simultaneously elevated and elegant.

Through our designs, we seek to create atmospheres that are both calming and captivating, where every corner evokes a sense of adventure, curiosity and tranquility.

MEET THE DESIGNER

Amy Horany of Amy Horany Interiors is a Fort Worth-based interior designer with an eclectic, laid-back, yet timeless aesthetic. Her ability to merge an array of varying styles sets her apart from the everyday designer.

Born from her love of antiques and old homes, Amy’s vision for every project is to breathe life back into the home. 

Amy offers residential interior design services for remodels, home additions, room renovations, furniture and accessory sourcing, room styling & more.
