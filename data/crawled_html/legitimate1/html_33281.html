Welcome from Michael Bamford - ARS President:

On behalf of the thousands of rhododendron enthusiasts worldwide "welcome to our web site." Here you will find valuable resources which enable you to learn more about the selection and care of rhododendrons. We hope this will encourage you to explore all that the genus Rhododendron (which includes azaleas) has to offer, and you will include these wonderful plants in your garden.

ARS 2026 Annual Convention

Join us on beautiful Vancouver Island, British Columbia April 21 – 25, 2026 for the American Rhododendron Society Convention, Rhododendrons and Companions in a Changing Climate. Enjoy public and private garden tours from Victoria to the Cowichan Valley and Qualicum Beach. Learn about the new Greig Species Garden at Milner Gardens and Woodland. Support the Rhododendron Species Botanical Garden at their AGM with Steve Hootman and his team. Hear the science and practical ideas for our gardens as our climate changes. More information

2025 ARS Honor Medals Awarded

The ARS takes great pleasure in presenting its Gold Medal honors to Bill Stipe of the Pilchuck Chapter and Tim Walsh of the Eureka Chapter. Silver Medal honorees are Sharon Bryson of the Atlantic Chapter and Maria Stewart, posthumously. Ron Miller of the Azalea Chapter has been given the Pioneer Achievement Award for his insightful field observations of rhododendrons in southeastern U.S.  View the ARS honors citations.

Rhododendron International Volume 8

The latest volume of this free online journal contains articles of interest to rhododendron aficionados. Read about new satsuki azalea hybrids, the Pukeiti NZ vireya collection, rhododendron gardens in Iceland, and maddenia rhododendrons at Logan Botanic Garden in Scotland.  These articles are deemed to have broad reader appeal.

2025 Rhododendron of the Year Awards

The ARS proudly announces its Rhododendron of the Year (ROY) selections for 2025. With excellent foliage and plant habit, beautiful flowers, and disease and pest resistance, the ROY selections are highly recommended for utilization in your landscape.
