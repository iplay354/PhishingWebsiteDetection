Real Trees

5 to 15% percent of Revenue Plants Real Trees!

A portion of all revenue that Tappy Tree receives from the mobile stores and advertising networks will be used to Plant Real Trees. We will periodically pay this amount to our current nonprofit third party reforestation partner (One Tree Planted). They will facilitate the planting.

In App Purchase Details

Players can play for free or purchase add on content. 5 to 15% of in App purchase revenue is allocated depending on the item. Purchasing specifically marked items guarantee the planting of one or more Trees. Revenue is the amount we actually receive from the app store.

Ad Revenue Details

Ads are reward based, non intrusive, and optional. 10% of Ad revenue is allocated. Revenue is calculated by taking the amount we receive from the Ad network and subtracting what if anything we spend to advertise Tappy Tree.
