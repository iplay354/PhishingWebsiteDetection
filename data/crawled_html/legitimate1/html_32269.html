Locations
Register Your Location

Filmmakers use all sorts of locations, from homes and castles to businesses and countryside and everything in between. Film Edinburgh keeps an extensive database of potential film locations in Edinburgh, East Lothian, Midlothian and the Scottish Borders. Register with us or simply find out more about what being a film location means on Register Your Location.

Production Guide
Add/Amend Your Details

Incoming productions make use of our online production guide to find local crew and facilities. Crew, facilities and production companies based in Edinburgh, East Lothian, Midlothian and the Scottish Borders can sign up free of charge. Add or Amend your details.
