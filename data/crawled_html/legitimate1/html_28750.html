Our Founder, Jerome Hill

Jerome Hill (1902–1972) was an Academy Award-winning filmmaker, painter, photographer, composer, and supporter of the arts and artists.

To this day, the program and values of the Jerome Foundation have been guided by Jerome’s artistic and humanistic concerns.
