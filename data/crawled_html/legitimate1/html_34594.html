  • Discover the world on our photography tours with renowned mentors!

    Since 2011, Darter Photography has been creating memorable photography experiences across the world for thousands of photography enthusiasts. Our philosophy of photography and travel weaves in new experiences in wonderful places, where you will not go on the beaten path, but embark on an exploration with us to whip up new experiences. We create the space for your photography to explore your line of thinking. To grow. To flourish.

    Mentored by people with in-depth knowledge of photography and extensive travel experience, we help you enhance your photography skills while travelling around the world. We offer myriad and diverse destinations – from the Arctic to Antarctica, from the high mountains of the Himalayas to the rainforests of South India, from the dry deserts of Rajasthan and Kutch to the islands of Andaman, from the eagle-hunters of Central Asia to the wildlife spectacles of Africa, and many many more.

    Every tour with us is unique, and we endeavour to make it very special for you. Learn photography techniques from our experienced mentors. Create amazing photographs. Make new friends. Capture the world with its myriad wonders. Join us on our wide range of photography tours and experience the wonders of the planet!
