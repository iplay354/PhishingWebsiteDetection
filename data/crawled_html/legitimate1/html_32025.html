LEARNING

You can learn about antique maps in my Guide. It gives you information on many early mapmakers and tips on dating them. Many of the maps offered for sale have a button linking you more information about the cartographer. Just like English hallmarked silver, antique maps can very often be dated to a particular year.

My goal is to inspire and ignite your curiosity, providing a platform for you to explore and appreciate the artistry and significance of antique maps. Visiting my website and exploring my collection will I hope give you the feeling of embarking on a journey through time. Feeling a connection to the explorers, mapmakers, engravers and publishers who worked tirelessly, often in candle-lit rooms to create maps and atlases that would serve as windows to the world for those of us who now follow.

GIFT

Giving someone an original antique map as a gift is a truly unique and thoughtful idea. Imagine the look on their face when they unwrap piece of history that has traveled through time. An antique map is not just a decoration; it's a window to the past, showing us how people from long ago viewed their world. It can spark curiosity and inspire conversations about history, geography, and even personal ancestry.

It’s more than just a present; it’s a piece of the past that they can treasure for years to come. And it is quite likely it might become a family heirloom.

It's a gift that stands out from the usual, making it perfect for that person who already has everything.

COLLECTING

When you think about collecting antique maps, there's a whole world of exciting themes to explore. Some people love gathering maps that show the world as it was seen hundreds of years ago, making it feel like you're time traveling to the past with just a glance. Others prefer collecting maps or charts of specific places like counties, countries, cities they love, almost like gathering memories of places they've visited or dream of visiting. Each map tells a story, not just of the lands they depict but also of the people who made them and the times they lived in. Starting a collection can be like starting a treasure hunt, where every map you find adds a new chapter to your own adventure.

FAKES

Thankfully here are very few fakes around but there are quite a number of different series of reproduction maps. So try and get a chance to see some real antique maps then you will realize what to look for. Buying from a reputable maps specialist as in any field of antiques is always best. They know all about the maps they are selling and very often the prices will be fair and reasonable and they will guarantee the age of the map.

BUYING

Every original antique map I offer for sale is clearly priced. As long as your order is over 15 pounds you can enjoy free postage to any UK address. There is an additional charge for parcels sent overseas.

GUARANTEE

I think that the number of maps on this site are 99.9 per cent original antique maps published in or around the year stated. I just may occassionally include a reproduction map that I have purchased with a collection of original old maps. These are very clearly described as not being old.

When you purchase an antique map or print from me it comes with a guarantee label that I recommend you put on the back after framing. The label gives my reference number, title, cartographer and date when it was published. Sometimes there is some additional information about the mapmaker. If you see a small c before the date this stands for the word circa meaning that the date given may be accurate but also that it might be just around this date.
