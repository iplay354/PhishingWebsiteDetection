The Word was made flesh

READ MORE

The Prayer Book Society has been actively campaigning for the wider use of the Book of Common Prayer for more than 50 years.

The Society with its members and Corporate Member churches strive to fix this “revolutionary” text at the heart of the worshipping life of the Church of England.

New generations are discovering the beauty, poetry, and majesty of the Book of Common Prayer.

Join us, as the Society embarks on its sixth decade. Help us to promote the Prayer Book’s use in all churches across the country and support the clergy, who use it regularly in the mission of the Church.
