Tully River Rafting

The World Class Tully River. The Tully River has earned an international reputation as Australia’s best rafting river. Enjoy awesome grade 3 & 4 rapids, amongst the spectacular scenery on this adrenalin pumping adventure. Experience is not necessary. A sense of adventure is. Raft site is approximately 2 hours south of Cairns or 1 hour from Mission Beach. Full Day Tour.

Barron River Rafting

Afternoon Rafting Barron River. An action-packed afternoon of fun and adventure. Raft your way through the World Heritage Listed rainforest of the stunning Barron Gorge, with Raging Thunder. These grade 2-3 rapids are ideal for first timers, or anyone wanting a couple of hours of fun. Just 20 minutes from Cairns. Pick-ups available from Cairns and Northern Beaches. Half Day Tour.

About Us

Raging Thunder has offered the best white water rafting tours from Cairns since 1984. We raft some of the world’s best rivers through World Heritage-listed rainforest year-round, providing thrilling adventures for beginners and more experienced rafters alike. Our guides are highly-trained and passionate about sharing the experience of a lifetime with you! Contact us today to book an unforgettable full-day or half-day Cairns white water rafting adventure.
