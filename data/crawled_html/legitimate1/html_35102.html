About Port of Huntsville

The Port of Huntsville is North Alabama’s gateway to global connectivity. With four unique services—Huntsville International Airport, Rail and Air Cargo, Space Port, and Jetplex Industrial Park—we deliver opportunities for businesses and travelers. Whether by land, air, or space, we link people to places and products to markets.

Learn More

Cargo Services

Efficiently Moving Products by Air, Rail, and Road

The Port of Huntsville is North Alabama’s inland port facility at the center of Foreign Trade Zone No. 83 offering seamless intermodal services through air, rail, and highway. With a state-of-the-art international intermodal center and extensive port facilities and infrastructure supported by a robust network of global logistics providers, the Port of Huntsville ensures efficient product delivery to market.
