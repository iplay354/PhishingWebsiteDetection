Welcome to The Ongar Academy or TOA for short! We are a unique village secondary school that has so much to offer every student within our care. We are Part of Bridge Academy Trust

First and foremost each school within the Trust is a place of learning, where young people ENJOY, ENRICH, ACHIEVE, ASPIRE.

Our core purpose at Bridge Academy Trust is to provide: High quality continuity of every child’s journey through education from 3 to 19.

A community and school-led school improvement system, where all children and adults are active citizens within the area they live and the school it serves.

Cookies

Website CMS

A 'sessionid' token is required for logging in to the website and a 'crfstoken' token is used to prevent cross site request forgery.
An 'alertDismissed' token is used to prevent certain alerts from re-appearing if they have been dismissed.
An 'awsUploads' object is used to facilitate file uploads.

Matomo

We use Matomo cookies to improve the website performance by capturing information such as browser and device types. The data from this cookie is anonymised.

reCaptcha

2026-2027 Admissions Applications

The deadline for 2026-2027 admissions applications for new Year 7 students was the 31st of October 2025.

If you need to submit a 'Late' application to Essex Admissions, the cut off date for this is the 21st of November 2025. This is also the date for evidence of change of address to be submitted by, to be included in the first round of offers on the 2nd of March 2026.

Please note: Late applications cannot be submitted online, please contact Essex Admissions directly for an application form.

Please see the Prospective Year 7's Admissions page on our website for more information & downloads.
