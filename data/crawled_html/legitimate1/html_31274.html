Breckenridge Gallery is a carefully curated collection of fine art from renown painters and sculptors. Since 1969, we have offered diverse work including abstract, landscape, impressionistic, wildlife, western, nature, contemporary, bronze, mixed media, and more. As the longest-running fine art gallery in Summit County, Colorado, we welcome collectors and visitors to experience our works in person or online. We're accessible, friendly, and can't wait to show you our gallery at 124 South Main Street in historic downtown Breckenridge, Colorado.
