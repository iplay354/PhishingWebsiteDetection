Susan Sheehan Gallery specializes in Post-War prints and works on paper.
Susan Sheehan is a leading expert in prints produced during the seminal era spanning the decades of the 1960s through the 1990s.
The gallery has been a member of the Art Dealers Association of America (ADAA) for over 30 years
and Susan is is currently the president and an executive committee member.
