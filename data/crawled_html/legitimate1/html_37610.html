Expanded Mammography Services Now Available

We are excited to announce that diagnostic mammograms and breast ultrasound services are now available at our Shiloh location (with a provider’s order). This is in addition to our 3D screening mammogram capabilities, which offer more precise breast cancer detection, in the same place as your trusted OB/GYN healthcare team. Call us today and make your appointment! (618) 997-5266

Heartland Women’s Healthcare is also thrilled to introduce an optional add-on service to screening mammograms called SecondReadAI. It uses advanced artificial intelligence to assist the radiologist who is already reviewing your images. It is an additional tool that helps identify subtle changes that might be harder to detect with the human eye alone, increasing detection of early-stage breast cancer by as much as 15%.

Our Wellness Partnership With KORB Health

Due to a high volume of interest regarding access to GLP-1 weight loss medication such as Ozempic® (semaglutide) and Mounjaro® (tirzepatide), we’ve partnered with KORB Health to offer access to a program featuring semaglutide or tirzepatide, supporting healthy weight loss and management:

1. Telehealth consultation with a KORB Health provider and ongoing care team support
2. Semaglutide or tirzepatide medication delivered to your doorstep
3. Nutrition & fitness recommendations
4. Strategies to help you overcome challenges and stay motivated
5. All-inclusive monthly pricing – Semaglutide $269/month or Tirzepatide $399/month (HSA eligible)

Additionally, KORB Health offers free live webinars with their Chief Health & Wellness Officer, Dave Appel, via the Circle app. The recorded webinars are exclusively available on their patient community app called Circle, providing access to valuable resources such as recipes, exercise videos, and more.
