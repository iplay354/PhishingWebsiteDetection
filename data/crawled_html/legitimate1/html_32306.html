Positive Impact

Embedded systems are having a profound impact on society as it transitions into the information era. With applications ranging from electronic wallets to information appliances, implanted adaptive insulin pumps, smart automobile air bag systems, and wireless wrist communicators – they are changing the way we live. CECS continually strives to be at the forefront of this exciting product and technology evolution.
