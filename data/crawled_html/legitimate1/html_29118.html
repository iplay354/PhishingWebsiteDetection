Did you know?

Waterloo Regional Health Network was a member of the St. Joseph’s Health System, one of the largest corporations in Canada devoted to health care. Founded in the healing mission of the Sisters of St. Joseph of Hamilton, St. Joseph’s Health System was established in 1991 to meet the challenges of the changing environment for delivery of health and social services and takes pride in a system-wide commitment to caring for the whole person: body, mind and spirit.

Waterloo Regional Health Network is situated on land
that is the traditional home of the Haudenosaunee,
Anishinaabe, and Attawandaron peoples. We recognize
and deeply appreciate their connection to this place,
and we are grateful to be on this land together.

We also value the contributions Indigenous peoples
have made in shaping and strengthening this community.
Waterloo Regional Health Network is committed to actively 
seeking Truth and advancing Reconciliation,
in our hospital and beyond.
