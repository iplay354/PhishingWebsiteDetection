Bliss Bridal is an esteemed boutique wedding planning and coordination company, established in 2002 by Sugako Johnson on the beautiful Hawaiian island of Oahu.

With an impressive portfolio of over 500 meticulously planned and coordinated weddings, Sugako and her team have delighted clients from across the globe, including Japan, the mainland US, Canada, and Australia as well as local couples. Their expertise lies in crafting exquisitely styled and flawlessly executed weddings, earning recognition in prominent Japanese bridal magazines numerous times.

OWNER, LEAD DESIGNER

Sugako Johnson is a highly experienced wedding planner in Hawaii with over 20 years of expertise. Her fluency in English and Japanese and knowledge of Asian, American and Hawaiian cultures allows her to understand and meet the needs of couples from diverse backgrounds. With meticulous attention to detail and keen eye for aesthetics, Sugako ensures flawlessly executed weddings and events.
