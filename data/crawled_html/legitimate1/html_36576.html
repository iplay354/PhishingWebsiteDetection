Telos Pay - A hassle-free way to pay

TelosPay allows a businesses to send payment requests via text message in less than 10 seconds. Customers enter their payment details and can elect to have their card details saved for even faster future payments. Our client asked us to create an automated exchange system, where transactions are seamless and secured with bank-grade technology. 

Shift - An App for Warehouse Shifts

The Shift App and Web Portal is designed to facilitate warehouse & factory management and shift supervisors as they manage workers and work process on the warehouse floor.  The Shift Mobile Application helps Shift Supervisors directly manage workers while the Online Portal allows Process Engineers to adjust processes, Managers to manage workers and workflows, and Executives to understand key areas of concern.

Sign Language Android app for Facebook community

Each day the 11-year-old girl Emily records a video to teach sign language to the classmates of her sister who has Down syndrome. Once she and her family decided to start a Facebook community and publish the videos there too it immediately became a hit and they got thousands of likes in no time. We made an Android app for them that through a single click publishing solution fetches their videos from Facebook and presents them categorised in a Sign Language app for Android.
