The One Stop Shop for All Things Mannequin


Shop for new and used mannequins,dress forms and mannequin parts or rent dress forms,garment racks and steamers. If you are interested in DIY projects with mannequins ,visit our project gallery and blog.

We recycle mannequins to keep them out of landfills when retailers close/remodel stores.

We host flower crown classes where used mannequin heads serve as your canvas - sustainability meets artistry.

Our mannequin showroom is available to be rented for photo shoots and film productions.

RECYCLE

You see, mannequins are made from materials that don't decompose. If they go into a landfill, people living a thousand years from now will discover them as if they were buried Egyptian statues. Sad. So, we offer free mannequin recycling and sell the used ones to artists and makers to give them a longer and more purposeful life. Wouldn't you?"
