What is the #1 most visited website? 

As of January 2024, the most visited website in the world is Google. Google is a multinational technology company specializing in Internet-related services and products. According to Statista, Google sites ranked first among the most visited multi-platform websites in the United States, with just over 274.49 million unique users and a market share of 61.4 percent among leading U.S. search engine providers in February 2023. Here are some more interesting statistics about Google:

  • Google has a market value of $739 billion. 
  • Google receives over 63,000 searches per second on any given day. 
  • Google has 90.46% of the search engine market share worldwide. 
  • An average person conducts 3-4 searches every single day.

Top 10 most popular websites in the world 

Here is a list of 10 most popular websites with short descriptions about them.  

Google.com 

Google is a search engine that helps you find information on the Internet. It was founded in 1998 and has since become one of the most popular websites in the world. Google’s mission is to organize the world’s information and make it universally accessible and useful. 

Youtube.com 

YouTube is a video sharing site that allows users to upload, share and watch videos. It was founded in 2005 and has since become one of the most popular websites in the world. As of January 2024, YouTube is the second most visited website in the world, after Google. 

Facebook.com 

Facebook is a social network that allows users to connect with friends and family, share photos and videos, and stay up-to-date on news and events. It was founded in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg, Dustin Moskovitz and Eduardo Saverin when they were students at Harvard University. As of February 2023. Facebook is the third most visited website in the world, after Google and YouTube 

Amazon.com 

Amazon.com, founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994, is a leading international e-commerce and technology company known for its vast online marketplace, diverse product offerings and innovative services such as Amazon Prime. 

Twitter.com 

Twitter.com is a popular social media platform founded in 2006, allowing users to share short messages called tweets. It has become a significant source for real-time news, communication, and networking. 

Instagram.com 

Instagram.com is a social media platform for sharing photos and videos, founded in 2010. Acquired by Facebook, it allows users to share visual content, connect with others and explore a wide range of interests through photos and short videos. 

Wikipedia.org 

Wikipedia.org is a collaborative online encyclopedia founded in 2001. It contains a wide range of articles on a variety of topics, created and edited by volunteers from around the world. Wikipedia is known for its open editing and accessibility model. 

Yahoo.com 

Yahoo.com is an Internet portal that offers a range of online services, including email, news, search and more. Founded in 1995, it played a significant role in the early development of the Internet and continues to provide a variety of online services. 

Baidu.com 

Baidu.com is a leading Chinese Internet search engine founded in 2000. It dominates the search market in China and provides services such as maps, news and online forums. Baidu is a key player in China’s Internet landscape. 

Bing.com 

Bing.com is a search engine developed by Microsoft, launched in 2009. It offers web, image and video search, as well as other features. While not as dominant as some competitors, Bing has a significant presence in the search engine landscape.
