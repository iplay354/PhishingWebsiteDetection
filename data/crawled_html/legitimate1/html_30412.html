PLACENTIA SPOTLIGHTS

  1. Holiday Schedule for Trash Collection and Street Sweeping Services

    Please note holiday service changes for trash collection and street sweeping. There will be no trash service on Christmas Day or New Year’s Day, with pickups delayed one day that week. Street sweeping will be suspended Dec. 24–Jan. 1. Read on...

  2. Recycle Your Holiday Tree

    Real holiday trees will be accepted at your curb by Republic Services starting Friday, December 26, 2025, through Friday, January 9, 2026. Read on...

  3. Support the City's Centennial Celebration

    The City of Placentia is preparing for a once-in-a-lifetime event—our 100-year Centennial Celebration in 2026! Learn more about our Sponsorship Opportunities and how you can get involved in this historic event. Read on...

  4. Youth Pledge of Allegiance Program

    The City offers local youth an opportunity to participate in a long-standing civic tradition through the Youth Pledge of Allegiance Program. This initiative invites youth to lead the Pledge of Allegiance at the beginning of a City Council Meeting. Read on...

  5. Sign Up for the City's Weekly E-Newsletter

    The City sends out a weekly e-newsletter to inform the community about upcoming City events, news, information, and more. Click Here to Sign Up
