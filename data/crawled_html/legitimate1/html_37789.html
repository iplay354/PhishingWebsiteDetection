Third-Party Caregiver Reporting

Accushield partners with third-party care providers—including Private Home Care, Home Health, Therapy, and Hospice—to ensure all company and caregiver information is accurate and verified, giving operators clean, reliable data.

The Accushield Dashboard centralizes all activity from third-party care providers and provides a clear record of who is in the community and what healthcare-related services were rendered. For clinical directors, it functions much like a lightweight EHR, tracking third-party healthcare services and tying every caregiver visit directly to the resident they serve.

  • Visitor, third-party care, vendor, staff, and resident sign-in and out reporting
  • Document acceptance and verification
  • Agency staffing hourly/daily log reports
  • Resident third-party care reporting & analytics
  • Sign residents out and keep up with where they are going and when they will return

Technology with a Heartbeat - #ProjectLoveMeter

Accushield is uniquely positioned to help prevent loneliness and social isolation among senior living residents. That’s why we created #ProjectLoveMeter — a bold initiative that blends data and human connection to improve resident well-being.

By combining visitation data from Accushield kiosks with insights from partners like LifeLoop, CarePredict, fall detection systems, and EHRs, we can identify residents at higher risk of disconnection — a leading indicator of loneliness and cognitive decline.

Once identified, we activate a network of credentialed nursing students and local volunteers, matching them with residents based on shared interests to foster consistent, meaningful relationships.

Visitor, Resident, and Staff Evacuation Management

Knowing who is in your community during an evacuation is critical. The Accushield kiosk gives management a quick and easy way to account for all on-site residents, staff, and visitors.

“The difference between [hurricanes] Irene and Sandy was major. Instead of dealing with all the paperwork, we now have the Accushield reports (Accushield Evacuation Management) that we can look at to identify, more efficiently, more accurately, more quickly, and in a very tumultuous time, who’s in our communities or where they have evacuated to. We can look at those reports and talk with families. “Your mother has exited this building, and she has been identified in this building.” So it was a much easier process.”

– Dr. Carol McKinley, President and CEO
Simpson Senior Services

VERIFY | Third-Party Care Provider Credentialing in Senior Living

Accushield VERIFY streamlines credentialing for third-party care providers—such as Private Home Care, Home Health, and Hospice—along with other vendors entering senior living communities. Through the Accushield kiosk, communities centralize verification to ensure compliance and enhance resident safety.

VERIFY uses sophisticated processes and Ai to and validate the following documents:

  • Employment verification
  • Criminal background check
  • Required immunizations
  • Proper insurance requirements
  • Agency BAA’s and registration forms
  • Proof of negative TB
  • Proper training documents
