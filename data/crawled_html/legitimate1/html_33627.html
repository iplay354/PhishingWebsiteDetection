About Us

Uniting Communities for Cleaner Creeks

South Bay Clean Creeks Coalition (SBCCC) is a volunteer-driven organization dedicated to cleaning and restoring the Santa Clara Valley watershed. Originating from a grassroots effort, SBCCC now encompasses the Los Gatos Creek, Guadalupe River, and Coyote Creek. Through our TEAM 222 Program, we have removed over 730 tons of trash, engaging the community in regular cleanups and educational initiatives. Join us in our mission to reclaim and protect our local waterways.
