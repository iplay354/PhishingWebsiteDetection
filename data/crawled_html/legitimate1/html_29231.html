    HOW IT WORKS
    The Problem

    Over 60% of heart diseases go undiagnosed.^1

    You know the difference between illness and health can be subtle — and made even more challenging to discern in noisy, distracting medical environments. And you’re only human — exhaustion, stress, and multitasking only make your job harder. Whatever the reason, missing the warning signs of disease can lead to serious complications and outcomes.

    The Solution

    Providers who use Eko are 2x more likely to detect heart disease.^2

    Our AI-powered solution transforms every patient encounter into an opportunity for earlier detection. With FDA-cleared AI, enhanced audio, and real-time visualizations, you can detect with precision and treat with confidence.

    FDA-CLEARED AI

    Enhance clinical expertise.

    Leverage FDA-cleared AI to detect murmurs, AFib, and low ejection fraction. Eko AI supports you when you need it most, helping you to provide consistently exceptional care.

    ADVANCED AUDIO

    Automatic Record Keeping
