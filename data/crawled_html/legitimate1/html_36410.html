Welcome to the Ministry of Finance, Economics and Development (MFED)

Kam na bane ni mauri!

Welcome to the official website of the Ministry of Finance, Economics and Development (MFED) of the Republic of Kiribati. We are pleased to provide this platform as a central resource for citizens, development partners, investors, and all who are interested in the economic and financial direction of our nation.

MFED plays a pivotal role in the stewardship of Kiribati’s public resources and long-term development. We also work to ensure that financial management and economic development are aligned with Kiribati Vision (KV20) and the principles of sustainable development, climate resilience, and equity for all I-Kiribati. We are committed to openness, service excellence, and inclusive growth. Whether you are a student, civil servant, investor, or member of the public, we invite you to explore the information available here and engage with us in building a more self-reliant, prosperous Kiribati.

Kam bati n rabwa. Te Mauri, Te Raoi ao Te Tabomoa.
