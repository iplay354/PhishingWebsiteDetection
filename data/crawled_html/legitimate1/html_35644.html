A New Vision
for Health

Medicine tailored to each individual’s molecular makeup.

PMC Promotes the Understanding and Adoption of concepts, services, and products that can provide physicians with a better understanding of the molecular characteristics influencing a patient’s health. The goal is to advance more informed prevention and early detection strategies, more precise diagnoses, and tailored treatment recommendations that work for patients and their families.

Why Personalized Medicine?

Personalized medicine helps direct physicians toward the prevention and treatment strategies that will work best for each patient. The approach is designed to keep patients healthier, longer, making medicine more efficient and effective for everyone.
