High Quality Wedding & Event Rentals on the Outer Banks (OBX) of North Carolina

Ocean Atlantic Event Rentals is a full-service wedding and event rental company located in Kill Devil Hills in the heart of the Outer Banks of North Carolina. We provide wedding rentals to the entire Outer Banks of North Carolina from Carova in the 4×4 area to Hatteras Island including all your favorite OBX beach towns like Kill Devil Hills, Kitty Hawk and Nags Head.

We carry a wide variety of tents, tables, chairs, dance floors, lighting, glassware, flatware, china, linen, staging, and other items you want or need for your wedding, event, party, or private function. We are the exclusive provider of pool cover dance floors for all OBX weddings.
