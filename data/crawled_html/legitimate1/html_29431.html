Healthy Family Shop

Designed in Oxford UK, our products help to protect and conserve our environment and ocean. For our plastic-free products we use only high-quality sustainable materials, plastic-free, biodegradable and eco-friendly.

All of our products are ethically and sustainably sourced. For packaging and shipping, we use only recyclable and up-recyclable materials that are completely plastic-free and biodegradable.

With Healthy Family Shop we want to apologize to our future generations for the harm we have done to our planet, and to prove that we can do things differently. Encouraging a circular economy and a sustainable way of living for a better future.

We are committed to raising awareness about plastic pollution and we know the change always starts with you! Every time you spend money on something you have the power to choose to support the kind of world you want!

We believe the power of the purchase can make a positive impact on our planet. We hope that through our actions we will inspire other people to reflect on their habits and reduce plastic consumption and waste.

With every purchase at Healthy Family Shop, you make a difference by reducing single-use plastic pollution. We also donate part of our sales to charities like Trees for Cities, Ocean Generation, and Friends Of The Earth.

Join our journey!

– Healthy Family Team
