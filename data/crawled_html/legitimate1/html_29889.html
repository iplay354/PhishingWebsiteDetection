MY MISSION...

As a Life Coach for Women Over 50, I help women to transform midlife into their best life…

I help women go from feeling frustrated and isolated that they’re not living their best life after 50 because they feel trapped in a cycle of self-sacrifice — where their life, dreams, and plans often get put on hold for the relentless demands of work and the needs of others.

I empower women to break free and prioritize themselves without guilt, so they can reclaim their dreams, put their plans into action, and embrace an amazing life filled with happiness and excitement, all while being surrounded by a community of like-minded women who are ready to accompany them on their midlife journey — and to support, uplift, and inspire them every step of the way. I’m here to remind you that it’s never too late to live the life you want.

Suzanne's Story

For over 25 years, I always put my career and work first. And when I wasn’t working so hard, I always put other people’s needs above my own, whether it was my significant other, aging parents, siblings, or friends.

The truth is, I rarely made time for the things I loved in life or myself because I was always “too busy.”

That is, until the day my life would forever be changed and I got the biggest wake-up call of my life…

It was an unwanted wake-up call… but at the same time, my much needed wake-up call!
