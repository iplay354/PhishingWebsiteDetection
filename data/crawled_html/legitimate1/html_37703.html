A passion for winemaking

Messina Hof celebrates wine, food, and family.

Winemaking

The Bonarrigo family has 200 years of winemaking history. Paul M. continues the tradition as the seventh-generation winemaker.

Dining

Dine with us at one of our two restaurants across Texas. The Vintage House Restaurant and Harvest Green Winery & Kitchen specialize in Vineyard Cuisine.

hospitality

We welcome you to Messina Hof where Old-World hospitality remains strong, where you’re treated like family, and there’s always a glass waiting.

Vineyard

Messina Hof works with approximately 700 acres of the best vineyards across Texas between our Estates and partners properties.

VINEyard cuisine

The art of infusing wine into every dish we serve. Vineyard Cuisine creates the perfect pairing of food and wine.

Bed & breakfast

Stay in a picturesque cottage at the Manor Haus in Hill Country or a quaint room at the Villa Bed & Breakfast in Bryan.
