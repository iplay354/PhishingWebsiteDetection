Event Details

The Refuel Retreat for Religious Educators is coming on January 23-24, 2026. We are excited to welcome keynote speaker Colleen Carroll Campbell. Colleen is an award-winning author, print and broadcast journalist, and former presidential speechwriter for George W. Bush. In this year’s Refuel Retreat, Colleen will draw on her extensive research and writing on saints to reveal how their lives can guide us in evangelizing a resistant culture, overcoming the traps of perfectionism, and resisting both dilution and distraction as we stay focused on our ultimate goal: helping Jesus save souls.
