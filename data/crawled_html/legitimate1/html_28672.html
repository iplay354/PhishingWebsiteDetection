Introduction

The International Centre for Waterspout Research (ICWR) is an international organization dedicated to the study and advancement of waterspout research. It is comprised of meteorologists, researchers, storm chasers, the media and weather enthusiasts from around the world. The ICWR provides daily updated waterspout forecasts for North America, live reports from around the world, climatology data and safety information.
