Enabling science around the world since 2009

Ossila was founded in 2009 by a team of researchers and academics from the University of Sheffield who faced challenges sourcing the right scientific instruments and materials at fair prices. Frustrated by hidden pricing and over-engineered equipment, they set out to remove barriers to research and make high-quality tools more accessible. Today, we design and manufacture a complete range of products developed to cut through the noise and accelerate the pace of scientific discovery.

We pride ourselves on our excellent standard of customer care, and are trusted around the globe as a result. With customers in over 100 countries, our products are used by many of the world's leading universities and research institutions. We dispatch most in stock orders within one working day to avoid unnecessary delays in your work, and 82% of all orders qualify for free tracked shipping.

Drawing on decades of hands-on laboratory experience, our team of scientists and engineers understand your challenges and are here to provide expert technical support whenever you need it. To empower your research further, our knowledge center is continually updated with free resources spanning a range of topics.
