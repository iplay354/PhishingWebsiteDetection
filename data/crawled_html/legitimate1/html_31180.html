Changing Lives, Creating Futures

The Louisiana Community and Technical College System is Louisiana’s higher education and workforce training provider. Our 12 colleges serve more than 130,000 students annually, empowering them to reach their fullest potential. Offering a range of pathways for our students, we seek to meet the state’s workforce needs by training, skilling, and equipping them to reach new heights.
