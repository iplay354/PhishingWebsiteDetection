From mountain biking and ORV riding, to fishing and kayaking, to golfing, rockhounding and bird watching – adventure abounds!

Epic Singletrack for Mountain Biking

Copper Harbor’s unique mid-western mountainous terrain is an unparalleled riding experience for all levels! Explore 40+ miles of world renown trails, with new milage being developed each year!

Mountain Biking

Superior Paradise for Paddling

Encounter the scenic rocky and rugged Lake Superior shoreline from the comfort of your cockpit — barrier islands, finger bays, sea caves and sea stacks abound!

Paddling

Diverse Trails for Hiking & Walking

A diversity of true wilderness beckons! From leisurely walks to extreme on-the-edge hikes, embark among deep old growth forests to fully exposed ancient Lake Superior shorelines.

Hiking

Awesome Terrain for Skiing

A 900′ vertical drop mixed with 270″ of snowfall (including Lake effect powder) makes for some pretty thrilling Western-like ski or snowboarding experiences on legitimate black diamond terrain.
