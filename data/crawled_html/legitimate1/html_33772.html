WONDERFUL AND STRANGE: THE COMPLETE TWIN PEAKS

See the original thirty-episode, two-season run, the unforgettable third season The Return plus TWIN PEAKS: FIRE WALK WITH ME. Now playing, through Thu, Jan 8 only!

THE PLAGUE

NY Times Critic's Pick! "A stunner of a debut." - Alissa Wilkinson. Now playing, select shows on 35mm!

IN THE MOOD FOR LOVE

Wong-Kar Wai's gorgeous romance, one of our biggest hits of 2025, returns for an end-of-the year encore Dec 26-Jan 1.

CRONOS - 4K Restoration!

Antiques dealer Jesús Gris happens upon an ancient golden scarab and soon finds himself the victim of its sinister, addictive powers. Guillermo del Toro's haunting feature debut opens Wed, Dec 31.

PANAHI & KIAROSTAMI: TWO MASTERS

Celebrate the history-making achievements of two master filmmakers with a selection of their most celebrated works. Tickets on sale now!

MAGELLAN

Sneak Preview + Q&A with star Gael García Bernal Wed, Jan 7 - Thu, Jan 8. Q&A with dir. Lav Diaz Sat, Jan 10 - Sun, Jan 11. Tickets on sale now!

OBEX

Q&As with dir. Albert Birney Fri, Jan 9-Sat, Jan 10 at 7:30, and Sun, Jan 11 at 2:50. Opens Fri, Jan 9.

SOUND OF FALLING

Sneak Preview + Q&A with dir. Mascha Schilinski and cinematographer Fabian Gamper Mon, Jan 12 at 6:45. Opens Fri, Jan 16

RESURRECTION

Dir. Bi Gan's love letter to cinema. In a world where humans have forsaken dreams for immortality, a dreaming monster embarks on an odyssey that takes him across the twentieth century to the end of time. Now playing.
