Welcome to Al Khamsa

Where you can learn about the Bedouin Arabian horse and those who seek to preserve it.

Today’s Al Khamsa Arabian horses comprise less than 5 percent of the registered Arabian horse population in North America. Their importance goes far beyond numbers, however, and symbolizes the proud heritage of their Bedouin-bred ancestors. Al Khamsa Arabian horses excel in sport competition, endurance, competitive trail riding and show ring events. Many serve as superb working ranch horses. The majority of Al Khamsa Arabians have amateur owners who love their rare desert treasures and consider them valued members of the family.

Welcome to the Al Khamsa, Inc. website and community. Our organization focuses on the Arabian horses living in North America who trace back to the desert without any admixture. Horses are automatically added to the roster based on their pedigree; no memberships are required to be part of the organization. We strive to ensure that the future of the Al Khamsa Arabian horse is a continuation of the Bedouin horse, not just a derivative of what it once was. We are here to educate and serve worldwide. Enjoy the complimentary horse roster on this website which provides extensive pedigrees, ancestral elements, strains, progeny lists and more. Our magazine Khamsat is a valuable informative and educational tool about the Arabian horse: order yours today by clicking the "Khamsat: Subscribe" link in the header of this page. Please accept this invitation to attend the AlKhamsa, Inc. Annual Conventions held each fall. To receive updated information and invitations to events “Join the Newsletter” in the lower portion of this page. You are also encouraged to follow us on Facebook.
Beth Minnich President Al Khamsa Inc.
