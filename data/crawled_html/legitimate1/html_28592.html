Great wine for everyone

At [yellow tail], we like to keep it simple – creating great wine that makes people happy. With our unique Aussie spirit, we’ve never overcomplicated things. We trust our gut to make vibrant, award-winning wines that are easy to choose, easy to understand, and most importantly, easy to enjoy. No rules, no fuss – just delicious wine, made to make you smile. Cheers to that!

Our story

[Yellow tail] is a family-owned winery based in Yenda, Australia, where the Casella family has been making wine for six generations. It all started in 1957 when Filippo and Maria Casella migrated from Sicily to Australia. With a passion for bringing family and friends together, they began crafting wines that were meant to be shared at any occasion.
