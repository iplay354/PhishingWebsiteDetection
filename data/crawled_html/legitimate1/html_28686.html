WHO WE ARE

From our humble beginnings in a dairy off Brick Lane 20 years ago, we've become the go to destination for style conscious shoppers the world over. With 13 stores in the UK, Sweden and Finland we've now rehomed millions of pieces of unique vintage fashion.

Handpicked globally by our trend-focussed team, we pride ourselves on offering the widest possible range of pre-loved clothing. From dazzling party sequins t0 Y2K branded sportswear, timeless 1950s team jackets to rare 80s band tees.

And when it comes to sustainability? We're serious! With proud collaborations with Converse, Wrangler and Coach, alongside our own reworked label garments, we're proud industry leaders in tackling the growing mountain of fashion waste.
