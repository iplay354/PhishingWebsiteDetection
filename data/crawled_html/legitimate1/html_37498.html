Middendorf Funeral Home

Proudly offering Burial and Cremation Services

Middendorf Funeral Home is dedicated to providing services to the families of Northern Kentucky with care and compassion.
For over 150 years the Northern Kentucky community has trusted the Middendorf Family with helping them plan the celebrations of lives lived.
We serve every family in our community with great pride. We are able to offer a wide range of services to meet your family's needs and customs. We will listen to you and your wishes to help plan a celebration consistent with your expectations. We will take the time to plan every detail and help to relieve the burden on your family during your time of loss.  Our website contains information about our caring staff, our services and links to resources to help you get through this tough time. Please give us a call to see how we can help.
