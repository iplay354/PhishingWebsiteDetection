Unlock international production incentives

The New Zealand Film Commission supports international screen productions looking to locate in New Zealand by offering incentives, expert guidance, and access to world-class locations and crews. 

Through initiatives like the Screen Production Rebate and tailored production support, NZFC helps global storytellers unlock the full potential of filming in Aotearoa.

Supporting local productions

The New Zealand Film Commission provides funding, resources, and expert guidance to help you amplify your storytelling and realise your screen ambitions. 

From development and production to marketing and international outreach, NZFC supports New Zealand filmmakers to succeed both locally and globally.

Partnering with international producers through an official co-production can unlock new funding opportunities, expand your project's market potential, and give you access to both New Zealand and international incentives.

Championing New Zealand film

For over 40 years, the New Zealand Film Commission has played a leading role in growing the New Zealand screen industry, supporting local filmmakers and promoting New Zealand as a world-class destination for international productions.

 We provide film funding, production incentives, and expert support to help screen storytellers succeed in Aotearoa and on the global stage. Learn more about what we do and how we champion filmmakers.
