InteractiveSpecial Project

Intent-The Road to Genocide

The crime of genocide requires both intent to destroy a group in whole or in part and one or more acts of genocide. Israeli leaders have made and continue to make clear statements of their intent to commit genocide against Palestinians. Intent: the Road to Genocide, our latest interactive visualization in partnership with Law for Palestine, documents the relationship between these statements and the conduct of the Israeli military in Gaza through four thematic chapters: civilian harm, starvation, destruction of infrastructure, and forced displacement.

InteractiveSpecial Project

Intent Portal: Law For Palestine

Since October 7th, 2023, researchers at Law For Palestine have been documenting genocidal incitement against Palestinians by Israeli government officials, military personnel, and public figures. Visualizing Palestine collaborated with Law for Palestine on a new portal to house this crucial data, which they regularly update as a resource for legal action, advocacy, and ensuring justice for the Palestinian people.

Gaza Lives-Resisting Cultural Genocide

In this story, in collaboration with Kontinentalist, we reveal how the cultural destruction we are seeing is not new but part of the Israeli state's long-standing strategy. Through interviews and snapshots of their lives and work, five Palestinian artists walk us through places in Gaza that shaped their lives.

InteractiveSpecial Project

Stop Killer AI

Over the past eight months, we have witnessed Israel’s use of data from mass surveillance systems to power “Killing Machines” and “Mass Assassination Factories” that automate genocide against Palestinians in Gaza. Today, we are releasing Stop Killer AI to shed light on two of the artificial intelligence systems Israel has used to accelerate the mass killing of Palestinians: Lavender and Where’s Daddy?

The Rise of U.S. Anti-Boycott Legislation

Scroll through 443 anti-boycott measures introduced by U.S. lawmakers since 2014 in an attempt to repress the Palestinian-led Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions (BDS) movement. See how this tactic has expanded to include climate action, gun control, trans rights, abortion access, and workplace equity.
