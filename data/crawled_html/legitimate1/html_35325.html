FCL Hosts 25th Annual MLK Weekend Celebration, January 17-19, 2026
12/16/2025
The Library is excited to announce the lineup of events for its 25th annual MLK Weekend Celebration, honoring the legacy of Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Over the past 25 years, the celebration has grown from a single day to a full weekend of community activities.
FCL Unveils 2026 Renovation Plans at Downtown Farmington 50-Year Celebration
12/12/2025
FCL celebrated the 50-year anniversary of its Downtown Farmington building by unveiling the 2026 renovation concept images. Learn about upcoming improvements that will create new study spaces, outdoor gathering areas, and enhanced children’s services at both Library locations.

Prepare for the SAT with this free, live virtual workshop series led by an expert test-prep instructor. This series is open to the public—no library card is required to register. Sessions cover reading, writing, and math strategies. Attend any or all sessions.
