Edge of Reason

Season Three of our podcast with Atlantic Re:Think explores the idea that art is not fixed in time but reverberates—echoing and reimagining itself across past, present and future. In the latest episode, Frances Morris and Jamieson Webster discuss Louise Bourgeois’s explorations of memory, trauma, and the body, reflecting on how her art transforms personal history into collective emotional experience.
