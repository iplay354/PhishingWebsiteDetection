The benefits of contact lenses
Contact lenses move with your eyes to give you a full field of focused vision, wherever you look. They're great for people who want freedom from glasses and a natural look.

Vision problems explained

Nearsightedness or myopia:

Difficulty seeing things that are far away.

Farsightedness or hyperopia:

Difficulty seeing things clearly close up.

Astigmatism:

Vision blurring from an irregular cornea or lens.

Presbyopia:

Difficulty focusing close up that happens as you age.
