Ready Mix Concrete in London

Read More

EasyMix Concrete UK Ltd specialise in supplying ready mix concrete in London, Surrey & the surrounding areas. From ready mix concrete to volumetric concrete and screed, we put our customer service at the forefront of what we do, and this is proven in our reviews! From our main office in Croydon, we provide a top level service for all types of concrete construction jobs, delivering the finest grade of concrete and can mix on-site, ensuring you only pay for what you need.

View our concrete prices or use the ready mix concrete calculator online to determine how much concrete you need. Contact us directly to find out what type of concrete you need for your project.

Concrete Services London

From flooring to foundations, we have the equipment and experience necessary to guarantee complete customer satisfaction every time, which is why we are one of the best reviewed concrete suppliers in London. We can provide same day or next day delivery in and around London and provide precise delivery times for you. Our range of vehicles includes ground line pumps. making it the perfect solution for work sites with access issues.

The EasyMix Concrete team are accredited by the BSI and all fully qualified, expertly trained and highly experienced and we have earned our reputation as the reliable, premier choice for all your ready mixed concrete needs. Our fleet is all top of the range and our volumetric concrete mixing mobile plant and pump vehicles ensure that we can deliver concrete in London and around the region, quickly and easily.
