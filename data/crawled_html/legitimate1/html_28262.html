Original Medicare

Part A is your hospital coverage and Part B is your doctor coverage.

Learn More

Medicare Supplements

Medicare Supplements, also known as Medigap, fills in the gaps in coverage from Original Medicare.

Learn More

Medicare Advantage

Medicare Advantage, or Part C, replaces Original Medicare and works like HMO’s & PPO’s.

Learn More

Medicare Part D

Medicare Part D is a stand-alone plan that covers your prescription medications from the pharmacy.

Why MedicareFAQ

At MedicareFAQ, our mission is simple; make sure each individual we help is educated on all their Medicare options so they can make an informed decision. Our online resource center is built to give you unbiased information regarding your Medicare coverage choices. Whether it’s just learning about Original Medicare Part A and Medicare Part B or any of the other Medicare plans like; Medicare Supplements (Medigap), Medicare Advantage (Medicare Part C), or Prescription Drugs (Medicare Part D).

We work with the top carriers in the nation to find the best Medicare supplemental options that fit each individual’s budget. We built our Medicare rates quoting tool to be fast, efficient, and user-friendly. You can compare all supplemental Medicare options with any carrier in any state. This includes Plan F, Plan G, and Plan N, and any of the best Medigap plans available in your area. 

Our team of licensed agents work around the clock to make sure our resource center is always up to date and accurate so that you’re always informed on your Medicare benefits. Our lead Medicare educator, Jagger Esch, shares expert knowledge on our website as well his features in the press. Our services are 100% free. Our agents & Client Care Team love what they do. They provide unlimited support for the lifetime of your policy. Let our licensed Medicare agents help you so you can get back to enjoying life! 

It’s free and no obligation!
