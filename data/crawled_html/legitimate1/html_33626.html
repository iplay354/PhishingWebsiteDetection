Experience Salcombe

The jewel in the crown on the South Devon coast.

The town extends a warm welcome to all, ensuring that its unique blend of natural beauty, maritime history and strong community spirit continues to make it one of Devon’s most treasured coastal destinations.

Nestled on the stunning South Devon coast, Salcombe is a picturesque seaside town renowned for its golden beaches, scenic estuary and rich maritime heritage. A popular destination for sailing, coastal exploration and relaxation, Salcombe has a unique charm that keeps visitors returning year after year.

Sail, Play, Explore

Unforgettable adventures await you in Salcombe! Set sail, paddleboard, kayak, swim or fish in clear waters. Wander scenic trails, explore charming villages or take on a new challenge— try pottery, chocolate-making or even crafting your own gin. Whether you seek thrills or tranquility, every moment here is a reason to return.
