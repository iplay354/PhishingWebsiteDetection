OUR COMPANY

1750s Period Home Building Packages Expertly Handcrafted for Shipment Nationwide

Get the look of a post and beam home without the hassle with a unique, engineered, open architecture timbered ceiling system that works with conventionally framed walls. Early New England Homes’ designs incorporate the traditional style and charming characteristics of antique homes found throughout New England, but with all the modern conveniences and efficiencies for today.
