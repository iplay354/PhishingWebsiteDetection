Do you ship across India?

Yes, we deliver to every corner of India. Shipping charges will be shown at checkout based on your location.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Most orders reach you in 3 to 7 working days. Metro cities are usually faster, and remote areas may take a little longer.

How do I track my order?

As soon as your order ships, you’ll receive a tracking link on SMS and email. You can follow every step from there.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong product?

No stress. Just reach out to us within 48 hours with photos and we’ll replace it or arrange a quick resolution.

Do you offer returns or exchanges?

Yes, we offer easy returns and exchanges on eligible products. Full details are available on our Returns page.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

You can cancel within the first 6 hours or before it’s shipped. Once dispatched, cancellations aren’t possible.

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm. We usually reply within a few hours. You can reach us through our Contact page.
