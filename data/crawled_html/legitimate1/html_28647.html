The 10 Best Movies of 2025

From Sinners to One Battle After Another to some less obvious choices, here are our top 10.

Is 'Song Sung Blue' the Movie to See this Christmas?

Hugh Jackman and Kate Hudson play a real-life couple who form a Neil Diamond tribute band.

'Avatar: Fire and Ash' is Pure Spectacle

Visually bold but weakly told, the new Avatar delivers diminishing returns.

Naughty Thriller 'The Housemaid' is Sexy Fun

Sydney Sweeney and Amanda Seyfried star in this gleefully trashy crowdpleaser.

'The Testament of Ann Lee' is Mesmerizing, Nothing Special

Amanda Seyfried stars in this entrancing musical that loses its voice by the end.

Does 'No Other Choice' Live Up to the Hype?

A man loses his job to AI, sets out to murder his competition.

'We Bury the Dead' Never Comes Alive

Daisy Ridley stars in the zombie thriller that has promise but never finds it.

'Is This Thing On?' Hits a Refreshing Tone

Will Arnett and Laura Dern play a divorcing couple in Bradley Cooper's dramedy.

The New 'SpongeBob' Movie is a Tedious Plunge

Even my 7-year-old struggled through these 90 random minutes of "entertainment."
