    Integrated Medical and Holistic Fitness Center

    Opened in January 1997, the multiple award-winning TriHealth Fitness & Health Pavilion has been a pioneer in offering medically based fitness to Greater Cincinnati and continues to set the standard for exercise, rehabilitation, disease prevention and health education. Open to the community and conveniently located in the northeast suburb of Cincinnati, Ohio in Montgomery at I71 and Pfeiffer Road, the TriHealth Fitness & Health Pavilion is Ohio's first certified medical fitness facility recognized by the Medical Fitness Association (MFA).

      Telling Our Story Through the Cincinnati Community: The Population Health Report

      At TriHealth, we believe health care should be more than just treating illness – it should be about helping people live healthier, fuller lives. That’s why we’ve embraced a bold, proactive approach called Population Health. Population Health flips the traditional health care model on its head. Instead of waiting until people are sick, we’re working to keep them well. And now, we’re proud to share our journey with you through the new TriHealth Population Health Report.

      We invite you to explore the Population Health Report and see how TriHealth is making health care better for you, your family, and your neighbors – whether you’re a patient, a caregiver, or a community partner.
