What Mana is made of

From root to RUTF

Mana is an essential blend of milk, multivitamins, and peanut butter. Our impact is felt from farms in Georgia to mouths around the world.

How Mana is Made

Leveraging a global network

Everyone plays a part in the eradication of malnutrition.

From the farmers who cultivate our ingredients to the  global aid organizations that distribute our products, we are all connected by the dedication to save lives and improve wellbeing worldwide.

The 100% Pledge

Because we sell Mana to customers like UNICEF and USAID, we’re able to reinvest any profits we make into food production facilities, staff salaries, and office support. This allows us to dedicate 100% of your donation to life-saving packets of RUTF and provide Mana to organizations that couldn’t otherwise afford it.
