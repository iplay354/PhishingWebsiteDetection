    About Normec Verifavia

    Normec Verifavia, accredited under ISO 17029, is a recognized leader in environmental claim validation and verification compliance across a variety of transportation sectors, including maritime, aviation, airports, and road transport, as well as in diverse sustainability initiatives such as recycling, carbon insetting, and offsetting, and beyond.

    Our approach extends beyond conventional methods, embracing innovative strategies to maintain relevance and effectiveness in evolving fields. We offer tailor-made assessments designed to meet the specific needs of our clients, covering areas like Inventory Hazardous Materials (IHM), Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF), and comprehensive services for carbon management (insets, offsets). We provide regulatory training and practical tools such as the Carbon Intensity Indicator (CII) Dashboard and HAZINVENT for efficient IHM management.

    Our commitment is to assist organizations in their journey towards implementing more sustainable practices through detailed evaluations, effective solutions, and comprehensive assessments.
