Welcome to Hartsville/Trousdale County

The Heart of Tennessee
Nestled in the heart of Middle Tennessee, our charming community offers a blend of rich history, natural beauty, and warm hospitality. Whether you're looking to explore our scenic landscapes, enjoy local events, or learn about our vibrant heritage, Hartsville/Trousdale County is a perfect destination. Join us to experience the friendly spirit of small-town life, with easy access to modern amenities and outdoor adventures. Explore, discover, and make lasting memories in our wonderful county!
