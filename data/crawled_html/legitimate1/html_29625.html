Utility Disconnects Postponed

Utility disconnects that were scheduled have been postponed due to weather. Disconnects will resume on the next weather-appropriate day. 
Please ensure payment or eligible arrangements are made today to avoid an interruption of service. Questions may be directed to Customer Service at 302-653-9231 option 3

2026 Business License Renewal Notice

Annual renewal fee to maintain active business status with the Town of Smyrna is due no later than January 31, 2026. 
Payment received after that date will accrue a ten percent (10%) penalty fee for each month or portion thereof that the license fee remains unpaid. (License fee based on business type).

2026 Application for Manufactured Home Placement Permit Reduction 

Qualified applicants will receive a 50% reduction of the annual fee which will remain $100.00 for the 2026 year. 
Applications should be completed and returned no later than January 15, 2026. 
Payment is due on or before March 31, 2026, payments received after that date are subject to a monthly late fee of 10% until paid.

Town of Smyrna 2026 Operating Budget Approved

At the recent Town Council meeting, Smyrna’s Fiscal Year 2026 budget was officially approved. The approved budget maintains the current tax rate with no increase to property taxes. 
An incremental adjustment to utility rates will take effect in the new fiscal year to support the continued operation, maintenance, and improvement of essential services. 
This budget allows the Town to remain fiscally responsible while continuing to invest in infrastructure, public safety, and services that support residents and local businesses. 
The Town remains committed to transparency, long-term planning, and serving the Smyrna community.
