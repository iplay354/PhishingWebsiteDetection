About TTI 

Touch Technologies, Inc. (1982) is a technology development & portfolio company, specializing in innovative software development, creative intellectual property, and exchange of equity projects.

 

We work with enterprise organizations, small businesses and startups.

 

We are the developers of the Sheerpower programming language. Sheerpower is specifically designed to rapidly develop and implement mission critical, highly scalable business applications.

IT CONSULTING AND SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT
