About the Arkwright Society

The Arkwright Society, the charity that cares for Cromford Mills, was formed to revive and expand the legacy of Sir Richard Arkwright and his fascination with innovation, industry and education. With the help of donations and our incredible volunteers, we continue to form pioneering partnerships and play our part in our thriving local community.

Walks at Cromford

Embark on crag-top hikes, and stroll through changing seasons along a beautiful woodland valley. Rest by the Cromford Canal, spot rare birds and other native wildlife, all amongst our UNESCO World Heritage site.
