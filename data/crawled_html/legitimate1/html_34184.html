Who we are

For centuries, we have built a strong reputation for teaching and academic excellence.

We are always looking ahead to consider how our academic endeavour can serve our future world, but we also value our long history and the achievements of our alumni.

Univ is a place where you can expand your knowledge, develop your personal and academic skills, and immerse yourself in an environment dedicated to learning and growth.

Learn more

Life at Univ

Life at Univ is about learning, growing, and making lifelong connections.

We are a vibrant academic community where students are supported by tutors and staff, fellow students, and a strong network of alumni across the globe. Life at Univ is rich in opportunities for relationships that will shape both your university experience and your future.

Learn more

Alumni

When you join Univ, you become part of a large and welcoming community.

Your time at Univ is the start of a lifelong connection to the College and the unique networks of people it creates. As an integral part of the Univ community, our Alumni are regularly welcomed back to the College for events such as guest lectures, celebratory dinners and reunions. We also make sure that our Old Members can keep in touch by sharing our news and achievements with them.
