High-Temperature CP Flexible Exhaust Hoses

An exhaust hose is a flexible hose used for safely transporting hot and cold gases and exhaust fumes away from the operator in demanding industrial environments. Ideal for high-temperature applications, these hoses are commonly used in ovens, foundries, furnaces, and smelting operations and across industries such as ceramics and glass.
  • Coated fiberglass fabric
  • SIL 460 is silicone-coated
  • Galvanized steel inner wire
  • Galvanized (SIL 460), stainless (HiTex 480), or INOX stainless (HiTex 481) steel outer helix wire

SaniSure® PLTS-Q50 Platinum-Cured Silicone Transfer Tubing

This platinum-cured silicone tubing is engineered for cost-effective performance in single-use fluid transfer applications. The soft, flexible profile is ideal for general-purpose fluid transfer, gentle pumping, biopharmaceutical processing, bioreactor process lines, production filtration and fermentation, and diagnostics and laboratory testing.
  • Platinum-cured silicone
  • Tasteless, odorless, noncytotoxic & nonhemolytic
  • Can be sterilized
  • Durometer hardness (Shore A): 50

Vena® SIL 650V Reinforced Silicone Hose

Vena® SIL 650V is a premium, platinum-cured silicone hose reinforced with stainless steel wire and polyester fabric, engineered for the safe and efficient transfer of liquids, semi-liquids, or solids in highly regulated industries such as food, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, chemical, and biotechnology.
  • VMQ silicone inner & outer layer with stainless steel wire & polyester fabric reinforcement
  • Translucent & smooth inner & outer layers; White reinforcement layer
  • Odorless, tasteless & completely non-toxic

Metric PETRO-BLEND Flexible Clear PVC/Polyurethane Tubing

PETRO-Blend PVC Tubing is extruded using a PVC/Polyurethane alloy that offers the clarity of flexible PVC with the toughness and abrasion resistance of polyurethane.
  • PVC/Polyurethane alloy with 30% ester-based polyurethane
  • Non-toxic
  • High clarity & flexibility
  • Available in multiple inner diameters

Novaflex® TPUW & TPUW LD Urethane Hose

Novaflex® TPUW hoses are designed with a special bonded urethane construction for superior abrasion resistance and flexibility in demanding material handling applications. The lightweight TPUW LD version is ideal for lighter-duty work and portability.
  • Bonded thermoplastic urethane construction
  • Highly abrasion-resistant
  • Reinforced with a rigid external ABS helix
  • Available in standard or lightweight (LD) format
