Pioneer in scent levels since 2014


Since 2014, our Malaysia-based candle shop has been celebrated for its distinctive, in-house blended fragrances, each hand-crafted to offer a unique sensory experience. Available exclusively online, our scented candles cater to a variety of scent preferences and sensitivities, with every fragrance meticulously blended by our team. Discover our collection, featuring soy candle jars, wax cubes, votive candles, travel tin candles and gentle home and car fragrance range. Whether you’re looking to buy candles online for personal use or as thoughtful gifts, our hand-poured soy wax candles use high-quality natural ingredients to ensure a longer, cleaner burn with minimal soot.
