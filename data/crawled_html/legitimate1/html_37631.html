The holidays are coming up fast (how did that happen already? 😅). If you don’t see something you love on our website and want to customize your own arrangement, we’d be happy to help, just give us a call!


Our cutoff date for Thanksgiving deliveries will be Thursday, November 21, so make sure to get your orders in early to guarantee delivery the day before Thanksgiving.


This is honestly one of my favorite times of the year for flowers, the fall blooms are so good. The colors, textures, and varieties that come in this season are some of the prettiest we get all year! From deep marigolds and rusty oranges to warm mauves and golden yellows, every arrangement feels like a little piece of autumn magic. 🍁


Here’s a fun fact: did you know chrysanthemums (aka mums) symbolize joy and longevity? They’re one of the most popular fall flowers and come in almost every color imaginable!


At Home Flower Care:
- Changing the water daily is the best way to help your flowers last longer.
- If you can, trim the stems every couple of days to help with hydration.
- Keep flowers away from direct sunlight and heat sources as they will last longer in a cool spot.
