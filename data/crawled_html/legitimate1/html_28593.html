WELCOME TO JOURNEY PLUS

Journey Plus has been a complete Destination Management Company (DMC) based in Dhaka, Bangladesh for over 27 Years. We are dedicated to providing our clients with a unique hospitality experience, be that a corporate meeting, a conference, an archaeological or classical tour, a cruise or any other travel related service. Our commitment for excellence, for all these years has never changed. Product quality at competitive prices with excellent service from the day our clients arrive in Bangladesh till we transfer them back to the airport for their departure is our motto. Our clients will have a great stay and bring back memories to last a lifetime! As a leading tour operator in Bangladesh we design, develop and deliver the tour that best suits the travel requirements of our clients.
