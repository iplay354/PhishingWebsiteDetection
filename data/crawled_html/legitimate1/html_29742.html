STYLISH, CONTEMPORARY STRING QUARTET MUSICIANS & JAZZ BANDS FOR WEDDINGS, PARTIES, & EVENTS IN AUSTRALIA
Stylish, contemporary string quartets, orchestras, pop & jazz bands for events, recordings, weddings, concerts & TV/film.
We have performed at Parliament House for Prince William & Kate Middleton - the Duke & Duchess of Cambridge.
We have performed at events for Chanel (string quartet), Louis Vuitton (jazz band), and Tiffany & Co.
Stringspace String Quartet performed by special invitation at the Welcome Reception for King Charles III and Queen Camilla at Parliament House, Canberra.
We have performed for the President of France, the PM of Vietnam, the President of China & the Prime Minister of Japan.
We regularly provide musicians to the Art Gallery of NSW, National Gallery of Australia and the National Portrait Gallery.
We have provided string quartets and jazz bands nation wide for MYER and strings throughout Australia for M.A.C Cosmetics.
Our Stringspace Orchestra performed at the CHANEL + Australian Ballet Dinner at the Sydney Opera House, and at the CHANEL + David Jones 100 Year celebration at the NGV.
Our talented musicians can often be seen in orchestras like The Australian Chamber Orchestra.
The orchestras that we assemble are vibrant, rich in sound and of the highest quality.
The Stringspace Orchestra performing "At Last" with Connie from Sneaky Sound System at a private event.
We have provided solo piano for David Jones, the Sydney Opera House & have collaborated with the Sydney Dance Company.
We have 24 albums on Spotify, Apple Music and iTunes! Our most popular streaming cities are New York, Los Angeles, Tokyo, Sydney & Melbourne.
Our musicians perform at events for the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet in all the major capital cities of Australia.
Stringspace have performed at events for Alannah Hill, Bono (from U2), Richard Branson, Justin Hemmes, Peter Weir, Harpers Bazaar and many others...
Our jazz band performing at Tiffany & Co.
We have a fabulous collection of classical, jazz & pop songs in our repertoire.
We regularly provide musicians for the National Library of Australia.

Our Quiet Nights: Jazz & Bossa Nova album is very popular in South America!
