Our Core Programs

Our diverse program and event roster includes ways for people of any age and background to get involved with G4C:

Games for Change Festival

Every year, the Games for Change Festival brings together social innovators and changemakers across sectors for a curated program of talks, workshops, and networking opportunities. For nearly two decades, G4C’s flagship event has been a destination for anyone who wants to imagine, inspire and ignite the future of social impact gaming and immersive media.

Learn More

G4C Learn

G4C Learn programs empower the next generation of game-changers to drive real-world impact through games. Youth and educators discover fun and powerful new ways to teach, learn, connect, and create through workshops, fellowships, game jams, and challenges featuring our signature game design curriculum and training model.

Learn More

2024 Impact Report

Covering our work throughout 2024, the report highlights a transformative year of using games and immersive media to drive real-world change. This year, our initiatives and partnerships have connected communities, empowered creators, and advanced social good. Projects & Programs

Games for Change offers a comprehensive suite of professional services, including program design, consulting, and executive production. We work with a range of partners across sectors — from gaming and tech to education, health, and social services — to develop programs, events, and content that tap into the power of games and immersive experiences to change the world.
