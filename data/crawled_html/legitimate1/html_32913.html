Pacicco & Pacicco was founded by Alfred M. Pacicco on Canal Street in Manhattan, New York City. His father-in-law, Rudolph Cacioli, had started in the diamond and jewelry business in the 1920s on Canal Street in Manhattan. He was a renowned setter. In 1945, Alfred took over his business and expanded beyond setting, beginning our tradition of making jewelry in-house. In 1950, he moved the store to its current location, 29 West 47th Street in New York City’s Diamond District. Alfred’s son Michael Pacicco is the current owner and operator of this business; he brings an unparalleled expertise in diamonds and fine jewelry, a trade that has been in his family for generations.  Peachy Deegan has been going to him before she started Whom You Know.

From the company’s inception to present day, Pacicco & Pacicco has specialized in fine quality diamonds and jewelry, dedicated to a philosophy of honesty and integrity. With an illustrious family history spanning 100 years, we combine cutting-edge computer-generated technology with decades of hands-on expertise to transform your dreams into reality. Michael Pacicco, leveraging his decades of experience as well as extensive training and certification from the Gemology Institute of America, infuses his knowledge and expertise into every facet of our business operations. Pacicco & Pacicco excels in crafting and customizing exquisite, bespoke diamond engagement rings, ensuring unparalleled craftsmanship and personalized service for every client.

Our legacy extends beyond fine diamonds and jewelry; it encompasses generations of satisfied customers who have entrusted us with their most precious moments. For decades, families have returned to us, from parents to their children and grandchildren, seeking not just jewelry, but an enduring commitment to quality and service. Our foundation is built upon a reputation of trust and excellence, earned through personalized care and impeccable craftsmanship. Whether creating a custom engagement ring or restoring a family heirloom, each piece reflects our dedication to exceeding expectations and ensuring lasting satisfaction. Join our esteemed clientele and experience firsthand why Pacicco & Pacicco Jewelers remains a trusted name in the heart of Manhattan’s Diamond District.

Michael Pacicco, the current owner of Pacicco & Pacicco Jewelers, is a distinguished graduate of the Gemology Institute of America (GIA), the nonprofit leader in gems and jewelry education, knowledge, and standards. GIA plays a vital role in protecting and educating the public within the gems and jewelry industry and is the authority on gemstone evaluation worldwide.

With rigorous training in gemstone evaluation and ethical standards, GIA's education division equips jewelry professionals with practical skills, ensuring they uphold the highest ethical standards. This rigorous education, combined with a heritage steeped in jewelry craftsmanship, has equipped Michael with a profound understanding of diamonds and fine jewelry. He has inherited decades of knowledge from his grandfather and father, ensuring that Pacicco & Pacicco continues to thrive on a foundation of expertise and integrity passed down through generations.

Twenty-two bottles of Jacques Fath perfume!

JACQUES FATH PARFUMS

Born in France in 1912, Jacques Fath’s passion for fashion led to the opening of his first Couture house in Paris in 1937. His unique and glamorous style quickly attracted success, dressing the ‘young chic Parisian’ and influential clientele. Notably, Hubert de Givenchy, Guy Laroche and Valentino Garavani worked as assistants in his atelier, later founding their own Fashion Houses.

The perfumes’ saga begins with the launch of Chasuble in 1945 and later, with the iconic creations of IRIS GRIS and GREEN WATER in collaboration with perfumer Vincent Roubert, solidifying Jacques Fath Parfums’ legendary status in Haute Parfumerie.

Three haircuts by Edward Tricomi!

Peachy Deegan has simple taste.
She likes only the best.
Warren Tricomi is the best because Edward Tricomi is the best.

Legendary Warren-Tricomi Salons co-founder Edward Tricomi is known for demanding excellence in everything he does. Be it hair, fashion, or music – when Tricomi is involved, perfection isn’t just expected: it’s inevitable. The roots (no pun intended) of his renowned career began after a suggestion from his sister that he try cutting hair to supplement his income as a musician. Growing up, Edward read Vogue religiously, devouring and absorbing the art within it. Moreover, as the grandson of a fashion designer, he was always surrounded and inspired by the world of fashion. Decades later, Edward is perhaps the most iconic and influential hair historian of our era.

Warren-Tricomi prides itself on being more than just a hair and beauty salon. All stylists trained at Warren-Tricomi are required to study and become experts in not only styling, but also fashion history. It is fundamental to the Warren-Tricomi brand that this extensive knowledge and perspective is present in every single haircut given in their salons. Few other salons can also make this claim, in the same way that few other salons are perpetually and actively on the forefront of beauty and fashion in the way Warren-Tricomi has been for the last 25 years. This significance and influence is evident in many facets of Tricomi’s business, one of which is the fact that they do at least three fashion shows each season, all over the world.

Throughout his prolific career Edward has mentored many well-known stylists including Italo Gregorio, Patrick Melville and Ric Popino. He has worked with virtually every celebrity, including Barbara Streisand, Mick Jagger, Nicky and Paris Hilton, the Kardashians, Scarlett Johansson, Jessica Alba, Justin Timberlake and Jimmy Fallon. Edward is constantly garnering inspiration from the people he works with: models like Patti Hansen, makeup artists like Pat McGrath and photographers like Roxanne Lowit.

The legacy of Edward and the Warren-Tricomi Salons continue to be a driving force and influential cornerstone of both the beauty and fashion industries. Their cutting-edge techniques, coupled with their deep appreciation and understanding of history and those who have come before and inspired them, enables Edward’s business to be the embodiment of modern fashion and beauty; at once both trendsetting and classic. Yet despite his significant impact and illustrious, celebrated career one esteemed fashion photographer and devoted Warren-Tricomi customer calls Edward a “phenomenon and one of the most talented hair dressers in the world” passionate and devoted beauty pioneer and mogul Edward Tricomi will tell you he’s never worked a day in his life.
