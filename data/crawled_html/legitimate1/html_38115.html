As of January 2023, we offer a guarantee for all tax returns generated by the app. That means that if you receive a fine because of an error or omission in any of the taxes you submitted through Accountable – even if it’s because of a mistake you made – we will cover that fine (up to €10,000 per declaration in a Max plan, excl. fraud and non-submission of taxes).

Accountable is the first tax software in Belgium to offer this guarantee, and the only one that also covers any mistakes made by the user.

It is our way to offer complete peace of mind to everyone using Accountable to submit their taxes.

For more information about our tax declaration guarantee, please take a look at this page. If you have any questions about the guarantee, don’t hesitate to contact us through the chat in your right hand corner.

Accountable offers a range of plans designed to grow with you, whether you’re just starting out or managing a thriving business. Each plan gives you the tools you need to stay on top of your invoicing, accounting, and taxes at your own pace and budget.

Free Invoicing is perfect for when you are just getting started. You can create and send invoices straight from your phone, completely free of charge. It’s the simplest way to get paid and keep your business running smoothly without any cost or complexity.

Invoicing Plus, available for €9.9 per month (excluding VAT) in the yearly plan, is made for professionals who want to take invoicing to the next level. It lets you create professional invoices from both web and mobile, personalise your templates, and save time with quotes and recurring invoices. Everything you need to manage invoicing like a pro, without diving into full accounting.

Bookkeeping, at €19.9 per month (including VAT) in the yearly plan, is ideal for those who want to manage their full accounting and expenses. You can track your income and spending, access all features on web, and collaborate easily with your accountant.

Important to note: if you don’t charge VAT because of your activity or you have a small business, this plan also gives you access to our tax coaches and to your yearly closing tax returns, so you can manage everything yourself, if you want.

Taxes, available for €29.9 per month (including VAT) in the yearly plan, is designed for those who want to handle everything themselves, without an accountant. You get full access to bookkeeping and invoicing tools, can prepare and file your taxes directly in the app, and enjoy the reassurance of a €500 tax guarantee. It’s the right choice if you want to stay in control and simplify your admin from start to finish.

Max, at €59.9 per month (including VAT) in the yearly plan, is for those who want complete peace of mind. It includes everything from the Taxes plan, plus personal onboarding, quarterly calls with your tax coach, and our strongest €10,000 tax guarantee. The best fit for freelancers who want expert guidance and confidence all year long.

Our AI Tax Advisor supports you with your taxes and answers all your questions about taxes, bookkeeping and self-employment.

The AI Tax Advisor is trained in Belgian tax law. If allowed by the user, it can also take your personal situation as a self-employed individual into account, for personalised answers.

That’s what the AI Tax Advisor can do for you:
– It ensures simple and error-free tax returns
– It answers all your tax questions
– It takes the stress out of taxes and offers a solution for the current accountant shortage

The Accountable AI Tax Advisor gives you personalised tax tips so that you can meet all your tax obligations without errors and have every possible question answered. Not only can the AI talk to you, it can also do things for you. For example, your details are automatically checked for errors. So you can be sure that your tax return is correct.

How do we do it?
1. We have trained our AI with the latest tax information from tax law, practical tips from our tax advisors and our own huge database of tax knowledge, making it a unique AI expert for self-employed people in Belgium.

2. Our AI Tax Advisor automatically checks and validates the data you enter so that everything is correct and meets the requirements.

3. Another unique feature is that you receive specific individual tips tailored to your situation. For example, you can now ask: “What haven’t I deducted so far?”, “The tax office has sent me this letter, what exactly do I need to do?”, or “How much money do I need to set aside for taxes?”

What about privacy and control? This is of course particularly important to us. That’s why you decide at any time what information you share with your AI Tax Advisor.
