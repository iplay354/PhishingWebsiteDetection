OUR PROMISE TO YOU

ORGANIC

Arbor Teas is deeply committed to organic agriculture. Every tea and herb we sell is certified organic. Our warehouse and tea-packing facility are certified organic by Global Organic Alliance, rest assured that the organic integrity of our tea and herbs are preserved from crop to cup. + more

QUALITY

We at Arbor Teas perform extensive comparative tasting whenever we consider adding a new tea to our catalog. If it isn’t up to snuff, we simply won’t offer it. Our inventory is selected with quality, organic certification, and Fair Trade certification in mind. + more

FAIR TRADE

We believe that we have a responsibility to support a higher standard of living and a sustainable future for the people who bring us these fantastic products. We are committed to offering one of the widest selections of Fair Trade Certified® loose leaf teas available, and we celebrate the communities from which they originate. + more

SUSTAINABILITY

At Arbor Teas, our mission is to find the world's most fantastic organic teas and deliver them to you as sustainably as possible. We constantly work to improve this process, envisioning a tea leaf that happily moves through its life cycle creating a healthier world and a healthier you. + more

COMPOSTABLE PACKAGING

We are proud to be the first and only company offering our full line of loose leaf teas in backyard compostable packaging! Our innovative backyard compostable packaging uses a cellulose film derived from wood pulp, and a label made of sugarcane-based paper. + more

HAPPY CUSTOMERS

We love our customers! They are intelligent, earth-conscious, fun-loving folks that continually suprise us with their insightful comments and dedication to a healthy, just planet. We are proud to have such a wonderful tea-loving community of customers! + more

DEPENDABLE PRICING

We understand that drinking tea is an everyday routine for most of our customers. We price our products with this in mind. We have worked hard to make sure that our products are priced dependably every day - for our customers, our producers and our company. + more

ABOUT US

Arbor Teas is a family-owned organic tea company based in Ann Arbor, Michigan. Our daily mission is to find the world's most fantastic organic teas and deliver them to you as sustainably as possible. We love what we do! + more
