Visit Us

89 East Strand Street
Kingston, NY 12401

Hours:
Closed for the 2025 Season, but we will see you
at the Wintertide Festival and Tree Lighting from 1-5pm!

Directions to the Museum:

Exit 19 (Kingston) on the New York State Thruway, then follow the signs for the Historic Rondout Waterfront and Urban Cultural Park. Continue to the end of Broadway and take a left. You’ll find us about a quarter-mile down East Strand on the left.

Driving time is about 2 hours from Man­hat­tan and 1 hour from Albany.
