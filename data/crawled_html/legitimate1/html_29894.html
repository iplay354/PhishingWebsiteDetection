Market Overview

Trayport’s Joule solution plays a pivotal role in enabling liquidity, transparency, and efficiency in European energy markets. Joule connects market participants for smarter, faster energy trading and its scale reflects the trust and reliance of the energy trading community.

In 2024 alone, approximately 16,907 TWh of German energy was traded through Joule, enough to power Germany for around 33 years.
