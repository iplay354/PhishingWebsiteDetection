  • Third Air Force Command Chief tours Ramstein AB

    U.S. Air Force Chief Master Sgt. Jason Trickey, Third Air Force command chief, took an immersion tour at Ramstein Air Base, Dec. 11. The immersion reinforced alignment with the 86th Airlift Wing’s mission priorities of readiness, Airmen and relationships.

  • 86 AW Airmen awarded Gallant Unit Citation

    Eighteen Airmen from the 86th Airlift Wing who deployed in support of the 40th Expeditionary Airlift Squadron received the Gallant Unit Citation Dec. 5, for their role in the 386th Air Expeditionary Wing’s combat operations from April 2 to Oct. 6.
