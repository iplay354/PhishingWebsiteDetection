Penguin Air, Plumbing & Electrical

Problem

A prominent Nexstar member servicing the Phoenix Valley, Penguin Air, Plumbing & Electrical first came to Blue Corona in 2019 in search of a new digital marketing partner. A once well-established brand in the Valley community, Penguin was quickly losing rankings, visibility, and customers to competitors that outranked and outperformed them on search engines. Historically, Penguin grew top-line revenue nearly 40% each year until 2019. Owners Wade and Will were ready to make the leap from the “biggest small guy” in the Valley to big-time players.

Solutions

A digital competitive analysis of Penguin’s online presence revealed multiple areas of opportunity across their website, organic search presence (SEO) and paid media (PPC, specifically).

Our Results

Revenue generated from digital marketing

12%

Reduction in cost per validated lead from digital sources

54%

Of total revenue generated by digital marketing

American Vintage Home

Problem

American Vintage Home, an award-winning residential HVAC and plumbing company servicing Chicagoland’s North Shore area, knew they were up against tough competition online. With multiple, large home service companies dominating the first page of Google search results, the company had an uphill battle to outrank their competitors to generate more qualified leads from their website.

Solutions

The company turned to Blue Corona to improve the performance of its digital marketing campaigns, with an emphasis on search engine optimization (SEO). After an in-depth client intake and onboarding process, Blue Corona’s team got to work installing a custom tracking and analytics setup, optimizing existing web pages, publishing new, SEO-optimized content, and claiming and optimizing local director listings on behalf of American Vintage Home.

Our Results

500+

Keywords ranked on page one of Google search results

10%

Year-over-year increase in validated leads from SEO

77:1

ROI from their SEO investment
