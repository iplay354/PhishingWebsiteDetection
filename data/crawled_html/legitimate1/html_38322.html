Marievele’s Marquee Hire

Outstanding Marquees

Marievele’s Marquees was established in 1974 and now has over 40 years’ experience in the marquee hire trade, offering party and wedding marquee hire to private, commercial and corporate clients. Since then we have earned an excellent reputation as a professional and reliable family owned and run company, offering the very best marquee hire services throughout London and the South East.

Professional, Experienced Marquee Hire

Being one of the most well established Clearspan marquee hire and events hire companies, we have been all over installing marquees from venues to car parks, for clients celebrating new year to exhibiting their new line of manufacturing equipment. Working within the constraints of your event, planning, logistics, health and safety and contractors. Our efficient planning, experience and knowledge means we are suited to almost any marquee challenge ahead of us.

Customer Service You Can Rely On

From the first point of contact with Marievele’s you can expect a friendly, personal and professional service. We deliver a complete end-to-end service providing not just Clearspan marquee hire but also the provision of associated core services such as furniture and catering, beautiful lighting, heating, seating, decorations, entertainment, special effects, and floristry etc.

Transform Your Event With Marievele’s Marquees

Your Marievele’s Marquee provides a highly flexible venue that can quickly and easily adapt to your needs. With the capability to hold up to 1,500 people, you can be sure that hiring a marquee for your wedding, party or event will give you plenty of space to entertain. Ensure your guests have the night of their lives, with options for lighting, flooring, heating, and tables and chairs. Whatever your budget, you will doubtless find a marquee hire solution you will love.
