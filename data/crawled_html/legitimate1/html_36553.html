Website celebrating May Day festivities in Oxford

Some 16,500 attended in 2025 

Above: Broad Street, May Morning 2025. Below: Jack-in-the-Green proceeds up the High in 2024

(Photos Tim Healey) 

May morning in Oxford is famous for the thousands who gather at 6am to hear a Latin hymn sung from the top of Magdalen College tower. It is an extraordinary ceremony, but only one feature of Oxford tradition.


Maytime revels take place all over the city, and were already controversial in Britain in 1250 when the Chancellor of Oxford University forbade âalike in churches, all dancing in masks or with disorderly noises, and all processions of men wearing wreaths and garlands made of leaves of trees or flowers or what not.â
