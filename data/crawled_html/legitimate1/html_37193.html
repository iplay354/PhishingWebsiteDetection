    Birmingham Coffee Trail

    No matter where you are in Birmingham or the surrounding cities, you’ll find no shortage of unique, locally loved coffee shops. From matcha lattes to creative seasonal flavors, each neighborhood has its own favorite spots worth exploring. Use this guide to sip your way through Birmingham’s coffee scene, one cup at a time.

    Culture

    Historically Black Colleges & Universities Tour

    Alabama is home to more Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs) than any other state—and there’s no better place to begin your journey than right here in Birmingham. Known for its rich civil rights history and vibrant culture, Birmingham serves as the perfect home base for exploring several of the state’s distinguished HBCUs. While you’re here, be sure to support and experience some of the city’s incredible Black-owned businesses along the way.’
