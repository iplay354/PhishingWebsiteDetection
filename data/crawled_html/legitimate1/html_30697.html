Our 5-Free Promise

Understanding what goes into your nail polish is crucial for making informed choices about your beauty routine. Our 5-free promise represents our commitment to formulating safer, healthier nail lacquers without compromising on performance or beauty.

What Does 5-Free Mean?

The term “5-free” refers to nail polish formulas that exclude five specific toxic chemicals commonly found in traditional nail lacquers. This designation has become the gold standard for health-conscious consumers who want to enjoy beautiful nails without exposure to potentially harmful substances.

Toxic Ingredients We Never Use

Top Shelf Lacquer proudly excludes these five harmful chemicals from every formula:

  1. Formaldehyde – A known carcinogen that can cause respiratory issues and skin irritation
  2. Toluene – A solvent that can affect the nervous system and cause headaches
  3. Dibutyl Phthalate (DBP) – An endocrine disruptor linked to reproductive issues
  4. Formaldehyde Resin – Can cause contact dermatitis and allergic reactions
  5. Camphor – May cause nausea, dizziness, and skin irritation in high concentrations

Benefits of 5-Free Nail Polish

Choosing 5-free nail polish offers numerous advantages beyond just avoiding toxic chemicals. Users often report improved nail health, reduced allergic reactions, and a more pleasant application experience. The absence of harsh chemicals also means our polishes have a milder odor, making the manicure process more enjoyable.
