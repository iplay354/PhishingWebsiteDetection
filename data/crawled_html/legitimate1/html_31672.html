The Best Beach in Texas

South Padre Island

With 34 miles of pristine white beaches, stunning waters from the Gulf of Mexico, and year-round temps averaging 74 degrees, South Padre Island is a beachgoer’s paradise. Ranked # 1 in “Best Beaches of Texas” according to U.S. News & World Report, SPI is the ultimate Texas Gulf Coast vacation experience. Whether you’re looking for a tranquil getaway or prefer a trip teeming with adventure, SPI has something for every beach lover’s appetite.
