Institutes & Initiatives

IAEAi

Institute for Advanced Enterprise AI

The Institute for Advanced Enterprise AI (IAEAi) is an applied research institute dedicated to developing and sharing a deep, practical understanding of how AI can be used by businesses to ensure trust and transparency. 

DSCI

Digital Supply Chain Institute

The Digital Supply Chain Institute is a leading-edge research institute focused on the evolution of enterprise supply chains in the digital economy and the creation and practical application of supply chain management best practices.

CRI

Cyber Readiness Institute

The Cyber Readiness Institute is a non-profit initiative that convenes business leaders from across sectors and geographic regions to share resources and knowledge that inform the development of free cybersecurity tools for small and medium-sized businesses.

Change Behavior. Be Cyber Ready.

D&TA

The Data & Trusted AI Alliance

The Data & Trusted AI Alliance brings together leading businesses and institutions across multiple industries to learn, develop, and adopt responsible data and AI practices.

AWEC

The African Women Entrepreneurship Cooperative

A 12-month leadership and management capacity building program that empowers women entrepreneurs in Africa to develop resilient businesses.

USAWEC

USA Women Entrepreneurship Cooperative

The USA Women Entrepreneurship Cooperative (USAWEC) is a 3-month business leadership and management blended-learning program run by the Center for Global Enterprise.

TrailblazeHers

The TrailblazeHers leadership program, established in 2021, pairs women in early-stage careers in supply chain with senior professionals in the field. To date, the program has trained 66 professionals through three completed cohorts.

Open eBL

Open-Source Electronic Bill of Lading

Open eBL is a non-profit initiative dedicated to transforming supply chain operations and trade document management by establishing open-source, standards-based electronic bill of lading (eBL) solutions.

Global Scholars Program

A free worldwide learning community for business-interested students, faculty and professionals.

The New Enlightenment Initiative

The New Enlightenment Initiative brings CEOs together to share insights and best practices, plan industry research, and discuss solutions to create an even bigger impact.

Applied Corporate Governance Institute

The Applied Corporate Governance Institute is a nonprofit organization founded by CGE to advise companies on how to understand, engage, and succeed with their investors in complex and contested shareholder matters.
