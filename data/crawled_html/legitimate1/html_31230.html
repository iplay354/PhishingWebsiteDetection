GO FUND ME CAMPAIGN

Dear Friends,

In today’s political climate, it has never been more critical to connect with scientists and encourage new research. Valerie is committed to fostering collaboration with European colleagues, advocating for new BPD treatments on an international scale, treating BPD with the urgency and care it deserves.

What is it worth to you if psychedelic research could give your loved one a chance for a better life? What would you give if new treatment could decrease BPD negativity and increase their positive feelings?

At Scientific Conferences we have a vital opportunity to exchange ideas with leading researchers and clinicians, to inspire new BPD treatment studies. It allows us to promote a shift in perspective—to see BPD as a disorder rooted in universal evolutionary DNA as well as family (genetics) DNA, rather than as diagnosis for social misfits. It provides us with the opportunity to include BPD in cutting-edge psychedelic research that has the potential to cure BPD.

Travel costs are a significant challenge for us. We need your support to make this possible. Your donation will directly help cover travel expenses, enabling us to participate in crucial conversations where research that can shape the future of BPD treatment is possible.

Join us in this effort, your contribution brings us closer to a world where BPD is no longer stigmatized but effectively treated and ultimately cured.

With gratitude,

All of us at TARA
