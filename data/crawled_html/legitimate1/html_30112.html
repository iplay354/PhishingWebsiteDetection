Who we are

America's longest-running open-air craft market!

The Saturday Market is in it’s 56th Season! We’re dedicated to fostering appreciation for local art, promoting new businesses, and strengthening community bonds. We invite you to experience the authentic products and supportive spirit that make our market a true community hub.

The Saturday Market is the perfect place to shop for locally handmade goods, listen to live music, and grab a bite to eat from our International Food Court.

Since its founding in 1970, the Eugene Saturday Market has been a cherished institution, serving as a vibrant main attraction and a cornerstone of the city’s rich cultural and artistic tradition.

Our Mission is to Promote and Advance:

Public appreciation and awareness of local art and artisans — Growth and development of new business and successful local artisans — The arts and sciences — Community education and networking — The social welfare of the community
