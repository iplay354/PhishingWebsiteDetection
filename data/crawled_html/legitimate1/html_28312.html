Brookvale Spares - The Home of Spare Parts in Sydney

Originally opened on Sydney’s Northern Beaches, Brookvale Spares has been servicing the Sydney car market with quality spare parts for over 30 years. Our Environmental Policy

Vehicles are recyclable. In fact 82% of a vehicle can be recovered and recycled into spare parts. At Brookvale Spares we pride ourselves on promoting recycling and reusing auto parts, minimising landfill for a longer period, and reducing the number of new parts manufactured each year.

Working to pave the way to minimal waste in the auto industry, we recycle and recondition parts to the industry standards set by the Auto Parts Recycling Association of Australia, and the United Recyclers Group. Our used parts are screened, tested and cleaned to ensure they exceed the standards and we’re supplying quality, reliable and long lasting replacements for your vehicle.
