Who We Are

It all started in 2012 when Joelle Mertzel, a mother of 3, wanted to give her family soft, spreadable butter without the slippery mess that a traditional 2-piece butter dish makes. She created Butterie, the Flip-Top Butter Dish, and the journey began.

From educating the public to store butter on the counter through an FDA petition, to creating new items; Joelle continues to bring color and convenience into her home - and yours.
