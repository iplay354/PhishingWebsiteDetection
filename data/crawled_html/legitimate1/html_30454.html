Thursday, 2 May 2019

Shadow Box Card | Creating Kindness Blog Hop





Hello, Friends :-)

Thank you for joining us on our Creating Kindness Blog Hop with special guest Heike Hűlle-Bolze.



This month's theme is "Shadow Box Card"

 Just being honest, I haven't done any crafting since last month blog hop. (I say that every month, don't I?! lol)
I don't know where my time has gone. The Easter bunny ate my whole 3 weeks!!!
Kids were right next to me 24/7 last whole 3weeks.
I am so glad that the holiday is over. 





I love being in this Creating Kindness blog hop group. It's  One of the reasons that It forces to make time to craft which means stop me being crazy. HAHA
Gladly, The theme was eaasy. all I need is to make a shadow box card and deco.
It was super easy to make, took 2mins to die cutting for window and scoring. then started to panic that I have no idea what should I deco it!?

No Time = Punch
"Butterfly Gala Photopolymer Bundle" was the best option for me for this project.
no mess colouring.
5 times Stamping, one punching. now I have 2 coloured butterflies.





Then WHAT!?
I still had no idea. I thought It would be too plain to cover the whole background.
(Now I am thinking that It will look good with few more butterflies covered as well instead of the photo.)

 I was staring at the butterflies and scored cardstocks for almost 20mins. couldn't think anything. Time Up! I had to pick up kids and go to GP the whole afternoon.

So Last night after kids went to bed, I started staring them again as I left.






Suddenly, Ping!
Photo Frame!!
I always wanted to send kids photos to In-law's house. But worry that a frame will break or too expensive to send.






This Shadow Box Card is a perfect solution!

 It will be good to send by mail!! now I am so excited to send lots of them out!
Also, I can write some message on the back of the card. It's perfect to be a photo frame or greeting card!
Tooooo simple, But Simple is the best, right? I love it so much!!!!
I can imagine making more of them with small time! 


***Measurement***
2 x base - 4" x 6"
Scoring - Long side 1/2", 1" each side
