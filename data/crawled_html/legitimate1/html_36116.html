Welcome to
By Design Ministries.

We believe all women are uniquely designed to contribute significantly to reaching our world for Christ.

Our purpose

By Design Ministries is dedicated to the equipping and development of women for ministry that will advance the kingdom of God. We accomplish this through events, educational tools and classes, ministry resources, and by developing relationships that encourage and equip women to grow in Christ and increase their competency to minister to others.
