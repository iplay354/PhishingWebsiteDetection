What are your turnaround times? Turnaround times depend on the complexity, urgency, and number of images in your project. In most cases, we deliver within 12 to 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and international bank transfers. For bulk orders or ongoing projects, we also support invoicing with flexible terms.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

There’s no long-term contract required. You can start, pause, or stop your service anytime. We believe in earning your trust with every image we deliver — not through binding agreements.

How do you handle complex images in the Basic Plan?

The Basic Plan is designed for simple edits — such as background removal on standard product photos. What file formats do you accept?

We accept all major file formats, including JPEG, PNG, PSD, TIFF, and RAW. If you have a specific format in mind, let us know.
