Our Sustainable Business
It's time to rethink waste. We are using our expertise and innovation to help the UK on its path to a circular economy, while championing sustainable communities. Together with UK business and organisations we are transforming waste into valuable resources and lessening its impact on the environment. Find out how you can be part of it.
Are you compliant with the Simpler Recycling legislation?
As of 31st March 2025, if your business or organisation has 10 or more full time equivalent employees, you need to be compliant. Failure to comply with the regulations could result in action or potential fines from the Environment Agency.

What makes us stand out?

When we first started over 100 years ago, waste management simply meant taking it away. Today, we help organisations of all shapes and sizes, manage their waste sustainably and cost effectively. We help them to reduce waste, recycle more and even transform it into valuable resources.

Let's work together

Small & medium enterprises

We work with all kinds of UK businesses. From restaurants on the high street, to printers in the business park, we provide efficient, sustainable, low-cost waste disposal solutions, supported by a 24/7 customer portal, to make life simpler and help you get on with what you do best.
Find out more

Larger organisations

Whatever your sustainability goals, we have the scale, expertise, and state-of-the-art facilities to help you achieve your aims in the circular economy and create value from waste. We also like to think boldly – helping Mitchell’s & Butler’s create a greener future, and Greggs’ divert 100% of their waste from landfill.
Find out more

Local authorities

Our household waste division works with 31 local authorities nationwide, to deliver cost-efficient, responsible and safe waste management services. Our efforts contribute to a sector-leading 49.6% recycling rate, creating and supporting more sustainable communities, and helping the UK build a more circular economy. We make being more sustainable with waste easier.
