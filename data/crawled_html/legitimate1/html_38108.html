    The Supper Club and Citi Strata Elite Continue Their Dining Series at The Corner Store

    A crisp fall evening in Soho framed the latest installment of The Supper Club and Citi Strata Elite dining series, this time inside The Corner Store. The restaurant's warm glow paired with an inventive menu and cult martinis, setting the pace for a night that captured why the restaurant has become a Manhattan favorite. As guests settled into the dining room and patio, Citi’s Courtney Joseph, Head of Rewards Products and Premium Customer Experience, and The Supper Club CEO Marc Lotenberg welcomed everyone with a celebratory toast.

    Signature cocktails became a centerpiece of the evening. The Corner Store’s flights—Cap’n Crunch milk punch, a pecan Manhattan, and savory riffs like sour cream and onion martinis—moved through the room with ease and encouraged lively conversation. The resulting dinner blended hospitality, design, and unmistakable New York character. For the Strata Elite community, the dining series is closely tied to the card’s benefit at restaurants and restaurant delivery services. The dining series brings that benefit to life.
