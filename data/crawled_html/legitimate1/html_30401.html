THE PROMISE OF AN UNFORGETTABLE WEDDING

IN THE SOUTH OF FRANCE AND PROVENCE
SINCE 2005

I am Céline Camy, founder of Wedding Angels, and I invite you to experience your dream Wedding, the one you’ve always envisioned. Whether you live in the region or on the other side of the world, I will guide you every step of the way to design, organize, and bring to life a unique event that reflects who you are.

Because planning a wedding is an art that requires expertise and dedication, I bring you 20 years of experience, a keen sense of listening, and my in-depth knowledge of the local area.

My curated selection of service suppliers allows me to offer a comprehensive range of options tailored to your vision. From scenography and floral design to furnishings, musical entertainment, catering, photography and videography, every detail is thoughtfully crafted to reflect your vision. And of course, the venue - a unique setting for your union. Two decades of connections with the owners of exclusive, preserved, and off-market venues enable me to open doors for my clients, even those usually closed to the public.

FOUNDER OF WEDDING ANGELS

Since 2005, I have been designing and planning weddings on the French Riviera and in Provence, fulfilling the desires and needs of local, national, and international clients.

With a naturally positive attitude, I thrive on meeting the most demanding challenges. Within my network, I am known for my meticulous attention to detail and my expertise in event logistics and design, ensuring that nothing is left to chance. These qualities have been essential over the years in building the excellent reputation of Wedding Angels.

I firmly believe that wedding planning depends on collaboration, and I work closely with my clients to ensure that their vision comes to life. My approach combines professionalism and creativity, transforming every moment into an unforgettable experience. I am committed to making the planning process not only seamless but also enriching, turning each step into an integral part of the magic of your big day.
