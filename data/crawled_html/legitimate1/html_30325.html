Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Blonde Envy Hair Salons offer?

«

Blonde Envy Hair Salons offer a wide range of services including haircuts, hair coloring, highlights, balayage, hair extensions, hair styling, blowouts, keratin treatments, and more.

Do you offer bridal or special occasion hair styling?

«

Yes, we offer bridal and special occasion hair styling services. Please contact us well in advance to discuss your needs and secure your booking.

Can I go from dark hair to blonde in one session?

«

Going from dark to blonde often requires multiple sessions to achieve the desired result while minimising damage. Our colourists will create a customised plan for your hair transformation.

How do I make an appointment?

«

You can easily make an appointment by calling our salons directly or using our online booking system. Choose your preferred location, select the service, date, and time that works for you, and follow the prompts to confirm your booking.

Can I request a specific stylist?

«

Yes, you can request a specific stylist when booking your appointment. Our team will do their best to accommodate your request based on availability.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my appointment?

«

We understand that plans can change. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to avoid any cancellation fees.

Do you offer consultations?

«

Yes, we offer complimentary consultations. It's a great opportunity to discuss your hair goals with a stylist, get personalised recommendations, and address any questions or concerns you may have.
