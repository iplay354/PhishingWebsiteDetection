We Believe Great Interior Design Can Be Easy and Accessible

Dream by MJS Interiors is a creative, sophisticated, yet affordable interior design service in Houston, TX. Dream evolved from 30 years of elegant residential and commercial interior design by Michael J. Siller Interiors. His projects have been featured in Architectural Digest, Southern Accents and Gloss. Dream is the culmination of his vision to bring fashionable interior design to a broader segment of society. Check out our work to see why we’re the top interior design firm in Houston!
