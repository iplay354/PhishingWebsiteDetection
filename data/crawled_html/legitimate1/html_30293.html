Doors designed to make life and living better

solutions

At Masonite, we create Doors That Do More™ for the people who pass through them every day. We believe doors should connect you with the people and things you care about most — and make life easier, safer and more beautiful, too.

Doors that reduce noise

Keep sound where it belongs—in or out of a room—with sound-dampening doors.

Doors that enhance safety

Feel safer and more secure with integrated features like multipoint locks or fire- and impact-rated doors.

Doors that increase light

Bring natural light into your home with doors that have more visible glass area.

Doors that conserve energy

Stay comfortable inside, whatever the weather is outside, with an exterior door system that provides better protection from the elements.

Doors that elevate style

Complement your home’s design with our curated collection of doors for the whole home.
