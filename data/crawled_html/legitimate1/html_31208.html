Frequently Asked Questions

How much moringa powder should I take?

The recommended dose is one teaspoon, once a day.

When is the best time to take moringa powder?

There is no set time you have to take your moringa powder, however people taking it for energy and focus tend to find it works best when taken in the morning. If you're after an immune boost, take your moringa at night to let it do it's work while you sleep. But the best time to take moringa is whichever time best fits into your routine - consistency is key for best results!

Does moringa powder have a taste?

Moringa powder does have quite a distinctive, somewhat "earthy" taste. It has been compared to a peppery green tea and others find it a little bitter. Some don't mind it at all, while others who are more sensitive to flavors don't care for it. Luckily, you don't have to take moringa powder straight - mix it into your favorite smoothie or for a tasteless option, go with the moringa capsules.

How to take moringa powder for weight loss?

Studies have found moringa powder to be most effective for weight loss when taken early in the morning as a tea. Steep 1 teaspoon of moringa powder in hot water for 5 to 10 minutes and enjoy. You could add some mint and lemon to your brew to enhance the palatability.

How much protein is in moringa powder?

Moringa has around 28 grams of protein per 100 grams of powder. This means a tablespoon contains around 1 gram of protein.

What are the moringa powder benefits for hair?

Moringa powder offers several benefits for hair due to its rich nutrient profile. Its high content of vitamins like A, E, and B, along with minerals such as zinc and iron, are all crucial for healthy hair growth and strength. The antioxidants in moringa also help protect hair follicles from damage caused by environmental stressors. Regularly incorporating moringa, either internally or as a topical treatment, can contribute to stronger, shinier, and more resilient hair.

What are the moringa powder benefits for male testosterone?

Some animal research suggests that moringa may help increase testosterone levels, particularly in situations of stress or oxidative damage to the testes. This is thought to be due to moringa's strong antioxidant properties, which can protect the Leydig cells responsible for testosterone production.

A small human study also indicated that moringa protein could increase resting testosterone levels in men with hyperlipidemia (high cholesterol).
