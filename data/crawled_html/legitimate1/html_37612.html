White Paper: Streamlining the Rolling Stock approval processes

The multiplicity of approval processes to operate rolling stock across Australia has been identified by the rail freight industry as a major deterrent to investment and innovation. To address this industry wide issue, RISSB has commissioned a White Paper: Streamlining the Rolling Stock approval processes.

Find out more

RISSB 2024-2030 Strategic Plan

RISSB’s 2024-2030 Strategic Plan is designed to address the Australian and New Zealand Rail industry’s most pressing challenges and opportunities. The plan is informed by broad engagement with key industry and government stakeholders and includes shifting RISSB’s focus from producing and maintaining Standards and related products, to delivering outcomes.

FIND OUT MORE

The Authorised Health Professional (AHP) Program

RISSB and Metro Trains Australia (MTA) have worked together to develop a new AHP Program to manage and provide support for AHPs, while also facilitating access to suitably trained and experienced AHPs for Rail Transport Operators and rail safety workers.

Find out More

Become a RISSB Member

Join over 160 rail organisations and become a RISSB member. As a RISSB Member, you will be part of a dynamic organisation that is at the forefront of safety and standards development in the rail industry. Becoming a member of RISSB will provide access to our Standards, Codes of Practice and Guidelines, our safety and data tools, and a range of RISSB programs and events. RISSB membership is open to all organisations involved in rail.

Horizons 7

RISSB's Horizons Program offers high-potential rail professionals the opportunity to hear from industry leaders, explore cutting-edge technology and innovation and gain the leadership and technical skills needed for a successful career in rail.

A strengthened RISSB set to become the industry-led technical standards setting body

The strengthened RISSB will be industry led, bringing public and private operators and suppliers to the table, with unions and governments to harmonise rail standards and to implement the national rail standards.

RISSB’s 2025-26 Work Plan

Our FY2026 Work Plan is formulated from a robust set of criteria including feedback from members, stakeholders, and governments, key industry safety risks identified through the Australian Rail Risk Model (ARRM), national reform and regulatory priorities, and major incident investigation outcomes.

View Work Plan

Industry’s Partner in Co-regulation

RISSB provides industry and governments with the systems expertise and technical underpinning that drive ongoing improvements in the standards of safety, productivity and sustainability for Australian and New Zealand Rail.

RISSB is the only accredited Standards development organisation for the rail industry in Australia. RISSB Standards are Australian Standards®
