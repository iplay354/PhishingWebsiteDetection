OUR SIGNATURE EVENTS

THE YAKIMA VALLEY - WHERE SPORTS COME TO PLAY

Athletes and event organizers throughout the Pacific Northwest appreciate the Yakima Valley for its outstanding sports venues, convenient location, supportive community and terrific weather. That level of service has earned us the title of Where Sports Come to Play. See the Our Services section to find out how the Yakima Valley Sports Commission can assist you.

The Yakima Valley Sports Commission is a division of Yakima Valley Tourism. Our primary mission is economic development of the Yakima Valley through sports. The goal of our staff is to provide exceptional customer service to sports organizers who stage their events in the Yakima Valley.

Take advantage of the outstanding facilities and hospitality you will find in the Yakima Valley.
