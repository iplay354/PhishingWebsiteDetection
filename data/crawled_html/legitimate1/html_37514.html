The 49th Christmas Race gets underway with four races on the third day

Youth Sailing World Championships 2025 – A Championship Defined by Adaptability

The 2025 Youth Sailing World Championships concluded in Vilamoura after a demanding week of racing. Ukraine secured the Men/Mixed title following a dramatic final day, while Great Britain claimed the Female crown after no racing was possible on the last day due to time limits.

The final day of the Youth Sailing World Championships delivered three decisive races for the Men/Mixed fleet, crowning Ukraine as champions after a thrilling finale. With no racing possible for the Female fleet due to time limits, Great Britain claimed the title based on the existing standings.
