Services

Prayer Room and Timetable

An essential aspect of our Cultural Centre is the prayer room, which offers a peaceful environment for spiritual reflection and community prayers. We provide a comprehensive prayer timetable to ensure that all community members can attend prayers at the designated times. The prayer room and timetable are integral to our commitment to support the spiritual needs of our community, fostering a sense of unity and devotion.

School Classes

The YCA offers dedicated educational programs for children, including Arabic language and Quranic studies. Our Arabic School teaches children aged 8 to 14, focusing on engaging and effective language learning. The Quran School aims to instill Islamic values and helps children read, understand, and recite the Quran. Both schools employ experienced and certified teachers, ensuring a safe and high-quality learning environment. Formal evaluations consistently praise our commitment to educational excellence.

Room Hire

We offer a variety of rooms for hire to suit your needs, including a spacious conference room, an IT learning centre, and an activity room. Our facilities are perfect for meetings, training sessions, and events. Refreshments and equipment hire are also available. Bookings can be made for weekdays, evenings, and weekends.

About Us

The Yemeni Community Association (YCA) is a community based organisation that seeks to represent and meet the particular needs of Yemeni and other Arabic speaking communities in Sandwell as well as extending its services and activities to other disadvantaged communities within the area. The YCA achieves this by providing services over and above those available in the mainstream sector, and also by acting as an interface between the community and various service providers. It was registered as an official charity in 1997.
