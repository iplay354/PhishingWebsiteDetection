Our Story

Upholding a legacy of craftsmanship and excellence established over 200 years ago and profoundly inspired by how people live and gather today, we’re a leader in the tabletop industry, delivering timeless products that elevate every day.

Our Family

Lenox Corporation encompasses a diverse portfolio of premium brands, all with a unique perspective. Just like your family, each member of ours brings something special to the table. We have something for everyone and every event.

Our Promise

To innovate and grow to meet modern needs while honoring our brands’ histories with a continued commitment to high standards. We promise to keep delighting you. Both with products that inspire you and with service you appreciate.

Lenox was founded in 1889 with a vision to set the highest standards for quality, beauty, and artistry. Today, Lenox Corporation encompasses six unique brands that honor these ideals with their passion for innovation and drive to delight.
