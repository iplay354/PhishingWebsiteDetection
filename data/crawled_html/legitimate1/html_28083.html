Standing Together for Education.

  • We believe in the power and promise of education.
  • We believe every student has the right to a high-quality education.
  • We believe in lifelong learning opportunities for all Californians.
  • Student learning conditions are our working conditions.
  • The strength of collective bargaining builds better workplaces.

CFT Legislative Scorecards
How do legislators vote on bills that matter to union members?

In each legislative session, the CFT tallies how legislators vote on bills of import to educators and union members. In these scorecards, you will find a description of key bills lobbied for and against, and a score for Senate and Assembly members on each bill. Find out if your elected officials are voting union!
