Portable Storage Options

Our Flexible Portable Storage Solutions Save You Time & Money

8' x 16' x 8' Storage Containers

Our 8' x 16' x 8' containers are big enough to fit about 3 to 4 rooms worth of stuff. These containers are 100% water resistant, roofs are made of galvanized steel and are corrugated to allow for optimal durability and water run off. Floors are made of marine grade plywood to provide a waterproof, non slip and non flammable surface. Door latch with option to add Padlock to secure container. Doors open outward for maximum space. Door Opening: 68" W x 78" H
