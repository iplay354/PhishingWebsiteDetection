Bead Manufacturing Is Our Business

We have been manufacturing plastic beads and other parts for crafts, jewelry, toys, and industrial applications in Rhode Island since 1932. Whether it was under the name Synthetic Moulding Products, Greene Plastics Corp., The Beadery® or Buick, llc, we have always delivered trend setting styles and quality that have lead the market. In the past nine decades, our company has grown tremendously, and is a world leader in plastic beads, acrylic rhinestones, cabochons, acrylic mirrors, decorative flower and leaf beads, pony beads – the list is endless! Check out our product lines to see what we currently have available.
