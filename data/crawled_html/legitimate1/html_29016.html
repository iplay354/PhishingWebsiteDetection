OUR STORY

SUSTAINABLY MADE, THOUGHTFULLY CRAFTED

At Seek & Swoon, sustainability starts with the thread. Our throws are made from recycled yarn, giving discarded textiles new life while reducing waste. Cozy up knowing your blanket is as kind to the planet as it is to you.
