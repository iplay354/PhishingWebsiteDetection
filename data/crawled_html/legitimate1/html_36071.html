      How to purchase online

      Here at Serramonte Subaru, we understand your time is valuable. That's why we provide options to make your next purchase or lease more convenient. If you prefer to do the process from home, you can start with a trade-in appraisal or a finance application. Once you select the vehicle you're interested in, you will have options to adjust your payments and purchase online. You can even get your vehicle delivered to your home!
