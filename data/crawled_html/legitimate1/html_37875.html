Why choose Bagpiper Online?

Quality

We use only the very best, hand-picked pipers, selected for their attitude and deportment, and their ability to the play the Highland bagpipes to the highest level. Our pipers are chosen from the cream of the Scottish Regiments and Grade One Pipe Bands, and all of our pipers have proven their ability at the highest level.

Reliability

We have been providing talented pipers for weddings and events throughout Glasgow, Edinburgh and Ayrshire since 2001. Our list of satisfied clients speaks for itself, but you can be guaranteed of the same level of professionalism and dedication from us each and every time we serve our customers.

Experience

Aside from those with regimental backgrounds and pipers who have won the World Championships, many of our pipers have performed for HM The Queen, The Pope, and various heads of state from Cairo to New York. We even have pipers who have performed alongside Pavarotti and West Life!

Service

Because we understand how important your event is, and because your Piper will be in your photographs and videos forever more, you can rely on Bagpiper Online to cater for all your individual piping requirements. From your initial enquiry until your piper's performance, our level of service and dedication will ensure you can concentrate on planning the other elements of your event with the peace of mind that the piper is taken care of.

Variety

Bagpiper-Online can also provide highland dancers and pipe bands to perform at your wedding reception or corporate event. Please contact us for prices and availability and to find out why we are the first choice of Scotland's leading hotel chains when it comes to providing pipers for weddings.
