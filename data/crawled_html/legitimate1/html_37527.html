Welcome to Michigan City, Indiana

Discover Michigan City, a vibrant lakeside community that balances the charm of small-town living with the opportunities of a dynamic economy. Explore our beautiful parks, thriving local businesses, and welcoming neighborhoods.

A Message from Mayor Angie

Welcome to the official website of Michigan City, where progress meets possibility!

Whether you’re a resident, business owner, or visitor, this site connects you to city services, events, public records, and local resources.

As our community continues to grow, we are embracing change while preserving the unique character that makes Michigan City special. From our scenic lakefront and historic downtown to vibrant neighborhoods and business districts, we are becoming a destination for families, entrepreneurs, and investors.

We invite you to explore, stay informed, and get involved. Michigan City is moving forward, and we are glad you are part of it!

A GREAT PLACE TO LIVE, WORK, & PLAY

Michigan City, Indiana is a vibrant lakeside community offering the perfect balance of small-town charm and big-city amenities. Nestled along the shores of Lake Michigan, the city boasts scenic natural beauty, including beautiful beaches and the iconic Washington Park. With a thriving local economy and easy access to Chicago, Michigan City is an ideal place to live, work, and play. Residents enjoy a variety of recreational activities, from boating and hiking to shopping and dining in the revitalized downtown district. The city’s strong sense of community, coupled with its rich history and forward-thinking development, makes it a great place to call home.
