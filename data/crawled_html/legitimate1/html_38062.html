Biocyclic Vegan Agriculture

ORGANIC FARMING BASED ON ETHICAL AND SUSTAINABILITY PRINCIPLES

Purely plant-based organic farming

Biocyclic vegan agriculture means purely plant-based organic farming. This form of cultivation excludes all commercial livestock farming and slaughtering of animals and does not use any inputs of animal origin. Discover the principles of biocyclic vegan agriculture, the Biocyclic Vegan Standard and the Biocyclic Vegan Quality Seal as well as the certification system connected to it.

The International Biocyclic Vegan Network

Biocyclic Vegan International is a broad network of associations, private individuals, companies and institutions involved in agriculture, processing, trade and science, as well as organisations whose mission is to promote animal rights and a vegan way of life. Among them are dedicated biocyclic vegan country organisations established to promote the development of biocyclic vegan agriculture in their respective territories. Biocyclic Vegan International is coordinated by the German-based not-for-profit Adolf Hoops Society.
