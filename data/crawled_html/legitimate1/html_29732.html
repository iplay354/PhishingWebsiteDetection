Join Us & Contribute with information

We encourage planters, ex-planters, their families and descendants, historians and enthusiasts to submit any relevant material for inclusion on this site. Images, documents, letters, publications and so on – we welcome anything that serves as an indelible record of roles performed by individuals and organisations. And anything that will embellish and enrich the tale of the History of Ceylon Tea for generations to come.
