How can USA Program serve you?

Academic/Educational Program

Students who wish to live and study in America at the secondary level (private high school), for Intensive English Program (IEP), Certificate courses, or at the university level (Bachelor, Master & Phds) with the F-1 Intl. Student Visa (with spouse & dependents if applicable).

I Can Do It Academy

Offer English Development Program, Middle/High School Program (Dual Diploma), and Career Technical Educational Program

Sports Scholarships Program

Students who wish to apply for sports scholarships to Universities in America with the F-1 Intl. Student Visa.

Cultural/Tourism Program

Participants who wish to tour America with the Tourist Visa (B-1 / B-2) with programs like: PET - Professonal & Educational Tour, City & Study Tour or Love Connection

Personal Service ASSISTANT

Participants desiring customized personal assistance for themselves, for their company, or for their entire family while they are visiting the United States of America.

Business Solutions

Clients who are looking for business opportunities, investments, Real Estate and immigration in the United States of America.
