Why Rocky Mountain Resorts?

Dog-Friendly Accommodations

Don’t leave your furry friend behind and rent one of our pet friendly cabins in Estes Park at Rocky Mountain Resorts. Discover our dog-friendly locations to learn more and find a beautiful place where you can bring the entire family!

Rocky Mountain National Park

Fawn Valley Inn and Lazy R Cottages are only one mile from the entrance to Rocky Mountain National Park. Rocky Mountain National Park offers year-round experiences that make lasting memories. Come and cross something off your bucket list!

Book Direct and Save

Best rate guarantee, less fees, better customer service, excellent lodging options, and a personalized experience. Take the hassle out of making reservations and book direct with us. When you book direct, always enjoy the best rate, GUARANTEED.

Estes Park Cabins, Cottages & Vacation Homes

Enjoy a little piece of heaven in Colorado with a stay at Rocky Mountain Resorts. Our Estes Park lodging options range from cozy cabins and cottages to spacious condos and vacation homes. Relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings of Colorado mountain life with all the convenient amenities of home in one of our affordable vacation rentals.
