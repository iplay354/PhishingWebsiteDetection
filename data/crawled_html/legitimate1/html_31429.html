Welcome to

Hodson Bay Hotel

As winter settles over Irelands Hidden Heartlands, Hodson Bay Hotel becomes a peaceful place to relax and unwind. Set on the quiet shores of Lough Ree just minutes from Athlone, our four star resort offers warm hospitality and easy seasonal comfort. Whether you want a romantic escape, a festive family break or time to recharge with spa treatments and fresh country walks, you will find cosy spaces, crisp winter views and the welcome glow of open fires. Breathe in the lakeside air, explore Athlone's shops and restaurants, then return to the calm and comfort that make a winter stay at Hodson Bay Hotel so special.

A Magnificent

Winter Location

Crisp mornings, golden sunsets and shimmering lake views await at Hodson Bay Hotel. Step outside to stroll the shoreline in the cool winter air, wander through forest trails, or take a leisurely trip into Athlone to explore boutique shops, cafés and castles. The beauty of Ireland’s Hidden Heartlands truly comes alive in winter, making our hotel an ideal base for seasonal adventures or quiet reflection.
Find Out More

Hodson Bay Hotel is a four-star lakeside resort in Athlone, beautifully positioned on the scenic shores of Lough Ree in the heart of Ireland’s Hidden Heartlands. Just five minutes from Athlone town centre, our hotel offers the perfect balance of relaxation, natural beauty and access to top local attractions and outdoor adventures.

Our dedicated Retreat Wing, reserved exclusively for adults, provides a serene escape ideal for romantic breaks and couples’ getaways. Unwind at The Spa at Hodson Bay, one of Ireland’s premier spa resorts, featuring a 20-metre heated indoor pool with panoramic lake views, thermal experiences and indulgent treatments.

Renowned as one of the leading wedding venues in the Midlands, Hodson Bay Hotel offers breathtaking lakeside ceremonies, elegant reception spaces and expert coordination for celebrations of every size. Whether you’re planning an intimate gathering or a grand affair, your perfect day starts here.

Guests can choose from a range of dining experiences, from casual bites to refined cuisine. Our newly opened outdoor restaurant is a guest favourite – perfect for al fresco dining with views across the lake and countryside.

Whether you’re escaping for a spa break, a midweek getaway, a family staycation or a wedding by the water, Hodson Bay Hotel promises genuine hospitality, four-star comfort and unforgettable lakeside moments in the heart of Ireland.
