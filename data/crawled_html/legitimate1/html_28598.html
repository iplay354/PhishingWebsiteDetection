Our Cafe: Behind the Bookstore

Photo credit Robin Gottesman

Nestled on the island of Martha’s Vineyard, off the shores of Massachusetts, Edgartown Books is a beloved independent bookstore and literary haven.

More Than Books

Where readers, leaders, and daydreamers can procure and browse an array of new books as well as thoughtful gifts for every occasion.

Open Year Round

While many businesses on Martha’s Vineyard are seasonal, we are a reliable hub for community, connection, and inspiration. Offering events too!

An Iconic Experience

Drop into our iconic Main Street location in the Historic District of Edgartown, MA for the ultimate old-fashioned bookstore experience. 

Your creative connection to Martha’s Vineyard

Housed in a charming 1880 building on Main Street in the Historic District of Edgartown, the bookstore provides a gathering place for local bookworms, island visitors, and renowned authors alike. We proudly showcase Vineyard-based authors, offering everything from beachy page-turners to captivating mysteries and heartfelt stories.

In the summer, our charming porch transforms into a welcoming gathering place for book lovers, where visitors can meet acclaimed writers, enjoy author signings, and immerse themselves in the unique literary spirit of the island.

Can’t make it to the island? Shop Edgartown Books online

Bring the charm of Edgartown Books to your home through our online bookstore on Bookshop.org. We’d be honored for you to choose Edgartown Books with the “choose a bookstore” button on the bookshop.org home page. Shopping online with us here not only makes it easy to stay connected to the island’s literary spirit from anywhere, but it also directly supports us and other independent bookstores like ours. If you prefer an ebook, you can browse and buy on bookshop.org, too.

If you want to enjoy an audiobook, please consider purchasing on libro.fm. Your purchases will support independent bookstores everywhere. If you select us on these sites, we’ll benefit even more. Thank you!

Please note if you are looking to pick up orders in-store, you must make your purchase in-store. Bookshop.org works as our warehouse and offers a wider selection than our physical shelves can accommodate. If you see a title when browsing bookshop.org that you’d prefer to pick up in our Main Street store, please call ahead to confirm availability. Our number is (508) 627-8463. You can order directly from us in-person or over the phone.
