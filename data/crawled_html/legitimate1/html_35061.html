WELCOME TO DARYL'S GUNS

If we don't have the guns, fishing, or hunting supplies you want, we'll get it. If we can't get it, forget it! The bitterness of poor quality lingers long after the sweetness of a low price is forgotten! We have a wide range of guns, knives, ammo, and more to fit your needs.

We carry most major brands for all of our guns, rifles, ammo, hunting, fishing supplies, and most of our other products as well. If we don't have the gun or ammo that you want, just ask and we will see if we can find it for you.

We have an indoor range, outdoor range, and trap range as well. We focus on education and safety at our gun range. We offer other products as well including scopes, archery supplies, and Aladdin lamp oil & chimneys, more that you won't find at just any gun shop in Iowa.

We are located just west of State Center, Iowa on Highway 30, accessible location from all these communities in Iowa:

  • Marshalltown, IA
  • Waterloo, IA
  • Ames, IA
  • Cedar Falls, IA
  • Des Moines, IA
  • Ankeny, IA
  • Grinnell, IA
  • Boone, IA
  • Jefferson, IA
  • Iowa Falls, IA
  • Newton, IA
  • Fort Dodge, IA

We repair guns. We don't perform miracles. We don't resurrect the dead!
