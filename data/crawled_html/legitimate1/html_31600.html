The last little beach town…

Nestled on California’s scenic Central Coast, halfway between Los Angeles and San Francisco, Cayucos is a hidden gem known as "the last of the California beach towns." Tucked between the Pacific Ocean and rolling hills of untouched ranchland, our town offers a tranquil escape from the fast-paced hustle of city life. Here, the quiet stillness of a small-town setting meets the natural beauty of oak-studded hills and grazing cattle. The Cayucos Chamber of Commerce proudly serves as a cornerstone in the growth and preservation of this unique beachside community. Cayucos is a peaceful haven and a true California original, offering a perfect retreat for those seeking simplicity, serenity, and a connection to nature.
