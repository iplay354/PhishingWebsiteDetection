Why Le Beau Clinic & Spa?

Le Beau Clinic & Spa Brings a unique experience for all our guests. Le Beau has been recognised Globally for the Quality, Experienced team, technology, skincare & Unique Delivery of its services and customer satisfaction.  This attention to detail has resulted in Winning multiple Award in the industry both Internationally and locally since 1997.
 
Facilities
 
With over 900 sqm, we have our own secure private car park.  Inside there are a lot of spaces to relax in resort style surroundings. Including 3 relaxation Areas :- Indoor Relaxation Lounge with fireplace and dinning area , alfresco water garden and alfresco health lounge includes spa and infra-red Sauna.  10 quiet treatment rooms, individually customised to the treatments, a nail lounge, change rooms with lockers and Skincare dispensary.

Our Service Goes Beyond The Front Door.
 
 
The Clinic
 
Delivering only the Best medical skin services in a luxurious Spa environment.  We believe feeding the soul is just as important as feeding the skin and body.
When you Step into Le Beau you enter a sophisticated enclave of healing, dedicated to achieving the best possible results for your individual skin and body needs, while not forgetting the importance of sensations that renew your spirit, refresh your mind and amplify your natural beauty.   Le Beau have a unique approach,  Delivering the very best and latest treatments, using medical grade equipment and clinic skincare ranges; all TGA approved; while holding onto tradition and delivering all our treatments with care and individual needs attended too. 
 
We believe  “You Time” to breath, away from reality, brings out the best in us.  The Goal is to take care of the whole person - the Mind, Body, & Spirit, which is important in today’s world; the idea of giving a person permission to have some time - quality time for themselves, with no guilt attached.
 
Every aspect of a person’s wellness experience and their journey to great skin and body is considered. Le Beau use a team of professionals to ensure the best possible results are achieved.  The team is made up of an amazing list of professional’s to ensure the best possible results. The team includes; Dermal therapist, Massage therapist, Clinical Beauty therapists, Nurse, Naturopath and Vascular Surgeon. With such a highly educated and skilled team, Le Beau are able to deliver individual programs designed to the client/patient’s needs. 
  
In keeping with Le Beau’s innovative approach, they offer an exclusive service to first time clients and clients on programs.  A complimentary SkinCoach® Session.  Where the SkinCoach® establishes the concerns, desired results and an individual program is designed.
