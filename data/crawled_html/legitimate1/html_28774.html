    Our Story Began With

    In 1960, during an Italian holiday, America’s style icon of the time discovered a flat sandal with whipstitched leather. Upon returning to Palm Beach, she brought her newfound shoes to a local cobbler to create additional pairs in various colors. This marked the beginning of the classic Jack Rogers sandal, a style that would become synonymous with timeless elegance.
