A world away,

yet right at home.

Welcome to the wild beauty of Tofino’s ocean edge in Cox Bay, where every visit is unforgettable.

Tofino,

just for you.

Whether you’re here to unwind in quiet comfort, savour coastal flavours, dive into outdoor adventure or indulge in spa-day luxury, you’ll find it here. However you imagine your Tofino escape, our beachfront setting and thoughtful hospitality make it yours.

Book Your Stay

Choose from a range of oceanfront rooms, spacious suites and cottages surrounded by greenery—all designed to bring you closer to the natural beauty of Tofino.

Stay

Wake up to ocean views

or retreat to a rainforest cottage.

Feast fireside

or savour by the shore.

Indulge in gourmet fare with stunning views and a cozy fireplace in the Great Room, or opt for a seasonal meal on our beachfront patio. For something more laidback, the Surf Club offers casual bites.

Dine

At the Surf Club, take a surf lesson, enjoy a game of pool or unwind in the hot pool and cedar sauna—ideal for relaxing after a day of adventure.
