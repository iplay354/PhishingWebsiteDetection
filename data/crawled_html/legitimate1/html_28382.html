Quilting Fabric, Patterns, Kits & More!

At Fat Quarter Shop, we're with you every stitch of the way. Along with being an established online quilting fabric shop, we also provide the tools you need to make every project a success. Whether you need fat quarters for quilting, cross stitch patterns, notions, or quilting kits, we have everything you need. We cater to experienced quilters and beginning stitchers alike — providing quilting fabric is just the beginning. Of all the online fabric stores out there, we'd like to be the one you turn to for inspiration, helpful tips and good advice. Although our fabric store is online, our customer service team is just a click away. Ready to check out our selection of quilt fabric? Check out our newest fabrics & have fun shopping!

Inspiration

    The Fabric Store That Caters To Quilters

    Every quilter approaches a new project a little differently. That's why we've arranged our online fabric store so you can shop the way that works best for you. When shopping for quilting fabric, you can sort by fabric designer, collection, manufacturer, theme or fabric color. Whether you have an idea when you come here or you're looking for inspiration, Fat Quarter Shop can help guide you to your next amazing project with our incredible selection of quilting fabric.

    Buying Quilt Fabric Online Is Only The Beginning

    Fat Quarter Shop is proud of the community we've built. Quilters come to us for our wide selection of quilting fabric and stay for the quilt patterns, helpful videos and blog articles. We're confident that you'll find the makings for the next breathtaking quilt you've yet to create within our extensive selection of quilt fabric online.

    Growing Our Fabric Store One Yard at a Time

    Avid quilter, Kimberly Jolly, started Fat Quarter Shop as a side business in 2003. Little did she suspect that her in-home, weekend-and-evening job would bloom and grow into the online fabric store it is today. These days, with husband Kevin by her side, Kimberly and a growing team of dedicated workers continue to deliver top-notch quilting fabric online and provide outstanding service for every customer. As we branch out with an ever-expanding fabric selection, exclusive kits, quilt clubs, BOMs, cross stitch and more, Kimberly's passion continues to fuel the business and provide customers with plenty of reasons to come back to Fat Quarter Shop time and time again!
