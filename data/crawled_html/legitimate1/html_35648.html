DEMOCRATIC WOMEN

She Votes, She Leads, She Changes the World.

The National Federation of Democratic Women (NFDW) is the official women’s organization of the Democratic Party, recognized by the Democratic National Committee since 1972. With members across the country, the Federation works to engage, educate, and empower Democratic women to take active roles in politics and public service. Its mission is to encourage women’s participation at every level of the political process—from grassroots organizing to serving in elected office—while advocating for policies that advance equality, justice, and opportunity.

Through its national network of state federations and local clubs, the NFDW provides training, resources, and a strong support system for women leaders. Our organization also hosts regional meetings and National Conventions, where members can connect, share strategies, and hear directly from Democratic leaders. By building a powerful coalition of women dedicated to Democratic values, the NFDW continues to be a driving force in shaping the future of the party and amplifying women’s voices in American politics.

NFDW Holiday Recipe Book

The NFDW Recipe Book is a celebration of the diverse traditions, cultures, and stories that unite Democratic women across the country. Filled with cherished family recipes, holiday favorites, and personal specialties, this collection reflects the warmth, creativity, and community at the heart of the National Federation of Democratic Women. More than a cookbook, it is a tribute to connection—bringing members together through food, shared memories, and a commitment to building a stronger future.

Communicating Our Values

  • Our voice is the most powerful resource of our organization, and effective communication is what gives it strength and reach. When we speak consistently, and collectively—sharing our message across communities, online platforms, and in personal conversations—we amplify our impact far beyond our membership. A united chorus of voices, backed by strong grassroots communication, helps us inform voters, counter misinformation, and inspire participation. Together, through open dialogue and coordinated messaging, we can shape public understanding, uplift women’s issues, and ensure that candidates who support women and their rights are heard, supported, and elected.
