In 2025, Santa Barbara's most beloved catering institutions—Pure Joy Catering and Catering Connection—joined the Schaffer family, bringing with them decades of creativity, craftsmanship, and community spirit. Their teams, long known for shaping the region's most memorable celebrations, became an essential part of our next chapter.

This moment of convergence inspired our unified brand: Solera & Co. Named for the solera method—where the old enriches the new and the new reinvigorates the old—Solera & Co. reflects exactly who we are becoming: a company where legacy and innovation work in harmony.

Schaffer was built on fine-dining foundations: the vision of culinary innovators whose experience spans Michelin-starred kitchens, iconic venues, and leadership roles across hospitality. By uniting with Pure Joy and Catering Connection—each rich in legacy, artistry, and heart—we strengthened our commitment to precision, elegance, and warm, genuine service.

With extraordinary talent from Pure Joy, Catering Connection, and Schaffer now under one roof, Solera & Co. is Santa Barbara's premier full-service caterer—bringing passion, craft, and unforgettable hospitality to every celebration.

We are excited for all of our clients, staff, vendors and the Santa Barbara community at large to join us in this next chapter!
