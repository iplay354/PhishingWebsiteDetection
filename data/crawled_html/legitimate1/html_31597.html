Why You Should Color

The several reasons why all of us should color. This just doesn't have to be a kids activity anymore. Coloring is relaxing. Colors and What They Mean

The spectrum of light is how we see color. Colors can provide a wide range of information that can be used in psychology. They can be interpreted and have been shown to affect mood. I remember hearing a story about a college football team that painted the inside of the visitors' locker room pink. The head coach requested pink locker rooms for the opposing teams because he read that pink can have a calming effect on people. For instance, blue symbolizes peace, cleanliness, water, and technology. Color combinations have meaning as well. A purple and yellow color combination can be seen as portraying royalty.
