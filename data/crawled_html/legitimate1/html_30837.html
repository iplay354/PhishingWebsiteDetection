Features

  • Submission

    Select from dozens of built-in fields or customize the submission form with your own fields.

  • Assignment

    Assign reviews manually or match up reviewers and submissions automatically by topic or bids.

  • Bidding

    Allow reviewers to show interest or expertise on submissions to aid with automatic assignments.

  • Review

    Use our default review form or customize the fields including those used for scoring.

  • Acceptance

    Make acceptance decisions individually, or in bulk based on score or recommendation.

  • Summary

    Get an at-a-glance summary of submissions, file uploads, accounts, reviews, and more.

  • Proceedings

    Provide access to a proceedings of accepted submissions with fully customizable submission details.

  • Program

    Schedule sessions and assign accepted submissions to create a web program or print booklet.

  • Multilingual

    Access the public, submitter, and committee member features in multiple languages.
