Good looking, effective dish rack/drainer

This is a good looking, high quality dish rack/strainer. It collects and drains water well. I was doubtful that I should have been buying the model with the draining happening on the long side of the tray, but it was definitely the right decision. (The water drains from the middle of the long side, not from all along the long side. As long as that middle of the long side overhangs into your sink, you don't need to worry if the ends extend past the edges of the sink.)

I got a few water spots in the first few days, but they cleaned up very easily by using a sponge and a little Barkeepers' Friend powdered cleaner. So far, so good!
