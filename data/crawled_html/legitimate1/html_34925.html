About VELUX

VELUX roof windows have been bringing daylight and fresh air into homes around the world for more than 80 years. Our products help create bright, healthy and energy-efficient places in which to live, work, learn and play.

Latest news

Niklas Dyg appointed Executive Vice President for Strategy & Sales Development in the VELUX Group


VELUX has appointed Niklas Dyg as Executive Vice President for Strategy & Sales Development. Niklas joins from an internal position as Senior Vice President for Group Strategy and M&A. With this appointment, Niklas steps into the Executive Group Management (EGM) team of the VELUX Group. 

 

Read the press release

VELUX Group and DOVISTA collaborate with a:gain to launch scalable, more circular product


Insulated glass with minor, non-performance-related variations can be used for facade window systems sold by a:gain, a Danish company developing circular building products. The aim is to optimise the use of resources and materials.

Read the press release

Aliëtte van der Wal-van Dijk steps down as Executive Vice President, Global Marketing at VELUX


Aliëtte van der Wal-van Dijk, Executive Vice President, Global Marketing, has decided to leave the VELUX Group to pursue an opportunity as Chief Commercial Officer in the BDR Thermea Group, a Dutch based developer of smart heating and cooling solutions.  

Read the press release

VELUX joins groundbreaking event for Children’s Living Places in Ukraine


Today, October 14th, the SOS Children’s Villages organisations in Ukraine and Denmark announce Lviv as the first of three locations for the Children’s Living Places in Ukraine. VELUX participates in a groundbreaking ceremony that marks the beginning of construction of family-based care homes that will provide an alternative to regular care institutions in Ukraine, and contribute to the vital rebuilding of the country.  

 

Read the press release

Registration open for Light of Tomorrow by VELUX 2026


The VELUX Group invites students of architecture to participate in Light of Tomorrow by VELUX 2026, formerly known as the International VELUX Award for students of architecture. The 2026 competition and award represent the 12th edition, which until now has collectively received over 6,000 exciting daylight projects from more than 800 schools of architecture around the world. 

Read the press release

New solar park in Spain to more than meet the VELUX Group’s renewable power needs is up and running


The second of two solar parks commissioned from BayWa r.e. and enabled by the VELUX Group to meet its renewable power needs is now fully operational and connected to the power grid in Spain.
