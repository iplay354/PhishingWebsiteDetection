I am Kyra Halland, writer of tales of magical worlds where complicated, honorable heroes and strong, smart, feminine heroines work together to save their world - or their own small corner of it - and each other.

"This book had me from page one. It has fantasy, mystery, and western with a great romance plot throughout. The intrigue kept me turning the page, and the well-developed characters made me care about what would happen next." - Amazon reviewer, Beneath the Canyons

"Lovely writing and a charming love story, this was an enchanting break from typical medieval style fantasy. Looking forward to reading more stories in this series." - Amazon reviewer, Beneath the Canyons

"A fantastic fantasy with superb world building and a dashing hero! ... I loved following him and Fransisia on their adventures. I appreciate this author’s style and I’m eager to read more of her work in the future. Highly recommend!!" - Amazon reviewer, Source-Breaker

New Release!
The Hunter's Fall, Book 2 of Children of the Wildings, is now available at an ebook store near you!

Blake heads into the forbidding, unmapped Dragonbone Mountains; Comfort struggles with the choice she must make between the man she loves and the father she's been searching for for years; Garis, Vera, and Kessie join the fight against the dragons and their master. Plus Silas and Lainie. And dragons!

A new series in the Wildings world, featuring the next generation of the Vendine family, filled with magic, adventure, and romance. Children of the Wildings comes after Daughter of the Wildings and Defenders of the Wildings. It is a self-contained story, but is best enjoyed after reading Daughter and Defenders.
