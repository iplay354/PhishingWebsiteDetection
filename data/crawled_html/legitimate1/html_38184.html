  1. Family Fun Day

    Join the Maitland Police Department for one of the biggest dates on the city's calendar. Family Fun Day from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. Saturday, Jan. 31, in Independence Square will include inflatable bounce houses and slides, games and more. Read on...

  2. Movies in the Park

    Maitland Movies in the Park will feature the music hit "Wicked" at 6:30 p.m. Saturday, Jan. 17, in Independence Square, 1776 Independence Lane. Bring chairs and blankets. Come early for movie trivia. Admission and popcorn are free. Read on...

  3. Getdown Downtown

    Orlando party band Blue Stone Circle will head up Maitland's Getdown Downtown street party from 6 to 9 p.m. Friday, Jan. 2, on Independence Lane. Stroll Independence Lane during the concert to enjoy vendors, food trucks and kids entertainment. Read on...

  4. Christmas Holiday

    Maitland City Hall and city administrative offices will close Thursday, Dec. 25, for the Christmas holiday. Offices will reopen at 7 a.m. Monday, Dec. 29. Residential yard waste and recycling will not be collected Thursday. Read on...

  5. Volunteer on a Board

    The City of Maitland is accepting applications to serve on various public advisory boards including Planning and Zoning, Parks and Recreation, Transportation, Lakes and the Police and Firefighters Pension Fund. Read on...

  6. Farmer's Market

    The Maitland Farmer's Market is from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. every Sunday on Independence Lane next to City Hall. The market features a unique selection of vendors with food, crafts, local products, plants, flowers and produce. Read on...
