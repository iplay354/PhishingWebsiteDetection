MILKEN ARCHIVE RECORDING

Osvaldo Golijov's Rocketekya

Commissioned for the twentieth anniversary of Merkin Concert Hall, Osvaldo Golijov's Rocketekya symbolizes an ancient sound (the shofar) being propelled into the future by a rocket. This recording features two of modern klezmer's most prominent and influential practitioners.

Read More
SHORT DOCUMENTARY

Ernst Toch's Cantata of the Bitter Herbs

Short documentary feature on Ernst Toch's Cantata of the Bitter Herbs, the composer's adaptation of the Passover Haggada oriented toward addressing universal experiences of oppression and longings for freedom.

Watch the Documentary
ORAL HISTORY

In Her Own Words: Mascha Benya

1995 interview with Mascha Benya focuses on her training as an opera singer, her immigration to the U.S., her involvement in the Jewish-American radio programs, and her relationships with other luminaries of her time.
