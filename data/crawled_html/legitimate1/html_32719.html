Home Remodeling & Design Services in Maryland, Virginia & Washington, DC

Signature Kitchens Additions & Baths is your premier design-build firm and full-service design remodeling company in Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, DC. We specialize in bringing your home remodeling dreams to life by providing comprehensive design and construction services.

As a design-build firm, we’re there from the initial design concept to project completion, providing support and guidance every step of the way. Our experts will work with you to create a personalized plan that meets your unique needs and budget, ensuring your project is completed to your satisfaction.

With our full-service approach, we take care of everything from the initial design to the final touches, providing a stress-free remodeling experience. Let us transform your home into a space that reflects your style and personality.

Bathroom Remodeling & Design

The bathroom is one of your most frequently used rooms, so it’s important to have a stylish and functional space. Our Signature Kitchens Additions & Baths team takes your needs seriously and will work tirelessly to create the bathroom of your dreams.

Whether you want to update your bathroom or completely remodel your primary bath, our team is here to provide expert design services. We’ll show you how we can transform your bathroom into the most comfortable and relaxing room in your home.

Kitchen Remodeling & Design

As the heart of your home, it’s important to work with a kitchen designer who understands your vision and goals for the space. At Signature Kitchens Additions & Baths, we offer kitchen design and remodeling for Maryland and Virginia homeowners.

When you work with our Rockville team, we help you create your ideal kitchen with the perfect balance of form and function. We make sure the cabinets, flooring, lighting, and other elements come together seamlessly to create your dream kitchen.

Signature Kitchens Additions & Baths is proud to help you choose features for your kitchen, such as:
