TESTIMONIAL

I wish all my purchases were of this quality.

I purchased a combination Masterlock in September, 1960, for use in middle school gym. Since then, it served me through high school and college, was the lock on my bike chain, including a 3800 mile ride in 1976, for years, and secures my locker at our fitness center. Most recently, it protected a spare tire on our van (photo) during an 8300-mile trip to Alaska. After 64+ years of service with exposure to the elements it still works flawlessly. I wish all my purchases were of this quality.
