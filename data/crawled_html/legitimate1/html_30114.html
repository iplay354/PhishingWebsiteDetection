Home

The Southeastern Regional Transit Authority (SRTA) serves 10 communities in Southeastern Massachusetts, including Acushnet, Dartmouth, Fairhaven, Fall River, Freetown, Mattapoisett, New Bedford, Somerset, Swansea,  and Westport.

Important Notices:
In the event of inclement weather please tune into one of the following stations or visit our Facebook page (www.Facebook.com/srtabus) for bus schedule changes, updates, or cancellations:

Radio: 1420 WBSM (New Bedford) 1480 WSAR (Fall River)
Television: WJAR Channel 10 (Providence)
