Meet Heather

Hi! I’m Heather, the creator of Fit Mama Real Food, cookbook author, nutrition coach, group fitness instructor, wife, and mom of four. I started this site in 2008 as a way to share my journey to living a healthy lifestyle. Now, you’ll find hundreds of healthy, kid-friendly, high protein, low sugar, and colorful recipes to help you with your own healthy lifestyle journey. I’m so glad you are here!
