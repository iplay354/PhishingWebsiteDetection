A Mission to Build India as Cyber Security Capital by 2035

The National Cyber Security Standards (NCSS) represent the $185.7 billion cybersecurity market, addressing the critical demand for skilled professionals and research infrastructure.
India requires around 3 million cybersecurity professionals by 2030.
The National Cyber Security Standards (NCSS) aims to establish 500+ National Cyber Security Research & Development Centres in India by 2027.
Presently, 78% of the corporate sector reports a staffing shortage in cybersecurity.
