Who we are

PQQ Bid & Tender Consultants UK

Executive Compass provides bid and tender writing services, including bid management and bid review, for companies across the UK.

Our bid writers and tender consultants have achieved unparalleled success assisting owner-managed businesses, SMEs and large multi-national companies to win public and private sector contracts via our efficient, professional and quality-assured bid and tender writing services.

Since 2009, we have worked on over 7,000 tender submissions across all industries, ranging from small, local contracts to large UK-wide projects, and we are trusted by hundreds of clients each year. No matter what your industry, our expert bid writers can work with you to win more tenders by providing our expert bid and tender writing services to your organisation. Read our case studies and testimonials to gain an insight into how we can help you with your next tender submission.

Our team of bid writers helped companies secure over £5 billion worth of public and private sector contracts. By working in partnership with you through our bid and tender writing services, we can help you to create winning bids. Call us now for free on 0800 612 5563 for an informal chat with a member of our team to see how you can benefit from our range of services, including bid and tender writing services, pre-bid consultancy services and interim bid support.
