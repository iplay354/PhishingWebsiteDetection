ISHA is Member Funded

ISHA is supported by the generosity of our donors and sponsors. Your tax deductible donations support the research and publication of Skiing History magazine, our website, and archives of our beloved sport. Your gifts enable our annual awards program recognizing the creators of the “Pulitzer Prizes of skiing” each year at our annual Skiing History Week.
