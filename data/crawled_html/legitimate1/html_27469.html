Find a Club

Joining an Orienteering Club is a great opportunity to learn from more experienced orienteers, to take part in club activities and to meet other orienteers. There are 19 Orienteering clubs affiliated to Orienteering Ireland

Ready to Go Training

This one-day course targets teachers, youth leaders, or those introducing orienteering in schools.
It aims to equip participants with knowledge, understanding, and practical skills to teach orienteering effectively and enjoyably.
