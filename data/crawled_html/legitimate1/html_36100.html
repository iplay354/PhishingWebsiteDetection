SURPRISING SPACES

 
 
 
 

Specializing in interior architecture tours since 2014, we created the “interior architecture tour” concept in Chicago’s tourism industry with our popular “The World Within: Dazzling Interiors of the Loop” tour, and we continue to offer Chicago’s best interior architectural tours. Copycats are out there — accept no imitations!

All of our tours feature interior architecture exploration — it’s why we’re called Inside Chicago Walking Tours! We believe the city’s story isn’t told best from a boat or bus — it’s inside its walls, under its sidewalks, and often hidden in plain sight. Our award-winning interior architecture tours are designed to uncover the beauty, history, and secrets behind the closed doors of Chicago’s world-renowned architecture, from the titans of its iconic skyline to the secret hidden details missed by tourists and locals alike.

Why Choose Inside Chicago Walking Tours?

  • We created the first and best interior architecture tours in Chicago: Though newer companies unfortunately try to copy our award-winning tours, they can never come close to the years of experience and research that goes into making every one of our highly unique and entertaining tour routes.

  • Expert-Led Exploration: Our guides are not just knowledgeable—they’re passionate, local storytellers with theater and teaching backgrounds who are highly skilled at making the city’s history and architecture come alive.

  • Hidden Gems & Modern Marvels: Explore the secret architectural interiors of Chicago’s most famous landmarks and discover even lesser-known treasures with fascinating stories that even locals don’t know.

  • Engaging & Memorable: Our tours are immersive, informative, and tailored to leave you with a deeper appreciation for Chicago’s fascinating history, stunning architecture, and worldwide cultural legacy.

  • Perfect for Everyone: Whether you’re a first-time visitor, a local, or an architecture enthusiast, we offer tours that captivate and inspire.

Come discover the soul of Chicago inside its gorgeous buildings! Inside Chicago Walking Tours doesn’t just show you the sights—we take you inside Chicago’s architectural wonders to experience the city in a way you’ll never forget.
