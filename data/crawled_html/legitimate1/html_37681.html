Empowering researchers to explore bold ideas and unlock new knowledge.

TopLine designs and manufactures advanced components that help engineers push the limits of performance. Our solutions simplify experimentation, extend reliability, and enable next-generation systems: • Daisy Chain test devices, zero-ohm PCB Jumpers and evaluation kits accelerate development. • CCGA Column Grid Arrays reduce strain from extreme thermal CTE mismatch, ensuring reliability in demanding environments. • Solder Columns for large BGA modules support breakthroughs in quantum and high-performance architectures. • Bonding wire provides secure interconnections to build durable, reliable circuits. Every product is engineered to improve durability, enable innovation, and give researchers the tools they need to explore what’s next.

TopLine empowers engineers and researchers in defense, aerospace and micro-electronics to solve complex problems, pursue bold ideas, and unlock new knowledge. Our mission is to deliver solutions that drive progress - advancing the frontiers of what's possible. We thrive in a world of global interdependencies, energized each day by the opportunity to strengthen innovation, support our partners, and improve the future we share.
