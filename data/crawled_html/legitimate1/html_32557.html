These are some of our most popular features ...

Time tracking

Time tracking - Your way

Kala offers flexible, intuitive time tracking that fits your team’s workflow - stopwatch, manual input, or hour selection. Customize rounding (to the minute, quarter-hour, etc.) to match your policies, ensuring accurate data for payroll and invoicing.
Read more about Time tracking
Project management

Complete overview of every project

Kala’s project management tools help structure work with clear tasks - each with titles, descriptions, estimates, deadlines, and assignees. Track progress in real time with task statuses or move tasks in a Kanban view like a scrum board. This boosts collaboration, saves time, and gives full transparency.
Read more about Project management
Planning

Make sure everyone knows their tasks

Kala’s Planning feature offers a visual Gantt-style timeline for easy scheduling. Assign tasks, set appointments, and view availability, vacations, and active projects at a glance. Linked to cases and tasks, every booking has context. Drag-and-drop and recurring appointments make adjustments fast, ensuring clarity and avoiding conflicts.
Read more about Planning
Quality assurance

Raise the bar with quality assurance

Kala’s quality assurance tools ensure standards are met by prompting documentation - photos, notes, etc. - Before tasks are completed. Use templates to standardize checks for recurring tasks. All data is saved in project history and can be included in reports, ensuring accountability, compliance, and customer satisfaction.
Read more about Quality assurance
