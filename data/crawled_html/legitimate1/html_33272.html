Welcome to the capstone network site.

The website is a resource on capstone curriculum across the disciplines, developed as part of a National Senior Teaching Fellowship funded by the Office for Learning and Teaching (OLT).

Professor Trevor Cullen’s Fellowship, Capstone units for journalism programs to facilitate the demonstration of graduate capabilities, was finalised in 2019. You can find the Fellowship report and information here.

For our analysis on capstone purposes across disciplines: Lee & Loton (2017) Capstone purposes across disciplines, Studies in Higher Education.
