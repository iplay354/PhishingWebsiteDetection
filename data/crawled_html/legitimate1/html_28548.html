The Art of Living Well

LUXURY LIVING BEGINS WITH COMFORT, DISTINCTION AND PERSONAL STYLE

Jennifer Connell believes that in order to be truly successful, you must understand the taste, lifestyle and vision of your client. Her design inquiry is an explorative journey and is the most important stage of her design process. She draws her inspiration from this understanding and reflecting the lifestyle and vision of her clients.

JC SIGNATURE STYLE

After a thorough understanding of the goals for each project, Jennifer brings her interiors to life utilizing her signature style of what she has identified as “house candy.” These are unique touches - objects of interest that Connell continually curates to create intriguing layers of texture, color and pattern — a simple

wool carpet lapping at the base of a tree trunk table with an overhead black lacquer ceiling, a lucite headboard backed with bold graphic sea art alongside soft layered linens.

Originality is the distinquishing marker of this bi-coastal interior-design standout. With over 25-years of bespoke residential and commercial projects, among them a classic European castle, a country house with a classic countenance, and a Bohemian eclectic Tribeca apartment. All are enriched with the comfort and solidity that comes from refined products and materials creatively handled.
