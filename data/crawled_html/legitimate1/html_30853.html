State-Of-The-Art Office, Industrial and Lab Space Designed to Advance Your Business

Move your business to our ready-to-occupy office rentals, industrial spaces and bioscience and tech laboratories in southwestern Ontario.

Whether you’re seeking state-of-the-art office suites, cutting-edge wet labs, versatile industrial spaces, collaborative shared areas, or a flexible events venue, Western Research Parks will provide all you need – and more.

Join our success-driven community in flexible, modern and secure facilities, strategically located in London, ON and Sarnia to fuel your growth and success. Base your business near London, Canada’s fastest-growing cities, and be within easy reach of the GTA.

Gain a competitive edge through our business support program and an extensive network of industry leaders and partners locally, regionally, and internationally. Tap into the diverse talent pool of faculty, researchers, and students at nearby Western University.

With flexible rental terms for offices, manufacturing spaces and labs, plus our exclusive support program, Western Research Parks offers any business an exceptional environment with access to Industry Leaders and Like-Minded Innovators.

Established in 1989, the Western Research Parks program now includes three locations on a total of 156 acres: Western Discovery Park, located adjacent to Western University; Western Advanced Manufacturing Park, located alongside Highway 401; and Western Sarnia-Lambton Research Park, located south of Highway 402 in Lambton County.

Why Choose Western Research Parks

As one of Canada’s leading science and technology communities, Western Research Parks has served as a strong link between academics and commerce. Since 1989, our parks have been the catalyst and key contributor for taking innovative ideas from concept to the commercial marketplace.

Start your journey from idea to implementation at one of our three exceptional locations in southwestern Ontario, Canada:

  • Western Discovery Park, adjacent to Western University in London
  • Western Advanced Manufacturing Park,alongside Highway 401 London
  • Western Sarnia-Lambton Research Park, south of Highway 402 in Lambton
