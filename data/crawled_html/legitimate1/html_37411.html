Bespoke Fine Jewelry, Made with Heart and Intention

At Erin Gallagher Jewelry, we don’t just make jewelry—we turn your most meaningful moments into heirloom-quality treasures. Based in Steamboat Springs, Colorado, we specialize in bespoke fine jewelry designed to reflect your personal story, your style, and your milestones.

Whether you're dreaming up a one-of-a-kind engagement ring, customizing a birthstone necklace to honor your children, or redesigning heirloom pieces into something entirely new, every design is created with care, artistry, and precision.

We are a custom jeweler rooted in personal connection. Each piece begins with your story—and ends with a handcrafted symbol of love, memory, or transformation.

What We Make

  • Designed just for you, with ethically sourced diamonds and gemstones
  • To honor your family, your journey, and those you love
  • Breathing new life into inherited pieces with contemporary elegance
  • For anniversaries, graduations, new babies, and life’s defining chapters

From concept to sketch to final polish, we collaborate closely with you—turning ideas into tangible keepsakes you'll wear for decades to come.

Our Promise

All of our jewelry is handcrafted in the USA using recycled precious metals and ethically sourced gemstones. Each piece is packaged in sustainable, gift-ready materials—because jewelry with meaning deserves a meaningful presentation.

Why Choose Erin Gallagher Jewelry?

  • Bespoke fine jewelry handcrafted in Steamboat Springs, CO
  • Ethically sourced stones and recycled metals
  • Personal design consultations and collaborative creation
  • Sustainable packaging, always
  • Jewelry that carries your story—made to last a lifetime
