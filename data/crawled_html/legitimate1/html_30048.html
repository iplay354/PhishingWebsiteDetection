      WE DON'T USE AI

      All decks and artwork are 100% human-made using traditional and digital art techniques. No AI tools are used in their creation, so you can shop with peace of mind.

      Canadian Owned

      All of our prints are made to order in Canada. Tarot and Oracle decks are designed in Canada and shipped from Canada, but are printed using a trusted production partner in China.

      Small Batch Printing

      Our Tarot and Oracle decks are printed in small batches, wrapped in as little plastic as possible. Each of our art prints are made to order to help combat waste.
