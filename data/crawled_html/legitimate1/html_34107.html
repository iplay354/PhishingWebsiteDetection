About Us

We are one of the largest international schools in Singapore

The Overseas Family School has been providing a modern education in the English language for overseas families living in Singapore since 1991.

OFS curriculum is the International Early Years Curriculum (IEYC) Pre-K1 (2 years old) to K2 (5 years old), the International Primary Curriculum (IPC) Grades 1 to 5 and International Baccalaureate (IB) Grades 6 to 12.

HS Internationalism Celebrations: CultureFest
11 December 2024

CultureFest is a vibrant celebration of the diverse values and traditions that make up the OFS community. Students, families, and staff come together to share food, music, dance, art, and customs from around the world through student-led performances and activities. The event reflects our school's commitment to maintaining a happy, safe, and inclusive environment where cultural identity is honoured and appreciated. More than just a showcase, CultureFest is a joyful reminder of what makes us unique as well as what brings us together, fostering deeper connections and a greater sense of belonging for all.
