OUR MISSION

The Blue Rose Foundation gives kids in financial need what they need most…preschool.

The Blue Rose Foundation strives to positively affect the future of economically disadvantaged children, their families, and society by raising funds to provide scholarship grants for children to attend a quality, accredited preschool and by bringing awareness to the fundamental need for all children to attend preschool.

Schools

Laurel Hill Nursery School

Laurel Hill Nursery School is a parent cooperative nursery school that provides a wonderful learning experience through play and connection in the heart of San Francisco. Children get a chance to engage in deep play allowing them to explore, manipulate, act out, improvise and learn about their environment through trial and error. By allowing each child the time and environment to discover and develop at their own pace, LHNS creates a love of learning that will continue through life.

Mustard Seed School at Loaves and Fishes

Mustard Seed School is an emergency school for unhoused children in Sacramento, CA. MSS provides a safe, structured and nurturing environment while the families of these children seek stable and permanent living situations.  Children receive healthy meals, new backpacks with school supplies, clothes, survival supplies, counseling, health screening, and access to routine and or urgent health care.
