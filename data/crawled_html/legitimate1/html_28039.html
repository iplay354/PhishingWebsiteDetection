About Kellerberrin
Celebrating its centenary in 2008, Kellerberrin’s longevity is attributed to the passion and commitment of its community, the beauty of its natural attractions and the fostering of new forms of artistic expression. Kellerberrin is a town where life is as rich as the landscape.
