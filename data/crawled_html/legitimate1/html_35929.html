FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What if I have bad credit or no credit at all?

You can still be approved for a payday loan even with bad credit or no credit at all. You must be employed and earn a regular pay check of at least $600 every two weeks in order to qualify for a payday loan.

2. How much money can I borrow?

First-time customers may borrow up to $300 while return customers may borrow up to $1500. The amount is based on your take-home pay. Loan limits may increase depending on your repayment history with us.

3. What types of fees are involved?

There are no extra fees or hidden fees anywhere. Our fees are very competitive and in fact are lower than many other online payday loan providers.

4. Can I get an extension or rollover my payday loan?

Eazy Cash Payday Loans does not offer extensions or rollovers. However, our customers are eligible to request another loan once they have repaid their original loan in full. Only one payday loan can be outstanding at any given time.
