WORLD CREATOR

Realtime Terrain and Landscape Generator

For Games, Movies, and CGI / VFX

This is World Creator

The most advanced real-time landscape authoring tool with a procedural and by design workflow. Universal compatible, stable and reliable.

Whether you're crafting environments for games, films, or visualizations, World Creator empowers you to create at an entirely new level of scale, detail, and realism in real-time.

Everything is Real-Time

World Creator puts the full power of real-time terrain generation in your hands. With a GPU engine up to 5000x faster than traditional CPU workflows, you can create massive, stunning landscapes in an instant. This real-time performance not only lets you sculpt, modify, and perfect terrains on the fly, but also unlocks unparalleled creative freedom — experiment with countless ideas, try multiple variations, and achieve high-resolution landscapes in a fraction of the time. Design faster, explore more, and bring your visions to life like never before.

Create Worlds That Stretch Beyond Imagination

With the fully revamped 2025.1 biome system, World Creator lets you create, store, and reuse complex biomes across any terrain or project. Sculpt mountains, volcanoes, craters, deserts, and more with total control, import real-world data, or instantly shape your environment with stamping tools. Add rivers, lakes, and seas, and fine-tune every detail with advanced editing tools and Wacom tablet support. Build vast, living landscapes where ecosystems blend seamlessly, and bring your imaginative worlds to life with breathtaking realism.

Bring Your Worlds to Life

World Creator can fill massive terrains with millions of 3D assets instantly. Start right away with included starter assets, or bring in your own models from Megascans, CGTrader, and all major 3D formats. Populate forests, rivers, and rocky cliffs procedurally for speed and scale, while placing villages, landmarks, or artistic details by hand with full precision. World Creator blends both workflows seamlessly, allowing you to create expansive, vibrant, and fully immersive landscapes — all rendered in real-time, with cinematic high-poly models included.

Start with a Full Asset Arsenal

Gain instant access to over 140 high-quality material scans and a complete 3D biome asset pack with the Professional, Studio and Enterprise editions, giving you everything you need to start building epic landscapes and immersive worlds right away. All assets are fully royalty-free, ready for commercial use in game development, film production, or any visualization project.

With regular updates, this ever-growing library of 2D and 3D assets ensures that your creative toolbox only gets bigger, empowering you to craft stunning, professional-grade environments faster than ever before.

Procedural Power Meets Full Creative Control

World Creator seamlessly combines the best of both worlds: advanced procedural generation and precise, hand-crafted design. Shape your landscapes with over 70 powerful filters, 50+ distributions, and 15+ effect systems that can transform your terrain into anything you can imagine. Sculpt every detail by hand, use stamping, path, and polygon tools, or leverage prebuilt landscape shapes and real-world data streaming to build terrains with ultimate accuracy.

Take your terrains to the next level with World Creator’s incredibly powerful material system. Colorize your landscapes with simple base colors, intricate gradients, PBR textures, or Adobe Substance materials, Quixel materials and Game Textures materials, achieving stunning realism and artistic control. Whether for cutting-edge games or cinematic productions, every terrain can be rendered with unparalleled visual fidelity, perfectly matching your creative vision.

Realistic Physics Simulations

World Creator delivers ultra-realistic, physically accurate simulations for any kind of fluid (water, lava), sand, debris, and snow — all in real-time. Watch as layers of sand accumulate, rivers carve their paths, snow blankets your landscapes, and debris flows naturally according to geological formations. Every simulation follows the laws of physics, allowing complex interactions like overlapping sediment layers that influence water and rock behavior. With World Creator, your terrains don’t just look real — they behave like real worlds.

Photorealism

World Creator’s viewport delivers breathtaking, photorealistic rendering while you work. Reflections, shadows, depth of field, bloom, clouds, water, atmospheric haze, and God Rays — all rendered in real-time with stunning fidelity. Fine-tune your scenes with advanced image controls, including LUTs and post-processing effects, for complete visual precision. Special render modes allow you to analyze terrain shapes and colors with ease, giving you full control over every detail. Design, sculpt, and perfect your landscapes with the confidence of seeing exactly how they will look — in real-time, at cinematic quality. Our upcoming hardware accelerated and updated ray-tracing renderer is in the works already.

Take it Anywhere

Everything you create in World Creator can be effortlessly transferred to your favorite applications — from Photoshop, Cinema 4D, and Blender to Houdini, Unity, and Unreal Engine. With our powerful Bridge Tools, synchronize your entire project with a single click, speeding up workflows like never before. Prefer the traditional route? Export heightmaps, splatmaps, occlusion maps, masks, splines, and more with full control. Whether for real-time engines or cinematic pipelines, World Creator makes moving your terrains fast, precise, and effortless — giving you more time to create and innovate.

With just one click, transfer your entire landscape — even high-resolution terrains — from World Creator to Blender, Godot, Unreal, Unity, Cinema 4D and Houdini, in milliseconds. The World Creator Bridge Tools are available to every customer, making your workflow seamless and lightning-fast.

Modding Community

With the latest version, World Creator now officially supports the GTA 5 modding community – including platforms like FiveM. Terrains created in World Creator can be exported seamlessly via our Blender Bridge Tool into Codewalker and brought directly into the GTA 5 game world. This means anyone can design their own custom GTA 5 maps in World Creator and share them instantly with the community. Beyond GTA 5, we are already working on additional integrations, such as Roblox, Fortnite, and Red Dead Redemption 2 – and we plan to support future titles like GTA 6, Chronicals: Medieval, and Battlefield.
