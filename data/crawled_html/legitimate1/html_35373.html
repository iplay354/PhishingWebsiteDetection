DEAN'S WORD ...

I am delighted to welcome you to the Faculty of Engineering, where you will have the opportunity to both learn and explore an engineering career path focused on fostering the sustainable development of our community for the coming generations. The Faculty of Engineering at Heliopolis University for Sustainable Development is more than just an academic institution—it is a hub for innovation, sustainability, and personal growth .We invite you to become part of our community, where your future will be shaped by knowledge, creativity, and innovation.Our mission is to train capable graduates who distinguish themselves through interdisciplinary, solution-oriented approaches, can compete in the labor market and produce innovative scientific research that meets the sustainable development goals and serves the community and environment.We believe in shaping the engineers of tomorrow by combining innovation, sustainability, and practical knowledge; offering contemporary engineering programs that address global challenges and equip students with the engineering tools to make lasting benefits.Our faculty members are dedicated to creating a transformative educational experience for the engineers of the future, and equip them to address environmental, social, and economic challenges.Sustainable engineering practices and solutions are rooted in all aspects of our academic, research and extracurricular activities, thus integrating the goals of sustainable development in all our engineering curricula.
