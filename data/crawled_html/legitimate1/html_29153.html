Seamless Checkout

Maximise conversion with one-click payment

Seamless Checkout

✕

Easily integrate a fully secure payment page into your checkout flow, offering a seamless buying experience for your customers.

Escrow Account

Insolvency-proof guaranteed payments

Failproof your funds

✕

Your funds are always safe in an insolvency-proof escrow account, stored until they’re paid out to you on schedule, no matter what happens.

Insolvency proof: A life-saver when it comes to mitigating financial risk. Funds from transactions always remain in an insolvency-proof escrow account until they are paid out to you.

100% compliance: Fully compliant with all regulatory requirements, which are regularly verified and documented by BaFin, German Bundesbank and auditors.
