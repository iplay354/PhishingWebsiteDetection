hooyoosay is a music recording project, having a variety of loose and unnamed contributors.
Their releases span a wide range of styles:
guitar pop, classic rock, blues, rhythm and blues, light entertainment, middle of the road, indie pop, acoustic pop, synth pop, fun pop, teen pop, vintage pop, quirky pop, rock and roll, cover songs.
 
 

hooyoosay is not a band actually.
It's a project of writing, playing, recording and releasing music.
The contributors have chosen not to be credited, and some may vary with each recording.
They take part merely because they enjoy it.
