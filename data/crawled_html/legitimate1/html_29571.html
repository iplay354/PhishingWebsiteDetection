Welcome to Barnett Fine Art in New Orleans

Patty Barnett a private dealer at Barnett Fine Art in New Orleans, specializes in locating fine art limited edition works on paper, master prints and contemporary original paintings, sculpture, glass-work and other fine art work locally, nationally and worldwide.

Barnett Fine Art  offers superlative fine art with access to all of your favorite artists as well as new and emerging talent. Our group of local, New Orleans and Louisiana, artists is growing as we discover more gifted local artists such as Lee Morais and Joan  Zaslow.   All of our artists are talented and produce exceptional and innovative artwork.

Barnett Fine Art is your source for Pop Artists  James Gill, Alex Katz, Gerald Laing, Roy Lichtenstein and Tom Wesselmann, as well as popular and contemporary artists Erte, Alexandra Nechita, Phillip Bertho and Yuroz, plus Master work by Picasso and Rembrandt.

Our access to Jazz Fest Posters is growing.  If you do not see the poster you want contact us and we will find it.

Examples of  available work can be seen through-out the website. Visit our online gallery to see images of every work on the site.  Here you will see favorites and also be introduced to unfamiliar work.  Click on any image that you want to explore further.

See the “Blog” tab and keep up with art trends, features and our thoughts by reading our blog.

Our advice: buy what you love and buy the best example of an artist’s work that you can afford: a poster, a work on paper, or an original painting!!!
