TorchioArchitects

Torchio Architects' emphasis on personal customer service is the cornerstone of the practice. Every project is crafted as a carefully considered response to our client's wants, needs, site and budget. The results are buildings that are a reflection of the client not the architect. Like our clients, our projects include a wide variety of types and styles and include churches, restaurants, offices, banks, houses, schools, golf courses, apartments, stores, factories, hotels and more. The firm strives to design buildings which create healthy and productive environments in which to work, live and worship.
