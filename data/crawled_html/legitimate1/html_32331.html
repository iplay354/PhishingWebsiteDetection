Photovoltaic energy at TAISM

TAISM now operates two photovoltaic farms on campus, taking advantage of Oman’s strong solar conditions to reduce reliance on fossil fuels. With the newest installation added in 2025, solar power now provides about 8.2 percent of the school’s electricity, supporting our commitment to sustainability and environmental awareness.

Read More about Photovoltaic energy at TAISM
High School Mentors Inspire Middle School Athletes at TAISM

​​High School athletes at TAISM volunteer as coaches for Middle School sports, bringing energy, experience, and mentorship. Grade 11 student Isla shared her experience coaching the Middle School volleyball team, focusing on skill development, mindset, and team community. The Athletics Director notes that seeing older students lead with confidence and character creates a powerful mentorship loop, helping Middle School athletes grow both on and off the court.

Read More about High School Mentors Inspire Middle School Athletes at TAISM
National Day: A Change That Honors the History of Our Nation, by Reem, Grade 11

In 2025, Oman celebrates National Day on November 20 for the first time, marking 281 years since the Al Busaidi dynasty was founded by Sayyid Ahmad bin Said. The change from Sultan Qaboos’s birthday highlights Oman’s history and unification, inspiring a deeper appreciation of the nation’s heritage.

Spacious Facilities

As the most expansive international community school campus in Muscat, TAISM sits on land generously donated by His Majesty Sultan Qaboos in 1998. Here, our students thrive and have space to explore, learn and grow.

Read More about Spacious Facilities
Libraries

 In TAISM’s three libraries, students are able to explore our extensive and carefully-vetted collection of books, periodicals, digital resources and equipment, all carefully curated to support their learning and curiosity. 

Read More about Libraries
The Bosch Center

The Bosch Center for the Performing Arts serves as a showcase of the arts for the school community, and provides an exceptional venue in Oman for approved local and international performances.
