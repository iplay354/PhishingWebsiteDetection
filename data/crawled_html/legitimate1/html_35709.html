CONLIN'S FURNITURE - A California, Minnesota, MONTANA, NORTH DAKOTA, SOUTH DAKOTA, Washington, and WYOMING FURNITURE STORE

Conlin's Furniture is a regional, family-owned furniture retail chain founded in 1937 in Williston, North Dakota. The company now operates stores throughout California, Minnesota, Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota, Washington and Wyoming. Our family of brands includes Conlin's Furniture, The Outlet by Conlin's and The Sleep Center by Conlin's. Our mission is to provide an outstanding customer experience through a family of knowledgeable and caring associates, selection of high quality home furnishings at great values, and endless service to our clients.
