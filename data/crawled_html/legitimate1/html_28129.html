Welcome To Redcastle

Oceanfront Golf & Spa Hotel In Donegal

The 4 Star Redcastle Oceanfront Golf & Spa Hotel in County Donegal is located on the  rugged Inishowen Peninsula, on the shore of Lough Foyle, part of Ireland’s Wild Atlantic Way, that runs from the most northerly point, Malin Head, to Kinsale, County Cork.

An elegantly-finished 4 star resort hotel, overlooking Lough Foyle, Redcastle Golf & Spa Hotel offers a range of onsite leisure facilities, including a swimming pool, gym, Cara Organic Spa, 9 hole parkland golf course, and direct access to Ireland's largest indoor amusement centre, Leisureland.  

Redcastle Hotel and Spa also offers a wide choice of dining options, from award winning locally inspired cuisine at The Edge Restaurant, to more casual options such as The Deck or the Captain’s Bar.

Redcastle Hotel and Spa’s location is perfect for guests who wish to explore the wider local area, taking in the stunning natural landscape of the Wild Atlantic Way, and a range of visitor attractions, including sea safaris and walks with alpacas at Wild Alpaca Way. Redcastle Hotel reception will be happy to help with your enquiries.  

Whether it is a family break, spa escape or a golfing holiday in Ireland that you are searching for, you will find all you need at Redcastle Hotel and Spa. 

The romantic lough-side location and dedicated function suite also make Redcastle Hotel a popular venue for communions, civil partnerships and weddings – a dedicated experienced team are available to help plan the special day.

Stunning location, excellent amenities and a warm welcome combine to make Redcastle Oceanfront Golf & Spa Hotel an ideal destination and venue, where every guest is welcome, whether visiting for the day or a longer stay.
