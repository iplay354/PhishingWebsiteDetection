LOCANDA DEL GALLO

BOUTIQUE HOTEL UMBRIA

Welcome to the Locanda del Gallo, a boutique hotel nestled in the Italian hills between Gubbio and Perugia.

Discover ancient stone walls sculpted by time, cool and welcoming rooms adorned with exotic antique furniture, and manicured lawns dotted with olive trees, lavender, and rosemary. Bask in the sun by the pool, explore the surrounding woods and stream, and enjoy breathtaking sunsets from our veranda. Indulge in candlelit dinners and peaceful nights under a starlit sky. Just beyond the hills lies the heart of Umbria: Gubbio, Perugia, Assisi, and Cortona, each offering a wealth of art, culture, and local craftsmanship. So close, yet a world away.

 







THE ROOMS


Holidays in Umbria

The Locanda del Gallo has 10 rooms all with private bathroom, each equally fresh and silent, each different from one another. The furnishings adorning every room were all hand-picked and brought back one by one from many distant journeys made by Paola and Irish, the owners of the Locanda del Gallo. There is no television, nothing to disturb the peace and tranquillity.

THE GARDEN AND SPA


Spa hotel Umbria

Prominently situated on a ridge which stretches into the centre of the valley, The Locanda del Gallo commands fine views over its 28 hectares of gardens, terraced lawns and woodland. Stroll at leisure through the olive grove, inhale the scent of roses and herbs, or simply curl up with a book under the shade of the old elm tree. The gardens hold many hidden gems and secret corners for you to discover. In one, an ecological chlorine-free swimming pool, encircled by comfortable sun loungers and close by the sybaritic delight of an outdoor Jacuzzi whirlpool spa.

Or descend a few steps and enter the small Moorish style spa, let your tensions slip away in the Turkish bath and then immerse yourself in a cloud of cool, refreshing, mint scented water, before heading for the peace of the salon overlooking the verdant landscape to sip a tisane, or read a book, or revive yourself under the hands of our masseuse.
