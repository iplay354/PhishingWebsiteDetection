DRCC Webchat is now closed

Our webchat support is normally open 10am to 5pm, Monday to Friday, except for public holidays. It is open Tuesdays and Wednesdays 00:00-3:00am. Please note our webchat is closed on Christmas Day, St Stephen's Day, and New Year's Day. Outside of these times, please remember you can talk to one of our support workers on the freephone National Helpline 1800 77 8888, which is open 24/7 all year round for confidential support & information.
