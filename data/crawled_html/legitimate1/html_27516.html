Stefan Bajic (Serbian Cyrillic: Стефан Бајић, romanized: Stefan Bajić; born 11 September 1997) is a professional footballer who plays as a right-back for Serie D Group C club Campodarsego. Born in Italy, he represented both Serbia and Montenegro at youth level.

Club career

[edit]

Bajic made his Serie C debut for Trapani on 1 December 2017 in a game against Catania.[2]

On 3 September 2019, he joined Serie D side Fiorenzuola[3] and he moved to Borgo San Donnino in summer 2021.[4] He later played for Prato[5] and was presented as a Grosseto player in summer 2022.[6]
