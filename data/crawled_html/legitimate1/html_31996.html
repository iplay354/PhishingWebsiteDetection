Denver Personal Injury & Accident Attorney Dan Rosen

I’ve been fighting for accident victims for 30 years, and have settled over $100 million in claims for my clients. Contact me today and learn how I can fight for you.

Have you or a loved one been injured in an accident due to someone else’s negligence? If so, there are numerous medical, personal, and financial issues you’re forced to deal with. It’s traumatic and it’s overwhelming, to say the least. Receiving a fair and just settlement for your injuries shouldn’t be another weight on your shoulders — it should be a welcome relief for you and your family.

I’m Dan Rosen, and I’ve been a personal injury attorney for three decades. I’ve helped thousands of people in Denver and across the State of Colorado overcome their injuries and receive the compensation they deserve. I have a wonderful and committed team who assists me on a daily basis, but I personally handle every single case that comes across my desk.

I’ve earned more than $100 million in settlements for my clients. But it’s never just about money. It’s my honor and privilege to help individuals and families overcome the physical, emotional, and financial hardships that result from an accident or personal injury, and help them live a better life from this point forward.

Contact me today and learn how I can fight for you.
