NJ Agritourism Grant Program

The New Jersey Department of State has launched the New Jersey Agritourism Grant Program, offering up to $100,000 per eligible applicant to support agritourism projects such as farm tours, hayrides, corn mazes, and pick-your-own activities. The application deadline is January 30, 2026 at 5:00 PM.

Click the button below for program details and to download the application. Completed applications and supporting documents must be emailed to NJATP@ag.nj.gov.

County Administrator

Christina Marks - Interim, County Administrator

Whether you are a County resident, a business person or a visitor to our great County, we urge you to take advantage of the many resources Sussex County has to offer. With our rolling hills and beautiful vistas, our farms, lakes, golf courses and charming villages and shopping areas, we're sure you'll find Sussex County is a wonderful place to live, work and raise a family or even just visit.

It is the County's policy to remain open during inclement weather, but should weather conditions warrant closing or opening delays, announcements will be posted here, on Facebook, on Twitter and on iHeartRADIO: 102.3 WSUS, 103.7 WNNJ and 106.3 WMAX. County employees can also call 973-579-0200 for delayed opening or closing messages.
