The UK's original Airstream holidays

We are proud to have been among the very first businesses offering Glamping holidays in the UK and the original Glamping holiday business on the Isle of Wight. We had 18 shiny summers of vintage Airstream holidays. However, the time came for us to close Vintage Vacations and focus on our next adventure – Tiny Homes Holidays.

Thank You!

As the Vintage Vacations story comes to a close we just want to say a big thank you to all who made it possible, all our friends and family for their support (especially our daughter Faber who had to grow up with the craziness) –and most of all to our fabulous guests – you made it worthwhile. It was eighteen shiny summers -the adventure of our lives.

Check out our history page for a run down of 18 years of tin-can-ery!

Helen & Frazer Cunningham
