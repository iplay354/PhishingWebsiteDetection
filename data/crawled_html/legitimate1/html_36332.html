Welcome to AWA Cabinets, COUNTERTOPS, AND FLOORING!

AWA Cabinets, Countertops, and Flooring is one of Salt Lake City, Utah’s premier kitchen designers dedicated to providing high quality cabinets, countertops, and flooring at an affordable price and in a timely manner. Our top priority is our customers. We understand that remodeling or building a new kitchen can be a hectic time, and we strive to make the process as smooth as possible through our services. We take pride in being involved in the design process, working closely with each customer to create a unique and beautiful kitchen that fits their taste and needs.

Explore our website for inspiration on custom-designing your new kitchen or remodel your existing one. We also offer Free in-home estimates.

We serve along the Wasatch Front from Spanish Fork to Ogden and from Park City to Tooele.

AWA Kitchen Cabinets has been in business since 1994. We originated in California and relocated to beautiful Utah in 2005. Give us a call. You’ll be happy you did!

Color matching to any Sherwin-Williams shade is now available!
