Legacy Hub
Explore the iconic soundtracks of film and TV...

Silva Screen Records has long stood as a cornerstone in the world of soundtrack releases. Bringing iconic themes, unforgettable scores and cult classics to audiences worldwide. The Legacy Hub is your gateway to that rich musical archive.

Spanning decades, the hub offers fans, collectors and media professionals a space to discover and rediscover the composers and scores that have defined the cinematic and television soundscape. Serving as both an archive and showcase, dedicated to Silva Screen's extensive catalogue of film and television soundtracks. The Legacy Hub honours the label's historical contribution and current output to screen music.

Doctor Who Music
Over the years Silva Screen’s catalogue of Doctor Who soundtracks has grown considerably in LP and CD releases, from The BBC Radiophonic Workshop, through to the present day Murray Gold scores. In the past the label has even seen to it that all the music fits within a TARDIS!

Explore the Doctor Who Music website to see all of our adventures through space and time...
www.doctorwhomusic.com
BBC Earth Music
From 'The Living Planet' to 'The Blue Planet', from George Fenton to Hans Zimmer... Silva Screen has released soundtracks to some of the most compelling natural history documentaries ever filmed. Scores that accompany Sir David Attenborough’s narration of the beauty, danger, wonder and excitement of the natural world.
