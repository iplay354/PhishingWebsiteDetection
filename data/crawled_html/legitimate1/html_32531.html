SPE/ICoTA Well Intervention Conference and Exhibition

Be at the Forefront of Change in Well Intervention

This conference and exhibition remains the largest, most popular, on point and impactful well intervention specific event there is, bringing together service companies, equipment manufacturers, and operator professionals to learn, exchange knowledge, and network.

Its core agenda is comprised of the SPE accredited technical program and its extensive exhibitor showcase, coupled with its coveted annual awards for outstanding technical paper and best intervention technology. This has been enriched further with the recent addition of an operator roundtable and an operator showcase booth.

What is ICoTA?

The Intervention & Coiled Tubing Association (ICoTA) was established in 1994 as a means of supporting, communicating, and promoting rapid technological advances within the well intervention industry. As a technology association, ICoTA seeks the participation and involvement of manufacturers, suppliers, service providers, and end-users of coiled tubing and associated technologies.

The mission of ICoTA is to enhance communication, gather technical expertise, and promote safety, training, competency, and industry-accepted practices.

The business and activities of ICoTA are generally conducted at two levels; the international level and the regional or Chapter level. The collective skills and perspectives of ICoTA members provide a unique asset and resource for the application of industry initiatives and cooperation with other industry bodies.
