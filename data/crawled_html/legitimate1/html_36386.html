Seeing is believing

LA is our home. Up in the hills or in the heart of downtown — you will have it all.

  • Explore Chalon Campus virtually﻿

    Where it all started. Chalon is the heart of our undergraduate program. Here you’ll find a community ready to help you come into your own.

  • Explore Doheny Campus virtually﻿

    Doheny is situated in Downtown LA and hosts both graduate and undergraduate programs. Take advantage of all the city has to offer, right next door.

  • Take an in-person tour﻿

    Weekday. Weekend. Whatever works for you. Visit the most transformative university and discover the power of a Mount education firsthand.

  • View our campus map﻿

    Explore Chalon and Doheny in your own time, online. Use our map to get familiar with what our two campuses have to offer.
