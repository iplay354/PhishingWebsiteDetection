    Our story

    Founded in 2006, TRAK engineers premium packable sea kayaks for paddlers who demand performance, safety, and durability - without compromise. We are proud to produce the only kayak in the world with real-time adjustable rocker.

    With our expedition grade construction, hard chine skin-on-frame design, epic seven-year warranty, and the best paddling performance of any folding kayak on the market, the TRAK 2.0 is ready to take you anywhere the water calls you.
