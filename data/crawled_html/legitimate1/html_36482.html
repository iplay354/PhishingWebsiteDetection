WUNYA NGULUM – Welcome Everyone to Kabi Kabi Country!

Experience Eumundi acknowledges the ongoing connection to country of the Traditional Owners of this beautiful region, the Kabi Kabi people. Visitors to Eumundi can enhance their experience by learning about the region’s connection to the people and stories of the Kabi Kabi. We pay our respect to Elders past, present, and emerging.
