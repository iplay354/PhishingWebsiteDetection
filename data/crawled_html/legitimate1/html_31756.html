IN PURSUIT OF THE UNIQUE AND EXTRAORDINARY

French Loft is a specialist dealer of antique and vintage furniture. Husband and wife Rowland and Mary, interior designers, collectors and connoisseurs have been sourcing furniture, homewares and artwork from France and Europe for over 10 years. With a love for the rustic and worn, French Loft has a reputation for selecting beautiful 18th, 19th and 20th century items with character and charm. Browse all their items online or if you’d like to see them in person, you can visit their showroom at The Vinery in Arundel, Please book an appointment weekdays and on the weekend for private viewings.
