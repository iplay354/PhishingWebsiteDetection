Who we are

CIBP is the first international organisation that brings together Popular and Cooperative banking players from five continents.

Since 1950 we share insights, take great care of our trusted business network, inspire strategic & cutting edge thinking and conduct researches around the globe.

Learn more about CIBP

Why choose us

74 Years of international learning forum and business networking opportunities among cooperative and popular banks.

  • Stay current
  • Gain industry insights
  • Be part of a global dialogue
  • Learn from the best
  • Support your customers abroad

Collaborative thinking and international learning forum

CIBP is the first international organisation that brings together Popular and Cooperative banking players from five continents.

CIBP is also an international organization recognized by the United Nations, whose mission is to provide cooperative and popular banks an international learning forum and a business network that promotes the development of the cooperative business model.

Members & Testimonials

A cooperative bank reconciles democracy and efficiency at the service of its members and the community.

Carlos Heller President, Banco Credicoop, Argentina

The popular and cooperative banks’ model is an example of success as its values are strong and topical, its contribution to the growth of economies around the world is palpable, and its adaptability and resilience have been demonstrated.

Mohamed Benchaaboun CEO of Banque Populaire du Maroc

A cooperative bank is a place where clients are shareholders and as such take advantage of the added value created by their company!

Alain Declercq President of the Board of Management, CPH Banque - Belgium

The ‘added value’ of popular and cooperative banks is represented by their special relationship with their clients/members. Being banks with people at the centre of their business and linked to the territories where they operate, popular and cooperative banks are able to develop the relations of mutual support between credit, business and civil society: an essential condition for building the economic recovery.

Giovanni De Censi President of Creval, Italy
