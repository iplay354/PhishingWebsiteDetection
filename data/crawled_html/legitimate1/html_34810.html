WE ARE THE KANSAS CITY POOL CONSTRUCTION SPECIALISTS 

Founded in 1973, Swim Things, Inc. is a highly respected swimming pool construction company based in the Kansas City area. Over the years, our company has grown to become the region's largest and most renowned provider of inground pools. Our reputation extends nationally, having earned recognition as one of the top 50 pool builders by Pool and Spa News. 

Our company places a strong emphasis on innovation in custom pool designs, ensuring that each project is uniquely tailored to the client's needs. Customer service is at the forefront of our business, and our experienced team supports clients through every step of the process—from initial concept through installation and ongoing maintenance. 

In addition to pool construction, Swim Things offers a full range of pool-related products both in-store and online. These include pool chemicals, patio furniture, pool toys, and swimsuits, making us a one-stop shop for everything related to residential and commercial pools.

WORKING WITH US 

In the early stages of planning your gunite or vinyl liner inground swimming pool, you will work with members of our pool design team to make critical decisions about the way your swimming pool looks and operates. From design, to pool construction, to completion, our staff is here to help every step of the way. 

Unlike so many other pool builders, our relationship with you is just beginning upon the completion of your pool. Our dedicated and experienced team is here to help take care of your swimming pool investment. By providing pool maintenance, pool service and education — as well as offering high quality, long-lasting pool products, parts and chemicals. We want to ensure the enjoyment of your pool and surroundings.

So jump in and experience our passion for swimming pools!
