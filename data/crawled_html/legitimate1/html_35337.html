How it Works

Each month, receive the best new fiction, paired with carefully selected items from the story that engage each of your 5 senses.

Pick from 3 of the best new books.
With 6-8 items inspired by your pick.

✨ At least 1 item for each of the 5 senses!

Enjoy your items while you read.
1. Sight

Pick from 3 of the best new books each month.

We then curate a book box experience around your book pick to be enjoyed while you read.

2. Smell

Candles custom made to your book pick.

Experience the ambience of the story with hand-poured, soy wax candles, custom made with scents to match your book pick.

3. Taste

Drinks & treats with flavors from the story.

Be transported while you read with drinks & treats chosen specifically with flavors inspired by your book pick.

4. Touch

Self-care goodies with clever connections to your book pick.

Relax as you read with self-care items chosen for how their scents, textures, and ingredients connect to your book pick.

5. Sound

A Spotify playlist custom made to match your book's mood.

Set the mood with an instrumental playlist custom made for your book pick.
