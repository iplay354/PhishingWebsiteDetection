The Angara Social Grilling Table transforms everyday dining into an immersive family experience. In today’s digital age, it brings everyone back together — allowing family members and friends to cook, eat, and connect at the same table. Investing in an Angara means investing in quality time, togetherness, and outdoor living. Life truly is better together.

The Angara stands out for its craftsmanship, innovation, and social design. It’s not just a grill — it’s a statement outdoor dining table built for shared experiences. Watch our video to see why the Angara is redefining outdoor dining and family gatherings.
  • Brings people together: Everyone can grill and dine side by side, making outdoor entertaining more social and fun.
  • Easy to clean: Features dishwasher-safe T316L stainless steel grates, resistant to corrosion and easy to maintain.
  • Better cooking control: Multiple burners let you cook different foods at different heat levels — perfect for varied tastes.
  • Versatile design: With the grill covers on, it becomes a stunning modern outdoor dining table, ideal for patios, decks, and backyards.
