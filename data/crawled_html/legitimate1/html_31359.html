Yellow-eyed Penguin Trust

We are a not-for-profit conservation organisation with a mission to see one of the world’s rarest penguins, the hoiho, thrive in its natural environment.

We are reforesting New Zealand coastlines, protecting reserves where hoiho nest and advocating for this rare and unique bird on the global stage.

Lost at Sea

The last ever hoiho found in Aotearoa are calling us into action for ocean justice.

Each year, we wait for adult penguins to return to shore to breed. But not all of them make it back. We must advocate for better protections of our oceans and marine life.

Support our campaign

Protecting the Hoiho

Our mission is to see hoiho thrive in their natural environment. But currently, hoiho face serious challenges both on the coast and at sea, which has resulted in a rapid population loss. That’s why we provide a system of front-line support for hoiho.

How to help

    The yellow-eyed penguin is a rare and uniquely New Zealand penguin species, known for its solitary nature and dependence on both land and sea habitats. Distinguished by its yellow eyes and a shrill call that inspired its Māori name, this "big diver from the southern lands" faces challenges, making its conservation a vital concern.
