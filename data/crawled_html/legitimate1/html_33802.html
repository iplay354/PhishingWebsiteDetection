Behind the Lens - Sara Seltzer
A Historical Viewpoint

As Metropolitan’s archivist, my job is to ensure our history is preserved for posterity. Over time I’ve come across images, documents, and artifacts that are visually intriguing and rich in meaning.

Sara Seltzer administers Metropolitan’s archival program, which collects, preserves, and protects permanent records of enduring value that document the institutional history of the organization.

Behind the Lens - Jennifer Sharpe

Behind the Lens - Jennifer Sharpe
A Photographer’s Viewpoint

When I first started working with the photo library of External Affairs in the spring of 2004, I came across a large folio size book filled with very dramatically lit, moody black and white photos, all of them with a monumental feel. The book was titled simply: Metropolitan Water District of Southern California.

Jennifer Sharpe provides support to Metropolitan’s External Affairs group and specializes in the preservation and archiving of our vast collection of historical photos. A photographer and artist herself, she recognizes the qualities that make a photograph memorable.

Honoring a Visionary Leader

Honoring A Visionary Leader

In recognition of the men and women who shaped Metropolitan as the agency it is today, the board of directors over the last several years has renamed key facilities after some of its most visionary and impactful former leaders. This October, Metropolitan celebrated the legacy of its former general counsel, N. Gregory Taylor, during a dedication ceremony to rename the Inlet/Outlet Tower at Diamond Valley Lake in his honor.

Workforce Investment___

Leading
the Way.

Supplying 40 percent of California’s water resources requires a workforce that is as agile and efficient as the water delivery systems we develop and operate. Succession planning, diversity, and asset retention are vital areas of focus that are key to the success of our organization.

Strategic Priorities___

Pathway to
the Future.

 

Learn More About our Vision and Values

With a focus on areas of change and opportunity, Metropolitan - in an inclusive process - identified five strategic priorities that form an action-based framework for our approach to transformation. They support long-term stability and are attuned to the challenges of climate change, responsibilities to the public and our workforce, the importance of a sound business plan and continued collaboration with the communities we serve.

EMPOWER

EMPOWER the workforce and promote diversity, equity, and inclusion

SUSTAIN

SUSTAIN Metropolitan’s mission with a strengthened business model

ADAPT

ADAPT to changing climate and water resources

PROTECT

PROTECT public health, the regional economy, and Metropolitan’s assets

PARTNER

PARTNER with interested parties and the communities we serve

Learning to Conserve___

Saving
Everyday.

Supporting Sustainability Through Conservation.

Southern Californians get it. They know our water resources are not limitless. They’ve learned to conserve. And they know they can do more. That is where Metropolitan comes in. We offer water-saving tips, classes, and rebates for water-saving devices and sustainable landscaping. We are your partner as you learn how to make conservation a way of life.
