At Alta-1, we believe that learning is a personal journey. There may be patterns and similarities in the ways we learn, but education is never a one-size-fits-all scenario. We are the antidote to a system that values compliance over character and marginalises those outside the mould.

Alta-1 is a safe space for students and families to try a different approach. At Alta-1 we prioritise student wellbeing and belonging over rankings and results.

Our teachers aren’t just educators, they’re mentors, trained to understand complex needs and foster interpersonal and life skills. Students at Alta-1 are given space outside of a fixed curriculum to explore, question, make mistakes, and grow while having the right educators and wellbeing staff to support them.

At Alta-1, we gain respect by giving it—acknowledging our students’ agency, individuality, and curiosity at every turn.

In the spirit of reconciliation Alta-1 acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of country throughout Australia and their connections to land, sea and community. We pay our respect to their Elders past and present and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples today.
