Welcome to the Langdon Library of Newington, New Hampshire!

Langdon Library has served the town of Newington, New Hampshire for over a century, as the local resource for books and information, but also as an important center of community for the townspeople.  Established in 1713, the population of Newington was 787 at the 2016 census.

Our Mission
The Langdon Public Library will perpetuate Woodbury Langdon’s interest in the intellectual welfare of the town and its people. The Langdon Library is committed to providing resources adequate to serve the varied needs of the patrons of Newington. Our goals are to furnish library patrons of all ages with a variety of informational resources, to assist members of the community in their educational and cultural endeavors, and to preserve the heritage of the library for current and future Newington residents.
