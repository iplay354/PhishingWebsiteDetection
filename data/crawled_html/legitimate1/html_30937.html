Development Pricing

No Hidden Cost | Minimum AMC | We Believe in Transparency and Quality Service

The pricing of a SaaS application is determined by several key factors, including the nature of the business, the complexity of the design, and the overall scope of development. These parameters are typically defined during our initial consultations, which may be conducted via teleconference or email correspondence.

We evaluate project costs in terms of person-months of design and development effort. Upon receiving client requirements, our team drafts a preliminary structural outline of the SaaS application. This framework is then incorporated into a comprehensive development plan, enabling us to establish an accurate pricing model.

A significant portion of the cost is influenced by the design and structure of the home page. Applications with dynamic banners, advanced image effects, multiple functional modules, and interactive forms generally incur higher costs than applications featuring a streamlined home page with standard navigation and static content.

To assist prospective clients, we provide a SaaS Price Calculator that offers an indicative cost estimate based on the specific requirements of the application.
