VolkerFitzpatrick is one of the UK's leading engineering and construction companies

Established over 100 years ago, VolkerFitzpatrick has grown to become one of the UK's leading engineering and construction companies, providing specialist building, rail, civil engineering and industry focused skills to a range of market sectors including commercial, industrial, education, rail, aviation and energy.

Read more

Our expertise

  • Aviation

    Airport infrastructure works are a core part of our business and our teams are experienced in providing the engineering skills and technologies necessary to deliver both airside and landside support facilities.

    Read more

  • Building

    Our building division has a strong track record of delivering demanding, high-quality projects, often to fast-track programmes and in live environments. Alongside our individual projects, we regularly work with our infrastructure divisions to form integrated project teams when a combination of specialist skills are required.

    Read more

  • Defence

    We are recognised for our expertise and reliability in designing, building, refurbishing and maintaining the nation’s vital defence infrastructure in highly restricted and secure environments.

    Read more

  • Energy

    We are helping the UK move towards a zero-carbon and energy-secure future by connecting new offshore wind farms to the national grid and developing new nuclear power and hydrogen/carbon capture utilisation and storage infrastructure.

    Read more

  • Highways

    The UK’s strategic and local road infrastructure is a vital asset for communities and businesses. As a major tier 1 contractor and National Highways framework partner, we play a key role in providing multi-faceted engineering services to keep these vital arteries running, ensuring smoother, safer, less congested journeys for everyone.

    Read more

  • Rail

    VolkerFitzpatrick has highly regarded specialist civil engineering and rail capabilities. We are the leading rail depot builder in the UK and are particularly well known for operating in live rail environments, and for providing single and multi-disciplinary rail infrastructure solutions.

    Read more
