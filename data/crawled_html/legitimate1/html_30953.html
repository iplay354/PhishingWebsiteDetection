The European Association for Jewish Studies (EAJS) is the sole umbrella organisation for Europe representing the academic field of Jewish Studies.

The primary aim of the EAJS is to encourage and support the research and teaching of Jewish studies at university level in Europe, and other places of higher education and learning.
