Maras Group is a dynamic, privately owned and operated South Australian company.  For over 40 years, the group has prided itself on being at the forefront of commercial and retail property investment and development.

The company is broadly considered to be one of the “pace-setters” in the South Australian market with a lengthy and impressive track record of quality and sustainable developments, underpinned by high calibre, long term occupiers.

The company is heavily committed to investment in South Australia and continues to develop with the view to holding its properties for the long term – giving its tenants (partners) confidence in forging strong, healthy relationships going forward.

Maras Group continues to grow its diverse commercial and retail property portfolio nationally, with several investments, particularly in the Melbourne and Sydney markets.
