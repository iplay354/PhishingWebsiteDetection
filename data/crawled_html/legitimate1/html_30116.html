Beware of individuals or illegitimate companies pretending to be Bridge Access, Only respond to the following registered ACCESS channels:

  1. Text message from our support mobile number 0919-912-4782

  3. Received e-mail from @bridgeaccess.life promo messages
  4. Chatbox from dashboard at https://corporate.bridgeaccess.life

It is a scam when they will request the following:

  1. Any form of down payment or collaterals in exchange of loan
  2. Sending of individual bank account details to deposit
  3. Not registered with ACCESS platform
  4. Communication from Facebook or Messenger
