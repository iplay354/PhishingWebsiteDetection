 NJ Wedding Officiant Andrea Purtell
I am a full-time, professional Wedding Officiant
​ certified to legally perform weddings in the state of New Jersey.
​Over the course of the past 13 years I have had the honor to officiate over 1000 weddings. 

I am located at the fabulous Jersey Shore and travel through NJ as well as parts of PA.
I provide religious, non-denominational, spiritual, non-religious, interfaith and multi-cultural personalized wedding officiant services.

I  have a selection of ceremony packages for you to choose from. They range in length and price so there is something for everyone: Packages/Pricing

I have put together some beautiful ceremonies for you to choose from. You may use one of these, or use my unique "Build Your Ceremony Menu"  to create your own ceremony based on your preferences. If you would like, I will work with you to create a more personalized and unique             ceremony.
