“Goodbye June” screencaptures
December 27, 2025

Screencaptures from “Goodbye June”, Kate Winslet’s directorial debut, have been added to the photo gallery. Winslet has amassed a quality cast – including herself, Toni Collette, Helen Mirren, Andrea Riseborough, Johnny Flynn and Timothy Spall – for a heartwearming yet convential story about loss, love and family. Toni Collette, Kate Winslet, Andrea Riseborough premiere “Goodbye June” in London
December 4, 2025

Toni Collette has celebrated “Goodbye June” at the film’s premiere, yesterday in London. Kate Winslet’s directorial debut follows four siblings (Winslet, Collette, Andrea Riseborough, and Johnny Flynn) when their mother’s (Helen Mirren) health takes an unexpected turn just before Christmas, forcing them to confront messy family dynamics in the face of possible loss. “It is hard to talk about loss and grief, whether it’s happened to you or not. It is something that ultimately does happen to us all. What I hope is that it opens up a conversation that people might otherwise struggle with,” Winslet told The Independent. “Goodbye June” is in select UK and US theatres from 12 December and on Netflix from 24 December. Pictures from the premiere have been added to the photo gallery.
