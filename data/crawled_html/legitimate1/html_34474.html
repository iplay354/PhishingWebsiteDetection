About OC

Our Purpose:
Make Lives Better

OrthoCarolina provides compassionate and comprehensive musculoskeletal care including operative and non-operative care, diagnostic imaging, and rehabilitative therapy. Widely known for musculoskeletal research and training, OrthoCarolina physicians have specialized expertise in foot and ankle, hip and knee, shoulder and elbow, spine, sports medicine, hand, pediatric orthopedics, and physical medicine and rehabilitation. Learn More

 

Why OC?

Access

Be seen your way. OrthoCarolina offer walk-in or same-day appointment options at many of our office locations along with extended hours on evenings and weekends.

Value

In addition to our primary clinics, OC multiple ambulatory surgery centers and a world-class research institute to lead the way in orthopedic technology, value and care.

Community

OrthoCarolina also focuses on community health through its foundation, supporting educational programs and initiatives in health, medical advancement, and youth sports.
