YOU'RE IN SPINNINGFIELDS.

Welcome to Manchester’s No.1 destination for leisure, work, culture & living.

SPINNINGFIELDS IS MORE
THAN A DESTINATION

It’s a place where city sophistication meets vibrant energy; an invitation to explore, taste, and connect in the city’s original work and leisure district.

Spinningfields is a place for working, dining, drinking, shopping, living and entertainment with over 45 restaurants, bars, shops and cafes to visit. It’s also the commercial heart of the city with more than 165 businesses calling it home. It’s the city’s largest, most vibrant destination for visitors and residents, creating a modern thriving community.
