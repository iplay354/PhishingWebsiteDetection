Lovella Bridal is a premier, luxury
bridal boutique located in Los Angeles, California.

The bridal collections at Lovella Bridal offer you a stunning array of couture, feminine, and modern chic bridal gowns. Hand selected from leading bridal designers for their elegant line and sophisticated style, we have the season's best collections for you to choose from.

Imagine yourself in a gorgeous bridal gown created from fabulous fabrics and adorned with exquisite embellishments and breathtaking details of pearls, beads and crystals. If you're in love with the sheen of sumptuous satins, soft sexy silks, layers of airy tulle or featherweight organza, our showcase has something special for you.
