Uncompromising, problem-solving flooring

Unnatural Flooring offers the appearance and texture of natural materials like sisal, wool, jute, seagrass, and wood, but is made from durable, easy-to-clean vinyl, polyamide, and polypropylene. This washable flooring is an ideal low-maintenance alternative for busy homes, offices, shops, restaurants, and hotels.

Unnatural Flooring Rugs

Get the benefits of Unnatural Flooring in rug form with a made-to-measure, bespoke rug. Perfect for kitchens, hallways and busy spaces in both domestic and commercial settings, our custom-made rugs offer all the advantages of our market-leading flooring.
