KLUSON MANUFACTURING COMPANY



The original Kluson® Manufacturing Company was founded in 1925 and expanded from a small machine shop by building its reputation as the music industry's premier supplier of tuning machines, tailpieces, brackets, bushings, and other stringed instrument parts during the golden age of guitar manufacturing from the 1940s through the 1960s.

While the Kluson® name will always be synonymous with the early years of guitar production, its future depends on our continued ability to provide the industry's most affordable, quality, parts and hardware. With so many years of guitar history behind us, we are more dedicated to that mission today than ever before.
