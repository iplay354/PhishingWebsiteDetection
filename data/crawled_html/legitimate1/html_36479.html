Curated Architectural Antiques and Building Materials of European Heritage

Architectural antiques in Houston TX featuring European antiques from France, Italy and Belgium. Our architectural antiques include antique stone flooring, wood flooring, colored cement tiles, marble tiles, and terracotta tiles. In addition, our inventory of architectural antiques boasts some of the finest antique doors, gates, shutters, stone surrounds, sinks, and reclaimed cheminees and mantles. We also offer unique and often one-of-a-kind lighting fixtures such as antique lanterns, chandeliers, sconces and candlesticks. We're also your best source for antique mirrors, furniture, garden decor and fountains.

We invite you to visit our Houston showroom to experience the beauty of our product in person.
