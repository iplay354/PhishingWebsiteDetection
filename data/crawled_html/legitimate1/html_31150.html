The Waliki Toys Story

Based in sunny Florida (and shipping from California) hopballs.com's goal is to help parents and teachers keep our kids active, healthy, happy and active, by making exercise fun! Waliki's, story started over 20 years ago, the first time it's founder bounced on Hop Ball at the tender age of 10. Thanks to his grandmother Lala (may she always be strong and healthy!) he was a toy expert and right away knew that he had found something special.

Two decades later we are bringing back this classic toy back to kids (and adults) all over the globe. Except that now the Hop Ball is new and improved, with cool designs, brighter colors, and made only with safe, non-toxic materials.

We also carry fun Bouncy Castles for even bouncier parties, family get-togethers, or for getting essential exercise during the winter months at home in your playroom, basement, or garage. Imagine your child's expression waking up to their very own bouncy castle? For toddlers, we proudly offer Benny the Jumping Bull! Not just for fun, our Benny also helps children develop a good sense of balance and coordination as they learn and grow.
