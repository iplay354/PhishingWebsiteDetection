Your Trusted Partner in Endpoint Data Protection

With over 30 years of experience and more than 1 million users worldwide, Jetico is dedicated to securing your data. We specialize in advanced data wiping and encryption solutions, ensuring complete endpoint protection against cyber threats and data breaches.

Wipe Files

BCWipe enables you to wipe files and folders selectively beyond forensic recovery – including data remanence, free space, file slack, temporary files, shadow copies, system-specific data and more.

Wipe Hard Drives

BCWipe Total WipeOut is a data erasure software designed to wipe entire hard drives – including boot records, file-system structures, operating system files and hidden areas such as the Host Protected Area (HPA) and Device Configuration Overlay (DCO).

Encrypt Files

BestCrypt Container Encryption allows you to encrypt files and folders on active computers, shared workstations, removable drives and network storage, including cloud storage services like Dropbox.

Encrypt Hard Drives

BestCrypt Volume Encryption allows you to encrypt entire hard drives and volumes, securing data on lost or stolen devices, external drives and bootable media. Featuring pre-boot authentication and multi-factor authentication, it ensures only authorized users can access your data.
