about us

The Society

The Thoresby Society was founded in 1889 as the principal historical society for Leeds and district. It seeks to promote interest, encourage research, and to collect and make available books, documents and other material for this purpose. It publishes annually, a series of articles, books and documents on a wide range of topics of local historical interest. It also provides members with engaging talks and excursions, all of which make Leeds history accessible to everyone interested in exploring its past. The Society is named after Ralph Thoresby (1685 – 1725), Leeds’ first historian.

Research

The Society’s Archive and Collections

The Society has collected an extensive Library, and also an archive of historical records, maps, images, and newspapers, which are housed within our space at the historic Leeds Library. The public area of this website contains the indexes to most of the archival material, which can be viewed by prior appointment with our librarians. Part of the archive collection is available online, including some rare maps, images and documents. Similarly the Society’s first series of publications up to 1990 has been digitised. This material is accessible to members via the members area or, via a one-off access fee, for non-members.
