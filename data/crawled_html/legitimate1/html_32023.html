Northeast American Diocese of theMalankara Orthodox Syrian Church

The Northeast American Diocese serves more than six states in the northeastern United States. It consists of more than 50 parishes, three missions, and one congregation. Through our local parish communities, the Diocese caters to the spiritual needs of over 20,000 Orthodox faithful, by proclaiming the Gospel in accordance with the Orthodox faith, sanctifying the faithful through sacred worship, and offering the loving presence of Christ in their spiritual lives.
