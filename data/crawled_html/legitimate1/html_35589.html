About Culture Fix

Culture Fix is a UK-based popular culture site. Our passions are music, film, and live theatre. Established in 2010, we’re devoted to great music from around the globe and aim to cover the latest releases with features, news and exclusive interviews. Culture Fix proudly backs rising talents, whilst also celebrating the best new releases from established favourites.

For enquiries, access the ‘Contact Us’ page in our site menu.
