Ben Aka The Bearded Jeweler

I started in jewelry 13 years ago as a simple bench jeweler working with gold and diamonds and I felt so out of place!  I remember freaking out because I was working with such expensive materials, but that soon became the norm. Now I specialize in stone setting, but found myself wanting for more artistic control. So I turned to silver and found a creative part of myself I never knew I had. All of my experience making jewelry lends itself to creating these designs along with my wife. I now strive to make something fun and accessible to everyone.

A little about the process

All handmade pendants are etched out of .925 Sterling Silver and then are oxidized and polished. They are then strung onto your choice of 16”, 18” or 20” sterling silver chains.

Thank you for supporting handmade!

I always want customers to be completely satisfied with their purchase.

If you have any concerns, questions or complaints please just send me a message and I'll be happy to help.
