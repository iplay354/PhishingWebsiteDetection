Welcome to Ditzy Media

Founded in 2014, Ditzy Media is an award-winning social media consultancy based in Eastbourne, helping businesses, charities and organisations shine online.

We offer complete social media management, strategic ad campaigns run by certified experts, and training in all areas of social media marketing—including Canva, video content, email marketing, and social media platforms.

Whether you want to hand it all over, get better results from your ads, or learn the skills to do it yourself, we’ve got a service to suit your goals and your budget.
