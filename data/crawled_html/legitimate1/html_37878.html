Our Story

We have worked closely with one of Europe's leading taxi despatch software developers and offer our customers the ability to book using our cutting edge Android and iPhone apps, alongside a web based booking facility, whilst we continue to offer a phone booking service for those who prefer to use it.

Who We Are

Smart Cars Taxis has been providing reliable taxi services across Staffordshire since 2016. We pride ourselves on delivering professional, safe and affordable transportation to the general public, to the public sector and to local businesses.
