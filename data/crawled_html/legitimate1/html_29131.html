IB by choice.
International by design.

ISP is proud to offer the full continuum of the International Baccalaureate curriculum.  ISP has been accredited by the IBO Organisation since 1982 and both the Council of International School (CIS) and the New England Association of Schools and Colleges (NEASC) since 1981.

Ranelagh Campus

Primary School (PYP)

Two converted residential buildings joined by an interior courtyard, playground and even a secret door, all in the heart of the 16th arrondissement! 

ISP's Early Learners to Grade 5 students engage with the transdisciplinary PYP Curriculum through play, inquiry and student agency.

Read more

 

Cortambert Campus

Middle School (MYP)

A tale of two time periods when a 19th century historic building meets its modern counterpart, joined by an interior courtyard complete with foosball and ping pong!

ISP's Grade 6 to Grade 9 students begin Secondary School by transitioning into the interdisciplinary MYP Curriculum.

Read more

 

Beethoven Campus

High School (MYP/DP)

With the end of Secondary School in sight, ISP's Grade 10 to Grade 12 students move to an open campus on a quiet cul-de-sac abutting the Seine.

The IB Diploma consolidates critical learning and research skills, woven together through community action, service and student engagement. 

Read more
