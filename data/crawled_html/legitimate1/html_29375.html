ALS/PACEM at a Glance

Headquartered in Perry, Florida, ALS/Pacem is the home to state-of-the-art facilities and equipment, highly qualified personnel, and high quality, reliable products for law enforcement. ALS/Pacem always strives to provide the highest quality munitions and services to our clients globally. We maintain all Department of Defense; Department of State; and Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosive standards. ALS/Pacem is a fully licensed Manufacturer and Exporter through the Department of State’s Directorate Defense Trade Control (DDTC).

ALS/Pacem is different from other less lethal companies because of the wide variety of products and training sessions that are offered. With our multifaceted property located in the panhandle of Florida and available year-round, training classes are hosted for all levels of law enforcement.  The facility is also available for agency rental and for outside training professionals. The property not only offers equipment that is capable of conducting multiple types of scenario training and range skills training, but can also accommodate up to 50 participants in a flexible accommodation bunk house.
