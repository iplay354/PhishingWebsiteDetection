Features included with your edu.au domain

Technical Support

Our dedicated customer service team provides technical support and advice regarding eligibility requirements, how to set up your edu.au domain name and how to connect your online services.

Client Console

The client console allows you to access registry and account functions associated with your edu.au domain name, including bulk updates and reporting, two-tier access and auto-renewal options.

DNS Record Hosting

The DNS record hosting provides the option to manage your domain registration and DNS records or zone file in a single management console, using anycast addressing for both speed and availability.

Forwarding

URL and email forwarding enables you to use your edu.au domain name without dedicated hosting services - great for starting out or transitioning from another domain name extension to edu.au.
