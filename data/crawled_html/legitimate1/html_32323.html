Who Are We

Dufil is home to Nigeria’s most loved noodle brand, Indomie Instant Noodles. Through consistent backward integration efforts over the past decade, Dufil has established itself as the leader in the instant noodles category.

Dufil Prima Foods Ltd Comprises of Subsidiary Companies and Divisions. Whilst Dufil has two (2) subsidiary companies in Nigeria namely: Insignia Prints Technology LFTZ Enterprise and Raffles Oil LFTZ Enterprise; it has three (3) foreign subsidiaries namely: De United Foods Industries Ghana Limited, Enriched Pte Ltd and Infinity FZCO; operating in Ghana, Singapore and Dubai respectively.

In addition to its Noodle Business, Dufil also operates divisions that manufacture the following products: Seasoning, Flour, Pasta, Snacks and Cassava.
