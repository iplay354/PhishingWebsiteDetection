Weddings at Walton Castle

Imagine standing on the balcony of a 16th century castle, glass of champagne in your hand surrounded by stunning countryside and coastal views with the person you adore standing just a breath away from you.

Hire your very own castle, exclusively for you and those extra special people in your life. With 3 beautiful areas to choose from for your ceremony, the castle is picture perfect with a fabulous backdrop for those precious photos. At the castle there is no compulsory catering allowing you to design your own menus and eat what you want, when you want it! We have a licenced bar which can be tailored to your requirments. To complete the dream, there is an Indoor heated swimming pool to relax in and a hot tub to revive you after a soak in its warming water.

Whatever the occasion, you can be guaranteed to have a fabulous time in intimate and impressive surroundings. Let the fairytale begin…….

Family, Holidays and Get Togethers

This stunning private Somerset castle is perfectly suited for bringing your loved ones together. Whether for a special occasion, an anniversary, a big birthday, a long overdue reunion with friends and family, or simply getting away from it all, it is the ideal destination where you will have an amazing experience, enjoyed across all generations and will cherish every moment, forging memories for life.

8 bedrooms, including 5 Turret bedrooms (yes you can have your own turret!) which are all self-contained with sofa area, toilet and shower, there is plenty of room for everyone. The property also boasts 2 big turrets (for larger occasions), an underground 10m heated swimming pool, a wood-fired hot tub and exercise area and, separately, a ‘games’ turret with bar, pool table, dart board and table football.

But it is not just indoors: Located at the heart of stunning Somerset, archery, medieval games, horse-riding, sailing, rock climbing, shopping, Bath, Bristol, Glastonbery, Wells and so much more ...

 

Special Occasions & Corporate Events

The Castle is the ideal location for celebrations and special occassion. Furnished and equipped to an excellent standard, with panoramic views over open countryside as well as secluded and secure gardens for the children to run free.  

For corporates, whether you are looking to hold a large scale conference, an exhibition, product launch or a small board meeting, we have the flexible facilities you need with break out rooms, conference areas and outdoor space as well as being the perfect place for team building days or weekends.

We work with the best local suppliers and have an enviable ‘black book’ of contacts to fulfil every possible event need from theming, entertainment, marquees, transport, logistical support… the list is long! 

Ideally located in the peace and tranquillity  of the rolling countryside, and with easy access from the M5/M4 corridor, we have a space for you, for whatever the occasion.
