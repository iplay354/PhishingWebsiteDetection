CCHP is the
National Telehealth Policy
Resource Center

The Center for Connected Health Policy is a nonprofit, nonpartisan organization working to maximize telehealth’s ability to improve health outcomes, care delivery, and cost effectiveness. Our expertise in telehealth policy was recognized in 2012, when we became the federally designated National Telehealth Policy Resource Center.

Telehealth Policy Webinar Series

CCHP’s Medicaid & State Telehealth Policy Fall webinar series was a success! These webinars took place the last two Fridays in the month of October 2022.  Both of the sessions focused on a different topic area including,  Federal Policy & Telehealth: What to be Aware of Going Forward, and What to Know about Practicing Across State Lines.  All webinars were recorded and are now available!
