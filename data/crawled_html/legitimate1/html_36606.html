Arrange your own wedding flowers

Wedding flowers are an essential part of any wedding by adding beauty to your special day. It just doesn't feel like a wedding without an amazing bridal bouquet at the forefront of it all. One of the most important aspects of wedding flowers is their ability to add color, theme, and style to the event. Bright, vibrant flowers can add a pop of color to a neutral-colored wedding, while soft, pastel flowers can create a romantic, vintage feel.

However, choosing the right flowers and arranging them in a way that complements your wedding theme and style can be a daunting task. This is where DIY affordable wedding flowers come in. By creating your own wedding flowers, you can add a personal touch to your special day and save money in the process.

When planning your do it yourself wedding flowers, it's important to first decide on a color scheme and overall aesthetic. This will help you choose the right flowers and greenery to use. For example, if you're going for a romantic and classic look, you might choose roses, peonies, and dahlias. If you're going for a rustic or bohemian look, wildflowers, eucalyptus, and succulents might be a better fit. Browse our collections to see which floral option matches your theme.

Next, plan out your flower arrangements. Consider the different types of flowers you'll need for bridal bridesmaid bouquets, boutonniere's, centerpieces, arches, and other decorations. Think about the sizes and shapes of the arrangements, as well as how they will be displayed. For example, if you're having a large wedding, you might want to create a grand centerpiece for the head table, while smaller arrangements or single flowers might be more appropriate for guest tables. Kukka will help you figure out the sizes of each arrangement for you.

When it comes to choosing your flowers, consider the season and availability. Some flowers may be less expensive or easier to find during certain times of the year. For example, peonies are typically only available in the spring, while dahlias are more readily available in the fall. Every flower on our website is priced based on the cost and is marked up as cheaply as possible.

Please consider that if you're planning on creating your own flowers, it's also important to have the right tools and materials on hand. You'll need a good pair of florist shears, floral tape, twine or ribbon, wire, a flower strippers, buckets, vases, and potentially floral foam and zip ties for your arrangements. We provide all the DIY arranging materials in our kit & arch decor orders.
