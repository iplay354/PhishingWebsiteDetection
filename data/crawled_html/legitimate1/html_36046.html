News & Updates

2024 County Population Estimates
March 13, 2025 — Population growth in U.S. metro areas as a whole was faster between 2023 and 2024 than in the previous year and outpaced that of the nation. Additionally, some metro areas that experienced population declines during the COVID-19 pandemic are now observing population gains, according to Vintage 2024 estimates of population totals and components of change released today by the U.S. Census Bureau.
2024 Population estimates for the U.S. and States
The U.S. population grew by nearly 1.0% between 2023 and 2024, according to the new Vintage 2024 population estimates released today by the U.S. Census Bureau.
U.S. Census Bureau Releases 2019-2023 ACS 5-Year Estimates
The U.S. Census Bureau announced the release of the 2019-2023 American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year estimates, the most relied-on source for up-to-date social, economic, housing, and demographic information every year.
