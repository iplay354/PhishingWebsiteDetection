About Amelishan Bridal

Amelishan Bridal is where brides come to find their dress the for most important day of their lives. We provide an exclusive, end to end experience for the bride from bridal gowns to bridesmaid dresses, to social occasion dresses for mother of the bride or groom, to suits and tuxedos. Our attention to detail, our vast, deep, and inclusive selection, and our warm, friendly and caring Bridal Stylists are why Milwaukee and Southeastern Wisconsin Brides choose Amelishan more than any other bridal salon.
