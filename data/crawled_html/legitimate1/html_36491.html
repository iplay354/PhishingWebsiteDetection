IMVE Visionaries

 

Visionaries is a year-round initiative from Imaging and Machine Vision Europe, designed to celebrate the most innovative integrators of the imaging and machine vision technology world.

Building upon the success of our Photonics100 project, conducted by our esteemed sister publication Electro Optics, Visionaries aims to highlight the brilliant minds behind the most thrilling advancements in imaging and machine vision.

Our dedicated content team conducts exclusive interviews with C-suite executives from leading system integrators, machine builders, and end-users who are harnessing the power of vision technology to push the boundaries of what is possible.
