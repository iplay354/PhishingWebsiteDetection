FEI TV Video :: About the Australian Stock Horse

A history of breeding Australian Stock Horses, filmed at Chalani Australian Stock Horse stud in South Australia, by a UK film crew in August 2011.

The horses are:
  • Chalani Apollo, liver chestnut gelding, shown under saddle with 16 year old owner Chari-lee Hoad
  • Chalani Trivia, brown mare, under saddle, ridden by Kim Ide (Apollo and Trivia have the same dam - Chalani Aurora)
  • Chalani Nightdance, black stallion shown at liberty with owner Paula Garrard, Ashborn Park Stud
  • Nuniong Beebie - taffy mare, led by Jeanette Gower
  • Chalani Jive - pinto mare, bareback with Kim Ide.
  • Foals are Chalani Sunstream (palomino) and Chalani Mawson (grey)
