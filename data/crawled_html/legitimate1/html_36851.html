The EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a data privacy law. The GDPR has stringent data protection and privacy clauses that affect any company subject to the regulation. A GDPR email archiving solution is specifically designed to meet the requirements of the GDPR. For example, under the GDPR, a data subject has the right to request information on how their data is being processed or to require their data be deleted; the latter is known as the “right to be forgotten.” In all cases, email-based data, including attachments, is subject to the data subject requests. Non-compliance with the GDPR will result in heavy fines up to EUR 20 million or 4 percent of a company’s total global turnover. To comply with the GDPR, emails must be easily and accurately retrievable. In the case of data deletion, emails must be irretrievably erased. Email retention periods may also apply in some circumstances. A GDPR email archiving solution must be designed to meet all of the stringent data security and privacy requirements of the regulation.
