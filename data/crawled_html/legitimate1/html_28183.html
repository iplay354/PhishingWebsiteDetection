- Food is energy -

We are a Frome based business on a mission to provide nourishing food at affordable prices, using ingredients that are as seasonal, locally sourced and environmentally friendly as we can. Cooked from scratch, using whole spices and fresh herbs. Yum!

Life is too short to eat food that isn’t tasty and satisfying. Our food includes all six tastes of nature at every meal so that the major food groups and nutrients are represented and you feel satisfied. We mix home cooked food made from scratch with stories and community focused events. 

Inspired by his mother’s wonderful home cooking, Mahesh set up Lungi Babas in 2014 in Frome. Our products and services include our regular street food lunches and evening Thali. We also do cooking workshops, bespoke events catering and popup events.

We use more sustainable packaging, compost our food waste with local composting org Loop, and our website is powered by an ethical, green co-op (Webarchitects Co-operative). We aim for minimum food waste (including asking for orders by 4pm for evening meals)⁠ and use local resources when we can, including local Frome Field 2 Fork veggies⁠.

Workshops

Give the gift of excellent food cooked at home

Why not buy a voucher as a gift for someone special or book a workshop for yourself!

Fun, educational and tasty cooking workshops. Delivered by Mahesh.

Do you need an exciting present for your family? Or do you want to learn how to cook a delicious vegetarian, South Indian inspired meal yourself?

Mahesh loves to share his wonderful style of cooking and storytelling, mixing fun, food and folklore with bespoke workshops designed specially for you. Let us know if there’s something specific you would like to learn!

1 to 1 (or 1 to  2) workshop = £160

Cooking workshop for 1-2 people. In person or online. 4 hours of fun, food and folklore. With tasty food to keep. This is a personal workshop just for you. You can have someone else joining you to help out (and learn). 

If more than 2 people, add £50 for each extra person (up to 6 people, space permitting)

Group workshop – £30 per person

In person or online. This is a workshop where you will join in with others who you may or may not know. Group sizes will be kept small. Dates to be confirmed soon. Or contact us to book it for you and your friends.
