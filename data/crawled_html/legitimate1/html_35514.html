Products and Services

Solar Installation

NOVEC Solutions has partnered with Prospect Solar and ElecTel Federal Credit Union to offer NOVEC's residential customers an easy and affordable way to install solar on their homes. Prospect Solar's turnkey service makes it easy to add solar panels to a home and ElecTel offers low interest, energy efficient loans for NOVEC customers who qualify.

Learn More

Home Repair and Replacement Services

Home emergency repairs can be expensive and time-consuming. With a repair plan from HomeServe, you can get access to a 24/7 hotline where you can schedule a repair for the systems in and around your home.

Learn More

Filter Replacement Program

The simpler way to change filters in your home with automatic delivery. Clean filters help your home's HVAC system work more efficiently and help you breathe cleaner air.

Learn More

Natural Gas

NOVEC Energy Solutions works hard to find savings where it purchases natural gas and how it routes that gas to your local utility for delivery at your home or business.

HVAC Referral Program

The HVAC Certified Contractor program provides NOVEC customers referrals for qualified HVAC contractors in our area.

Emergency Backup Generators

A whole-home standby generator can keep you safe and comfortable during power outages. Professionally installed and maintained, with affordable financing options.

Water Heaters

NOVEC Solutions works with a licensed local contractor that offers products and installation services for NOVEC customers in need of water heater solutions.

Learn More About NOVEC's EV-1 Rate!

This rate incentivizes owners to charge their vehicles when the overall demand for power is lowest (11 p.m. - 6 a.m.). Customers who sign up for EV-1 will get access to a rate that saves them nearly 50% on any electricity they use during this time window compared to the rest of the day.
