Our Plastic Surgeon and Team

Our founder and director, Leon Forrester Tcheupdjian, M.D., has specialized in Liposuction/Lipotherapy and related procedures since 1981. He has completed countless cosmetic procedures, including liposuction, in Chicago, Arlington Heights, Oak Brook, Naperville, and the surrounding areas. With a volume of procedures likely unmatched by any other American cosmetic surgeon, Dr. Tcheupdjian possesses unparalleled experience in liposuction and lipotherapy.


He is supported by a highly-trained team with decades of experience performing a variety of surgical and non-surgical procedures. Schedule a free consultation to discuss your aesthetic goals with our team. After understanding your desired appearance, we will recommend the appropriate cosmetic procedures to get you there.

Our practice is not limited to liposuction, plastic surgery, and cosmetic surgery.

Aside from liposuction and cosmetic surgery, we perform many additional surgical and non-surgical treatments, including:

  • Cellulite treatments
  • Vein treatments
  • Tattoo removal
  • Treatment for hyperhidrosis
  • Hair transplants
  • Microdermabrasion
  • Laser resurfacing for acne scars and wrinkles
  • Botox
  • Other dermal fillers

These treatments are excellent complements to plastic surgery, but they also stand on their own. We have offices conveniently situated in downtown Chicago, Oak Brook, Naperville, and Arlington Heights. Why Choose Liposuction and Cosmetic Surgery Institute?

For more than four decades, we’ve helped patients throughout Chicago, Oak Brook, Naperville, and Arlington Heights achieve their aesthetic goals with confidence. Our practice blends proven techniques with the latest innovations in cosmetic medicine, offering a full range of surgical and non-surgical treatments under one roof.


Every recommendation we make is guided by transparency, patient safety, and a commitment to results that look natural and feel empowering. From your first consultation to post-procedure follow-up, you’ll find personalized attention and a welcoming environment designed to make your experience as smooth as possible.
