KIA ORA FROM WHEELERS

Partnering with libraries to inspire Aotearoa

We are a New Zealand owned company with an unparalleled capability to supply international, New Zealand, Māori and Pasifika collections. We’re dedicated to supporting public libraries to inspire their communities through education, literacy and accessibility. With a vast range of services, passionate team of experts and a commitment to sustainability, we aspire to be New Zealand public libraries’ strongest ally.

OUR VALUES

What we stand for

Integrity

TAUTOKO

We champion the importance of books, literacy, and information to all New Zealanders. We value and advocate for the key role libraries play within their communities, and actively promote inclusivity and diversity in the resources we offer for a more just and equitable Aotearoa New Zealand.

Collaboration

MAHITAHI

Embracing strong partnerships and open communication, we are committed to understanding your unique objectives and challenges. Together we find innovative solutions that will respond to the changing needs of your customers and enhance your library services for greater outcomes.

Community

WHANAUNGATANGA

We foster meaningful relationships with libraries to ensure exceptional service and improvements. We engage with a range of community organisations, like Duffy Books in Homes and other local charities, to work towards a more equitable and positive future for all New Zealanders.

Sustainability

KAITIAKITANGA

Our interconnectedness with the environment is core to the way we work. We prioritise sustainability with eco-friendly materials like plant-based book covering and our partnership with Trees that Count helps plant native trees across New Zealand.

We are passionate about providing services to libraries. Talk to us about how we can work together to enhance knowledge, education, literacy, leisure, and engagement opportunities for the unique community you serve.
