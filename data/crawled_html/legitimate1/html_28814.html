Economic Development in Coatesville

It’s an exciting time for Economic Development in the City of Coatesville! Whether you’re starting a new business or looking to expand, Coatesville offers a strategic location, a rich history, and a supportive community that’s ready to help you reach new heights. They city is dedicated to attracting investments, nurturing local businesses, and creating a sustainable economy for everyone.

BID OPPORTUNITIES

The City of Coatesville invites qualified vendors, contractors, and service providers to explore our current BIDs, Requests for Proposals (RFPs), Requests for Information (RFIs), and Requests for Qualifications (RFQs). We value strong partnerships that help us deliver high-quality services and improvements to our community.Please review our available opportunities and consider submitting a proposal. We look forward to working with businesses and organizations that share our commitment to excellence, innovation, and community growth.
