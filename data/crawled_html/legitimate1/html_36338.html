OUR CHURCHES

Welcome!

We are 42,000 Episcopalians in 105 congregations and 10 campus ministries across central North Carolina—ranging from vibrant urban parishes to small, close-knit rural communities.

Wherever you go, you’ll find people living into our five missional priorities: racial reckoning, justice and healing; congregational vitality; formation; collaboration and new communities; and creation care. Together, we’re growing into God’s vision of the beloved community.

Rooted in ancient faith and committed to inclusivity, we celebrate the dignity of every person and strive to share God’s reconciling love with all especially those whom the church has harmed.

Welcome, we’re glad you’re here.
