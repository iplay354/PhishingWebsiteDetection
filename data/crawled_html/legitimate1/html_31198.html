About King Nut

Founded in 1927, King Nut Companies in Solon, Ohio supplies the best-tasting, fastest-selling gourmet nuts, snacks, trail mixes, chocolates, candy and gift tins to the retail, vending, airline, food service and private label industries. We challenge ourselves every day to be the best in the industry and to always offer a better value to consumers.
