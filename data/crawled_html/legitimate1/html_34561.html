    About Us

    ​

    Lincoln County Libraries has a rich history of serving the communities of Eureka, Libby, and Troy, Montana. Over the years, the libraries have grown and evolved, adapting to the needs of their patrons by expanding services, collections, and programs. The libraries aim to promote lifelong learning, provide access to a wide range of information, and foster community engagement through various educational and cultural events.
