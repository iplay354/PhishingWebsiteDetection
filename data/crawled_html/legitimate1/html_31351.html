BOT-3000E

The BOT-3000E (Binary Output Tribometer) is a precise digital instrument used globally to measure walkway surface coefficients of friction in both lab and field settings. Recognized as the industry-standard tribometer, it accurately assesses dynamic and static coefficients of friction under wet and dry conditions. Named in the American national standard ANSI A326.3, it is the only device meeting this criterion, a result of comprehensive research in the United States and Germany. This motorized drag-sled tribometer adheres to the “Daubert” rule of evidence, ensuring its reliability in federal and state courts for slip resistance measurement.

BOT-3000E Calibration & Repair

Annual calibration is crucial for the BOT-3000E, as with many precision measuring instruments. Stress, elapsed time, and environmental factors can impact accuracy, prompting the need for periodic adjustments. The BOT-3000E is intelligently designed to compensate for these effects through regular calibrations. Users are notified when it’s time to send the unit for the annual calibration and evaluation service at the factory. Failure to recalibrate by the due date is indicated on test result printouts as ‘out of calibration,’ signifying potential unreliability in the collected measurement data. This ensures the instrument’s continued accuracy, reliability, and compliance with industry standards.

Slip Test Services

WMG offers on-site Slip Test Services across the U.S., conducted by expert auditors using the BOT-3000E Tribometer—the sole device approved for ANSI A326.3 compliance. Adhering to the American National Standard, WMG validates COF values on new surfaces, monitors slip resistance affected by maintenance and foot traffic, and assesses floor care product effectiveness. Post-testing, a detailed audit report, complying with ASTM F1694-14 and F2048-00 standards, is prepared. It includes a testing diagram, hard surface flooring conditions description, an executive summary of ANSI A326.3, all DCOF measurements with statistical analysis, and comprehensive tribometer reports featuring timestamps, date stamps, photos, and graphs for each DCOF sample.

More about Slip Test Services

Lab Testing

Our lab is fully equipped to conduct Slip Resistance Testing Services. Adhering to stringent standards such as ANSI A326.3 (2022) using the BOT-3000E Tribometer, ASTM E303-93 using the British Pendulum Tester, ASTM F2913-19, and DIN 51130 (2014-02) for Anti-Slip Property using the German Ramp, WMG evaluates the slip resistance of various products, ranging from flooring materials through floor cleaning products and processes. The testing services conclude with a comprehensive report, aligning with ASTM F2048-00 requirements. Any additional requirements must be submitted to the lab prior to testing for thorough evaluation.
