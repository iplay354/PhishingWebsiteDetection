    About the University of Wales Trinity Saint David

    We have 20 subject areas for you to choose from, each with a comprehensive range of courses ready to inspire you. It’s time to grab your future with both hands and make your ambitions a reality.

    We offer a more personalised experience – small class sizes and engaging lectures, with plenty of student involvement, allowing discussion and a better understanding of your subject. You’ll learn from subject experts who’ll teach you the skills and knowledge you need to succeed.
