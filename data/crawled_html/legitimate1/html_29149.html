CBRNE Tech Index

Independent. Unbiased. Reliable.

CBRNE Tech Index is powered by one of the nation's leading research institutes, MRIGlobal. CBRNE Tech Index displays a wide range of Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear, and Explosives (CBRNE) equipment and products for use in the field or laboratory. We are the one-stop-shop for all things CBRNE, including detection, collection, decontamination, and personal protection. Technologies range from boots and gloves, to easy-to-use colorimetric strips and State-of-the-Art analytical equipment. MRIGlobal scientists regularly test and evaluate equipment for government and industrial clients, providing expert and end~user feedback for product development and upgrades. Whether you are a first responder, Hazmat member, researcher, student, government agency, food safety, pharmaceutical analysis, petro-chemical process controller, or countless others, CBRNE Tech Index makes our knowledge available to help counter the WMD threat.
