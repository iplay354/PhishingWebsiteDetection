ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF COUNTRY

We acknowledge the people of the Yalukit Willam and we pay our respects to their elders, past and present.

MISSION STATEMENT

The Williamstown Literary Festival (est 2003) brings together readers, writers and book creatives of all sorts for an annual celebration of story-telling, author talks, panel discussions and writing workshops at a historic seaside setting.

MAJOR PARTNER

The Williamstown Literary Festival is supported by Hobsons Bay City Council through its Make It Happen Grants
