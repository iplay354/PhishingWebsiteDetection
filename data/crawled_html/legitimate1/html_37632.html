Glasgow Airport Information

Glasgow on average handles just over 7.5 million passengers year, a number that has steadily increased over the last few years!

Glasgow Airport currently contributes around £1.44bn to the economy, as well as supporting around 30,000 jobs!

  • Airport code: GLA
  • Telephone: 0344 481 5555*
  • Airport Postcode: PA3 2SW
  • 8 miles from Glasgow
