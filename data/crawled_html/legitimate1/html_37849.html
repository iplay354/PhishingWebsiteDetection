 Title VI Compliance Requirements for ARTSPACE at Untitled

ARTSPACE at Untitled is committed to full compliance with Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, which prohibits discrimination on the basis of race, color, or national origin in any program or activity receiving federal financial assistance. To uphold these standards, ARTSPACE at Untitled ensures that all services, exhibitions, programs, educational initiatives, and public activities are accessible, equitable, and free from discriminatory practices.
