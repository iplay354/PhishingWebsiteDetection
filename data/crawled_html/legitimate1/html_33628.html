    About MassBio

    MassBio’s mission is to advance Massachusetts’ leadership in the life sciences to grow the industry, add value to the healthcare system, and improve patient lives. We represent the premier global life sciences and healthcare hub, with 1,700+ members dedicated to preventing, treating, and curing diseases through transformative science and technology that brings value and hope to patients.

    Empowered purchasing.

    MassBioEdge helps companies save on essential supplies and services with exclusive discounts, better terms, and personalized support. By working collectively with our 1,700+ members, we secure superior rates that surpass what individual companies could achieve alone— saving over $300M annually. These savings fund research, bridge financial gaps, and accelerate commercialization.
