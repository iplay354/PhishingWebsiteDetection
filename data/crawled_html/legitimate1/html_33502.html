About Bonusway

Bonusway is a Finnish company founded in 2011 and based in Helsinki. We are dedicated to driving more sales for e-commerce merchants by sharing the best online deals with our user community. We are a first mover in the social shopping area enabling our users to share the best findings within the community.

We work in close partnership with different types of corporate partners like banks, telecom operators and airlines and help them provide the best online shopping content for their customers.
