One Amazing Story: Grateful for those who serve

This month's One Amazing Story features what is quickly becoming a Leyden tradition: Hosting Thanksgiving for the Navy recruits! Our food services team gives up their holiday with family to prepare and serve a traditional meal for our Great Lakes Navy recruits. Our National Honor Society students, musicians and staff join in to interact and entertain the recruits, who are in the middle of basic training and haven't had a chance to socialize in eight weeks. And, lastly, Veterans from the local legion attend and support their fellow servicemen. This video features stories from recruits, food service employees, Veterans, and students who all connect over this very special holiday at Leyden.
 

Leyden and Rotary honor 12 inspiring eagles

On December 10, the Chicago-O’Hare Rotary, in partnership with Leyden High School District 212, honored the following twelve students in its 11th annual Inspirational Eagle Awards: Julio Andujar Araujo, Dominic Ganir, Jacob Ganir, Jesus Rosa Gomez, Lily Mason, Francisco Melendez, Hilliana Mendoza, Noala Morales, Lizeth Romero, Angel Marcos Payan, Maylen Parra Sales, and Jacob Sanchez.

Leyden end of semester schedule

The end of semester summatives will take place over the last three days of student attendance before winter break. This ensures that all students benefit from a maximum of three summatives in one day. Please see the schedules below and note: December 17, will be a Wednesday that runs on a regular schedule (no late start), with the school day starting at 7:35 a.m. Grab and go lunches will be provided December 18 and 19.

Dr. Nick Polyak named state Superintendent of the Year

An independent panel commissioned by the Illinois Association of School Administrators (IASA) has named Dr. Nick Polyak, Superintendent of Leyden High School District 212, as the 2026 Illinois Superintendent of the Year. The highly competitive selection process judges nominees on the following criteria: academic achievement, preparing students for the future, social-emotional support, teaching and instruction and leadership. This honor recognizes Dr. Polyak’s exceptional leadership, visionary initiatives, and unwavering commitment to educational excellence at the local, state, and national levels. 

One Amazing Story: Angelina Fakhoury-Siverts

This month’s One Amazing Story features Angelina Fakhoury-Siverts, East Leyden alum and trailblazer as the first-ever female football player in the West Suburban Conference. Today, Angelina continues to break barriers as the Vice President of Patient Care Services and Chief Nursing Officer at City of Hope Cancer Center–Chicago. Her journey from the Leyden field to hospital leadership is a story of courage, dedication, and compassion.

Katy Boehm appointed to Board of Education

The Leyden High School District 212 Board of Education is pleased to announce the appointment of Katy Boehm as its newest member. Boehm will fill the vacancy left by the recent passing of her mother, longtime Board Member Sandra Boehm, serving the remainder of her term through the spring of 2027.

Leyden earns "commendable" designation

The Illinois State Board of Education honored Leyden High School District 212 with the distinction of “commendable” on the Illinois Report Card for the 2024-25 school year. This honor represents gains for both East and West Leyden, and reflects the district’s ongoing commitment to student achievement and attendance efforts.

Leyden hosts annual Women in Tech Panel

On Wednesday, October 29, Leyden hosted a Women in Tech Panel, featuring five inspiring women from diverse technology-related fields, including nuclear fuel energy, industrial technology education, nursing, insurance, and engineering. Each speaker shared her unique journey, challenges, and successes in her career, offering valuable advice to students interested in pursuing tech-focused paths. Leyden students attended the event to learn more about opportunities in technology after high school and college, gaining insight and motivation to follow their own ambitions in these growing industries.
