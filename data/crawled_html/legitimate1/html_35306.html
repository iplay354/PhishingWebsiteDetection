About Us

DAIR is a consortium of companies, academic and research institutions, and stakeholders with a shared commitment to strengthen Ontario’s aerospace innovation ecosystem.

Events

Register for upcoming DAIR events and review content from previous sessions.

DAIR Innovation Network

The Innovation Network is designed to accelerate innovation by engaging participants in a cyclical and iterative process.

Supplier Development Initiative

The DAIR Supplier Development Initiative helps SMEs assess and address potential gaps in their capacity and capabilities and provides funding to implement solutions.

DAIR Green Fund

The DAIR Green Fund provides financial support for collaborative R&D projects that can demonstrate a significant impact on sustainable and green aerospace and aviation.

DAIR Upskill

DAIR can connect you with resources to ensure your staff is equipped with the credentials and know-how to achieve your goals.

History

The Downsview site in North York, Ontario is a significant part of Canada’s aviation history and 100+ year tradition of aerospace excellence.

The Hub

Our growing collection of facilities and state-of-the-art equipment is designed to optimize collaboration, networking and the advancement of next-generation aerospace R&D.

PRIVACY POLICY

Downsview Aerospace Innovation & Research (DAIR) respects the privacy of its members and those who visit its website. Subject to the provision of applicable laws, any information you submit to DAIR will not be used in any manner to which you have not consented.

DAIR does not collect data using cookies; however, it does use Google Analytics to better understand its website visitors and how content can be improved on its platforms. 

DAIR does not sell, lease or otherwise distribute its member lists to third parties, nor any information provided through the website or registering to receive the newsletter.
