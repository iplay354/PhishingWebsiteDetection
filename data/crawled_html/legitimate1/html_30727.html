Slower is stronger. Gentle is advanced.

J. Brown’s signature offerings are at once both a revolution and a return to the ancient roots of yoga, J’s is a deeply breath-centered therapeutic yoga practice, inspired by the TKV Desikachar tradition, geared towards feeling well and lessening of pain. Whether you are a complete beginner or an advanced practitioner, you are in the right place. Join thousands of students and teachers around the world and experience J' Brown's singular voice and teaching.
