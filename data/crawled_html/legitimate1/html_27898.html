Key Insights on Top Websites in the World in November 2025

As of November 2025, google.com is the most visited website in the World, attracting 99.41B monthly visits. youtube.com follows with 48.6B visits, and facebook.com records 9.02B visits.

In terms of user engagement, the average number of pages per visit across these sites is 6.06, with x.com leading at 9.29 pages per visit.

The average bounce rate is 44.11%, with whatsapp.com having the highest at 61.11%.

And pornhub.com leads the way with the lowest bounce rate at 23.34%.

These insights come from the .Trends platform, enriched with extensive clickstream data.

Our AI algorithms analyze petabytes of information from a blend of proprietary and external data pools, reflecting the online activities of over 200 million anonymized users.
