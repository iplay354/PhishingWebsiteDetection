Welcome to Aberdeen Hindu Association (AHA)


Aberdeen Hindu Association (AHA) is guided by our vision to work towards 'Truth, Wisdom and Bliss' and run by a group of dedicated volunteers in the North East of Scotland. In pursuit of our vision,we conduct Kalpa - Children's forum, celebrate Hindu religious festivals and organise cultural and educational activities.



Hindu Festivals and KALPA, Children's forum
Hindu Festivals: We organise Ganesh Chaturthi and Shivratri celebrations in collaboration with the Aberdeen Hindu Temple Trust (AHTT).
