Made from Organic Cotton & Merino Wool

We use only the finest natural fibers, GOTS-certified organic cotton and ethically sourced merino wool, to create basics that are soft and gentle.

Ethically Made

Every Q for Quinn piece is made in family-owned factories in Portugal and India where workers are treated with dignity and paid fair wages.

Giving Back, One Pair at a Time

For every pair you purchase, we donate a meal to a child in need through our partnership with Mary’s Meals.

Designed With Purpose

We don’t believe in fast fashion. We believe in thoughtful design that lasts; soft fabrics, classic styles, and seasonless staples.

Hi, My Name Is Melita.

I founded Q for Quinn to dress my son, who suffered from eczema. I was alarmed to find that around 90% of baby and kids' socks contained BPA and Parabens. This compelled me to seek a safer alternative.

Today, my small team and I strive to create healthy, comfortable organic cotton basics – gentle on our skin, planet and community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Need more info? We’ve got you covered.

How do I pick the right size?

We have created a sizing guide for each of our products to help you pick the right size. If you still have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We'd love to help!

How much does shipping cost?

USA: US$8 flat rate, 2-8 business days

Canada: C$9.50 flat rate, 2-8 business days

Free shipping over US$75/C$95

International: US$15 flat rate, up to 60 days

As a small business, we appreciate your support as we work to get your orders to you as quickly and affordably as possible.

Where do you ship to?

We ship to all 50 states in the USA and Canada. We ship from Toronto, ON, Canada. Shipping is usually 2-8 business days.

We ship to select international countries and are working on improving our service for international destinations.

Where are your products made?

We are proud to work with GOTS certified, fair trade, family owned facilities around the world!

Our socks and tights are made in Portugal.

Our underwear is made in India.

Are Q for Quinn products suitable for sensitive skin?

All our products are designed first for sensitive skin and conditions.

In fact, our very first pair of socks was designed to be gentle enough for a little boy who suffered from eczema.

The integrity of the materials and the rigor of being a GOTS-certified brand enable us to create products that are gentle on our skin and our planet.

You can read several reviews from our customers with sensitive skin here

Are there any synthetics in your products?

At Q for Quinn, you will find basics with some of the highest composition of organic, GOTS-certified, natural materials in the world!

However, we also understand that everyday living calls for basics that fit well and stand the test of time.

Some of our basics are made from 100% cotton, most are made with 95%+ natural materials, and a few contain a small amount of synthetics to improve comfort and durability.

Some synthetics such as elastics are used for our waistband and cuffs. These are all OEKO-Tex certified to ensure they are free from harmful chemicals and approved by GOTS.

The fabric composition of our basics can be found in every product listing under “Fabric and Care.”

You can read more about our journey to transition to biodegradable products here.

Q for Quinn Inc. is on a mission to create happy and healthy organic cotton and merino wool products that are gentle on our skin and our planet. 

We are a small, family owned business dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of quality and ethics.

Q for Quinn brand represents our children's collection and Basics by Quinn is our adult collection of basics.
