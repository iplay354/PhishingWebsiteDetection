Established in 1963, the French International School of Hong Kong is committed to excellence in education. We invite you to explore our programs for primary and secondary education and discover how we can support your child's educational journey.

We are proud to welcome 2,800+ students from 45 countries across our four campuses. As an all-through international school offering two internationally accredited streams in French and English, FIS embodies 60 years of excellence in a multilingual, inclusive and caring academic environment.

FIS nurtures trusting relationships that help develop each learner’s talent and passion while supporting their needs. We empower every student to reach their ambition and fullest academic potential. As an engaged community, we cultivate critical thinking and value social and personal responsibility to ensure students launch into their life journey as globally-minded citizens of tomorrow.

What sets the French International School Apart?

You want your child to have the best possible education. So do we. Find out why we are the school of choice for hundreds of international families in Hong Kong.

60 years of excellence in education, demonstrated by outstanding exam results

A multicultural school underpinned by French and English bilingualism, reinforced in our two streams

Support tailored to each student’s needs in an encouraging and caring environment

One school for your child’s entire education, from kindergarten through to upper secondary


A wide range of extracurricular activities

Reasonable tuition fees and a debenture that is reimbursed at the end of your child’s schooling

French Stream

Students in the French stream follow the French national curriculum, from kindergarten through to the French Baccalauréat.
