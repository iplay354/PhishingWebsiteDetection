Meet Adam

Senator Adam P. Ebbin fights for progressive priorities including preventing gun violence, making it easier to vote, preserving reproductive freedom, and equality for all Virginians.

Adam took office in 2012 after serving eight years in the House of Delegates. 

He was reelected in 2023 in the newly-redrawn 39th Senate District to continue representing Alexandria and portions of Arlington and Fairfax counties.
