Academic Excellence At SRM University, Sonepat

Our exemplary educational model is unmatchable as it incorporates the world's leading techniques, theories, resources, and standards.

Faculty of

Engineering & Technology

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology aims to emerge as a leading world-class faculty that creates and disseminates knowledge upholding the highest standards of instruction in Engineering and Technology. Along with academic excellence and skills, our curriculum imparts integrity and social sensitivity to mold our graduates who may be best suited to serve the nation and the world.

Explore

Faculty of

Finance and Commerce

The Faculty of Finance and Commerce examines economic decision-making. Our faculty actively engage with business leaders and students to develop and share new insights that span areas like Fintech and Governance, Corporate Finance and accounting, etc. Our highly experienced faculties explore both theoretical and applied concepts.

Explore

Faculty of

Hotel Management

The Faculty of Hotel Management offers an excellent teaching and hospitality environment through competent faculty members of various specializations, like Food & Beverage Production, Front Office, etc. The faculty focuses on Skill Based international Hospitality management education more than just business management. The commitment is to impart our students with a deep understanding of the Hospitality business.

Explore

Faculty of

Law

The Faculty of Law at SRMUH is established to promote excellence in the field of legal education and fulfill the ever-increasing demand for quality legal professionals for global employability. Law programs at SRM Faculty of Law is designed to provide holistic opportunity to our students to learn not only the legal skills but life skills and practical skills as well. The curricula focusses upon building the Knowledge, Skills and Attitude of our learners.

Explore

Faculty of

Management Studies

The Faculty of Management Studies provides the students with a solid foundation in core business principles while developing a broadened global mindset and understanding of the role of business in society. Students are challenged to delve deep into the areas that interest them and gain the technical, conceptual, and strategic skills that will assist them to lead a successful careers.

Explore

Faculty of

Science and Humanities

The Faculty of Science and Humanities strives to impart knowledge in a wide and diverse range of research and teaching programs. The various departments of Humanities are committed to adopting and implementing multidisciplinary approaches to teaching, learning, and research. We aim to develop a cohesive and inclusive academic ecosystem to promote high-quality academic delivery.
