North Coast California Native Plant Society

• Evening Program. Wednesday, January 14, doors open at 5:45 p.m., lecture at 6:00, social hour afterwards. D Street Neighborhood Center, Arcata. Transforming small garden spaces in suburban Eureka. Gretchen Zeigler (retired as Director of the Eureka Zoo) will chronicle her gardening journey adding native plants in her backyard, in gardens of friends, and at the Zoo. She has found joy not only in the plants but also in the ever-increasing array of pollinators and other insects that use these gardens. Details HERE. To Zoom, click HERE.
