Caiplie House is a large Victorian house, situated in the picturesque East Neuk village of Crail. 

Sleeping up to 15, it is perfect for your group or family gathering.  

It has seven bedrooms, a large lounge, additional sitting room, and a dining room able to seat your whole group. Bedrooms are all en- suite and include doubles, twins and one ‘family’ suite.  The house has 3 levels and the top floor has sea views to the Isle of May. 

It is ideal for family gatherings, reunions, significant birthday celebrations, corporate groups, girls weekends, walking groups and golfing parties.

There is something for everyone in Crail, with it’s famous pitusresque harbour, galleries and pottery, golf courses, beaches and Fife Coastal path.

15 mins from St Andrews, 75 mins from Edinburgh airport
