Events

G'day Little Queenslanders

Prepare for a fun-packed outing as G'day Little Queenslanders is bringing free community events to Moreton Bay, Townsville, Redlands Coast and Cairns. This heartwarming free event program is all about showering love on Queensland's new babies and their families, bringing communities together, and making sure parents have access to all the must-know government resources and local services.

Ability Fest

Queensland proudly supported its first-ever AbilityFest at Victoria Park - a truly inclusive and accessible event where people of all abilities came together to enjoy an unforgettable day of music and connection.

Queensland Greats Awards

The Queensland Greats Awards celebrate and honour exceptional individuals and institutions whose contributions or achievements have significantly influenced the history and progress of Queensland.

Queensland Cabinet and Ministers

The Cabinet is the key decision-making body of the government, made up of the Premier and ministers. The Premier, as the leader of the government, also serves as the chair of the Cabinet.

DPC Annual Report 2024–25

The annual report details our financial and non-financial performance to 30 June 2025. It highlights our work, achievements and strategic initiatives, and satisfies the requirements of Queensland's Financial Accountability Act 2009 and the Financial and Performance Management Standard 2019.
