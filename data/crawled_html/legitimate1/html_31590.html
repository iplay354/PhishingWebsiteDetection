Welcome to Champlin | EOP

At Champlin | EOP, our team members combine passion with creativity and expertise to plan, design, and deliver amazing results to our clients. Our innovative, design-driven firm is invested in pushing creativity and collaboration to deliver unique spaces that inspire form and delight through function. We are motivated by a vision of enriching lives through innovative design and sharing our creativity with the world while defining a sustainable future.

We are devoted to service and life-long learning and encourage our employees and clients to Think, Create, and Realize with us.
