With access to state-of-the-art facilities, boys at Tonbridge are well-equipped for exceptional learning experiences.

From an award-winning science centre to an Olympic sports centre, we’ve got the facilities to support your passions.

Our facilities

We actively encourage boys to explore their interests.

We have a wide variety of clubs and societies which cater for diverse interests, including literary, cultural, political, scientific, artistic, and sporting pursuits. The Societies Fair at the start of each year enables the boys to find out more and make their choices.
