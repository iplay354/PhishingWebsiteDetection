About Us

Welcome to Bavarian Motorsport

At Bavarian Motorsport in Kennett Square, PA, we provide dealership level auto repair and maintenance with the personal service of a local expert. Our team specializes in European car diagnostics, top tier alignments, and factory recommended interval maintenance to keep your vehicle performing at its best.

Since 1990, our independent, family owned auto repair shop has been a trusted name in Kennett Square. Specializing in European vehicles is how we got our start. Today we work on all makes and models and pride ourselves on delivering an exceptional service experience to all. Our goal is to build trust with our clients by doing what's right and providing expert solutions tailored to their needs.

At Bavarian Motorsport in Kennett Square, PA, we deliver true dealership level service with the personalized care only a dedicated European specialist can offer. Here, you’re a valued client—not just a number. We take the time to understand your needs so we can provid service customized to you and your vehicle. Our knowledgeable service advisors keep you informed every step of the way, while our dealership level technicians perform comprehensive inspections and share a transparent digital report complete with photos. After answering your questions and receiving approval, we complete your repairs with high quality parts backed by a 3 year/36,000 mile parts and labor warranty. For your convenience, we offer secure online payment, 24/7 vehicle pick up and drop off, complimentary service loaners when available, and local shuttle service. At Bavarian Motorsport, our goal is simple: exceptional service, honest communication, and a stress free experience from start to finish.
