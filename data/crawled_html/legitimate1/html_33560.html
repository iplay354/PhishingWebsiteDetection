We are delighted that you would like to learn more about our school and about a Montessori education for your child. We are a vibrant community of families, friends and faculty working together to provide a stimulating and developmentally enriching Montessori experience for our children. We offer programs for children from 15 months of age through the 6th grade.
Our mission is to ensure that children become self-confident and thoughtful individuals who collaborate with their peers, think critically for themselves and engage responsibly within their community. As an AMI-recognized school, we provide our students with a strong educational foundation and a love for learning that they will carry throughout their lives.
