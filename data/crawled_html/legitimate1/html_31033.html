Welcome to Lukenda Dental! We take great pleasure in inviting you to our dental office in Clark, New Jersey. Please take a few moments to browse through our website to know more about our team and services.

Dr. Richard Lukenda is a diversely-skilled Certified Dentist dedicated to the personal care of each patient. Dr. Lukenda’s advanced training in dental implants and the exceptional skills of his dental assistants and dental hygienists gives his patients an added advantage. Our dental office has extensive training in the latest digital smile design technologies where patients can view a proposed aesthetic smile before any work is done. If you have an idea of a perfect smile, come speak to our team and we will work towards bringing your dream smile to reality.
