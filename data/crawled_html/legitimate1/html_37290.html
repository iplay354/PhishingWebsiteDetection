The Willmar Lakes Area Chamber of Commerce is a leader in community promotion, advocacy, relationships, and economic development representing business in Kandiyohi County. A membership with the Chamber is an investment in your community and a commitment to goodwill, education, and development.

Testimonials

"It is an honor to be part of the Willmar Lake Area Chamber of Commerce. It is very important for a small business to have the support of other businesses in our community, in particular from the Chamber of Commerce. The Chamber provides many resources for businesses that we often ignore. As a Latino, I feel proud to know that diversity is something very valuable to the Chamber, and to feel that support as a business owner in this country is fundamental."

Solange Cooley
Ag Jobs LLC

"I am part of the Willmar Lakes Area Chamber of Commerce because it is a place of belonging, community building, people connecting, learning experiences, advocacy champions, and leadership growth!"

Pablo Obregon
City of Willmar

"Intuition Brewing has been part of the Chamber since before Intuition even opened its doors. Becoming part of the Chamber was important to us, as it is a vital place to connect with other businesses, explore partnerships, and see what's happening in the Willmar area. The Chamber has been a huge piece of our journey in making our dream of opening a brewery come true."

Liz and Micah Larson
Intuition Brewing
