Meet the founder & podcast host of Cubicle to CEO®, Ellen Yin!

My journey from "Cubicle to CEO®" began when I quit my corporate marketing job five years ago...without a backup plan.

After landing my first freelance marketing client a month into my job search, I swore off sending out resumes for good. By re-investing my profits back into the business over and over again, I bootstrapped that first $300 client project into millions in revenue and created programs, events, and resources that have advanced the economic opportunities of women. 

To date, we've provided mentorship and education to more than 14,000 entrepreneurs - mostly women - and our podcast has been listened to 1 MILLION times worldwide.

Women are historically underfunded (receiving less than 2% of all venture capital), so I've also personally angel invested in three women-owned startups to help change that narrative.

For more transparent insights, catch me every Monday on Cubicle to CEO®, a globally charting top 10 entrepreneurship podcast where we ask successful founders the business questions you can't Google.
