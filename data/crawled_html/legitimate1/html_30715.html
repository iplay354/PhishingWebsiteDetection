The development of secure and transparent voting systems is essential to the future of our country. NAVO, working with community members and election officials exists to aid in the effort toward solution. We would appreciate your support in this important endeavor.

NAVO's Mission:

The mission of the National Association of Voting Officials is to develop and make available open source voting systems for use in public elections, as well as to provide training, education and management practices to election officials for the effective employment of technologies.
