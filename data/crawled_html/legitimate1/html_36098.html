*Candy Factory Projects

A platform for collaborative art projects by Takuji Kogo. Founded in 1998 in a former candy factory in Yokohama, Japan. *Candy Factory Projects produces abstract views of sites and architecture in political environments. It also transforms fragments of information such as propaganda, personal ads, online comments into music videos with vocal synthesizers. *Candy Factory Projects is visual and musical, constantly relocating its activities, conducting site-specific research, producing exhibitions with multi-channel video and sound installations, screening events, online projects and publications. *Candy Factory Projects is archived at: https://artonline.jp/

Contact : candyfactory(at)icloud.com

 *Candy Factory Projects was originally set up as an exhibition space housed in a former candy factory in Yokohama, Japan in 1998. It moved to the Yokohama Triennale in 2001, the Akiyoshidai International Art Village in 2002.
