Winter Lights

Discoveries, flavours & authenticity

Winter sets in, the ocean reveals its character and the sand retains the imprint of your salty sea breezes. The green pines contrast with the colour of the paths, the narrow streets smell of open fires and the stones lighten under the soft light of the season. Gourmet restaurants celebrate the flavours of the island, generous and welcoming.

Climb up to the Baleines lighthouse for a thrill, stroll among the market stalls decked out for the festive season, discover the craftsmanship of local artisans, take some time for yourself in a spa or by watching the ocean from the warmth of your window.

Between wind and light, enjoy a timeless interlude.

One island, 10 villages
  • Le Bois-Plage-en-RéA seaside resort in the centre of the island, Le Bois-Plage-en-Ré boasts 6km of beaches that hide, behind its dunes, a vast wooded area ideal for beautiful walks. The town centre is bustling with shops and, in season, a daily market - the largest on the island - where you can stroll at your own pace to discover the local flavours.More about the village
  • Ars-en-RéArs-en-Ré is renowned for its black-and-white bell tower, once used as a landmark for sailors, and its lively market selling delicious local produce with a wide range of flavours to be (re)discovered without moderation! Ranked as one of the Most Beautiful Villages in France, make a stopover in Ars-en-Ré to discover its famous port and salt marshes.More about the village
  • Saint-Clément-des-BaleinesWelcome to Saint-Clément-des-Baleines, an enchanting village on the Ile de Ré where nature reigns supreme. With its vast stretches of beach, unspoilt countryside and famous lighthouse, this village offers the ideal setting for lovers of the sea and wide open spaces.More about the village
  • LoixWant to discover a village where time seems to stand still? Welcome to Loix, the beloved peninsula of the Ile de Ré. Surrounded by salt marshes and wild landscapes, the village offers a genuine natural spectacle. The marshes, tended by local salt-marsh farmers, are home to exceptional biodiversity, where serenity and authenticity combine to offer an unforgettable getaway. Loix, which has been awarded the Cittaslow label, cultivates a lifestyle that contributes to its gentle ambience. You'll quickly succumb to the charm of its landscapes, between ocean and salt marshes, where living heritage and natural beauty come together.More about the village
  • Saint-Martin-de-RéThe island's main town, Saint-Martin-de-Ré is famous for its fortifications, built by Vauban and listed as a World Heritage Site by UNESCO. But it's also famous for its dynamic and engaging local life and its emblematic marina, located in the heart of the citadel.More about the village
  • La FlotteWith its typical alleyways, lively port and medieval-inspired market, the village of La Flotte is guaranteed to take you back in time. Listed as one of the Most Beautiful Villages in France, La Flotte is not to be missed!More about the village
  • Sainte-Marie-de-RéSituated on the south coast of the Ile de Ré, Sainte-Marie-de-Ré is a picturesque village where vineyards meet the ocean. This charming village has a rich heritage, a historic church and 2 lively village centres: Sainte-Marie and La Noue.More about the village
  • Rivedoux-PlageRivedoux-Plage, a charming seaside village, is the first town to be discovered when crossing the bridge from the mainland. It is renowned for its two large sandy beaches, ideal for swimming and water sports. It is also renowned for its many hiking and cycling trails, offering breathtaking views of the ocean and surrounding countryside.More about the village
  • Les Portes-en-RéThis village offers a gentle way of life, authenticity and the feeling of being at the world's end. It's also an unspoilt location in the heart of a protected environment, bordering the Lilleau des Niges nature reserve, the salt marshes and the pine forests that are typical of the north of the Ile de Ré, and ideal for some beautiful, relaxing walks.More about the village
  • La Couarde-sur-MerLocated right in the heart of the island, La Couarde-sur-Mer is the ideal starting point for your cycling tours. Boasting a unique labyrinth of flower-filled alleyways, the village invites you to let go as you wander down a colourful, fragrant lane, or past its emblematic bandstand.More about the village

    ✨ Le dicton du saunier ✨ Dans les marais salants, on raconte : « Entre Noël et la Fête des Rois, le temps annonce les douze mois. » Ce n’est qu’un dicton ancien, transmis de génération en génération, mais il fascine encore. Selon cette croyance, chaque journée du 25 décembre au 6 janvier refléterait le temps d’un mois de l’année suivante : ☀️ Le 25 décembre pour janvier, 🌧️ le 26 pour février, 💨 le 27 pour mars, … et ainsi de suite. Une belle tradition qui relie les sauniers au ciel et au rythme des saisons. ✨🧂 🔭 À vos observations dès le 25 décembre ! #iledere #ilederé #maraissalants #maraissalant #dicton #météo #meteo
