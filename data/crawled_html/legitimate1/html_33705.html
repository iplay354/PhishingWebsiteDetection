Advanced Technology For Your Comfort
Dr. Tamara Smith has been setting the highest standards of excellence in orthodontics since 2011. As a certified Invisalign provider, she has extensive experience in creating beautiful, straight smiles with the convenience of clear, removable aligners. With no metal brackets or wires, Invisalign makes orthodontic treatment comfortable and hassle free!
Messy, uncomfortable putty impressions are a thing of the past at Blaine Orthodontics! Our state-of-the-art iTero Element scanner creates highly detailed digital images that allow Dr. Smith to properly diagnose your orthodontic needs and create an effective treatment plan to give you the smile you’ve always wanted.
