Our school lockers have been specially designed with the storage needs of different pupil age groups in mind. Available as full height 1800mm high  lockers, 1382mm mid height lockers or low height 900mm lockers, our range of school lockers offer the ideal choice for nurseries,  primary and junior schools, high schools, colleges and academies.

All our quality UK lockers are manufactured with a selection of different door colours and available with a choice of locking options suitable for educational establishments. You can choose from metal lockers, laminate door or mdf timber lockers to suit your budget and decor and don't forget we even offer staff lockers to keep valuables, keys and phones safe.

These education lockers will look stylish in any school corridor, hallway or changing room.  So if you want to have a tidier and hazard free school environment why not contact your trusted school locker supplier 3D Lockers for expert advice and pricing.

At 3D Lockers we are experts in all thing’s gym lockers. We have an extensive range of gym lockers available to order securely online. Our gym locker range is extremely popular with health clubs, leisure facilities, private gym studios, sports clubs, and swimming pools. We have supplied lockers to gyms up and down the country offering great prices and a great selection of high-quality products to choose from.

Quite often, gym goers will need a secure place to lock away their spare clothes, car keys, bags etc. while they use a gym or health club. Having a lockable space for personal items means users have peace of mind while they use the gym facilities.

We have a locker to suit any environment from cost effective standard metal lockers, £1-coin operated lockers, stylish MDF door lockers or swimming pool lockers with an aluminium body, designed for wet or humid changing areas. Available as single metal lockers, two door lockers, three door lockers, up to 8 door configurations in a range of compartment sizes and door colours. So if you are looking for white lockers, black lockers or any other standard doors such as red lockers, blue lockers or purple lockers to suit your gym decor, you are sure to find them in our gym locker section.

We also have plenty to choose from when it comes to locking options. Camlocks with 2 keys or hasp & staple locks to use with your own padlock are a great budget friendly lock option. However, if you need something more sophisticated, we can offer scroll combination locks or digital combination locks.

In addition, we also have a selection of cloakroom equipment to choose from to compliment your lockers. Browse through our slatted wooden benches, single- or double-sided island seats, hook benches and more. Available in a range of sizes and frame colours to suit any changing room area.

Not sure where to start? Why don’t you give our friendly sales team a call, we would love to chat about gym lockers and offer our expert advice along with a free quotation.

We also have a huge range of metal lockers which can be utilised for a variety of purposes in the workplace. From lockers for the NHS, staff lockers, valuables lockers or workwear lockers, if you need secure storage lockers you have come to the right place. Metal storage lockers come in handy to store clothing, bags, shoes, personal items, keys or mobile phones. Choose from a single door locker which offers full length hanging for garments, two door metal lockers right up to ten door lockers depending on what you are intending to store inside and how much space you need. You can opt to have your lockers in nests of 2 or 3 wide, this means lockers are joined during manufacture. It saves a little on cost and installation time.

Our range of metal storage lockers includes staff lockers for the office so employees have a lockable space to keep their belongings during the working day. If you have a no phone policy, then our metal mobile phone lockers are hugely popular. Available as a four-door phone locker or there is an eight-door version. Phone lockers are great for use in high security areas such as prisons, police stations or the MOD. In addition, our metal laptop lockers and iPad lockers offer a safe sanctuary for laptop and media devices.

Metal lockers come in a huge selection of door and frame colours. We can offer standard red door lockers, blue lockers, and green lockers. Or we also have more contemporary pastel door colours and autumn orange or purple lockers. If you have a specific door colour in mind, then we can also quote for bespoke paint colours. The choices are endless!

All metal lockers come with anti-bacterial protection as standard. An anti-microbial additive is applied during the powder paint process which is proven to reduce the spread of harmful germs and bacteria such as MRSA and E.coli by 99.9%. This unique feature makes our metal locker range an excellent choice for use in environments where cleanliness and hygiene are extremely important.

For larger projects we can offer a free site survey.  Our technical expert can visit your premises to measure and offer advice on locker options.  We can then design and produce a layout to best suit your space.  On site survey subject to area.  To take advantage of this survey please call our sales office on 01924 240291.

We do our utmost to offer a tailored service and to supply the perfect lockers to each and every one of our customers. For more information about our lockers, our services, or our quantity discounts, please don't hesitate to get in touch with us by phone or email, or by using our contact form.
