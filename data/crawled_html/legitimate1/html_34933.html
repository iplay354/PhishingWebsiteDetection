It is your nature

Breathwork with Richard Tryon as seen in

I've been breathing my entire life, so what is Breathwork?

The way you breathe affects every system and function in your body. If your breathing changes, whether consciously or unconsciously, then all these systems will change accordingly. Breathwork teaches you to take advantage of this wonderful phenomenon. By building a relationship with your breath, you can learn to improve every aspect of your physical, mental and emotional health and well-being.

A revolution in healing

Breathwork takes many forms, each offering unique benefits—from regulating the nervous system and enhancing athletic performance to facilitating meditative flow states. However, at the forefront of its evolution is its potential to induce altered states of consciousness, enabling rapid and safe emotional healing and deep spiritual inquiry, much like the therapeutic use of psychedelic medicines.

No magic pills or potions, just your breath

A growing body of scientific research is validating what people have experienced for decades: that breath alone can reduce stress, enhance self-esteem and self-awareness, improve overall well-being, alleviate anxiety and depression, and support healing from trauma-related disorders.
