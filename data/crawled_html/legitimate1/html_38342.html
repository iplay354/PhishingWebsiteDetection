Nursing Made Incredibly Easy!

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​The Nursing Made Incredibly Easy​ journal is based on Lippincott Williams & Wilkins' popular and successful Incredibly Easy! book series for nurses. The mission of this peer-reviewed journal is to support nursing students, educators, and life-long learners through the application of challenging clinical concepts and discussion of fundamental professional concerns in a refreshing and accessible manner. Articles are contributed by experienced clinicians, ensuring that the content is evidence-based. At least two continuing-education articles are offered in each issue.

The Nurse Practitioner

​​​​​​​With a circulation of 75,000, The Nurse Practitioner nursing journal is the leading monthly source for clinical, practical, cutting-edge information for advanced practice nurses and other primary care clinicians. Each issue presents peer-reviewed articles that range from clinical topics and research to political and practice issues to assist nurse practitioners. In addition, The Nurse Practitioner provides regular features, columns, continuing education, staff development education, and more.
