Dear beautiful soul

It is time to reconnect with your body, feel your emotions and feel safe within.

If you yearn to live a fulfilled, joyous and empowered life, it’s important you understand on a mental, physical, emotional and spiritual level what is holding you back and how to shift it lastingly.

Yes, life may have thrown you some curveballs, but this doesn’t define you for the rest of your life. You can break free from it, you can shake off what’s holding you back and you can live the life that you envision. For that, however, you have to connect back to your body.

And that’s what I am here for: Guiding you back into your body and assisting you to re-establish that oh so powerful body-mind connection in order to truly live the life you desire.

Hello, I am Anne-Christin

A dancing mermaid and a student of life.

Through my own personal journey and my work I have seen and experienced first-hand how important it is to understand your beliefs and patterns on a MENTAL level AND also on a BODILY LEVEL.

When body and mind are re-connected a powerful team is being reunited and life can unfold magically. You feel safe in your body and understand its cues and together with the mind you can take inspired and aligned actions towards your heart’s desires.

Following the above, life led me back to Mallorca living a life of joy and flow. It still has its challenges, but instead of being consumed by emotions and/or fear, I now dance with the sensations, knowing that I am safe and will find my way out…. Dancing and moving.

And so CAN YOU!
