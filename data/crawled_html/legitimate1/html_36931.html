Welcome

On the slopes of Epanomi, a few kilometres from the city of Thessaloniki, a meticulously-tended vineyard surrounds the modern winery, where the renowned Ktima Gerovassiliou wines are produced.

Ktima Gerovassiliou has been involved in vine cultivation and winemaking since 1981.
