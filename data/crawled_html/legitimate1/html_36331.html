Brian Conroy

Co Founder of the business in 2014, Brian is the current Managing Director at Pop Up Races, having moved into a full time role in the business in January 2017. A Chartered Accountant by trade, Brian became interested in running in 2013, when he ran his first 10km race. Having completed 10 marathons and an Ironman to date at various locations around the world, Brian has used his experiences at some of the worlds biggest running events to shape his vision for how Pop Up Races operates.
