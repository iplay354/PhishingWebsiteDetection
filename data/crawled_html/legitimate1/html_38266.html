ISAH Newsletter 016-2025 is out now!

Dear ISAH followers,

As the summer time begins, ISAH returns to bring you some new information about the Society in this mid-year e-Newsletter.

Hot topics today relate to:

- letter from the President,
- latest news from ISAH members,
- news on research projects from ISAH members,
- interesting publications,
- announcements of forthcoming congresses and meetings.

Enjoy reading the Newsletter of your Society!.

Enjoy reading the e-Newsletter of our Society!
|

You are all invited to submit information for the next newsletter. Please send information, news, text, photos and logo to: christelle.fablet@anses.fr

Take time to rest, recharge, and enjoy the simple joys of summer.
Wishing you a wonderful and peaceful holiday season!

Best regards,
On behalf of ISAH EB
Dr Christelle FABLET
Secretary General of ISAH
