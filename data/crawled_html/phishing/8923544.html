What is a digital business card?

Digital business cards are on online version of a "regular" busness card. It's how professionals share contact info and most important calls to action

How do digital business cards work?

A digital business card works by storing contact information and your calls to action in an electronic format that can be easily shared and accessed by others as a way to have your details saved to their device and futher your network

Do I, or the person I'm sharing my Linkaroo with need an app?

Not at all. We work via a browser, which every smartphone, tablet and computer has.

Can I have more than 1 Linkaroo digital business card?

Yes! You can activate an unlimited number of Linkaroo's in your account. Whether they are for yourself, or your team, it's simple to manage them all in one account.

I'm not a "techie" is this easy to use?

We've made Linkaroo as easy to use as possible, both as a user and recipient of your Linkaroo Digital Busienss Card

What support do you offer?

We offer email support to every one with an active subscription to the pro or agency packages.

I'm not good at designing or how to build my Linkaroo, can you help?

Yes. Although our platform is designed so that you can log in and update your information and details at any point, if you're struggling to get started, we can help set up your card, branding and calls to action for a one-off fee of £150.
