Welcome to Creeksidehomeloans.com, your one stop source for all your home financing needs!

At Creekside Mortgage,  Our experienced staff is committed to providing excellent customer service and integrity throughout the loan process.  We strive to educate and involve you in every facet of your loan application.  Our conveniently located office has access to the largest selection of lenders and programs both locally and nationwide, to best suit your financial needs.  The benefit is that this provides you with a variety of options whether you have excellent or less than perfect credit including programs like:

  • 0% down programs
  • First-Time Homebuyer Programs
  • Self-Employed Borrowers
  • No Mortgage Insurance Loans
  • Previous Bankruptcy (Chapter 7 & 13 Buyouts)
  • Debt consolidation
  • Interest only loans
  • Home improvement loans
  • Land Contract conversions
  • Investment Property loans
  • VA Loans
  • Modular/Manufactured housing
  • Bi-weekly and automatic bill pay available
