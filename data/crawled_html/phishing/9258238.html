Frequently asked questions.

What is Link-in-bio?

A "Link in Bio" is a single clickable link in a social media profile, usually leading to a page with multiple links or content. It's commonly used to direct followers to websites, blogs, products, or other important resources.

Why do we need link-in-bio?

We need a link-in-bio because most social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok allow only one clickable link in the bio. A link-in-bio solves this limitation by gathering all of your links in one single page where they can access your websites, products, blogs, or other key content in one place.

Can I connect my custom domain to Nuelink’s link-in-bio?

Currently, this feature is not possible, but it will be live very soon.

How many link-in-bio pages can I create?

You can create a link-in-bio page for each brand you have, the number of brands depends on which plan you are in.

Is it possible to use Nue Bio without using Nuelink?

Once you sign up for your 14-day free trial you get automatic access to both Nuelink and Nue Bio.
