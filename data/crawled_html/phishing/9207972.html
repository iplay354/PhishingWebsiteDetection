Why are you here?
This is a domain for kajomi email marketing software. You have probably come across this site because you received a newsletter that was sent by one of our customers using kajomi MAIL. We use this domain for tracking links, among other things. If you have any questions about the content of the newsletter you have received, please contact the person who sent you the newsletter. If you wish to unsubscribe from the newsletter, please follow the unsubscribe link in the email or use the ‘List unsubscribe’ link in the header. Complaints should initially be directed at the person responsible for sending the newsletter, and subsequently at us, using this email address: abuse@kajomi.de.
