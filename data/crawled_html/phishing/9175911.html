Lesson 1: A Life-Changing Decision


Households of Faith seek divine blessing by honoring God, pursuing his will, and obeying his commands. Members of each household work together to experience love, health, and peace in their home and generously extend this blessing to others. They live a life of meaning and purpose. 


The Abundant Life


Households of faith base their hope on a commitment that Jesus, the Messiah, made to his followers (John 10:10).

 

“A thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy.
 I have come so they may have life. 
I want them to have it in the fullest possible way.”

 

Sadly, reality often contradicts our wishes for this abundant life. The Bible says that our first ancestors, Adam and Eve, rebelled against God (Genesis 3), letting chaos loose into the world. Evil, disease, environmental decay and death are the consequences of this break with God’s lordship. Thankfully, God has made a way for mankind to be forgiven for this rebellion and receive his blessing.

 

The rebellion in our hearts not only separates us from God but is the cause of our broken relationships. Jealousy, quarreling, resentment, infidelity, manipulation, apathy, greed and hate are just a few of the ways this brokenness manifests itself. Our world is full of evil, corruption, wrongdoing, illness, and broken relationships. 

 

Inviting God to rule in our households doesn’t mean we won’t have trials. But he promises to help us overcome these troubles and give us victory over the destructive consequences of past decisions. He gives us a peace that nothing else in the world can offer us (John 16:33).

 

“I have told you these things, so that you can have peace because of me.
In this world you will have trouble. But be encouraged! 
I have won the battle over the world.”

 

The abundant life depends largely on making the right decisions. Jesus sent the Holy Spirit into the world to guide those who choose to follow him into truth. His work is to show us the consequences of our decisions and help us make the right choices. He even has the power to understand and lead us into the future (John 16:13).

 

“But when he, the Spirit of truth, comes, he will guide you into all the truth.
He will not speak on his own; he will speak only what he hears, 
and he will tell you what is yet to come.”

 

Households who commit to Jesus’ lordship will begin to feel his presence, peace, and blessing. Learning how to hear what the Holy Spirit is saying comes through reading the Holy Scriptures, discussion with other believers, and prayer. We encourage you and your household to embrace this kind of relationship with Jesus the Messiah. Even though your household may have serious issues, the Holy Spirit, sent by Jesus, is willing to help you face and overcome these problems.


A Big Change


Receiving the Spirit starts with making Jesus the Lord of our lives. This is a life-changing step. It means being willing to humble oneself before God, confessing our need for him, and asking his forgiveness. When we do this with sincerity and a real desire for change, the wonderful, amazing, and miraculous fact is that he forgives us, accepts us, and welcomes us joyously into his family. He then seals the relationship with the gift of the Holy Spirit. 

 

If you haven’t already asked Jesus into your life and home, do it now! Do it together with those in your household who want to make this commitment to pursue a life of blessing. Simply ask Jesus to come into your life and home, believing that he will. He is waiting patiently (Revelation 3:20).

 

“Look! I have been standing at the door, and I am constantly knocking. 
If anyone hears me calling him and opens the door, 
I will come in and fellowship with him and he with me.”

Assignment:

Discovery Bible Study (DBS) - John 3:16-21

Invite the Holy Spirit into your meeting, read the assigned text, retell the text in your own words, discuss its meaning with the group, listen for God’s instruction, and act on what you hear.


(Instructions for how to lead a Discovery Bible Study can be found in the HoF Facilitator guide.)


Group Activities:

  • Discussion: What does it mean to invite Jesus into your life and home? What does it take?


  • Homework: If Jesus begins to dwell with you, list the differences you might expect to see in your household.

Nothing changes if nothing changes.
