👋Welcome to RevoWallet!

What is RevoWallet?!

RevoWallet is a multi-cryptocurrency custodial wallet designed as a Telegram mini-app. Our mission is to make cryptocurrency as accessible, simple, and profitable as possible for everyone. Forget about complex interfaces and hidden fees — with RevoWallet, you can manage your assets directly within your familiar messenger.

Opening a wallet takes less than 5 seconds. You instantly gain access to storage, transfers, and staking with daily accruals. All operations are transparent, convenient, and free of internal fees.

Even if your funds are just “waiting for the right moment,” they can still generate income.

Key Features at Your Fingertips

  • 🔒 Secure Storage: Safely store your assets protected by a PIN code and biometric authentication (Face ID / Fingerprint).

  • 💸 Instant Transfers: Send and receive cryptocurrency as easily as sending a chat message.

  • 📈 Profitable Earning (Earn): Make your money work! Earn some of the highest passive income rates on the market through our flexible and fixed staking programs.

  • 👥 Referral Program: Recommend RevoWallet to those who could benefit from it and earn rewards for contributing to the growth of a high-quality crypto service confidently expanding into the global market.

Our Philosophy: Your Benefits We focus on three key aspects to deliver maximum value:

  1. Zero Fees We believe your money should work for you, not intermediaries. That’s why RevoWallet has no internal fees:

  • No fees for depositing funds into your wallet.

  • No fees for transferring your funds.

  • No fees for staking operations in the Earn module.

By default, you only pay the standard blockchain network fee (e.g., gas fees on Ethereum), which is required for network operations and beyond our control. However! Users with an active fixed staking plan (90-Day Locked Earn) of $100 or more in any cryptocurrency equivalent enjoy a privilege: all their transactions in RevoWallet are completely fee-free, including network fees. This way, you not only earn but also use the platform without additional costs, maximizing your benefits.

  1. High Profitability Your assets shouldn’t just sit idle. We offer tools to multiply them: exclusive rates in the Earn program and a generous referral program with real monetary rewards.

  2. Maximum Convenience Forget about complicated interfaces and the need to download multiple apps. All functionality is available directly in Telegram, making cryptocurrency management intuitive and straightforward.
