Tax your vehicle

You need to tax your vehicle to use it on the road.

Important

Your vehicle tax expired on December 25, 2025

Current status: NOT TAXED

You could be fined up to £80 if you do not tax your vehicle. The fine increases the longer your vehicle remains untaxed.

Tax your vehicle now

It usually takes around 5 minutes

How to pay

You can pay by:

  • debit or credit card
  • Direct Debit

What you need to know

  • Penalties: You may be fined if you do not tax your vehicle on time. The DVLA will send you a penalty notice.
  • Compliance record: Keeping your vehicle tax up to date helps you avoid penalties and maintain a good record with DVLA.
  • Legal requirement: You must tax your vehicle even if you do not have to pay anything, for example if you're exempt.
