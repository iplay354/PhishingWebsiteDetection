The Problem Jumper Solves

Crypto is multi-chain and difficult to navigate.

The DeFi landscape is increasingly fragmented, with multiple investment opportunities scattered across various blockchains. For users, this fragmentation means navigating Ethereum, alt-L1s, L2s, and app-chains, each of which operates in isolation. This lack of direct communication between blockchains has led to the creation of numerous cross-chain bridges, designed to move tokens and data between chains. However, these bridges often support a limited range of tokens, necessitating the use of decentralized exchanges (DEXs) or Solvers to swap tokens at both the source and destination.

The result? A cumbersome, treacherous journey filled with sketchy websites, illiquid tokens, and frustrating searches. For users seeking the best yield and reward opportunities, keeping track of everything has become a nearly full-time job.

How does Jumper Work?

Jumper automatically recommends the most efficient route for any token combination, balancing security, speed, number of clicks, and cost. Each trade includes detailed information, such as:

  • estimated time of tx
  • estimated gas fees
  • price impact and slippage
  • name of bridge + DEX or Solver being used
  • estimated providerfees
  • number of clicks necessary
  • conversion rate between the 2 asstes

🙂

Jumper offers users the flexibility to optimize their bridging experience according to their preferences, whether they prioritize speed, cost, or security.
