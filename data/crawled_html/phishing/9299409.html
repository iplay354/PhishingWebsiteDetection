Ledger Live – Securely Manage Your Crypto in One Place

What is Ledger Live?

Ledger Live is the official crypto management app from Ledger, designed to simplify how you buy, sell, and store digital assets. It’s a secure and intuitive platform that integrates directly with your Ledger hardware wallet to give you full control of your cryptocurrency holdings. With Ledger Live, you can manage your portfolio, check live market data, and access DeFi or staking services without compromising security.

The app is available on both desktop and mobile, providing seamless access to your crypto portfolio wherever you are. Whether you’re tracking Bitcoin, Ethereum, or thousands of other tokens, Ledger Live keeps everything organized in one secure dashboard.

Unlike web-based wallets that rely on external servers, Ledger Live operates locally on your device. This means your private keys remain offline and protected within your hardware wallet, offering unmatched security against hacks or phishing attacks.

Ledger Live does more than just store your crypto. It’s built to help you grow your portfolio with powerful financial tools. You can buy, sell, swap, and stake assets directly within the app using trusted partners. Ledger Live provides a modern, minimal interface that’s easy to use for beginners yet powerful enough for advanced users. From DeFi integration to NFT management, every feature is designed with privacy and simplicity in mind.
