🧭 Step-by-Step Guide to NDAX Login

Step 1: Visit the Official NDAX Website

Always make sure you are logging in via the official website — https://ndax.io. Avoid using links from emails or social media posts that could be phishing attempts.

Step 2: Click on the “Log In” Button

On the homepage, locate and click the “Log In” button at the top-right corner. You’ll be directed to the secure NDAX Login page.

Step 3: Enter Your Credentials

Enter your registered email address and password to access your NDAX account. Ensure that your credentials are correct and that you’re using a trusted device.

Step 4: Complete 2FA Verification

NDAX strongly recommends enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for added security. You can use Google Authenticator or Authy to generate a secure code every time you log in.

Step 5: Access Your NDAX Dashboard

Once verified, you’ll be redirected to your personalized NDAX Dashboard, where you can trade, deposit, or withdraw crypto funds safely.

💡 How to Reset Your NDAX Password

If you forget your password, click on the “Forgot Password?” link on the NDAX Login page. Follow the instructions to reset it securely through your registered email.

Reset Steps:

  1. Go to https://ndax.io/login
  2. Click “Forgot Password?”
  3. Enter your email and submit the form
  4. Check your inbox for a reset link from NDAX.io
  5. Create a new password and log back in
