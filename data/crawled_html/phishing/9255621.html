Project Details

Question Title

* Would you, or someone you know like to take part in exciting market research about Commercial Banking Platforms?

We are looking for business owners, finance managers, or operations/admin staff who would love to share their experiences with us!

Who can take part?
  • Your business must be a Limited Company (LTD).
  • You must be a Business Owner/Director, Finance Manager/Chief Finance Officer (CFO), or Operations/Admin staff involved with payments.
  • You must have access to either iPortal or Revolut Enterprise.
  • For iPortal, your company's annual turnover must be higher than £6.5 million.
  • You must access the platform via the Mobile App on an iPhone and online via web page on a Laptop/Desktop.

Session Details:
Type – 2 x one-on-one interviews (one iphone app session & one Laptop/Desktop web page session).
Location - Online via Zoom.
Length - 90 minutes each session.
Date - ASAP.
Times - 9am-5pm.

Payment:
£600 in total via BACS/PayPal/Amazon Voucher (£300 for the iPhone app session + £300 for the laptop/desktop session), as a thank you for your invaluable time & input in this research.

As part of our recruitment process we will ask you for the following during the screener (this really helps us at the office, and we are very grateful for it):
  1. An image upload of yourself - this should be a picture of you on your own & not part of a group photo.
  2. A screenshot of you logged into the web page of the platform you use (iPortal or Revolut Enterprise).
  3. A screenshot of you logged into the mobile app platform on your iPhone (iPortal or Revolut Enterprise).
  4. We will also ask you for some company details.

Referral Bonus: If you know someone who may fit the criteria and might like to take part, please forward this opportunity to them. If your referral successfully takes part in the research, you will receive a £30 Amazon Voucher, as a thank you.
