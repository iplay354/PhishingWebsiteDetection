Portland's weather swings from rainy winters to warm, dry summers create challenging conditions for stored belongings. Our climate-controlled storage units maintain consistent environmental standards, protecting your investments from:

  • Moisture damage and mold growth during wet seasons
  • Heat warping and cracking in summer months
  • Paper degradation and photo fading
  • Wood furniture splitting and metal corrosion
  • Electronic component failure from condensation

Expert Packing Tips for Short-Term Storage

With 79% of storage customers needing units for under 6 months, proper packing ensures your belongings stay pristine:

Use Quality Boxes

Invest in new boxes rated for weight. Reused boxes often collapse, risking damage to contents.

Label Everything

Mark boxes on multiple sides with contents and destination room for easy retrieval.

Protect Fragile Items

Wrap breakables individually in bubble wrap and use dish packs for glassware and china.
