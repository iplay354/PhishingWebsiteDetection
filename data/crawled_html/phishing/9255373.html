It's been more than a quarter of a century...
View this email in your browser

Dear Friend,

I wanted to send a quick note to say welcome to The Orphans Hands Family!

It's been more than a quarter of a century since we first set out to rescue orphans from human trafficking, enabling them to become missionaries and reach those in need. Since then, we've seen God do some incredible things through the prayers and support of friends like you.

You see, what many people don't know is that there are more slaves today than there have ever been in history.

In fact, right now, the International Labor Organization estimates there are 40.3 million people who are victims of global human trafficking.

Two of the highest-ranking source countries for trafficking? The Republic of Moldova and Ukraine.

That's why this work is so important.

Through God’s grace and your generosity, this ministry is reaching the lost and changing the nations of Moldova and Ukraine, all by helping the orphans and homeless who are most vulnerable.

From providing a home and a family at Vatra Village to feeding the homeless and giving life-changing medical help, we’re so glad to have you join us on this mission of bringing hope, mending hearts and changing lives.

We look forward to sharing more stories of hope with you in the days ahead. In the meantime, welcome to the family and thank you for supporting Orphans Hands!

 Philip Cameron
P.S. — Want to stay up to date on all that’s happening? Be sure to follow us on Facebook and Instagram.
