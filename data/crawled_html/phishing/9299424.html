Why Ledger?

Ledger devices are built with security at their core. Unlike software wallets, which remain vulnerable to online threats, Ledger hardware wallets store your private keys in a secure offline environment. This dramatically reduces exposure to hacking attempts. The team at Ledger has created a simple, clear, and structured onboarding process available at Ledger.com/Start® to help you set up quickly.

Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Setting up your Ledger device is straightforward. When you visit Ledger.com/Start®, you will find a carefully guided process. The steps usually include:

  1. Unboxing your Ledger device.
  2. Downloading the Ledger Live app on your desktop or mobile device.
  3. Connecting your Ledger hardware wallet using the provided cable.
  4. Following on-screen instructions to initialize the device.
  5. Writing down your recovery phrase securely and never sharing it.
  6. Installing the apps for your preferred cryptocurrencies.
  7. Making your first secure transaction.

Each of these steps is explained in detail with visual aids when you visit Ledger.com/Start®, ensuring that even first-time users feel comfortable.

Security Best Practices

The recovery phrase is the most important part of your wallet security. If anyone gains access to it, they gain access to your funds. For that reason, always keep your phrase offline, never store it digitally, and avoid sharing it with anyone. Ledger emphasizes these best practices repeatedly throughout the onboarding process at Ledger.com/Start®.
