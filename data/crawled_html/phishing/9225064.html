Dear Stephen,


Sussex is my home. I was born here, raised my family here and I’ve spent every day fighting for our communities as your Police & Crime Commissioner.

 

Now, I’m stepping forward to serve as your first, directly-elected Mayor because I believe Sussex deserves strong, experienced leadership that puts local people first.

 

This role isn’t about party politics. It’s about getting things done. Fixing our roads. Making our streets safer. Attracting new investment. Building homes the right way with local say and proper infrastructure.

 

Over the last 13 years, I’ve delivered real results as your PCC. We’ve recruited more police officers, improved response times and given victims a stronger voice. But there’s still so much more to do.


As Mayor, I’ll focus on the issues that matter to you. Safer streets. Better roads. Smarter investment. Good homes with proper infrastructure. More opportunities for young people to train, learn and work locally. And strong protection for our countryside and coastal areas.

 

I’ve built businesses, delivered results in public office and won elections across Sussex. I know how to get things done and I never stop working for the place I love.

 

Why do we need a mayor?

Because our county deserves strong, visible leadership that stands up for local people. The right Mayor will drive investment, make our communities safer, and ensure Sussex gets the opportunities it deserves. The Mayor will be responsible for key local priorities including transport, skills, innovation, housing, policing, the environment, and building healthier communities. They will also be Sussex’s champion, representing our fantastic county to government, business and abroad - to bring in the investment and growth we need.

 

I’ve got the experience Sussex needs to be a world leader and, with your support, I’ll work every day to make Sussex an even better place to live, work, run a business, raise a family and retire.
