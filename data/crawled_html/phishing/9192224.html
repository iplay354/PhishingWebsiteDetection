Vibe: Tigermilk is the place to bookmark for next time you’re in the West End looking for somewhere to grab dinner or drinks. Less than two-minutes’ walk from Tottenham Court Road station, this hidden Latin gem is already quickly gaining traction. The showstoppingly beautiful interiors are teamed with a carefully curated menu and friendly service.

What we ordered: Must-order dishes include the made-at-your-table guac, Tuna Turner (AKA fresh tuna tostada), Pipián Verde (slow-cooked chicken cooked in spicy green mole), and, if you’re T-totalling, the alcohol-free margarita.

Price point: ££
