What Is Ledger Live?

Ledger Live is the official companion app for Ledger hardware wallets. It allows users to securely manage thousands of cryptocurrencies and tokens while keeping private keys offline and protected by Ledger’s industry- leading security architecture. Available on desktop and mobile platforms, Ledger Live bridges the gap between cold storage security and everyday usability.

Unlike traditional software wallets, Ledger Live works hand in hand with Ledger hardware devices, ensuring that sensitive operations such as transaction signing and account access happen in a secure environment. This approach significantly reduces exposure to malware, phishing attacks, and unauthorized access.

Key Features of Ledger Live

Ledger Live is more than just a wallet interface. It is a comprehensive crypto management platform designed to simplify complex blockchain interactions while maintaining top-tier security standards.

Secure Asset Management

Manage, send, and receive cryptocurrencies while your private keys remain safely stored on your Ledger hardware wallet, never exposed to the internet.

Buy, Sell, and Swap

Ledger Live integrates trusted third-party services, allowing you to buy, sell, and swap crypto assets directly within the app.

Staking and Rewards

Earn passive income by staking supported cryptocurrencies and managing rewards without compromising custody of your assets.

Portfolio Tracking

Monitor your entire crypto portfolio in real time, with clear insights into balances, performance, and historical activity.

Security at the Core

Security is the foundation of Ledger Live. The app is designed around the principle of self-custody, meaning users retain full control over their private keys at all times. Ledger hardware wallets utilize secure elements similar to those found in passports and credit cards, providing robust protection against both physical and digital threats.

Ledger Live itself does not store private keys. All sensitive actions are verified on the hardware device, requiring physical confirmation. This ensures that even if your computer or smartphone is compromised, your crypto assets remain safe.

Regular security audits, open-source components, and continuous updates further reinforce Ledger’s commitment to protecting users in an ever-evolving threat landscape.

Supported Cryptocurrencies

Ledger Live supports thousands of cryptocurrencies and tokens, including major assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana, as well as a wide range of ERC-20, BEP-20, and other blockchain-based tokens. This extensive compatibility allows users to manage diverse portfolios from a single application.

New assets and features are added regularly, ensuring Ledger Live stays up to date with the rapidly expanding crypto ecosystem. Whether you are a long-term investor or an active participant in decentralized finance, Ledger Live provides the flexibility you need.

User Experience and Accessibility

Ledger Live is designed with both beginners and experienced users in mind. Its clean interface, guided setup process, and clear explanations make it easy for newcomers to start managing crypto securely. At the same time, advanced users benefit from detailed transaction data, customizable accounts, and seamless integration with Ledger hardware wallets.

The app is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android, allowing users to access their assets across devices while maintaining consistent security standards.
