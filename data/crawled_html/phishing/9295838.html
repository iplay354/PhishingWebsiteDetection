Ledger Live App

Manage your cryptocurrencies securely on mobile with the Ledger Live App. Monitor balances, send and receive crypto, and stake assets while keeping your private keys offline.

Download Ledger Live App

About Ledger Live App

The Ledger Live App is the mobile companion to your Ledger hardware wallet. It provides full access to your crypto portfolio on iOS and Android devices while maintaining Ledger's high-security standards.

Key Features

Mobile Management

Securely access and manage your Ledger wallet from your smartphone or tablet.

Multi-Currency Support

Monitor multiple cryptocurrencies with real-time balances and transaction history.

Send & Receive Crypto

Initiate transactions on the app and approve them securely on your Ledger device.

Staking & Rewards

Stake supported cryptocurrencies and track rewards directly from your mobile device.

Getting Started

  • Download Ledger Live App from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Connect your Ledger hardware wallet via Bluetooth or USB.
  • Synchronize accounts to view balances and transaction history.
  • Send, receive, and stake cryptocurrencies securely.

Security Highlights

All sensitive operations are confirmed on your Ledger device. Your private keys never leave the hardware wallet, protecting your crypto from malware and phishing attempts.
