DHOLERA SMART CITY PROJECT

Dholera Smart City is set to become India's first Greenfield Smart City, revolutionizing urban planning and development in the country. Located in the state of Gujarat, Dholera is being developed as a modern, sustainable city that will cater to the needs of its residents and promote economic growth. Dholera SIR is strategically located between the cities of Ahmedabad and Bhavnagar, making it an ideal location for businesses looking to set up operations in the region. The development of Dholera SIR is progressing rapidly, with key infrastructure projects such as roads, utilities, and industrial parks already underway. 

Dholera Smart City is an upcoming greenfield smart city located in the state of Gujarat, India. Dholera Smart City is part of the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) project and is being developed as a special investment region Dholera SIR.The project is expected to be fully functional within the next few years, transforming the region into a vibrant and modern urban center.

Dholera Smart City Project is a Fastest Growing City In Gujarat, India. Dholera SIR plans a world class Infrastructure Development. Prime Minister Modi imagined Dholera. Dholera SIR is the first planned Greenfield Smart City in Gujarat. Dholera Smart City is the most promising and fastest developing city of India. Dholera SIR is located in the Ahmedabad district of Gujarat and spans over 920 square kilometers. It is being developed as a greenfield industrial city and is part of the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor project.

FAQ's

Dholera SIR is an upcoming greenfield smart city located in the state of Gujarat, India. It is part of the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) project and is being developed as a special investment region (SIR).

Dholera is designed to be a smart city, incorporating features such as world-class infrastructure, efficient transportation systems, uninterrupted power and water supply, state-of-the-art telecommunications, and advanced waste management systems.

Several major projects are planned for Dholera, including the Dholera International Airport, Dholera Industrial City, Dholera Smart City, and various residential and commercial developments. These projects aim to create a sustainable and self-sufficient city.

Investment opportunities in Dholera are available through various channels. Interested investors can explore opportunities in real estate, industrial projects, infrastructure development, and other sectors through partnerships with the Dholera Industrial City Development Limited (DICDL) and other authorized entities.

The development of Dholera is an ongoing process and is expected to be completed in phases over several years. Some infrastructure and industrial projects are already underway, while others are in the planning or implementation stages.

Investing in Dholera offers numerous benefits, such as access to a prime location in a rapidly developing region, potential for high returns on investment, a business-friendly environment, and the opportunity to be part of an innovative and sustainable smart city.
