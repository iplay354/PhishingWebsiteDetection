Slash Business Dashboard Login

Slash Login gives you secure access to the ultimate banking platform for the hustler economy. Manage your business finances, issue unlimited virtual cards, and automate revenue splitting effortlessly.

Monitor your cash flow with real-time transaction tracking, manage subscriptions, and keep your personal and business expenses separate with ease across desktop and mobile devices.

How to use your Slash login

Once logged in, you can instantly create virtual cards for ad spend or subscriptions, set spend limits, invite team members with specific roles, and view detailed spending reports. Always ensure your contact method for 2FA is up to date.

Scale with virtual cards

Use the dashboard to generate cards specifically for platforms like Facebook Ads, Google Ads, or SaaS tools. This helps isolate merchant risk and prevents one compromised service from affecting your entire budget.

Tips for your first Slash access

Customize your dashboard to separate revenue streams from different side hustles or businesses. Set up auto-splitting rules to pay yourself and your partners instantly as money comes in.

Optimize your workflow

Connect your external bank accounts for seamless funding, utilize the "Slash Earn" features if available, and download CSV statements for easy accounting at the end of the month.

Steps to complete your first login

  1. Navigate to the official Slash website.
  2. Click the Login button in the top right corner.
  3. Select your login method (usually Phone or Email).
  4. Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your device.
  5. Verify your identity if prompted on a new device.
  6. You will land on your Slash banking dashboard.

If you have trouble receiving the code, ensure your phone has signal or check your email spam folder for the login link.

Quick fixes for login issues

Ensure your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) is up to date. If the OTP code isn't arriving, wait 60 seconds and click "Resend". Clear your cache if the dashboard fails to load. For account locks, contact Slash support via the chat widget.
