Why Choose Trézór Bridge®?

In today's fragmented blockchain ecosystem, Trézór Bridge®™ provides the critical infrastructure needed for seamless cross-chain communication. Our patented cryptographic technology ensures that your digital assets remain secure during transfers while maintaining the highest standards of decentralization.

Unlike traditional bridges that rely on centralized custodians, Trézór Bridge® implements a novel approach using zero-knowledge proofs and multi-party computation. This means you can transfer assets between chains with the same confidence as on-chain transactions, without compromising on speed or cost-efficiency.

Our solution supports all major blockchain networks and is continuously updated to integrate with emerging protocols. Whether you're a DeFi platform looking to expand your cross-chain capabilities or an institutional investor requiring secure asset transfers, Trézór Bridge®™ delivers the reliability and security you demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Trézór Bridge® different from other cross-chain solutions? +

Trézór Bridge®™ employs a unique combination of zero-knowledge proofs and secure multi-party computation, eliminating the need for trusted intermediaries. Our architecture ensures that private keys are never stored in a single location, dramatically reducing attack vectors while maintaining high throughput.

Which blockchains are currently supported? +

We currently support Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, and Solana, with Polkadot and Cosmos integration coming in Q3. Our modular design allows for rapid integration of new chains based on market demand.

How does the security model work? +

Trézór Bridge® uses a decentralized network of nodes that collectively manage bridge operations without any single point of failure. Each transaction requires cryptographic signatures from a threshold of nodes, ensuring that assets are always protected even if some nodes are compromised.

What are the transaction fees? +

Fees are typically 0.1-0.3% of the transfer amount, significantly lower than most custodial solutions. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom fee structures based on volume commitments.
