Oscar Sun Apartments

The Oscar Sun Apartments are located in the first leg of Halkidiki, on the Kassandra peninsula of cosmopolitan Kallithea.
Located 400 meters from the magnificent coast, and just two minutes walk from the village center, the excellent location and the beautiful surroundings are an attractive destination for your vacation.
The quiet family atmosphere of the Oscar Sun Apartments with the services offered, intends to make your vacation the most relaxing and beautiful ever.
It is ideal for customers who want a quiet stay close to all popular destinations.

 
 

There Are 3 Types Of Rooms

We are pleased to offer a variety of room types to suit your needs during your stay with us. Choose from our comfortable double, spacious triple, or accommodating quadruple rooms. Whether you're traveling solo, with a partner, or as a group, we have the perfect accommodation for you.
