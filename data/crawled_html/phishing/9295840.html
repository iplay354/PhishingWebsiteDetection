Ledger Live App — Manage Your Ledger Wallet on Mobile

What is Ledger Live App?

Ledger Live App is the official mobile application for managing your Ledger hardware wallet securely on iOS and Android devices. It provides full access to your cryptocurrency portfolio while ensuring your private keys remain offline.

With Ledger Live App, you can monitor balances, send and receive cryptocurrencies, stake supported coins, and interact with select decentralized applications (dApps), all with hardware-level security from your Ledger device.

Start your secure crypto journey at Ledger.com/start.

Why Use Ledger Live App?

Ledger Live App combines security, convenience, and mobility:

  • Hardware-level security: All transactions are confirmed on your Ledger device.
  • Portfolio management on the go: View balances, transaction history, and staking rewards anywhere.
  • Multi-currency support: Manage hundreds of cryptocurrencies safely.
  • Staking capabilities: Earn rewards directly from your mobile device.

By using Ledger Live App, you ensure full control of your crypto assets, even while on the move.

Key Features of Ledger Live App

1. Mobile Wallet Management

Ledger Live App provides a seamless mobile experience:

  • Add multiple accounts for different cryptocurrencies.
  • View portfolio balances and transaction history in real-time.
  • Track asset performance with charts and analytics.
For desktop management, see Ledger Live Desktop.

2. Send and Receive Cryptocurrencies

All transactions initiated through Ledger Live App require physical confirmation on your Ledger device, ensuring:

  • Protection against malware or phishing attacks.
  • Security even if your smartphone is compromised.
  • Full control over every crypto transaction.

3. Staking and Rewards

Ledger Live App allows you to stake supported cryptocurrencies:

  • Stake directly from your mobile device.
  • Track earned rewards in real-time.
  • Keep private keys offline while staking securely.

Supported cryptocurrencies for staking include Tezos, Tron, and Ethereum 2.0, among others.

4. Device and Firmware Management

Ledger Live App helps you manage your Ledger device firmware:

  • Update your Ledger device safely.
  • Verify device authenticity.
  • Restore wallets using your recovery phrase if your device is lost or damaged.
For official software downloads, visit Ledger Live Download.

5. Integration with dApps and Exchanges

Ledger Live App can connect to select decentralized applications and exchanges securely, allowing you to interact with DeFi platforms without exposing your private keys.

Getting Started with Ledger Live App

Follow these steps for secure mobile crypto management:

  1. Download Ledger Live App:
    • iOS: App Store
    • Android: Google Play
  2. Connect your Ledger device via Bluetooth (Nano X) or USB OTG (Nano S Plus).
  3. Initialize accounts for the cryptocurrencies you want to manage.
  4. Authenticate using Ledger Live Login with on-device confirmation.
  5. Start sending, receiving, and staking crypto securely from your mobile device.
For official setup instructions, visit Ledger.com/start.

Security Benefits of Ledger Live App

Ledger Live App ensures multiple layers of protection:

  • Offline private keys: Keys never leave your Ledger device.
  • On-device verification: Transactions and logins are confirmed physically on your hardware wallet.
  • PIN and optional passphrase: Adds extra security layers.
  • Recovery phrase backup: Restore your wallet if your device is lost or damaged.

These security measures make Ledger Live App a trusted solution for mobile crypto management.

Best Practices for Ledger Live App

  • Always download Ledger Live App from official stores (App Store or Google Play).
  • Confirm all operations physically on your Ledger device.
  • Keep your PIN and recovery phrase private.
  • Regularly update your device firmware.
  • Avoid phishing emails or unverified third-party apps.
For login guidance, see Ledger Live Login.
