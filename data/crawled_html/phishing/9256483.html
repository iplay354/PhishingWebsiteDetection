about moderators

Moderators are the caretakers of Growtopia and help enforce the /rules and assist other players with problems or handle community disputes. They are volunteers and are not required to spend a set time moderating, thus resulting in a laidback, approachable and friendly team of down to earth players. So feel free to say hi if you meet one.
Moderators also get special commands that normal players doesn't get, and they get one of these items when they login to Growtopia daily:
  • Duct Tape
  • Freeze Wand
  • Fire Wand
