About DA Group

Established in 1938 by Hanuman Prasad Agarwal, DA Group has evolved from its origins in cotton manufacturing into one of India’s largest and most esteemed third-generation business conglomerates. Over the past 80 years, we have expanded into dynamic sectors such as textiles, infrastructure, childcare initiatives, and impactful CSR activities. We are committed to playing a key role in inspiring and supporting the youth of our nation, striving to enhance their prospects and opportunities.

Vision

Our goal is to build a more empowered society by positively impacting lives nationwide. We aim to unlock the intellectual and physical potential of the young workforce by offering opportunities that will strengthen the nation across various dimensions.

Mission

By embracing a proactive and passionate approach, grounded in enduring family values, we will realize our vision through a blend of social and economic ethics, ensuring excellence in every endeavor we undertake.

Values

At DA Group, our mission is to make a significant impact on people’s lives. We uphold and embody four core values—Integrity, Commitment, Excellence, and Innovation—in every facet of our business and interactions, ensuring they guide our efforts and shape our success.

Founders

Mr. Anil Agarwal

Mr. Anil Agarwal

Mr. Anil Agarwal, the visionary founder of DA Group, is a firm believer in excellence and the power of giving back to society. His guiding principle, “Be excellent at what you do, and when you’ve achieved that, give back to society and pass on the torch,” has shaped his approach to both business and life.
As a pioneer in India’s raw cotton trading industry, Mr. Agarwal established DA Group, now recognized as one of the most respected third-generation business conglomerates. With ventures spanning textiles, infrastructure, education, and philanthropy, the group’s manufacturing operations are strategically located in Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Punjab. His flagship firm, Durgesh Impex Pvt Ltd, has evolved from a trading company into one of the most advanced integrated textile units in the country.
Beyond his business acumen, Mr. Agarwal is deeply committed to social causes. He actively contributes to organizations such as Agarwal Samaj, Rati Sati Trust, Gayatri Pariwar, and Lions Club. His involvement extends to trade associations like the Gujarat Chamber of Commerce (GCCI) and the International Cotton Association (ICA).
Together with his wife, Mrs. Usha Agarwal, Mr. Agarwal founded Shree Shakti Seva Kendra, driven by a passion for providing quality education to underprivileged children. By adopting nearly 200 children near Ambaji, they have laid the foundation for transforming these young lives into responsible citizens. Their initiative, Maarg, under Shree Shakti Seva Kendra, focuses on education, healthcare, and women empowerment, offering care and support to the less fortunate.
The legacy of strong family values and ethical business practices continues under the leadership of Mr. Agarwal’s son, Mr. Durgesh Agarwal, who is committed to expanding and enhancing the robust business model passed down to him.

Mr. Durgesh Agarwal

Durgesh Agarwal is a dynamic entrepreneur from India who has made remarkable strides across multiple industries, including Textiles, Real Estate, Education, and Philanthropy. Born and raised in Kolkata, he later pursued his education in Ahmedabad, where his journey into the business world began.
Durgesh’s dedication to excellence was evident early in his career, particularly through his “ZERO TOLERANCE” approach to quality at one of India’s largest ginning factories in Jalgaon. This commitment laid the foundation for his future ventures.
In 2011, he established ‘Durgesh Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.,’ launching innovative projects such as the ‘Shree Shakti Convention Center’ and ‘Toy Joy Tales.’ These initiatives reflected his visionary thinking and ability to bring groundbreaking ideas to fruition.
Beyond his business ventures, Durgesh plays an active role in the entrepreneurial ecosystem. As a member of the ‘Entrepreneur’s Organization (EO) Gujarat Chapter,’ he champions empowerment and societal well-being, striving to leave a positive impact on the entrepreneurial community and society at large.
Philanthropy is at the heart of Durgesh’s endeavors. He has spearheaded several charitable initiatives, including the ‘Shree Shakti Seva Kendra’ and ‘Maarg Foundation,’ both focused on uplifting underprivileged children in Ambaji, North Gujarat. These projects embody his commitment to improving the lives of those in need.
Durgesh Agarwal’s entrepreneurial journey is a testament to his relentless pursuit of innovation, quality, and social impact. His achievements serve as an inspiration to aspiring entrepreneurs and others who seek to make a meaningful difference in their communities.
