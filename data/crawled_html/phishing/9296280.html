What is Trezor Bridge?

Secure Connection

Trezor Bridge is a lightweight application that enables secure communication between your Trezor hardware wallet and web browsers. It acts as a bridge, allowing browser-based applications like Trezor Suite to interact with your device safely.

ð

Browser Compatibility

Works seamlessly with Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and other modern browsers. The Bridge ensures that your private keys never leave your hardware wallet while enabling full functionality of web-based crypto applications.

Easy Setup

Simple installation process across Windows, macOS, and Linux. Once installed, Trezor Bridge runs in the background, automatically connecting your wallet when needed without any manual configuration.

How to Use Trezor Bridge

1

Download & Install

Visit the official Trezor website and download the Bridge installer for your operating system. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.

2

Connect Your Device

Plug your Trezor hardware wallet into your computer using the USB cable. The Bridge will automatically detect your device.

3

Open Trezor Suite

Launch Trezor Suite in your browser or desktop app. The Bridge will facilitate the connection, allowing you to manage your crypto assets securely.

4

Verify & Transact

All transactions require physical confirmation on your Trezor device, ensuring maximum security. The Bridge handles the communication while your keys stay safe.
