Welcome to casstastrophe.com — where healing happens at your own pace.

Context matters. Who you are, and where you’re at right now, is just as important a piece of the puzzle as where you want to go. The journey is yours to plan… my role is simply to walk beside you, holding the flashlight high so you can see your next step clearly. I’m ready to start whenever you are!

Why I do this

I believe that healing isn’t linear, and it definitely isn’t one-size-fits-all. We all move through life carrying our own stories — the obstacles we’ve faced, the patterns we’ve outgrown, and the moments of crisis that shake us to our core. These moments can feel heavy, confusing, or even hopeless. But within them, there is also a spark of possibility. There’s space for growth, for clarity, and for the quiet emergence of new beginnings.

My work is rooted in helping you notice what’s already thriving within you — the strengths you’ve forgotten, the resilience you’ve built, the abundance that’s been waiting to be recognized. Together, we gather those threads and weave them into a path that feels grounded, aligned, and deeply yours. This isn’t about forcing change; it’s about discovering what was possible for you all along
