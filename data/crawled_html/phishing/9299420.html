Getting Started with Your Ledger Device

Why Choose Ledger?

Ledger hardware wallets provide the highest level of security for your cryptocurrency assets. Unlike software wallets, Ledger devices keep your private keys offline, protected from online threats. With over 3 million units sold worldwide, Ledger is the trusted choice for both beginners and experts in the cryptocurrency space.

Military-Grade Security

Ledger utilizes Secure Element chips, similar to those used in credit cards and passports, providing CC EAL5+ certification level security for your digital assets.

Multi-Currency Support

Manage Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, and more than 1,800 other cryptocurrencies and tokens all in one secure interface.

User-Friendly Interface

Ledger Live app provides an intuitive dashboard to manage your portfolio, send and receive crypto, and install apps with ease.

Backup & Recovery

Your 24-word recovery phrase allows you to restore your accounts if your device is lost or damaged, ensuring you never lose access to your assets.

Setting Up Your Ledger Device

Getting started with your new Ledger device is straightforward. Follow these steps to ensure your cryptocurrency is secure from the moment you begin.

1

Download Ledger Live

Visit our official website to download Ledger Live, the desktop application that you'll use to manage your crypto assets. Always ensure you're downloading from our official source to avoid phishing attempts.

2

Initialize Your Device

Connect your Ledger device to your computer using the provided USB cable. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your device and generate your unique 24-word recovery phrase.

3

Secure Your Recovery Phrase

Write down your 24-word recovery phrase on the provided recovery sheet and store it in a secure location. Never share this phrase with anyone or store it digitally.

4

Install Apps

Use Ledger Live to install apps for each cryptocurrency you want to manage. These apps enable your device to support different blockchain protocols.

5

Create Accounts

Generate cryptocurrency accounts in Ledger Live. Each account has a unique public address that you can use to receive funds.

Best Practices for Security

While Ledger devices provide industry-leading security, your actions play a crucial role in protecting your assets. Follow these guidelines to maximize your security:

Keep Your Recovery Phrase Offline

Your recovery phrase is the key to your cryptocurrency. Never take a photo of it, type it into a computer, or store it in cloud storage. Keep it written on paper or metal in a secure location.

Verify Transactions on Device

Always verify transaction details on your Ledger device's screen before confirming. This ensures that malicious software on your computer cannot alter transaction details.

Keep Firmware Updated

Regularly update your Ledger device's firmware through Ledger Live to benefit from the latest security enhancements and features.

Beware of Phishing Attempts

Ledger will never ask for your 24-word recovery phrase. Be cautious of emails, websites, or support agents requesting this information.
