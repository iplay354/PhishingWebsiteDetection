Ledger Live

Ledger Live is the official application for Ledger hardware wallets, offering a secure way to buy, sell, swap, stake, and manage your cryptocurrencies all in one place.

What is Ledger Live?

Ledger Live is the companion app developed by Ledger to make crypto management simple and secure. It provides a user-friendly dashboard that connects directly with your Ledger hardware wallet, ensuring your private keys never leave your device while giving you full control of your digital assets.

Key features of Ledger Live

  • Secure asset management: Send, receive, and store your crypto with hardware-level protection.
  • Portfolio tracking: Monitor your entire crypto portfolio in real-time.
  • Buy & sell crypto: Purchase and sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other supported coins through trusted partners.
  • Swap & stake: Exchange one crypto for another and earn rewards by staking supported assets.
  • Multi-asset support: Manage thousands of coins and tokens, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, and ERC-20 tokens.
  • Cross-platform availability: Available for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.

How to download and install Ledger Live

  1. Visit the official site: ledger.com/ledger-live.
  2. Download the app for your operating system (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, or Android).
  3. Install the application and connect your Ledger hardware wallet.
  4. Set up your accounts and start managing your crypto safely.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Ledger Live free?

Yes, Ledger Live is a free application available on desktop and mobile. Some third-party services within the app, like buying or swapping, may charge fees.

Can I use Ledger Live without a Ledger device?

You can explore the portfolio tracking features, but full functionality requires a Ledger hardware wallet for maximum security.

How secure is Ledger Live?

Ledger Live is highly secure when used with a Ledger device. Your private keys remain offline, and all critical actions must be confirmed on the hardware wallet.

Which coins can I manage in Ledger Live?

Ledger Live supports over 5,500 cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Dogecoin, and ERC-20 tokens. The list continues to grow with updates.

Does Ledger Live support staking?

Yes. You can stake assets like Ethereum, Tezos, Polkadot, and more directly through Ledger Live to earn rewards.
