Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about Ledger Live

Do I need a Ledger device to use Ledger Live?

While you can download and install Ledger Live without a Ledger device, you'll need a Ledger hardware wallet (Nano S, Nano S Plus, or Nano X) to access all security features and manage your cryptocurrencies.

What cryptocurrencies does Ledger Live support?

Ledger Live supports over 1,500 cryptocurrencies and tokens, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Polkadot, and all ERC-20 tokens. New coins are regularly added through app updates.

Is Ledger Live free to use?

Yes, Ledger Live is completely free to download and use. There are no subscription fees or hidden costs. You only pay for blockchain transaction fees when sending cryptocurrencies.

How often is Ledger Live updated?

Ledger Live receives regular updates with new features, security enhancements, and support for additional cryptocurrencies. Updates are typically released every 2-4 weeks.

Can I use Ledger Live on multiple devices?

Yes, you can install Ledger Live on multiple computers and mobile devices. Your account and crypto assets are accessed through your Ledger hardware wallet, so you can securely manage your portfolio from any device.
