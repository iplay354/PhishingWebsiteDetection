How to Secure Your Crypto with Ledger Live

1. Use a Ledger Hardware Wallet

Security starts with cold storage. By combining Ledger Live with a Ledger hardware wallet (Ledger Nano X or Nano S Plus), you can store your private keys offline, away from hackers, malware, and phishing attacks. Even if your computer is compromised, your crypto remains safe.

2. Manage Your Assets Securely with Ledger Live

Ledger Live is your all-in-one crypto management solution. Send and receive crypto, monitor real-time balances, track your portfolio performance, and even stake coins – all in a secure environment. Every transaction is verified and approved directly on your device.

3. Stay Protected with Constant Updates

We regularly release firmware and app updates to stay ahead of threats. You’ll receive notifications within Ledger Live whenever an update is available for your device or supported coins. Keeping your device updated ensures maximum security.

4. Protect Your Recovery Phrase

Your 24-word recovery phrase is the key to your funds. Never share it online. Use a secure offline storage solution, such as Ledger’s Cryptosteel or Billfodl, to ensure it’s protected from fire, water, or theft. If someone gets your recovery phrase, they can access your assets — no exceptions.
