Hi guys today I will be talking about my fragrance collection ranked by least favorite to most favorite.

7. Versace Blue jeans: It hurts to put this in last because it has a nice powdery lemon candy smell but is a bit one dimensional and if your overspray it, it is too strong.

6. Dolce and Gabbana: Light Blue: Now don't get me wrong I love this scent as it is fresh and citrussy warm, however its projection SUCKS I heavily regret not getting the Eau De Parfum as it fixes this problem and is critically acclaimed. The only time I wear this is on warmer days or on trips as I have a travel size version.

5. Polo Red: This is my most complemented cologne, but it has fallen off in my tastes. It smells like warm ginger with strong notes of clary sage. I wear this in the winter lots as it has a Christmas vibe.

​4. Valentino Born in Roma Green Stravaganza:  This is a great floral scent with warmth in the undertones. Again, it is a bit one dimensional, but overall amazing.

3.  My custom scent from Grand Cayman: I love this cologne as it is warm but also very tropical. I bought this from the Cayman Perfume Lab, and it has floral notes with fruity undertones and a initial sweetness. This is my 3rd go to everyday wear and is best in the spring or fall.

2.Bleu de Chanel: This is my everyday wear and has a fresh citrus smell that never gets old. This has great projection and cedar undertones. I wear this to indoor events and in the spring and early summer.

And finally,
#1: Invictus Parfum by Rabanne: I LOVE this scent and would wear it every day, but I am almost out of it. It is aromatic and musky but at the same time has a beautiful citrus twist after a bit of projection. I'm telling you, when the citrus undertone hits, I want to shed a tear. This is my favorite scent I own and is a top buy.

THANKS FOR READING!
